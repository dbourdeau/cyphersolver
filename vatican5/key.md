# Recovered key

Simon Klee's recovered key for AAV Segr. Stato, Spagna 1A, ff. 70r–73v. Source and notation:
[“The Farnese letter”](https://simonklee.dk/farnese-letter).

```text
5=a 8=e 6=i 4=o 1=u/v 7=n 2=r 0=s 3=m
03=l 73=d 80=t 57=p 27=c 72=b 37=f 75=g 07=z
81=et 9=NULL
```

A caret marks the dotted digit: `2^7` means a dot on `2`; `27^` means a dot on `7`.

```text
27^=ce 2^7=qua 7^2=que 72^=ci 57^=non 5^7=qui
0^5=S.S.ta 40^=S.M.ta 6^0=V.S.
```
