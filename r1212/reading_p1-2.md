## Resolution of the first pass's doubts

**Gloss/sign mismatches**
- p1 **esse** → the signs are Π A σ y = **ipse**, and the gloss itself reads *ipse*: "Franciscus Kendi ter
  perfidus; ipse ad hoc induxit…". No mismatch.
- p1 **debeo**: the signs are ꝛ(with a cross-stroke) y L x = d-e-b-o. One *e* is not enciphered (or the
  crossed ꝛ is an abbreviation). The gloss *debeo* is right.
- p1 **nostri** → the signs are P K σ Π = **nisi**, which makes the sentence work: "nullus enim alius causa est
  istius tumultus nisi Franciscus Kendi". The gloss is probably *nisi* too, written cramped.
- p1 **homo**: the first sign is a Π with a crossbar, and the same sign stands for h in *hanc*
  (Π̄ΔP9), *hunc* (Π̄CP9), *hoc* (Π̄x9, p2) and *hos*. So in this hand the barred Π is the key's # (h):
  **homo** = h-o-m-o, exact. p2 *homo* is Π̄ Ϙ v = h-m-o, with one o left out.
- p2 **querela** → the signs are Ҁ y Ɔ y 4 y = q-e-r-e-l-e (u after q not enciphered) = **querelae**. The gloss
  dropped the -e.
- p2 **insolentiam**: K P σ v 4 y P 9 Π Ϙ = i-n-s-o-l-e-n-c-i-m. The a is left out and the word is spelled
  with c. Read *insolenciam*.
- p2 **dicam**: ꝛ Π 9 A Ϙ. The 4th sign is a heavily inked Δ (a) whose stroke runs past the base, not the key's A=p. Read **dicam**.
- p2 **iterum**: Π Ω y Ɔ C Ϙ = i-t-e-r-u-m (6/6). Pass 1's "9" is the dotted Ɔ, and its final φ is Ϙ.
- p2 **fit**: o x Ω = f-(x)-t. p2 **nihil** also has x where i is expected (P x Π̄ Π 4). In this hand x
  stands in for other vowels as well: *tumultos* (x for u, p2), *coniugat-x-rum* (x for a). It looks like a
  scribal habit. It is not a separate key value.
- p2 **evenissent** → the signs are y (overwritten C?) P ~ σ σ y Ω = e-[v]-n-i-s-s-e-t = **evenisset**
  (singular, one e dropped). Pass 1 read the ~ (i) as x.
- p2 **inflammare** → the signs are Π P o Ɔ Ϙ 5 C Π Ω = i-n-f-(r for l)-m-a-v-i-t = **inflam(m)avit**. The
  gloss has *inflamare*, but the signs and the syntax ("ipse alios … excitare inflammavit") both give the
  perfect.
- p1 **L1 "motuum"**: the signs are Ϙ v Ω = m-o-t. The word breaks off at the line end, and the gloss is
  *motuu(m)* (pass 1 read "molem"). So it is an abbreviated **motuum**: "horum omnium motuum".
- p1 **maledicti** (gloss) → the signs are ϘΔ4yꝛ | Π9Ω C Ϙ = **maledictum**. The looped sign that pass 1
  took for a stray is Ϙ, and "σ" is this hand's open C. That gives "hunc … maledictum proditorem".
- p1 **obruant**: θ L Ɔ C 3 P Ω. The C (u) is drawn σ-like, as in *maledictum*, so the reading fits exactly.
- p1 **Melchiorem**: Ϙ y 4 6 Π̄ θ Ɔ y Ϙ = m-e-l-(6)-h-o-r-e-m. 6 stands for c (the Kendi k-sign), and i is left out.
- p1 **hoc** (l.18): 6 x 9, with 6 for h. 6 therefore serves both k and h/c in this hand.

**Unglossed short runs**
- p1 l.9 **"AA"** after *et*: two signs shaped like 4 or A, partly overwritten by *Copia*, which is itself
  struck. They are **unread**: probably an abandoned start, like the struck *instructa pro certo* that
  follows.
- p1 l.10 **"o"/9ΔΩ** → the signs are o 9 Π Ω = f-c-i-t = **fecit** (e not enciphered). The gloss read
  "ferit" is *fecit*. The run is Π, not Δ.
- p1 l.11 **σΔP9** → the first sign is the barred Π (h): Π̄ Δ P 9 = **hanc**. The gloss read "sanc" is *hanc*.
- p2 l.12 **trailing sign** → this is Ω, the **t of trium**, which runs over the line break (Ω | ƆΠCϘ). So
  the word is *publica*, not *publicae*.

**Code 41.?.55.78.18**: the second number is **70**. In both the text and the margin column (`c1_marg2.jpg`)
its first figure has the same closed-top 7 as *78*, and it has no dot. The *10* of "15 et 10" (l.17), by
contrast, is written with a dotted i-shaped 1.

**Words on the wrong line in pass 1**
- p1 rows 2-3: the row under *Commendavi…hominem* is **clear text** in the main hand: "de bonis omnibus,
  quibus omne de[…], insigni(?)". It ends with AyƆ [per]. The cipher row below it is fidia · acusare · debeo
  · merito. *merito* belongs to that row, not to row 4.
- p1 rows 6-7: "ter perfidus **ipse** ad hoc | induxit Antonium Kendi et …". Pass 1 put "esse" before
  "ad hoc" and out of order.
- p1 rows 13-15: "hunc bene maledict|**um** proditorem Copiae **q|ui** perdidit hoc Regnum". Pass 1 misread
  the first sign of row 15 (Π = the i of *qui*) and left *qui* unciphered.
- p2 rows 9-10: pass 1 put *querelae contra insolentiam* on a line after *militum Transflumen*. The signs sit
  on one row, so the order is "**querelae contra insolentiam militum** Transflumen Vai|uodarum passim fuere…".
- p2 row 3: *Maiestate* is Ϙ5Πyσ Ω Δ Ω y, where the narrow Ω is the one that looks like 2. It is not the ꝛ-form.

**Heading and opening lines (p1)**
1. The top line is cut by the page edge. It reads "D[.]  ꝭ … Thur. Prim." and is most likely an address,
   "D[omi]no … Thur[zo] Prim[ati]"(?). The middle word is lost. Then comes the enciphered
   "[horum] [omnium] [mot(uum)]", perhaps a subject line.
2. "Commendavi hactenus semper V[est]rae Sacr[atissim]ae M[aies]t[a]t[i] hominem". There is a superscript *a*
   over Sacr and *as* over Mt. A small "Illmi(?)" is smudged at the left above row 3.
3. "de bonis omnibus, quibus omne de[…](?), insigni(?) per-". The word read "missimi" in pass 1 is "n̄ ſigni",
   most likely *insigni* ("insigni perfidia"). *omne de…* is not resolved; it may be *omne debet*.

Other clear-text notes. *Dij* (p1 l.12) follows a struck "2ij" and is probably *Dii* ("may the gods…
overwhelm this cursed traitor"). *bene* appears three times as a clear word (p1 l.12, l.13; p2 l.3). In
"Maiestate bene Vestra" (p2) it looks like a null. "q[…] recursiones" (p1 l.23) is a bare q, perhaps *quae*.
"interfecti(?) sunt" is not certain.
