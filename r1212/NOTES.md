# R1212 — Pál Bornemisza's cipher report to Ferdinand I on the Kendi treason (Transylvania, c. 1556)

Status: read (22 Sept 2026). Key: recovered (DECODE R1211, used unchanged). Written up: docs/r1212.html.

ÖStA HHStA Staatskanzlei Interiora, Chiffrenschlüssel Kt. 13, Fasc. 20, ff. 38–39 (DECODE R1212, 4 pp., Latin).
Catalogue #267 "Unknown sender to Paulo Borne?".

## What it is

- Two reports under the same head ("D[omi]no … Thur. Prim." p. 1; "Practica Thur. Prim." p. 3), each opening with the
  enciphered "horum omnium motuum". Clear Latin interspersed with runs of graphic signs.
- Contemporary interlinear decipherment over nearly every run (read at the time).
- Docket p. 4: "Sibenbürgische Ziffern(?) das ist mit dem Bischof daselbst dno Paulo Bornemißa" → Pál Bornemisza,
  Bishop of Transylvania 1553–56. pp. 3–4 are in his first person ("ego in arce Gialu residerem", "Hec sunt verba
  Francisci Kendi ad me in Vasarhel sub comiciis"): the sender is Bornemisza, the addressee "Vestra Sacra Maiestas" =
  Ferdinand I. DECODE's receiver "Paulo Borne?" comes from the docket.
- Content: Ferenc Kendi "ter perfidus" sole cause of the revolt; drew Antal Kendi, Menyhért Balassa and other nobles
  in; lost the kingdom to the king; atrocities of the voivodes' soldiers trans flumen before the three nations; Kendi
  urged the bishop to give up Alba and live at Gyalu; Babócsa and other castles lost. Date c. 1556 (inferred).

## Key

DECODE R1211 (f. 37, the leaf before): A=Δ,3,5 B=L C=9 D=ꝛ E=y,M,2 F=o G=B H=# I=Π,Ꝛ,~,k K=ϐ L=L,4 M=Ϙ,Λ N=P O=φ,x,v
P=A Q=Ҁ R=Ɔ S=σ T=Ω U=C,Λ,ɧ X=π; codes 34 = Transsylvania, 27 = Petrovics. Writer's habits: x/v also u, ~ also u/o,
9 for t (prodicionem), barred Π = h, ϐ also c/h. p. 4 foot sign rows = sign inventory of the heading, not a key.
R1210 (f. 36, "Paulo Bor?") = older numerical key c. 1540 (Rex Joannes, Fr. Georgius, Verbőczy, Bebek); not this cipher.

## Reading and measurement

reading_p1-2.md and reading_p3-4.md (full-resolution second passes; each run deciphered from signs, then compared
with the gloss). transcription_p1-2.md / transcription_p3-4.md are the first passes.
Cipher tokens 288 = 274 words + 14 codes. Words: 257 secure, 14 uncertain but in sense, 3 unread. Codes: 5 keyed
(34 ×4, 27), 9 not (24, 15, 10, 41, 70, 55, 78, 18, 52). Read as sense 276/288 = 0.958.
Gloss corrections by the signs: ipse, nisi, maledictum, evenisset, inflammavit, intellexerunt, perniciosarum, in celo,
vexillum(? gloss auxilium), habiturum. Apparent codes 0399 (fac-), 134 (am-), 34 in alias/aliquando/alliciebat = letters.
