Conventions: clear text as written; `[word]` = cipher run deciphered by me from the signs with the key
(gloss agrees unless noted); `{nn}` = numeric code; `(?)` = uncertain; `[…]` = unread.
Scribal habits found: `~` is used for i and also for u/o (nullus `P ~ 4 4 C σ`, praetextu `…Ω ~`, isto
`…Ω ~`); `Π` and `#` are hard to tell apart (Π often stands where h is required: hoc, hanc, Vasarhel);
x/V (=o) sometimes stand for u (qui `¢ V ~`, nunc `P x P 9`); 9 = c also for t/ct (comiciis, defeccionem,
residencia).
