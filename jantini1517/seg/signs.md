## Upper tier (superscripts)
o, 2 (hooked 2/z-like, very common), 1 (short vertical), b, & (8-like loop, written "&"), c, m, L, s, d, n,
9, zf (long z/f with descender), x, t, k, u, f, 8, y, z (flat z), ^ (caret), r, h, l, a, p, e.
Distinction 2 vs z is the least safe: "2" = open hook with foot, "z" = flat zigzag.
