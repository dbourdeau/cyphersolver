## Conventions and doubts
- **Which base an upper sign belongs to.** An upper sign is assigned to the base whose RIGHT end it sits over or just
  after, because the scribe writes the upper sign slightly to the right. This decides cases like `a/m o/4 zf/7` (L04).
  It is the main systematic risk: if the rule is wrong, many uppers shift one base to the left.
- **`2/7` vs `2/7?`.** Two upper shapes both got the label 2: a clear flat "2", and a hooked "ʔ/ɔ"-like sign (often
  looped). The hooked one is always flagged `2/7?`. It may be a separate sign (9? a reversed c?). About 25 of the
  49 `2/7` are the hooked form: treat these as a possible second class.
- **`-/[9]`.** A bracket enclosing a bowl-plus-ascender (looks like b or q with a b-stroke). I labelled all of them [9],
  including L01 (first written [b]). L18 also has a plain "L9" with no ascender, labelled `-/[9]?`.
- **`-/[2]` vs `-/[a]`.** A looped 2 inside the bracket sometimes looks like an a (L01). Kept as [2] and flagged.
  `-/[a]` is kept for the clearly open a or ^ inside the bracket.
- **`-/[l]`.** A bracket with a second small L or l inside ("LL").
- **`-/[d]`.** The "Ld" bracket. A d written between a 7 and the next bracket is transcribed `d/7`, not `d/[x]`.
- **`&`.** The looped Q/& sign. Its tail sometimes rises to a small 1 or a hook, which I did not count as a separate
  sign (L15, L23).
- **`zf`.** The x+f ligature. `x` alone is used for a plain x-shaped upper. L03 `x/7?` is a struck-looking ∞ shape.
- **`8`.** A g/8-shaped upper (L02, L11, L15, L25) and a bracket inner (L13, L14, L18). It may be the same as the upper 9.
- **`L/`.** A small L-shaped upper. L09 `7/m?` is a mirrored tall L above m and may be the same sign.
- **`p`.** A p/ρ-shaped upper (L03, L06, L10, L08). It may be the same as b written in a different hand.
- **`h`, `k`, `u`, `^`, `c`, `s`.** These are consistent and fairly clear.
- **Illegible (`#/#`).** Blots and erasures in L03, L05, L09, L13 (two), L19. There is a crossed ‡ sign in L17,
  a small m written below the line in L21, and the ends of L01, L14, L15 and L25 are damaged.
- **L17.** A struck-through stretch (probably -/y -/7) has an interlinear m-over-n above it, which also shows under
  L16. It is recorded as `{struck: ...}` and not counted.
- **Line ends.** The last unit of a line at the burnt right edge is often cut off (marked ? or #).
