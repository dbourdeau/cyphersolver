# BL Add MS 32280 ff. 1-2 (DECODE R8870): numeric code letter, "Duplicata"

Status: no write-up (attempted, closed unread: flat code of about 1,650 groups, no key on DECODE or in the volume, language and date unknown)

Catalogue: "... to unknown recipient", London, BL Add MS 32280 f 1-2, DECODE R8870 (DECODE date 1723/1842 is the span of the volume).

## What it is (viewed 21 Sept 2026, DECODE images P1-P4, already downloaded under r8871/)

- ff. 1r, 1v, 2r: a letter written entirely in numeric groups, 1 to 1647, with hyphens joining groups into one word
  (e.g. `141-346-583`, `413-455-303`, both twice) and long dashes as punctuation. It ends with "Duplicata" in an
  Italian or Spanish hand. f. 2v is blank apart from show-through and the British Museum stamp. It has no address,
  no date and no interlinear or marginal decipherment.
- Transcription: `transcription.txt` (901 groups, 463 distinct, 257 used once; top 1021 x13, 250 x12, 616 x9).
  Recurring phrases: `769 1527` (x3, each time preceded by 1143/1031/1146), `1009-84-969` (x2), `162-85` (x2).

## System

A one-part or two-part code of about 1,650 entries with syllable groups for words not in the vocabulary (the hyphenated
compounds). The group distribution is flat (463 types in 901 tokens), which is what a large code looks like, not a
homophonic letter cipher.

## Key search

- The volume's 27 DECODE key records (keycheck.md): R8890 is a French code 6001-7600+ (1790). R8894-R8919 are the
  Deciphering Branch's reconstructions of Dutch diplomatic codes of 1823-42 (Lisbon, London, Bogota, Paris, Washington,
  Spain, The Hague). None of their "Principal" frequency lists puts 1021, 250, 616 or 289 among the frequent groups.
- f. 3 (R8871) is an unrelated three-character code (r8871/NOTES.md).
- No calendar entry can be searched without sender, recipient or date.

## Why it stops here

About 900 tokens of a flat 1,650-entry code, with the language unknown, gives too few repeats for a ciphertext-only
rebuild. It needs the key or a decipherment, most likely in the unimaged part of Add MS 32280 or another Deciphering
Branch volume (BL physical access).
