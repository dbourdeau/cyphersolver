# R8870 vs the Add MS 32280 key records (R8890, R8894-R8919), checked 21 Sept 2026

The DECODE metadata and the images show that every record from R8894 to R8919 is a Deciphering Branch working
reconstruction of an 1823-42 Dutch diplomatic code (Lisbon, London, Bogota, Paris, Washington, Spain and The Hague;
Dedel to Falck 1842). They are sorted-number grids with Dutch and French syllables and words, plus "Principal" frequency lists.
None is a clean number-to-word table for an Italian, Spanish or Latin code. R8890 (1790) is French, 6001-7600+.

The "Principal" lists (the most frequent groups with their values) on R8895 (London-Hague 1825: 397 er, 2 e,
53 de, 309 du, 258 de, 159 den, 747 a ...), R8898 (Bogota 1829: 925, 375, 1644, 981 de, 1541, 906, 1570,
1631 ...) and R8903 (Hague-Paris 1831: 146 ing, 610 en, 506 de, 130 ie, 357, 553 de, 785 re ...) share none
of R8870's top groups (1021, 250, 616, 289, 263). The lone overlap, 357, is not frequent in either text.
Conclusion: no key in this set fits R8870. Its language and date are unknown, and it is not shown to be Dutch.
