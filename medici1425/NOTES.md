# Faenza 1425: Averardo de' Medici and Piero Guicciardini to the Dieci di Balìa, 27 October 1425

Status: in progress (blocked: the ciphertext is not online; ASF photographs needed)

Session of 24 September 2026. Catalogue entry 337. Goal set by Daniel: decipher the letter that Rinaldo degli
Albizzi's embassy in Rome could not read.

## What is known

- **The letter.** Guasti, *Commissioni di Rinaldo degli Albizzi* II (1869), Commissione XLVII, pp. 457-458
  (archive.org `bub_gb_3ZkvD-9ug8IC`, djvu text l. 27950-28000; `guasti2.txt`, git-ignored). The Dieci forwarded
  to the embassy in Rome, with their letter of 30 October 1425, "quella de' vostri commissari da Faenza" and asked
  for it back ("Pel primo ci rimandate la lettera, quando l'avete adoperata"). Rinaldo's register copies it headed
  "Copia. Magnificis dominis Decem balie": the clear opening (the Lord of Faenza's news from the Governor of
  Bologna; Caldora and Antoniuccio dall'Aquila going over to the Duke of Milan; messer Sbardellato and the ransom
  of Perin Turco, with Niccolò da Tolentino), then breaks off at *"Quanto più pensiamo etc. quia multa sunt
  ciferata, nobis ignota. Sed infra, in fine: Faventie, 27 octobris 1425. Vestri servitores Averardus et Pierus"*.
  Guasti's note 2 names them: Averardo di Francesco de' Medici and Piero di Luigi Guicciardini.
- So the cipher part starts at "Quanto più pensiamo" and runs to the date; its length is unknown ("multa").
  The embassy did not have the commissioners' key. The original went back to the Dieci and should be in
  **ASF, Dieci di Balìa, Responsive**, the filza for October 1425.
- **No key known.** Gabbrielli's key volume (ASF *Alfabeti ... carteggio dei Dieci di Balìa*, 1863-64, Ilardi reel 58,
  Yale Dataverse doi:10.60600/YU/XKVOWE) has 1424 keys (pp. 1-4: Galeotto Fibindacci da Ricasoli twice, "Johannes",
  "Zaninus et Conradinus") and then 1430 keys (pp. 5-9, filza 8). Nothing for 1425-29. The filmed sender index
  covers only A-B, so whether Gabbrielli saw an Averardo cipher can't be checked from the film.
- **Likely family.** The 1424 commissioner cipher of Ricasoli (`florence1429/`, key extended there) is the nearest
  known Dieci commissioner system in the Romagna. The Faenza letter of 1425 may use a related table. That can be
  tested only when the ciphertext is in hand.

## Where the siblings may be (lead for when ASF is back)

The 1955 inventory of the *Mediceo avanti il Principato* (archive.org, `map_inv1.txt`, git-ignored) lists
Averardo's incoming letters as commissioner at Faenza, July-December 1425: **MAP I** nos. 89-207 (among them
the Dieci, 28 July 1425, n. 89; Cosimo, 14, 18, 21, 27 and 30 October; Alamanno Salviati; Lionardo de' Bardi, the
Oriolo commissioner; Niccolò Fortebraccio; letters "ad Averardo e Piero Guicciardini, commissari a Faenza") and
**MAP II** nos. 1 and 375 (October 1425). MAP is fully digitised on the ASF site. The inventory marks no piece of
these as "in cifra", so they are probably clear. They could still hold a cipher passage, a key or a clear
draft of the 27 October letter. None of them has been seen.
