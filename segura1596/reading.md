## A — f. 196v, view 337

…que no quieren hazer gracia sino mera justicia, con que estaría yo contento si se guarda. **A Ponte creo cierto** se le deve amistad, y no sé si se conforman **conforman él y Escudero**, ni si ay razón de encuentro entre ellos, que si la huviesse a entrambos les costaría su dinero; el de V.Ex. no querría que menguase por esta causa, y los demás cada uno le ayude su suerte, y así yo me bolveré a Toledo a dar mate a estas cosas, y avisaré de lo que huviere.

Literal decrypted runs:

```
apon
tecreocierto
conformanelyes
cudero
```

The first name may be segmented **A Ponte** or **Aponte**; the signs alone do not decide. No first name is established for this person or for Escudero. The apparent clear/cipher repetition *se conforman / conforman* is retained. Its awkwardness must not be hidden by silently replacing cipher text with a different name or verb. In sense, Segura discusses friendship owed to Ponte/Aponte, agreement between him and Escudero, and the financial consequences of a quarrel.

## B — f. 197r, view 338

Un consejero muy grave me ha dicho que han hecho aquí junta sobre la cometa que ha avido estos días, y **dizen que el mundo se acabará o morirá el rey por setiembre. Él bien cerca dello paresce que anda. Creo en Dios.**

Literal decrypted lines (the first begins with the two cipher signs at the end of the introductory line):

```
dizenqueelmundoseacabara
omoriraelreyporsetiem
breelbiencercadellopa
rescequeandacreoendios
```

English: “A very weighty councillor has told me that they have held a meeting here about the comet that has appeared these days, and they say the world will end or the king will die around September. He seems to be very close to it. I believe in God.”

This reports what a councillor told Segura. It does not establish an official council decision or the astronomical identity of the comet. The punctuation and tone of the final pious formula remain editorial.

## Other pages

196r: money, expenses, debts and payments involving the Council of Italy; concern for services and reputation. 196v: royal help and consultation with Don Alonso Idiáquez, followed by passage A. 197r: passage B, truce and military news, Carmona, the adelantado and Cebiaur, ships and men, Lisbon/Portalegre, and the sale of Don Blasco's estate. 197v–198r: marriage negotiations involving García de Alvarado, the Duke of Alba's sister, Jerónimo de Salamanca, Gaspar Bullón and Haro; dowry and vocation; the Cortes' grant of thirty million over six years. The last page supplies Madrid, 8 August 1596, and Matheo de Segura's signature, with a postscript about infantry for the expedition. These are contextual summaries, not additional recovered ciphertext.
