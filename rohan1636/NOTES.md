# Rohan papers and Franco-Dutch letters, 1635–36

Status: attempted, cipher open. Catalogue #320. Full clear-prose working transcription and source audit: [research report](../rohan_valtellina_1635_1636_report.md). Public summary: [site write-up](../docs/rohan1636.html).

## Documents and access

- BnF Français 5190 nos. 171–172: d’Émery to Rohan, 25 January 1636, and Rohan's reply. The supplied Gallica reproduction btv1b9059471x ends at manuscript p. 80; the target is later in the volume. No ciphertext image in hand.
- Same volume nos. 214–215: Savoy and Canisy to Rohan, 27 May 1636. Their text is printed deciphered in *Mémoires et lettres* (1758), III, but the matching cipher is beyond the supplied reproduction. No paired cipher/plaintext sample in hand.
- BnF Français 3758 no. 100: Louis XIII to Châtillon and Brézé, 30 June 1635, views 126–130 (manuscript pp. 114–118). Much of the copy is clear French; substantial bands of mixed digits and invented signs remain ciphered.
- Same volume no. 104: Châtillon to Servien, 3 August 1635, views 135–140 (pp. 123–128). Clear French surrounding three substantial cipher passages and shorter runs. Châtillon says Brézé gave him the cipher previously used with Servien on a German journey.

## Reading result

The readable prose of nos. 100 and 104 has been transcribed in the report. Louis XIII estimates the Valtellina army at more than 12,000 infantry and 500 cavalry and warns the marshals to cipher important dispatches. Châtillon describes the loss of Schenkenschans, Orange's hesitation, troop sickness, poor provisions and the decision to cross the Rhine. The printed Savoy/Canisy plaintext directs Rohan toward the Val Mesolcina and the Francesca post. No cipher sign was assigned a verified value in this work.
