# HCPortal harvest (23 Sept 2026)

`index.json`: compact metadata of all 1,493 approved HCPortal (hcportal.eu) cryptogram records, fetched 23 Sept 2026.
Solved 795, not solved 693, partially solved 5. The unsolved ones are mostly 20th-century postcards (T. Schrödel
collection), WWII items and Czechoslovak cryptanalysis-course exercises; the historical (pre-1900) unsolved records are
the Hessisches Staatsarchiv Marburg files (ids 494-523), taken into the catalogue as entries 338-341 (the key-volume
samples 519/520/523 left out).

Access: `https://api.hcportal.eu/api/cryptograms/<id>` answers only with a browser User-Agent and the header
`Origin: https://crypto.hcportal.eu`; images at `https://api.hcportal.eu/media/<n>/<file>.jpg` (and `conversions/...-large.jpg`).
