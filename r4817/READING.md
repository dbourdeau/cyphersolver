# Reading of the cipher flyleaf, SCH R 201

Primary source: e-manuscripta, image 1033442, canvas [2], the inside front board / first blank surface before the title page. The book is catalogued 1618; the cipher itself carries no date or signature.

## Literal substitution

```text
PRIMA MATERIA IST
DIE GALLE UOM WIDDER
DAS GEFAE IST EINE
HALB RUNDE KUGEL DER
OFFEN IST DIE WAGE
```

U and V share one sign: UOM is read VOM. Commas are visible after GALLE, WIDDER, KUGEL and at the end; they are not cipher tokens. The first one need not be modern syntactic punctuation. Spaces above are editorial word divisions consistent with the broad manuscript spacing.

## Conservative reading

Prima materia ist die Galle vom Widder. Das Gefae [wohl: Gefäß] ist eine halb runde Kugel. Der Offen ist die Wage.

## Normalized German (one explicit emendation)

Die Prima Materia ist die Galle vom Widder. Das Gefä[ß] ist eine halbrunde Kugel. Der Ofen ist die Waage.

The final ß of Gefäß is an editorial replacement of the E actually obtained from the cipher, not a recovered sign. The f is an inferred key value, supported again by both crosses in OFFEN.

## English

The prime matter is the gall of the ram. The vessel [emended] is a half-round sphere. The furnace is the balance [or Libra].

These are the words, not a demonstrated chemical recipe. Whether ram and balance carry zodiacal or other alchemical meanings remains undetermined.

## Confidence and coverage

All 79 signs in 19 classes receive values. Of them, 78/79 (98.73%) read without a conjectural textual correction; line 3 sign 8 is clearly the E sign, but GEFAE does not make a satisfactory word in context. GEFAS / Gefäß is the likely intended word, graded M. The remaining reconstructed mappings are I under this repository's provenance grades (inferred from repeated words and context, not read from an archival key). This is a reading assisted by prior partial work, not an independent ciphertext-only break.

## Prior work and the changes

Anne-Simone Rous's partial reading is printed by Uwe Maximilian Korn (2023), p. 178 n. 41. It was found before this attempt and used. The main corrections are PRIMA MATERIA (distinguish the two forms of curled staff and the triple angles), VOM (ring rather than dot; triple rather than double angles), F for the crosses, and N for the double angles ending OFFEN. The anomalous final E of GEFAE is retained as a source discrepancy. No full earlier decipherment was found in the searches recorded in NOTES.md.
