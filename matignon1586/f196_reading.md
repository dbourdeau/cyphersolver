# fr. 15572 f. 196 — "Sire, depuis le partement du S.r de Bosseval…": the cipher read

Canvas 203 right. Clear as far as *auquel*, then the rest of that line and 30 full lines of cipher to
the foot (31 in all). **26 are transcribed**: the part-line, the first three full lines and the 13th
("mo£7om…") are not. Measured coverage (`measure.py`): 45% of transcribed tokens, 38% of the leaf.
Transcription in `f196_cipher.txt`. Same key and hand as ff. 143, 154, 173.

**The cipher, as it reads.** This leaf is the most substantial of the three: it carries names.

> … **qui** … **Dieu mercy, il n'y reste** … **advertir Monsieur le [mareschal]** … **je fais
> encore** … **il ne fauldra** … **qu'il advancera encore … quand il aura** … **nous maintenant
> plus que** … **nous avons ung [Mon]sieur de Sainct M[…] grand malade, de sorte que** … **ce
> siège** … **[y sont] demeuré[s] Messieurs de la Bar[…] et Montb[…]** … **il est extrêmement** …
> **nous avons perdu de maladie** … **de la [F]ontaine(?) … qui avoit servy de mareschal de camp
> de l'armée, qui estoit près de mondit sieur le mareschal** … **pour le reste desdits vivres** …
> **il fault faire des** …

**What the despatch is.** A siege report to the King. The writer names casualties: a gentleman
"de Sainct M—" gravely ill; "Messieurs de la Bar— et Montb—" left at the siege; and, most clearly,
the death **by sickness, not by shot**, of the officer *"qui avoit servy de mareschal de camp de
l'armée, qui estoit près de mondit sieur le mareschal"*. Then the victualling: "le reste desdits
vivres".

**Coverage, honestly.** About half the words come out. The three names break off exactly where the
figures are ambiguous, so none of them can be completed from this leaf alone — ff. 201 carries the
same despatch and is the place to finish them.
