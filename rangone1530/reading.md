## B. BnF fr. 3082 f. 42r, Venice, 24 December 1529 (Clair. 330 f. 206 is a copy)

Clear: "La S.ta di N. S. haura saputo che non si mancava cercar di satisfarli et n'haura obligo a S. M.ta senza
dubio sopra le cose di Ravenna et Cervia; ancora che la recuperi per questa altra via, haura visto sua bona
voluntade"

B1 **il Fre[·]goso** (the fifth sign, `vt`, is a null)

Clear: "li giorni passati con spada et cappa"

B2-B5 **tolse fare la cosa de [? ·] en mai piu volse r [? ? ?] a le cose ben disposte in casa, e hara il f[?] da
rovi[n]are, e ho una pratica di lei mi dispiacera.**

- "de [?]en": the sign `a` occurs once (Fregoso's Genoa enterprise, "la cosa de Gen[ova]", would give `a` = g, but
  the word is then cut short; "de ben" is as good).
- "volse r[? ? ?]a": three signs (`c d 9`) that occur only here; "ruina", "dina[r]" and similar fit equally.
- "il f[?] da rovi[n]are": the tailed sign `e` reads f in "oferte" (A); here "il f" makes no word; `rovi[n]are`
  lacks the n.

Translation (the sure part): "... will have seen his good will. Fregoso, in these past days, 'with sword and cloak',
undertook to do the business of [?] ... never again wanted [?] the things well arranged in the house, and will have
the [?] to ruin [?], and I have a negotiation about her [or: Your Excellency] which will displease me."

## A. BnF fr. 3070 f. 81r, Venice, 10 January 1530 (Clair. 331 f. 15 is a copy)

Clear: "Io vi scrissi ultimamente Ill.mo S.re uno certo caso acaduto mi et non li dissi piu oltra per alhora;
adesso lo diro piu brevemente che io potro. Mons.r"

A1-A5a **'l fa instantia a [? ?] de ven[i]re al servicio loro, [? ?] le mie ... malissimo satisfato di lui; ha
mandato a dirlo al [s]ignore Cesare e farli grande oferte.**

Clear: "Vida V. Ex. che tratti sono questi"

A5b-A6 **essendo seguito la [? ·] icon [?] uorano a tenderui.**

Clear: "Hora lassamo andare questi mei particulari; a qualche tempo faro cognoscer a V. Ex. che non mancaro al
debito mio ..."

- "a [? ?] de venire": `y S` = "me" or "mi" reads naturally ("fa instantia a me de venire al servicio loro"), but
  `y` then gives "o m le mie" in A2, which is not Italian; the annealer prefers `y` = b ("o b le mie"), which does
  not read either. Both `y` places are counted unread.
- "ven[i]re": the sign `6` is h everywhere else ("ha mandato", "e hara", "ho una"); here it stands for i
  (encipherer's slip, or h/i share a sign).
- "al [s]ignore": enciphered with ɸ (t) for ρ (s), probably a slip between the two long-tailed signs.
- "Cesare e farli": θ (i) for "e".
- "la [?]icon[?]uorano a tenderui": `xo` and ✳ occur once each here; ✳ is p in "mai piu" (B3), which gives
  "la [?]icon puorano"; not read. "atenderui" = "attendervi".

Translation (the sure part): "My Lord, he urges [me] to come into their service ... is very ill satisfied with him;
he has sent to tell it to Signor Cesare [Fregoso] and to make him great offers. See, Your Excellency, what
treatment this is, it having followed [?] ..."

## Context

Cesare Fregoso married Rangone's sister Costanza in autumn 1529 (DBI, "Fregoso, Cesare"); he had commanded Ravenna
and Cervia for Venice since September 1528, the towns the clear text of B is about, and in January 1530 the Venetian
Senate made him military governor of Verona at 2,500 ducats a year: the "grande oferte" of A. Rangone's grievance
("che tratti sono questi") is that Venice courted his brother-in-law while slighting him. In October 1530 the French
dismissed Rangone over his complaints about a reduced pension (condottieridiventura.it).
