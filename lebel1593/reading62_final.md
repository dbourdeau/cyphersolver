Settled at native resolution (from pass 2): the X in *remedié* is i. The "g" sign before an i-sign is a flourish
that goes with the i (*qu'avoit, soit, seroit, voir*). o→ is h in *proches*. "g u" is *en*.
Still doubtful: the four signs after clear "que" at the head of the page (read as nulls or "l"); the sign read as
∀ in l.8 *sur ces[te] …* (1 to 2 signs); the stray "11" in *estoit* (l.3), read as a null.

## Full text, f. 130v (cleaned strips W_line1-3 and this pass's Z9 crops)

… Gondy envoié par le Duc de Florence … present auprès du Roy de Navarre [100 ?, open here] *a e[n/s]t… oma*
[252 dict, C] [191 le juriste, C] … *pes* [221 mariage, C] *b… de la niece du duc* [280 de] *Florance … au …* [46
Lorraine] | *et …* [223 open] *…* [45 le duc de Lorraine] *avec* [29 le Roy de Navarre] [4+ null] [blot: 1 sign,
probably i] *l se* [251 faict] [247 open]. Je suis attendant …

- Line 1: the run between *a e* and *ma* has 8 signs: pp g 11 ł t s # # δδ 12. That is "a e n l/s t s o o m a"
  in clean ink with no blot. It does not segment into good French. The reading *a esté …ma* is the best offered (M).
- Line 3: the dense blot after [29] is exactly **one sign**. It sits between the null 4+ and h(l): the h, ooo and m
  after it are legible on the autocontrast crop Z9_l3b. With i it reads *il se [faict] [247]*, and 247 is probably
  a participle (M: *fort*? *declarer*?), left open.
- Line 2: about 6 signs before 46 are under recto ink and cannot be recovered.
