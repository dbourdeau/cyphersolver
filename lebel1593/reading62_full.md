## Key corrections and observations

- ∇ (triangle) is used for **y** as well as p: *ayant, d'y*; p is also 8T (*impossibilité, partira*).
- δ̂ (δ with a cross) is mostly **d** (*duc, docteur, disputter, droict, grandes*) but also **i/j** (*poincts, soit,
  jamays, partira*): a homograph, or the table's "λ with cross above" is written almost like δ̂.
- "X" is i in *remedié*, not always the null.
- ∠ is a further **s** (*ministres, poincts*); λ̶ / δ̶ with a bar also s (*cents*).
- "g" (the table's second e) is written before an i-sign in *qu'avoit, soit, seroit, voir* and seems to go with it.
- o→ is h in *proches* (table: g; g is right in *ung, grandes, assignation*).
- Long h/ƒ is used for both **n** and **l** (*quelque, Borbon* vs *contantemant*); ł/h = l.
- "g u" (e + 11 joined) = *en*; "bm/vm" = d confirmed (*deu, de, conduysant*).
- 292 once follows *le* ("le [292]"): *royaume* is possible there, *couronne* elsewhere (*la [292]*).

quadratic displacement field fitted to ~200 template-matched tiles in the band y 3000-4100 (residual <1 px).

set to paper. Cleaned strips (2x): W_line1.png (native y 3190-3350), W_line2.png (3330-3490), W_line3.png (3450-3610).
