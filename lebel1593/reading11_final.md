## Decisions this pass

- l.2 end + l.3: *le..* + *quelles* = **par lesquelles on luy escrit** (the "quelqu'on" of pass 2 dropped; the two
  signs after *le* are an s-form, C). Checked on m00r/m01l.
- 113 = *luy* in all seven places (grammar forces it every time; the harvest's "celuy" gloss is best read "[ce]luy").
- 64.280.50 re-read at resolution (n04l): digits certain. With 64 = *le*, "le de [50]" is the usual "Mr de Lyon"
  ellipsis for Pierre d'Épinac, archbishop of Lyon, the third member of Mayenne's council (history; esp. 336 has
  the same "le .. de 50" pattern). 50 = Lyon, M.
- 250 re-read at resolution (m13r, m14r): all three are 250, not 251. Kept as *tout* (H). Two uses ("luy tout
  presenter", "le tout rechercher") read awkwardly. The sense is still clear: Spain offers everything, four millions.
- 130 and 305 are certain as digits. Both sit in a "… la couronne pour luy" slot, so M: *avoir* / *pretendre*
  (forced; 310 is *avoir* in the key, so 130 may be a slip for 310).
- 292: the harvest gives *promesse* (C, 362.292); here "la [292] pour luy" twice needs *couronne*. Kept couronne (M).
