# Lebel to Charles Emmanuel I, Paris, 27 March 1593: final reading (third pass)

BnF fr. 3983 ff. 194r-195r, canvases 333-335. This pass builds on `reading100_full.md` (the sign-by-sign second
pass, which holds the sign labels) and applies every value in `codes_harvest.tsv`. Clear text is in roman,
*deciphered text in italics*, and codes are shown as [n value]. A "?" marks a code value inferred from context
(grade M).

## Decisions taken in this pass

- f.194r l.6 "h w .227." = *le* [227 seigneur] (h = l; w taken as the e form here, forced by the sense).
- f.194v l.5 "+ □ g 2e m 3ſ g ʃ 8 # ʌ̇ ooo" = *que je reçois*: at 2x zoom the doubtful sign is the tail of g
  with a cedilla-like stroke before 8 = c. Settled.
- 103 = **venir**, from two contexts that fit each other: "il en faudra *venir la*" (f.194v l.10) and "doibt
  faire *venir incontinant* a [49] argent" (f.194r l.21, *faire venir argent*). This also splits the f.194v l.10
  sentence: "... *venir la*. [64 le] [170 Cardinal] [71 Pelvé] a aussi tiré quelque mot ...".
- 257 = **faveur(s)** in all three places ("les faveurs que je reçois", "faire faveur a V.A." twice), matching the
  gloss "en la faveur de l'Infante". The "parvenir" gloss is not needed here.
- f.194v l.7 "280.108." before 257: the last digit is blotted. It is read as [105 faire] ("resolue de faire faveur
  a V.A."), the same wording as l.14 "inclinoit de faire faveur a V.A.".
- "pp 12" / "12 pp" (three places, set between dots) = *a* (à), written with two a-signs. Taken as one word.
- 142 = **serviteur** ("au but que tout bon serviteur de V.A. desire"); 115 = bon.
- ✱ with a g-tail = o; barred T/□ = b; struck 4t signs in l.15-16 are the writer's own cancellations and are dropped.

## Remaining uncertainties

1. Codes with a sense but no word: 22 (a Roman figure beside the Pope: Legate or Cardinals?), 27 (a man who could
   seize the porteur on the road; Nemours, governor of Lyon, by history), 49 (where the money is to be sent:
   Paris?), 50 (the porteur's route, "par [50]"; Lyon?), 104 (title before Tassis; Tomokiyo's "dire" does not fit
   here, so the digits may be misread), 150 (amis/serviteurs), 153 (grandeur/devotion/service), 230 and 274
   ("en [274] beaucoup de [230]": recevoir/tirer ... commodité/contentement), 278 (before "par mes aultres
   lettres faict sçavoir": comme j'ay?), 302 (an a- word from the addendum, affectionné?), 305 (an a- word
   between 302 and 310 avoir: avancer?), XC (a large sign not in the key: the person Mayenne will send).
2. 164 "filz" (harvest, from "le filz de Vaudemont") gives "du filz Tassis", which is odd; Commandeur fits history
   better. 104 and 164 both stand before Tassis and are different numbers.
3. f.194v l.7 code 108/105 before 257 (blotted digit; read 105 by the parallel phrase); l.16 278 and 108 are also
   blotted in their last digit.
4. 227 "seigneur" is C from esp. 336; "amy" would also read in most places.
5. Letter signs settled by sense, not shape: f.194r l.7 3ſ in *arriere*; l.23 m in *cesseroit*; f.194v l.4 h in
   *impossibilité*; l.5 the sign before 8 in *reçois*; f.195r l.23 h in *m'en ayant* (an extra sign, dropped);
   the "pp 12" doublings for *a*.
6. Clear text: "partant" (f.194r l.2) and "garre" (f.194v l.21, the porteur's name) are uncertain readings of the
   clear hand, not cipher.
