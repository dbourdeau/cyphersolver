WHAT IT IS: FOUR drafts of the same short Italian letter from conspirators ("noi") to a "frate" ("Salve frate" /
  "Salve fr(at)e"), c. 1455-58 (mentions "la l(ette)ra di Calisto" = Pope Calixtus III, 1455-58).  Same content
  twice with words and twice with small numbers, so the drafts are their own key (a small nomenclator of
  numbers 2-19 for code words; ordinary numerals written between slashes are NOT code: "/3.o/" = terzo,
  "/4/ spingarde" = quattro).  Signals agreed: letters, or fires/smokes "alle pietre" / "al palazzo delle pietre",
  "tre fuochi", "sparare quattro spingarde", "fate fare due fummi ... che durino una hora".
  No date, no address, no signature on any draft.  P1 foot: "vollete" (?, a word in another hand/ink, bottom
  centre) and pencil "23" at top (archive number of piece 23).
  - DRAFT A = P1 (tall leaf, 27 lines, brown ink, pencil "23"): clear words, no numbers except ".L." and "lo 6(?)".
  - DRAFT B = P2 (square leaf, 23 lines, black ink): the SAME text as A with numbers for the key words.
  - DRAFT C = P3 left (tall leaf, 20 lines): second letter "Date in ogni modo alo apportatore ...", clear words.
  - DRAFT D = P3 right (small slip, written sideways, 22 lines): the SAME text as C with numbers.
  Numbers are plain Arabic numerals (no special signs).

SIGN LEGEND: digits as themselves; '/' = slash written around a numeral; '.' = point as written.
