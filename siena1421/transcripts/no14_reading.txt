SIGN FINDINGS
-------------
1. "8" covers TWO glyphs.
   (a) compact 8: two closed loops of about equal size, the whole sign within or barely above
       x-height, upright = v/u ("vostro" L05, first sign of "trovandosi" L06, "servidori", "a voi",
       "salvi", "tuta", "seguire").
   (b) tall "theta": a larger top loop standing at ascender height, joined by a slanting stroke to a
       smaller bowl on the line (a written theta, or an 8 whose top loop is taller and leans right) = n.
   (b) is the glyph I usually transcribed as D, so all its "8" readings are D: "matina" L06, "noi" L03,
   "considerano" L09 (both), "camino" L02, "ragionare"/"ragionando" L07, "con" L12, "inte(n)tione" L09.
   Clearest pair: L06 "trovandosi" = IB VB P [8] EQ [theta] OR 6 RR Q4, both forms in one word.
2. My "D" itself probably covers two glyphs: the looped theta = n (above), and a "delta" with a solid
   round bowl and a straight stem rising from it, often with a knob at the top, which decrypts as c:
   "cose" L17, "co(n)servacione" L14 (x2), "ci racomandiamo" L23, "acordo" L19.  Seen side by side in
   L03 "noi" (open theta) vs L17 "cose" (solid knobbed delta).  Not yet re-checked word by word; the
   words where D must be c are marked in TEXT.
3. F = "o-o": two small o's joined by one horizontal stroke is one sign = f ("Mangnifici" L01,
   "significhiamo" L05, "fati" L07, "afferma" L10).  In no14b it was split as "OR O"/"OL? O".  Other
   "OR O" pairs (L01 "da sera", L08, L19, L23, L24) may also be F: not settled.
4. PLUS^dot ("+" with a dot) = i ("noi" L03, "disposti", "qui", "in", "significhiamo").
5. N? (arch with a loop at the left foot) = u/v ("uno" L06, "unita" L12, "tuti" L14, "altrove", "vi
   sarano" L18).  Plain N = n ("quanto", "ancora").  The sign in L02 "compa(n)gnia" is m-shaped (two
   arches): renamed M.
6. P ("rho") = o in most words but must be i in "Signoria" L15, "gratissime" L04, "presencia" L17,
   "sperasi" L19 and "speriamo" L20: possibly a second glyph (a phi-like i, cf. OSTEM? = i in "Domenico"
   L06).
7. IB and EQ differ only by a short vertical; L05 "[T]oscana" is written with EQ (or the vertical is
   invisible).
8. Arcs drawn over groups are abbreviation marks: co(n) L06, discopertame(n)te L13, ma(n)dare L22, the
   overline on "Sig(noria)" L05.
9. "gn" is spelt "ngn"/"ng" (D Z): Mangnifici, compangnia, singnifichiamo.  In L07 "ore" the VB's
   lead-in diagonal had been read as OL.

SUMMARY
-------
An Italian letter, entirely in cipher with word division, from envoys of Siena on a journey ("noi
arivamo qui ieri da sera salvi ... siamo pronti a seguire lo nostro camino"; "partiremo per seguire
vostro viagio"), addressed to the Signoria of Siena (dorse: Priori and Capitano del Popolo).  They report
that this morning their man Domenico talked for two hours with a servant of <7H> (a code name: "lo deto
7H", later "7H e a Napo[li]?").  7H is displeased with "la inpresa".  "Carlo [M]alatesti" and the others
feel jealousy, and 7H and Carlo think that Siena and the rest of Tuscany, united with "the three powers",
could openly wage war with <Z7> to preserve (420).  They will report at length when they return.  The
signature block L24-L26 is in cipher: "Roma" (or the cipher group r-u-m-a) looks like the place of
writing, and "li servidori vostri" and a name ending "Marco" are legible.  No date was read (a "10" in
L20 may be a day).  Carlo Malatesta (d. 1429) would fit the 1421 date Meister gives for the early pieces,
but that identification rests on one doubtful word.
