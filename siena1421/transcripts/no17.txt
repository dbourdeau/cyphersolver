WHAT IT IS
- Head: "1528." ; salutation "Molto mag(nifi)co et generoso cavaliere etc."
- A Sienese letter to a cavaliere who is away from the magistrato di Balia: "since V.S. is absent from the
  magistrato di Balia, some important matters of the government are communicated so that you may understand
  what happens ...". Clear Italian with cipher runs embedded (m.03-m.10), clear words kept inside the cipher
  (quando, secondo, et, tertio, non so se e vero, and the abbreviation HBAR = che).
- End (clear): "parlo breve e in substantia perche la cifra vuol cosi. m(esse)r Bart(olo)meo T?antucci quale era
  segretario da la S?-/pratica(?) e la po leggere agevolmente. D? Siena(?) alli 2σ di agosto 1528." The day is
  written 2 + a sigma-shaped digit (read 20 in the brief, 25 in NOTES; not settled here). Followed by a monogram.
- Foot of P1: address fragment "...[Ill]ustrissimo Bart(olome)o T?antucci" (?) and the docket
  "questa cifra e quella di balia"; small "Mag(nifi)co" near the fold; pencil "17".
- P2 (address side): "Al mag(nifi)co et generoso cavaliere / M(esser) Ant(oni)o del Vescovo oratore / d(e)l
  Ill(ustrissi)mo ... Car(dina)le ... (last words uncertain)" ; below, "Cifra". Show-through of the P1 text.
  No cipher on P2.

NOTES ON THE READING
- m.03-m.10 carry all the cipher; lines m.01-m.02 and m.10 (after "p 6")-m.13 are clear.
- "1 0 6 1" after [tertio] (m.08) is written like a plain number and may be clear (a sum or a date?) rather
  than cipher; counted as cipher tokens here.
- m.06 ". 0 ." : the 0 is set between two points, the medieval way of marking a numeral or a single-letter
  word (could be clear "o" = "or", or a code number).
- m.08 "k y bl e? x? u N_" is written in a more cursive, word-like way than the rest (could even be a clear
  word in the scribe's hand, where r looks like x); readings uncertain.
- The word-final pairs "p LAM", "c LAM", "h LAM" and the gaps after them suggest LAM ends a group.
- Doubtful 9/7/y distinctions: 9 = closed bowl + curved tail; 7 = hooked top, no closed bowl; y = open u-like
  head + long right descender; q = closed bowl + straight stem (1x). Marked "?" where the shape sits between.
