WHAT IT IS: dispatch(es) of the Sienese orator at the court of Charles V, in Italian, mostly clear, with cipher runs
  written in a mixed sign set (Latin capitals and lower-case letters, digits, Greek-letter forms, circles and ties).
  Photographed order is not the text order:
  - P1 = last page of a letter DATED "Di Hellsprunth(?) alli 29 di X.bre MDXLVI" (Heilbronn? 29 Dec 1546), then
    "Poscritta" of 3 Jan: "Poi si e' ritenuta questa perche credevo mandarla per Alisandro Pie|ri fin oggi il di 3
    di Gennaio ... lettere di VV. SS. Ill.me indirizzate insieme a m. Ambrogio [N...] et a me delli 20. 27. 29.
    del passato [interl.: di Novembre / di dicembre] ... ritenuta fino a li XI di dicembre".
  - P2-P7 = a second letter; P7 ends: "... Di Ellspruth(?) a li 4 di Gennaro 1546" (Sienese style = 4 Jan 1547; the
    4 is written as a loop on a stem, the same shape as the cipher sign OI below) -- subscription
    "Ser.or di VV. SS. Ill.me / Mario Bandini(?)" (signature "Mario Bandini", reading of the surname fairly sure).
  - P8 = clear postscript/enclosure note: "Sara con questa la copia d'un capitolo d'una lettera venuta al R.mo
    Nuntio di S.S.ta ... preparationi del Turco ... Mons. di Granvela ... Piero Salvestri ...".  No cipher.
  - Sender: Mario Bandini, Sienese orator.  Recipient: VV. SS. Ill.me (the Balia / Signoria of Siena).
    No address leaf photographed (P8 lower half is bleed-through only).
  Place written "Hellsprunth"/"Ellspruth" (I read it as Heilbronn, where Charles V was Dec 1546 - Jan 1547; unsure).
  Pages P1, P3, P4, P5, P7, P8: checked, no cipher runs.  Numbers in clear text are NOT cipher: P1 "fin 4. o. 5."
  (4 in loop form), P3 "per 8 . o . X di ancora", P7 date.
  Cipher runs: P2 (lines 15-22 of the page) and P6 (lines 1-6).

SIGN LEGEND (ASCII names; plain letters/digits are written as themselves)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8 9 1 2 x s o m q a e h R A L T V X Z N   letter/digit-like signs, as seen (lower-case where written small)
  1      upright with a hooked flag at the top and short foot (ɿ/ʎ-like); distinct from LAM
  O2     double circle / bullseye (◎)
  DOT    solid filled round blob (●)
  OO     two joined circles (∞)
  OO:    ∞ with a dot above and a dot below
  OL     small circle with a horizontal tail to the right (o—), the tail running into the next sign
  OS     small o crossed by a slanting stroke that ends in a hook at top right (ø / "o7")
  OI     circle standing on a descending stem (ꝗ / period form of the digit 4)
  PHI    φ (stem through a circle)            PSI  ψ (fork on a stem)          TH  θ
  LAM    λ (long left leg, foot often drawn out horizontally)
  LV     Λ without crossbar (inverted V, left foot hooked); "A" when a crossbar is present
  MM     two slanted λ/Λ strokes on one base line (cursive M-like, "ᏗA")
  U      two uprights joined at the foot (⊔, "LI")
  H      two short uprights joined by a crossbar (ʜ, "tt")
  #      two uprights crossed by two bars
  M3     horizontal bar over three legs (ᴨ with a third leg, "m" in capitals)
  P      thorn-like p (þ): stem above and below a bowl on the right
  P+     P with a horizontal bar across the descender (ꝑ)
  RHO    ρ with a long descender ending in a horizontal foot stroke
  D      small round bowl with an ascender curving up/left (δ / ∂)
  DF     D with the bowl filled solid
  ST     upright ending in a small loop at the foot (top plain or with a top bar, "ƍ"/"5"-like)
  STB    ST with a heavy blob at the head (usually preceded by OL, "o—¡")
  TB     T whose stem carries a filled wedge/blob (Ŧ)
  TO     T with a small circle (open or filled) at the right end of the bar and a loop at the foot of the stem
  XT     x with a horizontal bar on top (and sometimes at the foot) (⊼ / ⊻)
  Q+     q whose descender is crossed by a horizontal bar
  AMP    "&"/8-like loop with a tail ending in a small o
  GX     g/9-like loop with a crossbar and a curled descender
  K      k-like sign with a looped arm (ꝁ)
  SW     large composite: loop on a stem with a heavy loop/blob at lower right (♀/ȣ-like)
  STAR   asterisk (✱)
  TRI    V/triangle with a bar (∇)
  E?     e-like sign with a long descending tail (could be clear e)
  DIV    ÷-type sign: one or two horizontal bars with 1-3 dots above/below.  Stands at the START of most runs
         (after clear text) and sometimes inside runs; probably a null/marker, kept as a token.
  {dash} short hyphen-like stroke at line start
Tokens separated by spaces; '?' after a token = uncertain; '??' = illegible sign.
NB "abs": the lower-case word "abs" occurs three times right before a cipher run (P2 L20 "la o ST abs reducesse",
  P6 L1 "Ces.a abs DIV", P6 L3 "fuoco in abs DIV"); it may be an abbreviation or a clear-letter code word.
NB "bus"/"buss"/"bos": a short clear-looking word recurs next to runs (P2 L18, L20, L21); reading uncertain
  (abbreviation, e.g. "bisogna"?).
