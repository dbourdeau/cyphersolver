  I      short x-height minim (ı), standing alone before a round o ("I 0"), NOT joined as in W.  May be a
         reduced W ("ıɷ") or a separate sign; kept separate so it can be merged later.
  (W as used here: the ɷ/ω sign in all its forms: "cω", "ıω" (a short stroke joined to ω), and bare "ω".)
  COLON  two stacked points ":" inside the line (m.04, 1x); DOT = a single point (no06 legend).
  SSL    SS whose top bar runs on into a short third leg (m.09 line start, 1x); perhaps SS + a stroke.
  BIGX   large bold X standing between two points ". X ." (m.13, 1x); much bigger than x.
  RX+    the RX sign (R-like: stem, bowl at top right, diagonal leg) with a cross on top, written between two
         points (m.17 "DOT RX+? DOT", m.19 "DOT RX+ DOT", the m.19 one large and bold). Cf. no06 m.14 "RX?".
  B+     bold B with a cross at its upper left (m.19, 1x).
  A+     A with an upright crossed above its loop (m.11, 1x; could be F + a stray cross).
  Pointed signs: CIRC (m.18 "DOT CIRC DOT", m.20 "DOT CIRC DOT" and a final "DOT CIRC"), BIGX (m.13), RX+ (m.17, m.19) and one
         blotted sign (m.05 "DOT 6 DOT ??") stand between points; they look like set-off nomenclator or numeral
         groups rather than letters.
  Line-end filler: m.02 ends with a small ":" and "-" after the last sign (like a hyphen/filler); not counted.
