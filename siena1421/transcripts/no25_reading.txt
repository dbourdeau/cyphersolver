SUMMARY
  Sienese envoys (writing "noi") warn the Signoria that a lord ("questo signiore") will not ride the way
  "those here" would like: he has decided, without stopping(?) here, to leave and go to Siena's territory
  ("d'esare chostà").  They expect him on Saturday next, by way of Laiatico (Volterra country), then
  Radicondoli and Casole (Sienese Val d'Elsa / Colline Metallifere) and so towards Siena.  They ask the
  Signoria to have bread, fodder (biada) and victuals made ready in those places as it sees fit, and promise
  to report further by a messenger of their own ("per fante propio").  Undated; the route Laiatico ->
  Radicondoli -> Casole suggests a lord and his escort passing from Pisan/Volterran territory into the
  Sienese state.  No names except places; the lord is probably behind the TH sign or unnamed on purpose.
