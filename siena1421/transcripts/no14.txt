What it is: letter entirely in cipher, no clear salutation, date or signature visible on the text side
  (L01 begins with a cross [+], the usual invocation). L24 contains a group that can be read as the clear
  word "roma" (R? O M A?) -- perhaps the place of writing -- followed by more cipher; L25-L26 set to the
  right look like the date/subscription, still in cipher.
Address (dorse, P2, centre): "[Mag]ficis et potentibus d(omi)nis / Prioribus .. Capit(ane)o / Civitatis
  Senarum." (i.e. to the Priori and Capitano del Popolo of Siena); pencil "11"? / "14" at left of it;
  red wax seal remains to the right.
Dorse, right edge, written sideways in a later hand: a small family tree of names:
  "m. Guido / franchi(?) -- m. Nicola -- Bruno -- Ran[ieri?] ; m. Mut(in)elli(?) ; m. Guido ; Benzi(?)
  ; Giovanni(?)" (not part of the cipher; reading uncertain).

SYSTEM AS SEEN: word-separated. Every cipher word is closed by a point on the line; the points are
transcribed as ' / '. (One ':' in L05 and one '::' in L10 are kept as written.)  Signs are a mix of
Latin letters, digits and invented shapes; several signs recur as fixed pairs.  Arches ('arcs') drawn
above or below some groups are recorded in {notes}; they may be abbreviation/nomenclator marks.
Numbers are written in the cipher digits inside parentheses: "( 4 2 0 )" = LP Q4 2 O PAREN, twice (L09,
L13) -- so Q4 doubles as the numeral 4, 2 as 2 and O as 0.

  VB     "v with loop": a check-mark whose right arm closes in a small bowl ("\o", "ʋ")
  IB     I-beam: two short horizontal bars joined by a short vertical ("工")
  EQ     "=": two parallel horizontal bars, no vertical
  TR     "⊢": vertical with a bar to the RIGHT from its middle (r-like)
  TL     "⊣": bar with a vertical at its RIGHT end
  PLUS   "+": full cross
  PLUS^dot  + with a dot above right ("+'")
  PLUS^curl + with a curl (L01, once)
  H      H: two verticals joined by a bar that runs on to the right (always in "B H TR" / "7 H")
  OSTEM  open cup or loop joined to the left side of a tall vertical that runs above and below the line
  PAREN  ")"-like tall curve descending below the line (closing bracket; also after O in "O PAREN" groups)
  LP     "(" opening bracket (only before "4 2 0")
  LAMBDA A-like sign without crossbar, with a flag at top right (L25)
  7X     7 whose stem is crossed by a horizontal bar (L25)
  CIRC   a large circle enclosing a vertical and a b/3-like sign (circled figure or monogram, L21)
  SLASH  a long thin oblique stroke (L18, L23 end)
  BAR    a free horizontal stroke standing as a sign
Marks and damage:
  ITICK  short vertical with an apostrophe-like tick;  IDOT? i with dot
  X^tick / X^arch / ^OBAR : a tick or arch attached to a sign (kept as part of that sign's name)
  TILDE  free tilde-like mark (L02)
  DIV?   bar with points above/below (L07, once);  DASH? free dash
  BLOT   a sign struck out by an ink blot (cancellation)
  ?      doubtful reading of that sign;  ??  illegible / lost (paper damage or blot)
