What it is: Donato Acciaiuoli (Florentine orator in Rome) to Lorenzo de' Medici, "ex Urbe die 13 junii 1478".
  Pencil "1478" top left (later hand). Salutation "Mag.ce ac gen.se frater hon. etc."
  Signature lower right: "Donatus Acc. or. etc." ; pencil (modern) "Donato Acciaiuoli" and "11".
  P1 (address): "Mag.co ac gen.so Viro / [La]urentio Medici tamq(uam) / [f]ratri hon.do etc. / Florentie"
  (hole through "La-" and "f-"); pencil "Donato Acciaiuoli" at top; red wax seal remains; loose seal fragment.

Two cipher blocks: block 1 = tail of text line 6 + 4 full lines (C1-C4); block 2 = 5 full lines (D1-D5)
ending in clear "Vale". No word division: signs run on continuously. Numerals set between points occur
(".3 1.", ".8 O.", "8 7.", ".TAILP.") -- recorded with DOT tokens.

SIGN LEGEND (names used below)
------------------------------
Letter-like and digit-like signs (named as themselves):
  A      a
  B      b with straight (slanted) stem, bowl at the foot
  6      b-like with a clearly curved stem (like the digit 6). B/6 may be one sign written two ways;
         kept apart because the curvature is consistent where it occurs. "6?" = could be B.
  C      c
  C^tick? c set high with a small oblique tick above it (D1 only; tick may belong to the preceding Q)
  D      d with loop and ascender curving back left (delta-like "δ"); all d/δ forms merged
  E      epsilon-shaped E ("ε"), open to the right
  G      g with looped descender
  N      n
  P      p
  Q      q/9: loop with straight descender, no bar
  S      s
  O      o (plain small loop). In ".8 O." probably the numeral 0.
  1      single short stroke with a small hook at top (numeral 1)
  2      z/2 shape with flat foot
  3      3 with descender ("ʒ")
  4      the "4" form: short curl top left, horizontal bar, vertical crossing it and going below the line
  7      7
  8      8 / B-like double loop
  8^dot  8 with a dot above it (D1 start only; the dot may belong to the numeral "87.")
  II     two short upright strokes "11" (one sign; ii / numeral 11 / u?). Always written as a pair.
  M^n    m with a superscript n (always carries the n; a plain m never occurs)
Invented / Greek-like signs:
  OMEGA  ω
  PI     π
  TAU    T with the left end of the bar hooked ("τ")
  REV3   reversed 3 / reversed epsilon ("Э", "∋"), rounder than 3, sits on the line
  RHO    loop with a curl rising to the upper right and a descender, no bar ("ρ"/"ϱ"-like)
  OSTEM  loop attached to the left side of a tall vertical that goes above and below the line
  ODAG   loop (d/δ-like) whose ascender is crossed by two short bars above the line ("‡" standing on an o)
  QB1    loop with descender crossed by ONE bar (usually long, sloping); often with the top-right curl
  QB2    loop with descender crossed by TWO bars below the line (q with a "‡" hanging from it)
  QB3    same with THREE bars (C2, once)
  ODOT   circle with a dot inside ("⊙")
  TAILP  small loop entered by a bar from the left, long tail slanting down-left (D4, between points)
  ZX     z whose tail crosses below the line as an x (z + crossed strokes); might be two signs "2 X"
  NX     n whose last leg continues as a slanting descender crossed by a horizontal bar (crossed "ɲ")
Cross / stroke signs:
  PLUS   + : vertical crossed by ONE bar (the bar often runs on into the following o: "+o")
  DAG2   ‡ : vertical crossed by TWO bars (bars sometimes both high "Ŧ", sometimes low "±";
         treated as one sign)
  PERP   ⊥ : horizontal bar with a short vertical standing on it (vertical above the bar only)
  HASH   # : horizontal bar crossed by TWO short verticals ("H" with long bar)
  HASH3    : horizontal bar crossed by THREE short verticals
  SL2    // : two long slanted strokes
  SL3    /// : three long slanted strokes
  EQ     = : two horizontal bars, no vertical (D5, once)
  OBAR   o with a long horizontal stroke running through/over it to one or both sides ("-o-").
         Distinguished from O by a bar clearly longer than the o. Borderline: "OBAR?".
Punctuation:
  DOT    a point on the line (only around numerals)

Marks: bars on descenders/ascenders (QB1/QB2/QB3/ODAG), the superscript n (M^n), the dot (8^dot) and
tick (C^tick?) are kept as separate sign names. "?" = uncertain reading of that sign; "??" illegible.
