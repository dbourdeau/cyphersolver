WHAT IT IS
- Salutation: "Magnifici d(omi)ni d(omi)ni mei singularissimi post debitas recomendationes etc."
  A short report in Italian (plural "we": "scrivemo", "siamo stati", "partiamo") by Sienese envoys
  travelling with or waiting on a "S. Cap(itan)o" (Signor Capitano), to the Sienese government.
- Content (clear): since our last letter of the 6th ("a di vj"), sent by a messenger of our own, we have
  been several times with the S. Capitano and have pressed for our departure; he keeps saying that,
  since we are here, we may wait until he has an answer, which will come very soon; and he said so all
  day yesterday, "che fumo di octo al presente di marzo" (which was the 8th of this March) ... "domattina
  partiamo col nome di Dio" (tomorrow morning we leave, in God's name). Then cipher.
- Date: the letter was written on 9 March (yesterday = 8 March "al presente"), year not stated.
  No place. No signature (the letter ends in cipher); no address visible on this side (the dorse was not
  photographed or is blank).
- The clear text keeps phrases between the cipher runs: "e in effecto afferma" (L08), "per ogni modo e
  l'altro" (L08), "che venne" (L09), "per ogni modo / se altro non nascera" (L10). These are cribs for
  the syntax around the cipher.
- Pencil "19" at top right; a pencil "-6"(?) below it.

NOTES
- L07: after "dio /" the writer has "2 a" (Tironian et + a, cursive), then a hole in the paper with a
  faint "!"-like trace at its right edge, then the heavy FLAG sign. "e a..." could be clear ("e a
  [bocca]"?) and the cipher start at FLAG; the 2 is counted as cipher with "?", "a" as clear.
- The runs include many letter-like signs that look like plain Latin letters (l, r, u, p, q, m, c, S,
  f, k, s, x, w) together with digits and a few special signs (TRI, DIA, LAM, B, OB, SI, TT). The
  letter/digit mix and the frequent TRI (10x) suggest a monoalphabetic substitution with several
  signs per vowel; "1 0" (4x) may be one sign "10".
- Signs between points (". 1 .", ". c .", ". S .", ". BIGB .", ". DIA .") look like single-sign words
  or code items (names?). ". c . S . DIA ." in L09 is a run of three pointed signs.
- Repeats: "TRI 3 TRI" (L08 and L10), "1 0 7o"/"1 0 p", "B OB 7" twice (L08), "SI 6 7" (L07 end and
  L09 start: "s SI 6 7 r" / "s SI 6 7 w").
- The ruling-like horizontal grey band under L10 is the edge of a mount/support, not writing.
