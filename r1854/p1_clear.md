## Context for the runs

- **RUN 1** (L04 end to L06, 58 signs): follows "Qua de re ... rogare ac simul decrevi ne" and precedes "donec
  sciam; alioquin non possim". So a clause "ne [verb + object]" ending before "donec sciam", e.g. "ne ... aliquid
  (faciam/scribam/moveam) ... donec sciam". The line before speaks of plans "valde utilia" conceived "pro nobis"
  after the news of the peace between the ducal dominium (Milan) and the league (Venice etc.).
- **RUN 2** (L07 end to L09 start, 70 signs): after "Nam sibi tue ... me sciat quo modo valeat", then "precor
  itaque prefatam amplitudinem vestram ...". Probably names a person or place and what they can do.
- **RUN 3** (L10 end to L13 start, 134 signs): after "(ne) ... amplitudinem tantam vendicare nolit rem ipsam
  negligere", ending just before the closing "Recommendo me".

  RUN 3 = L10+L11+L12+L13.
