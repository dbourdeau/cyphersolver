## English (summary translation)

Yesterday Giovanni de Orlandis told me, as soon as he saw me, that ser Vittorio came to him that same day saying the
countess would do everything that pleased (you) and the said Giovanni; but as he was then starting his dinner, he
said he would come back and tell him more. Later, yesterday evening, when many of us were gathered in the house of the
cardinal of Siena for an academic act and to dine with the cardinal, ser Vittorio — who is often there because his son
lives there — came up to me in the hall before dinner and, after long talk, said in conclusion that last Thursday,
coming from Calabria, he passed through Rome with a cousin of lady Palma who was escorting his own sister, married
beyond Viterbo, and returned the day before; that on behalf of the three other cousins of lady Palma, Antonio Colonna
has always excused himself, saying they are not content …; that the countess has sent to say, as before through him,
that as far as she is concerned she agrees to the match, but her subjects did not seem content, because your
lordship is too far away if anything untoward happened there, and because Carlo is too young for such things; and the
girl was not content either … The mother, doubting …, (will) do what (you) order; her wish seems to be that it be done.
