## Changes and readings, line by line

**L01** — post recessum florentini fui ad dominum n(ost)rum eum ad uos au(i?)
- after `9`: added the small `t` (⊢) that the first pass missed (`post recessum`).
- first `b` (post) -> `bo`; `b` in florentini -> `I` (long slanted stem).
- `9` in flor- -> `n` (η shape, o).
- note: a macron stands over `8 OTO 4` (n(ost)rum abbreviation).

**L02** — ad plenum de aauentrem(?) familiaris d(omi)ni marchionis er(et?) uinundum(?) ?enit respon-
- `+` after D -> `t` (it is the small ⊢, not a cross).
- `o t 4` -> `c t 4` (the first sign is an open c).
- `Y` after familiaris' ooo -> `L` (plain Λ, no loop): familiaris.
- `b` in familiaris and marchionis -> `I`.
- `9` in marchionis -> `n`.
- `r o` -> `r c`: the sign after ɾ looks open (c); `et` would need o. Uncertain.
- line end `T Z S` -> `T 3 S` (plain ʒ): respon|dit.
- unresolved: `D t 2 L OTO x S = c t 4` and `A Y S OTO S D A 4 ?`; the first sign of that OTO is u-shaped.

**L03** — dit ego facciam libenter magnificus dominus Carolus michi dic(u)it si

**L04** — creaerem(?) negocium leon(e)bi(?) non habere locum re?b sper quirerem parentelam

- `9` in "le?n" and "locum" -> `n` (η): locum (was "lccum").

## Page 5

Verso of Cattanei's second letter, Rome 21 Feb 1430 (IMG_R1854_I8415_P5.png, rotated 180°). Three bold cipher lines,
transcribed sign by sign with the p4v2 legend into `p5v2.txt` (crops 11-15x). Key: key4_v2.txt, plus one new token.

Changes against p5b.txt:
- L01: `L Y H = n 8 o Y b OTO H c` -> `L H = n g OO I OTO H C`: Astorgius (o~o is OO = g; ς is the "et" sign, not c).
- L01: `2 8 = n Z b 4` -> `2 g = n Z bo 4` (Bartolomeus); `L S + 2 H O 2 = 3 8 x H` (anbasiatores); `D S b 8 A 4` -> `D S bo g A 4` (d(omi)norum).
- L01 end: `D a Q 8 Y 4 O S b D b x` -> `D a Q g Y 4 O S bo Dv I x`: de Arimino die.
- L02: `Z b A S` -> `Z A S` (no sign between ʒ and anchor): lune. `3` -> `3c` (see below).
- L02: `9 b c 2 4 9 b 4 b H 2 8 O Y H` -> `9 bo g 2 4 9 bo 4 I H 2 g O Y H`: coram comisariis.
- L02: `9 OTO` -> `9 OXO` (producxerūt; a macron over the following OTO stands for the n).
- L02: `2 8 = 2 H 9 b T O Q H` -> `2 g = 2 H 9 bo T O Q H`; final `m` confirmed as -rum (bullarum).
- L03: `t 8 = Y 9` -> `t S = Y 9` (auctenticatas); `D b 9 = O` -> `D I 9 = O` (dicti).
- L03: `9 b + Y H L 8 Y b 2 L 4 b H t 8 A S =` -> `9 bo 4 Y H L g Y I 2 D 4 I H t g A S =`: comisarii admiserunt (the "+" was a 4; the Λ before 4 is a Δ).

- L03: `9 8 t D b T 2 8 OTO 4 b A OTO 2 + O =` -> `9 g t D bo T 2 g OTO 4 I A OTO 2 + O =`: credo parum iuuabit.

New token: `3c` is a ʒ whose tail runs long below the line and curls to the left. Here it is read as the con/com sign
(ꝯ), so (com)paruerunt. It is the only occurrence, so the value comes from the sense.

Reading:
1. dominus Astorgius et dominus Bartolomeus anbasiatores d(omi)norum de Arimino die
2. lune (com)paruerunt coram comisariis et producxerū(n)t mandatum et certas copias bullarum
3. auctenticatas. dicti comisarii admiserunt. si et in quantum, credo parum iuuabit.
   [clear: denarios non habeo pro expensis. Rome die xxi Februarii 1430]

Remaining doubts on p.5:
- `3c` = com: the value rests on one occurrence and on the sense.
- "si et in quantum": the ς has a small tick above it (L03 x≈432). It is either the "et" sign, as elsewhere, or a
  c, which would give "sic in quantum". The sense does not settle it.
