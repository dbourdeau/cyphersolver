# Key: BnF fr. 3995 no. 11 (f. 23), the Nevers key book

Read 22 Sept 2026 from Gallica `btv1b525085665`, canvas 52 (full resolution, 3816 x 6100). Tomokiyo, *Catalogue of
Ciphers in BnF fr. 3995*, no. 11 ("1586"). No endorsement on the sheet. The same key is used in the correspondent's
letter of 25 February [1586] from Clermont (BnF fr. 3413 f. 102), which carries an interlinear decipherment; Tomokiyo
takes that letter to be the cardinal de Guise's, but the hand and the monogram signature are this correspondent's.
