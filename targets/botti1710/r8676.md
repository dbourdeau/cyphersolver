## Summary (a guess from the decode; it is no reading)

A trip is rumored to be disciplinary action over demolition of embankments in a water dispute. An alternative explanation concerns dowry or inheritance finances.

## Reading

None. This letter is **decoded, not read**: the decode does not build sentences yet. No word of it is counted as read.
