# The transcription

Credit:

- DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project: records R8468 to R8470, R8617, R8618 and R8659 to R8718.
- DECODE's record pages: https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/ followed by the record number, for example https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8659.
- The archive: British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- The database: Héder, M. and Megyesi, B. (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397.
- No DECODE image is published.

## What it is

- My own transcription of the DECODE images. Each page had two readings and a reconciliation. The rows here are the reconciled text.
- The two readings agree least on the 1710 letters, and best on the 1711 to 1713 letters.
- The decode and `check.md` use these rows.
- DECODE's record pages mark this ciphertext as private. No DECODE transcription file is in this folder.

## Where the rows are

- `transcription/<record>_<page>_digits.txt`: one file for each page that holds cipher digits.
- The page label (`T001` and so on) follows the order of the images in DECODE's record. It is not a folio number.
- Four header lines start with `#`. Then each row is one line, `C01 <body>`, in page order.
- The labels `C01`, `C02`, ... are the rows that the transcription labels as cipher. The decode lines of the record pages name them.
- Clear rows of the page are not copied.
- Some rows labelled as cipher hold clear words: the clerk's words beside the codes (R8714), the writer's words, a date line or a signature. `[not copied]` stands in their place, once for each run of such words (342 places, in 288 rows of 82 files; the 533 given here at first also counted the header line of each of the 191 files, which names the mark: corrected by the maintainers on 20 November 2026). Notes of the transcription outside brackets are dropped in the same way.
- The decode does not take those words as codes. It shows `?` for them.
- Each row body is as transcribed. Only one position word in nine notes of the transcription was changed, to "top" or "opening".
- Three pages hold rows labelled as cipher with no digit: R8663 T009, R8671 T005 and R8671 T008. They have no file.

## The marks

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| a digit, `.` | a cipher digit; a dot of the page after a code |
| a space | a space of the page |
| `a`, `u` | a letter-like sign inside the codes |
| `[a\|b?]`, `⟦a\|b?⟧` | one sign with two or more readings; the leading reading stands before the bar |
| `[d?]`, `[?]` | a doubtful sign |
| `ω`, `g`, `β` and other labels | on some pages without dots: a sign that the transcription labels; a label means one sign on its own page only |
| other `[...]` or `⟦...⟧` | a note of the transcription: a stretch not read, damage, a mark |
