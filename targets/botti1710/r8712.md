## Summary (a guess from the decode; it is no reading)

Strengthened foreign troops and occupation of a tower cause concern despite peace rumors, especially for the border consequences of a settlement. Later fragments discuss diplomatic reception and rank.

## Reading

None. This letter is **decoded, not read**: the decode does not build sentences yet. No word of it is counted as read.

## The decode, row by row

The decode is the output of the key on the rows, with nothing edited after it. Where a sign has two readings, the decode takes the leading one, unless this page says otherwise. Each line names its page and its cipher row in `transcription/`. The row of each line was matched by its letters. A label after `≈` is placed by its order on the page, because the row shows too few letters to match.

- `·`: a code that the key leaves open.
- `?`: an item of the row that the decode does not take as a code: a sign with a doubt mark, a note of the transcription, or a stretch that is not read.
- a space: a space of the page, or a break at a note of the transcription.
