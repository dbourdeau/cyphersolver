## Summary (a guess from the decode; it is no reading)

Botti and the provincial treasurer, Conti, dispute who gives way to whom in the street ("il dritto della strada"). The delegate promised Botti precedence over all in the province. Botti says that precedence is not the same as taking the road from someone. Ruffo broke his word to the delegate, and Botti had to present a memorial in terms that shamed him. Conti says that he settled with Botti only as a private person, not as treasurer. Botti asks the cardinal to support him with Monsignore, in case the vice-legate cannot settle the matter.
