## Summary (a guess from the decode; it is no reading)

Botti had fever and a bad headache, so he could not write, and he asks pardon. Ferrara is ill. Ruffo said that he would give Botti time until he was well. Then he ordered the lieutenant to serve Botti another injunction ("precetto"). Botti says that he is wronged, and he thinks of leaving. He names the Abbate, a count, and a return from Florence.
