## Summary (a guess from the decode; it is no reading)

A patron's journey abroad is discussed, a rumored royal appointment remains unconfirmed, and travel details are requested. The writer then weighs ecclesiastical promotion rumors and doubts that income will be sacrificed for ambition.

## Reading
