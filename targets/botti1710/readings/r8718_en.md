# R8718: English translation

### Page T004

[...] in Livorno on the occasion of his journey to France [...] awaiting further news of his departure so that I may be punctually at the appointed place [...] to have the reply to this letter at Sarzana, my hometown, for which I shall set out one day this week.

### Page T005

[...] The Archbishop is preparing to come *ad limina* in spring, hoping to be well received by Our Lord and also placed in office if he should wish it.
