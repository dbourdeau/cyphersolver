Marks: words written in cipher are in **bold**; clear text is plain, with doubtful clear words in `{braces}`. Joined groups (a word split over two groups at a line end) are written as one word and listed in NOTES.md. Period spelling kept (*materjały*, before the 1936 reform).

Count (words written in cipher): 34 words; sure 29, probable 4, unread 1, after a blind digit-shape sheet on five doubtful numbers (see NOTES.md). Sure share 0.853 → **read in part**. With probable words: 0.971. The cipher part is short (183 letters): the 95 % / 300-character bar is not claimed. Point 6 (clear text, read only in part: {Ostatni} {wykaz} transportu …) is not given.
