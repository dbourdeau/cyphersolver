Normalised: "De buena parte se ha tenido aviso que olandeses han ydo a asentar fatoria al puerto de Pinda en la costa de Angola y que allí y en otras partes van entablando su trato contra lo capitulado en la tregua, y porque no es bien pasar esto en disimulación, os encargo y mando procuréis que se remedie por los medios que os pareciere, sin que vaya persona a este effecto a las Islas, pues no convendría que fuese por la reputación, y avisaréis me de lo que se hiciere."

## Translation

From a good quarter word has been had that Dutchmen have gone to set up a factory at the port of Pinda on the coast of Angola, and that there and in other parts they are entering upon their trade contrary to what was agreed in the truce; and because it is not right to let this pass in dissimulation, I charge and command you to see that it is remedied by whatever means seem fitting to you, without a person going to the Islands for this purpose, since it would not be fitting that he should go, for reputation's sake; and you shall inform me of what is done.

## Notes

- "Fatoria" is kept as deciphered (not modernised to "factoría"); "tregua" is read from the sibling I 242,48 (242 = tregua); that it is the Twelve Years' Truce is the contributor's contextual interpretation, the body names no article. "Islas" is kept as deciphered; reading it as the Dutch provinces is an interpretation. The group "je" ends "olandeses"; it does not license adding "se" before "han ydo".
- Open signs, by the contributor's own table: U01 (buena), 101 and 114 and 241 (contextual codes, no direct parallel), e = mu, fo = so (persona), the nulls 28~ and 58~, the anomaly m = as giving "otraas".
