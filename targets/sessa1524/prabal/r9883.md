- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9883_ff168-169_rows.txt`.
- **Read before:** The ledger read its cipher (f. 168v) against the period decipherment ff. 172-173 to build its key (key_working.md, passes 4, 5 and 9).

- **Words, estimated:** 303: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 98. Sure with my added values: 122. Holes: 181. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 31 of the 451 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

Most of its rows are masked or do not read. The war will fall on the Pope's shoulders; the French show more contentment; the Cardinal of England, the archbishop of Capua, the Swiss. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
