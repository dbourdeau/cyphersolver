# R9894: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 29 July 1524 (probable)

- **DECODE:** record R9894, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9894.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 504-508.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9894_ff504-508_rows.txt`.
- **Units:** 129 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 62 with letters and 0 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 30. My added entries: 4 with letters and 0 nulls (2 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 9. No value: 24. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 55; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 5.
- **Words, estimated:** 62: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 27. Sure with my added values: 29. Holes: 33. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 30 of the 129 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

Help; the enterprise of France; time; what would be necessary. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
