## Summary

The datary and the Pope; the Pope 'is here with the King of France'; people very warm with the datary; the nature of his master; Alberto Pio da Carpi; no provision from the viceroy; the need. Madame was content that the second son of the King of France should have an investiture. The suspicion that was taken; this article; after his letter came, no provision has been made. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
