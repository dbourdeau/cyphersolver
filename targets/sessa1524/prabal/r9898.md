## Summary

The Pope and the datary, the datary's attempt to bring the Duke over to the French, money, the Swiss, and the government from here on. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
