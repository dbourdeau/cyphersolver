- **Doubt:** 31 of the 265 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

Close to the clerk's decipherment of the time. All the war will fall on the Pope's shoulders. Neither the Pope nor the Florentines have made more provision of money than Sessa has written. The Cardinal of England and the Regent. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
