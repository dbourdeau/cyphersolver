## Summary

Its rows read poorly. The Pope; the viceroy; help; the same phrases on friendship and money as R9889. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
