- **Doubt:** 64 of the 534 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

It shares passages with R9905. The mother of the King of France sends an ambassador; the King of England; the Pope; whether they will come; resistance. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
