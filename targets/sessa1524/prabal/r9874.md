## Summary

The last leaf of the letter of 14 April 1524 (R9873): the cipher postscript. Lombardy, the archbishop of Capua, help and money. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
