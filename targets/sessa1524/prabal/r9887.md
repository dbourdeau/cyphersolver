# R9887: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, not dated (between 22 April and May 1524)

- **DECODE:** record R9887, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9887.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 274-277.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9887_ff274-277_rows.txt`.
- **Units:** 581 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 326 with letters and 53 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 52. My added entries: 14 with letters and 2 nulls (4 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 19. No value: 115. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 60; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 5.
- **Words, estimated:** 294: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 145. Sure with my added values: 155. Holes: 139. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 52 of the 581 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

The Pope; it is impossible to secure; the enemy; Siena; someone in France was not very content; matters of Your Majesty. Several code values here are doubtful. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
