## Summary

Close to the clerk's decipherment of the time. Sessa has understood that the archbishop of Capua did not leave France content, nor with any of the talks, above all that of peace or truce. Madame the Regent sent a person after him to assure him, so that he would go on with the enterprise; of the one and the other nothing is said. The Pope by no means wants to show displeasure with the French. Of England and of other parts nothing more is known than what Sessa has written. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
