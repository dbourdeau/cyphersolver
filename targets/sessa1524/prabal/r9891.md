- **DECODE:** record R9891, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9891.

- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.

- **Words, estimated:** 278: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 118. Sure with my added values: 138. Holes: 140. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 57 of the 449 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

The nuncio; the archbishop of Capua; a suspicion; help to someone in France; no money given; the datary; Alberto Pio da Carpi; the viceroy; the war. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
