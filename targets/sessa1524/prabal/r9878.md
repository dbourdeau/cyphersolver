# R9878: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 18 April 1524 (duplicate of R9877)

- **DECODE:** record R9878, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9878.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 130-131.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9878_ff130-131_rows.txt`.
- **Read before:** The duplicate of R9877. The ledger collated it with f. 128 before me (NOTES.md: "token-for-token the same").
- **Units:** 78 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 31 with letters and 1 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 16. My added entries: 2 with letters and 0 nulls (2 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 2. No value: 26. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 58; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 0.
- **Words, estimated:** 48: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 14. Sure with my added values: 14. Holes: 34. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 16 of the 78 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key. This record is the duplicate of R9877.

## Summary

The duplicate of R9877; its rows read poorly. The letters that were not shown, the suspicion, and the talks. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
