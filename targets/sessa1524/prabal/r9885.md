- **DECODE:** record R9885, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9885.

- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.

- **Words, estimated:** 8: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 2. Sure with my added values: 2. Holes: 6. No read share, because the record is not read. The record has under 40 words by this estimate. The floor for short texts is not used, because the record is not read.
