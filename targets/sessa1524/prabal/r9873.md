## Summary

The French army is breaking up. The Pope will not declare himself in a league, defensive or offensive, and is thought to lean towards the French. Lombardy, the coming of the archbishop of Capua, Micer Agostino Foglietta, money for the army and the viceroy's need. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.
