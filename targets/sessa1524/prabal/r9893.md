## Summary

Close to the clerk's decipherment of the time. While he waits for Monsieur de la Roxa (as the clerk spells him), the Pope will not talk of anything that touches the state. There is no mention of peace, truce or war, as if he were not Pope. He will not show himself for one side or the other, so everything is in suspense. Sessa does not know what the Pope will do when that man comes, whom he awaits with desire. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
