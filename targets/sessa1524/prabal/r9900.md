## Summary

Lombardy and the need; the viceroy; the datary; matters of state; it would be time to come; from England nothing; to enter France; the French would not be acceptable; room given to the enemy; the defence; money; Siena, where the viceroy is. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
