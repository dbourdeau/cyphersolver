## Summary

The decode shows: the said letters were not shown to Sessa; a suspicion; the Swiss; the talks and the truce. The ledger's reading of f. 128 gives the full sense: the Pope's secretary would not show the letters of the bishop of Veroli, and the news agrees with what the bishop wrote to the postmaster. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
