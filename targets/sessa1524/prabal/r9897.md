# R9897: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 7 January 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9897, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9897.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 10-16.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9897_ff10-16_rows.txt`.
- **Read before:** The ledger read its cipher against the clear of ff. 14v-15 to build its key (key_working.md, passes 10 and 11).
- **Units:** 1641 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 851 with letters and 92 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 200. My added entries: 77 with letters and 22 nulls (21 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 121. No value: 278. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 302; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 31.
- **Words, estimated:** 802: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 340. Sure with my added values: 380. Holes: 422. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 200 of the 1641 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

The said letter; no time has been lost; Parma; Your Majesty was content; at length; any preparation. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
