- **Doubt:** 145 of the 1438 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

The Pope awaits someone's coming; he is very content that nothing has been done in Lombardy; the viceroy's coming is awaited each day; of England no memory; the league; the defence. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
