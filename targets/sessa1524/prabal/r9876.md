## Summary

A copy of part of the letter of 14 April 1524. The Pope will not declare himself in a league, defensive or offensive. Sessa holds it for certain that, under pressure, the Pope would lean to the French out of fear. Little help, the affairs of Lombardy, and what the archbishop of Capua will bring. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
