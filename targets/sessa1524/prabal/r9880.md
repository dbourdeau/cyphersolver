# R9880: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, April 1524; text shared with 17 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9880, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9880.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) f. 137.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9880_f137_rows.txt`.
- **Units:** 258 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 154 with letters and 2 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 41. My added entries: 18 with letters and 9 nulls (4 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 2. No value: 32. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 41; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 1.
- **Words, estimated:** 116: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 64. Sure with my added values: 71. Holes: 45. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 41 of the 258 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key. This record is a copy of the cipher of R9881.
