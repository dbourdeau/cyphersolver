# R9901: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 18 May 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9901, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9901.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 300-303.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9901_ff300-303_rows.txt`.
- **Units:** 41 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 12 with letters and 0 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 7. My added entries: 2 with letters and 0 nulls (0 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 2. No value: 18. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 32; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 8.
- **Words, estimated:** 29: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 7. Sure with my added values: 8. Holes: 21. No read share, because the record is not read. The record has under 40 words by this estimate. The floor for short texts is not used, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 7 of the 41 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

Too little cipher (41 units) to say. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.
