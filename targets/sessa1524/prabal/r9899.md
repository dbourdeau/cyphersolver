# R9899: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, March 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9899, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9899.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 194-195.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9899_ff194-195_rows.txt`.
- **Read before:** The ledger read its one marginal decipherment (`hog` = querria; key_working.md, pass b23).
- **Units:** 218 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 106 with letters and 1 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 56. My added entries: 12 with letters and 0 nulls (1 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 6. No value: 37. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 51; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 2.
- **Words, estimated:** 119: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 47. Sure with my added values: 56. Holes: 63. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 56 of the 218 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

Too little is decoded to say. Time, place. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
