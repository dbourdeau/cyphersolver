# R9889: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 19 May 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9889, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9889.
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Madrid, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 320-321.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode does not build sentences.
- **Key:** from key: the ledger's key (KS4 of Brice Dudout, on Daniel Bourdeau's `key_working.md`), with the values that I added (`key/added-values.tsv`). The full table of this decode is `key/key.tsv`.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R9889_ff320-321_rows.txt`.
- **Units:** 384 cipher units, as I classify them. Ledger key, no doubt mark: 204 with letters and 45 switch marks or openers. Doubtful: 51. My added entries: 30 with letters and 5 nulls (5 of them carry my `~` doubt in the rows). Clerk value only: 18. No value: 31. `?` positions of my rows (`…`): 128; some may be cipher signs that I did not read. Clear words cut: 5.
- **Words, estimated:** 179: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 94. Sure with my added values: 110. Holes: 69. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 51 of the 384 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key.

## Summary

The duplicate of R9890's cipher in part. The Pope shows; the due fulfilment; friendship; the Pope has given no more money; the French. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
