## Summary

The same cipher passage as R9881. The archbishop of Capua left France not content. In none of the talks, above all those of peace or truce, did things go well. Madame the Regent sent a person after him to assure him. The Pope by no means wants to show displeasure towards the French. Nothing more is known from England or elsewhere. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
