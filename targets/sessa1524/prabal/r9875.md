- **Words, estimated:** 904: the tokens of the decode lines (an estimate; see README.md, "Words, estimated"). Sure by the ledger's key alone: 379. Sure with my added values: 465. Holes: 439. No read share, because the record is not read.
- **Doubt:** 320 of the 2075 cipher units carry a doubt mark: a sign that I doubt in my rows, a sign map off its aligned page, or a clerk-copy value that differs from the key. This record is probably the duplicate of R9873.

## Summary

Much of its text recurs in the letter of 14 April 1524 (R9873), so it is probably the duplicate of that letter. Peace and truce, the King of France, England, Lombardy, the viceroy's need, Micer Agostino Foglietta, Alberto Pio da Carpi and the datary. This summary gives the sense as far as the decode carries it. It is not a reading.

## Reading

None. The decode does not build sentences in Spanish, so I give no reading and no translation.
