His Holiness told me yesterday of a letter he had from Lyon in France, and almost all the substance of what it

But Sigismondo had not yet arrived when the King's mother made that provision, nor had Alberto da Carpi returned here,

The Marquis of Pescara had taken the whole marquisate of Saluzzo, and the marquis withdrew into France with some
