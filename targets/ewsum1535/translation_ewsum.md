# Translation of the cipher opening in Johan van Ewsum's register (Groninger Archieven 696 inv. 595)

English, from `reading_ewsum.md`, with the same holes. Square brackets: `[...]` hole; `[n]` letter supplied by a
guess under a blot; `[word]` added for English sense. A question mark in brackets marks a doubtful rendering, not a
doubtful sign.

**A. Letter (L01-L20).** Good friends, the pay of the soldiers arrived here yesterday with the envoy[s] and Hendricus.
So please send over the account and the receipt for it at once, and consider how you may get it over safely; and
consider how you may get it over safely. Here there are about twenty soldiers who have been out with the colonels and
have fallen behind [?: *sich verechtert hebben*, perhaps "have been left short" or "have been harmed"]; one could use
them for it as well, so that at Wedde, on the tenth of July, the envoy and those who are not [included] in the
payment may be summoned [?: the syntax of L17-L20 is doubtful; this English supplies more than the words carry].

**B. Note (L21-R06).** Lord Drost, the truce between my gracious lords and the cities of the Sticht has been extended
by one month, but Jorien [probably Georg] Schenck will not keep to it. Therefore it will be dangerous to travel over the open
country. Hendricus.

**C. Address (R07-R09).** To Hackfort and Meynert van Ham, colonel(s).

**D. Formula (R10).** Ergo : tergo : be[...]lo. (Followed in clear Latin by "ad perforandum caput gallinae", "for
piercing the head of a hen".)
