# Sign conventions (shape names only, no values)

Groninger Archieven 696 (Familie van Ewsum) inv. 595, the cipher opening (scan 193, photographed again as scan 1).
Each cipher sign is written as one ASCII code chosen for its shape. Words are separated by a space, as the writer leaves
gaps between words.

| code | shape |
|---|---|
| `A` | arrow: a caret (inverted V) on an upright stem |
| `8` | figure eight |
| `3` | ezh: a 3-shape with a tail below the line |
| `o` | small open ring on the line |
| `P` | pi: an upright gate, two legs and a top bar |
| `X` | circle filled with ink, crossed by two lines |
| `E` | comb: a box open at the bottom with three or four uprights (lying-E shape) |
| `H` | double cross: an upright with two crossbars |
| `q` | staff ring: a small ring on a long descending stem |
| `K` | orb: a ring with a small cross standing on top |
| `x` | saltire with one long stroke falling to the left |
| `*` | star: asterisk of six or eight rays |
| `N` | lying eight (infinity) |
| `B` | small box with one or two inner uprights |
| `b` | hooked staff: an upright with a bowl/hook on the right at the top |
| `Q` | bar with a ring or ink blob on its middle (a horizontal line through a small ring) |
| `D` | hook: a ring with an ascender curving back to the left (delta-like) |
| `R` | knot: a loop on two crossing legs, the left leg long |
| `T` | hash: three uprights crossed by one horizontal |
| `F` | F-shape |
| `S` | open square |
| `Y` | big hook: a tall loop with a long tail turning back below the line |
| `V` | venus: a small ring on a stem with a crossbar on the stem |

Other marks:

- `/` at the end of a line or `//` at the start: the writer's own double stroke for a word carried over to the next line.
- `?` a sign that cannot be made out (ink blot); `[del:...]` a crossed-out group; `[sup:...]` a group written above it.
- `,` and `-` as written.
