## The letter

ASMo, Carteggio Principi Esteri, 1623, **b. 2/7, 11** (not b. 2/2); DECODE R1154, two images (the letter and the address
side). Ordinary Italian chancery writing and abbreviations, no substituted signs. Beatrice asks Ippolito to forward
letters to the cardinal of Esztergom by a trusted messenger travelling as if on Ippolito's own business, and thanks him
for the company of his household official Francesco. The decisive sentence:

> Et per non esser certa che le littere pervengano secure, et per non havere cifra: non ce extendemo altramente per questa

The date ends "M° quingentesimo quinto"; the photograph label and docket also give 1505. DECODE's 1504 is wrong.
