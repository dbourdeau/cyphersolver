# Reading the July pencil glosses

Two new readers independently transcribed twelve anonymous crop pairs after the October tests. They saw a native crop and a contrast-enhanced view of the same marks. Neither saw the table, letter-level context, proposed values or the other reader's answer. The crops were deliberately chosen by a reader who knew the table. This spot check tests manuscript legibility and cannot estimate the accuracy of the complete key.

Both readers gave the same number and the same unqualified pencilled letters for 141 = que, 78 = en, 112 = ma, 163 = to, and 39 = u. On three separate appearances of 133, both recognized pa or the same beginning of a longer string. Neither pair independently agreed on a definite final r at any of those three locations. The 71- and 78-entry tables used par. That remains a context-supported expansion of an ambiguous gloss. On 169 the readers could not distinguish ve from vi with confidence. At 176 the first reader offered ra/re, and the second gave za with its initial marked uncertain. The word PLAZA on 14 October therefore has support from the frozen table and from recurrence. The isolated July crop does not independently establish its final emission. In the settled transcripts 133 never occurs on 14 October. It occurs once on 15 October, in unresolved run 6. No credited word on either date uses it. The uncertain 169 occurs on neither date. The test set contains a single 176, at the end of PLAZA on 14 October. All reported October scores are for the originally frozen tables.
