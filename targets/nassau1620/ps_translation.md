# English translation — Postscript "Post dat.", fol. 53r, to a letter not identified [spring 1620]

Working translation, close to the text; […] = unresolved word; nomenclator signs stay in brackets.

Your Dilection’s letter makes me doubt whether [the officers] have, besides the [∇] florins fixed by the late Elector as yearly pay, florins for allowance as well; in which case I hold that, if they were in actual service in the field, they should always be allowed enough to keep their table according to their quality and office, especially in the absence of the Lord Margrave, as is fitting […] (the line runs to the edge of the leaf).
