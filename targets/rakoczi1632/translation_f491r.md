## English

**Memorandum.** [Committing himself to the journey?] with people known to him, let him venture to reach the **King of
Sweden** as quickly as possible; arriving there in peace by God's providence, let him [deal?] **about these matters**
with the **King of Sweden** and **Saxony** and the other Christian **Electors** and the **United Princes**. Above all it
is necessary that he travel as **circumspectly** and **watchfully** as possible, lest he fall into **hostile hands**.

If therefore they **ask** us to **join** them and to **unite** for the **common cause**, we rightly **demand** this
**of them**: that they **bind** themselves by a **firm** **sacrament of oath** that they will not **desert** **us**
**in** **good** or **bad fortune**, [even] to the **loss** and **forfeit** of **life**, **treasure** and
**substance**; nay, if the matter required it, that they **come to our aid** **in person**; and that they will make
or **conclude** **peace** with **no one** at all **without** our **knowledge** and **will**, or to our **ruin** or
**loss**. Moreover, if in the meantime it should befall **us** (which God mercifully avert) to **die**, they shall
