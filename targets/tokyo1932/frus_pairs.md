# The 53 telegrams compared with FRUS

Japanese copies (JACAR C05022467800-C05022469300) set against the text printed in Foreign Relations of the United States
(FRUS) 1932 vol. III and IV, and Japan 1931-1941 vol. I. The Japanese copy is typed English clear text recovered by the
Japanese Navy's cryptanalytic office; FRUS gives the American text. "dNNN" = document number in the Office of the Historian's
online FRUS; NARA = State Department decimal file. "?" after a 米特情 number = number partly legible.


The rates were not measured the same way in every batch:
- **a**: estimate per message (sense);
- **b**: identical words (difflib) and, in brackets, sense recovered;
- **c**: exact words (difflib) and, in brackets, counting correct truncations;
- **d**: manual count on the part read, ± 5 points, and in brackets on the whole message.

Comparisons between batches are therefore indicative.
