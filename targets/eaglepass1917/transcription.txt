# eaglepass1917 - cipher text of the telegram, Mexican embassy (Washington) to J. M. Arredondo, consul at Eagle Pass, 17 Apr 1917
# Source: transcribed from the halftone figure in R. Narváez, Criptografía diplomática, política y militar en México (1813-1926), SRE 2019, p. 339, fig. 10
# (photographs of a loan copy; no image committed). Original: AHGE-SRE, Embajada de México en Estados Unidos, leg. 507, exp. 6 (not seen).
# Three transcriptions, two of them blind: transcription_pass1.txt, transcription_pass2.txt, review/blind_transcription.txt.
# A fourth blind reading of the doubtful letters (23 Nov, later): review/blind_reading_doubtful_letters.md. Its new doubts are bracketed here; the reading used stays the majority reading.
