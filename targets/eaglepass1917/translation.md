# eaglepass1917 - English translation of reading.md

Words in {braces} are holes in the Spanish (a letter read from the sense); the translation follows the reading.

To {present} them to the censorship committee appointed {by} {President} Wilson, I {need} you to send me as soon as possible
the names of {journalists} and information agents {who} spread {false} {news} and stir up opinion, chiefly with news of
{German} plots. We must be able to {prove} it if {necessary}.

Notes. "Comité censura nombrado por presidente Wilson": the Committee on Public Information, created by Executive Order 2594
of 13 April 1917, four days before this repetition (the formal Censorship Board came only in October 1917). "Presentarlos"
looks forward to "nombres": the names are to be handed to that committee.
