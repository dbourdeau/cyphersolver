[P1:1-4] Dem heutigen schreiben d.(superscript "d." over the end of "schreiben") 181 werde die continuationes der grossen progressen in brabant beygeleget zu befinden seyn.
  codes: 181=? (genitive after "schreiben d[es]": 181 writes his own letter to Schenck in this post, which carries the Brabant news; masculine via "des"). The superscript "d." is the writer's own, same ink: "des" (or a dotted letter sign placed above the line).

[P1:4-9] Auch 187 brieffe wird ersehen, dass man weiter glückliche 515. auf[?] [ink blot] vor 150 sehr profitable aussehe, und glaube dass 204 desseins dürfften einen Stoss bekommen.
  codes: 187=? (owner of "brieffe"; i.e. 187 also writes to Schenck; the 7 has a small hook/mark above it, writer's ink). 515=? (object, "glückliche 515" = favourable [events/operations/news], plural noun; written "5ı5." with a dot after). 150=? ("vor 150 sehr profitable" = to the advantage of 150: King Augustus as elsewhere). 204=? (possessor of "desseins" that will "einen Stoss bekommen" after the Allied victories in Brabant: an enemy power, most likely France / the French King, or Sweden).

[P1:10-17] Ich bitte|befehle[?] 189 der 186. hauptsächlich mit Vergebung, dass sie früher so wenig schreiben; kann sie wan sie nicht 2. stunde diesen tag zu Ausfertigung seiner brieffe und übrigen schreiben erhalten können.
  codes: 189=? (dative/accusative object of the writer's request; 189 = Schenck as elsewhere; first digit with a curved stroke under it, the 9 clear at full resolution). 186=? ("der 186." genitive or dative feminine/plural; 186 may be the person who "so wenig schreiben" kann: "sie" here is the writer's polite third person for a superior, used also for 187 and 181; "seiner brieffe" then makes the subject masculine). "2." = two (hours), plain numeral, not a code.

The rumor clause: "rumor d. 150 gehen und b. 150 e. 208 und 157 zu soutenieren" (sense: rumour that 150 ... and that 150 and 208 [would?] support 157).
