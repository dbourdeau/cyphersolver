# Sieniawski letters R7468-R7485: page map (9 Oct 2026)

Archive page numbers are the pencil/ink numbers top right (rectos) or top left (versos). Key A = Brâncoveanu chancellery
2-digit key (Kaczka 2012 fn. 21); key B = Turculeț key (Kaczka fn. 22). "Gloss" = interlinear decipherment written
letter by letter (or word by word) above the cipher line. Kaczka numbers are his edition numbers (Danubius XXX, 2012).

Kaczka's nr 13 / nr 5 citations, from his headings (print/kaczka2012.txt): V = pp. 1-5 (28 VI 1709); VIII = pp. 7-10
("original of the postscript", 30 VIII 1709; main letter BC 5769 no. 3367); IV = pp. 11-14 (15 VI 1709); XXIII
(Cantacuzino) = pp. 15-16 (15 VI 1709); XIII = pp. 17-24 (25 VIII 1712); XXVI (Cantacuzino) = pp. 25-28 (8 IX 1714);
XXV (Cantacuzino) = pp. 35-38 (7 V 1714); XVII = pp. 39-41 (10 VIII 1713); XIX = pp. 43-46 (24 I 1714); XX = pp. 47-49
(29 III 1714); XXI = nr 5 pp. 3-4 (Brâncoveanu postscript <1714>). Note: the brief omitted VIII (pp. 7-10).
