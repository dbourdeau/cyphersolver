== Dating (pass 3) ==
The reading supports c. May-July 1714, Constantinople:
- XVIII "Hospodar multański" brought in and shut up in Yedikule "za suspicje dawne o Moskwie ... nieoddawanie prowiantu do
  Chocima": Constantin Brâncoveanu of Wallachia, deposed late March / early April 1714, held in Yedikule, executed
  15/26 Aug 1714. "ten, co jest" = his successor Ştefan Cantacuzino. Chocim was being refortified by the Porte in 1713-15.
- Charles XII still in Turkey, his creditors pressing him, "firmiter tu siedzi" (he left Demotika in Sept 1714); Stanisław
  "będzie się przebierać do swoich" (he left Bender in disguise in 1714).
- The envoy is waiting for his dispatch after the peace (the Adrianople treaty of June 1713 is settled: "po skończonych
  interesach"), and asks for carts ("nie dano mi wozów"): the end of Chomentowski's embassy (he returned in 1714).
- "Dewlet Girej Han" fits either way (Devlet II Giray, deposed 1713, then in exile); it does not move the date.
Not before April 1714, not after mid-August 1714.
