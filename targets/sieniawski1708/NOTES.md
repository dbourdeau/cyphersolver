# Constantin Brâncoveanu and Constantin Cantacuzino to Hetman Sieniawski, 1709-1714, in the Wallachian chancellery key (DECODE R7468-R7474, R7477-R7480, R7483)

Status: read from existing decipherment (Kaczka 2012), sample checked; written up 9 Oct 2026, narrowed 14 Oct 2026.

**Split on 14 Oct 2026** under the one-key rule (George Lasry, 14 Oct 2026). Until then this folder held all of
catalogue entry 333 (DECODE R7468-R7500): the Vienna-Schenck letters of 1706 and four groups of letters to Sieniawski
in four different keys. Each key now has its own entry and page (a different cipher in each):
[`schenck1706`](../schenck1706/NOTES.md) (R7486-R7500, letter table recovered ciphertext-only),
[`turculet1709`](../turculet1709/NOTES.md) (R7481-R7482, key B), [`r7475`](../r7475/NOTES.md) (the adviser's table,
c. 1707) and [`chomentowski1713`](../chomentowski1713/NOTES.md) (R7476, R7484, R7485, the syllabary). This folder keeps
the letters in key A, the Wallachian chancellery key, which Kaczka printed. Files moved to those folders on 14 Oct 2026;
`sien/map.md` (page map of all the Sieniawski records) and `sien/R7473_check.txt` stay here.

## The cipher

R7473 (nr 13 item 6): dot-separated 2-digit substitution, Polish, full interlinear decipherment ("Jaśnie wielmożny
M. P. Kasztelanie krakowski hetmanie w. k. ..."): J/i=29 a=19 s=73 n=72 e=9 w=65 l=74 m=76 o=39 z=69 y=59 P=62 k=67
t=77. The same numbers, unseparated, fill R7468-R7472 (digit strings with clear "WM Pana"). Kaczka's key A (fn. 21,
after A. Mareș 1987): a=19, b=60, c=66 ...; 900 = August.

## Check (9 Oct 2026)

R7473 p. 17, ten lines, decoded with key A = the interlinear gloss = Kaczka's text (`sien/R7473_check.txt`; two
encipherer slips, 69 for 67 in "krakowski", "prszez"). The other letters were not transcribed: their text is
Kaczka's, and this entry adds only the identification of the DECODE records with his edition.
