# Ercole II d'Este to Cornelio Bentivoglio, Ferrara, 28 May 1559, cc. 50-51

A transcription job. A contemporary interlinear decipherment in brown ink covers all the cipher of c. 50r. The text below follows the signs, re-copied with that gloss masked. Where the signs and the gloss differ, the signs stand, and the differences are listed at the end.

Marks. `<x>` is a letter restored or changed against the signs; each one is listed below. `[?x]` is doubtful. Italics stood in clear on the leaf; all the rest of the numbered text stood in cipher. Word division, capitals and punctuation are mine. The clear text is a first reading from a quarter-size image.

Address, c. 51v: *All'Ill.mo S.or S.or Cornelio Benti[voglio], Log.te di sua M.ta Chr.ma in Toscana.*

## c. 50r, cipher

*Ill.mo S.or: Non heri l'altro receuei la l[ette]ra della S. V. delli xxi dello instante, et hauendo per essa ueduto quanto la mi scriue*

1. Circa il non h[?a]<u>er ancora uoluto il Papa dar audienza alli ambassatori de cotesti signori, andati alli piedi di Sua Santita per impetrare fauore da lei, et la risposta fatt<a> li dal signor Gioan Antonio di Grauina, il che si era anche inteso da Roma, ne sono restato tutto sospeso, considerando che Sua Beatitudine, la qual ha magior forze et auttorita di me, non uole attend<e>rui ne intrincarsi nele cose loro.
   On the Pope's not yet having been willing to give audience to the ambassadors of those lords there, who went to the feet of His Holiness to obtain favour from him, and the answer given them by signor Gioan Antonio di Gravina, which was also heard from Rome, I have been left quite in suspense, considering that His Beatitude, who has greater forces and authority than I, will not attend to it nor entangle himself in their affairs.

2. Tanto p[?i]u bisogna ch'io guardi bene di non inciampare, et masime hauendo inteso che la capitulatione tra li duo Re sta in manera che Sanesi non [?si] p[?o]sono d<a>re a chi loro uogliono, che s<a>ria un gran passo, s<e> fosse uero, il che spero di poter saper fra doi o tre di.
   All the more must I take good care not to stumble, above all since I have heard that the capitulation between the two Kings stands in such a way that the Sienese cannot give themselves to whom they wish, which would be a great step, if it were true, and I hope to be able to learn this within two or three days.

3. Nel qual tempo douera parimenti uenir mi da Vostra Signoria la risposta de [?l]le ultime mie littere che le ho scritto, esendo ben sicuro che se prefata Vostra Signoria [?haura] inteso cosa alcuna del prefato auiso non mancara de auisarmelo, insieme con dirm<i> la sua opinione in tutto quelo di piu che [?l]la giudicara bene che da me sia considerato [?i]n questa materia et sue dep<e>ndente per tutti quei risp<e>tti che ui concoreno.
   In that time there must likewise come to me from Your Lordship the answer to my last letters that I wrote to you, being quite sure that if your said Lordship [?has] heard anything of the said report you will not fail to let me know of it, and at the same time to tell me your opinion on everything further that you judge it good that I should consider in this matter and what depends on it, for all the respects that come together there.

*con che prego Dio che prosperam[ente] et feliciti la S. V. et le adempia ogni suo desiderio: Da Ferr.a alli xxviii di Mag.o MDLIX.*

## Where the signs and the gloss differ

The gloss is quoted from `gloss_c50r_firstread.txt`, a first reading. These are the places where the signs go their own way.

| row | signs | gloss |
|---|---|---|
| 08 | chio; masime | che io; massime |
| 09 | duo; manera | due; maniera |
| 09-10 | Sanes\|i | sane\|(new row) |
| 10 | uogliono; sria | uoglino; saria |
| 12 | parimenti | partimente |
| 13 | littere | litere |
| 16 | in tutto quelo | intanto quello |
| 17 | [?i]n | per la |
| 18 | questa materia | quella materia |
| 18 | et sue dependente | a sie de pandente |
| 19 | concoreno | son coreno |

The last five change the sense of the end of the letter. The cipher writes one long sentence from *Nel qual tempo* to *concoreno*, where the gloss loses the thread at *intanto*. The decipherer, or our first copy, took *quella* for *questa* and *son coreno* for *concoreno*. So this reading rests on the signs and not on the gloss, and it should be checked on the image.

## The clear postscript, c. 50r foot and c. 50v (first reading, a different hand and ink)

*Non voglio restar di dir a V. Summary: Ercole wants to reassure Philip II before he sails for Spain, and plans to go to both courts on the occasion of the weddings. His son, kept in France for the jousts, delays this.
