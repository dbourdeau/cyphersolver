### c. 24v

*... with every affection and care;* I do not know how Monsignore will take it. *... the enclosed letter of the Lord Duke of Torre Maggiore ...*

*... whether I should send it on or not.* I have taken the sheet in cipher out of the letter of the [?Lord] [Duke], [and] it is the following: [200] I hear [165] [Your Excellency] tells me about the matter of the grain, [and] I shall wait for the month of October [to] carry out [185] [162] [that] you point out to me, [to] carry out the loading when the boats come, while you tell me [that] they will not be lacking, and that they [?will be got] from [?this] business, in which I am persuaded you will be ready [to] favour me effectively.

I do not fail, then, to tell [Your Excellency] [that] this blessed plague, [which] keeps people in distress and has ruined [?this] [168], no one knows [what] it is, nor can one get at the truth, *when in* these parts people live in good health, and from the province of Bari the news comes that they have other sickness [than] hunger (the Italian has no negative; read in context, no sickness but hunger), of *[q.ste: which?]* many are dying of it.
