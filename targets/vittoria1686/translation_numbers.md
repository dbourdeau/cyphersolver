# Catalogue 1570: Vittoria Gonzaga, nun at Santa Chiara, Naples, to her brother: the number passages, 1686-91, English

### c. 3v

Don Paolo Ruffo [?I reckon] of [?sperate] in no way wants to lift the sequestration from San Paolo, and we fear he will proceed to the sale of the goods.

We have begged him for [...] and love, and we have achieved nothing.

The Marquis of Casalnuovo was arranging, [?through a deputy], to send the [?sequestrator] to San Paolo for his [?revenues]. Has Your Excellency [?held] [...] of this matter? We know nothing of it. Do us the favour of telling us what is going on.

We have now come to the time when the dogs have woken all together; I see no other [?remedy] but a safeguard from [?Spain], and in particular cartloads for many others. This lord does not know how to please anyone, and the more conspicuous things are, the more [...]; just as a good part of the [?wives] have died, houses in the castle, and the others, if God does not provide, will follow after, so not [...]

### c. 21r

I say that Your Excellency should look into the whole load and then settle it [literally: give a cut]. Enclosed here are the items of the bank notes and receipts.

The [109] of Torre Maggiore has had Pompeo write to me that he [?wants] me to get him the cartloads of licensed grain. I [?answered him] with ceremonies, and that until now the licences had not been opened and the Camera kept them behind iron doors, as is the case, reserving them for the State of Milan. I am suspicious, Your Excellency knows it, and so I stand firm in the notion that he needs money.
