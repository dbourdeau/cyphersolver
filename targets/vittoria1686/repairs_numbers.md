Summary. Estate and trade business from a convent in Naples: the sequestration of San Paolo by Don Paolo Ruffo, the Marquis of Casalnuovo, the Collaterale, a shipment of grain and the licences for it. The longest passage, c. 21r of 16 Oct 1691, reports that a [109] of Torre Maggiore asked through Pompeo for licensed grain, that she refused politely because the Camera held the licences for the State of Milan, and that she thinks he needs money.
