# Catalogue 1570 (`vittoria1686`) — c. 3r–4v, 2 April 1686: Vittoria & Faustina Gonzaga to Vincenzo Gonzaga (cipher on cc. 3v–4r) (English)

Plain text translates the cipher passages; *italics* translate the surrounding clear text. Doubts match the Italian reading word for word; explanatory insertions and resolved nomenclator words appear in square brackets.

## English

### c. 3v

*Concerning Don Paolo Ruffo,* we are desperate in *our own way*; he wants to lift the sequestration from San Paolo *and* we fear [that] he will proceed to the sale of the property; we have *begged him by letter and in person* and have accomplished nothing.

*The Marquis of Casalnuovo* was negotiating in the Vicaria to dispatch a writ of execution (*secutorio*) against San Paolo *for* his revenues; *Your Excellency has* kept [us unaware] *of this matter; we know nothing of it. Please do us the favor of letting us know what is going on.*

We have already reached the point [that] all the dogs have awakened *together; there is no other* remedy [than] *a* safeguard from Spain, *to settle everyone for at least 10 years, just as many houses enjoy it, and in particular* [the Prince of] Cariati *and many others. This Lord* [Viceroy Marquis del Carpio] knows not how to do a favor *for anyone, and the more prominent things are,* the more *he* strikes them down, *just as a great part of the foremost* houses have died in the castle [prison], *and the rest, if God does not provide, will follow after;* thus *there is no person one can trust.*

### c. 4r

*At the* Collateral Council, *since [?Galeota] passed away, they are* all Pulcinellas [puppets], *and do not remember* friends, *and no longer* let [decrees] go out; *and from experience we know that after granting the favor and spending the money they revoke it. In sum, Your Excellency can do whatever you wish, but not like the Regent of Sanctoro, because* San Paolo is slipping out of our hands; *one would need to act in haste (di volata). When you are pleased thus, and when not, please favor us with instructions. For the love of God have pity on us, for we cannot* trust anyone; *we are nuns, and besides today is a time when there are no longer gentlemen, and thinking that something might happen* [that] is not to your liking pierces our soul.

*This gentleman* of Rome is the most gracious and virtuous son *one could wish for,* how *attentive he is to* expenses, to *accounts; he is a marvel of Nature. Your Excellency kindly tells us* [that] you wish to provide for him, *so he has need* of no one. *We thank you infinitely, yet we beg Your Excellency for charity's sake to* let us see the *effects, since our* sleep *is always interrupted by distress over every peasant of San Paolo. [Every peasant] has* the means to live and *this* son does not have a crust of bread assigned [to him].

*When he was in Naples he spoke these words to us: "I pray God shorten* my own years *and* add [them] to *my Lord* Father, and grant health to *my Most Serene Lady,* that [she] soon hear the birth *of a little Prince, so that I may have the honor of being his servant, and when* I *see myself* at his feet, I know well *that he will not cast me away* at least *out of* his generosity."
