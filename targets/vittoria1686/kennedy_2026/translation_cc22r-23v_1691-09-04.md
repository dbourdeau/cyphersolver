# Catalogue 1570 (`vittoria1686`) — c. 22r–23v, 4 September 1691: Vittoria Gonzaga to Vincenzo Gonzaga (cipher on cc. 22r–23r) (English)

Plain text translates the cipher passages; *italics* translate the surrounding clear text. Doubts match the Italian reading word for word; explanatory insertions and resolved nomenclator words appear in square brackets.

## English

### c. 22r

*Friar Basilio has remained and went to San Nicandro because* the bishop wanted to imprison him. *Don Giovanni has written to the Monsignor of Molfetta.*

### c. 22v

*It is true that* with the bishop it is a wretched life. *The Patron of Marsilia.*

### c. 23r

*And if I did not have* the bridge [dispute] [that] troubles me, *I would enjoy a few months of peace.*

*He is here in Naples and is completely in league with the* Lieutenant of the Chamber and [?letters] [of] injunctions (*precetti*); *I could not afford to alienate him.*
