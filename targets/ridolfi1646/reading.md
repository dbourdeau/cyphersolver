## Blind re-reading on the images (11 Nov 2026)

Brice Dudout re-read on the PARES images, blind, the doubtful digit of the 19 groups behind the restored words that a shape
can settle. Each question showed the line, the group enlarged and two reference shapes (thumbnails of the same digit position
taken from certain words elsewhere in the letter), in random order, with no value or gloss; two control questions with a
known answer were mixed in. The integration rule was written and hashed before the sheet was opened (frozen/BLIND-REREADING.md).
Both controls were answered as expected, so the rule was applied: where the shape chosen is the corrected digit, the word
becomes certain (every syllable then reads directly with the frozen key); where it is the digit of transcription v1, the
word stays restored. Result: 12 corrections confirmed (now certain), 6 images match transcription v1 (still restored:
probable cipher-clerk error), 1 undecided, 2 not on the sheet. One reader only; the images are not included.
