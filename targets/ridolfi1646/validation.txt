Order of operations (contributor's folder; files and SHA-256 in frozen/, protocol in French):
1. Transcription v1 of pp. 1-5 made sign by sign and frozen on 28 Oct 2026, before any key existed (frozen/transcr/).
   Page 5 (99 groups, 120 decodable letters) was kept apart from then on: earlier attempts (C01, C03, cribs of 5 Nov) used
   pp. 1-4 only.
2. Key proposed by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic, API run of 11 Nov 2026) from pp. 1-4 only; the files sent to the model
   were the transcriptions of pp. 1-4 and shape notes for pp. 1-4 (run manifest checked: no page-5 file).
   Copied unchanged: frozen/KEY-PROPOSAL.json, frozen/KEY-PROPOSAL.tsv, frozen/ctrl.py, frozen/load.py.
3. Our own deterministic decoder (frozen/scripts/decode.py) applies only the letter and syllable values of the proposal
   (no nomenclator value, no new value); on pp. 1-4 its output is identical to the proposer's raw decryption.
4. Protocol frozen/PREREG.md written and hashed before page 5 was decoded by anyone:
   SHA-256 ce607b24705b4ed2b6106e5a77395f3832429bdcae8c2eae902a42a8f9c2691d (frozen/FREEZE.sha256, 11 Nov 2026 10:54 CET),
   together with the key, decoder, metric, calibration, Italian lexicon and transcriptions.
5. Metric COV = share of the decoded letters of p. 5 covered by words of 4 letters or more of this repository's Italian
   lexicon (lang/lexicons/it.json.gz, decompressed SHA-256 f4d2f270..., entries of frequency >= 3; v->u, j->i, accents
   dropped), maximum cover per segment; X, 3-digit codes and groups outside the key are breaks. There is no decipherment
   of the time and no parallel clear, so no character-by-character reference exists; COV is the proposer's own control
   metric, fixed before the measure.
6. Controls computed on p. 5 in the same run: 200 shuffled keys (values permuted among letter codes and among syllable
   codes) and 200 shuffles of the decodable groups of p. 5 among their positions (breaks kept in place), seed 5.
7. Thresholds calibrated beforehand on pp. 1-4 only (frozen/scripts/calib.out), on 51 windows of 99 groups (the size of
   p. 5): key COV min 0.783, median 0.861; margin over the best shuffled key min 0.256; margin over the best group shuffle
   min 0.098 (the group shuffle is a strong control: syllable codes give Italian syllables in any order). Our usual rule
   (>= 0.85 and >= best control + 0.30) would reject 44 of the 51 in-sample windows, so it cannot be applied at this size.
   Rule fixed in advance: C1 COV >= 0.75; C2 COV >= best shuffled key + 0.25; C3 COV >= best group shuffle + 0.08;
   validated if C1 and C2 and C3. Sense check fixed in advance: S1 page 5 must close the sentence left open at the end of
   page 4 ("che mi sara data subito la ..."); S2 at least 3 consecutive lines of p. 5 must read as Italian on the raw output.

Reservations, declared:
- The held-out part is small: 99 groups (120 letters), below our own later rule of 150 groups (10 Nov 2026); the letter
  has no other page, and no other document in this cipher is known. The thresholds were therefore calibrated for this
  size instead of our usual 0.85 / +0.30.
- The reference is a lexical cover, not a clear of the time: the test shows that the frozen key turns unseen ciphertext
  into Italian words far beyond chance, and the sense check that the text continues page 4; it does not check each letter.
- Two groups of p. 5 lie outside the key (47, restored as z) or read better as a neighbour (26 for 76); both were
  settled after the measure and are counted as restored.
- Nomenclator values (100-243) are not part of the test; they are given by the sense only.
- The transcription is the frozen v1 (four passes by AI agents), not checked by a human palaeographer.
