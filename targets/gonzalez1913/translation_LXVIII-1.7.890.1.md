# Translation: Pablo González to Andrés Saucedo, Matamoros, December 1913 (CEHM LXVIII-1.7.890.1)

Coded parts in capitals, with the same holes as the Spanish reading.

> H. Matamoros, December 2[?], 1913. Colonel Andrés Saucedo, Sabinas Hidalgo.
>
> I received your message of yesterday with satisfaction. Today [?] left TO JOIN his forces AT CERRALVO. Try to finish operations AT LAMPAZOS, VILLALDAMA, as far as they are possible; otherwise try TO CUT OFF THE ENEMY who is AT LAMPAZOS AND to advance TO THE NORTH DESTROYING THE LINES OF communication, being IN THE NEIGHBOURHOOD OF LAREDO by NEXT FRIDAY.
>
> I have already given orders TO CASTRO, DÁVILA SÁNCHEZ AND SANTOSCOY to leave and TAKE UP POSITION THAT same DAY NEAR LAREDO, a place WE MUST ATTACK at the LATEST ON the coming SATURDAY OR SUNDAY.
>
> The day after tomorrow I LEAVE FOR SAN YGNACIO, where I AWAIT THE NEWS FROM YOU, in order to send you INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE ATTACK I refer to. I congratulate you on the good success of your operations so far. The General in Chief.
