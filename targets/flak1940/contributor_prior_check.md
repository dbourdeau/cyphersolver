# Search for earlier work (10 November 2026)

We looked for an earlier recovery of the key of 17 May 1940 or an earlier decryption of frames 0993 and 1025-1027. Two readers searched: the one who made this reading, and a second reader who had not seen it (Claude Sonnet, instructed to try to refute the claim).

**Result: nothing found in the places below.** Not finding something is not proof that it does not exist.

To be clear:
- **The plaintexts survive in the same file in their contemporary form** (frame 0993: the recipient's fair copy on frames 0994-0995; frames 1025-1027: the teleprinter message on frame 1024). What was recovered here is the key of the day and the letter-by-letter correspondence between cipher text and plaintext.
- The ledger's own page for catalogue entry 704 suggests this kind of attack: use the typed teleprinter messages on neighbouring frames as cribs and run a double-transposition solver with key lengths 15-25. The method used here is of that kind (the second key turned out to be 28 long, outside that range).
- The messages read here lie inside the frame ranges listed by catalogue entry 704 (964-1003, 1025-1056).
