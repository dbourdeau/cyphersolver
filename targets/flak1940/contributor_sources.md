# Sources

- U.S. National Archives (NARA), RG 242, microfilm publication T405 "German Air Force Records: Luftgaukommandos, Flak, Deutsche Luftwaffenmission in Rumänien", roll 9 (NAID 332033680, https://catalog.archives.gov/id/332033680). Frame images: `https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-242/7794463/T405/T405-0009/T405-0009-NNNNN.tif` (NNNNN = frame number).
- Frames used:

| Frame | Content |
|---|---|
| 0349, 0350 | Exercise radio message of 25 February 1939 and the key card of that day (keys 21 and 19). Used to test the tools |
| 0993 | 17 May 1940: cipher text of a radio message on teleprinter tape (SMU 0113 17/5 1500, to Flugabw. Kdo. Schwarzwald, four parts of 179, 178, 167 and 130 letters), with pen marks by the recipient |
| 0994, 0995 | The recipient's hand-written fair copy of 0993 ("Gkdos. 1.) Flakgruppe Friedrichshafen gibt an …") |
| 1024 | 17 May 1940: teleprinter message from Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald Ic (SRO 0165, "Am 16.5.40 wurde dem Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald …") |
| 1025, 1026, 1027 | Radio version of 1024 (hand-written cipher text, two parts of 205 and 194 letters; 1026 is the back of the sheet of 1025 with the last group) |

- No images are included in this repository; they can be fetched from the URL above.
- The material is entry 704 of the unsolved catalogue of the Cyphersolver ledger (https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver, https://cyphersolver.com/solve/704.html). The reading of the 1939 exercise message with its key card follows the ledger's research/nara_triage_g/dw1939.py.
