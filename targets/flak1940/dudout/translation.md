# English translation: radio message of Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald Ic, 4 June 1940 (T405 roll 9, frames 0863-0867)

Translation of `reading.md`, with the same marks: [?word] = probable word, ‖ = boundary between the five parts.
Flak unit designations are kept as written: II./2./942 = platoon II of the 2nd battery of Flak battalion 942.

Secret command matter. To protect army unloadings (detrainments), the following go into position on 3 June 40:
II./2./942 from Immendingen station to Sankt Georgen station, ready to fire since ‖ 19.45 hrs.
II. 2./851 from Bodersweier to Triberg, ready to fire since 20.30 hrs.
II./4./251 from Hattingen to ‖ Peterzell, ready to fire since 20.45 hrs.
II./10./351 from Ettlingen to Haslach, ready to fire since 20.45 hrs.
I./Light Reserve Flak Battery ‖ 453 from Horb to Marbach, ready to fire [?since] 21.00 hrs.
I./3./851 from Ichenheim to Hornberg station, ready to fire since 21.00 hrs. ‖
4./321 (Eis) from Hüfingen [?to] Villingen, ready to fire since 4 [?June] 40, 04.35 hrs.
Flugabwehrkommando, Ic, [?No.] F 225 [?/] 40, secret command matter.

Context: the Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald (Luftgau VII, Munich) commanded the anti-aircraft defence of the Black
Forest and the Upper Rhine. On 3-4 June 1940, during the campaign in the West, it moved Flak platoons and batteries to
railway stations and towns of the Black Forest (St. Georgen, Triberg, Peterzell, Haslach, Marbach, Hornberg,
Villingen) to cover the unloading of army troops. "(Eis)" is written so in the teleprinter copy as well; it is left
untranslated.
