# klogesoren1858 - English translation (summarised)

The book holds about a hundred charms and recipes, most in Kloge Søren's cipher under headings in clear Danish. A full
translation of 8,055 words would repeat the same frames many times (a sacred precedent, "so surely as ... so surely shall ...",
then the closing formula "In the name of God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Ghost. Amen", and directions for use), so
the translation is given as: (1) a summary of every charm in the order of the book; (2) full translations of a few typical
charms. Holes as in reading.md: [?word] doubtful, [?] or [...] unresolved. Curses (against thieves and others) are summarised
neutrally, without their formulas. "Begreb" (chapter) numbers are those written in cipher; "(7th)" = number inferred from the
"No" series under the same heading; "-" = no number read.

## 2. Some charms in full

**003 R, At Dölge Blod, No 1 (to stop blood).** "As surely as [?Venus, for Longinus] with his spear pierced [?Jesus] Christ in
the side, so surely shall this red blood flow no more. In the name of God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Ghost. Amen."
Three times.

**004 L-R, No 3.** "God sets his staff against the flood, he stills the water, but I still the blood. In the name of ... This blood
I forbid, so that [...]. And that as surely and as certainly as the Virgin Mary bore Jesus Christ under her breast for forty
weeks. In the name of ... Amen."

**008 L, Fifth Begreb, for colic.** "Eve was my mother, whom the serpent [?cruelly] bit, and [?] I [promise] to crush the serpent's
head. In the name of ... Amen." No 2: "When you will still colic, stroke the belly with your hand while you say the words three
named times."

**009 L, Seventh Begreb, for toothache, No 1, of the worm.** "O you worm that eat this person's flesh and drink this person's
blood: as surely as the Virgin Mary was a pure maid, so surely shall you die this night. In the name of ..." These words are
written on a note and hung [?] on the breast of the sufferer.

**021 L, No 2, against a spell on the butter.** Against a kind of witchcraft by which one cannot churn butter and the cream will
not [?come] either, because witch-folk have taken it by their art: buy at the apothecary's devil's dung (asafoetida) 8 skilling,
castoreum 1 ounce, musk 1 ounce, black cumin 8 skilling, cumin 4 skilling, long pepper 8 skilling, [?] 8 skilling, amber 4
skilling; put it in 9 pægl of brandy and give it three Thursday evenings in a row after sunset, so that each [?head of cattle] gets
half a pægl each of the three evenings, in silence.

**030, 21st Begreb, the gun.** "Gun, hold your shot straight, as pure as the Virgin Mary kept her maidenhood in her. In the name of
... Amen." To release it: "Gun, let go your shot straight, as Samson let go his strength [?]. In the name of ... Amen." To be proof
against shot: the words "Amal Asmal Artila"; "this shall be borne on the body against the gun".

**031 L, 22nd Begreb, to conjure a fire.** "I saw fire kindled, burning high in the air. The Virgin Mary threw her [?cloak] over the
world. I forbid it to burn in, until there is found a [?maid] [?] who bears a son who never was and never shall be. In the name of
... Amen." (Three times.)

**031 R, 23rd Begreb.** A curse meant to make a thief waste away "like dew before the sun" within the year: sawdust from beside
the chopping block is taken with three fingers of the right hand in the name of the Trinity, carried to a churchyard and buried in
the north corner of the last grave, on a Thursday evening after sunset, in silence.

**045 L-046 L, No 3, the three nails.** A ritual to make a thief bring back stolen goods: before sunrise, at a pear tree, three
unused nails (from a coffin or a horseshoe) are held towards the rising sun while a formula is spoken that drives each nail, in
words, into the thief, comparing his pains to those of Judas and Pilate, until he brings the goods back to their place.

**058 R, to put out a fire.** "When one writes these letters [SATOR AREPO OPERA ROTAS] on each side of a plate and throws it into
the fire, it goes out at once."

**098 (loose leaf).** "In the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost I forbid [?] [?] heifers to cast their calves."
