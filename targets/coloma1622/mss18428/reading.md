Por horas me parece que podemos aguardar* las capitulaciones de los tratados de {Grisones} ‹…›; *yo he comenzado a prevenir, porque cuanto más los adelantare se* ‹13› ‹41› *sabremos menester* mantenerlos, *si vinieren primero a mis manos que a las de V.S.*

Notes, 14 Nov 2026.
- **ō = t** (here "tratados"; the sign was transcribed Ð on 5 Nov and re-read ō on the image on 14 Nov): the o with a cross above is held = t by the clear copy images 39 right - 40 left ("trae", "contra", "ministros"). It is an **addition** to C04, not a correction: the Ð of coloma1622 is another sign (= n, "conveniencias", "rompimiento"). No count changes.
- The letter runs on across the page turn: the last cipher token of image 14 right (xo) and the first of image 15 left (D H cer) make "nombre" ("en nombre de"). Part B was the held-out passage H4 of coloma1622 (burned without measurement on 5 Nov 2026: its margin was show-through, not a clear) and then of M01.
- "Luinas" = Luynes (the constable died on 15 December 1621); "las francesas" = the French ladies of Queen Isabel in Madrid, sent back; "los españoles que están acá" = the Spanish household of Anne of Austria in Paris. The lady the King visits in part A is very probably Luynes's widow ("pues aún no había bien muerto"); "su marido" in part B = Louis XIII. The name ‹Jamuila› (j·a·m·ui·la) is not identified.
- Signs read by sense only, not held by any clear: c̣ir = ri (×4), xix = si (×2), f‡ = a (×2), fir = que (×2; fiz = que is held), I = o after 202 (×2), 20~ = Su Majestad (×2; 20ʳ = SU MAG in C04), g‡ = b, p‡ = f, D‡ = m, x‡ = t, fizt = qui, zan = ya (one place each). Unresolved codes: 24 (×2, restored once), 322, _1_, _13_, _41_, md, 87, 48~.
- "a lo hecho {a}" and "{al} [correspondencia]" do not construe cleanly; "sar" (= ca in the key) gives no sense in "que ‹…› es el Rey nuestro señor".
