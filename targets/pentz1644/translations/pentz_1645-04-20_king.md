# Pentz to Christian IV, 20 Apr 1645 - English
Most gracious King and Lord. The heavy assault made last March on the fortress, especially on the Reithoffel and the new castle, did [560] damage ... I have already reported, as was my duty, and meanwhile have not delayed {having} what serves the safekeeping [1000] of the fortress {repaired}; and since the Reithoffel cannot for the time be {brought} back to its former state, I am having an inner retrenchment made on that [1011] side, especially as troops are badly {lacking} here, {so} [to] secure it that in any event the place can very easily be held with a small garrison. [753] This work has been contracted for [two thousand] thalers. Repairing the bridge at the new castle with timber will cost 330 rixdollars in wages. The blockhouse dam is also badly ruined, but that matters less. The damage done at the rampart [...] the old [1400] {town gate} would also need repair. {I} am having the most necessary finished first ... [orders as to] where the money is to be found.
