# Clear texts of Haldimand to Clinton, 16 March 1782

Both were read on the images after the cipher had been decoded and the frozen-key test run (see `test/`).

## 1. Haldimand's letterbook copy

Library and Archives Canada, MG21 B.148 p. 21 (British Library Add MS 21808 fo. 17), nineteenth-century Canadian
Archives transcript, reel H-1649 frame 1096 (https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_h1649/1096).
Several words are underlined with dots in the transcript; not interpreted here.

> A few days ago, I wrote more fully – This will I hope go directly to you – I have not heard from you since your
> letter of the 2d Augt, nor received the least Intelligence of Moment from Any Quarter since that Time – Common
> Reports make me anxious to hear from You, & I intreat you will let me have that Pleasure, not trusting to a
> single messenger, but by different Routes – I request the same of Govr Robinson. –
> St Johns March 16th
> [paraph]
>
> fo. 17v [endorsed:] 1782 Copy. Cypher to Sir H. Clinton from St Johns March 16th

Against the cipher: word for word the same, except St Johns for Sorel, and "Robinson" for Robertson (the cipher
spells r-o-b-e-r-t-s-o-n). The key text gives "will" where both cipher copies have the impossible 14.21.

## 2. The American translation

Papers of the Continental Congress, item 51 vol. 1 p. 738 (NARA M247 roll 65, Fold3 file 407691), written on the
leaf after the vol. 1 cipher copy. Hand not identified.

> dupl. A few days ago I wrote more fully. This will I hope go directly to you. I have not heard from you since your
> Letter of 2d August, nor received any other Intelligence of Moment from any Quarter since that Time. Common
> Reports make me anxious to hear from you; and I entreat you would let me have that Pleasure, not trusting to a
> single Messenger, but by different Routes. I request the same of Governor Robertson.
> Sorel March sixteenth.
> (perhaps) A. F.

Against the cipher: right in all but four places. "any other" for "the least" (lines 12 and 13 of the page,
used only here; the vol. 1 copy has an x against 13.26 in "least"); "would" for "will"; "the" before 2d August
left out; the signature guessed as "A. F." for F. H. "Sorel", "Robertson" and "sixteenth" are right. The errors
are those of a decipherer working without the key, filling short gaps from sense; we take it that the letter was
broken by analysis, not with a captured key. Nothing written on the cipher pages shows the method beyond the x and +
marks beside a few numbers.
