# Haldimand to Clinton, 16 March 1782: reading

Two intercepted cipher copies in the Papers of the Continental Congress (National Archives, RG 360, M247 roll 65),
item 51 (intercepted letters): vol. 2 p. 227 ("Duplicate"; docketed on p. 228 "Letter in Cypher from J. R.") and
vol. 1 p. 737 ("dupl."). Read with the Army List title page (`../key/armylist_titlepage_1778.txt`, unchanged) and
collated word by word in `alignment.tsv`. Words in the clear in the cipher are given as written. Where the two
copies differ, the reading follows the copy that gives a word; both are listed in `COLLATION.md`. Punctuation is supplied.

## Reading

A few days ago I wrote more fully. This will I hope go directly to you. I have not heard from you since your letter
of the 2d August, nor received the least intelligence of moment from any quarter since that time. Common reports
make me anxious to hear from you, & I entreat you wi[l]l let me have that pleasure, not trusting to a single
messenger but by different routes. I request the same of Governor Robertson.

Sorel, March sixteenth.

F. H.

## Plain statement

Haldimand tells Clinton at New York that he has heard nothing since Clinton's letter of 2 August 1781 (received
21 September), that rumours make him anxious, and asks Clinton, and Governor James Robertson of New York, to
write by several routes. The fuller letter of "a few days ago" is Haldimand to Clinton, Quebec, 5-6 March 1782
(B.148 p. 16 in Brymner's calendar).
