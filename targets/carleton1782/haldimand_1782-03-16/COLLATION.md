# Haldimand to Clinton, 16 March 1782: notes on the two cipher copies

See `alignment.tsv` for every word.

- Signature 6.11 11.1 = F H (Frederick Haldimand). The docket "J. R." on vol. 2 p. 228 is a misattribution; the
  1781-82 American translator wrote "(perhaps) A. F." (second letter not certain).
- wi[l]l: both copies write 14.21, past the end of line 14 (17 letters); Haldimand's letterbook copy has "will".
- Where the copies differ (one slip each time; the other copy is right): directly (vol. 1 omits 13.3 i);
  since (vol. 1 18.3 d for 18.13 s, marked x on the page); reports (vol. 2 16.16 t for 16.10 e); Sorel
  (vol. 1 omits 1.4 e); sixteenth (vol. 2 has an ink blot over three numbers, vol. 1 gives .10 .9 .9); received
  (vol. 1 has a blot over the fourth number).
- Q is written with a circled dot (no Q on the page), as Haldimand's key sheet describes.
- Place: both cipher copies spell Sorel; Haldimand's letterbook copy and its endorsement say St Johns
  (Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu). Not resolved; the cipher is the text that was sent.
