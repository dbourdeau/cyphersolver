# Key text of the Haldimand-Clinton line-letter cipher [V on image]: title page of the annual Army List
# ("A List of the General and Field Officers ...", London, J. Millan). Lineation of the 1778 edition,
# Internet Archive listofgeneralfie00grea, leaf n12 (copy in sources/raw/deep_2026-11-12/haldimand_command/armylist/).
# Haldimand (B.147 p.402, fo.323): lines numbered 1-29, the year line cut off; letters counted left to right in each line;
# no Q on the page, so Q is written with one of three marks. '&' counts as a letter (example "&c" = 27-9,10 and 22-32,33).
# Lines 28-29 (LONDON / imprint) are inferred from "1 to 29" [I]; no attestation yet.

2	The Secretary at War.
3	A
4	LIST
5	OF THE
6	GENERAL and FIELD OFFICERS,
7	As they rank in the Army;
8	OF THE
9	OFFICERS in the several REGIMENTS
10	OF
11	HORSE, DRAGOONS, and FOOT,
12	ON THE
13	BRITISH and IRISH ESTABLISHMENTS.

15	An Alphabetical Index)
16	The Royal Regiment of Artillery, and Corps of Engineers,
17	the Irish Artillery and Engineers,
18	And the MARINES on FULL and HALF PAY.
19	WITH
20	The Dates of their Commissions, as they rank in each
21	CORPS and in the ARMY,
22	The Governors, Lieutenant Governors, &c. of his MAJESTY's
23	Garrisons at Home and Abroad, with their Allowances.
24	All the Officers on Half Pay: And
25	A SUCCESSION of COLONELS,

27	Clothing, &c.

29	Printed for J. Millan, opposite the Admiralty-Office, Whitehall.
