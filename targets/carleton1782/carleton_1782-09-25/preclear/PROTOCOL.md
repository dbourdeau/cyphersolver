## Witness and ordering

Main transcript: pp.123–125 (frames1205–1207), 200 editorial units. Each column continues down the following pages before the next column begins. The bottom notes and differently arranged duplicate pp.126–128 establish this. Six main columns, with column6 ending on p.124; five columns occupy p.125. All visible numeric groups in the main witness are inventoried. S denotes marked/underlined spelling sequence; W an unmarked pair; C already clear text/suffix. Ditto coordinates are expanded. Records crossing pages carry both locations. Physical baselines are not claimed by the editorial unit count.

The first W3-4 (predicted as uncovered) is likely a duplicate heading, because it does not appear at the start of p.126. The notation “Cypher Signal.” at p.125 is uncertain and excluded from word prediction scoring as archival notation. Column6 contains a coded place/date and a fully unciphered naval-news sentence/signature. These are preserved separately from numeric prediction.

Duplicate pp.126–128 were inspected as another cipher witness, not as a clear key. Most repeated sequences agree, with different column packing. Explicit observed differences: p.123 initial3-4 absent in p.126; p.123 S29 sequence reads escapedo (last9) versus p.126 apparent escaped d (last7); p.124 S9:19,23,19,24,27,29,28 appears against, while duplicate p.127 matches; initial p.124 23-sequence assembled and final Boston repeat in duplicate. The duplicate was not independently exhaustively transcribed; do not infer full collation.

## Transcription iteration before freeze

An initial numeric pass was followed by fixed-key decoding and image reinspection. The 24-6 spelling group was corrected from an accidental intercolumn allocation to 24-6,13,10,6: literal **erie**, mechanically produced after correcting the column allocation. Expedition's last two characters have first coordinate14, printed in the adjacent tight space: 14-11,14-10. Both corrections were made from enlarged source before clear consultation. Known context and the key may have influenced grouping; this is not a key-naive glyph pass.

Other conspicuous literal outputs remain: incuosions, susnuehannah, escapedo, reuuire, repairin. There is no Q on the title page and prior key documentation states separate Q marks existed. A q-like mark could explain susnuehannah/reuuire, but this pass conservatively preserves the apparent coordinate readings rather than treating the letters as proven. Such alternatives are isolated in PRE_CLEAR_PROPOSALS.md and excluded from strict mechanical totals.

The frozen artifacts must never be tuned after clear-copy inspection. Any later correction, new code association or improved reading belongs in the separate postclear directory, with an explicit before/after ledger.
