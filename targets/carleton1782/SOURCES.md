# Sources

All images are Library and Archives Canada (LAC) microfilm reel H-1649, on Canadiana (`oocihm.lac_reel_h1649`). The reel carries the nineteenth-century transcripts of the Haldimand Papers made for the Canadian Archives: MG21 B.146 = British Library Add MS 21806 (letters from Sir Guy Carleton, 1782-83), B.147 = Add MS 21807 and B.148 = Add MS 21808 (correspondence with the commanders-in-chief, 1777-83). The British Library originals were not seen. Images are not in the repository (Canadiana terms); the `sources.json` files give the IIIF addresses and the hashes of the copies we read.

## Key

- Haldimand's key sheet of late 1781, B.147 pp. 402-405 (BL Add MS 21807 fo. 323 ff.), describing the Army List line-letter cipher with worked examples.
- A List of the General and Field Officers, as they rank in the Army (London: J. Millan, 1778), title page; Internet Archive `listofgeneralfie00grea`.

## Printed calendars

- Douglas Brymner, Report on Canadian Archives 1887, Haldimand Collection calendar, B.148: English summaries of Clinton, 22 Feb 1782 (p. 8, "The cypher of this letter. 11") and Carleton, 25 Sep 1782 (p. 102), and the entry "Carleton to Haldimand. Letter in cypher" for the 6 Sep duplicate.
- Douglas Brymner, Report on Canadian Archives 1885, calendar of B.146: summary of Carleton, 6 Sep 1782.

Both read in the archive.org OCR texts; summaries only, no full text and no cipher figures.
