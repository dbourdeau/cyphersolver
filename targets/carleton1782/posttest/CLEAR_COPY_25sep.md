# Existing clear witness, first consulted after freeze

B.148 p.102, H-1649 frame1183; subscription and docket on p.103, frame1184. Nineteenth-century Canadian transcript of BL AddMS21808. This is an existing historical translation/decipherment, not newly recovered plaintext. Light punctuation/spacing normalization; source wording retained. The p.102 margin calls the cipher a transcription on pp.123 and126 and points to another copy in B.146 p.54.

Congress and the Pensylvania assembly determined to make two incursions into the Indian Country the principle one under Major Genl Potter will consist of four hundred Continental Troops and six hundred Militia and Volunteers are to assemble at fort Munsey near the West branch of Susquehannah the eighth of Oct and to march from there to the open of [Piers/Pieres?; second witness Pines] Creek head and into the Seneca Country the other under the Command of General Irwin will consist of one thousand Men a very few of Whom are Continental will assemble at fort Pit early in October and march towards lake Erie. The principal Object is supposed to be against the Seneca Country and the Information given by several Prisoners who have escaped from thence have contributed much to forward the present Expedition — As there is a large force in Nova Scotia I have given Orders to Major General Paterson who Commands there to furnish you with any Assistance You may require —

The French and Continentals under Genl Washington are assembled at Verplank's Point —

Our Fleet here and the French mostly at Boston repairing.

[Dateline, editorial separation:] New York — Septr25th.

The L'Aigle of44 guns Capt Latouche and a valuable Ship of20 guns with Bale Goods from France were lately taken in the Delaware — (Signed)

[p.103] (Signed) Guy Carleton / Rec'd26th Octr / Copy.

[Docket] From Sir Guy Carleton / 25thSeptr / Rec'd26thOctr / Express / Entd.

## Important alignment cautions

- Source punctuation around “Boston repairing / New York — Septr25th” is thin; New York and date function as dateline, not the object of repairing.
- Clear copy omits the extra cipher phrase “to Pines Creek” visible in the two cipher arrangements. Do not remove those frozen units to improve agreement.
- The creek name is not paleographically settled in the first witness; B.146 p.54 (frame616), independently inspected after freeze, clearly supports **Pines Creek head**. Frozen letter output **pines** remains separate evidence.
- Clear subscription, docket and unciphered naval news are not numerical prediction successes.

## Second clear witness inspected

B.146 p.54, H-1649 frame616, is headed New York Septr25th / Recd by Express26thOctr, No.10. It confirms the body through the start of the naval-news sentence, including Pines Creek head, one thousand Men, General Irwin, and the place-name Erie. Its continuation was not acquired: the complete naval news and signature are available in B.148 pp.102–103 and the cipher witness. This is not a claim to have collated every allographic difference between both clear copies.
