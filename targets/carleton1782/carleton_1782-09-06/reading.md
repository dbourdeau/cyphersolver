## Reading

New York, 6th Sept.

Sir, The duplicate of your letters dated 29th July and 11th August I received the 4th. I cannot do other ways than approve of your whole conduct and management with the Vermonters. I lament the cruelties which have been committed by the Indians whose excursions in this kind I was always well assured had not received your countenance or concurrence. It is not possible for me to go into your Province this fall, and wholely unlikely that I shall do so at any time. I did {literal: dit} not quit that government with a purpose upon any event of returning thither. Part of Admiral Pigot's fleet are arrived and the rest upon the coast.

GC
