## Finding B.45

B.45 is BL Add MS 21705, "Letters from the English Ministers to General Haldimand, 1781-1784" (label on the film). It is
on reel **H-1434** (which begins with B.39), near the end of the reel: B.45 p. 37 is frame 950, p. 57 frame 972, p. 61
frame 976. (Reels checked on the way: H-1429 begins with B.5, H-1435 with B.47, H-1436 with B.54, H-1437 with B.58,

## What this settles

- The creek is **Pine Creek** (the West Branch tributary): the cipher's *pines* is "Pine's"; Q 20 writes "Pine Creek's Head".
- All three clear copies of 25 Sep read **there**, **thousand** and **have contributed**; none has the cipher's
  repetition. The three failed predictions stand.
- For 6 Sep a clear text written by the sender's office exists beside the decodes. It is the best witness of the plain
  wording; it differs from the decodes at *upon*/*in* (code 29-30).
