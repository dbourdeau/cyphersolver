# vibejuel1651 - reading of the cipher passages in the new alphabet (Danish, spelling of the letters)

Letters of Peder Vibe to Peder Juel, 1651 to 6 May 1655, in the new alphabet ("ny Alphabet"; Arkivalieronline unit 261583, images
77-93). Read by sense from `decryption.txt` with `key.tsv` (Brice Dudout's agents, PR #410), after the measurements
(`validation.txt`). The re-reading of the held-out sets was done AFTER the measurements; the measurements were not run again. The
letters of 1649-1651 in the old cipher are in `targets/vibejuel1649/reading.md`.

Marks: CAPITALS = cipher words, certain (every number read, the word is Danish of the time); {X} = probable (one number corrected
with a reason taken from the image, or an old spelling; see the list); [x] = restored (word supplied from the sense, not counted);
<...> = unresolved; #211 = nomenclator code, not identified; lower case = clear text of the letter, abridged with "...". Word
division and punctuation are the contributor's. Spelling of the time kept (u for v, w for v, ff, hafuer).

## What the letters say about the cipher, in clear

- Image 62 (11 Jan 1649, an old-cipher letter): the new alphabet ("dett ny Alphabet") will be sent.
- Image 80 (end of N. 334, 6 Jan 1653): two numbers are to be added to the common nomenclator ("sub N° 349" and "N° 561"); Juel
  writes his numbers in letters ("dine bogstaffuer ... denotere tallet") and is asked to write t and k more clearly.
- Image 93 (P.S., 1655): the last cipher sent by Erich Krag had no code for the States General nor for the Øresund.

## Why the holes

The cipher words in letters read to 98.0%. What does not read are the 73 nomenclator codes (49 different numbers): no list of
them is in the unit, the interlinear gloss of N. 319 covers letters only, and only two codes are defined in clear (349 and 561,
on image 80, names read as probable only). Some invite a guess from the sense (770 Sweden? 222 Queen Christina?), but a guess is
not a value, so they stay holes.
