# vibejuel1651 - raw decryption of transcription.txt, letter by letter (Vibe to Juel 1649-1655; Krag 1648 not solved)
# Deterministic decode by scripts/decode.py with the fixed tables of key.tsv (no language model, no correction):
#   python3 scripts/decode.py transcription.txt
# CAPITALS = decoded cipher words; {n} = nomenclator code (not identified); [ ... ] = clear text of the letter; ?n = number out of the
# alphabet; <n.n.n> = cipher word not decoded (Krag letters: other system, no table). Misread numbers are left as they come out
# (e.g. RACQUET for pacquet, BLRT for bort on image 78); the reading by sense, with every correction justified, is reading.md.
