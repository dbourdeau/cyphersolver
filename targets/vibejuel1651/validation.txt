0. System, found on image 78 (development set, before any test): each cipher word = opener + body; body number = rank of the
   letter + k; the opener gives k; three-digit numbers between the words are a nomenclator. The gloss of the time on image 78
   agrees with the mechanical decode wherever it is legible (desbau-scheret, dreng, brefue, kunde, forscha[ffe], charles ...).

1. First test (PROTOCOLE.md, hashed 11 Nov 2026 14:44) - FAILED, reported.
   - Hypothesis frozen: ONE opener table for the whole unit, the one of N. 319 (24-29 -> 0, 30-34 -> 1, 35-39 -> 2, 1-3 -> 3), and
     a 23-letter alphabet without w.
   - Held out: images 74 (N. 330), 77 (N. 316) and 79 right (N. 334), never opened before; transcribed number by number before any
     decryption and hashed (RESERVE.sha256, 14:52): 54 words, 335 numbers.
   - Criterion C1: M1 >= best of 600 controls + 0.30 (A opener tables shuffled, B random k per word, C body alphabet permuted).
   - Result (MESURE.out): M1 -3.068; best control -3.281 (A): margin 0.21 < 0.30, C1 FAILED (the lexical C2 failed too).
   - Cause, found after the measurement and outside it: N. 316 and N. 334 read with the frozen table ("til at faa ... signet ret
     udstucket", "deris instruction"), but N. 330 does not. N. 330 (20 Sep 1651) is in the OLD cipher, which Vibe names himself on
     image 62 ("woris forige gamble Ciffres"): a cyclic 23-letter alphabet with k = -(opener mod 3). The hypothesis "one table for
     the whole unit" was wrong, not the shift-per-word system. Set 1 was not measured again.
