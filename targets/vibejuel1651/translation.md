# vibejuel1651 - English translation of the cipher passages in the new alphabet (Vibe to Juel, 1651-1655)

Translation of `reading.md`, letter by letter (the contributor's, PR #410). Clear and cipher words are translated together.
Marks: #211 = nomenclator code, not identified (left as a number); [x] = restored word; {x} = probable word; <...> = unresolved
word; ( ) = gloss or doubt. Guesses at the codes are given only as such, in the notes; none is part of the reading.

**Image 77, N. 316.** To have the seal (signet) of #876 rightly engraved, #355 must press the ordinary seal of #876 in wax two or
three times, clearly; then another can be made exactly like the first, so that all stays unnoticed.

**Images 78-79, N. 319.** Some time ago #545 debauched the boy of #818, who took a big {packet} of letters; #818 wrested it from
him and carried it {away} {secretly}, although there was nothing in it that could be {prejudicial} to him. Perhaps there were some
of #873's and #479's letters. The impudent #485 took his leave with the valet of #254, to do his best to debauch my former
{servant} Charles, to see if he could procure some letters of #872. Quo poterant ibant, quo non poterant ibi stabant. Work is now
going on to intercept some letters of #872 of {importance}, for which the wedding, or the Diet (Herredag) <...>, will give an
occasion ... so that one may find some cause to take his estates in #73.

**Image 79 right, N. 334, 6 Jan 1653.** #353 leaves with the first occasion for #770; #397 likewise with #353. I will say nothing
here of their {instruction}, since it will be communicated to you; it can be dealt with at a border meeting (grentze møde) ...
negotiated here, so everything is referred there.

**Image 81, N. 332.** #876 and the young lady (frøken) Hedwig, whether they too were among the {courtiers} who have done ...

**Image 83 left.** Würtz #623, creature and confidant of #376 #357, will persuade #454 that #770 has resolved #463 against #217. It
is said for certain that he has lately been ennobled by #222 in #770 and has received estates from [her]; whereupon one does not
know how far one may trust him here.

**Image 83 right, N. 331.** ... which informs me of Würtz's (#620) credit and esteem in Sweden. Meanwhile I only let you know that
#872 (sends) a {procurator}, #95, with a recommendation to #583.

**Image 84, N. 333.** Since #222 has so many fireships (brandt) #791 built, it would be needful to learn by what name or sign each
of these fireships can be told from the other ships; for there is no doubt they will be fitted out like the other ships.

**Image 87, N. 335.** I will not fail to let you know that {letters} by the new post have come to her from #872 ... what is found
... your and #872's relation ... #872 writes that #601 #397 shall have ... copies of ... letters of #872 ... that #397 ... that no
act was given in writing.

**Image 90, N. 336, 13 Sep 1654.** Let me know what is decided {with} him according to the order that #397 has received about it;
and also what the letter of #879 that was opened contained.

**Image 91, N. 143 / 321, 1654.** I beg and urge you, by our trusted friendship, to let me know who it is that #879 trusts most:
is he the worst, and the best {friend} of #872? I swear (in French) on the salvation of my soul to keep it secret, and I wish to
know it only to manage my friendships hereafter.

**Image 93, N. 167 / 337, 6 May 1655.** ... we are told for certain that #1590 has a design on #1274 <...> #1195 #671, which makes
the States General somewhat perplexed for #430 #889 #428 #487 #770, and they begin to #467 how this #1588 #978 is to be prevented.
#797 #467, if one #798 {must} {pass} them, to #428 #126 #1135 #1590 #1771 by water and lie #448 #600 in the Sound.

Notes
- Codes that the sense invites guesses for (not part of the reading): 770 Sweden (it stands where Sweden is meant, next to
  "Swerrig" in clear on image 83)? 222 Queen Christina ("hende", her)? These are the contributor's guesses; no key supports them.
- "Herredag" is the Danish diet/court of the realm; "brandt" = brander, fireship.
- The letters of 1649-1651 in the old cipher are translated in `targets/vibejuel1649/translation.md`.
