# Lit. L: Erlund's period decipherment checked with his own key (Lit. F)

Erlund deciphered the Stenbock-Malmberg letters in 1714. His German decipherment is written between the lines of his
copies: Riksarkivet SE/RA/760004/42 (Geheime Nachrichten, 1732), Lit. L, pp. 377 ff., images R0000422_00144-149. The key
(signs above, letters below) is Lit. F, p. 160, image R0000422_00086, transcribed in `key_litF.json`. The escape contract
with the master Christian Rötcke (5 July 1714) is Lit. Q, pp. 365-367, images 138-139, with Erlund's period "Explicationen".

**Check (27 Oct 2026, protocol written before decoding).** The key was applied to two cipher lines of the letter of
21 July 1714 to Malmberg at Lübeck (p. 377, image 144 right, lines L5 and L8): 62 of 63 clear signs agree with Erlund's
decipherment (0.984), 67 of 77 counting all signs (0.870); control (shifted by one word) 0/40. Reading:
"Schiffer nichts entrepreniren wann es nicht [...] Certepartie muss der Schiffer für allen Dingen [nicht an Bord haben]"
("the master must undertake nothing unless [he can succeed] ... above all the master must not keep the charter party on
board").

This is a **verification** of a period decipherment on a small sample (two lines), not a new reading. The transcription
was not blind (Erlund's text was visible under the cipher). The rest of Lit. L was not re-checked sign by sign.
