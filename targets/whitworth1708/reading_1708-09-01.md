# whitworth1708: the duplicate of 1/12 September 1708 (SP 91/5, stamped f. 49), its coded stretches with the rebuilt key

State: **decoded, not read.** With the key alone 47 of the 77 coded words come out whole (0.61); 13 of the 15 coded
stretches have at least one hole. The despatch itself was deciphered by the Secretary of State's office in 1708 and
printed in 1886 (Sbornik of the Imperial Russian Historical Society, vol. 50, No. 22), so nothing here is a first
reading: the passage is given to show what the key does on a letter it was not built from.

Conventions. Clear text is as written in the duplicate (from `transcription_1708-09-01.txt`; lower case there, capitals
and stops added here). Words in code stand between ⟨ and ⟩. A coded word the key gives whole is written as the key
spells it (warnd). A coded word the key does not give whole is a hole and stands in square brackets in lower case,
filled from the 1886 print: `[money]` is the print's word, not ours and not the key's. `[?a]` is a value the key gives
that the print does not have. The unedited output of the key, group by group, is in `decryption_1708-09-01.txt`.

## Notes on the count

- **The count is an estimate.** The code groups do not map one to one to words, and the word list is the 1886 print's,
  with two adjustments from the decode: S04 has an eleventh word, the key's "a" before "battle", which the print lacks
  and which is counted as a hole; S05 is counted in the decode's order.
- A word is counted as whole only when every one of its groups is in the key and the values spell it with nothing
  supplied: "engag" (print "engage"), "other-[117]" (print "others") and "[59]-all" (print "fall") are holes.
  "warnd" is counted whole: the code spells it war-n-d, and the print's "warned" is the editors' spelling.
- S03: 1114 is one group where the print has "king of Sweden"; counted as three unread words. In S06 the print's
  "that king" ends with a different number, 679.
- S05: the code has "... and persons", so the manuscript's order is probably "effects and persons" where the print has
  "persons and effects". The clear word "allways" between the two halves is not in the print.
- S11: the key gives "court" for 505 (the office writes "court" above 505 on the original of 22 Sept / 3 Oct), which
  does not fit "seeming"; the word is counted as a hole and the value is marked doubtful in `key.tsv`.
- S12: the key's "post" for 236 is wrong; 236 is almost certainly "st" (under-st-and), which would leave no group for
  the print's "well". Both words are counted as holes.
- The clear text here is the duplicate's and differs from the print in small ways: "if he still threatens" (print
  "as he"), "awake" (print "awaken"), "notions of the shame" (print "notions of shame"), "mony", "allways", "concernd".
- The 34 groups not in the key (33 different numbers): 49, 54, 56, 59, 79, 81, 116, 117, 123, 153, 177, 200, 211, 226, 235, 239 (twice), 297, 323, 354, 399, 461, 495, 531, 563, 630, 679, 750, 935, 990, 1114, 1194, 1235, 1237.
