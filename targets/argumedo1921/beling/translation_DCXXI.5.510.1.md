   **Oaxaca** to begin his work; he authorised me to tell you so, [...] I learnt of his vast plan, which enables him to
   take **Oaxaca** with relative ease, and he goes very confident that, when he does so, you would send him with all

   urgent notice from that gentleman, according to my arrangement with him, my communication with you has to be very
   rapid."
9. f. 2, signature: "With my best wishes for your personal well-being and complete success in everything, I remain,
