# English summary of what the runs say, letter by letter

Words in bold are the ones hidden in the runs; the rest is the clear text around them, summarised. Every run with its Spanish words and marks is in `reading_marked.tsv` and in its sentence in `reading_<letter>.md` (`round2/` for 25 Mar 1922).

- 9 Sep 1921: money raised to rescue Gral. Vicente Benítez; through his delegate Fco. Mata, Argumedo met Rafael
  Delgado, Hermenegildo Heredia and Aurelio C. García; García, who signed in the US the memorial Lic. Del Villar
  presented asking for **belligerency**, offers the means of the cadets of the **Colegio Militar**, where he teaches
  **Ordenanza**.
- 26 Dec 1921: Antonio Escobar has left for the US; Francisco C. González, with leading **railwaymen**, has the means to
  build **wireless sets** for "our future campaign"; railway **strikes, stoppages, divisions and upheavals**; Dr. Arturo
  de la Fuente ("el Médico de la casa") has men ready in the **State of Mexico**; Aristeo Pérez helped in the case of
  Gral. Epigmenio Jiménez; a meeting of Lic. Pimentel, Dr. Castillo, Pablo Ariza, Arturo Enríquez de Rivera and don
  Pancho C. González; Pimentel to go to **Oaxaca**; new **claves** sent.
- 26 Jan 1922: Fidencio López in **Puebla**; one Charles Bounellie carries a letter of authority from Díaz to go to
  **Michoacán** and receive **war material**; $135 raised with Francisco Mata; Antonio Cordero (for the **Junta
  Directiva**) and Feliciano Guerra; a letter from **San Antonio, Texas** delivered by Ernesto Argaiz on his way to
  **Laredo**, for **Gral. Sánchez Díaz** at the Hacienda de Coapa; Díaz then in **Los Angeles**, not New Orleans; Ing.
  Carlos M. Peralta, put forward in 1913 for the felicista ticket (president and vice-president), offers Argumedo a post
  in the **ministry**; trips to **Guadalajara** and **Toluca**.
- 5 Feb 1922: **Esteban Cantú**'s manifesto is circulating; Lic. P. confirms that **Enrique Estrada, ex-minister of
  war**, with **M. Caraveo**, is preparing a defection, one of them to take the **provisional presidency**, Díaz to be the
  **legitimate mandatary** afterwards; the **government** is divided.
- 22 Feb 1922: don Pancho has arrived; the press confirms the probable occupation of **Teziutlán**.
- 25 Mar 1922 (round 2): he waited to write until he had finished with the **orders** they had the honour to carry;
  the **documents** were handed out; the first step of don **Pablo Ariza** was a meeting; in **Puebla** he brought
  **Isauro and Fidencio López** to agree before **don Pablo**; **Guadalajara** went badly for want of money, after the
  events that brought down **Governor Badillo and the police inspector, Colonel Francisco Pérez**; if **don Pablo** has
  not told Díaz, the press will; a journey to **Toluca, Zitácuaro** is next; **Dr. de la Fuente** met **don Pablo** and
  came to formal agreements; **don Pancho** left last night to see Díaz; when the **fight** starts he may have to
  emigrate with Lic. P. or the doctor; he used Díaz's name before **don Pablo** to raise money to put his wife and
  children **safe**, "since we know they will **pursue** us **mercilessly**"; signed **C. M. Argumedo**. In the margin,
  in ink: "I beg you to say something to **Escobar**, who is working among us to break us up. It is urgent; forgive me."
- 10 Apr 1922: **documents** and **orders** delivered; **war** preparations and a hoped-for **terrible conflagration**;
  the **junta** pestered for money; one man asked for $20,000 to raise **fifty men, armed and supplied**, saying "my life
  I sell, but dearly"; **lack of money**; Lic. Pimentel, the **organiser** in **Oaxaca**; **Gral. Ismael Hernández**;
  **Mario Ferrer**'s premature **manifesto**; De la Hoz, Escobar and Mata (the run DE LA HOZ ESCOBAR is probably two persons, as in 26 Dec 1921) bypass Pimentel.
- 9 Jun 1922: trips to **Toluca** and **Mixquiahuala, Hidalgo**, to arrange with **Dr. de la Fuente** the handing over
  of 60 **armed men** and $500 to **Gral. Agustín Ramírez**, named by don **Pablo Ariza**; **De la Huerta**'s journey to
  **New York**; Lic. R. Pimentel has left for **Oaxaca**; don Pancho González failed to help don Rafael P. in the
  **south**.
