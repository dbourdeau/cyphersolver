## Conventions

- One TSV row per written line, in the order written, top to bottom. Columns: `image	slip	line	text	doubt_note`.
- Each image holds one slip, so every slip is `a`.
- The writer's spelling, abbreviations, capitals and accents are kept as seen. Where no accent is visible, none is written; the doubt_note says so where a missing accent might matter.
- `[?word]`: uncertain reading. `[a|b]`: two candidate readings. `[illegible, ~N letters]`: not read. `[struck: ...]`: struck-out word. `[stamp: ...]`: rubber stamp. `[rule]`: drawn horizontal line. `[mark: ...]`: a non-letter mark.
- `✓` is a tick mark written after a line (images 9 and 10).
- Several words carry a horizontal bar over "te"/"ta" (Vicente, Mata, Estado, Presidente, Ministerio, ente, Aristeo). These look like the writer's long t-crossing. They are not written into the text but are noted in doubt_note.
- The CEHM / Fundación Carlos Slim watermark in the top-right corner of every image is a digitisation overlay. It is not transcribed.
