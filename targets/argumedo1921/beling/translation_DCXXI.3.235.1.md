1. f. 1, address: "Mr **Gen. Díaz**."
2. f. 1: "For my part, wishing to carry out your charge to go to the aid of our good friend **Gen. Vicente Benítez**,
   I have the pleasure of informing you that your wish has been met."
3. f. 1: "For, through the care and zeal of my good delegate Mr **Fco. Mata**, I secured a most interesting interview
   between Messrs **Rafael Delgado Hermenegil{30/d}o Heredia** and **Aurelio C. García**."
4. f. 1: "The first handed me $30.00, the second $50.00, and the third, who states that he signed in the U.S. the
   memorial that Lic. **del Villar** presented on your behalf asking for **belligerency**, has solemnly offered me to
   place at your disposal, at the right moment, all the means that the cadets of the **Military College** now have,
   in which he is professor of **Regulations** (Ordenanza); for to that end he has directed all his work for a long
   time, [...]"
5. f. 1: "The first two persons I mention took a keen interest in the fate of **Benítez**, and from them, as you see,
   I obtained $80.00."
6. f. 1: "[...] over the sums mentioned something more was obtained, and we have now collected and handed to the House
   Doctor the sum needed to put **Benítez** in safety, and in my next I will tell you what I discussed in the interview
   I shall have with him very soon in the city of Zitácuaro."
7. f. 1: "Likewise I am pleased to inform you that, continuing my enthusiastic work, I brought about a new and very
   profitable rapprochement between Messrs Lic. P. and our good friend don **Pancho**; in which [...] of the help that
   in practice the second of these gentlemen may give to the first, [...]"
8. f. 1, signature: "Your most affectionate and very attentive servant, **C. M. Argumedo**."
