## Notes

- Sentence 3, R08: the clear text already has "los Sres" before the run, and the run itself begins with SRES
  (16-15-01-16). The title is therefore written twice, once in clear and once in cipher. This does not make me doubt
  the counted word; it is how the page reads.
- Sentence 3: R08 names two people (Escobar; Lic. de la Hoz) in one run. In DCXXI.5.444.2 (run 31) the cipher has
  DE LA HOZ ESCOBAR; this letter supports reading that as two people, not one name.
- Sentence 4: R12, R13 and R14 are typed one after another with only the full stops that close each cipher word
  between them; the commas are mine. The clear "y" stands between R14 and R15.
- Sentence 6: "elemntos" is the typed spelling. A later ink hand wrote "Dr" and "de la fuen[te]" above R19 (not used).
- Sentence 7: the clear "(No López, como erróneamente lo llamé ...)" confirms R22 against R21 (Pérez, not López).
- Sentence 9 begins f. 2 in lower case; f. 1 ends with R26 and a full stop.
- The typeface makes "o" and "e" hard to tell apart in places; clear words are given in their normal spelling where
  the type is ambiguous ("desarroyo" or "desarrollo" in sentence 3 is cut with "[...]" for that reason). None of
  these places touches a run.
