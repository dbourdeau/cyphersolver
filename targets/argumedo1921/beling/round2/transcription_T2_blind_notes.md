## Conventions
- `run_text`: digits and visible punctuation as on the page; `|` = line break inside the run; a space = a real gap.
- `[a|b]` = unsure digit, alternatives in order of my preference; `?` = unread digit (none used).
- `-.` at the end of a run = a hyphen and a period struck in the same character space (looks like a hyphen above a dot). It is one character cell, not an extra hyphen.
- Leading or trailing hyphens are kept where they touch the run on the page.
- `before`: up to 5 clear words just before (as typed, accents as on page where clear; "via-je" etc. keep the line-end hyphen); `after`: up to 3 words.
- Runs are numbered per letter in reading order.
