## Notes

- Sentence 6: "le indiqué en esa" ("I indicated to you there") means in Díaz's city, before the journey.
- Sentence 7: "por virtud de lo que en otros lugares entrará en actividad" is unclear in the Spanish; the English is
  literal ("by virtue of what will go into action in other places").
- Sentence 11: "con saña" is rendered "with savagery", so that the {z/s} mark can sit on the English initial s;
  "perseguirá" has no English letter for the {i?/g} mark, which therefore stands at the end of "pursued".
- Sentence 12: the Spanish puts the closing ("su atento y S. S.") after a full stop following "Willy"; the English
  joins them with a comma.
