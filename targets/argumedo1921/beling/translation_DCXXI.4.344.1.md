1. f. 1, address: "Mr **Gen. Félix Díaz**."
2. f. 1: "I can tell you nothing about the friend you ask about, who, you know, went to **Jalapa** and is now in

3. f. 1: "For four nights running, the 16th to the 19th of this month, and at the special invitation of Mr
   **Charle{u/s} Bounellie**, I attended meetings prepared by him, at which he let me know that he carries a letter of

   to receive material **of war** that will be consigned to him."
4. f. 1: "Several persons were present (all friends); a broad plan **of campaign** was made known to us; we were told
   of money for urgent expenses, [...] I offered my mite, which, with the help of my good friend and delegate Mr
   **Francisco Mata**, and by troubling our friends, we managed to bring up to the sum of $135.00, which we handed in
   cash to the said Mr **Bounellie**."
5. f. 1: "Before what is set out above, Mr **Antonio Cordero**, with the authority of the **Directing Board**,
   introduced to me Mr **Feliciano Guerra**, so that, as a matter of urgency, I should give him every kind of facility
   on the **railway**, so that he might leave the Capital without danger and also be safe on the road towards Laredo,
   since the said Mr **Guerra** was going there to talk with you,"
6. f. 1: "After I had had my assurances about this gentleman, I set about serving him, and between my fine friend and
   fellow partisan Mr **Edo. Fdez. de Castro** and myself, we handed **Guerra** in cash the price of a ticket from this
   City to that of Nuevo Laredo"
7. f. 1: "When I had almost forgotten this matter, there suddenly reached me by post, in a sealed envelope, at my exact
   address and under my name and without any precaution, a letter from **San Antoni{-/o} Texa{u/s}** containing a
   letter of 14 January this year and an attached long writing of January 12, without signature or address."
8. f. 1: "On reading these documents I was left perplexed, [...] and I did not come out of it until the 20th, when the
   aforesaid Mr **Guerra** came back to me and assured me that the said packet was handed to him in **San Antonio** by
   Mr **Ernest{r/o} Argaiz**, who was bringing it from your hands towards **Laredo**, to be given to a safe channel
   that would get it into the hands of **Gen. Sánchez Díaz**, who lives on the [struck word] Hacienda de **Coapa** in

   **New Orleans**, since you were at that time in **L{o?/o}s Angeles**."
9. f. 2: "[...] there is no agreement between what you tell us and the violence with which the said writing, "and as

10. f. 2: "I will be most glad if you tell me soon what there is on the matter, as well as what concerns don

11. f. 2: "Mr Ing. **Carlos M. Peralta**, whom I brought out as the winner when in 1913 we were campaigning in the

12. f. 2: "Our good friend don **Pancho** showed me the letter you sent him a short while ago."
