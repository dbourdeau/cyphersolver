## Notes

- Identifications are by the names as written. "Don Mateo" is Cristóbal Mateo Argumedo (Narváez 2015). Agua Prieta:
  the Sonoran group of Obregón and Calles, after the Plan of Agua Prieta (April 1920); "la horda reinante" is
  Obregón's government. Panindícuaro is a municipality of Michoacán. "Don Miguel" and "Lic. Mena" are not
  identified further here.
