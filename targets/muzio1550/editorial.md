## reading_c5.md

Status: read in part. The letter is in clear with two cipher passages (c. 5r nine lines, c. 5v three lines). The key of c. 3r rests on this letter: an early hand wrote letters over one cipher line of c. 5r and a decipherment of the c. 5v passage in the margin.

Marks. `⟦ ... ⟧` = text deciphered from cipher (transcription_c5.txt, decryption_c5.txt); everything else is the clear text of the letter. `[?word]` = value proposed from context only. `[Cardinale]`, `[V. Ecc.]` = code group whose value the c. 5v margin gives. `[?]` = unread sign. Letters in brackets inside a word are supplied. `(?)` = doubtful word. The clear text was read once at quarter scale and is not collated; the cipher passages were read once at 60-70% scale by a reader who knew the key. Spelling is the writer's; u/v, capitals, accents and punctuation are ours.

### The early hand

- c. 5r, over cipher line C8: the letters t r a d ij o a over the signs P + m g I E m ("...ntradi·a..."). They agree with the key on six signs and put an o over the stop sign E. The same hand, or another, drew the word dividers.
- c. 5v, left margin, beside D1-D3: "scriver al Car.le n[ost]ro, ma poi no[n] havendo risposta di q[ue]llo ch[e] V. Ex. gli ha ma[ndato]". The cipher has "scrivere al CE nostro ma poi non havendo risposta di quello che 25 ha mandato". This fixes CE = Cardinale and 25 = V. Ecc.; the margin adds "gli", which the cipher does not have. The abbreviation after "V." is read "Ex."; the end of the note runs into the fold.
- The hand is not Muzio's. Whether it is of the time is not established.

### Other pages

The letter turns the page at "et mille / altri rispetti". c. 6r is blank. c. 6v has the address to Ferrante Gonzaga and the docket "Mutio XII Genaro".

### Notes

- `Siena (?)`: the sign after "sie" is doubtful (n, or a struck sign before the stop).
- `e ha (?)`: the signs give "e ha per farsi"; the verb before it is plural ("havevano"). Left as written.
- `dallo [?]`: one sign (ring with a crossed tail), found nowhere else; by position a code for a person.
- `contradi[?o] [?g]agliardo`: the signs give "contradi", the stop sign, "a", a null, "gliardo". The early hand writes o over the stop sign. Our proposal is "contradio, gagliardo officio".
- `raff[r]edato`: the signs give "raffedato".
