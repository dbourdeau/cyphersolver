**Our reading of the evidence:** Wimarson paraphrases; he prints no Bonde despatch, and his notes give no sign of the cipher. The
volume's chancery glosses stop about Nov 1677 (b118 survey), so the late-1677 despatches were, as far as the volume shows, never
deciphered at the time. That is an inference from the volume, not a finding about other copies.
