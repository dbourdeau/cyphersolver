# Carl Bonde (Paris) to Charles XI, 24 Nov/4 Dec 1677, R0003797 image 489R: reading

Riksarkivet, Diplomatica Gallica, SE/RA/2105/I/A/24/54 (R0003797), despatch on images 488R-490L, dated "Paris d. 24 Novemb./4 Decemb. 1677" on 490L and signed by Bonde; the only cipher is on 489R (17 short runs, 169 numbers). Swedish.

Conventions: text between asterisks is from cipher, the rest was written in clear. Only the clear words next to the cipher runs were transcribed (b119); [...] = clear lines not transcribed (the line numbers of the transcription are given in the margin note at the end of each paragraph). [418], [435] = numbers with no value in key v9. A letter in brackets inside a word = supplied: the unit is counted not read. Word division, capitals and punctuation are editorial; spelling is the writer's (dröya = dröja, diuersion = diversion, fahra = fara).

## Reading

[... lines 1-9 not transcribed] här wid Hofwet myckit *i[l]la tilfredt* [... line 11] kommandes till Lijfland och förnimmandes *Paezes opposition* skulle willia *dröya och instäl[l]a [418]en* till des man saken *med Pae[z] afgiordt* hade, och sådes [... lines 16-17] uthi en *forme af een fåfäng negotiation*. Jag kan intet mehr häröm [... lines 20-22] ordsakerne, som thetta Hoff uthi *sådant alarme* satt hafwer. (lines 10-24)

Thet samma att betaga, hafwer Jag [... lines 25-27] intet högre åstundar, än att the *desseinerne med en prompt diuersion uti [435]* med thet fordeligaste *executerade*, och att Edert Kongl. Maij:ts FeldtMarskalk i des regard lärer *[418]en maturera* som thet någonsin skee kan, på thet han [... lines 34-35] rådzplägningen, måtte sådana fattade *mistanckar fahra* låta, och sinom [... rest of the page not transcribed]. (lines 24-37)

Notes:
- "alarme": the first transcription reads 61 (l); the blind second transcription reads 67 (x), "axarme". The only difference between the two in 169 numbers. Counted read on the first transcription, not read on the blind one.
- "<418>en" twice: a word code with the ending -en. The later key v10 (not used here) gives 418 = march from a gloss: "marchen" would fit both places. Not read.
- "uti <435>": a word code, the place of the diversion. The later key v10 gives 435 = Preussen from a gloss. Not read.
- "Paezes", "Pae[z]": Pac (probably Michał Kazimierz Pac, grand hetman of Lithuania: our identification from context; Bonde's despatch of 3/13 Nov 1677, decoded below our threshold in b118 and not filed, concerns the passage of Lithuanian troops and Hetman Pac).
