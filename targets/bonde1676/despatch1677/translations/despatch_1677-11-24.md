## Translation

[...] *very ill pleased* here at court [...] coming to Livonia and learning of *Pac's opposition*, would *delay and hold back the [418] [?march]* until the matter had been settled *with Pac*, and so [...] in *the form of a vain negotiation*. I can [say] no more about this [...] the causes that have put this court in *such alarm*.

To remove that, I have [...] wants nothing more than that *the designs, with a prompt diversion into [435] [?Prussia]*, be *carried out* to the best advantage, and that Your Royal Majesty's Field Marshal in this regard will *bring the [418] [?march] to ripeness* as soon as it can ever be done, so that he [...] the deliberations, such *suspicions* once formed may be *let go*, and in due [...].
