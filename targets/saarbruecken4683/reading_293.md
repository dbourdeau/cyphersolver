# Reading: copy of a letter received on 23 Jan 1674 (fol. 293r-293v, first 41 lines), system A

Conventions as in reading_290.md; `<word>` = nomenclator code with a value in key.tsv. The copy carries no decipherment, but it is **not a new text**: fol. 293r ff. is a number-for-number copy of the letter fol. 279r-281r (signed "Johann Melchior ...", 14 Jan 1674, received 23 Jan), whose decipherment of the time is written between its lines. Of 54 runs of three or more numbers noted on fol. 279r-v while the key was built, 41 recur in our transcription of the copy number for number and in the same order, 10 with one difference and 3 with two (in a shuffled number stream of the copy, 0.08 runs match on average; in the outgoing letter fol. 297, none). The comparison was not pre-registered and the key was partly built from these very glosses, so this reading is a check of the key against the gloss of fol. 279r-281r, not a first reading. The clear text was transcribed once by an agent and is rough (`?` marks doubtful clear words).

## Corrections from the comparison with fol. 279r-v
They come from the number-for-number match with the glossed original, not from the key; decryption_293.txt, the decoder's output from key.tsv, is left as it was.
- "rozan liese" (293r l. 13) is the code pair 46 45 48 = 464 548, *Fürst Schwartzenberg* in the gloss of 279r-v, followed by 66 121 57 130 76 147 100, *anliesse*. "rozan" is not a word.
- "aller Arten" (293r l. 16) reads *alle rahten* (raten).
- "418" (293r l. 30) is a misreading of 148 (= e): 40 76 48 154 51 77 56 148 *Metzische*, then *Sach*.
- 744 (293r l. 30) = *Fürstenberg* in the gloss of the original (745 is Fürstenberg elsewhere in the file).
- The restoration "M[ajestät]" (293r l. 17) is withdrawn: the original has 283 *Kaÿserin* and 284 *Reich*. The copy writes **253** (Kaiser) and **184** (m), as transcribed. This was settled by the blind shape review (relecture aveugle sur forme, Brice Dudout, planche saarbruecken4683-2711; see reading_290.md and `review/plate_2711/`). These are two copying slips against the original, a 5 for an 8 and a 1 for a 2. The text above prints the original's words.
- The heading of the copy carries *Wien* in cipher (62 58 52 49), with "d. 4ten Jan." and an uncertain year (1673?); the original is signed on 14 Jan 1674, its place of writing not checked.
- Not used in the text above, as tentative: for 330 and 314, unresolved in the copy, the glosses of the original give, as far as we read them, *Schweden?* and *Spanien?*; for 326 171, *Frantzösische*.
- Other differences (fortgesezete : fortgesetzet, fouirten : fovirten) are spelling variants between cipher and gloss.

## Justification
- Probable: Ottweiler ("oqweille": q for t), Leidmuht, Notthurft ("notthuft"), rahten (from "allerahten", split by the gloss of the original), stillschweigen ("stillschwligen": e missing), zugemutheten ("zugemhthete"), Metzische ("mezisch" + 148 misread 418), favorirten ("fouirten").
- Restored: [be]clagen (one illegible group before "clagen"), particu[lar] (line cut at the stop of the transcription).
- Codes from the gloss of the original: Fürst Schwartzenberg (464 548), Fürstenberg (744), Kaÿserin (283), Reich (284).
