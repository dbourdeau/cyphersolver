# Translation: Waldeck to the Elector, 7 September 1655, the cipher passages (scans 787-789)

English of `reading_w787.md`, with the same holes; [... clear text not edited ...] marks the clear German between the passages, not translated here.

The conditions with Sweden could hardly be worse. And because of the bad state and arrangement [...] I have sent to Jacob Weiher, and also to Elbing and Danzig. I will also speak with the Bishop of Ermland at the first opportunity. [...] to build ships here, and that they should be ready [...] not fail in my diligence. The city of Danzig has written to me that it [...] the declaration it asked for [...] assurance about the march [...] consent and communication of the commons [...] could be sent there, they [...] offered to provide ships. And to obtain provisions (vivres) either [against] cartage money or on security; [we] will also send news to General Sparr by this post.
