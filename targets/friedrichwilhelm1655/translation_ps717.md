# Translation: Postscriptum, August 1655 (scan 717; foot repeated on scan 715)

English of `reading_ps717.md`.

So We are still being kept in suspense. One must therefore not make treaties binding with the people of that region, especially with the Weihers, so that one can withdraw from them when it suits. With Ermland one can proceed. They will not allow Us any other protection. With Elbing [one] can deal subject to ratification. To the Oberräte [the high councillors of Ducal Prussia] one can communicate with caution what is contained in the letters, and what concerns them. Our troops are marching.
