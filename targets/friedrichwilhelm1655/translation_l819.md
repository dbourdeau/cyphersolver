# Translation: Elector Frederick William to Waldeck, September 1655, the cipher passage (scan 819)

English of `reading_l819.md`. The clear lines before it say that the voivode of Marienburg and Prince Albert Stanislaus Radziwill, Grand Chancellor of Lithuania, [...] found it necessary to correspond with Us and

... to join counsels. In particular the said Prince Radziwill gave to understand that they knew no other course than to place themselves under Our protection.
