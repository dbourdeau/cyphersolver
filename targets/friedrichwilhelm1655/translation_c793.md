# Translation: Elector Frederick William to Waldeck, Cöslin, 4 September 1655 (cipher body, scans 793-795)

English of `reading_c793.md`, with the same holes. [...] = text lost in the binding of scan 794.

[... in our] favour. [We let you know] that We are now with Our troops, who have continued their march as far as here to Cöslin; tomorrow, God willing, We will set out from here again, and hope with God's help to stand with the army on the Vistula within ten days. By that time you must be there with your troops, and that at Nebro [place not identified]. And since what matters most to Us is ships for the crossing, you will, in line with Our earlier order, have seen to it that the ships are ready in the Königsberg Haff, so that they can come to the Vistula when they are called for. Of provisions, about twenty-four thousand pounds of bread a day will be needed, as much beer and perhaps more, and the meat and salt that go with it. The proportion will also show it: forage must be reckoned for at least six thousand horses, the artillery and general staff included. For this [...] you are to make arrangements so that there is no shortage [...]; and We have had orders given to Gerhard Roggen at Danzig and to Christoph Melchior to [...] and assist you in this to the utmost. With the artillery [...] We have some three hundred horses: of the artillery you may leave all the pieces behind except two, and bring the artillery horses with you to the Vistula. Up to then We will have them brought on from Pomerania by relays of draught horses. The Danzigers have made difficulties over passage through their territory, and still less will they allow it through the city. But We are sending Our Hinterpommern court administrator (Hofgerichtsverwalter), von Krockow, there, to negotiate with the city and with the voivodes of that region, and await his good report.
