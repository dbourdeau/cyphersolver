# Emendations, holes and doubtful words

The letter is in clear Italian with single words and short phrases in cipher; `reading.md` gives both as one text and `decryption.txt` shows which words are cipher (capitals). Marks in the reading and the translation: `[...]` not read; `[?word]` a word proposed and doubtful; a bracket inside a word (`scr[i]tura`) is a letter supplied or emended; `[I.]`, `[PP.]`, `[25]`, `[50]` are codes for persons, not identified. Word division, capitals, accents and punctuation are added; the spelling is the writer's. u and v are one sign; v is printed where modern spelling has it (a convention, not counted as an emendation).

Places are line, then word and sign counted from 0, as in `units.tsv` and `overrides.tsv`. "Key output" is what the key table and the label aliases give with no override (`decryption.txt`, the K lines).
