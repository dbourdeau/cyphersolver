## Folio 2

**Paragraph 4.** All these events, and the consideration of what the nation's history shows in the matter of revolts
and governments, make us fear that past events may repeat themselves, chiefly THAT DISCOURAGEMENT SPREADS in OUR RANKS
on SEEING the GOVERNMENT'S METHODS and the LACK of a VIGOROUS and UNITED FORCE to COUNTER them and prepare the future.

**Paragraph 5.** We await the SENDING OF GUIDANCE more in KEEPING with the IMMINENCE of the DANGER TO which OUR {LARGE}
GROUPS ARE EXPOSED that {HAVE} not ENTERED the STRUGGLE ONLY FOR LACK of MEANS, DANGERS that MAY TURN into
BREAK-UPS [?] [...] likewise more SUITED to the FAVOURABLE CIRCUMSTANCES of the MOMENT, in which Obregonism
seems in a hurry, as if on purpose, to discredit itself even among its own people.

**Paragraph 6.** A concrete case of the dangers I speak of is the following: GENERAL MALLÉN, now in the service OF
OBREGONISM, came to an ARRANGEMENT with TORRUCO of CÁSTULO PÉREZ'S FORCES so that he would {BETRAY} him; the
TRAITORS soon CAME TO TERMS and now TORRUCO is the one who ACTS AS CHIEF ENEMY to CÁSTULO PÉREZ, doing a vile piece of
work. This justifies THE FEARS I have mentioned.

**Paragraph 7.** FEDERICO CÓRDOBA, WHO IS {UP IN ARMS}, is trying to deceive our friends by telling them that he acts
in agreement WITH PELÁEZ AND WITH YOU; and as other individuals are trying to exploit these stories, we shall be much
obliged if you tell us expressly whether there is any NEW DECISION OF YOURS on the matter, in order TO RECOGNISE IT
AND ACT DISCREETLY.

## Folio 3

**Paragraph 1.** As to our LATEST WORK I must tell you that {WE HAVE} ALREADY PREPARED A MOVEMENT WITH BRANCHES
THROUGHOUT THE STATE, being ready to go in as soon as WE RECEIVE YOUR LATEST {INSTRUCTIONS}. We also remind you to be
so kind as to give us YOUR [?OPINION] on THE CHIEF OF OPERATIONS to whom I referred in my last, dated the 25th of last
month.

**Paragraph 2.** MR. CORONA has told me that since he does not KNOW YOU TO BE [?IN] [...] and does not KNOW FOR CERTAIN
WHERE YOU ARE, he has POSTPONED HIS JOURNEY {TO} THE UNITED STATES. I therefore think it right to make known to you
THE NAMES [...]: LIC. {JOSÉ} MARÍA CAMACHO, PRESIDENT; FIDENCIO E. LÓPEZ, SECRETARY; MIGUEL MÁRQUEZ GALINDO,
DEPUTY SECRETARY; DR. ALBERTO MORENO, TREASURER; AND FRANCISCO MACHORRO, MIGUEL ALVARADO, LIC. ARTURO OSORIO,
LIC. LUIS GARCÍA [?] AND POMPOSO BONILLA, MEMBERS. == THE PERSONS WHO {MUST} SIGN [?DOCUMENTS], WHO ARE THE PRESIDENT,
THE SECRETARY, THE {DEPUTY SECRETARY} AND THE [?TREASURER], HAVE TAKEN AS {WAR} NAMES RESPECTIVELY: [...].

**Paragraph 3.** AS TO THE PERSON WHO, WITHOUT BEING A MEMBER OF THE JUNTA, DOES THE MOST DELICATE WORK AND STUDY WITH
ME AND {WHO IS} LIC. RICARDO SUÁREZ ESCALANTE, OF WHOM I HAVE SPOKEN TO YOU CONSTANTLY IN MY {LAST} LETTERS, FROM
TODAY I SHALL REFER TO HIM BY HIS WAR NAME, WHICH IS RICARDO LOBATO.

**Paragraph 4.** At the time THE JUNTA WAS FORMED, LIC. {JUAN} CRISÓSTOMO BONILLA WAS ELECTED, but this person left
the STATE and so CEASED TO BE A MEMBER. Nevertheless, as THE BONILLA FAMILY deserves every consideration from us for
ITS IDEAS, we agreed TO APPOINT MR. DON POMPOSO [?] [...].

**Paragraph 5.** With nothing further for the moment, your devoted friend and servant, [signed] J. W. Wellington.

**P.S.** YOUR REPLY matters very much to me; I therefore URGE [?].
