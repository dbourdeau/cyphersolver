## Image 0001 (page 1)

### Head of the letter (date, address, salutation)

[illegible: paper torn away] 10, 1922.-

Señor don José González,
N.O.----U.S.A.

My very dear sir and good friend:

### Paragraph 1

As the news I can give you, so that you may be fully informed of our constant work, grows more important every day, I
take the liberty of sending you this third letter, after my previous ones dated the 25th of last month and the 5th of
this.

### Paragraph 2

Strong groups are getting ready to throw themselves into the struggle in our region: TOMÁS HUERTA [L6.01], with many
followers, is preparing to INTENSIFY THE MOVEMENT IN THE DISTRICTS OF TEHUACÁN, TECAMACHALCO, TECALI, {TEPEXI},
ACATLÁN, CHALCHICOMULA AND SAN {JUAN} {DE} LOS LLANOS [L6.02];------------ JOSÉ LÓPEZ GUILLEMÍN IN THE BORDER REGIONS
BETWEEN PUEBLA AND TLAXCALA [L6.03];---------- ROMÁN JIMÉNEZ IN THE {DISTRICTS} [L6.04] of {CHIAUTLA}, MATAMOROS,
{ATLIXCO}, CHOLULA, HUEJOTZINGO, TEPEACA AND THE TOWNS AROUND THE MUNICIPALITY OF {PUEBLA} [L6.05];-------- YSABEL
GUERRERO [L6.06] in the REGION OF THE VOLCANOES [L6.07]. That is as regards the regions of the SOUTH OF THE STATE
[L6.08]; as for THE SIERRA [L6.09], the work OF INTENSIFYING [L6.10] has been taken on by MESSRS MIGUEL MÁRQUEZ
GALINDO, FRANCISCO {MACHORRO} AND SERGIO BONILLA [L6.11]. All these men wait only for THE JUNTA TO GIVE THEM {ITS}
LATEST INSTRUCTIONS [L6.12] to take part, some of them being already in the field.

### Paragraph 3

Both THIS STATE AND THAT OF TLAXCALA [L6.13] offer every day a better field for intensifying the campaign, chiefly
because of the discontent that reigns among the peasants. The methods of the immoral employees of the local
administrations have sown alarm, ill feeling and hatred towards the government among the towns, which see themselves
ill-treated and exploited by men without conscience. Moreover, in these same States the story has spread that the
Lamont-de la Huerta Pact is ruinous for the Country; and this story is gaining ground in the minds of the common
people, which leaves them deeply disgusted, ready to follow any banner that may come to destroy this regime of
oppression.

### Paragraph 4

Through very good channels I have learnt that the unionised workers' groups are organising on political ground in
agreement with the baneful General Plutarco Elías Calles for future Bolshevik machinations, and that if he now makes a
show of giving little help to the Soviets, it is because, together with Obregón and de la Huerta, he is trying to
ingratiate himself with the U.S. in order to obtain "recognition"; but, I repeat, they are fully agreed that in the
future the Bolshevik preachings, entirely like those of Russia, shall be carried out in the Republic, a sample of
which we are already seeing in the unfortunate states of Veracruz and Yucatán.

### Paragraph 5

It is again being asserted in political circles, and even among the government men, that Murguía, Peláez and other
Chiefs are going to intensify their movement, organised throughout the Republic, and it is even said that Peláez, as
Chief of that same revolt, will bring a ship with arms and ammunition bound for the Gulf coast.

### Paragraph 6

All these antecedents lead us to beg you to be so kind as to bear in mind that this news has disoriented our friends;
we shall therefore be grateful if you refer to it when you are so kind as to answer us.
