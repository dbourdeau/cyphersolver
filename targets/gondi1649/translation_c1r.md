# Gondi to Commendator Galilei, 11 May 1649, c. 1r: English translation

Same marks as `reading_c1r.md`: **bold** = from the cipher; `[...]` = clear text not read; `[rough: ...]` = clear text
read only roughly; `[?x]` = doubtful.

... sent a copy. It will be well, however, for Your Lordship **to hint whether this may come from His Excellency's
weighing [the matter] (pesar; decipherer: "thinking") that nothing more is truly needed, or from his not liking to see Your Lordship there**. But [...] **His Highness
has not yet resolved on Your Lordship's recall, and meanwhile he is waiting for the categorical answers of Signor
Vincenzio de' Medici on the chapter of the Signor Viceroy's paper that was sent to Your Lordship last week.** And having
[...] **His Highness's reply to His Excellency's letter will be sent to Your Lordship**, not wishing then [rough: to bring it to
His Highness] to do **it before [5 8, unread] having seen the said answers of Signor Vincenzio** and [...]

[rough: If I had already told Your Lordship ...], I tell you now that **in Milan those notions of the Signor [?Viceroy] were
very badly received [?and] blamed, at which His Highness with reason had taken offence**.

(V.S. = Vostra Signoria, the recipient; S.A. = Sua Altezza, the Grand Duke of Tuscany, Ferdinando II; S.E. = Sua
Eccellenza, here apparently the Viceroy named in the same passages.)
