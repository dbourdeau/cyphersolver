# Gondi to Commendator Galilei, 11 May 1649, c. 1r: reading of the cipher (Italian)

The cipher passages are given in their place in the letter. Text in **bold** is from the cipher; plain text is the letter's
own clear text, read only far enough to place the cipher (`[...]` = clear words not read for this job;
`[rough: ...]` = clear words read only roughly, not checked). The line-by-line,
mark-checked form of the cipher reading is `reading_marked_c1r.txt`; `scripts/check_marks.py` checks it against the signs.

Marks in the cipher reading: `(x)` letter with no sign (the cipher writes double letters once); `[?x]` doubtful sign or
value from context only; `{che}` read as *che* where the sign shows a bare 5 (o) without its bar. Codes are expanded.

## Italian

... mandata copia. Sarà però bene che V.S. **ac(c)en(n)i se ciò pos(s)a derivare o dal pesar S.E. veram.,
{che} non oc(c)or(r)a più altro, dal non veder volentieri V.S. costà**. Ma con[...] **non risolve per ancora S.A. la
richiamata di V.S., e sta intanto aspet(t)ando le risposte categoriche del Sig.r Vincenzio di Medici sopra il capo
del(l)a scrit(t)ura del Sig.r Vicerè, che si mandò a V.S. la set(t). pas.** E havuto [...] **si manderà (a) V.S. la
r[?e]plica di S.A. al(l)a let(t)era di S.E.**, non desiderando poi [rough: portarla a S.A., or portar all'A.S.] da far**la prima di [5 8 ?] aver veduto le
sud. risposte del Sig.r Vincenzio** & [...]

[rough: Se già havessi detto ... a V.S.], le dico adesso che **a Milano furono sentiti malis. [?et] biasimati quelli con(c)et(t)i
del Sig.r [?Vicerè], de quali S.A. con ra[?g]ione si era risentita**.

Notes on the text:
- *pesar*: the signs give p-e-n/s-a-l/r, read literally *pesar* ("weighing"); *pensar* would need one more n/s sign.
  The decipherer wrote "pensiero di"; no code for *di* stands before S.E. Kept as a difference, not repaired.
- *{che}*: the bar over 7 (*non*) may cover the 5 too; *che* is the decipherer's reading and fits the sense.
- *[5 8 ?] aver*: two signs (5 and a B-shaped sign) stand before *aver*; the decipherer wrote "havere". Not explained,
  not read.
- *malis.*: the cipher abbreviates *malis[simo]* ("very badly"); the decipherer wrote "malam.te" (malamente). Kept as
  in the cipher.
- *[?et]*: a bare 4 (u/v) where the clear has "et"; the barred 4 is *e* on L03.
- *farla prima di*: the cipher *la* is the object pronoun joined to the clear *far* (*farla*), and *prima di* is
  "before": "to do it before having seen". Word division in the cipher is ours.
- The two `[rough: ...]` clear phrases were read loosely; the independent check reads the second as "portar all'A.S.".
- *[?Vicerè]*: code 273 stands where the decipherer's clear line ends "Vic.Re"; 223 is Vicerè on L06. Context only.
