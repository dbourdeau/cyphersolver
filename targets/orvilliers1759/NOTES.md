# D'Orvilliers, Lemoyne and Morisse (Cayenne) to the Navy minister, ciphered letters 1751, 1757-62 (Unsolved Catalogue no. 543)

Status: no write-up. Blocked on access: none of the 36 C14 items has a digital object on ANOM despite the "numérisé" flag.

Blocked on images: the C14 volumes are not online. ANOM marks every notice "document numérisé", but none carries
a digital object, and the cipher letters cannot be counted, transcribed or attacked from here. Nothing below rests on
a sight of the documents.

## The documents

- Archives nationales d'outre-mer, COL C14 (Secrétariat d'État à la Marine, Correspondance à l'arrivée de Guyane),
  inventory FRANOM_00017, branch "Administration de Guillouet d'Orvilliers (Gilbert), gouverneur d'avril 1757 à mai
  1763" (https://recherche-anom.culture.gouv.fr/archive/fonds/FRANOM_00017/view:1908367).
- The catalogue entry spoke of "about 10 letters". A sweep of all 227 notices in that branch (27 Oct 2026, both pages
  of the inventory view) found **35 items with ciphered text** in C14 24 and C14 25, Apr 1757 to Dec 1762, plus
  the 7 July 1751 letter in C14 21 that the catalogue also cited. All 36 are in `letters.tsv` with cote, date,
  writer, ark and the inventory note.
  - Writers: Gilbert Guillouet d'Orvilliers, governor (alone, 17 items); d'Orvilliers with the ordonnateur Antoine
    Philippe Le Moyne (12, the 1751 letter included) or with his successor François Louis Morisse (2); Le Moyne alone
    (3); Morisse alone (1); Jacau de Fiedmond (1, 5 Aug 1762).
  - Extent: 6 letters "entièrement chiffrée" / "chiffrée en entier" (12 Apr 1760, 10 Jun 1761 F° 36, 12 May 1762,
    29 Jun 1762, 5 Aug 1762, 12 Dec 1762), 14 "lettre chiffrée", "en majeure / grande partie chiffrée" or "chiffrée
    en partie", and 16 with one or more "passages chiffrés". Several are marked "non traduit(s)": the bureau left them undeciphered, or the decipherment
    was not filed.
  - Duplicates exist (26 Sep 1760 F° 167 is the duplicata of 8 Jun 1760; 21 Dec 1760 F° 162 the duplicata of the
    December 1760 letter, F° 148 or F° 145 v°; 12 and 20 Mar 1759 are duplicata). Two copies of one cipher text are a
    transcription check once images exist.
- The catalogue's "start with the 1759 duplicata in C14 24" points to F° 167 and F° 170 (Le Moyne, 12 and 20 Mar
  1759, "en majeure partie chiffrée").
- Content (inventory): famine and the supply of the colony during the Seven Years' War (Saint-Eustache and Dutch
  ships, flour, manioc, lamantin salt meat), the garrison's strength and pay, the suspended bills of exchange
  (arrêt of 15 Oct 1759), defence works (Renauld's project, Oyapock), the plan to retreat inland with the colonists'
  slaves, the fall of Martinique (29 Jun 1762), and d'Orvilliers' project for an expedition against Brazil with the
  Amazon as the eastern frontier (12 Dec 1762).

  D2 C matricules and Moreau de Saint-Méry. The Guyane C14 series is not among the online fonds.
- "Document numérisé" in the communicabilité field therefore means the original is withdrawn and a digital copy is
  consulted in the reading room (Aix-en-Provence), not that it is online.
- The inventory PDF (FRANOM_00017_all.pdf) downloads only in a browser (curl gets the Anubis page); not needed, the
  notices were read directly.

## Key material

- None found. No interlinear decipherment is noted for any item; several say "non traduit".
- DECODE: no record in the harvest list (10,106 records) mentions Cayenne, Guyane, Guiana, d'Orvilliers, Le Moyne
  or C14.
- Leads, all offline:
  - **Archives nationales MAR/G/178**, "Chiffres employés dans les dépêches aux diverses colonies, 1741-1776"
    (see targets/mercier1761/NOTES.md): the Navy bureau's colonial keys. The Cayenne key of 1757-62 may be there.
  - **COL B** (ordres du roi et dépêches, outgoing): the minister's letters to Cayenne of 1757-63, in clear minute,
    answer these despatches point by point and are the first crib source. Several items here reply to a "dépêche
    ministérielle du 6 juin 1760". COL B registers were not checked for online images.
  - The Navy's printed two-part syllabic form (Martinique 1765, C8 A 67 f° 178; transcribed in
    targets/mercier1761/key1765_ennery.tsv) shows the kind of code the colonial governors used. The 1761 Martinique
    key (targets/mercier1761/key1761.tsv) is a different filling of a similar form; whether Cayenne shared it is
    unknown and testable as soon as one cipher line is seen.
  - Duplicata pairs and the clear passages of the partly ciphered letters are cribs.

## Escalation

- Siblings: the 36 items are siblings of one another; the duplicata pairs give two copies of the same cipher.
  Martinique 1761 (catalogue 542) is read in part from a deciphered sibling; its key is the first thing to test.
- Clear pages: most items are partly in clear; the clear context around each passage is a crib once imaged.
- Known keys: MAR/G/178 (Archives nationales, Pierrefitte), not online.
- Print: COL B outgoing dépêches (clear minutes of the minister's replies); any printed history of Guiana in the
  Seven Years' War.
- Next step: order reproductions from ANOM (Aix-en-Provence), starting with the six wholly ciphered letters and the
  Mar 1759 duplicata (C14 24 F° 167, 170), and ask for MAR/G/178 at Pierrefitte. Then transcribe, test the 1761
  Martinique key and the 1765 form, use clear-text and COL B cribs, and only then a ciphertext-only attack with a
  French model from `lang/` and a same-length control.

## C14 25 fol. 36 (10 June 1761) read, Brice Dudout (28 Oct 2026)

The C14 volumes can be viewed on IREL at volume level. In C14 24 the 1757-59 cipher passages are glossed between the lines (or duplicates of glossed copies); from them the Guiana code (about 500 numbers) was rebuilt, frozen and validated on a held-out glossed letter of 10 June 1761. The letter of the same day 'entièrement chiffrée et non traduite' (fol. 36) is read: Des Essarts's plan to clothe the garrison through Rotterdam and Surinam, and a breach of twelve toises in the royal bastion. See `dudout/README.md`.
