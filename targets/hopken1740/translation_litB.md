Most Excellent Lord.

Everything that Your Excellency told me about the confines that are to be marked out, and what the law and custom is in
dividing the islands, I explained all of it often and very often, and the whole [dispute] was [about] those foundations,
so that the legate of Germany was astonished [and did not know] what to answer.

And I believe [that] he will no longer [dispute about] the islands, [...] about the confines, because they have
[dealt] [very] harshly about everything, and as if the last answer had been given [to him].

If he does not [accept], he must give a final answer, and I believe [that he] will answer today or tomorrow that he
accepts everything, because it cannot be otherwise.

However, what Your Excellency [ordered me], today I shall communicate everything to those who need it.

They had given me leave [1463] [yesterday], that is for Monday, [to come] to Your Excellency, but early in the morning
they called me [again], and gave me business until the evening, and I must stay at the Porte every day and all day.

I ask Your Excellency to [forgive] me [that] I could not visit, and yet I have a great desire; as soon as I have free
time, I shall not neglect it.

And I remain ready for your service,

Ibrahim Effendi.

To the Most Excellent Lord, Lord A[h]me[t] [1909], our most respected Lord.
