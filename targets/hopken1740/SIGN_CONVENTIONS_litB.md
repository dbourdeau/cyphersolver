# Sign conventions for Lit. B (catalogue 2242, Diplomatica Turcica I/9/29, R0003653)

Set before transcription began (12 Nov 2026) and applied to every transcription in this folder. Shapes only; no values.
They follow the conventions of `targets/hopken1740/SIGN_CONVENTIONS.md`, so the files can be compared.

- The cipher is written in Arabic digits, in four-digit groups separated by spaces (sometimes run close together; a
  group is always four digits). Transcribed as written, one row of the page per line, labelled B01, B02, ... for the
  right page of image R0003653_00081 ("Doupplette", "Lit. B"); row 00 is the heading line set apart under "Lit. B".
- A short stroke written above or after a group (an acute-like tick or a raised comma) is recorded as `'` after the
  group (e.g. `1006'`). Its meaning was not assumed while transcribing.
- Points, commas, colons and semicolons written between groups are recorded as `.`, `,`, `:`, `;` where they stand.
- Plain (unenciphered) words inside or between cipher rows are recorded as `[clear: ...]`.
- The large pen strokes crossing the page (cancellation marks of the archive or the chancery) are not recorded.
- `?` after a group: one digit could not be settled on the image (the group is given in its likelier reading; the
  alternative is named in the reconciled file's notes).
- Digits that look alike in this hand and were checked on 2x crops: 3/8 (closed upper loop = 8), 5/6, 2/7 (open lower
  stroke = 2), 7/1, 4/9.
- No interlinear writing occurs on this page; there is no gloss.
