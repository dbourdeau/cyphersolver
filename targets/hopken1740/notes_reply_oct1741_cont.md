# Notes: reply continuation, image 50 right to 54 right

## Summary

The reply argues that Sweden and the Porte should act together against Russia now. Sweden is ready to fight. Russia
is stalling on its treaty with the Porte and trying to split the Porte from Sweden. France's guarantee can only
work through Sweden. The envoys ask Castellane to press the Porte's ministers. If the Russian envoy (Romanzof?)
offers terms, the Porte should be told a Swedish-Russian rupture is near, and it would then be bound to help its
ally. They offer to give the grand vizier a memorandum and say they have full powers to negotiate. The letter
closes on 54 right: "ce 15 de Juillet, à 3 heures du matin". No year is given. The "2 Oct 1741" note on image 49 is
a receipt note.
