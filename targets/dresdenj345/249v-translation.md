If you wish to write in the sun’s [circle?] so that it can be read.

Take lion’s blood at the hour when the sun enters Leo. It is then good from that time onward. Whatever month you wish to write in, observe the hour of the sun and write on the little leaves of the *solis* herb with the lion’s blood, which is mixed in that same hour with egg white and the juice of the sun-root. On one leaf put the character of the angel of the sign that rules in that month, together with the character of the sun. On the other leaves write your wish, what [you want] to happen.

Take rosemary wood and light a fire with it. When the sun is shining and the sky is clear, lay the leaves on it [*in iren*, sense uncertain] and burn them. Then you will see wonders. On your life, beware of the smoke. If it touched your body, you would turn as yellow as gold, and the colour would never come off again.
