On 249r, *maletzei* names historical leprosy or leprous disease; the cure is the writer’s claim. The *solis* herb cannot be identified to species here. “Little sprig”, “should” and “virtue” are tentative interpretations of *ſt?delin*, *ſo?l* and *tugen?*. *Median* means the median [vein] in this bloodletting context. *Abgon* in a *balneo marie* is literally to pass off or over in a water bath; distillation would be an interpretation. *Darnach ine, ſo er erwacht* has a loose repeated pronoun in the manuscript. No quantity has been guessed.

On 249v, the heading appears to say “circle”, but the last sign remains open. The words *fir und fir* are the idiom *für und für* (“continually, from then on”), whereas *ein fir* at 249v.17 is “a fire”. *Aier clar* is egg white. “Sun-root” renders *ſonnen wurtzen* literally; the text gives no secure species. *Welchs beschehen* stops without a finite verb; “[you want]” supplies the sense in English. *In iren* is present in the signs. Its interpretation remains open. [Historical examples of *für und für*](https://www.fwb-online.de/go/je.s.4adv_1754618493)
