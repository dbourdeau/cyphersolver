Measure S = share of cipher letters whose group deciphers (Vigenere, key restarted per group) to a word of a Portuguese lexicon,
or to two words of it. Controls: C1 = 1000 random keys of the same length on the same portion; C2 = the tested key on 1000
copies of the portion with letters shuffled inside each group. Criterion: S >= 0.85 on the held-out portion and S > max(C1, C2).
