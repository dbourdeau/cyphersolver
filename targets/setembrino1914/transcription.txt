Conventions:
  - cipher: = groups that are not plain Portuguese words, in tape order; " / " = tape line break; the "-" separators printed between groups on the tape are NOT reproduced (noted).
  - cipher-repeat: = the same for a repeat/collation ("Ct") strip on the same sheet, transcribed separately.
  - tape: = full verbatim tape (clear + cipher) for sheets that mix clear text and cipher groups.
  - [A|B] = doubtful letter (options); ? = illegible letter.
  - doubtful: positions are line-group#.letter#  (L = tape line of the "cipher"/"tape" field), or named group + letter#.
  - Pencil annotation "FSC pa 1914.03.10/4" (archive call-number style) appears on top right of almost every sheet.
