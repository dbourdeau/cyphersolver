# Sign conventions (catalogue 1465, CEHM LXVIII-1)

All cipher text in these documents is written as two-figure numbers separated by hyphens
(typed in 14.1943.1, pencil in 17.2435.1 and 23.3165.1). The signs are the ten digits; a group
is two digits. No values are given here.

- `NN`      a group read with confidence.
- `N?`      a group whose second digit cannot be read; `?N` first digit unread.
- `[A|B]`   a group whose shape allows two readings (e.g. `[20|80]`); the transcription does not
            choose; the reconciled file settles it on the image where it can and says why.
- `/`       line end on the original (kept only in pass files).
- `{ov:A>B}` typed over-strike in 14.1943.1: figure A struck over by B (the visible top figure B
            is the group read).
- Joined groups typed without a hyphen (`6922`, `2942`, `6838`) are split into two groups.
- Clear text between coded runs is not transcribed figure by figure; each coded run is one
  segment (`S01`, `S02`, ...) and the segment file names the clear words before and after it.
- In 14.1943.1 the pencil interlinear decipherment on pp. 3-4 (the typed copy headed
  "CLAVE EXTRA 3.") is a separate layer (`gloss`); it was covered while the figures were
  transcribed and is kept in its own file.
