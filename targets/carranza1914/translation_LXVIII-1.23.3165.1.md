# Translation: Carranza's orders to Pablo González on Villa and Saltillo, [May 1914] (CEHM LXVIII-1.23.3165.1)

Coded parts in capitals, with the same holes as the Spanish reading. Letters supplied for illegible figures are in brackets.

> I have the honour to transcribe to you the following message dated yesterday at S[...], of today's date: FORCES BELONGING TO THIS DIVISION [ARE] TO MOVE ON THE PLACE OF SALTILLO.
>
> Order one of the chiefs, the one you think most suitable among those who are at Monterrey, TO GO OUT AND COOPERATE IN THE OPERATIONS AGAINST THE PLACE, putting himself under the immediate orders of General Villa. THE GENERAL YOU APPOINT may put himself in telegraphic communication at once with the said General Villa.
>
> ORDER GENERAL COSS TO take up position WITH the forces under his command SOUTH OF SALTILLO, the line THE ENEMY MUST TAKE IN RETREAT, SO THAT HE ATTACKS HIM WITH ADVANTAGE WHEN he withdraws from the place, having no other task than this, acting in concert WITH GENERAL EULALIO GUTIÉRREZ, WHO is on the line FROM SALTILLO TO SAN LUIS, TO whom I give orders to the same effect.
>
> I greet you affectionately. V. Carranza. Respectfully, the Captain Aide, A. H. Castañeda.
