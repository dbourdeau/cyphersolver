## Cipher system

Schilling-family vertical-bigram two-rank numerical cipher over a 31-symbol normalized Russian alphabet (`а б в г д е ж з и к л м н о п р с т у ф х ц ч ш ы ь ю я` plus word-space `-`, comma `,`, and full stop `.`; `ъ -> ь`, `ѣ -> е`, `і/й -> и`, `щ -> ш`, initial `э -> е`). Plaintext is written horizontally across consecutive pairs of 24-character rows (`U` over `L`). Each vertical pair `(U[c], L[c])` is enciphered as a three-digit code (`000–999`, leading zeroes significant) drawn alternately from **Rank 1** (odd-numbered tokens `1, 3, 5, ...`) and **Rank 2** (even-numbered tokens `2, 4, 6, ...`). For clerical dispatch on paper, the concatenated digit stream is re-divided into two-digit groups (`51 07 15` -> `510 | 715` -> `I/510 = сн`, `II/715 = т-`).

## Key reconstruction and validation

Run `python3 targets/nyconsulate1891/reproduce.py` to verify all hashes, bijectivity, mechanical decode files, coverage figures, and the pre-registered holdout permutation test.

1. **Stage 1 (400-entry baseline on Nos. 5078, 5015, 3902, 4945):** Direct alignment of the 24-column consular decipherment grids on frames 758 and 772 yielded the `K` (directly witnessed) skeleton, extended by finite-state alignment against the numbered copy on frame 753 (No. 3902) and the note on frame 754 (No. 4945) to 400 values.
2. **Stage 2 (471-entry frozen checkpoint on No. 519):** Before reading the second half of Olarovsky's cipher reply No. 519 (frames 733–735), the 400-value table was tested against the phrase `за время моего управления` from draft frame 736, matching all 13/13 covered characters (`0/9,999` random permutations across the second half). Constrained alignment on No. 519 expanded the frozen lookup to 471 values (`frozen_471_key.tsv`, `285 K` + `186 A`).
3. **Stage 3 (Holdout test and two-manuscript gate on No. 4166):** Before fitting No. 4166 (frames 742–743), `frozen_471_key.tsv` was tested against the locked phrase `на-письменном-вашем-заявлении` from frame 745 (`preregister.txt`). On the initial 198-group transcript it scored `11/14` (`6/8` with `K` alone) at cell 199; after restoring four line-initial groups (`00`, `24`, `22`, `49`) on frame 743 (`201` groups = `134` triples), the frozen lookup scored **`15/15` with `K+A` and `9/9` with `K` alone** (`0/9,999` within-rank random permutations reached 15). Leave-one-out calibration rejected single-witness extensions (`10/11 = 90.9%`, under 95%), while the two-manuscript rule (`24/24` on hidden historical truth) promoted only `I/726 = шт` (Nos. 4166 and 3902) and `I/692 = еа` (Nos. 4166 and 3902), alongside optical address repairs on No. 3902, producing a 472-entry table.
4. **Stage 4 (Verification and cross-check in [issue #803](https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/issues/803)):** Daniel Bourdeau reproduced all counts with an independent clean-room decoder and checked ~861 groups on frames 751, 752, 732, and 742, catching three transcription slips (`751_L06` g12 `96 -> 76`, `752_L04` g6 `49 -> 79` unlocking `II/179 = мт`, and `732_L06` g13 overwritten). Cross-checking against Brice Dudout's independent unpublished reconstruction (`464/468 = 99.1%` agreement on shared cells) settled two further scan readings: `757_L05` g16 is `78` (`I/387 -> I/787 = яв`, simultaneously decoding `[я]нваря / [в]осемь` at No. 3902 token 399 and vacating the spurious hit at No. 519 token 39), and frame 753 line 6 reads `предлагается` (`I/528 = рт`), which also resolved all `6/6` occurrences of `II/925 = те` across Nos. 3902, 4945, and 4166. Under Cyphersolver's strict word rule (a vertical gap `??` breaks words on two rows simultaneously), sure-word shares are ~31%, 43%, 75%, 68%, 27%, and 17% (~46% overall), so the texts are classed `decoded, not read` with a `partial` key (`472` active entries: `240` Rank 1 + `232` Rank 2; `285 K` + `187 A`).

## Remaining gaps

- **Unrecovered cells of Key No. 375 (`1,450` of `1,922` theoretical `31 x 31 x 2` vertical bigrams):** The six dispatches contain `664` distinct valid `(rank, code)` types across `1,249` triples, of which `472` are assigned in `key375.tsv` (`269` unread triple occurrences). Additional material in the same key on Roll 83 (notably dispatch No. 5012, identified by Brice Dudout in issue #803) closes further cells.
- **Quarantined and single-witness candidates (`II/925 = те`, `II/494`, `II/249`, `II/506`, and single-occurrence cells):** `II/925 = те` is now consistent across all `6/6` occurrences in Nos. 3902, 4945, and 4166 after the frame-753 reading fix, while `II/249` vs. `II/506` (`ео`) and `II/494` remain suspended pending cross-collation (`compare_375.tsv`).
- **Incomplete contemporary witnesses for Nos. 4945, 519, and 4166:** Frame 732 breaks off after 60 triples of No. 4945; frame 736 is Olarovsky's revised draft rather than a literal decryption of No. 519; frame 745 preserves the Russian body summary of No. 4166 without the exact opening/closing enciphered wording.
- **Later circulars in catalogue 756 (1895–1910+):** Keys 381, 404, and subsequent early-twentieth-century circulars on M1486 Roll 83 are separate systems outside key No. 375 and remain open in catalogue 756.
