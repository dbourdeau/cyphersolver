rucellai1482 (catalogue 2350 and lead 2323): what has been measured, and what has not

1. The one pre-registered measurement of this cipher system (6 Nov 2026)
   Key K_T, built from the pair 207/208 only, decoded the cipher passages of LXXII 204 and was compared with
   their contemporary clear copy 205 (character agreement, Levenshtein alignment).
   Agreement 0.6018 (176 operations over 442); 200 shuffled keys: best 0.2281, mean 0.1446.
   Threshold set in advance: max(0.85, best + 0.30) -> not validated. 204/205 is used up.
   A second test (13 Nov 2026) on the glossed groups of LXXII 201 against their interlinear glosses gave 0.5088
   (best of 200 shuffled keys 0.2832) -> not validated; 201 is used up too.

2. This key (K_HP_v1, 14 Nov 2026) has NOT been measured on a fresh portion
   There is no HP passage with a contemporary clear left that has not been used: 204/205 and 201 are used up,
   207/208 is the training pair, the five letters 196, 200, 202, 206, 211 are bare. So no validation figure is
   given for this key. What follows are consistency checks, not tests.

3. Fit on the training pair 207/208 (not a test)
   1 - Levenshtein / length of 208 (535 letters):
     key of 6 Nov (K_T) ................................ about 0.68 (first transcription)
     lot 11 proposal, 14 Nov ............................ 0.7701 (transcription of 13 Nov)
     lot 12 proposal, 14 Nov ............................ 0.7776 (same)
     K_HP_v1 on the v2 transcription of 207 ............. 0.8093 (200 shuffled keys: best 0.340, mean 0.228)
   The gain comes from one finding: the token "g" of the earlier transcription merged two shapes, a q with a
   barred descender (= o) and a "g" with an open lower loop (= e). Re-examined sign by sign on the image of 207
   (57 tokens re-classed), the two shapes take different values; the bare letters had been transcribed with "g"
   for the first shape and "c" for the second, which made their decodings print e for o and t for e.

4. Lexical consistency on the bare letters (not a test)
   Share of the decoded letters covered by Italian words of 4 or more letters (a 16th-century Italian word list),
   K_HP_v1 against 200 keys with the same values shuffled:
     200: 0.319 (shuffled best 0.150)   202: 0.320 (0.223)   206: 0.378 (0.188)   211: 0.432 (0.127)
     200-211 together (740 letters): 0.374 (shuffled best 0.122, mean 0.044; none of 200 reaches the key)
     196: 0.368 (0.131), given for the record only: 196 was used for a lexical measurement on 14 Nov.
   Before this work, with the lot 11 key and the earlier transcription: 196 0.246, 200 0.321, 202 0.256,
   206 0.324, 211 0.186.
   Every value change was accepted only if it did not lower the fit on 207 and raised the Italian letter-trigram
   likelihood on held-out letters (one letter left out at a time, 200/202/206/211). The two new shape classes
   pass on 207 (+0.034 and +0.037) and on 3 and 4 of the 4 letters. An automatic search for further single-value
   changes found nothing that held out of sample. Signs absent from 207 (I, the dot, l with small o, crossed
   equals) cannot meet the 207 condition: I = rr and dot = tt are kept as probable from the reading; the others
   are left out of the key and only restored in the reading cards.

5. What would validate it
   A fresh HP letter with its clear (MAP LXXII 189-215 or XLVIII, not yet surveyed sign by sign), higher-resolution
   images from the ASF, or Rucellai's printed keys (R. M. Comanducci, Il carteggio di Bernardo Rucellai, 1996,
   Appendix III), which we have not seen.
