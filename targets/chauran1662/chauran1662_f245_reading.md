# ff. 245r-245v: Spanish translation of the separate article of the Franco-Dutch treaty of 27 April 1662 (fifty sols a ton)

TNA SP 84/165 ff. 245r-245v (image 3 right and image 4 left of the State Papers Online copy of ff. 243-247), in the
Spanish-vocabulary cipher of this folder. Bare: no gloss on the page.

How to read this file. The text is joined from the line-by-line decryption
(`decryptions/chauran1662_f245_decryption.txt`, both blind transcriptions aligned and decoded with the frozen table and
the extension). Plain words are read from the frozen table on groups where the two transcriptions agree. `[?word]` is a
word that is not read from the frozen table: an extension value (many taken from the French text printed by Dumont,
see NOTES.md section 6), a group where the two transcriptions disagree, or a word completed from the French where a
syllable is unread. `[...]` is one unread word, `[... n]` n unread words; the sizes are estimates, the least number of
words the unread groups can stand for (a group is a word, a syllable or a letter). Spelling is lightly normalised and
syllables are joined into words; line numbers of f. 245r and f. 245v are given in brackets.

**Supplied, not read.** Every `[?word]` that matches Dumont's French (for example "navios", "estrangeros",
"subditos", "articulo veinte") is there because the French says it, not because the cipher was read at that place.

## Against the French

The text follows Dumont, *Corps universel diplomatique* VI part 2 (1728), pp. 418-419, "Article séparé touchant
l'imposition de cinquante Sols par Tonneau", clause by clause; "sols" is rendered *placas*. Read from the frozen table
alone, without any crib value, are: "ha estipulada", "estados", "gualdad", "cargas", "trattado de", "no derogara",
"cincuenta placas por tonelada establecida en Francia", "su Mad", "en lo venidero", "atendiendo a", "resolucion",
"una ... sola", "viage", "cargados de sal", "con condicion", "hacer pagar", "quedando", "que queda", "el dia de hoy",
and on f. 245v the signatories' syllables "de lo me ni e", "le ie u ne", "ie an de", "bo re el" (Dumont prints
Seguier, Villeroy, De Lomenie, Tellier, De Lionne, De Lomenie, Colbert, J. de Gent, C. van Beuningen, J. de Huybert,
G. Boreel). The words marked `[?word]` that agree with the French are supplied from it.

Hole sizes are estimates (see the head of this file). Extent unknown: none beyond the estimates given.
