Clause 1 ("Il a esté stipulé ... ne derogera pas à l'imposition de cinquante Sols par Tonneau, establis en France
sur les Navires Estrangers"). A Spanish version should carry, in roughly this order:
- a verb of stipulation/agreement: *estipulado / capitulado / acordado / convenido*, "de parte del Rey
  Cristianisimo" (or *Rey de Francia*), *consentido* by the *Estados Generales* of the *Provincias Unidas*;
- *igualdad* to be observed between the *subditos / vasallos* of both allies and the *naturales*;
- *derechos, cargas, imposiciones*;
- a reference to **article 20** (*vigesimo / veinte*) of the *tratado de alianza* concluded *oy / este dia*;
- *no derogara* / *no perjudicara* the **imposition of fifty sols per ton** (*cinquenta sueldos / sols por
  tonelada*) laid in France on **foreign ships** (*navios estrangeros*).

Clause 2 ("et que les Subjects des Seigneurs Estats ... seront obligez de payer, comme tout autres Estrangers: si ce
n'est que sa Majesté sur les remonstrances ... en disposât autrement"):
- the subjects of the States (*subditos de los Estados*) shall be bound to pay (*obligados a pagar*) like all other
  foreigners (*como todos los demas estrangeros*);
- unless His Majesty (*su Magestad*), on representations (*representaciones / remonstrancias*) made hereafter by the
  States, decides otherwise (*dispusiere otra cosa*).

Further content a fuller version should show (clause 3 onward): orders given at once that the duty be taken
**only once per voyage** (*una vez por cada viage*) on **leaving** (*saliendo*) the ports of the kingdom, not on
**entering** (*entrando*); ships **laden with salt** (*cargados de sal*) pay **half** (*la mitad*); if the States
lay a like duty on foreign ships it may not exceed what Dutch ships pay in France; article 20 otherwise in force.
Date: Paris, 27 April 1662.
