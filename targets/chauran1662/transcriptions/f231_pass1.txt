### Mark codes used for THIS cipher only (not the codes of cipher A)
D1 one dot; D2 two dots; B1 one bar; U a small "u" (two short upright strokes joined at the
foot); T2 a bar crossed by two short upright strokes (like a # with one bar); T1 a bar crossed
by one upright stroke (a plus sign); CF circumflex (^); AC one short stroke rising to the
right (acute); SG a small circle with a tail to the right (like a sigma); BV a breve (small
curve open upward). No mark: number alone. `X` before a group = struck through by the writer.
Records: `T:` clear French in the agent's hand, `C:` cipher, `G:` the gloss written above that
cipher line, as written, in reading order (not aligned to the groups).
