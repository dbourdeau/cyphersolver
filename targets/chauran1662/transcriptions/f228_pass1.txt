## The system (one paragraph)
Groups are numbers separated by a full stop on the line; digit 2 is written "v"; 4 has a
straight upright descender, 9 and 5 a curved tail, 7 a flag and a long slanting descender, 8 a
heavy flat top. Low numbers 1 to 134 make up most of the text and usually carry a mark; a few
numbers between 150 and 265 occur, mostly under a bar (170 and 252 in the sample lines, 262
and 265 on f. 229; others such as 173 seen only at page scale); and
scattered high numbers 400 to 988 (in the first four lines: 433 466 507 534 556 591 822 832 871
880 896 899 911 954 960 970 988), which on the glossed leaves stand under whole words. Marks
above the numbers: none; two dots (very common); a small "u"; a bar crossed by two short
strokes; one bar; a circumflex; an acute stroke; a small circle with a tail; a bar crossed by
one stroke (plus sign); a breve; one dot (rare, perhaps two dots seen imperfectly). The same
number is found with different marks (in L02 of the sample: 20 with "u" and 20 with the
twice-crossed bar, 22 with two dots and 22 with "u"), so the mark is part of the sign. f. 228r (image 1, right-hand page) has 20
written lines: lines 1 to 16 are cipher from margin to margin, line 17 begins with two cipher
groups and goes on in clear French, line 18 is clear French, line 19 begins in clear French
and ends with 18 cipher groups, line 20 is cipher (22 groups) ending in a row of dots. So
cipher stands on 19 of the 20 lines; lines 1 to 4 hold 23, 22, 24 and 23 groups (92), and at
that rate the page holds roughly 410 groups (not counted one by one). Nothing on f. 228r is
glossed. f. 229 (image 2, right-hand page) has 24 lines of clear French and then two cipher
lines (23 and 25 groups), both glossed in French, written above the cipher line by a second,
small upright hand (the decipherer). f. 228v (image 2, left-hand page) is blank apart from
endorsements and show-through; the left-hand page of image 1 is blank.

## Mark codes used for THIS cipher only (not the codes of cipher A)
D1 one dot; D2 two dots; B1 one bar; U a small "u" (two short upright strokes joined at the
foot); T2 a bar crossed by two short upright strokes (like a # with one bar); T1 a bar crossed
by one upright stroke (a plus sign); CF circumflex (^); AC one short stroke rising to the
right (acute); SG a small circle with a tail to the right (like a sigma); BV a breve (small
curve open upward). No mark: number alone.
Records: `T:` clear French in the agent's hand, `C:` cipher, `G:` gloss written above that
cipher line, as written, in reading order (not aligned to the groups).
