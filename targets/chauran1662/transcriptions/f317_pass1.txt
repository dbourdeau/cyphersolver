## Notes
Reading: every line was read from crops of about a third of a line's width at scale 2.5 (files in work/t317/crops/),
with scale 4 crops of some doubtful places. The microfilm resolution, not the crop size, is the limit. This is a
single first pass by an AI model; no person has checked it.

Line counts (actually counted): f. 317r 21 lines; f. 317v 21 lines; f. 318r 22 lines; f. 318v 3 lines. Total 67.
No gloss on any page (so the addendum's question of gloss position does not arise). No heading. No clear words.

Two styles of writing. f. 317r lines 1-20 and the first three groups of line 21 are in the hand of the conventions
(2 written "v", 7 a long slanting stroke, 5 and 9 with long tails). From the fourth group of f. 317r line 21 to the end
(all of ff. 317v, 318r, 318v) the figures are smaller and upright, 2 is written like a modern "2"/"z", 7 like a modern
"7" with a flat top, and "13" is written as a ligature (looks like "y3"; I read it 13 throughout, e.g. 130, 134, 139).
Same marks, same number range. Whether it is a second writer or the same writer with another pen I cannot say.
