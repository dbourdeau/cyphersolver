# noailles1558: English translation

Translation of `reading_original.md`, line for line, with the same marks: `[?…]` unread signs or letters, `{?…}`
tentative values, `[word]` supplied. The reading is *read in part*, so this translates a partial reading, not the
letter.

## Block A (f. 60r)

```
A1  [for] the bad order and poverty-
A2  {?} [or] little {?} conduct that he gives to his
A3  affairs [?3 signs] [?]
```

"… on account of the bad order and poverty, [or] little conduct, that he gives to his affairs …" The subject is not in
the cipher; the clear text before it is about news from the Emperor's lands (see Charrière II, 505, where Noailles
writes on 7 Nov that the Emperor "still gives bad order to his affairs").

[Correction added 12 Nov 2026, not by Brackx: the phrase is on p. 505 of Charrière II, but in the letter headed "Venise, 7 et 20 octobre 1558", not in the letter of 7 Nov (Andrew Beling, `beling/charriere_check.md`, confirmed on the OCR at the verification).]
