## Result: the 13 Nov 1558 letter is not printed [V on the OCR]
- Charrière's Venice letters of late 1558 follow in date order: 18/27 Aug, 20/28 Sept, 7/20 Oct, **7 Nov**, (Constantinople
  10 Nov), **15 Nov**, 24 Nov, 3 Dec, 10 Dec. There is no 13 Nov letter. The 15 Nov letter seems to be to M. de la Vigne, as far as the garbled OCR heading shows [I]; I found no letter to the Cardinal de Lorraine in this run, but did not check every recipient.
- None of the gloss phrases of block C ("plaisir … prouffit", "ravitailles", "plus hardiz") occurs in the 7-24 Nov letters.
  "hazard", "Hongrie" and "la Toussaint" occur there in other contexts (15 Nov letter, pp. 529-532), and "plaisir singulier"
  once, about the grand prieur (p. 517).
- So Charrière gives no plaintext for blocks A-C, and no held-out test can come from it.

## A correction to the repo's citation [V on the OCR]
- The repo (NOTES, from Brackx) cites "Charrière II 505, letter of 7 Nov 1558 ('donne encore mauvais ordre à ses affaires')".
  The phrase is on p. 505, but it belongs to the letter headed **"Venise, 7 et 20 octobre 1558"** (heading on p. 503);
  the 7 Nov letter starts after it, on p. 505.
- Context of the phrase [V]: "Le dict empereur se faict fort vieulx et donne encore mauvais ordre à ses affaires".
  The emperor is Ferdinand I. Block A ("…pour le maulvais ordre et pauvreté … conduitte qu'il donne a ses affaires")
  is very probably about him too [I].
