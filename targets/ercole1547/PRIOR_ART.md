## Same system as a neighbouring key? No.

- ariosto1559 (cat. 2159, Brice Dudout): same writer and recipient, same architecture (sign + raised figure for the
  vowel), different table. Its signs are Greek-shaped (image of c. 39r viewed, ref/1559_39r.jpg); ours are Latin-shaped.
  Its raised figures run 5 a, 4 e, 3 i, 2 o, 1 u, 6 a, 7 e, 8 i, 9 o; ours run 4 a, 5 e, 6 i, 7 o, 8 u. Its doubling mark is a
  raised ring; ours is a bar. Brice applied his key to this letter and reported it does not read it.
- ercole1559 (Bentivoglio, WAINUT): one-to-one alphabet of 20 signs, no raised vowels. Different.
- ercole1544 (Ercole Gonzaga, WAINUT), capilupi1545, capilupi1546, bresegno1548, francescoeste1546: other courts or
  other tables (homophonic alphabets, digit codes, Spanish syllabary with figures 2-6). Different.
