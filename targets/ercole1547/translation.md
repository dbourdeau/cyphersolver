## c. 16v, cipher lines 1 to 8

Since you will hear what has newly happened in Genoa, the matter being* of the great importance* that it is, we want you to [?icciate] (make?) every effort to learn as much as you can, both about* the causes of such a rising [motiuo], and also how [?L4s] means to punish the presumption* of [?quesot] [?delill] [?sot] [?] [?rit] [?diare] (perhaps "... and to remedy") the affairs of that city; which, being* the ladder of Spain into Italy*, it is to be believed that [?L4s] will do everything not to let it fall into the hands of the French*. [?]oi* (You?) who use* extraordinary* diligence* to [?arirui] (inform yourself?) fully of everything.
