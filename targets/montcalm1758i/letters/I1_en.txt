The news from England reports the taking of Île Royale [Cape Breton, Louisbourg] so positively that I thought I should send a corvette to Canada at once, despite the late season, to let MM. de Vaudreuil and Bigot know my observations on this unfortunate event. His Majesty expects that you will spare nothing to join with them in keeping up the zeal and ardour of his troops and of the inhabitants in this circumstance, and to make his enemies' undertakings useless by your prudence and firmness in carrying out the measures that will have been agreed for the defence and safety of the colony; for my part, I shall see to every means of helping it as far as circumstances allow.
