# 9 March 1865 — railway equipment, provisional reconstruction

Meridian. Brig Gen D.W.Adams, Montevallo, Alabama.

Your two ciphers received. Do not desire to MOVE ROLLING STOCK now unless
there are MORE CARS [?IN] MOBILE and [?MONTGOMERY] [?ROAD] than needed for
current [?TRANSPORTATION]. You should be prepared however to save as much
of this ROLLING STOCK as possible at last moment, particularly SERVICEABLE
[?ENGINES]. As Lieut Col Levy is in Montgomery, will direct him to confer
confidentially with [?SUPT] OF R.R. as to propriety of MOVING NOW such [?CARS]
as are NOT NEEDED for DAILY [?USE]. It might be done ON PRETEXT OF
helping Georgia Rail Roads.

By order of Lieut Genl Taylor, W.F.Bullock Jr., Assistant Adjutant General.
