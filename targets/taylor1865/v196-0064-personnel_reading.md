# 13 March 1865 — proposed replacement of Brandon by Tucker

Meridian. Secretary of War, Richmond.

I think the interests of the service require the REMOVAL OF BRIG GEN BRANDON
[?COMMANDING] MISS [?RESERVES]. The infirmities of [?EXTREME] [?OLD] AGE and
severe wounds render him very inefficient. Brig Gen TUCKER, a Mississippian,
has lost use of arm in service, is now here without assignment and would be
an efficient commander of [?RESERVES].

R. Taylor, Lieut. General.
