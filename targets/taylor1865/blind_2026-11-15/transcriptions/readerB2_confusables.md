# Capitals the clerks write alike

There are three hands on these frames. The 1865 April block-capital clerk wrote v11_0039, v11_0044, v11_0045 and the second telegram on v11_0046. A copperplate (flowing capitals) clerk wrote the first telegram on v11_0046. A neater block-capital clerk wrote v10_0056.

## Block-capital hands (v11_0039, v11_0044, v11_0045, v11_0046 telegram 2, v10_0056)

| Pair | How they differ (when they differ) | Example group, frame |
|---|---|---|
| C / G | C is an open curve with a small hook at the top right (looks like "Cr"). G is a C with an inner bar or E-like stroke. I read the hooked form as C throughout. | XBJXZC and OCPK (v11_0039); OCHI (v11_0044); G in UGEQ (v11_0044) |
| G / C+E | The G's inner stroke can look like a whole small E, so G can be misread as two letters, CE. | VV[G/CE]XZDFX (v11_0039) |
| E / F | Both have a top bar ending in a hook and a short middle tick. E has a bottom bar running right from the stem. F has at most a short foot serif. A faint foot is easy to take for a bar. | FED[F/E]X (v11_0044); [E/F]WP and MEFM (v10_0056) |
| M / N | M is written /\/ plus a short extra down-stroke, often with a dot. N lacks the extra stroke but often has a hooked top-right, which can look like that extra stroke. | W[M/N][M/N]F (v11_0039); SM (v11_0044) |
| L / Z | L has a top serif, a slanting stem and a foot, often with an extra short stroke or comma at the right. Z has a full top bar, a diagonal and a bottom bar, sometimes with a comma. | [L/Z]IM (v11_0039); [L/Z][Y/I]WVEW (v11_0045); CGEJX[L/Z] (v10_0056) |
| L / H | The L form with an extra short stroke at the right resembles a small H. | Q[L/H]WM (v11_0044) |
| I / T | I has top and bottom bars. T has a top bar, often curled or blobbed, and only a small foot. | W[I/T] (v11_0045); Y[T/I]AK (v10_0056) |
| I / J | J has a loop at the lower left, otherwise like I. | XBJXZC vs PCDXYIKZ (v11_0039) |
| H / A | In the v10_0056 hand, H has a flat top bar and parallel legs, so it looks like a flat-topped A. A in that hand is a script A. | L[H/A]VEQVCZ (v10_0056); LFG[H/A] (v11_0045) |
| Y / I | Y is an I-like stem with an arm or descender. A light stroke crossing an I can make it look like a Y. | [L/Z][Y/I]WVEW (v11_0045) |
| Q / O | Q is an O with a tail. When the tail is drawn inside the bowl, it can look like an O. | [Q/O]Y (v11_0046) |
| D / B / P | The D bowl is open at the bottom. P can look like D when its bowl reaches the baseline. | PQZY, DP (v11_0044) |

## Copperplate hand (v11_0046 telegram 1)

| Pair | Note | Example group |
|---|---|---|
| Q / Z | Both are shaped like a "2" with a loop. Z drops a loop below the line; Q has only a short tail flick. | QB[Q/Z]; FVXZ... (Z with descender) |
| U / V / N | V and U begin with the same looped single stroke. V ends with a hairline rising to the top; U has a second down-stroke; N adds a hairline that comes back down to the baseline. | XC[V/U]; BSAB[V/U]G; YO[V/U]N |
| F / T / A | F and T are a loop at the lower left, a heavy diagonal and a cap scroll; F also has a crossbar. A is an oval plus a heavy stroke, sometimes with a crossbar. | JB[F/T]XA; Y[F/A]Z; UB[F/A] |
| I / J / Y | These are big looped stems. J and Y descend below the line; I stays on it. | [Y/J][I/J]; [V/U]ASUQ[I/J]E |
| C+L / D | A small C followed by a tall narrow loop looks like a lowercase d. It may be one letter (D?) or two (C, L). | CLY; GA[N/V]EC[F/T]CL[E/C] |
| N / H | A heavy stem with a looped top and an arch. | S[N/H]; YN[A/H] |
