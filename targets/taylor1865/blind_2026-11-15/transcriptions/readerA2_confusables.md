# Capitals this clerk writes alike

Letter-forms are the clerk's flowing "copperplate" capitals with an underline/base stroke on many letters. Pairs that look alike in these frames:

| pair | how they are confused | example group (frame) |
|---|---|---|
| E / F | Both have a crossed upright and a tick at the top right; E is told only by a base stroke joined to the upright, F by a separate under-stroke. Underlining makes them nearly the same. | `[F/E][E/F]JJFX` (v196_0065); `Q[E/F]FBCM` (v196_0071); `KI[E/F]VOSUIPWP` (v196_0061) |
| I / T | I is written with a top bar and a base bar, so it looks like T; T's bar is only slightly longer. | `Q[J/T]VVZCDC`, `QRNVID[I/T]` (v196_0053); `WIDOY[T/I]M` (v196_0061) |
| J / T | J has a top bar and a hooked foot to the left; when the foot is faint it reads as T. | `[J/T]N` (v196_0053) |
| I / L | L is an I-form with a short foot turned right and usually a comma-tick after it; without the tick it looks like I. | `KUI[I/L]N` (v196_0061); `V[I/L]` (v196_0053) |
| C / G | G is C with a small inner bar or dot; when the inner mark is faint, G reads as C. | `A[C/G]` (v196_0053) |
| M / N | N is drawn with a long base stroke like the last stroke of M. | `DC[M/N]ID` (v196_0053); `QDUN[N/M]` (v196_0061) |
| H / Z | Script-style H with crossed bar is drawn like Z with a crossbar. | `Y[H/Z]MIO` (v196_0053) |
| Z / P | Z with a crossed descending tail resembles P. | `QG[Z/P]QR` (v196_0053) |
| P / I | P's bowl can be very small, leaving a slanted upright like I. | `[P/I]WV` (v196_0061) |
| B / E | A B with an open lower bowl and tick looks like E. | `NJYE` final letter (v196_0065) |
| T / F | T with a heavy top bar resembles F. | `TCXPZRZ` (v196_0056) |

Notes on marks that are not letters: the clerk often puts a comma-like tick after E, L, I or Z (e.g. `E,` `L,`) and dots between repeated letters (`K V.V.V.`, `VW` as `V.W.`). These are transcribed as nothing; they are noted in the transcription.
