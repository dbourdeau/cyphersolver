# Finance telegram — source located, partial alignment only

NARA RG109 chapter II vol196 frame0022 manuscript p38, Taylor to Secretary
of War, Richmond. [Record/images](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/45485022).

The manuscript date reads **13 February1865** on the enlarged image.
The matching complete printed telegram is dated **14 February1865**,
received23rd, in OR I/49/1 pp978–979:
https://archive.org/details/warofrebellion014901rootrich/page/978/mode/1up

It warns that unless current expenses are met the department cannot be held;
credit is exhausted, railroads, steamboats, citizens and soldiers will no
longer work/sell/fight, and cotton sales should finance expenses, particularly
if Richmond communication is cut. The printed text supplies the entire
sense; this is not a new plaintext discovery.

Partial image transcription with known COME RETRIBUTION strongly matches
the first~150 cipher letters. The W-like sign in the group before COUNTRY
must be segmented as **VV**, producing VVQ = THE; treating it as single W
shifts the later key phase. Native serif ambiguities (barredE/F,I/J,M/N,etc.)
remain throughout. No full ledger transcription or complete new alignment
is claimed, and this pointer is outside the six-document Nov1 alignment.
