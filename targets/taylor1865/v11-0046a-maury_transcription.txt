Meridian April 11th 1865
Maj Genl Maury
Mobile Ala
- QBQ - SH -
- XAV - BSHBVG - from Mobile last night
brought BJN - MC - GAVEFCE - CY -
- HFVXAFJ - HVXZCJN - YHF -
- ZF - UASUQJE - and quite a number of
- JBFXA - CS - ECFXFR - YFZ - UBX -
- YCVN - FAEU - GHDXBHR -
Have you seen Mr ... order him to
run the trains according to your requirements.
E Surget AAG
