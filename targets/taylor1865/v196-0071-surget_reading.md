# 17 March 1865 — stores and an attributed New Orleans report

Meridian. Lieut Genl Taylor, care Capt Graham, Post Quartermaster, Selma.

Col Rains has been placed in charge of all arsenals South. He directs officers
at Demopolis to move his stores to Macon, Ga. Constant and ready access to
these stores is continually required and there is no surplus. Have therefore
directed Capt Bryce to reply that you do not deem the removal expedient.
Understand Col Moore is moving stores, perhaps under same orders.

Genl Maury received message yesterday from Orleans that ENEMY WILL NOT ATTACK
[?MOBILE] DIRECTLY BUT [?COMPEL] [?EVACUATION] BY OCCUPATION OF [?SELMA] and
[?MONTGOMERY]. He suggests that freest use be given citizens of steamboats and
railroads to throw provisions into the city.

E. Surget, Assistant Adjutant General.
