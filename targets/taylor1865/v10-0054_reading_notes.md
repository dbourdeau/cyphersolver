NARA RG109 chapter II vol10 frame0054 manuscript p102, Taylor to Davis.

Method: existing decipherment checked with known COMPLETE VICTORY. The
candidate image transcription was aided by that print and key. It is not an
independent plaintext recovery or blind test. All213 cipher letters follow
one phase from offset0;190 candidate positions agree with the proposed text.
Every discrepancy remains visible in nov9_alignment.tsv; reconstruction is
not raw evidence. INADEQUATE in particular has several uncertain glyphs.

Differences/segmentation:

- GZHWC gives **RODDY** exactly. Printed **RODDEY** inserts an E, which would
  shift the rest of the manuscript alignment. Preserve the manuscript spelling.
- HISOWN's AWJMYB was initially oversegmented as AVJJMVB; the W-like joining
  strokes are read as one W. This is an aided glyph interpretation.
- The first V-like sign of VVAHP (THOSE) contains two V strokes; one was
  initially missed. Retaining both restores the later continuous phase.
- THIRTY THOUSAND and THREE THOUSAND are cipher letter words, not clear
  numerals; the print instead uses30,000 and3,000.
- GARRISON spans GARRIS / ON across a line. QG is two letters, not the earlier
  false QCE. FORME has a final M, initially mis-segmented JN.
- The clear opening reference appears to say **11th inst.** on a fresh crop,
  while the printed edition says **the9th**. It remains bracketed rather than
  silently importing the printed date. This date-reference disagreement does
  not affect the cipher stream or the15January message date.

The corresponding *_cipher.txt preserves the revised working glyph candidates,
not the exact unsegmented shapes. Nov9 JSON notes the phase-sensitive revisions.
Independent handwriting review is still needed. The earlier prior-print
