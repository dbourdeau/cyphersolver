# Vol. 196 fr. 0031-0032, MS pp. 57-58: Taylor (W. F. Bullock Jr., AAG) to Brig. Gen. D. W. Adams, Selma or Montevallo, Meridian, 21 Feb 1865

> All public stores and property IN NORTH ALA[BAMA] LIKELY TO FALL INTO ENEMY'S HANDS IN CASE OF MOVEMENT FROM TENNE[SSEE]
> RIVER should either be placed IN FORTIFIED PLACES OR SOUTH OF ALA[BAMA] RIVER. You must without awaiting further orders
> from here send CAHABA PRISONERS TO ANDERSONVILLE immediately on first receipt of accurate information of ENEMY'S ADVANCE
> TOWARDS CENTRAL ALA[BAMA]. IF GOV[ERN]M[EN]T EMPLOYEES AT DEMOPOLIS have not already been organized and drilled for local
> defense, send competent officers there immediately to organize and drill them. This can be done without DEPRIVING
> WORKSHOPS of any great part of their labor.
>
> Engineer negroes have been ordered back from Georgia to Central Alabama ... to meet emergency.
> By order of Lieut. Genl. Taylor: W. F. Bullock Jr., A. A. G.
