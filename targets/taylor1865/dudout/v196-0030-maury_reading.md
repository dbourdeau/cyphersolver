# Vol. 196 fr. 0030, MS p. 55: Taylor (W. F. Bullock Jr., AAG) to Maj. Gen. D. H. Maury, Mobile, Meridian, 21 Feb 1865

> Latest information indicates that enemy has gone down the river from Vicksburgh without avowed intention OF ATTACKING
> MOBILE. It is now of the highest importance to develope what the enemy is DOING AT PENSACOLA. If you cant otherwise
> ascertain with certainty, you must have [a] FORCED RECONNAISANCE MADE AT ONCE to develope real condition of affairs
> there. If there no longer exists probability of enemy attacking from DIRECTION OF PENSACOLA, most of the CAVALRY YOU
> NOW HAVE ON EASTERN SHORE should be placed on SELMA AND MONTGOMERY LINE to repel anticipated movement AGAINST CENTRAL
> ALABAMA.
>
> By order of Lieut. Genl. Taylor: W. F. Bullock Jr., A. A. G.
