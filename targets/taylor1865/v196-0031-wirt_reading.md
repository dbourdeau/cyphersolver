# 21 February 1865 — instructions to Wirt Adams (provisional)

Should the enemy move from [?BATON] ROUGE or other point BELOW VICKSBURG
in direction of [?M & O R R] you will as rapidly as possible concentrate
all your force in his front, FALL BACK SLOWLY impeding his advance as much
as possible, and if finally forced back take position ON M and O R R
NORTH OF where the enemy WILL STRIKE IT, so as to keep in communication
with these and Genl Forrest's Head Quarters.
Should the enemy come out from [?VICKSBURG] ALSO it will of course be
necessary to divide your troops so as to keep a force in front of each
of enemy's columns. In any event you will keep in constant communication
with these Head Quarters. These instructions are subject to such
modifications as Major Genl Forrest may direct.
By order of LieutGenl Taylor, W.F.Bullock Jr, AAG.
