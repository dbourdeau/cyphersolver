Meridian March 9th 1865
Lieut Col Wm M Levy A A Genl
care Post Comdt Montgomery Ala who will find Col Levy and deliver this -
" The Lieut Genl Comdg under-
stands there are one hundred & fifty five - EODH - or - XS
UMGM - and - [O/C]HBKEQAQGJ - V - K - and thinks
such of them as are not needed for daily use should
immediately be - XMIPLTVPTSP - DY - XKYXSU - to -
- PWUC - JJMVP - EM - GJTW[F/E]PLQI[O/C] - he wishes
you to confer confidentially with - EJAIKMIBGGRVLV -
CR - G - C - and suggest propriety of doing this.
It might be done - YGHZZ - RKSKCPGQ - DQ - ELWDA
VBBX - PQOPH - in Georgia
W F Bullock Jr AAGenl

# Where the four blind readings differ (letters not listed agree in all four):
# - UMGM (A1, verifier, helper) / UNGN (A2): this clerk's M is an N with a long base stroke; A2 read N in 4 places.
# - XMIPLTVPTSP: 5th letter L (A1, A2, verifier) / Z (helper). ELWDA: L (A1, verifier) / Z (helper) / I (A2).
#   This clerk's L and Z differ only by a small tail; the key needs L in both (TRANSFERRED, ASSISTING) and Z in YGHZZ.
# - GJTW[F/E]PLQIO: 5th letter F or E (A1 F/E, verifier E/F, A2 and helper E); COLUMBUS needs F.
