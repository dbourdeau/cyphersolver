# 8 March 1865 — safeguarding boats and railway equipment

Meridian. Brig Gen D.W. Adams, Montevallo or Selma, Alabama.

AS ENEMY may soon move on [?MONTGOMERY] OR SELMA, you should make arrangements
TO INSURE SAFETY OF STEAM BOATS and the ROLLING STOCK ON RAIL [?ROADS]. Some
[?STEAMBOATS] will probably not SHUN [?CAPTURE]. You will therefore have to
watch them closely and arrange to PREVENT [?THEIR] CAPTURE.

If railroad [?MACHINERY] cannot be [?MOVED] OFF towards [?COLUMBUS] GA, you
must see that some indispensable part of [?EACH ENGINE; local key phase differs] is taken away so as to
prevent [?ENEMY] [?USING] THEM. Answer and explain what arrangements you have made.

By order of Lieut Genl Taylor, W.F. Bullock Jr., Assistant Adjutant General.
