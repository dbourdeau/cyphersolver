# 21 February 1865 — instructions to Wirt Adams (provisional)

NARA RG109 chapter II vol196 frame0031, left manuscript p56.
Meridian, 21 February1865, BrigGen Wirt Adams, Jackson, Mississippi.
[Record and images](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/45485022).

Cipher-derived text capitalized; uncertain proposals have question marks:

> Should the enemy move from [?BATON] ROUGE or other point BELOW VICKSBURG
> in direction of [?M & O R R] you will as rapidly as possible concentrate
> all your force in his front, FALL BACK SLOWLY impeding his advance as much
> as possible, and if finally forced back take position ON M and O R R
> NORTH OF where the enemy WILL STRIKE IT, so as to keep in communication
> with these and Genl Forrest's Head Quarters.
>
> Should the enemy come out from [?VICKSBURG] ALSO it will of course be
> necessary to divide your troops so as to keep a force in front of each
> of enemy's columns. In any event you will keep in constant communication
> with these Head Quarters. These instructions are subject to such
> modifications as Major Genl Forrest may direct.
>
> By order of LieutGenl Taylor, W.F.Bullock Jr, AAG.

Method: known COMPLETE VICTORY applied; continuous phase through cipher
letters only. Clear "and" between M and O R R does not consume the key.
Do not expand the uncertain railroad abbreviation as a certain reading.
80 cipher letters; raw candidates and every proposed correction are in the
Nov1 alignment. No complete-reading measurement or novelty claim is made.
Bounded normalized OR I/49/1 searches did not find this exact telegram.

This is direct, recipient-specific evidence of COMPLETE VICTORY in the
Wirt Adams channel on21 February, before the9 March order to adopt COME
RETRIBUTION. It fits contemporaneous use of different keys in different
channels; February4 Taylor–Davis already uses COME RETRIBUTION.
