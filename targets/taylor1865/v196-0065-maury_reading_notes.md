# 13 March relief telegram — provisional known-key reading

NARA RG109 chapter II vol196 frame0065 manuscript p125, first telegram.
Bullock for Taylor to D.H. Maury, Mobile. Source URL in nov9_alignment.json.

COME RETRIBUTION reads all87 candidate letters continuously.80 match the
proposed reconstruction. The first group is five-letter TSMHP=READY, not
an earlier six-letter TSNEPP interpretation. Native crop separates M/H strokes.
FIELD spans FIEL/D across a line; TROOPS is written TR/OOPS across cipher groups.

Several remaining single-glyph corrections are contextual and marked in the
prose, notably FOR,EIGHT,THOMAS,ON,NORTH,RELIEVING,OF. THEM is the exact raw
reading, referring to the troops. Do not change it to THEN.

The message reports an intention/expectation, not proof that eight thousand
men actually became available or that Mobile was relieved. No complete prior
plaintext was found in bounded OR45/2,47/2,49/1,49/2,52/2 or exact web phrase
