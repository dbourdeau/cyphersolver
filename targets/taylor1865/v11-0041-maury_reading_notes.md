# 8 April 1865 — readiness of Col.H.Maury's regiment and Richmond news

NARA RG109 chapter II vol11 frame0041 manuscript p79, first telegram.
Surget to MajorGeneral D.H.Maury, Mobile, Meridian8 April 1865.
[Record and images](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/12127054).
Existing printed plaintext: OR I/49/2 p1216,
[volume](https://archive.org/details/warofrebellion014902rootrich/page/1216/mode/1up).

Cipher-derived words capitalized; last place-name has unresolved segmentation:

> Genl Taylor directs in case of necessity to move Col.H.Maury's Regiment up
> by rail to re-inforce Wirt Adams. Will it be in readiness to move at a
> moment's notice? VICKSBURG PAPERS OF YESTERDAY REPORT CAPTURE OF RICHMOND
> ON THE THIRD. LEE MOVING TOWARDS [?DANVILLE].
>
> E.Surget, AsstAdjtGenl.

COME RETRIBUTION, continuous through cipher letters only, no restart.
82 raw candidate letters versus83 reconstructed. Last FOZZZPY produces
DANVILF in the raw decryption; the printed DANVILLE supports FOZZZPEV, with
Y-like final shape proposed as EV. This segmentation is explicitly provisional.
The manuscript TOWARDS differs from the printed TOWARD by final S; forcing
printed TOWARD would wrongly disrupt the key phase at the last word.

YESTERDAY is split across lines, with RDAYREPORT joined in the cipher.
RICHMOND and MOVING also span lines. The full reading was obtained first
with the known key, then located in OR by Vicksburg papers; this is an
existing decipherment checked, not a newly unread plaintext or independent
ciphertext-only break. No other cipher was identified on the frame in the
visual inspection; "Give reason in cypher" in the following telegram is a
request for a reply, not an encoded passage in that telegram.
