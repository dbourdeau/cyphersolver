# 17 March Surget telegram — provisional known-key reading

NARA RG109 chapter II vol196 frame0071 manuscript p136, second telegram.
Surget to Taylor, Selma. Source URL in nov9_alignment.json.

The cipher begins only after the long clear paragraph about Col Rains's stores.
COME RETRIBUTION starts at offset0 with ENEMY WILL NOT ATTACK and continues
through the report about Mobile, Selma and Montgomery. The report is attributed
to a message Maury received from New Orleans; it is not asserted here as an
accurate account of Union plans.

A fresh crop has five-letter WXCNB, not the false six-letter WXCCNB. SELMA
still requires the N/U glyph correction. MONTGO is exact. Its continuation
has raw AVP3 versus proposed MERY4/AQVP4; the joined AQ hypothesis is not
established on the image and Montgomery remains marked uncertain. Other
uncertain words are also marked. The total raw stream79 versus reconstructed80
letters reflects this explicit final-name discrepancy.

No complete prior plaintext located in bounded OR49/1,49/2,52/2,47/2 and web
phrase searches; not proof of nonpublication. Overall collection in progress.
