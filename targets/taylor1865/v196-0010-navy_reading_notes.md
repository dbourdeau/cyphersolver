# 4 February1865 — Taylor to Davis, Navy at Mobile

NARA RG109 chapter II vol196 frame0010 manuscript p15, Meridian4 February 1865.
Taylor to President Davis,Richmond,Virginia.
[Record and images](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/45485022).
Existing printed text: OR I/49/1 p955,
[volume](https://archive.org/details/warofrebellion014901rootrich/page/955/mode/1up).
Date checked on manuscript; printed OCR date is unreliable.

Cipher-derived words capitalized. The garbled vessel word remains marked:

> The Navy AT MOBILE IS A FARCE. ITS [?VESSELS] ARE CONTINUALLY TIED UP AT
> THE WHARF NEVER CO-OPERATING WITH THE ARMY. The payment OF ITS EXPENSES
> IS A WASTE OF MONEY. I send by mail a communication giving my reasons for
> these expressions.
>
> R.Taylor,LieutGeneral.

COME RETRIBUTION through112 cipher letters.107/112 raw candidate positions
match the reconstruction, counting a speculative eight-letter VESSELLS for
the garbled MILJTMEL group. Do not treat VESSELLS as an established spelling:
the printed edition supplies VESSELS, seven letters, while the manuscript's
group has eight. Raw decryption gives VESSLLKS. Other candidates retain E/H,
T/Z,V/B glyph ambiguities in AT,CONTINUALLY,WHARF.

A narrow I in UYIFPG was missed initially, then separated on a fresh enlarged
crop. It gives A FARCE exactly; no inserted cipher letter is required.
FTXIIUCGO gives OPERATING exactly. The manuscript's "never co-operating"
differs from printed "never in co-operation"; no omitted IN or incorrect
final pair of cipher letters need be assumed. MONEY spans YS/EIR across lines.

Method: existing decipherment checked with known key. The long continuous
matching stream supports COME RETRIBUTION use in this channel on4 February,
not a first-use date for the key in general.
