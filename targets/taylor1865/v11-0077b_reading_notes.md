# 20 April 1865 — equipment and movements

NARA RG109 chapter II vol11 frame0077 manuscript p149, second telegram.
Taylor to the Secretary of War, through MajGen Cobb, Columbus or Macon, Ga.
[Record and images](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/12127054).
Existing plaintext: OR I/49/2 p1255,
[volume](https://archive.org/details/warofrebellion014902rootrich/page/1255/mode/1up).

Cipher-derived words capitalized:

> Have completed EQUIPMENT OF MOBILE GARRISON FOR THE FIELD. NO INDICATION
> of any DECIDED MOVEMENT FROM MOBILE NOR FROM Mississippi or Tennessee
> Rivers. Former very high all its lowlands being under water.
>
> R.Taylor, Lieutenant-General.

Known COME RETRIBUTION runs through80 cipher letters, ignoring clear words.
The final FROM has raw candidate TDSF, giving FROO, versus reconstructed TDSD;
the last glyph is ambiguous D/F and the printed text supplies FROM. The
remaining79 letters agree directly after image transcription aided by the
known key and published reading. This is not an independent/blind test.

Crucial boundary: **of any** is clear, not cipher. Omitting these words from
key advancement restores continuous alignment after INDICATION. MOBILE is
split DWCC / EM across lines. There is no key restart. The earlier
`v11-0077b_prior-print.md` is preserved as a historical pointer; its claim that
the second cipher passage included OF is superseded by this image check.

No new-plaintext or ciphertext-only claim: **existing decipherment** checked
against the manuscript. This is a separate telegram from the repeated14 April
dispatch ending above it on the same manuscript page.
