Position agreement is a comparison of revised working glyph candidates with
known-key reconstructions. It is **not** a blind solver accuracy score or the
fraction of prose read. The candidates were aided by keys, context, printed
plaintext and, on 8 March, an existing partial pencil decipherment. Every
correction and unequal-length group remains visible in `nov9_alignment.tsv`.
The script also prints the uncorrected uninterrupted raw decryption.

keyword instruction. It fits COMPLETE VICTORY while the 8 March Brent message

it does not establish a universal switch. The9 March Adams follow-up on

decipherment. Relative times of this dispatch and the keyword instruction

## Unsuccessful work and limits

Revisited the difficult11 April lower telegram, vol11 fr0046 p88. Tested five
known keys, all phases, reversed keys, Vigenere/Beaufort/variant operations,
and all 26 constant shifts with the existing shared English fourgram model.
Same-length synthetic convention controls recovered their intended plaintext;
no coherent real plaintext was found. All real outputs were rejected.
These tests do not model unknown letter joins/omissions and do not establish a
new key or cipher system. See `nov9_apr11_conventions_results.txt`.

found no exact full counterpart for the new March candidates. Search coverage
varied by message (see reading notes); OCR can miss matches. No claim of
unpublished or newly discovered plaintext follows. The January and finance
printed sources were located in the previous session, before these alignments.

Remaining April gaps, the provisional18 January message, other February/March
