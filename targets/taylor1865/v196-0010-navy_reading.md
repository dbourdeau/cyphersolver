# 4 February 1865 — Taylor to Davis, Navy at Mobile

The Navy AT MOBILE IS A FARCE. ITS [?VESSELS] ARE CONTINUALLY TIED UP AT
THE WHARF; NEVER CO-OPERATING WITH THE ARMY. The payment OF ITS EXPENSES
IS A WASTE OF MONEY. I send by mail a communication giving my reasons for
these expressions.

R. Taylor, Lieut. General.
