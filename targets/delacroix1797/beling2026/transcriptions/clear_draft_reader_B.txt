Minute: the Minister of External Relations to Letombe, consul at Philadelphia, Paris 25 messidor an 5 (13 July 1797)
Conventions: long s rendered s; superscript letters as ^x; [word?] = uncertain; [...] = unreadable;
[letters] in square brackets without ? = letters hidden in the binding gutter, supplied; <del>/<ins> as briefed.

(Citoyen, Consul, Celui, Vous, Votre ...). Initial s is always the tall form and is recorded lowercase.
