[...] By letters from Dandino [Girolamo Dandino, Papal Nuncio in France] we are advised [?that] [?the] said Protonotary is coming to sound out His Holiness regarding the league, to press urgently for the promotion of [87] of Reims [Charles de Guise] to the [cardinalate], and for the extension of the authority to [confer] churches, monasteries, and priories which the late King [Francis I] held in the person of the King [Henri II].

[80] His Holiness is steadily regaining his strength; although he is not yet entirely free of his catarrh, we shall nevertheless continue [?to] keep him without audiences ⟨and give him as little trouble as possible⟩ on account of business, or to preserve him all the longer ⟨for the public good, and our consolation⟩.

## Historical context

Independent published dispatches in M. Comelli (ed.), *Corrispondenza Giovanni Della Casa – Alessandro Farnese (1546–1547)*, vol. II (2022), pp. 475–496 (Letters 333, 335, 337–341) corroborate four distinct threads of this text:
- **9 July 1547 (Letter 333):** Lancelot de Carle (*il Protonotario Carlo*) in Venice proposing an anti-Imperial defensive league (*lega difensiva*) involving France, Venice, and the Swiss, met by Venetian neutrality.
- **16 July 1547 (Letter 335):** Carle's southbound route from Venice through Ferrara (`68̄`) and Piacenza to Rome.
- **23 July 1547:** Pope Paul III's recovery from catarrh (*cataro*) while being shielded from audiences.
- **27–30 July 1547 (Letter 341):** Creation of Charles de Guise, Archbishop of Reims (*de Riens*), as cardinal on 27 July 1547, Carle's arrival in Rome, and preparations to send Michele della Torre, Bishop of Ceneda, to France to replace the ailing Nuncio Girolamo Dandino (brief dated 20 Aug 1547).

These corroborations support **late July 1547** for the underlying Farnese letter assuming its news was current when written (shortly before Reims's 27 July promotion and Carle's arrival in Rome); the archival copy on `c. 78r` itself remains undated. The addressee (Pier Luigi Farnese) is supplied in parentheses by the published ASFi inventory (*Carteggio universale di Cosimo I de' Medici*, III, p. 103).
