# The Spanish enclosure of 14 March 1553

## Philip to Ferrante Gonzaga, 14 March 1553

The two earlier readings of the surviving Spanish enclosure often differ by a single pen stroke that changes the tense. Two further AI readers in separate contexts saw sixteen plain-hand snippets labeled A–P, without the cipher, proposed words, shelfmark or each other's answers. Their independent responses agree on the disputed internal **r** at 37r M and 37v A, I and K and 38r N. This supports the first reader's future forms at those five places, including “no se havran podido escusar” at 37v A and “ya su Magestad os havra escripto” at 38r N. The new readers differed over u/v and some word endings. At 37r G both give **subiria** with an i after the r, supporting the second transcript's conditional about the cost of replenishing the light horse. One reader also saw an accent. These are partial adjudications in the plain enclosure. Neither frozen reference was changed. On 38r, **H** is a struck word and **D** the replacement written above it. **B** occurs six lines later, after “En lo de las”. Both readers saw the strike and gave similar accounts of the middle of B and H. They differed over the terminal flourish and over the letters of the insertion. A diplomatic edition should preserve all three places. The two occurrences of the directional adjective in “a … partes” at 37r E and 38r J also remain unresolved by these readers. Both identified the conjunction at control F and Piemonte at control L up to the stipulated vowel uncertainty. The first reader gave the main word at C as an alternative, the second as “fuere”. The controls check the procedure on clear examples. The cipher-word count does not change.
