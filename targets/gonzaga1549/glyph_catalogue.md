# Glyph catalogue, Imperial Cipher, Estense series (13 May 1549, c.30r, against the c.29 slip)

**Superseded 2 Nov 2026 as a value table by `sign_table.tsv`. Kept as the record of how the values were first reached; its values, lines counts and `sign for sign` claims are not to be relied on.**

Made 2 Nov 2026, by eye. Method: the slip (`y penseys en este medio de donde y como se han de saber los dineros para la obra y tambien los materiales
porque se gane tiempo sin que por ninguna manera se haga demostracion por donde se comprenda ni imagine que sea tal nuestra intencion`, 117 syllable units)
is cut into units (consonant + vowel, lone vowels and lone consonants; `n` stands for `m` before b/p: tanbien, tienpo).
Cipher line 1 has 19 signs for 19 units, line 2 about 22 for 21, line 3 21 for 20: one sign per unit, so the alignment is read off sign by sign.
Masia's key (`ref/CharlesV3.gif`) names the shapes; where the key and this hand differ, the hand rules (for example `be` is not the key's 7).
Crops: `crops/c30z_*.jpg`, `crops/c30_l2r.jpg`, `crops/c30_l3.jpg`, `crops/c30_l4.jpg`.

## Passage 5, read
- **c.64v, after `ni se deve`**: `te-n-ta-r e-n ta-l ti-e-n-po` = `tentar en tal tiempo` (note: `tentar en tal tiempo`). One sign per unit, 12 signs for 12 units; the `ta` before `r` has two dots on my crop, one more check needed.
- **c.64v, after `elegido pontifice`** (4 lines): `pa-re-s-ci-e-re  o-tra  co-sa  y  di-e-re  o-ca-si-o-n  se po-dra  fa-ci-l  men-te  ha-ze-r`
  = `paresciere otra cosa y diere occasion se podra facilmente hazer` (note: `paresciere otra cosa y diere occon se podra facilm.te hazer`). Signs read:
  `[pa][re][mmo][s][ci][e][re]` `[o][tra][co][sa][y]` `[di][e][re][o][ca][si][o][n]` `[se][po][dra][fa][ci][l]` `[...][...]` `[ha][ze][r]`; `mmo` before `s` and the `men-te` signs are not fixed.
  The clear text round it: `... ni se deve tentar en tal tiempo, pues quando despues de elegido pontifice paresciere otra cosa y diere occasion se podra facilmente hazer.`

## c.76r / c.77 (23 Dec 1549), third sitting
c.77 is a clear continuation of the first cipher passage on c.76r: it starts with the clear words before the cipher (`La consideracion que hazeis`) and goes on
`sobre lo de Niça, siendo aquella plaça de la importancia que es y estando fuera della el Prior de Lombardia` (58 units; the cipher passage has 3 lines, about 52 signs).
Line 1 (12 signs) aligns unit for unit with `so b re lo de ni ca si e n do a`:
`[yo-shaped = so] [7 = b] [g with bar = re] [6o = lo] [x-o = de] [ring two dots = ni] [ring with dot above = ca] [j two dots = si] [w = e] [ap = n] [9. = do] [n = a]`.
This confirms de, ca, si, e, n, do, a, re from c.30r and c.64v and adds so, b, lo, ni. One conflict: the ring with two dots was read `di` in c.30r (medio) and is `ni` here;
the two may be one shape with a small difference, to be resolved on both pages. Line 2 begins `[c-slash = que] [L = ll] [n = a] ...` (que + ll + a = `aquella`), consistent with the key;
lines 2-3 are not yet aligned sign by sign (transcription by eye does not separate the signs reliably enough, 21 and 19 signs against 21 and 16 units).
The same subject (Prior of Lombardy, the castle of Nice) is the 30 Sep 1549 block on c.50r, so c.77's vocabulary is the crib for that block.

## c.76r passage 1 lines 2-3 and passage 2 line 1 (fourth sitting)
Reading against c.77 (`sobre lo de Niça, siendo aquella plaça de la importancia que es y estando fuera della el Prior de Lombardia`; `que se den al dicho Prior a cuenta de la dicha paga ...`).
- **Passage 1, line 2** (20 signs for 19 units, one extra sign): `[c-slash = que][L = ll][n = a][a-w = pla][6 and 8 dotted: ça, one extra][x-o = de][6 dot = la][ring-hook = i][a-tail = m][L-o = po][no = r][triangle dot = ta][ap = n][ring two dots = ci][n = a][c-slash-w = que][S = e][mo = s][loop-g = y]`
  = `aquella plaça de la importancia que es y`. Confirms que, ll, a, de, la, i, po, r, ta, n, a, s, y; adds pla, m (a plain `a`-shaped sign), ci (ring with two dots).
- **Passage 1, line 3** (about 22 signs for 24 units; the middle stretch `fuera della` has 7 signs for 7-8 units and is not separated): the ends align exactly:
  `[mo = s][triangle dot = ta][ap = n][9. = do]` (`estando`), then `[cw = e][-o = l][X two dots = pri][ap+ = o][no = r][x-o = de][6o = lo][a = m][7 dot = ba][mo/no = r][6 two dots = di][n = a]`
  = `el prior de lombardia`. The X with two dots is the key's own `pri` sign; `-o` is a lone `l`. `mo` and `no` look alike in this hand and are interchangeable for `r` here (r = no in c.30r, mo here).
- **Passage 2, line 1**: the clear text `que se den` precedes the cipher; the cipher begins `[n = a][-o = l] [ring two dots = di] [8][e-ring: cho, two signs for one syllable] [X two dots = pri][ap+ = o][no = r] [n = a] [8f = cu][cw = e][ap = n][triangle dot = ta] [x-o = de][6 = la] [ring two dots = di][8 = cha] ...`
  = `al dicho prior a cuenta de la dicha ...`. Same X-two-dots `pri`, same `l`, `de la`, `di`.
- Corrections: the ring with two dots is `di` here and `ci`/`ni` elsewhere; it is probably one drawn sign with three readings in my eye, not in the cipher (the key separates ci / di / ni by the base shape). To be fixed by measuring the base shapes, not the dots.

## 30 Sep 1549, c.50r block, read against its margin note (fifth sitting)
The clerk's margin note is almost the plaintext of the block (a summary that keeps the words in order): `y aunque yua con fin de tratar al Prior di Lombardia castillo de tal manera
que fuesse tanto como echarle de alli por meter dentro a Mos de Brissac`. The note has 71 units for about 105 signs, so the cipher says more than the note, mostly in lines 1-2.
- **Line 3**: `[ca = ring dot][s = mo][ti = triangle two dots][ll = L dot][o = ap+] [de = x-o] [ta = triangle dot][l = dash-o] [ma = loop-hook dot][ne = e-reversed cross][ra = g dot] [que = c-slash] ...`
  = `castillo de tal manera que ...`.
- **Line 4**: `[co = 8o][mo = l-o shape] [e][cha = 8][..][r = no][le = 6] [de = x-o][a = n][ll = L][i = ring-hook] [po = Lambda-o][r = no] [me][te]`
  = `como echarle de alli por meter`. New: cha (ring, as in `dicha` on c.76r), `mo` in the l-o shape (the `mo` of `como`).
- **Line 5**: `[de = x-o][n = ap][tro = bar with o] [a = n][mo = l-o shape][s = mo shape] [de = x-o] [bri ..]` = `dentro a mos de bri..`; `tro` is the key's own bar-with-o sign.
- **Line 1** begins `[y = loop-g][a = n][u = 9][n = ap][que = c-slash]` = `y aunque`, and about thirteen signs reach `de` (12 units in the note); the rest of lines 1-2 is not matched.
So the Prior of Lombardy is to be ordered to hold the castle of Nice, so that the Duke of Savoy's men cannot expel him and put Brissac in: it is the same business as c.77.
Values added or corrected: 9 = u (alone) beside 9. = do; `l-o` shape = mo; `mo` shape = s; tro; cha.
