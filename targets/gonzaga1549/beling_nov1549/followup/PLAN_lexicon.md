# Can separately transcribed clear letters distinguish the standalone cross?

18 November 2026. No new substitution value will be fitted. The previous test used an exact, same-document comparison of two 1547 crosses and missed. This exploratory cross-document test takes every standalone plus sign in the five extant 1553 Genoa working transcriptions (one to Vázquez de Molina and four to Philip). Those readings were made by two independent shape readers and an arbitrator; the local exported text does not retain each reader's individual vote. We have looked at sign names and counts, not compared neighboring signs against an outside glossary. No November 1549 term or key entry learned on that letter is used in constructing the glossary.

Glossary: all alphabetic tokens of six letters or more from the two 1547 Figueroa plain-copy excerpts, the Charles/Philip collection's five WAINUT clear text files, May 27 c.12r and the first March enclosure reading. Skip comments, provenance, editorial brackets, and tokenized line identifiers. Lowercase, strip accents, merge i/j and u/v. Keep each spelling together with witnesses; do not use modern dictionaries or edited readings. For each word, mask an internal substring of zero to three letters, keeping at least two exact consecutive letters on each side and at least five observed letters in all. For each cross seek whole dictionary words for which the complete left and right flanks match the adjacent mechanical K4 output on their respective sides. Join signs across line ends within a page, keep unknown signs as holes; do not cross a page boundary. Keep only the best total number of observed letters. Show every tie. Vote only if all best candidates put the same string in the gap. Count distinct glossary words supporting tt and ct. A spelling test passes only if one of these alternatives is supported by at least two distinct words in at least two separate Genoa documents, no opposite vote occurs, and the winner's number of distinct words exceeds the maximum of either spelling's count in each of 1,000 shuffled-key controls. Seed 1383351, shuffle emissions of observed nonempty, nonhole labels (other than the plus) among those same labels. Other strings, ambiguous matches and absent matches cannot support either alternative. This is fixed-table comparison to other contemporary spellings, not a matching copy or a transcription agreement of the 1549 target. Even a pass would be indirect evidence for another correspondent.
