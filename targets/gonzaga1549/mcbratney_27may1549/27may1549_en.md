# Charles V to Ferrante Gonzaga, Brussels, 27 May 1549: c.31r (cipher blocks B1-B3 in part), in English

Working translation of the contemporary decipherment (c.12r), not checked by a second reader. Clear passages of the letter in *italics*.

*As to* the stirrings and preparations the French are making in Piedmont, and the insolences they still keep up against our
vassals and those of the Duke of Savoy, *it is very well to have the consideration and care you speak of, so as* to forestall
their designs and moves. But from all that can be learned of the state of their affairs, the little money they are known to
have, and fewer men of war, and besides that their being entangled with the English, it is not seen that they have any aim or
means of moving or doing anything towards this side; rather, they fear that trouble will be given them from ours.
*As to the rest, touching* the novelties they keep making, it will be well for you to advise our ambassador in France continually
of what passes in this, and of the effort and representations he should make; giving at the same time to understand to the
King's lieutenant in Turin that he should remedy what has been and shall be innovated against the treaties, because otherwise it
would not be [tolerable ... continues on c.31v].
