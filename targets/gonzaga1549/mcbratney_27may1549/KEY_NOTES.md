# 27 May 1549 letter (Charles V to Ferrante Gonzaga, Brussels): notes for the gonzaga1549 key

Contributed by Malcolm McBratney (Brisbane), working with Claude (Anthropic). Decoded, not read: c.31r only (22 of 184 cipher lines), first pass 68.7% of letters and 64.1% of units against the contemporary decipherment (cc. 12-15), under our own 65% line. Nothing here is emended; the key used is this folder's `sign_table.tsv`, unchanged (copy in `key_cv1549.tsv`, frozen, see FREEZE.txt).

The reason to file it now: comparing the decode with the contemporary decipherment shows consistent disagreements with the sign table that may help the other letters in this folder. They are findings for the key, not emendations.

1. V with a dot: table pa (V-base row); decipherment da (5) or ta (2) at all 7. V with two dots: table pi; decipherment di (2), ti (1).
2. Bare 6: table a; decipherment i at 4 of 10.
3. "ro"/"no" sign: table r; decipherment o at all 5 (label or table).
4. x with loop ("fish"): de agrees 18 times; decipherment no 6 times (two signs probably lumped under one label).
5. y with tail to the right: table se; decipherment su (2), so (1).
6. 3 with a dot to the right (no value in the table): o at all 3.

A second, separate pass on cc. 31v-36v (162 lines) with a key rebuilt mechanically from sign_table.tsv only scored 38.71% of units (998/2,578), far below the line, and is not filed. Its judge's evidence for missing or wrong table values [unverified, from that pass]: 9 = s, XC = de, BALL = que, 8T = y, x = no, EH+ = me, EH^ = ma, CW = lo; e^ ha (table lo), u: di (ci), 3+ ne (je), u^ da (ca), EHo mo (ho), a+ n (o). Sign naming there may differ from this folder's labels.

The clear dating line on c.36r reads 'De Brussellas @ xxvij de Mayo. M D xLviiij' = 27 May 1549, agreeing with the decipherment heading.
