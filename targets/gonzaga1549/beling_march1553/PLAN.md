# March 1553 retrospective calibration of the WAINUT reader’s sign labels

The historical question is whether repeatable reader-specific label assignments improve WAINUT's single visual transcript of March 1553. The contemporary Spanish enclosure has already been transcribed. The six boundaries were set in the previous collation after viewing the texts. This is a new training/holdout comparison on a known document. It cannot prove how the key itself was written, how a second reader sees the signs, or recover a sentence independently.

Use the repository's unedited B-01 to B-64 token stream, its blind_name_map.json and key.tsv. Remove trailing uncertainty and braces exactly as its blind decoder does. Map each raw label to the normalized inherited emission, use the existing fallback to the raw label, and retain one unmatchable character for every unvalued or bracketed emission (including ink blots). Suppress only the signs WAINUT treats as canceled. Preserve raw label identity for fitting, including when two labels have the same baseline emission. Do not reread cipher images or relabel a sign. Normalize letters to lowercase, drop accents, whitespace and punctuation, equate v/u and j/i in both texts. Include all text from the fixed transcript in both conditions, including formulae. Report an extra check with the frequent formula “su magestad” removed under both models from both witnesses. No search of paragraph boundaries, word segmentation, or local exclusions.

Fit paragraphs 1–3 only (B-01–15, B-16–21, B-22–40). Test paragraphs 4–6 only (B-41–47, B-48–55, B-56–64). Never transfer into October from this experiment. No cipher word can be counted from this one-pass transcript. No new value will be called independently verified by this experiment.

Use ordinary unit-cost global Levenshtein distance, with cost one even for a missing letter and all positions retained. For each raw label in descending training frequency, breaking ties lexically, try its inherited value, every normalized unbracketed value of length 1–3 in the existing table, and each single lowercase letter (normalized). Exclude the literal blot or anonymous unknown, canceled labels, brace-delimited code groups, and any already valued above three letters. Require five training occurrences in at least two paragraphs. Make at most three coordinate sweeps in fixed order. Change a current emission only when it cuts total training distance by at least three edits, improves at least two training paragraphs and worsens none. Choose greatest total gain among eligible candidates, break ties in favor of the inherited value, then lexically. Keep the current emission on a tie with it. No fitting controls are selected from test evidence.

Primary statistic is held-out aggregate character error rate (total edits divided by the fixed total count of Spanish letters). Compare with the identically processed inherited baseline. Pass only if the fitted table lowers aggregate CER by at least 0.025, lowers CER in at least two held-out paragraphs, worsens none by over 0.02, and meets both controls. For the 1,000 shuffled keys permute all nonempty non-hole fitted raw-label values across the fixed known raw labels observed in the March transcript, keeping duplicate values and holes. Require the observed CER below every shuffled key. This control asks whether the complete table carries information. It cannot identify the contribution of the fit. Therefore also redistribute the changes among training-eligible raw labels within their frozen training frequency strata (5–9, 10–19, 20+). Randomly permute the targets inside each stratum, apply the actual corrected emissions to the shuffled destinations on top of the original baseline, and compare CER. Require at most nine ties or wins in 1,000 against the fitted table. If a permutation has no effect or uses the identity, retain it. Python random.Random seeds 15530314 and 15530315, independently. Save per-paragraph distances, all 2,000 control scores, training changes, final assignments, input paths/hashes and reproducible commands. Denote any miss as a miss without modifying the plan. Diagnostic only on later historical material, only if the calibration passes and independent material comparable to the same reader’s labels can be identified.
