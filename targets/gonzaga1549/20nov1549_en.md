# English, 20 November 1549, the cipher passages with the clear words around them
Clear: as to what you ask, in case the College should wish to confirm and defend [2 lines of signs not read]:
1. in Parma in the name of the Church. Clear: given what is said above of our intention.
2. Clear: in no way let [anything] be moved in Parma on our part, nor in any other place.
3. Clear: claim that we wished to move war against the Church, directly or indirectly.
4. Clear: have, to offend and occupy on this occasion the state of the house of Farnese, because of the neighbourhood it has with that of the said [Duke of Florence].
5. Clear: nor should it be attempted at such a time, since when, after the pontiff has been elected, something else should seem right and give occasion, it can easily be done.
