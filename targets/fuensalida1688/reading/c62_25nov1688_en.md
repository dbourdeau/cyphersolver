# Charles II to the conde de Fuensalida, Buen Retiro, 25 November 1688: English translation (exploratory)

This translates `c62_25nov1688_reading_K5.txt`. It is an **exploratory** reading: the key (K5) has not been validated at
the programme's bar (see NOTES.md). The words marked probable there are given here without marks.

> In view of what you report in your letter of the eleventh of last month, enclosing a copy of the one that the
> Prince-Abbot of Saint Gall wrote to Count Casati about the French having seized and sacked some places of the Swiss,
> I approve what you have done in this matter, and I charge you to keep me informed of what comes of the negotiation
> and of the negotiations of the French.

The 19th-century archivist's note on the cover of the bundle (PARES 3962172, images 1-2) agrees with this: all the letters
come with their translation "menos una de 25 de noviembre de 1688 (lugares esguízaros)". "Conde Casati" is probably
Alfonso Casati, Spanish envoy to the Swiss cantons; we have not checked this identification.
