## Sign shapes and marks the notation does not cover (with my convention)

- 6: a small sign like a 6 or a short b (loop at the line, stem curving up to the right), very common after u y
  ("u y 6") and in "1 6", "7 6", "d 6", "x 6". It is shorter than the tall-stemmed b. Written as 6; it may be what
  the notation means by b. Everywhere, e.g. P2L14, P2L12, P1L19.
- 8: an 8 sitting on the line (closed top), usually followed by a P-like sign ("8 P", very often underlined). Written
  8. The g of the notation (open-topped 8 whose lower loop hangs below the line) is written g. E.g. P2L12
  "<8 P> | g", P1L30 "4 g 8 t".
- t: a small crossed stroke (+ or t-like, a short vertical with a crossbar, not reaching far below the line), usually
  attached after 7, x, y, d, 8, 9 ("7 t", "x t", "d t", "9 t", "8 t") or standing alone; also the H-like double
  crossed sign before 4 ("t 4"). Written t throughout.
- "uff"/"uH" cluster: u followed by two tall strokes reaching below the line. With a loop at the top of the second
  stroke written "u S P"; with a crossbar through both strokes written "u S T". With a single crossed tall stroke
  written "u T" (P1L28). Readers may want to treat this as one sign.
- S t: long s with a crossbar running out to the right (like an st ligature); written "S t", not T (e.g. P1L18,
  P1L27, P2L01, P2L03, P2L14). T alone only at P1L06 ("T P").
- 7 vs Z: a 3-like sign with a flat top is written 7, with a rounded top and tail below the line Z. Several are
  between the two (marked Z/7 at P2L07; also P1L01 "d 9 7 t", P1L23 "m d 7 t", P1L29 "9 Z m z 9 7 t").
- 1: a short upright stroke (like i without dot or the digit 1), often after d, b, 6 ("d 1", "b 1", "6 1").
- s: a 5- or s-shaped sign on the line (P2L10, P2L12, P1L07, P1L28, P1L29, P2L01, P2L09). Written s.
- B: b with a stroke running out to the right from the top of the bowl (b with a flag); I have written all such
  b's as B (P1L04, P1L05, P1L08, P1L09, P1L14, P1L17, P1L27, P1L29, P1L31, P1L32, P2L02, P2L08, P2L13, P2L14).
- Q: a large open loop like an oversized a or a Q lying on the line, P1L28 "9 Q" and P2L13 "Q m". Written Q.
- R: a capital R shape, P1L28 "a u y | R" and P2L14 "S t | R". Written R.
- c: c shape, P1L01, P2L06, P2L11 (with a small stroke above), P2L03, P1L15, P1L18.
- Marks above/below signs: a small "w" or tilde above u (written u~: P1L24, P1L26, P1L29, P1L31, P1L32, P2L03); the
