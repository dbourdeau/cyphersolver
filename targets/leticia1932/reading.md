# Reading (Spanish), cipher words only, Daniel's rule

Plain word = certain (mechanical decoding gives the expected Spanish word, or a one-letter keying slip of a kind seen in the held-out test); {x} = probable (two or more letters corrected, or a proper name not attested elsewhere); ‹x› = unresolved. No restorations. Clear words of the telegrams are not counted and are mostly left out; copies are counted once. Telegraphic style, no punctuation in the originals.
