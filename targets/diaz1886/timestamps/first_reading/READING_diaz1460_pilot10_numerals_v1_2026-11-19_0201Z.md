## The notation
- **Digits** are a number group as written. A point, comma, colon or `=` after it is the leaf's own.
- **`~`** after a group: a small stroke above it. **`(?)`**: doubtful, see the list under the leaf.
- **`/`**: a tall stroke that may be the digit 1 or the tail of a letter in the line above.
- **`cs`** and **`q'`**: the two marks. `q'` is a q with a small raised letter; it nearly always comes twice.
- **`{21}`**: a numeral in the clear sentence, not a group. **`[...]`**: the copyist's note.
- Everything else is clear text on the leaf.
