# English rendering: Vaudreuil to the minister, Montréal, 16 Apr 1759 (ANOM COL C11A 104 ff.75-76)

Working translation of `reading.md`. `[...]` = supplied from the sense; `[?]` = groups read but not understood.

Montréal, 16 April 1759. M. de Vaudreuil. Monseigneur,

Fearing that we shall find ourselves next year in the same shortage as this one, I have engaged the munitionnaire to order his
correspondents, both in France and, in case they should learn that some of the ships they will have loaded with provisions for him have been taken,
to replace them at once and to send him fresh consignments from whatever ports they can.

I have the honour, Monseigneur, to inform you of this reprisal [replacement] and to beg you to be so good as to have the ships they need furnished
to this munitionnaire's correspondents. Supposing they cannot find them, the munitionnaire will pay the King in Canada the freight of these ships
at this year's rate; the [?] [?] are too great for him to be able to pay, at any price whatever, this freight once earned,
which obliges him to write to his correspondents that he does not intend to pay this freight anywhere but in the Colony.

For the rest, Monseigneur, I can only repeat my wish that you add a supplement to the shipments of provisions that will be made to us this
year: the preservation of the Colony depends especially on it, and without it the Colony would fall of its own accord.

I am with very deep respect, Monseigneur, your very humble and very obedient servant, Vaudreuil.
