# R4940: two enciphered expressions

Working reading, 23 September 2026. The complete letter has two short cipher spans on page 1; page 2 is the address and seal. Both images inspected.

Sender: Rákóczi Zsigmond, signed. Recipient: Ferenc Bethlen, explicitly addressed on the verso. Date/place: Munkács, 2 August 1648 (in clear on page 1).

| Cipher | Mechanical reading | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| adqgzndl | votumiok | their votes / opinions (Latin-derived Hungarian expression) |
| inyhqodkduadkiv | niertdologvolna | nyert dolog volna: it would be a won/settled matter |

The first expression follows “Ha ...”; the second follows the clear address to the recipient. The spelling `niert` is retained rather than silently modernized to `nyert`. The glyph distinction q/g was checked in `R4940-cipher.png`: q=t, g=u. This is supported by the broader alphabetic substitution family in R4937/R4942; it is not an independent ciphertext-only solution of these 23 letters.

Used key assignments: a=v, d=o, g=u, h=r, i=n, k=l, l=k, n=i, o=d, q=t, u=g, v=a, y=e, z=m.

The cancelled word before the first cipher span is not transcribed. It is a deletion, not counted as surviving ciphertext. The main goal remains in progress; this letter is not yet written up or assigned a final novelty claim.
