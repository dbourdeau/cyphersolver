Checked 31 October 2026 (before starting) and 1 November 2026 (before publishing).

## Verdict: **the method was pointed out before us; no reading is to be found.**

Item 818 of Daniel Bourdeau's catalogue of unsolved ciphers records, as of 28 October 2026,
"vowels replaced by digits, trivially readable". **The identification of the method therefore
predates this work.** What this repository adds is:

1. that the substituted letters are not only the vowels but nine letters, **a e i o u l m n r**,
   including l=6, m=7, n=8, r=9 (the catalogue mentions only vowels);
2. a transcription and reading of all 38 images that carry cipher;
3. the shuffled-key control and the list of what is still unresolved.

We do not say "first solved". The catalogue states the method and asks for exactly what is done
here: "Read the cipher sections from the IIIF images".
