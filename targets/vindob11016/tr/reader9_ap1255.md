# P.Vindob. A.P. 11016, secret lines 1-11: blind reading against A.P. 1255 (reader 9)

## Method

- Blind pass. Opened only `tr/ap1255_study.md`, the A.P. 1255 images in `img/ref/` (`AP1255_1.jpg`, `AP1255_1_big.png`,
  `AP1255_l1..l3.png`) and the 11016 images. NOTES.md, profile.json, WORKLOG and the other files in `tr/` were not
  opened. No web search.
- Step 1: the A.P. 1255 forms were fixed first (γ Y with long right arm; "b" 1.3; "ч" 1.4; ring ه; ε; 9-shaped و;
  ϛ = ε/ع-shaped head on a short flat stroke; ز; ζ = "7" with long oblique to lower left; ح; η = "h" whose right leg
  falls below the line; ط = tall stem with flat closed bowl on the baseline; θ = oval with projecting bar; ι = long
  oblique with a hook at the foot; ي; ك = angular S/5 whose baseline runs far back to the left; κ = three-pointed
  comb; ل = tall upright with foot to the left; λ; م = round head with tail to lower left; μ).
- Step 2: `img/lines1.jpg`, `lines2.jpg`, `enh01..11`, `z01a/b`, `z02a/b` were looked at, but `enh08..11` are cut
  through the line, so the readings rest on my own crops from the 636-px source `img/uk4nGb4kQHe3msbC.jpg`
  (Lanczos 6-8x, contrast 1.5, plus the red channel), with a pixel ruler. Montages put each 11016 sign class beside
  the A.P. 1255 sign.
- x ranges are pixels in the 636-px image. A stroke is 3-6 px wide; loops under about 8 px cannot be judged open or
  closed.
- Token conventions: `a/b` = likelier first; `*` = illegible; `|` = divider (two dots or a tick over a dot, the
  upper mark usually a little right of the lower).
  - `س` is my token for the line-end sign the brief calls the "kāf-like wavy bowl": two or three small waves on the
    line running left into a deep bowl. To my eye it is an Arabic final sīn (teeth plus bowl). It does **not** look
    like the A.P. 1255 ك. I kept `ك` for the sign that does (see below).
  - `Δ` = a triangle standing on a base stroke (capital-delta form), kept apart from `δ` = the "6"/b form.
  - `ه/ο` = plain ring. It is the A.P. 1255 ه exactly; a Greek ο would look the same. The image cannot decide.

### Matches to the A.P. 1255 table

- Secure in shape (high): η 4, ك-form 3, ring 16 = 23 signs. For the ring the shape match is secure but the script
  is not (ه or ο).
- Probable (medium): θ 8, "6" δ 7, ط-form 4, م 1 = 20 signs.
- Loose (low to low-medium): ζ 4, κ 1-2, γ/V 2, ل-like 6.
- No counterpart on the table: χ 6, X-on-base 2, Δ 1, π 1, "<" 2-3, cups 4, strokes 3, س 6-7, slit-loop 3, "8" 1.

### The three b/S forms

| form | count | instances |
|------|-------|-----------|
| flat-bowl ط-form (tall straight stem, flat angular bowl, base stroke projecting left) | 4 | L1 275-318; L7 150-215; L8 422-475; L9 410-462 (crossed stem) |
| S-head form (angular S/5 on a long baseline) | 3 | L5 355-405; L6 245-290; L7 225-285 |
| round-bowl "6"/b (δ) | 7 clear | L2 360-395; L3 414-447, 337-372, 163-200; L4 397-420; L8 495-535, 185-215 |
| "6" variants | 3 slit-loop (L5, L9, L10), 1 double-loop (L8), 2 faint (L5 215-240, L10 165-200) | |

- ط-form against "6": separable at this resolution. The ط-form always has a base stroke running left of the stem
  and a stem that stands nearly upright; the "6" never has the base stroke and its stem leans about 45 degrees.
  All four ط-forms and the seven clear "6" forms fall cleanly on one side. Confidence in the split: high.
  Confidence that the first is the 1255 ط and not a "b" δ on a base line: medium.
- S-head: cannot be confused with either b-form. The three instances match the 1255 **ك** better than the 1255 ϛ:
  same four-stroke S and the same long baseline back to the left. I read them ك/ϛ. No sign in the eleven lines has
  the ε-headed shape of the 1255 ϛ itself.
- The tailed sign (ζ/ϛ, 4 instances) is a different sign from the S-head: no baseline, long descending tail.
