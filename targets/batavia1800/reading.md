# batavia1800: reading of the letter Smissaert -> Prediger, 20 November 1800

Nationaal Archief, The Hague, 2.01.27.02 (Raad der Aziatische Bezittingen en Etablissementen), inv. 209, scan 0002
(right-hand page, the letter as sent in partial cipher); clear minute of the same letter on scans 0001, 0002 (left) and 0004.

Marks: cipher passages in *italics*, the rest is clear on the page; {x} = probable; [x] = restored; ‹…› = unresolved (none).

## Text (Dutch, 1800)

Geacht vriend! Behalven de U bekende en vóór uw vertrek reeds *geprojecteerde verzending van een corps troepen voor
Indie, welke door veele tegenspoeden tot hiertoe opgehouden is geweest*, maar evenwel, zoo wij hopen, eerlang *voortgang
zal hebben*, is de Asiatische Raad *geautoriseerd* en reeds bezig met *de arrangementen tot eene {andere} [s]oortgelyke
expeditie te vertrekken naar Batavia uit eenen anderen oort van Eur[o]pa*, waardoor, indien deselve *reusseerd, eene
aanzienlyke ver[ste]rking der militaire magt voor Java zal worden aangebragt*. Ik ben gelast U daar van deze
onderhandsche en voorlopige kennis te geven, met verzoek, dat Gij den *Heer Gouverneur Generaal zulks in {secretesse}
{gelieft} te communiceren* tot deszelfs vertrek. Mijne bezigheden laten mij niet toe meer ...

## Translation (English)

Esteemed friend! Besides the *sending of a corps of troops to the Indies*, known to you and already *planned* before your
departure, *which through many setbacks has been held up until now* but which, as we hope, will nevertheless *go
forward* before long, the Asiatic Council is *authorised*, and already busy with *the arrangements for another, similar
expedition to leave for Batavia from another place in Europe*, by which, if it *succeeds, a considerable reinforcement of
the military force for Java will be brought*. I am instructed to give you private and preliminary notice of this, with
the request that you *be pleased to communicate it in secrecy to the Governor-General* until his departure. My
occupations do not allow me more ...

## Count (cipher words only: 60)

- certain: 54
- probable {x}: 3
  - {secretesse}: enciphered k-e-c-r-e-t-e-k-k-e, with 42 (k) written for s three times (encipherer's slip);
  - {gelieft}: the only occurrence of 54 = f;
  - {andere}: followed by a stray 44 (a).
- restored [x]: 3
  - Eur[o]pa: top digit of the o illegible;
  - [s]oortgelyke: first column struck out;
  - ver[ste]rking: the encipherer wrote "verking", omitting three letters.
- unresolved: 0
- read share (certain + probable, restored excluded): 57/60 = **0.95**. With the restorations, which the clear minute
  confirms: 60/60.

The minute marks every passage to be enciphered by underlining it, with small vertical strokes under it that count the
letters. Only those passages are in cipher; the rest of the letter is clear on the sent page.

## Note on the content

The text is not new: the clear minute of the same letter is filed with it (the pair). What is new is the key, rebuilt
from the minute and validated on a held-out passage (validation.txt).
