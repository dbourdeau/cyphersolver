SELECTION RULE (written before any candidate cipher beyond 1623/1634 was viewed)

Candidate pool:
 (a) every letter in Coolhaas, Generale Missiven I (1610-1638) and II (1639-1655) that the edition marks as written partly
     in cipher (cijferschrift, cursief = cijfer, verklaring in margine), from a full-text grep of all downloaded page HTML;
 (b) every page in GLOBALISE HTR of NA 1.04.02 inv. 1070-1145 that the blind detector (scripts/detect.py, values never printed)
     flags and that masked context shows to be number cipher in running text (not bale numbers, dates, prices, soundings).
Exclusions: the Batavia letter of 25 Dec 1623 with the resolution of 2 Oct 1623 (all copies), and the Batavia letter of
 20 Feb 1634 (all copies). These are training only.
Rule: the test letter is the FIRST candidate in date order after 25 Dec 1623 that is not excluded. If its cipher cannot be
 located on NA scans, take the next. If more than one copy exists, the base copy is a copy with no margin gloss; a glossed
 copy is opened only after the score is fixed. The printed edition text of the test letter (Coolhaas italics) is not read
 before the score is fixed.
Also tested if found: every later candidate in the pool, each scored separately, in date order (no cherry-picking).
