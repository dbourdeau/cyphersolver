  `zzrgt` = Sergt, `8orpl` = Corpl, `sor gair8` = For Guard.
- Image 14 right: `the +or-i-g after wzit.-g it, was Tuesday` = "the morning after writing it, was Tuesday".

## The system as I see it, blind
A simple substitution with word breaks in which most consonants and o, i, r, l are written plainly or barely altered, and the vowels a, e, u plus m, n, y, th are replaced by altered forms. My transcription merged several signs (ezh family; minims; loop signs), so the key above has known collisions.

## Second alphabet (from 4 Oct 1779), first look
New shapes: `1` (S18), `^` (S17), `:` (S24), looped `L` (S15 used heavily), m-shaped `w`, `6`. From `11zL -.ghL` = "last night" and `L^ohghL` = "thought", in the second alphabet L = t, 1 = l or a, ^ = h, z = s, - = n, . = i, g = g, h = h. Settled in phase 2.
