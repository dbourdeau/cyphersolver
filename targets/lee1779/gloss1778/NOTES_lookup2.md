# lookup2 — method and hard cases

**Sources opened:** only the 1776 Entick page images (both folders), the 1776 hOCR and page_numbers files, and the 1781 hOCR and page_numbers files. Nothing else was opened.

## Method
- **Rendering the columns.** `strips.py` finds the column rule on each page image. It crops the left part of each column and labels the detected text lines with numbers. Where that failed (lower-resolution files, odd pages near the binding) I used `crop.py` for manual crops, and zoomed in to full resolution for doubtful lines.
- **Counting.** Every REAL position was counted by reading the image line by line. I did not trust the automatic line labels, which sometimes missed a line (pp. 94, 262, 332, 339, 389, 355).
- **What was left out of the count.** Indented run-over lines, section headings ("Y.", "G.", "Q.") and running heads were not counted. Bracketed overflow fragments set on the same line, such as "(flounces", were not counted as lines either.
- **Page numbers.** The printed page number was checked in the running head of every REAL page. For odd pages I checked it on a composite of top-right crops.
- **Controls.** C1, C2 and C3 were taken from images wherever an image exists (marked [img]). Otherwise they come from the 1776 hOCR via `ocrcount.py` (marked [ocr]). `ocrcount.py` groups OCR words by column, separates indented run-overs from entries, and detects missing lines from the regular line pitch. A control is marked doubtful when the OCR had gaps or garbled text.
- **C5.** All C5 values come from the 1781 hOCR (OCR only). "Illegible" means the 1781 OCR is missing too many lines in that column to count.
- **Working notes.** Per-page counts are in `work_counts.txt`. The table is generated by `build_lookup2.py`.

## Hard cases
- **G22 (page 22).** The 1776 copy has no arabic page 22. Its dictionary numbering starts at p. 33, after the roman-numbered introduction (i–xxxii). The file `p022_n021.jpg` is p. xxii, which is prose. REAL and C1–C3 are therefore "none". The 1781 printing does have a dictionary page 22, so C5 is filled.
- **Odd pages (word starts hidden in the binding).** This affects pp. 43, 63, 81, 99, 101, 165, 191, 211, 237, 253, 255, 295, 299, 325, 333, 335, 339, 347, 361 and 389. Each word was supplied from its visible ending, its definition and its alphabetical neighbours.
- **Judging run-overs where the indent is hidden.** In these cases I decided by sense:
  - 81a "archer in the zodiac" (Centaur)
  - 101a "...country, form of government" and "...lished government"
  - 361a "...ans of life"
  - 425a "...everence, adoration"
  - 169a, 315a and 325a
  - 299a: a zoom showed that "matter of a well digested sore" is the entry "Pus, s." and not a run-over.
- **Last lines of columns.**
  - G07: Sink, v. is followed only by its run-over.
  - G09, G16 and G48: the entry asked for is the last entry in its column.
  - 82a, 426a and 427b are short columns, so some controls are "none".
- **Doubtful controls.**
  - 299b37: depends on whether "Q. abbrev. for question" counts as an entry.
  - 107a9: line 3, "...a married woman", was taken as the entry Covert, s.
- **No REAL position was rated doubtful.** All 63 existing positions were counted on images. G22 does not exist as a page.
