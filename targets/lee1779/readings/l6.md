Conventions as in `l1.md`. The rest of the letter (his sailing in the Alliance, Lafayette's return, Rodney and the

## Reading

My love to yr family; to London & all our friends. Do you think it practicable to get myself, appoint[ed] from yr
state, to Congress. It will be of great importance. Think of it, in time. Adieu

## Summary

On the eve of sailing home, Lee asks his brother whether he could get himself appointed to Congress from Virginia.
