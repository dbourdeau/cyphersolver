Source: Papers of the Continental Congress, M247, item 102 vol. 3 (Arthur Lee letter book), NARA catalog id 502968528.
Images: `sources/raw/sweep_pcc3_2026-11-14/full/502968528/` (1610 px wide files, not the `_1000x` copies).
Transcribed 14 Nov 2026 by Claude (AI agent), from the images only, with 2x to 7x crops. Nothing was decoded.

## Conventions

- One manuscript line per text line, roughly. Long code-free stretches are given in full where legible; a few code-free stretches on image 0123 are given in a looser reading and marked so.
- Code groups are normalised: `243,a,XVII` -> `243a17`; endings after a dash kept: `115,b,XXXVIII-s` -> `115b38-s`.
- Line numbers: roman in the manuscript unless noted "(arabic)".
- `?` marks a doubtful reading; alternatives follow in brackets, e.g. `392b28?(397)`.
- `[...]` = illegible or lost; `[struck: ...]` = struck-through text; `[interl.: ...]` = interlined text; `[edge]` = text running off the scan edge or into the gutter.
- `{G}` before a group = page number possibly incomplete because the line starts in the gutter or the previous line runs off the right edge.
