## The record

Ff. 96–114, 17 images: "Post Scripta", dockets "…83". Squared grille sheets with dots, laid over a clear cover letter:
the dotted squares mark the letters of the secret text in the cover letter beneath. One cover letter shows through
under f.102 ("Die 4 od 5000 Cronen…"), but the cover letters themselves (ff. 115–118) are not among the DECODE images.
Without them the grilles give no text.
