# R9589, Castelcicala to Luigi de' Medici, Paris, 9 Oct 1823

[?Having] the liberty of the King of Spain, and the orders Your Excellency gave me in your despatch in cipher no. 688
of 28 Sept., there can no longer be any question of my passing to the Conference the Note Your Excellency sent me with
the said despatch. I submit them for the Sovereign's information. ⟨I have the honour to be with deep respect, Your
Excellency's ...⟩ The Duke of Angoulême will not be back here for two months.
