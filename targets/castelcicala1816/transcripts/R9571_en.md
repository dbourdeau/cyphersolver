# R9571, Castelcicala to Cavaliere Luigi de' Medici, Paris, 8 September 1823 (no. 3871): English translation

France wishes Cardinal Castiglione for Pope; but the Duc de Laval is blamed for having made this too visible,
and it is feared that this may harm the said cardinal in his elevation to the Pontificate. There is no agreement
between France and Austria for the election of the Pontiff.
