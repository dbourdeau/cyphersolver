The state of the situation of the troops on the 1st of this month. I await the recruits with all the more impatience because the minister of war has announced them to me. The shortfall of eight hundred and seventeen men does not yet allow me to undertake anything against some English islands, as I cannot put a sufficient garrison there, [enough] to garrison Martinique, where, after deducting the hospitals and the garrison of Dominica, only three thousand three hundred effectives remain. I shall [shortly] send you the state of the situation of the garrison of this last island, which is supplied half by Martinique and half by Guadeloupe, whether in regular troops or in militia.
