== Summary ==
An unsigned, undated Polish letter (with Latin phrases) to the Grand Crown Hetman Adam Mikołaj Sieniawski (docket "Do JwJmP. Hetmana Wielk. Koronn."), written by an adviser in his camp who addresses him as "WMM Pan". The writer does not believe the Warsaw gazettes that Sieniawski has accepted the mediation of the Bishop of Chełmno (Teodor Potocki, Leszczyński's man) and granted him passports to come to Warsaw; he warns that the bishop's "character" was given secretly by Stanisław (Leszczyński) without the consent, even without the knowledge, of the Swedish king (code 600), who is given to deceiving and disavowing, that under pretext of treaties the aim is to detach the army from Sieniawski, as with the Wiśniowiecki princes, and that by the Treaty of Warsaw (Sweden-Leszczyński, 1705) no amnesty may be granted without the Swedish king. He advises putting the bishop off "preliminariter", demanding copies of his instruments and full powers "ab utroque principali", waiting for the expected Swedish march on Moscow and the outcome of the battle between the Swedes and Moscow ("ante eventum", "ante evacuationem realem"), fearing the discredit of Moscow and its allies, and concluding only together with all, not one by one, lest the country be led into a labyrinth and its final ruin.
Dating: after Sieniawski became Grand Hetman (1706) and the Treaty of Altranstädt, while Charles XII was still expected to march on Moscow and Potocki was offering mediation: most likely 1707 (spring to autumn), before the Swedish invasion of Russia in early 1708.
