## The document

Archiwum Narodowe w Krakowie, Zbiór Dzieduszyckich 26/32 nr 13 pp. 29-30 (DECODE R7475, catalogued with the
Brâncoveanu letters of nr 13; status N/A). Not printed by Kaczka 2012. Unsigned; an adviser in Sieniawski's camp,
c. 1707 by content: against the mediation of the Bishop of Chełmno (Teodor Potocki) and Stanisław's treaty offers,
"lubo nie wierzam gazetom warszawskim, nec respektem acceptowania mediacyi biskupa chełmińskiego".
