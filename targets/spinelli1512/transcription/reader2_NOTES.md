## Decisions applied throughout
- **Tall 6 / b:** a tall stem with a closed bowl is always "6". The open-bowl tall form is "h".
- **p variants:** p with a long horizontal flag (pf) is kept apart from plain p, barred p (pbar) and p with a bar above the bowl (ptop). A pf right after clear text (B1.01, B2.01) could be the clear abbreviation "per", but I transcribed it as a sign.
- **Nplus:** N with a joined + is treated as one sign.
- **Dot groups:** dot-stroke-dot ( .|. ) is one sign, dsd. Three dots in a triangle are dot3; in A1.01 the top dot is a short dash.
- **Margin crosses:** the crosses in the left margin (A1, B1) are reader's reference marks, not signs.
- **Bars and interlinear text:** bars across q/p/d descenders or ascenders are signs (qbar, pbar, dbar, ptop) unless they may belong to an interlinear word crossing the sign. Those cases are marked with ?.
