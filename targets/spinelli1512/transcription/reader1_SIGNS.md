Doubt markers in the transcription: `name?` = doubtful reading of that sign; `?` alone = a sign is there but illegible.

Decisions applied throughout:
- A bar through a descender (qx, px) or across an ascender (dt, pc, ht) is treated as part of a separate sign, not as a mark. Where the bar could equally belong to an interlinear word (an interlinear t-bar, a strike-through line, or an underline), the sign is marked with ? (qx?, px?, dt?).
- bx is written in two ways (tall straight ascender, or a short ascender hooked at the top); both forms are transcribed bx because the x-stroke and bowl are the same. Xb (the big X with a bowl) is kept separate.
- Nx is taken as one sign because the N and the small cross are always written touching; all 3 cases are in letter A.
