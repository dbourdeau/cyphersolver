# Translation of the cipher runs (English), from reading_A.md and reading_B.md

Holes are left as [...]. Not checked by a person.

Letter A: (everything will be restored) and made our affairs strong, to remove the [...], and [fearing] that they
might go back to negotiations with the French. As [I fear], for a Council of Ten held yesterday with the Giunta,
I believe only for this ... I have [encouraged] ... to give heart to [the Pope] ... If one wishes to negotiate
[with the Pope], if we did not have the ten thousand Spanish foot, making them up with Italian foot ... forty
thousand ducats. I am sure that [the Signoria] will do it if [the Pope] moves it and [decides].

Letter B: ... and [...]. He must go to Naples to hold him there and bring him back to the field, for [...] superior,
and all the more with lord Prospero coming with three hundred men at arms. ... and the royal train has fallen into
the hands of peasants. ... I have given heart ... six thousand ... many of our principal men are dead. ... Of the six
thousand I am of the contrary opinion. If they took the [...] of the state of Milan so as not to [restore] it to
His Imperial Majesty, he would come to terms with the King of France.
