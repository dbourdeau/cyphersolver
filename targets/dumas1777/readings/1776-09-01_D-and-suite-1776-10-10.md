## Normalized text

47 (1 Sept): "vous ne devez pas être embarrassé de lire ce qui sera chiffré ici, & à l'avenir. [Ce Hortalez] dont Mr. A. L. votre ami m'a parlé ..."

51, Deane's letter of 3 Oct (English): "Since my last, in which I mentioned the [King of Prussi(a),] I have attained a Method of sounding [that monarch's sentiments] more directly thro another channel ..." (Dumas's parenthesis: "Il m'avoit parlé d'un Mémoire, sur lequel j'aurois composé une Lettre pour [that monarch]") "... The attention to my Business here, the critical situation of affairs [at this court,] and the anxious suspence for the Events at New York ..." The key has no k: Dumas writes "cing" with c (28).

51, Lee's letter of 23 Sept (English): "I have been apprized by [Hortalez] that the business for which I recommended him to you is to be transacted thro [France...] which is the reason of your not seeing him."

52 (10 Oct): "Continuez, m'a-t-il dit, ... & vous finirez par entrer enfin en correspondance avec [= le ministre même.] Je le serai fréquemment & cet hiver, & je travaillerai à ménager cela."

53 (Suite, 10 Oct): "je crois ne pas devoir différer un moment de la confier à [Madam V. Loder...] Ayez, je vous supplie, la bonté de vous souvenir ..."

## Translation

47: "This Hortalez of whom Mr. A. L., your friend, spoke to me ..." — 52: "Go on, he told me, ... and you will end by entering at last into correspondence with the minister himself." — 53: "I think I should not delay a moment in entrusting it to Madam V. Loder ..." (Dumas's forwarding address in The Hague was "chez Mad. V. Loder": Committee of Secret Correspondence to Arthur Lee, 12 Dec 1775, Wharton vol. 2). — 51 is English.

## Print check

Classification: **printed with the cipher read** (corrected after the independent check; the first reading had "part not found in print").
- 47 [Ce Hortalez]: printed in translation in Sparks vol. 9 pp. 288-290 under the date "September 30th, 1776" (`print/sparks9_12593.txt`) and in Wharton vol. 2 pp. 134-136 under 1 Sept 1776 (`print/wharton2_07710.txt`): "M. Hortalez, of whom Mr Arthur Lee spoke in two of his letters, has not yet appeared". Agrees.
- 51 Deane extract: printed by Sparks vol. 9 p. 291 as "Silas Deane to C. W. F. Dumas, Paris, October 3d, 1776": "Since my last, in which I mentioned the King of Prussia, I have obtained a method of sounding that monarch's sentiments more directly through another channel ... the critical situation of affairs at this Court". Agrees with our reading ("Prussi[a]", "that monarch's sentiments", "at this court"). Differences: print "obtained" for Dumas's copy "attained" (plain text); print adds "which is not merely political, but partly commercial", not in Dumas's extract.
- 51 Lee extract: printed by Sparks vol. 9 (London, September 23d, 1776; line 12569 of `web/diplomaticcorres09sparuoft.txt`) and Wharton vol. 2 (line 8634): "I have been apprized by Hortalez, that the business for which I recommended him to you is to be transacted through France". Agrees, including "France" (our 600 666 660 679 680 681).
- 52 [= le ministre même] and 53 [Madam V. Loder]: **in print.** Peter Force, *American Archives*, fifth series, vol. 2 (1851), "Letters of Charles W. F. Dumas to Committee of Secret Correspondence, September and October", columns 99-103, prints the French text with the numbers ("612 663 671 528 330 100 302 101 230 200 630 420 422 488 300 401"; "confier à 517 234 444 243 498 641 88 99 244 307 299 83 194 401"), an interlined decipherment ("— le ministre même"; "Ma d a m U l o d e r") and an English translation ("you will finish by entering at last into a correspondence with the Minister himself"; "confide it to Madam [V. L]oder", the OCR has "Utoder"). Found by the second agent of ours; the first reading had searched only Sparks and Wharton, whose texts stop before these pages. Earlier note kept for the record: Sparks's "September 30th" text and Wharton's 1 Sept text stop before the passage of image 51 bottom ("Je terminerai cette Dépêche ...") and do not include the Suite of 10 Oct. (Sparks and Wharton print "Madame ___ has taken the trouble to send me your letters" in the 1 Sept part, a blank for a name; that sentence is on a page not in this group and is not the 53 passage.)

Decipherer: glosses on 47, 51, 52, 53; hand not identified. On 53 the gloss over 641 is u, i.e. the decipherer wrote the letter the number gives, not the sense.

## Corrections after the independent check (14 Nov 2026)

A second AI agent of ours found these pages in Force, *American Archives*, fifth series, vol. 2, columns 99-103 (numbers, interlined
decipherment and translation, 1851), and showed that 641 is the "V." of "Madam V. Loder" (Wharton vol. 2 prints Dumas's address as
"chez Mad. V. Loder, à la Hague", Committee of Secret Correspondence to Arthur Lee, 12 December 1775). Both points are applied above:
images 52-53 are **in print**, and the 641 emendation is withdrawn. The column text was confirmed in the Internet Archive OCR of Force
(14 Nov 2026). Force also reads the plain word after the passage on image 52 as "Je le verrai fréquemment" where the first reading has
"serai"; that plain word was not rechecked on the image.
