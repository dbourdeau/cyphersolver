## Normalized text

Image 39 (Lee's letter of 11 June, quoted in English): "Mr. Story goes from hence directly to America. A french Gentleman named [Horte(a)lez, having something to negotiate for the Congress] I have given him your adress."

Image 41 (Dumas to "the person you know", July 1776): "Après y avoir longtemps & bien pensé, il me semble que pour répondre complettement à leur intention, je dois [me présenter aussi à l'hôtel d('E)spagne, pour y être simpl(e)ment connu comme char(g)é] d'une telle comission, m'ouvrir par-là les voies de servir mes comettants à l'avenir dans diverses occasions qui peuvent se présenter d'un moment à l'autre, & ne pas encourir le blâme qui pourroit rejaillir jusque sur ces Messieurs, d'avoir négligé [une p(u)issance si digne aussi de leur confiance & recherche]. Au reste je ne ferai ni ne dirai rien à cet égard ..."

## Translation

Image 41: "After long and careful thought, it seems to me that to answer their intention completely I ought to present myself also at the Spanish embassy, so as to be known there simply as charged with such a commission, [to open by that the ways of serving my principals ... and not incur the blame that might fall even on these gentlemen for having neglected] a power so worthy also of their confidence and of being sought out."

## Print check

Classification: **printed with the cipher read**, in English: Sparks vol. 9, 10 Aug 1776 (`print/sparks9_11883.txt`) and Wharton vol. 2 pp. 107-110 (`print/wharton2_06261.txt`). No Franklin Papers text in `print/`.

- Ours 39: "[Hortalez, having something to negotiate for the Congress]" — Sparks/Wharton: "A French gentleman named Hortalez having something to negotiate for the Congress, I have given him your address." Agrees (Sparks prints Hortalez, i.e. the corrected form).
- Ours 41: "[me présenter aussi à l'hôtel d'Espagne, pour y être simplement connu comme chargé]" — Sparks/Wharton: "I ought to present myself also to the Hotel d'Espagne, to be known there simply as charged with such a commission". Agrees.
- Ours 41: "[une puissance si digne aussi de leur confiance & recherche]" — Sparks/Wharton: "of having neglected a power so worthy of their efforts." Difference: print has "efforts" for "confiance & recherche" and omits "aussi"; the translation is free; no name differs.

Decipherer: full interlinear glosses on both pages; hand not identified.
