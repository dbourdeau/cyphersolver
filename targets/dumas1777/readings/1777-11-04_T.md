- No disagreements between pass A and pass B on any of the three images (56 + 78 = 134 numbers on 114-115; 134 on 117).
- Letter and extract compared number by number: identical (134 = 134, no differences).
- Glosses: the transcribers' gloss letters disagree with the key at a few places on 114-115 (7 "p", 15 "A", 80 "g", 50 "a", 82 "c"); on the image these are the decipherer's letters set a little off their numbers or loosely formed (the crop of 115 shows "a v o u s" over 234 102 99 132 110, etc.). ## Normalized text

"... Leur parti, dans ce pays, continue de perdre jusqu'à des personnages distingués. [— C'est votre serviteur, qui a fait insérer la pièce au Mercure, & publier la Lettre hollandoise, etc.] J'ai toujours [sur le corps ici toute la cabale ennemie. Mais je suis à vous.] Cela me rassure & me console. Dieu sauve & bénisse l'Amérique unie ..."

## Translation

"... Their party in this country keeps losing ground, even among persons of distinction. — It is your servant who had the piece put into the Mercure and published the Dutch letter, etc. I still have the whole enemy cabal here on my back. But I am yours. That reassures and consoles me. God save and bless united America ..."
(The "Lettre hollandoise" is the "Lettre Hollandoise d'un Négociant de — à son Correspondant ... sur le Crédit public de la Gr. Br. & de l'Amérique unie" enclosed with T, image 114.)
