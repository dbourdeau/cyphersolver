## Normalized text

"... Je dirai seulement en général en quoi elles consistent: [cimenter] & [fortifier] le parti [formé] depuis un certain temps pour [ruiner l'influence angloise ici, détacher cette République de] vos ennemis, [la concilier à vous & à vos amis. Amsterdam, Harlem, Dort] & [Delft sont pour nous.] De tout cela je rends compte régulièrement ..."

## Translation

"I will say only in general what they [my operations] consist of: to cement and strengthen the party formed some time ago to ruin English influence here, to detach this Republic from your enemies, and to reconcile it to you and your friends. Amsterdam, Haarlem, Dort and Delft are for us."

## Emendations

- 54 (n) in all three copies, "[Delft] ?ont pour nous": `{emended: 54 n -> s, conjectural, by sense "sont pour nous"; no neighbour gives s (53 i, 55 e)}`. Read as written it is "Delft n'ont pour nous", which does not make a sentence with what follows. The M332 image 20 decipherer's gloss over 54 was read as t by the transcriber.

## Print check

**Not found in print.** Wharton vol. 2 and Sparks vol. 9 have no Dumas letter of 14 June 1778. No Franklin Papers extract in `print/`.

Contemporary gloss (M332 image 20) compared with our reading: 127 of 134 distinct glossed numbers agree (transcribers' gloss readings, both passes pooled). The seven others (54 t, 250 e, 302 l, 330 z, 384 l, 429 t, 515) are mostly i/l/e shapes and one alignment; only 54 bears on the sense (see Emendations). On N2 (image 160) the decipherer wrote double letters "tr ex px ux bx lx ix ga" over 355 494 459 646 163 526 384 80 ("républiq-"): the first letter of each pair is the right one, the added x looks like a mark of a second pass, not a different reading. The decipherer wrote s over Dumas's R.

## Note after the independent check (14 Nov 2026)

"sont" rests on a marked emendation: all three copies have 54, which is n in the key; s is supplied by sense (`54*` in the numbers).
The second agent of ours, transcribing image 160 on its own, found the same 54 and the same reading. The gloss over 54 on M332 image 20
was read as t by the transcription passes and as s (medium confidence) by the first reading (`tests/test1/pairs.tsv`); either way the
number as written is n. Everything else in this passage reads as written.
