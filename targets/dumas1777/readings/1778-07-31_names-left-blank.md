## Normalized text

"Voilà, Messieurs, un vrai Liberty-Tree-Man. [Voici les noms laissés en blanc dans ces lettres: — le Pensionnaire d'Amsterdam — La Haie — le Grand Pensionnaire — Amsterdam — Leurs Hautes Puissances — E. F. van Berchel.]"

## Translation

"There, Gentlemen, is a true Liberty-Tree man. Here are the names left blank in these letters: — the Pensionary of Amsterdam — The Hague — the Grand Pensionary — Amsterdam — Their High Mightinesses — E. F. van Berckel."
(Dumas spells "Berchel": no k in the key, and he uses c for k.)

## Print check

**Printed with the names partly filled, partly still blank** (in translation). The two letters themselves are printed: Dumas to Van Berckel, 27 July 1778, Wharton 2:666-667 (`print/wharton2_38367.txt`) and Sparks 9:333-334 (`print/sparks9_14407.txt`, misdated "1776" by the OCR); Van Berckel to Dumas, 31 July 1778, Wharton 2 (`web/revolutionarydip021889unit.txt` lines 38809-38837) and Sparks 9:334-335 (lines 14448-14490). Dumas's key list itself is not printed.
Comparison:
- Addressee and signer: both editors print "Van Berckel, Pensionary of Amsterdam" (our list: "le Pensionnaire d'Amsterdam", "E. F. van Berchel"). Agreement.
- Wharton: "I have carried this morning to Mr. ———— a like copy"; Sparks: "to Mr [blank] a like copy". Our list supplies "le Grand Pensionnaire" for this blank (inferred from the order of the list; the blanks on images 171-172 were not mapped one by one).
- Wharton/Sparks in Van Berckel's reply: "the inaction of [blank] until the present time". Our list supplies "Leurs Hautes Puissances" (inferred, as above). The other names in the list (La Haie, Amsterdam) correspond to the place-names left blank in the copy.
