Sir: I have had the honor of informing you that I intended answering ..."; Sparks 9 (`print/sparks9_14494.txt`) "To M. Van Berckel. The Hague, August 17th, 1778." Both editors name the addressee; our deciphered heading gives his office, the Pensionary of Amsterdam, which Van Berckel was (and Dumas's key list of 27/31 July gives "le Pensionnaire d'Amsterdam" = "E. F. van Berchel"). No difference in sense.
