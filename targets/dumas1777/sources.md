# Sources

## Manuscripts (federal records, free in the National Archives Catalog)

- Papers of the Continental Congress, item 93, vol. 1: letters of C. W. F. Dumas, 1775-1781 (National Archives
  microfilm M247, roll 121). https://catalog.archives.gov/id/502964761 ; a page is reached with `?objectPage=N`, the image
  number used throughout this folder ("img0081" = image 81).
- Miscellaneous Papers of the Continental Congress (M332), roll 2, Dumas file: duplicates and copies.
  https://catalog.archives.gov/id/464815090 (211 images; the catalog has no image 212).
- Item 93 vols. 2-4: screened on contact sheets only.
- For the hand comparison only: item 51 vol. 2, image 186 (James Lovell's number-letter table filed with his letter to
  Livingston of 19 April 1782).

Images were fetched one at a time at 2600 px (or full size where 2600 px failed), at least 1.5 s apart, with an honest
User-Agent; they are kept locally and not committed.

## Key

- Dumas to the Committee of Secret Correspondence, 30 April 1776 (item 93 vol. 1 images 14-19; Papers of Benjamin
  Franklin 22:403-412): the system, the line totals and the counting rule.
- S. Tomokiyo, Cryptiana, "Dumas' Cipher - Benjamin Franklin's Favorite Cipher" (2008-09),
  https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/franklin.htm : the 13 key lines from the Committee's enciphering table.
- Vattel, *Le Droit des gens*, ed. Dumas, Paris 1830 reprint, HathiTrust uc1.a0000513937, seq. 62-63 (wording of the
  preface, adjusted to the 1775 line totals).
