# Bilicich, Penn Ms. Codex 88 f. 80r (catalogue 897)

Status: in progress (first pass, 30 Oct 2026; transcription of the cryptogram unconfirmed sign by sign)

## What it is
Penn Libraries (Kislak) Ms. Codex 88, Sebenico (Šibenik), 1449-53: Guillaume de Lanicia, *Via salutis* plus a miscellany
(ff. 79v-94v) mostly in the hand of Stephanus Bilicich, abbot of St Nicholas. Images: OPenn
`https://openn.library.upenn.edu/Data/0002/mscodex88/data/web/6778_0162_web.jpg` (f. 80r; master TIFF `.../master/6778_0162.tif`).

The catalogue says only "a cryptogram (f. 80r)". The page actually holds three things:

1. Top right (page no. 119 in a later hand): **a key**, a 3x3 pigpen grid. Each cell holds three letters, each with a
   column of 1, 2 or 3 dots above it.
2. Left, top: an Italian recipe (aqua/ink for making letters "retornar", alum + lemon juice) for secret writing.
3. Bottom: the **cryptogram**, a line of 3 words (5 + 8 + 5 signs) in pigpen cell shapes with 1-2 dots inside, and under
   it a faint line in another (lighter) hand that is a gloss of the plaintext.

## The system (read from the page, no outside key)
Dotted pigpen: the cell outline gives the cell (1-9, row by row), the number of dots gives the tier.
Cells: 1 walls right+bottom, 2 left+right+bottom, 3 left+bottom, 4 top+right+bottom, 5 box, 6 left+top+bottom,
7 top+right, 8 left+top+right, 9 top+left.

| cell | 1 dot | 2 dots | 3 dots |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a | k | t |
| 2 | b | l | u |
| 3 | c | m | x |
| 4 | d | n | y |
| 5 | e | o | z |
| 6 | f | p | abbrev. sign (et?) |
| 7 | g | q | abbrev. sign (con/us?) |
| 8 | h | r | sign, unread |
| 9 | i | s | sign, unread |

The third-tier symbols of cells 6-9 are hard to read in the web image and are not needed so far (no 3-dot sign seen
in the cryptogram).

## Per-sign transcription from the master TIFF (30 Oct 2026, 4143x5524)
Cell/dots per sign, word by word (17 signs; words separated by a dot):

| word | signs (cell/dots) | letters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9/2 9/1 3/2 4/2 5/2 | s i m n o |
| 2 | 4/1 8/2 7/1 5/1 4/2 6/1 1/1 | d r g e n f a |
| 3 | 9/1 9/2 3/2 5/2 4/2 | i s m o n |

No 3-dot sign occurs. Cells 4 and 5 in word 1 sit against each other (shared wall) and the left one is narrow, so n-o
is the shape order; the gloss reads m-o-n. Word 3 is **not** identical to word 1: it is i-s-m-o-n.
Both outer words are anagrams of SIMON (simno, ismon).

Result: the key applies cleanly (every sign is a valid cell + 1-2 dots), but the plaintext string
`simno drgenfa ismon` is not a recognisable sentence in Latin, Italian or Croatian. Either the plaintext is a
nonsense test text, a name formula in a language not yet identified, or a second layer sits under the pigpen. Word 2
is settled as a transcription (7 signs, not 8), not as a reading.

## Reading so far
- The faint gloss beneath the cryptogram reads, in three groups, roughly **S-i-m-o-n | D-y-g-e-n-f-e-i | s-m-o-n**.
- Word 1 (and apparently word 3, same shapes) read from the shapes with the key: cell9/2 s, cell9/1 i, cell3/2 m,
  cell4/2 n, cell5/2 o = S I M N O (the last two signs share a wall, so their order is uncertain; the gloss gives
  M-O-N). Consistent with **SIMON**.
- Word 2 (8 signs): shape reading d r g e n s g ? is unreliable; the gloss gives D Y G E N F E I. Only d, g, n agree
  at the same positions. Not settled.

## Open

- Word 2 and word 3 need a careful per-sign read before any claim. Whether the plaintext is Latin, Italian or
  Croatian (a name formula, "Simon ... Simon") is unknown.
- The gloss hand is not identified; it may be a later reader's decipherment.

## Remaining gaps
Words 1-3 transcribed; meaning of `drgenfa` and the word-1/word-3 order unresolved; the 3-dot letters of cells 6-9
are not needed (none occur). Outcome: key applied, text not read (letters give no sense).
