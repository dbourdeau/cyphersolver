Shape classes used in transcription.txt (T-S K24.37, angular dash-divided signs). Descriptions of the printed shape, not
claims of identity. Many classes merge several look-alike signs (the first pass could not split them reliably).
L  upright stem with a foot bar running to the right
J  upright stem with a hook or foot to the left (mirror of L), incl. tall hooked stems
U  open-topped cup, often with a taller right stem
N  arch / gate open at the bottom
B  closed rectangular box (often with a flag at the top)
C  bracket: stem or ring with bars opening to the right, incl. ring-with-hook forms
P  stem ending in a closed loop (like a 6)
Z  zigzag (Z / S-angular), incl. stem with curved top bar
H  stem carrying side bars or a diagonal (T-with-stem, K-like)
S  curved bar or wave
V  diagonal stroke(s), V or backslash with a dot
O  large ring with a cross (one clear example, L5)
c  small curl or comma (under half height)
b  horizontal bar with a thick ball end
-  dash divider (line between sign groups)
.  dot divider
?  blotted or torn: not classifiable
