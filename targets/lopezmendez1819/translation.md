# English translation (f. 227v l. 12 to f. 228r l. 9)

Bold = the cipher words, with the same hole as the reading.

So that Your Excellency may not have the least doubt of how urgent this measure is in every respect, I at once inform Your Excellency, with great sorrow, that **I have been jailed five times and have come out on bail**. The matter on all these occasions was so handled that only a few persons knew of it, and it has not spread.

**Mac Gregor** has done untold harm to our cause. Formerly there was a fairly favourable idea of him, and I held it too, and on the strength of it I joined an expedition, **for [?Maracaibo]**. I left it, because I had the fullest evidence that his intention was another, which was going to cause us much harm.

He is thinking of going, and making efforts to go, to **Santafé**. I do not know whether he could manage it, but I do know that he is quite determined to land on the coast and to enter all that territory, making himself Supreme Chief of it.
