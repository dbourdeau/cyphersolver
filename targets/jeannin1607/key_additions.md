# Additions to Lasry’s System A chart

Use with [George Lasry’s reconstructed chart](lasry_p084_1.png). A caret indicates an overbar and two periods indicate two dots above the group. The [source locations](SOURCES.md) give manuscript links and attestations. The [verifier](verify.py) records the chart values used in the samples separately from these additions.
