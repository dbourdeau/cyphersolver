116v.5 | dd Xlow U3 longS x^ 84^ U3 fCurl eps U2 ba 96^ eq x^ 56^ eq 90^ U3 fStraight ro
116v.6 | 94^ c^ longS cross no hook^ 54^ eq U2 dots3 zLoop cross ba fCurl tallLoop eps ba hook uf ro

The short horizontal stroke after the final ro is preserved here as an
end-of-line mark. It is outside the cipher token stream. Spaces separate
signs, never words. ^ denotes a horizontal overbar. Labels describe shape
or are mnemonic lookalikes. They do not give the sign's plaintext value.
ba and ro distinguish the closed-loop and open-lettered forms of r.
U3 is the curl with three short strokes. U2 is the two-stroke curl.
Xlow is the squat ornamental x. cross is the wide, slanted cross.
zLoop is the descending 3-like sign. x^ and hook^ are separate
nomenclator signs. eq is a literal two-stroke sign resembling =.
90^ is a single barred numeral group. Its expansion is inferred.

113r.penultimate-tail | oval zLoop loopCross dots3 loopCross eq rr 53^ x^

oval is the small round a sign. dots4 is the square of four dots.
loopCross is the small circle below a crossed stem and repeats the
previous sign. nn is the two-humped whole-word group. qStem is the
long descending letter sign with two short cross strokes. pComma is
the taller syllable sign followed by a comma.
