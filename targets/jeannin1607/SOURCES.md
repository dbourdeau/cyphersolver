## Printed witnesses and scope

- Pierre Jeannin, *Négociations diplomatiques et politiques du président Jeannin (1598–1620)*, with a notice by J.-A.-C. Buchon (Paris, Abel Pilon, 1875), p. 88, bottom of the left column. The direct page image was checked against the OCR. The printed reading is *les navires*. The date and signatures appear at the end of the letter near the top of the right column. Villeroy’s letter of 15 June 1607, which includes the f. 87v–88r text, is also present in this edition. [Page 88 in the reader](https://archive.org/details/negociationsdiplomatiquesetpoli/page/n113/mode/1up) · [Direct page scan](https://archive.org/download/negociationsdiplomatiquesetpoli/page/n113/mode/1up) · [Searchable OCR](https://archive.org/download/negociationsdiplomatiquesetpoli/negociationsdiplomatiquesetpoli_djvu.txt)
- Pierre Jeannin, *Les negotiations de monsieur le president Ieannin* (Paris, Pierre Le Petit, 1656). The first-edition page image on p. 72 has *qu’ils ayent occasion* in Villeroy’s 15 June letter. Page 96 has *les Nauires* in Henri IV’s 8 July letter. Both were checked in the page images. [1656, p. 72](https://books.google.com/books?id=yahDAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA72) · [1656, p. 96](https://books.google.com/books?id=yahDAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA96)
- Pierre Jeannin, *Négociations diplomatiques et politiques du président Jeannin* (1819), vol. 1, p. 222, bottom. The visible type reads *qu’ils ayent occasion*. The Dieppe reading in this edition remains unverified. [1819, vol. 1, p. 222](https://books.google.com/books?id=10cUAAAAYAAJ&pg=PA222)
- The BnF catalogue identifies other editions. The Biblissima notice identifies another manuscript copy of the broader negotiations. The study compares the digitized manuscript with the editions cited here.
