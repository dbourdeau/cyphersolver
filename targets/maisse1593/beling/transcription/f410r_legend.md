# f. 410r cipher transcription: legend (reader c, 11 Nov 2026)

Companion to `f410r_cipher.tsv`. These readings go by shape only. The key was used only to name signs. No clear text was consulted.

## Line numbering
- l. 1 = "Mons. de Maisse, depuis ma derniere ..."; l. 9 ends "de ma bonne grace et faveur".
- The cipher starts at l. 10 and fills the rest of the recto down to **l. 31**. That is 22 cipher lines, not the ~24 the overview suggested. Line pitch ≈ 136 px native; l. 10 baseline ≈ y 2442 and l. 31 ≈ y 5300. Text faintly visible below l. 31 is show-through from the verso.
- WAINUT's f410 l.10 and l.11 match l. 10 and l. 11 under this numbering. Each sign he gives matches mine. He stopped short of the end of both lines: l. 10 has two more signs (`8 y`), and l. 11 has eight more (`6b? T La 10 de~ 8 y 16 E~`). His `UNK` on l. 11 is my `16?`.

## Prefixes
- `^x` = written above the line. `#x` = over-inked or blotted. `-x` = struck through.
- `m:x` = sign in the right margin past the line end, at the paper edge. Four are raised: `m:^d` (l. 23), `m:^6?` (l. 24), `m:^3?` (l. 25). Two sit at line level or below: `m:y?` (l. 26) and `m:x?` (l. 28). They may not belong to the line; drop them if they do not fit.

## Recurrent doubts (shape pairs hard to tell apart)
1. **`2+` vs figure `4`**: `2+` is a Z-topped crossed sign, and in figures like 14 and 24 the 4 has a closed triangle. Where the top is not clear the reading is marked (l. 13 `2+?`). The free-standing 4 on l. 19 is closed.
2. **`to` vs `varpi`**: `to` has a t-ascender above the bar, and `varpi` has a flat bar over two bowls. l. 16 `to?` is in between.
3. **`E~` vs `f~` vs `Z3`**: the long s with a mid-loop (`E~`, about 112 times) sometimes has a straight cross-bar (l. 12 `E~|f~`). `f~` (l. 24) is a crossed long s without a loop. `Z3` (l. 18) has a flat top.
4. **`9` vs `que` vs `19`**: `que` has a straight vertical tail. Figure 9 with a long swash tail curling left occurs alone (l. 12 ×2, l. 16). Most `19`s are written `1` + that swash 9.
5. **`16` vs `16?` (dotted)**: l. 11, 12 and 31 have a 1 + open-bowled 6 with a dot, or a dot-like 1. Read as 16 with doubt. WAINUT's `UNK` on l. 11 is one of these.
6. **`y` vs `gam` vs `Y`**: `y` has its tail curling left under the line, `gam` has a closed bottom loop with the tail to the right, and `Y` is a cup on a straight stem.
7. **`26` vs `2`/`z` + 6**: the 2 sometimes has a flat z-like top (l. 15 `26`). Always read 26 when tight.
8. **`d` variants**: d is sometimes written as c + l (l. 25) or with a looped ascender (l. 23). All are read as `d`.
9. **`Dot` without dot** (l. 25 `Dot?`), and `Lambda?` on l. 30, a looped λ, unlike the plain caret.
10. **`phi`** (l. 10) follows WAINUT. The key's `phi` (i) and `ephi` (e) are both φ-like and could not be told apart on shape.

## Corrections and odd features
- l. 15: a blotted and struck group, `#Cm? 3 s5? desp? -w? -E~`. Only the last two signs are clearly cancelled, by one horizontal stroke.
- l. 21: `#E~? ^19`, an over-inked sign with 19 written above it, probably a correction.
- l. 22: `-6? f~?`, a blotted 6 struck by a long stroke that ends in an f-like sign.
- l. 26: `#8?`, an over-inked figure.
- Raised small signs inside lines: `^de~` (l. 18, followed by a full stop), `^ct` (l. 22), `^8` ×2 (l. 29), `^d` (l. 30).
- l. 20: a thin horizontal stroke runs through `d E~`. It may be a connecting stroke or a strike.
- Free dots: l. 12 between `Lambda` and `z`, l. 13 after the last group, l. 18 at the end, and l. 30 before the first sign.
- The first sign of l. 19 and of l. 20 lies in or against the BnF stamp.
