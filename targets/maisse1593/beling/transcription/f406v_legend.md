## Naming decisions to check first
- **`de~`**: a short stroke plus a ∂-like loop whose ascender arches back over to the left, sometimes as a big arch over an ω. The ∂ part never stands alone, so I took the pair as one looped-ω sign. Another reader may write it `1 d`. It occurs 18 times.
- **`w`**: a minim plus a cup or ω, which I tokenised as one sign. At least once (l. 8) there are clearly three minims (`w|uu`). I did not count minims at every occurrence, so some `w` may be `uu` (m), or `1 w`.
- **`ephi`**: the commonest letter-sign. It has a small top loop, a crossing curl at the left and a stem ending in a heavy foot, which matches the key's row-5 sign under e. Tall bold versions are marked `ephi?`, and could be `f_` (long s) or `g8`.
- **`y`**: every looped y-like sign, without separating `y` (o) from the key's `yy` (y). `yy?` is used only for the zeta-like form with a long tail sweeping left (ll. 8, 12).
- Figure groups (`14`, `16`, `18`, `19`, `10`) are tokenised as figures where they are written tight. Where a `1` stands apart before a non-figure sign, it is `1` or `1?`.

## Shape pairs that are hard to tell apart
- `x` (small, ꝛ-like) vs `pi` (x with a top bar) vs `alpha` (bold ∝): `pi|x` once.
- `24` (the 4 closed) vs `2+` (an open cross through the tail of the 2). Tomokiyo draws `2+` looking like "24", so any `24` could be `2+`.
- `que` (a loop on a long straight stem) vs `rho2` (a loop with a tail curling left).
- `26` with a closed, a-like 2 vs `a~ 6` (ll. 4, 7: `26?`).
- `2` vs `z` (`2|z`); the £-like form is `z`.
- `phi?` ("ꝕ", a 4-like loop on a long stem) vs `q~` (a q with a bar across the descender) vs figure `4`.
- `8` (flat-topped "ɤ") vs `E~`. I read every flat-topped looped figure as 8.
- `6b` (barred b with a top loop) vs `h`.
- `T`, and once `T?` (Γ-shaped).
