## Recurrent doubts and how I decided them

1. **`w` vs `de~`.** The page has two classes of ω with a leading minim. In the flat class the right bowl stays at x-height; these are `w` (10 cases). In the other class the right stroke rises above the minim and arches back left over the bowl; these are `de~` (32 cases). Three middling cases (ll. 3, 6 and 7) have a smaller arch and went to `de~`. The comparison montage is `work/f_round2/mont_w_de.jpg`.
2. **`>o|20`** (ll. 17, 18, 22, 30). The head is z-like, with a flat top and a foot, and is joined to o. Neither the `>o` curl nor a rounded 2 fits cleanly. All four are followed by `uy`. The `>o` with a true hooked curl is kept as `>o` (11 cases). See `work/f_round2/mont_gto_20.jpg`.
3. **Dots.** Free dots and strokes before a sign are written `1?`, as are the stroke or dot before `k`, following the catalogue. Dots sitting on the foot of a preceding `k` are very common (`k.`), so they are put in the notes, not tokenised. The same goes for dots above signs (`pi`, `0`, `de~`). Two or three dots in a group are written `dots3`.
4. **`E~` with a crossbar.** Several long-s signs with the top loop also have a full crossbar. Most are left as `E~`; two where the bar dominates are `E~|f~` (ll. 8, 24).
5. **`22` vs `22?`.** Where the two figures have round tops they are `22`. Two linked z/2 shapes that look like "vr" are `22?` (ll. 10, 34).
6. **`yy`.** Here `yy` often has a cup (Ψ-like) head and a crossbar, with the stem ending in a foot turned right (ll. 4, 26, 27, 29). It is kept as `yy`, not `Y`, because of that foot.
7. **`0 y`** (ll. 3, 5, 7). A small o is joined by a hairline to a following y. I wrote two tokens; it could be one sign.
8. **`5? 1`** (ll. 4, 16). A small s-curve is followed by a minim, the second time with a dot above. I read it as a figure 5 without its flag, but it may be a separate sign.
9. **`26?`** (ll. 17, 22). The 2 is written as a closed loop (α- or e-like) before the b-like 6.
10. **`D~`.** Besides the θ with a bar, a t-like form (tall stem, crossbar, bowl at the foot) is also written `D~` (ll. 9, 21).

- **Long vertical stroke.** On l. 31 a long vertical stroke crosses the whole line, with the following 6 joined to its foot. I wrote it `16?`; it may instead be a mark.
- **Margin marks** (not tokenised):

  - a short dash in the left margin between ll. 31 and 32
- **Show-through and offsets.**
  - The left margin shows mirror-image clear writing from the verso and a faint library stamp ring near ll. 23-25. Neither touches the cipher.
  - Faint grey bands run above and below `23 0 6~ +` on l. 35; they are offset or show-through, not a strike.
- **Gutter and fold.** No sign is lost at the gutter: text starts about 100 px right of the crop edge. Every line ends before the page fold, at right-page x ~3530.
