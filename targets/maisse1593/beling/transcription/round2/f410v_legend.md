## Recurrent doubts

1. **`va?` (ll. 3, 4, 14).** A bold minim joined to a closed a-bowl, with no descender. One clear `va` (u/v + small a) appears on l. 25. The doubtful ones could be `va`, `mm` or `my`.
2. **`8|Z3` (ll. 19, 20).** A flat top bar with a small loop at its left and a bowl below. It is open at the right on l. 19 and closed on l. 20. Flat-topped 8s with long top bars are common on the page (ll. 26, 28, 29), so these may simply be `8`.
3. **`2+` against `24` against `2 +`.** The joined sign (a 2 whose foot is crossed by a slanted descender) is written `2+` (19). A 2 standing apart from a closed 4 is `24` (10). A 2 standing apart from an upright cross is `2 +` (l. 7). Three gutter cases are left open: `2+|24` and `2+|4` (ll. 13, 16) and `2?` (ll. 11, 33).
4. **`Sr?` (ll. 8, 11, 33).** A short stem with a hooked or flourished top, ringed by three dots (left, right, below). It fits the catalogue's `Sr` (a l. 38 only) but is not the same drawing. It may be a punctuation or separator group.
5. **`III a~?` (ll. 12, 16) and `III a~` (ll. 6, 24).** A long thin slash rises from below the line into two minims, followed by a separate small a. On ll. 12 and 16 the a is open, like a c. `III` without an a follows on ll. 12, 21, 28 and 30.
6. **`#+?` (ll. 21, 31) and `#q~?` (l. 2).** These are heavily inked cross-like signs. On l. 2 and l. 5 (`4?`), a horizontal stroke runs from a blot through the sign into the next `E~`. That may be a strike-through.
7. **Gutter.** The text runs into the binding gutter at x≈3705, and the last sign of many lines is cut, including ll. 10, 15, 17, 20, 21, 24, 26, 28, 29, 30, 32 and 33. These are marked `?` or `[?]`. Signs near the gutter are often written smaller.
8. **Dots.** Small dots are frequent:
   - after the bar of `+`
   - inside `2.6`
   - after `k`
   - above or below signs.

   Only a free dot at x-height standing before a sign is tokenised as `1?` (ll. 4, 10, 20, 21). The rest are listed in the notes. `uy` always includes its leading dot or stroke.

## Odd features

- **Large `de~`.** At the start of ll. 1 and 20 and inside ll. 6 and 7, `de~` takes a large ornate form: an ω with a loop arching back over the whole sign. The ordinary form is a minim + ∂.
- **Underlines and `^arch`.** A thick stroke runs from the left margin under the first two signs of l. 21. Another underlines `23` on l. 30. `^arch` (a cap-shaped loop above the line) stands over `E~` near the end of l. 1 and over `pi` on l. 29.
- **Left-margin marks** (not tokenised, noted on their lines):
  - l. 2: a short stroke with a dot
  - l. 4: a faint stroke
  - l. 8: two strokes joined at the top
- **Blots.** There is a heavy blot between ll. 23 and 24, and a blot on a crease in l. 10 (`#[?]`). A round blot before the last sign of l. 15 is also `#[?]`.
- **Spacing.** On l. 32, two dots (`dots3`) are followed by a blank of about one sign width. On l. 33, a faint vertical line (ruling or crease) stands after `Sr?`, and the last three signs are written smaller.
- **Descenders.** The long descender of `my` on l. 21 reaches into l. 22. The tail of `19` on l. 23 reaches above `to` on l. 24.
