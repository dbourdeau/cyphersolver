## English
"The free man of my republic. Nature has built him the most beautiful palace — his little art makes itself comfortable for him. With a grateful look he praises his Creator, and goes cheerfully to work. The mother is a mother — children are children — no prince, no senate rules them — the laws of nature are stamped on their hearts — everything belongs to all. To honour the All-Father, several built him a temple; but they praise God through their whole conduct."
Vienna, Oct. 178?. Dreamt and drawn by Paul M…ll…ieur.

## Probable words
- freye: 4th sign close to s; 'freye' is the usual 18th-century spelling.
- Republick: two-stroke capital R; b and p share one sign; '-blick' sure.
- wenige: the flat-oval w occurs only here; 5th sign a flourished 8 between t and g.
- ihn: last sign looks like n ('ihm' would fit as well).
- bekuem: the writer has no q.
- Senat: a joining stroke between e and n.
- gehoert: one extra mark before t (blind pass: 'gehöret').
- einen: blind pass read 'seinen'; same sense.
- Oct: capital O, c, t.
- Getraeumt: 9 signs g-e-t-r-a-e-u-m-t; the blind pass did not read this word.
- Paul: P-a-u-l.

## Unresolved
- The surname (9 signs, M a l l ? i e u r in both passes): no name fits; perhaps a Masonic or Arcadian pseudonym. The album's register (fol. 73v-77v) numbers entries, not folios (entry 28 is 'v. Laibel', entry 31 'Hauslab', while fol. 31r is signed by a 'K.K. Börse- u. Wechsel-Censal'), so it does not name the writer of fol. 28r directly.
- The year: disguised digits (a 7 turned round, a plain 8); the first and last signs look alike.
