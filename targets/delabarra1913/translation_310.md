# X-1.4.310.1: translation (English)

Telegram, New York, 11 August 1913, Francisco León de la Barra to the Secretaría de Relaciones Exteriores, Mexico.
Words in cipher in the original are in italics.

"Mr. *Long*, chief of the *section of Latin-American affairs* of the *Department of State*, *came* yesterday from
*Washington* to hold an *interview with me*. Surely [he was] *sent* by Messrs *Wilson and Bryan*. Tell him that I
*would* gladly *confer* with these gentlemen if I *received* a formal *invitation*. If I *receive it*, I *shall leave*
[for] *Europe* next *Saturday*; if I do not *receive it* tomorrow, I *shall leave* on *Wednesday*. Point out to him
in suitable form the reasons of justice and expediency of *immediate recognition*. Delabarra."

No holes. "Bryan" rests on a marked repair (rr for y); "next" (próximo) rests on a key value taken from this word.
