# Sign conventions: X-1.4.310.1 (written before any key was applied)

Two copies of one telegram: image 0001 in pencil (cursive Spanish hand), image 0002 typed. The cipher words are lower-case
Latin letters inside clear Spanish. Each sign is transcribed as the letter it is written as; no values are implied.

- Clear words are written `=word=` as they stand (accents as written: Señor, mañana; manifestele, Indiquele without accent).
- Cipher words are written as single tokens with the pen copy's spacing. The typist regrouped the letters freely
  (umnoti qezi -> umnotiqezi); spacing is not part of the cipher.
- Pen-hand shapes that need care:
  - `q` is written like a figure 9 (99omrezpniz in the pen copy, qqomrezpniz typed).
  - `u` / `n`: u open at the top, n open at the foot; the typed copy decides where the pen is unclear.
  - `z` is the looped z with a descender; `g` has an open lower loop.
  - `t` carries a bar (tudognojuzni, juteoni); `l` is a plain tall loop (hizlug).
- Pass 2 is the typed copy, read as a separate witness; disagreements between the copies are listed in
  transcription_310.txt.
