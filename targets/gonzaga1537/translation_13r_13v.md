## Historical Notes

- **Attribution of voice:** The date of 6 May 1537 belongs to Federico II Gonzaga's clear covering letter on `c. 12r`, which introduces `c. 13r–13v` as *"copia d'avisi ch'ho di verso Piamonte qual gli mando allegata"* (*"copy of dispatches I have from toward Piedmont, which I send enclosed"*). Although written by Federico II's Mantuan chancery secretary in the Gonzaga cipher, the first-person remarks (*"ch'io non ho parlato di sopra senza raggione"*, *"Me l'ha detto il vescovo dela Barba in secreto"*) reflect the voice of the copied Piedmont informant rather than necessarily Federico II himself.
- **Fall of Hesdin (`la fortezza de Edim`, L19):** During Francis I's spring 1537 Artois campaign, the town and castle of Hesdin (*Edim*) capitulated to the French on **13–14 April 1537** (confirmed by Francis I's letter 55 of 14 April 1537 to Henry VIII in David Potter's edition, and the IRHT manuscript record dating the capture to 13 April). Arrival of this news in northern Italy fits a late April / early May 1537 enclosure forwarded on 6 May.
- **Named historical figures (contextual identifications of the manuscript's shorter references):** *Il vescovo de Casale* (L14) / *il vescovo dela Barba* (L20) is contextually identified with Bernardino Castellari, called Bernardino della Barba (birth year uncertain; d. 27 June 1546, *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani*), Bishop of Casale Monferrato; *el Speciano* (L15) with Giovanni Battista Speciano (1490–1544), Imperial *Capitano di Giustizia* and Ducal Senator of Milan; *Il Iovio* (L21) with Paolo Giovio (1483–1552), Bishop of Nocera and historian.
