Summary: the writers at Louis XII's court report that once Rocciafort arrives the King will send an ambassador to Rome (believed to be the bishop of "Lancres"), empowered to negotiate everything with the Pope, and that their informant (code .51.) expects the same quarrel as the present one between the Pope and the bishop of Marseille; after Alfonso Ariosti's visit for the Duke of Ferrara, the King asked them to approach the Venetian ambassador so that the Signoria would give back Monselice, Este and Montagnana, which the Este hold as imperial fiefs.
