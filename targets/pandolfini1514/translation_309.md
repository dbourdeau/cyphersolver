# Translation (English): ASF MAP LXXII 309, 26 Feb 1515

From the reading by sense (`reading_309.md`). The gaps are real; {x} probable and ‹…› unresolved are in the Italian reading.

Letter from Francesco Pandolfini, Florentine orator at the French court, to Lorenzo de' Medici, Paris, 26 February 1515. In clear
he refers Lorenzo to the enclosed copy of his letter to the Otto (no. 310) and assures him of the King's good disposition towards
the Pope, Florence and the Medici. The cipher passages add: (A) proof of it, besides the King's words, are the offers Madame
d'Angoulême (Louise of Savoy) made a few days ago to the papal orator, about the ".48.", of a marriage between the daughter of the
Duke of Valentinois (Louise Borgia) and Lorenzo himself; (B) the King is eager to see what resolution and declaration the Pope will
make regarding the newly concluded league, from which he expects to learn the Pope's real mind towards him. Part of block B is unread.

Key v6 reads A3 "del parentado della figlia del duca Valentino per vostra Magnificentia": the marriage of the Duke of Valentinois's daughter *for* Lorenzo. A Borgia match for Lorenzo is mentioned in the literature, but this offer by Louise of Savoy (Feb 1515) is still to be checked in the printed sources: nothing is claimed beyond the text.
