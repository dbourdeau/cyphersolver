# Translation (English): ASF MAP LXXII 311, 4 Mar 1515

From the reading by sense (`reading_311.md`). The gaps are real; {x} probable and ‹…› unresolved are in the Italian reading.

Continuation ("kept back to the 4th", March 1515) of a letter from Pandolfini to the Otto, sent via Lyon. (A) The court will not leave
Paris until the agreement with England is concluded. (B) There is talk that the Duke of Suffolk will marry the "white queen" Mary
Tudor, Louis XII's widow; the rumour is growing and English members of the queen's household complain of it. (C) Letters from Rome of
the 24th report, from a sure source, that the Pope and the Florentine Signoria have joined the newly concluded league; the news is all
over the court. At the request of the Florentine consul in Lyon, Pandolfini asked the King not to believe it; the King answered that,
given Florence's loyalty to the Crown, he would not believe it until he saw it. Yet the Roman informant is well placed (through the
Pope, the Emperor's and the Catholic King's ambassadors, and someone who saw the letter), and the King may privately hold it as
certain. (P.S.) The King has been told that the Pope and Florence joined and signed the league, which pays 30,000 ducats a month to
the Swiss and 30,000 to the Viceroy of Naples, shared equally by the Pope and two others (codes .16., .17.). Pandolfini doubts it but
fears the Pope will arm the Emperor and the Catholic King in Italy; if confirmed, the King's goodwill towards Florence could turn, and
he might even come to terms with the Emperor and the Catholic King, who reserved that right in their capitulation with the Swiss.
