# Translation (English): ASF MAP LXXII 333, 27 Dec 1514

From the reading by sense (`reading_333.md`). Every word read; the content is also given in clear in 332.

Pandolfini to Lorenzo de' Medici, Paris, 27 December 1514: he writes only to send the copy of his letter to the Otto. In cipher: on
this side (the French court) there is little of weight to report for now; everything hangs on the decisions to be taken "there" (in
Rome), which are awaited here with very great suspicion.
Full translation of the cipher block: "On this side there is at present little of moment to report, everything depending on the resolutions that will be taken there [in Rome], about which we are here in very great suspicion."
