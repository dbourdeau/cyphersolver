# Labels as in n57_transcription.txt. New labels used here:
#  mt  = "m+" (m-like stroke crossed by +) - a third homophone for p (providencia, paz, propósito, podreys, proteccion)
#  tt  = crossed double-t (like H/ħħ) - homophone for r (escusar, procurareys)
#  /   = free-standing long s / virgule between words - probably punctuation (appears after -cia, venecianos)
#  oh  = small o with double bar (ᵒ‡), in "las [oh] lanças" - unresolved
#  Vs  = "three bars over a small o" (≢) - a separate sign from V (z with one bar = u/v).
#        Value looks like s: retirasen (N57), estoviesen, posible, viese, siguiesen, desseara, pasar.
#        Two places want v (proveer, Gurca), so it may be a second v-homophone instead.
#  Vd  = bars-over-o with a dot, once only (L1201), value unknown
#  X4  = the blot-like x sign (dexeys, dexara)
#  4h also covers the short "ɼ4" form (pase, siente, aprovechar)
#  40 also covers the 4-with-curl "ɥ" form (seguirle, ventura, concordia)
#  Uncertain: q reads m in mando/campo/cumple/mundo/fagamos/rompimiento but a in
#  atajada/dilataran/pasar/estrecha - either two near-identical signs or a second value.
