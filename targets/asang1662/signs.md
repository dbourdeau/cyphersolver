# Sign groups (code letter, shape, description)

Each letter of the plaintext is one composite group of three or four strokes.

- A: ☐78O — square with vertical bars + 78 + O
- B: 15☐c — 15 + square crossed diagonally + c
- C: Z47z — tailed Z + 47 + z
- D: ⊕di6 — crossed circle + d i 6 (a cross above = ą)
- E: ʃ37H — long s + 37 + H-shaped box
- F: 47qŦ — 47 + q + barred E/F
- G: 50lF — 50 + l + F
- H: B14ℓ — B + 14 + looped l with a ring
- I: ∃a17 — reversed E + a 17
- J: 83yΔ — 83 + y + filled triangle
- K: F39λ — F + 39 + lambda
- L: S14€ — S + 14 + looped euro-like sign
- M: 93h⋕ — 93 + h + double-barred hash
- N: 12+n — 12 + cross + n
- O: ϕt33 — rho with a small ring + t 33
- O2: Ꝑt33 — same group with a large-loop P (once, reverse l. 2)
- P: 70▽u — 70 + open inverted triangle + u
- Q: 55▽t — 55 + inverted triangle + t
- R: ȯkzo — o with a cross above + k z o
- S: Ꝝeig — barred lozenge 8 + e i g
- T: x-69 — barred x + dash + 69
- U: gꝐ50 — g with a large P loop + 50
- W: Mig4 — M i g + barred 4 (once, reverse l. 4)
