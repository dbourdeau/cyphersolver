# Check of the contributor's key and reading (9 Nov 2026)

What an AI model (Claude) checked of the submission by William Echols (issue #351). The CEHM scans and the key card are not reachable from the project's environment, so **no transcription and no key card was compared with a scan by the project**. No human has repeated the check.

## 1. The key

`key.tsv` has five pairs, L-A, N-E, R-I, S-O and T-U, each swapping in both directions, so the key is an involution on ten letters and leaves the others unchanged.

## 2. The decryption

The four coded words VRAALIINLA, OLADIL, AANVLEDS and CLESE (10, 6, 8 and 5 letters, 29 in all) were decrypted again with the pairs by an own script: VILLARREAL, SALDRA, LLEVANDO and CANON. All four agree with `decryption.txt` and make Spanish words that fit the clear text around them ("... SALDRA miércoles ó jueves, LLEVANDO CANON"). A wrong key of this kind would not give four words that join the clear sentences.
