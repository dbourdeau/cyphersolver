# guarna1447 - reading of Nicolò Guarna to Francesco Sforza, Milan, 21 May 1447 (BnF ital. 1584, ff. 153r-154r)

Read in part (5 Nov 2026). Counted by script on the lines below (cipher segments only; the clear frame of the letter is not counted): **852 words: 583 certain, 33 probable, 0 restored, 236 unresolved** (the unresolved count is an estimate, see below), so 616 = **72.3%** certain or probable. Under 95%: read in part. Our working notes gave 862 words and 72.6%; the recount by script on this file, counting each word split at a line end once, gives the figures above.

Conventions (spelling of the cipher kept; 15th-century Lombard Italian):
- a bare word is **certain** (given by key K3, clear sense);
- `{word}` is **probable** (one doubtful sign, or one of the sign's other values);
- `<word>` would be **restored** (none here);
- `[?n]` is a stretch of **about n unresolved words**: n is estimated as the length of the decoded stretch divided by 5, because a stretch that does not read cannot be cut into words. The total of 236 unresolved words is therefore an estimate.

The end of the letter (f. 153v from about V47b, and f. 154r) agrees word for word with the 15th-century clear text on f. 156 of the same day ("le gente de Cremona restano … fra otto o diece di, o no prima … piu uno fiorino per uno gran spacio … ultimum de potentia de venire presto"). That part is therefore **not new**: it was in clear in the volume. The rest (f. 153r and f. 153v to about l. 46) has no known decipherment.
