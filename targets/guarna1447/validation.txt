guarna1447 - tests of the Guarna key (contributor's protocols, not docs/_validate.py)

Key material. No Guarna key was known (Daniel Bourdeau reads Guarna's leaves by their late clear copies and publishes no table).
The key was rebuilt here from the cipher of Guarna's letter of 27 May 1447 (ff. 163r ll. 6-50 and 163v ll. 1-2, later
163v l. 3 to the end), aligned with its 18th-century clear copy (ff. 161r-162v; 165r). The protocol (PROTOCOLE-CRIBS,
aead6d7d...) fixed the held-out block H before any key work.

Test 1 - lexicon coverage on a held-out block of the target letter
- H = f. 153r ll. 1-20 of the letter of 21 May (ll. 1 and 20 clear only), transcribed by shapes and hashed (95e803c0...).
- Key K frozen (9203ccf9...); amendment K2 (^ = t, W = l, 2# = q, three signs of the pair forgotten in K; 10352f61...),
  amendment 2 (metric) and amendment 3 (controls) written and hashed before H was viewed.
- M5 = share of decoded letters covered by Italian words of 5+ letters. Controls: 200 keys permuting the value lists of the
  one-letter signs.
- Result (one measurement): M5 = 0.441 (297/673); controls max 0.118, mean 0.054; gap 0.323.
  Thresholds (fixed): >= 0.60 and >= +0.30 validated; gap alone partial. DECISION: PARTIAL.
  Likely cause of the low absolute value: 15th-century Lombard spelling (single consonants: dovese, potese, spese) is poorly
  covered by the modern lexicon.
- Slip, declared: line 20 of H (clear only, no cipher sign) was seen before H was frozen (a modern heading was counted as a
  line). It carries no cipher letter and does not enter M5.

Test 2 - characters against a contemporary clear, on a new held-out block of the training letter
- Amendment 4 (082aaf15...), written and hashed before K3: H' = all cipher segments of f. 164r (letter of 27 May), cut at
  clear anchor words; reference = the late copy f. 165r; segments whose reference has the copyist's dotted blank excluded.
- Declared: f. 165r was seen at low resolution (legible) while choosing H'. It was not used for the key or for the language
  model (K3 = K2 + values from 163v only; language model = the copy of ff. 161-162 only). f. 164r was seen only at quarter
  resolution, where the signs cannot be read.
- Key K3 frozen (8a16f1de...); tokens of 164r and the references hashed before decoding.
- Metric: 1 - Levenshtein distance / reference length, after normalisation. Controls: 200 keys (seeds 1447-1646), same
  permutation rule.
- Result (one measurement): CHAR = 0.759 (78 edits / 324); controls max 0.315, mean 0.226; gap 0.445.
  Thresholds (fixed): >= 0.85 and >= +0.30 validated; gap alone partial. DECISION: PARTIAL.
  Whole phrases come out right ("ne deliberatione veruna pure se ne fa ... in generali", "de li inimici", "del vostro venire",
  "li di passati"). Most errors come from look-alike signs not separated on the scans (h-like sign = 4 or hk; sigma / o with a
  hook; t / T / z), and from "dinari" written in clear [spes] inside the cipher.

External agreement (not a pre-registered test)
- The end of the cipher of 21 May (f. 153v about l. 46 to f. 154r l. 3), decoded by K2, agrees word for word with the
  15th-century clear text of f. 156 of the same day. That clear was not used to build the key.

Pending

  has not been answered. No new held-out block is left in the training pair (H and H' are used).
