# No. 207, Kerlérec to the Minister of the Marine, New Orleans, 13 July 1759 (first copy): the coded passage

ANOM COL C13A 41, ff. 87-98v, a long despatch in clear; the coded passage runs from f. 97r (image
FRANOM50_C13A_41_0194, not 0193, which is f. 96v) to the first two lines of f. 97v (image 0195).
Code transcription: `transcriptions/unread/no207_reconciled.txt`; raw decode `readings/no207_decoded_raw.txt`.

**Groups.** 191 legible code groups (f. 97r 14 lines, f. 97v 2 lines), all decoded (100%); 2 groups lost in the gutter
at the ends of the two lines on f. 97v, so at most 191/193 = 99% recovered. One plain word inside the code (*Enseigne*).

## 2. Normalised French reading

[... en leur disant qu'autrefois le poste] **défendoit directement l'entrée du fleuve ; c'étoit sans doute le poste
d'honneur ; que même en temps des guerres passées il y a[voi]t toujours eu un capitaine à ce titre, mais qu'à présent
il étoit à peine bon pour un Enseigne, puisqu'il étoit absolument hors de portée de battre l'entrée du fleuve par le
changement survenu de son cours ; que lorsqu'il la protégeoit il y avoit trente et quelques canons, et qu'aujourd'huy il
y en avoit deux, tant bons que mauvais, pour répondre aux signaux des bâtiments ; et qu'enfin mes ordres étoient
d'évacuer ce poste sans difficulté à [la] vue d'un certain nombre d'ennemis [??].** (Kerlérec is reporting what he told the officers.) Je terminay enfin cette conversation
par dire que ...

## 3. English translation

[... telling them that formerly the post] **commanded the very entrance of the river; it was then no doubt the post of
honour; that even in the past wars there had always been a captain in that capacity, but that now it was hardly fit for
an ensign, since it was quite out of range to cover the entrance of the river because of the change in the river's
course; that when it guarded that entrance it had some thirty-odd cannon, and that today it had two, good and bad
together, to answer the signals of ships; and finally that my orders were to evacuate the post without difficulty
at the sight of a certain number of enemies [??].** I finally ended this conversation by saying that ...

## Words that rest on Pusch's values alone

*évacuer* (va 479, cu 208), *ordres* (247), *canon* (485), *tant bons que mauvais* (tant 229, bons, mauvais) decode only
with Pusch's values; *deux*, *trente*, *fleuve*, *poste*, *capitaine* and *Enseigne* (clear) do not need them.
Pusch 2008 p. 475 already summarised this passage (two cannon kept for signals; commander directed to abandon the post
in the event of an English attack); he quotes no French.

## Doubtful points (reading)

- f. 97r l. 4, group 2: written 666 (*doute*) with its first digit gone over; *avoit* needs 636 (*voi*), which the same
  clerk writes in l. 12 (also retouched). Taken as 636.
- f. 97v l. 1: last legible group half in the gutter, read "54..."; *à la vue* suggests 547 (*la*).
- Pusch 2008 p. 475 summarises this passage (Balize stripped of all but two cannon kept for signals; commander ordered
  to abandon the post if attacked). The decoding agrees with his summary.
