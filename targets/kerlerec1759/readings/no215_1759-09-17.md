# No. 215, Kerlérec to the Minister of the Marine, New Orleans, 17 September 1759 (second copy)

ANOM COL C13A 41, ff. 111r-111v (images FRANOM50_C13A_41_0222-0223). Pusch 2008 reproduces the first lines of this
leaf as his example of the cipher (p. 465) but prints no decoding.
Code transcription: `transcriptions/unread/no215_reconciled.txt`; raw decode `readings/no215_decoded_raw.txt`.

**Groups.** 322 legible code groups (f. 111r 224, f. 111v 98), all of which decode with the key (100%); at least 7
groups are lost in the gutter at the line ends of f. 111v, so at most 322/329 = 98% of the text is recovered. Three
decoded groups are evident clerk's slips (below).

## 2. Normalised French reading

J'ay l'honneur de vous rendre compte que près de la moitié de la [in clear up to here] **nation chactas est totalement déterminée à recevoir
chez elle des traiteurs anglois. J'ai mis [tout] en usage pour leur en fermer le passage par les nations alibamons ; il
y a plus de deux ans que j'ai fait jouer à cette occasion tous les ressorts imaginables, mais le défaut de marchandise
de traite a enfin déterminé les premiers à prendre ce parti ; et suivant les dernières lettres des commandans de
Tombekbé et des Alibamons, lesdits traiteurs anglois étoient en marche pour se rendre dans la nation chactas, et il est
très à craindre, Monseigneur, que dans ce cas ces traiteurs n'engagent quelques guerriers à frapper sur le François. Il
s'ensuivroit une guerre avec cette nation que nous ne sommes nullement en état de soutenir faute de marchandises, et la
colonie seroit dans le plus grand danger. J'ai [??] représenté sans cesse nos besoins dans cette partie, et je ne vois
rien arriver. Il [??] nous manque près de neuf cens hommes pour être au complet de 2000. Quant aux poudres de guerre,
nous sommes au moment d'en manquer totalement [??]. [Voi]là une situation bien accablante pour un gouverneur.**
Je suis avec un profond respect, Monseigneur, votre très humble et très obéissant serviteur, Kerlérec.

## 3. English translation

I have the honour to report to you that nearly half of the [in clear up to here] **Choctaw nation is altogether resolved to receive English
traders among them. I have used [every means] to close the way to them through the Alabama nations; for more than two
years I have pulled every string imaginable to this end, but the want of trade goods has at last decided the first of
them to take this course; and according to the latest letters from the commandants of Tombigbee [Fort Tombecbé] and the
Alabamas, the said English traders were on the march to enter the Choctaw nation, and it is much to be feared, my Lord,
that in that case these traders will induce some warriors to strike at the French. A war with that nation would follow,
which we are in no state to sustain for want of goods, and the colony would be in the greatest danger. I have [??]
represented our needs on this head without cease, and I see nothing arrive. We are [??] short of nearly nine hundred men
of the full complement of 2,000. As for gunpowder, we are on the point of running out of it altogether [??]. That is a
most crushing situation for a governor.**

## Words that rest on Pusch's values alone

*traiteurs* (trai 294), *hommes* (42), *poudre* (641), *quant aux* (675, 344), *guerre* (569), *grand danger*
(589, 420) and *manque* (ma 558) decode only with Pusch's values. "Près de la moitié" is in the clear opening, not in
the cipher. Choctaw, English, Alabamas, *neuf cens*, 2000 and *marchandise* do not need Pusch. Pusch 2008 gives this
despatch only as an image of its first coded lines (p. 465), with no content.

## Doubtful points (reading)

- f. 111r l. 3: 505 (*om*) where *tout* (105) is needed: clerk's slip (no. 228 of 1760 writes the same phrase *j'ay tout
  mis en usage* with 105).
- f. 111r l. 11: group 3 has a pen stroke across its first digit; read 268 (*enfin*) on both passes, but *traiteurs*
  needs 258 (u). Taken as 258.
- f. 111r l. 12: pass 1 read 245/642 (d); pass 2, checked on the image, 245/647: the lower number is 647 (c), the upper
  245 (d) is the clerk's mismatch. *craindre* needs c.
- f. 111v l. 7: 57 (*sou*) where *totalement* needs 573 (*ta*): slip or a dropped digit.
- f. 111v: groups lost at the gutter on ll. 1-4, 6-8.
