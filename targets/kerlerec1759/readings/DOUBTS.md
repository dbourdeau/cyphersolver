# Doubtful groups in nos. 206, 207 and 215: alternatives and choices

Codes with no value in any key are a two-part nomenclator's gaps: the numbers are not in plaintext order, so a value
cannot be interpolated from neighbouring codes; only context can suggest one. Each choice below is marked as a
choice; the transcription keeps what is written.

## The half-hidden group, no. 206 f. 81v l. 8

Small and cramped at the line end but fully visible, followed by a comma: "3oo," (capture `vzbj8g`; zoomed at 1000 px
on IIIF crop pct:84,32,16,8 of image 0163). The group is whole; nothing after it on that line.

- 300 as a plain numeral: "une forte frégate et 300 troupes suisses". The cipher clerk writes numerals plainly in this
  series (90 and 37 in no. 194, 2000 in no. 215). The first digit has the round top of this hand's 3, not the flat
  flag of its 5 (compare the 5 of 185 just before it).
- 300 as a code: Pusch 300 = *France*: "une forte frégate et France troupes suisses" makes no sense.
- 500 (*des*): "et des troupes suisses" makes good sense, but needs the first digit to be a 5.

Not settled. Numerals are written plainly in no. 194 (90? 37) and no. 215 (2000), and Kerlérec asked in 1754 for "au
moins trois cents Suisses" (Villiers 1904 p. 51, found by our second agent); but no. 207 uses 90 as a code (*tant*), so
numbers and codes cannot be told apart by form. The reading keeps "300 [?]": the numeral 300 or a code group.
