# Sign conventions (nunez1899)

The "cipher" is a word code: the telegram is written in ordinary Latin script, in Spanish dictionary words, some
of which stand for other words. There are no special signs. Conventions used in the transcriptions:

- One token per written word, in the order written, lower case; accents and the tilde are left out (the clerk
  writes them rarely and not consistently).
- Line labels `L01`... follow the written lines of the telegram form (pages 2-5 of the CEHM record; page 1 is the
  address leaf "ministro Limantour, 16 avenue Friedland, Paris").
- Numbers are written as the digit groups on the form (`23 13 16`, `24`).
- A full stop written by the clerk as a separate dot is kept as `.`; the final `.=` of the form is left out.
- `?` inside a word = letter not settled; `..` at the end of a word = ending hidden (watermark).
- The recipient's pencil docket written across the lines ("Ministro / Hacienda / 26 Agosto 99") is left out.
- Transcription was made with no key and no translation in view; the code values were applied afterwards
  (`scripts/decrypt.py`).
