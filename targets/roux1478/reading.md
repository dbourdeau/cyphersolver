# Fonds Roux MS 35c, f. 26v: the copyist's colophon

Source image: Nakala 10.34847/nkl.756deh54, file ROUX_MS_035c_0028.jpg (right-hand page), 300 dpi.
Crops: `colophon.jpg` (the four lines), `name_group.jpg` (the name group, four times enlarged).

Marks: `(?)` = probable word, `[...]` = one unread word, `[... n]` = n unread words (least number).

## Arabic

1. كملت الدرة بحمد الله تعالى وحسن عونه والصلاة التامة على سيدنا ومولانا محمد وعلى آله وصحبه وسلم تسليما
2. على يد العبد [...] الحقير(?) المذنب الفقير [...] الراجي عفو ربه تعالى [... 9] [... 3]
3. وكان الفراغ منها في أوائل المحرم عام ثلاثة وثمانين وثمانمائة عرفنا الله خيره بمنه وكرمه
4. كتبها لنفسه ولإخوته ثم لمن شاء الله بعده

## English

1. The Durra was completed, with praise to God Most High and by His good help, and full blessing on our lord and
   master Muhammad and on his family and companions, and abundant peace,
2. by the hand of the [...] lowly(?), sinful, poor servant [...], who hopes for the pardon of his Lord Most High,
   [the name, nine or more words, not read; three or more small words in the margin not read].
3. It was finished in the first days of Muharram of the year eight hundred and eighty-three; may God let us know its
   good, by His grace and bounty.
4. He wrote it for himself and for his brothers, then for whomever God wills after him.

## The name group (line 2, after "ربه")

Not a substitution alphabet as far as the strokes can be followed. The scribe wrote the end of line 2 in two tiers
that share the height of one line, with long parallel connecting strokes, and ran the last words up into the left
margin. Seen in colour and in a flattened blue channel with local contrast (`name_group_enhanced.jpg`).

Secure:

- upper tier, first word: تعـلى, two dots over the first letter, a long stroke to a tall lam. It closes the formula
  "عفو ربه تعالى".

Two readings of the lower tier's first word, not decided:

- محمد: a head, a long stroke, a looped blot (mim), a tail (dal). The three dots under the blot would then belong to
  what follows.
- عبيد الله: an ayn head, a long stroke, then ba and ya (one dot and two dots underneath, in that order), dal, and
  the faint uprights of الله. This accounts for the three dots; it would be the scribe's humble epithet, with the
  name proper after it.

After that, candidates only:

- a word beginning with ayn over a large bowl with one dot: عبد, or عمر followed by بن.
- a word beginning الـ with a kaf-like top stroke, descending strokes and a final mim with a tail: الكريم fits best,
  الحكيم and الرحيم are possible. With عبد before it this would give عبد الكريم.
- a small stacked group, a word with two uprights (a nisba in الـ), and tiny marginal words.

At 300 dpi the pen strokes of this group are about three pixels wide and the tiers overlap. No word of the name is
read with confidence.
