# Reading of CMXV.18.1770.2-4

**Route.** With the archive's own key card, not from the ciphertext alone.

The key is the card headed "Herrera. Moreno." in the same fonds (CMXV.16.1557.1, scan 7). One signer of this telegram is S. Herrera Moreno.

## The text

Coded words are in capitals as the card gives them. Accents and punctuation are mine.

> Ocurra [a] Julio Peña [para la] traducción. Anoche salieron tres AZENTES KINEDA norte. ZENERAL HUERTA díjome témese [en] ésta MOTÍN MILITAR CONTRA usted, causa renuncia DÍAZ. Creo necesario tome PRECAUCIONES, o no VENFA.

Fausto E. Miranda, S. Herrera Moreno. Mexico City, 19 May 1911.

## What is not settled

- "KINEDA" may be a name that I cannot fix. "Pineda" is a guess. It needs one wrong letter.

- "VENFA" is read as "venga", with one wrong letter.

- "o no" is read as clear text, typed together as "ono".

- The addressee "W. Olliphant" is an alias of Federico González Garza. The "usted" may mean him, or Madero, for whom he acted. The telegram does not say.

## Checks

- The same card reads a telegram of the next day by the same signers (CMXV.18.1777.5-6). A hand of the time glossed that telegram in pencil, and all 10 coded words agree: general Reyes, científicos, patrios, nacional, sacrificio, personalidad, Reyes, Habana, Limantour. One of them has a wrong letter (n for d).

- CEHM's record for this telegram gives "Texto en clave y palabras descifradas", with the subject "Armisticio fue firmado". It does not mention Huerta.

- No decipherment stands on the sheet. Its pencil notes do not touch the coded words.
