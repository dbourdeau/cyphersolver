print word for word. Aligned pairs located on canvas 264 (region x 0.25–0.97, y 0.15–0.85 of the page,

Confirmed cursive pairs (read at 3.2×, R264_* crops, 17 Sept 2026):

- **"absoulz du"** (pair 6): cipher `x ∞ Ⱡ ⱶⱶ o– Ŧ 12` = a(x) b(∞=oo) so(Ⱡ loop-L) u(ⱶⱶ=++) l(o–?) z(Ŧ) du(12).
  Table check: x=a ✓, oo=b ✓, ꝑ/loop=so ✓, ++=u ✓, 12=du ✓. The copyist's ∞ is the table's oo run together;
  his Ŧ (crossed T) = z matches table ±/F– family.
- **"seroient tenus"** (pair 7 end): `2o2o x m T ᏺ. g` — 20 written twice = u+u? No: gloss aligns *seroient*
  over `oo+ 6 g 18 n 2+ d 2o2o` and *tenus* over `x m T ᏺ`; here 18=ce (syllabary ✓ table), 2+=p→*present*? flag
  for re-check; T=n ✓; x=a used as final -s null? — mark uncertain.
- **"et juré à leur sacre"** (pair 8): `et(clear) Ᏽuró a ℂᵕ ᵹ sacre et ᏺ ꝉᏂ` — the words *juré*, *sacre* are
  left IN CLEAR inside the cipher line by the copyist; only small words and endings are ciphered here. So the
  Instruction cipher is a **mixed clear/cipher** text like the letters, which makes alignment much easier.
- **"La tiercem[ent]"** (pair 7 start): line-initial `λ ᏺ 3 a+ ✗`= la(λ) ... — λ=la ✓ as on f. 146v;
  `3`=ri (table) inside *tiercement*? gloss reads "La tiercem" over `λ ᏺ 3 a+`: ᏺ(h-loop)=?ti, a+=g? — flag.
- **gloss below cipher on this page** (clear beneath the symbols), unlike f. 151 (clear above).

First cursive identifications from pair 1 and 5–6 (provisional, to be confirmed by full alignment):
- line-initial `λ` at "Le second" = article *la/le* (matches table λ = la);
- `10 ꝑ E r to` under "du droit": the copyist's `E` = *r/re*, `to` = *i* as in the table; `10` here is the
  syllable *d* group, not table '10' = p — the copies renumber some figures, or the gloss placement is off
  by one symbol; do not carry table digit values to the copies without a check;
- gloss "quites et" over `L tt(#) ꝺ ſз ꝉo …`: the `ſз` tall-s+3 compound recurs where the print has *qu/que*;
- `du dw et`-shaped glosses sit *below* their cipher line on this page (cipher line first, clear beneath),
  the reverse of f. 151 where the clear is above — keep the direction straight when aligning.

## B. Henri IV to Nevers, Chartres 7 October 1593 (fr. 3986, canvases 296–297 = f. 151)

Same copyist, office decipherment interlined (clear above the cipher). Thirty common signs confirmed on
17 Sept 2026, recorded in `key60.txt` (cursive section): plusieurs, contre, de la, promesse, respondre,
il y a, Bellièvre, Villeroy, Zamet.

- **"la couronne"** (pair 8, R264_4_1): `< π ⱶⱶ ᵛ ✗ T ⊥` = c(<) o(π) u(++) r(v-flat) o(✗=X) n(T) n(⊥):
  five table values confirmed in cursive at once (π, X, T, ⊥, ++), plus `<`=c and the flat-v=r; final -e dropped.
- **"ne soit le relig[ion]"** (pair 8 next line): `x˙ ✗✗ ᵻ to Ɛ ᵀ ᏺ` under way — x˙(dotted)=n?, to=i ✓; the
  doubled ✗✗ where the print has *soit* suggests ✗=o with doubling; complete on the next pass.
- **"sceptre"** (R264_4_0): `ᵽi λ Ŧ y ᵴo` — ᵽi=pi? no: gloss *sceptre* sits over `10 ᏺ. E ᴎ`; E=re? A tall-E
  as *re* would match table E=re ✓. Flag the rest.

Lower half of canvas 264 (S264_* crops):

- **"partira le"** gloss with `ꝉ 4ᵹ to n 2ᵮ` beneath: 4=x? no — here `4ᵹ` under *parti-*: ᵹ=g→gu?; to=i ✓.
- **"royaume"** (S264_0_1): gloss over `2ⱶⱶ X ⱶⱶ(underlined) ꝛᵱ λ s 4`: X=o ✓ inside *ro-yau-me*; the
  underlined 11 is the table's ⱶⱶ=u with the doubling bar → *au*? flag; λ=la as final -me? no — flag.
- **"dignitez"** (S264_2_1): gloss under `Ⱡ ⱶⱶ ᵴ ꝛ ᵹ ꝉᵴ y`: y=s final ✓ (table y=s), ᵹ before it = te/z?
- **"dans la roiaume [le royaume]"** (S264_2_2): `T 6 to n Ꮮⱶⱶ x ⱶⱶ` — to=i ✓, x=a? (x=a table ✓ inside
  *roiaume*): reading r-o-i-a-u-m-e with 6=di? — 6 is table *di*; the copyist uses it inside *roiaume* for
  the syllable? more likely mis-cut; flag.
- **"et responderez [respondit]"** partial pairs with `y Ꮮl Ŧ ꝛ` under *y'll* gloss — Ŧ=z ✓ again.

Solid cursive equivalences so far (double-confirmed): x=a, ∞/oo=b, <=c, π=o, X=o, T=n, ⊥=n, ⱶⱶ(++)=u,
Ŧ=z, 12=du, 18=ce, to=i, y=s (final), λ=la, E=re, Ⱡ(loop-L)=so, flat-v=r, 3=ri.
