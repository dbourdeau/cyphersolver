# Translation: A. Fuentes D. to Pablo González, Monterrey, 13 June [1914] (CEHM LXVIII-1.17.2435.1)

Coded parts in capitals.

> Monterrey, 13 June [1914]. General don Pablo González. As there is no uniformity in THE PAY THAT the various chiefs of forces ARE DRAWING, in my PAY SHEETS THE FIRST CAPTAIN is assigned $5.00, in others $4.00 and in others $3.00, and the same happens with THE OTHER OFFICERS.
>
> I take the liberty of proposing to you, in order TO ESTABLISH UNIFORMITY, that the following rate be set: private one peso, corporal $1.10, 2nd sergeant $1.25, 1st sergeant $1.50, sub-lieutenant 2.50, lieutenant 3 pesos, 2nd captain 4 pesos, 1st captain 5.00, major 6 pesos, lieutenant colonel seven and colonel eight pesos.
>
> I beg that your respected decision be communicated to all the chiefs. Respectfully, the Chief of Staff, Colonel A. Fuentes D.
