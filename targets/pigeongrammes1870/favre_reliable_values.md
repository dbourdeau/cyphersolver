# Values for the "A Jules Favre" pages (shared code, about 2,300 numbers, NOT alphabetical)

Source: the Chaudordy letter (Favre to Chaudordy, 18 Dec 1870) read against Favre's printed text, checked across the other pages
by date. A group is "firm" only if every occurrence fits the word and the other pages behave as the history predicts.

FIRM
1792 = conférence (9 of 11 uses in the letter; absent on 24 Oct and 14 Nov, present from 28 Nov: the Conference question)
977  = sauf (as in sauf-conduit; 3/3)
1413 = "10", 1024 = "12" (32 1413 43 1024 1319 = "des 10 et 12 décembre")
1319 = décembre
1915 + 161 947 + 620 235 55 82 151 = armistice avec ravitaillement (variants: 567 for 161, 231 for 235)
2232 = Bruxelles (3/3; only on 18 Dec pages)
1980 = ballon (3/3)

FAIRLY SOLID (position fit in the Chaudordy letter, 3 or 4 of 3 or 4 uses)
944 = question, 723 = première, 862 = comme, 835 = cependant, 182 = négociations, 998 = partir

TENTATIVE (opening groups of the letter; verify before use)
57 = de, 5 = un/une, 62 = la, 2066 = l'Angleterre, 740 = la Russie (an earlier guess said Angleterre), 1894 = l'Autriche, 308 = l'Italie

NOT SETTLED
780 (a common word, never used before 28 Nov), 64, 947 (also in the armistice run), 587, 2, 352

CAVEATS
Homophones: common words have several groups (de = 25, 57, 81, 967 ...). The printed text is not word-for-word the cipher after about
group 330 of the letter. Transcription slips (5/8/9) exist in the sheets.
