# Inspectors' cipher (Steenackers to Mercadier, Tours 2 Nov 1870): tentative syllable values

TENTATIVE: read from repeated runs and cross-checks in one telegram; not verified on any other document.
Beacons 0101 / 0201 / 0301 / 0102 bracket a word spelled in syllable groups (Pillon: "beacons indicating syllables").

| spelled run | proposed reading | support |
|---|---|---|
| 0101 8328 9214 0218 4906 3316 8328 0201 (opens and closes the telegram) | S-TEE-N-ACK-ER-S = Steenackers | first and last group equal; heading "Steenackers a Mercadier"; same run at both ends |
| 0102 5504 1122 2514 3316 0102 (also 0101 ... 0101 after "voici la reponse de") | MER-CA-DI-ER = Mercadier | follows "a" after the sender; 4 groups; ends in 3316 as above |
| 0101 6819 3412 0102 / 0201 6819 3412 0101 | ER-NEST? (first name) | precedes Mercadier at the start; repeated near the end; 6819 3412 both times |
| 0201 6816 4001 6129 8328 0201 (x3, "le service par ... d'abord restreint", "par ces ... mais il ne sera plus") | PI-GE-ON-S = pigeons | 3 identical occurrences; topic is pigeon fanciers (20 and 48 in clear, "qui restent ... et reviennent") |
| 0101/0201 0919 5121 6129 0201 (x2) | BAL-L-ON = ballon(s)? | next to the pigeons run; ends in 6129 as in "pigeons" |

Cross-checks that support the values:
- 8328 = S: word-initial in Steenackers, word-final in pigeons (follows a beacon in 8 of its 14 occurrences).
- 3316 = ER: word-final in both Steenackers and Mercadier (followed by a beacon or by the final S).
- 6129 = ON: inside both pigeons and ballon.
Open: the meaning of the different beacon pairs (0101..0201, 0201..0101, 0301..0301, 0101..0102, 0102..0102); a slip in the
transcription (2514 vs 2534 in one Mercadier); the remaining ~320 groups; the whole-word codes (4914, 2119, 6009 ...).

see whether they give French names that fit pigeon fanciers.
