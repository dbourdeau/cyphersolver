# Data not included in this folder

The scripts here read transcriptions and scans that belong to Alexandre Pillon (collector); they are unpublished third-party material and are **not** in this repository:
`inspecteurs_2nov_partial.txt` (his transcription of the 2 Nov 1870 telegram), `inspecteurs_runs.txt`, `chaudordy_nums.json` and `pool.json` (group lists transcribed from his sheets),
the alignment and decode outputs that reproduce those sequences (`align_*_out.json`, `apply_telegram_out.json`, `joint_decode_final.txt`, `*_cloze_out.txt`), and all scans.
Ask him for the files; the analysis (notes, wiki, lexicons, scripts, results) is complete without them, but the scripts will not run until the data is put back next to them.
Public-domain material used: Favre, *Le Gouvernement de la Defense nationale* (1871), via archive.org (`chaudordy_18dec_plain.txt` is the printed letter).
