`engine.py calibrate`. The 22 supported gold groups were blinded (removed from the accepted lexicon) and the gate was run on the 10 gold units with all their contexts. Result for margin thresholds M = 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16: at M = 2 one correct accept (pigeons) and no wrong accept; at M >= 3 no accept at all, right or wrong.
Best blind proposal per unit: spelled runs of 3 or more groups were right 4 of 4 (trochu, mercadier, pigeons, steenackers) with margins 0.2-2.7 nats; single words 1 of 4 (general right; est -> et, bonne -> ce with margin 4.9 above its null 95th percentile 4.0, sante -> son); portante -> par; ballon -> citoyens.
Reading: margin does not separate right from wrong when the neighbours are unknown, so the gate is deliberately conservative (M = 2, zero wrong accepts) and the engine records the best non-accepted proposals as hypotheses with the evidence they would need. Supports [[C031]].
