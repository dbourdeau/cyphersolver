`favre_engine.py`. For a verified anchor group (1792 conference, 1915 armistice, 2232 Bruxelles, 977 sauf) a group belongs to its topic if it occurs within 12 positions of the anchor far more often than chance in the eight dated letters (Poisson, Bonferroni over 400+ groups used >= 4 times).
Passing groups: conference: 628 (10 of 25 uses next to 1792, expected 1.5, p = 0.004; the Chaudordy words aligned to 628 are 'la', 'la', 'la'); armistice: 620 (11 of 23, p = 7e-6), 1908 (9 of 25, p = 0.0015), 1440 (5 of 8, p = 0.023); sauf-conduit: 146 (8 of 19, p = 0.0002), 121 (5 of 12, p = 0.04). Bruxelles has 4 occurrences: too few to test.
This engine finds topic structure, not words: the Favre pages have no clear words and the code is not alphabetical ([[C022]]). Supports [[C035]], [[C036]], [[C037]].
