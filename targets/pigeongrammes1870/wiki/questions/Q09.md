The Algeria volume prints December 1870 telegrams headed '(Chiffre des inspecteurs)' in plaintext only ([[S05]]); their ciphertexts, or any other inspectors'-code telegram, would be a test set for the lexicon ([[C038]]). Archives to try: Archives nationales (F90 telegraph administration), AMAE, the Musee de la Poste.
