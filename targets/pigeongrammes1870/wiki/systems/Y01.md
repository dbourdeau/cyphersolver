Four-digit groups 0101..9924. An alphabetical lexicon of words and syllables over 0000-9999 ([[C001]]): the number predicts the initial letters of its entry (A ~0200, B ~0900, C ~1100, D ~2500, E ~3300, G ~4000, K ~4900, L ~5100, M ~5500, O ~6100, P ~6800, S ~8300, T ~9200). Beacons 0101/0201/0301/0102 bracket words spelled in syllable groups, mainly proper names ([[C002]]); clear words stay in clear. Known pieces: see `lexicon_inspectors.json`. Own table, not R1 ([[C020]]).
