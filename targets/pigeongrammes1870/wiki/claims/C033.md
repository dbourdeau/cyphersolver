Free segmentation (spelled chunks anywhere) added no gold hit in the joint decoder (T08) and produced nonsense spellings in the engine (insurrection as insu|r|rectio|n; le as l|e). One exception is known by hand: `7027 0312` = PORT-ANTE ('est bien portante') sits between clear words, so the rule may be too strict; kept for now because the engine cannot tell that case from noise.
