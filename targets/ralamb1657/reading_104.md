# Image 104: reading of the bare cipher run (one short phrase, 33 groups, 4 words)

Riksarkivet, Diplomatica Turcica, SE/RA/2119/2119.1/I/2/2, image 40008933_00104 (https://sok.riksarkivet.se/bildvisning/40008933_00104),
right-hand page. A despatch to Charles X Gustav, Constantinople, after 14 June 1657 (the left page mentions the arrival
"den 14 Junii" at Constantinople). One cipher run in clear Swedish; no decipherment on the page.

<!-- marked -->
L01	romerske keiserlige residentens tålk
<!-- /marked -->

The clerk put a word space after "rom" (47 69 42 . 8 10 6 13 82); read as one word. 38 is the å/o sign (tålk = tolk).
Code 50 (k) is not in the frozen key; it was added after the held-out test, is backed by the contemporary gloss of image 75
(skatt), and holds at all three occurrences (75 skatt, 114 skrifuit, here). Every other value used here is in the frozen key.

Status: **not prose**: one deciphered phrase of 4 words set into a clear letter, all 4 verbatim from the signs, with no
holes. There is no ciphered sentence to score, so it is labelled not prose rather than read; the phrase itself is deciphered.

Frame (clear text approximate): "... Wiziren plägar der till bruka den **romerske keiserlige residentens tålk**, medh hwilken
iagh ..."

English: "... the Vizier is wont to use for that **the interpreter of the Roman Imperial (Habsburg) Resident**, with whom I ..."
