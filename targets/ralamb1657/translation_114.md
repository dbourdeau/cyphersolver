## Running sense (cipher words in bold, clear frame approximate)

... Your Royal Majesty's **assurance to [...]** ... and so **their giving** ... **the words(?)** and their **emphasis**,
and if their **intention** were not so **set down** ... could be **sufficiently assured** ... **entangle** (proposed) ...
for what has once been **written** with them, that stays **written** ... provided that we [...] the **assurance** when
they now asked for it ... In their camp **a great tumult** (proposed), in that they **seized the Vizier** and **brought him** to
the Sultan, and wanted him to be **put to death**; and the Sultan barely obtained, **through** great **intercession**,
that he got **off this time** ... It is soon [to be expected?] from **the Mufti** that **they**, with **the Tatar [Khan]**
and **the Pasha of Silistra**, should join forces, to **the Russian's** **assistance** ... and this **Mufti** is given to
speaking extravagances, for he is **newly come** [into office]. And **so** ...
