# Image 114: reading of the bare cipher runs

Riksarkivet, Diplomatica Turcica, SE/RA/2119/2119.1/I/2/2, image 40008933_00114 (https://sok.riksarkivet.se/bildvisning/40008933_00114).
A despatch to Charles X Gustav from Constantinople, June 1657 (it mentions "den 4 Junii"; the letter goes on to image 115,
where it is dated and signed; image 115 was not read in this round). The letter is in clear Swedish; only single words
and short phrases are in cipher. No decipherment is written anywhere on the page.

`romerske` etc.: spelling is the clerk's. u/v/w and i/j/y are single letters in this code (33 serves u and v; 17 i and y).
L20: code 77 is y in the key pairs (dyren, 23R); the sense needs ö (förbön), and the held-out gloss of image 75 also
writes 77 for ö (förmögen): 77 serves y and ö, marked as a change here all the same.

it is an approximate working transcription of the clear text, not checked letter by letter. Cipher words are in **bold**
