# duncan1730: Joan Duncan to Hobbe van Aylva, letters partly in cipher, 1730-1731 (Gelders Archief 0439, inv. 124; key inv. 126; catalogue 1945)

Status: in progress

Catalogue **1945**. Gelders Archief, 0439 Huizen Waardenburg en Neerijnen, inv. **124**: one folder of Joan Duncan's
letters to Hobbe van Aylva, 1730-1731, in French, "gedeeltelijk in geheimschrift". The key is filed in the same fonds as
inv. **126**, "Sleutels tot het geheimschrift, 1730, 1731". Scans of inv. 124:
https://permalink.geldersarchief.nl/CA57BD7A9396402C8AFC64E42D37AC0E

## What is known (catalogue vetting of 3 Nov 2026, viewed in a browser)

64 scans. Cipher on 4 scans of two letters: scans 1-4 (dated "20 Janvier 1730"; about two cipher lines on scan 2) and
scans 5-8 (docket "1730 ... 30"; about seven lines on scan 6, six on scan 7, two on scan 8), about 17 lines in all. The
cipher is runs of digits mixed with a few letters set inside clear French, with cover names in clear (E: E:, F: F:,
A, C, D, L); samples: "703u567n3672 4609m2570e465m3246009754", "67035524609732en875 he4056 907713625e4370". No
decipherment between the lines or in the margins.
