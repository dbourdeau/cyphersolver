# Translation: Condé to du Chambon, Paris, 6 August 1651 (postscript 8 August)

AD Dordogne 2 E 1841/18-14, photos P1370710-711. English translation of `reading_1651-08-06.md`, passage by passage.

- Only the **cipher passages** are translated, as in the reading file. The clear words quoted there to place each passage are
  given in *italics*, translated; the rest of the clear text is in `transcription.txt` as rough anchors and is not translated.
- Same holes as the reading: plain = certain; {x} = probable; [x] = restored from the sense; ‹…› = unresolved (codes kept as
  numbers). Where one French word becomes several English words, or the reverse, the mark covers the whole English equivalent.
- Words in (parentheses) are added for the English only and are not in the cipher.
- Key words: *aboutissement* (50ˇ in Brianson's key) is rendered "outcome" (the issue, the coming to terms); *les Chauvesouris*
  ("the Bats") is a party nickname used in clear in these letters; *le Palais royal* is the Palais-Royal in Paris.
