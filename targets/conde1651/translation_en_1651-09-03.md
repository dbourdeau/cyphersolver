# Translation: Condé to du Chambon, Paris, 3 September 1651 (postscript 4 September)

AD Dordogne 2 E 1841/18-10, photos P1370700-701. English translation of `reading_1651-09-03.md`, passage by passage.

- Only the **cipher passages** are translated, as in the reading file; the clear text between them is in `transcription.txt`
  as rough anchors and is not translated. Passages are numbered as in the reading (P1-P19).
- Same holes as the reading: plain = certain; {x} = probable; [x] = restored from the sense; ‹…› = unresolved (codes kept as
  numbers). Where one French word becomes several English words, or the reverse, the mark covers the whole English equivalent.
- Words in (parentheses) are added for the English only and are not in the cipher.
- *Despescher*: to send (a messenger) to someone. *Toutes fois et quantes que*: whenever.
