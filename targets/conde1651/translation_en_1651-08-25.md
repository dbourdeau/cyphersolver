# Translation: Condé to du Chambon, Paris, 25 August 1651 (postscript 27 August)

AD Dordogne 2 E 1841/18-11, photos P1370702-703. English translation of `reading_1651-08-25.md`, passage by passage.

- Only the **cipher passages** are translated, as in the reading file; the clear text between them is in `transcription.txt`
  as rough anchors and is not translated. Passages are numbered as in the reading (P1-P23).
- Same holes as the reading: plain = certain; {x} = probable; [x] = restored from the sense; ‹…› = unresolved (codes kept as
  numbers). Where one French word becomes several English words, or the reverse, the mark covers the whole English equivalent.
- Words in (parentheses) are added for the English only and are not in the cipher.
- *Aboutissement* (50ˇ in Brianson's key) is rendered "outcome" (the issue, the coming to terms). *Monsieur le Président*:
  a président of the Parlement of Bordeaux, here Dafis. *Les députés*: the Parlement's deputies.
