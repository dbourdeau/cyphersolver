#   [..] = clear writing between cipher runs. ^{..} = signs written small above the line. ? = doubtful sign.
# Labels:
#   x4  the sign like a looped x with a tail (stands before runs and inside them; no letter value found)
#   n   small n-like sign (stands at the end of runs and before a name; no letter value found)
#   to  cross with a small circle on its right arm (one sign)      ot  small circle with a cross on its right (one sign)
#   o   small circle        -o  circle with a long stroke to its LEFT        o-  circle with a stroke to its RIGHT
#   C-  reversed c with a long stroke to the right       c  small c       -c  small c with a stroke to its left
#   Q   e-like loop with a long tail to the right (like a Q lying down)     r  small r      R  capital R
#   G   large 9-like sign with a long tail below the line     9  small 9 with a curved tail    q  circle on a straight stem
#   H   two uprights on a base line crossed by a bar     h  h-like sign with a tall stem     d  d with the stem bent left
#   A  inverted V     V  V     F  upright with one arm to the right at the top     E  upright with two arms to the right
#   &  loop above crossed legs     Y  small v on a loop (gamma-like)     xo  x with a looped arm
#   =1  two bars with one upright at their right end     =/  two bars crossed by one slanting stroke
#   #   two bars crossed by two uprights      +  plain cross     PI  pi-like sign     L  tall L
