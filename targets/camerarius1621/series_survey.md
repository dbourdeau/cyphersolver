# Clm 10397 n.129-210 other than n.194: what this folder holds

Munich, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Clm 10397 (Camerariana 47), n.129-210: Camerarius's letters to Solms of
February 1621 to late 1622 in the same code as n.194 (MDZ bsb00116446, scans 581-906, microfilm, 1418 px wide).

None of these letters is transcribed here: no cipher text and no decipherment of any of them is in the folder.
What is recorded (NOTES.md, "Survey of n.129-210", and `key.tsv`):

- An AI language-model agent counted the code groups page by page on scans 540-912: about 4,500 code groups in
  about 75 pieces, the densest n.198-210 (scans 813-896). The count is approximate and its working files are the
  contributor's (WAINUT), not in this folder. Its "glossed / unglossed" figures proved unreliable.
- Gloss presence was then checked by eye on bands of scans 582 (n.129), 589 (n.131), 597, 601, 609, 619, 625, 637,
  641, 646, 651, 703, 715, 724, 728, 741, 749, 754, 757, 795, 797-799, 805, 821, 831, 838, 853, 859: every letter
  checked carries interlinear decipherments over its code groups, with single groups left unglossed here and there
  (484, Camerarius, often; the margin postscript on scan 646 partly). No wholly unglossed letter was found. The rest
  of the pieces were not checked by eye.
- Code values read from glosses of other letters of the series are in `key.tsv` with their source (count of rows
  naming each, 14 Nov 2026): n.193 13, n.149 6, n.196 5, and n.129, n.133, n.152, n.172 one each, beside n.194 (42)
  and n.63 of Clm 10398 (35).

Each letter is a transcription job with a decipherment of the time on the page; n.193 and n.196 are named as next
in NOTES.md (Escalation).
