# GLC02414.185, Toland to [Scammon], undated (1863): reading

Deciphered at the time on the same sheet (the grid below the cipher). This was the held-out pair of the round 2 test: the frozen key predicted 60 of the 66 grid cells before the grid was scored.

After making a thorough search of army wagon, I have to announce that powder and fuse are not here. Madden thinks they were left in staff train. We started at three this morning. If the enemy has retreated and suspect[s] our plan, it will be a close race, they having the shortest road. Would like to know positively where the enemy is going. Signed Toland.

Code words: whistle = enemy, plated = retreated, nile = Toland (the last two learned from this sheet after the test).
