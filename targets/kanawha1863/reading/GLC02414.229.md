# GLC02414.229, White to Scammon, Fayette, 2 July 1863: reading

Deciphered in 1863 at Scammon's headquarters: clear copy GLC02414.137 ("Recd telegram"). The values of apricots, apple, vermillion, gild, gilding and coat were taken from this pair, so this reading is not a test of them.

Fayette, July second. Scammon, Charleston. [Lew] Jacobs found path from Soak road open for infantry, not for cavalry. [Enemy] fortifying at ford [of] Piney and are in front in the forks [of] Beaver. Forty-fifth at Pack's Ferry; twenty-second reported advancing to reinforce Raleigh. Officer[s] heard to say if we did not attack them soon they would [attack] us. They expect to advance with six thousand, take Fayette and clear the valley. This very doubtful. White.

Telegraph slips shown by .137: "we" = Lew, "Jacks" = Jacobs, "ffine" = from, "beacon" = Beaver, "of" = if, "the" = They. The words in [ ] are not in the cipher or in .137; they are supplied for sense.
