# GLC02414.344, White to Scammon, Fayette, 3 July 1863, 9.35 p.m.: reading

Deciphered in 1863 at Scammon's headquarters: clear copy GLC02414.138, marked "Cipher". The values of whistle, cedar, currants and cotton were taken from this pair (cedar and cotton also from the corrections in .110), so this reading is not a test of them.

Fayette, July third. Scammon. Enemy's cavalry came within sight [of] outpost last night. Have sent all my cavalry force to the front to run enemy's pickets into Raleigh. Ordered them to attack at two o'clock in the morning. Enemy's scouts still hanging around; pickets only seen by citizens. We are on the lookout. White.

Telegraph slips shown by .138: "rain" = run, "these" = them, "laying road" = hanging around, "air" = are, "out post" = outpost.
