Fayette July fourth Scammon Charleston Major
Cadot reports from front two men
killed and five wounded asks for
surgeon two ambulances and flag truce
I have sent these forward with
the remaining four companies ninety first
under Col. Turley to be used
if enemy is in force otherwise
to attack them all estimated force
two to three hundred were attached(?)
[second block]
front left and near on the
hill this side Godby Mrs Rees?
brother came from Lewisburg few days
ago and report Echols would soon
move to Raleigh if this place
