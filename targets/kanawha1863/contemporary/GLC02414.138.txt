Cipher.  Fayette July 3  Genl Scammon
Enemy's cavalry came within sight outpost last night, have sent all my cavalry force to the front to run enemy's pickets into Raleigh.
Ordered them to attack at two o'clock in the morning. Enemy's scouts still hanging around, pickets only seen by citizens.
We are on the lookout.  Col. C. B. White
