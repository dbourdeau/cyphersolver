## Translation (summary)

The rest of George I Rákóczi's conditions for joining the Swedish-Protestant alliance: the allies will defend his estates and
heirs and make no peace to his harm; while the war lasts they will pay him 500,000 florins a year in two instalments, the first
year in one sum, before he takes the field; they will keep for him 16,000 picked German foot and 4,000 cuirassiers, whose
officers depend on him and are bound to him by oath; after the peace they will help him against any attacker and, even in
peacetime, if a secret threat appears, keep 2,000 musketeers and 2,000 cuirassiers at their own cost; all members of the Union
are bound by these four points; before he enters Hungary they will send 16,000 foot and 4,000 cuirassiers, cover him against
the King of Poland in his rear, and send him a trusted man at once; if he is to campaign in winter with the Hungarian army, the
sum asked for is to be paid to him; and both sides will act by mutual agreement, so that neither waits for the other.
