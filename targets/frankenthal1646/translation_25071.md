# Translation of inv. 25071 (to Ottavio Piccolomini, camp at Ilenstatt [?], 12 August 1646)

English of `reading_25071.md`. Cipher passages in normal type, plain-script passages in italics. The marked places
of the reading are kept: "that" in "that Prince of Darmstadt" rests on a sign under an ink blot; "categorical" has
one added letter; "disarm" rests on one group of doubtful shape (61 s, possibly 51 u); "today" rests on one figure
read 56 (y) where it could be 50 (u).

*I hope Your Excellency will have seen from my earlier letters what is happening here, and the resolution His
Highness has taken on what I had been charged with. We are still on the same terms now, however hard one presses;
in short, I see that from here* there is no intention of seeking out the enemy, nor of taking a risk, *unless it is*
the occasion *that* the enemy *attacks* Giessen, *and that we have* to relieve it, *as is right:* that Prince of
Darmstadt must not be abandoned, *if only for the* common interest. I do not believe *that we will* seek the enemy *on
this side of the Main. The Vicomte de Turenne arrived the day before yesterday at the Swedes' camp, and with this
they demanded of the* Prince of Darmstadt *a* categorical *resolution to declare himself and* disarm, *or else that
they* would attack *him, according to the order they had for it from the Queen of Sweden. He answered them that*
he would absolutely not do it, *and that he* would defend himself. *All of which I report* today *to the*
Archduke, *who has assured him of* relieving him *in* any way; *and accordingly we shall have* to wait for *the
outcome, of which I shall inform Your Excellency ...*

Notes: "the Prince of Darmstadt" is code group 72 and "the Archduke" code group 67, both glossed on inv. 25067
(Archduke Leopold Wilhelm commanded the imperial army in 1646). The plain-script passages are read for context only.
