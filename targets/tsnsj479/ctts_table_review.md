# Line-by-line review of the J479 CTTS sign table (21 Nov 2026)

Reviewed on full-resolution crops of `docs/tsnsj479_ctts_key.jpg` (the 592 certain boxes in 39 rows). Cut-outs are counted
left to right within a row. "Box problem" = the box holds a neighbour's stroke, a hole or mount edge, or two signs.
Nothing here has been fixed; this is a list for the person checking the project in CTTS.

## Errors that change the key (fix first)

1. **Row U (ב) mixes two signs.** Side 1 has 14 U boxes and no `a` at all; side 2 has 12 U and 15 `a`. On side 1 the
   corrector (and the first transcription) put the loop-with-rising-tail into U. In the table, cut-outs 1-9 of the U row
   are the same tailed loop as the first seven of row `a` (ת). So the tailed-loop boxes on side 1 are probably `a` (ת), not U
   (ב), and the U row's Hebrew letter is wrong for them. Check every side-1 U box against the tailed loop and the cup.
2. **Row 7 (ת) is not one sign.** Of 12 cut-outs only 1, 4, 8, 9, 11 have the curled seven. Cut-outs 2, 5, 6 are plain
   diagonal or horizontal strokes (probably the long top stroke of `<` or `E`, or a pen drag); 3 and 7 are doubtful; 12 is
   faint. Also, on side 1 the 7-shape stands for ב in the text and ת in the alphabet rows (`tr_hard_rows.md`), so a single
   ת label over side 1's five 7 boxes is unsafe.
3. **Hebrew letters printed in the left column that the notes never established:** `y` → ר (the notes call y an e-variant
   ח on side 1 and the `//` mark on side 2, not a ר), `n` → ה and `o` → ע (guesses from shape). Treat those three as unverified.
4. **Resh is split across five rows** (9, s, 8, y, 5) and the 9 row itself holds two shapes (cut-outs 1-3 are tailed
   figure-eights, the rest a plain 9). They may be one letter drawn four ways; the table does not say so.

## Likely wrong boxes, row by row

- **I (א, 76):** cut-outs 5 and 14 sit on the blue mount at a hole edge (not a sign); 2 carries extra marks.
- **U (ב, 26):** 10 and 11 (faint, V-shaped) are a different sign; 12 is a dark blob. See error 1.
- **E (ג, 15):** 1, 3, 4, 5, 10, 14 include a second sign beside the E (4 contains an A); 11 is a dark stain.
- **A (ד, 22):** 14 (tangle) and 17 (tiny) doubtful.
- **N (ה, 31):** 2 is a tiny box with a mount edge; 11-15 are not plain arches (N with dashes, a square form): probably
  other signs or two signs.
- **4 (ו, 17):** 5 carries a second sign on its left; 13 has a cross through it; 16 has a 9-like tail under it.
- **c (ז, 9):** 5 is a large open arc, more like C (ש) than c; 3 and 4 carry extra strokes.
- **B (ח, 20):** 8 and 9 are cluttered. The row mixes the cursive lying-8 (1-7) and the printed B (10-20) deliberately.
- **d (ט, 22):** 1 and 2 carry neighbour strokes. Dotted and undotted d (ظ/ض against ط) are merged in one row.
- **- (י, 45):** two forms in one row (round hook, flat dash); 5 (tiny with a dot), 9 (small hook) and 15 (dark, with an
  extra stroke) doubtful.
- **< (כ, 36):** the left half (thin diagonal with a hook) and the right half (bold angular check) look like different
  signs; 4, 5 contain a neighbour (an A, an F-like sign); 10 contains a P and an S.
- **p (ל, 36):** looks consistent; 11 is a dark stain.
- **P (מ, 35):** about nine cut-outs (3-8, 11-13) lack P's extra lower-left curl and look like plain `p`: the p/P split
  is unreliable (the transcriber warned of it).
- **q (נ, 24):** several cut-outs (3, 5, 6, 8) have the stem above the loop like `d`: d/q confusion, as the notes said.
- **w (ס, 24):** 4 (a dipped wave) doubtful.
- **O (ע, 20):** 3 is a plain ring without the inner stroke; 7 is a dark ring; 14, 15 and 16 are faint.
- **6 (פ, 15):** 9 (6 with a dash), 10 (two signs) and 12 (dark, with a mark) carry extra strokes.
- **S (צ, 5):** the five cut-outs differ from each other (3 looks like an N); low confidence for the whole row.
- **X (ק, 17):** consistent; 7 is tiny.
- **9 (ר, 13):** 1-3 are the tailed figure-eight form; 8 is a dark blob; 14 is a tiny double mark.
- **C (ש, 5):** 2 sits on the blue mount edge.
- **2 (-), 5 (-), K (-), g (-), m (-), z:** single or few signs. K is a tangle of strokes, probably an overwritten cluster
  rather than a sign; z's second cut-out (with a `#`-like mark) differs from its first.
- **8 (ר, 6):** 1-2 are figure-eights; 3-6 are messy and probably other signs.
- **a (ת, 15):** 1-7 are the tailed loop; 8-10, 12, 13 are solid dark ovals (blots or rings, possibly O).
- **b (ל, 4):** 4 sits on the mount edge.
- **e (ח, 7):** 4 is a cross-over tangle; 5, 6 and 7 are fragments with an upright stroke.
- **f (מ, 9):** only 1 and 6 match the sign (stem with a loop crossing into a lower-left loop); 2-5, 7-9 are clusters of strokes.
- **h (-, 4):** the four cut-outs are four different shapes (a heart in the last).
- **o (ע, 3):** 1 sits on the mount edge.
- **s (ר, 7):** 7 is a squiggle with a tall stroke, more like S; 4 and 6 are dark and cluttered.
- **t (ן, 1):** a tiny box at a hole edge: probably a stain, not a sign.
- **y (ר, 1), n (ה, 1), m (-, 1):** single signs; see error 3.
