## 2. The pieces

### S1L03: an alphabet row written upside down (new)

- Signs as photographed (left to right): d < < p z | U h N B y e. Read as text this gave טככלו בדהחחח, nonsense with
  three signs outside the key (z, h, y).
- **Turned 180°, every sign is a chart sign: U E A N 4 c B d - < p**, i.e. **ב ג ד ה ו ז ח ט י כ ל**, the alphabet
  from ב to ל, left to right in the turned view (the same direction as S1L09). The "new" signs were the chart's E, 4
  and c seen upside down (z = c, h = 4, y = E). The scribe wrote this one row with the page turned the other way.

- Consequence: drop S1L03 (11 signs) from the "unread text" count and list it with the alphabet rows.

### S1L02 / S1L04: the verb יבכ / ויבכ

- S1L02 word 5: ↄ, U (loop hung under a bar, the plain ב of כדב, זבי, רב), < with tail: **יבכ**, then a gap, then
  E 4 I N גואה. Three signs only; there is no room for ינ֯יך (four letters), and the second sign is a clean U, not q.
  The 7-shape in this line belongs to the next word but two (7 a I O = בתאע), not to the verb.
- S1L04 word 4: 4, ↄ, a small 7 (blob, bar, diagonal down-left), < with tail: **ו י 7 כ**. With the side-1 values of 7
  (ב in S1L14 and in בתאע): **ויבכ**; with 7 = ש (S1L14 ש slot, S2L20): וישכ.
- Lexicon: **Lisān al-ʿArab, بكك: «وبَكَّ الرجلُ المرأةَ إذا جهدها في الجماع»** ("bakka, of a man with a woman: he
  exhausted her in intercourse"); the root's general sense is pressing, crowding, ramming. So يبُكّ is attested in a
  sexual sense in the classical lexicon (no Egyptian colloquial source checked).
- Best reading: S1L02 يبكّ جوّاه "he rams (it) inside it"; S1L04 ويبكّ جوّه كسها "and he rams inside her cunt". The
  parallel (יבכ גואה / ויבכ גוה) and the attested sense make بكّ better supported than ينيك, which the signs do not
  give. Alternative for S1L04 only: ويشُكّ "and he pierces" (7 = ש). Confidence: יבכ = يبكّ high; ויבכ medium-high.
- Elbaum: over S1L02 his letters read **[Elbaum] י ב ? ג ו א ה** (the third letter not legible); S1L04 too blurred to
  read.

### S1L10: letter trial

- Signs: N < < Λ | Λ | N f e | B < P < | I w - | P < e < I O I <.
