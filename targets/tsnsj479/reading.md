# Checked line-by-line reading (second pass, 20 Nov 2026)

The summary above was written by another session while this pass was running; it is kept as found. This pass

## Method

Each cipher line was read from its row crop (`img/rows/*.png`), enlarged where needed, sign by sign; the machine
labels of `tr_side1.md` / `tr_side2.md` were a starting list only. Signs were converted under the key and the
words set down in true order. `[x]` doubtful letter, `?` illegible sign, `[...]` hole. Arabic rendering normalises
JA spelling (ט for ض/ظ, ד for ذ, כ for خ, ג for ج, ה/ת for ة, final נ for tanwīn). Elbaum's annotated copy was not
opened during this pass (see last section).

## Key

As photographed, left to right: I א, U ב, E ג, A ד, N ה, 4 ו, c ז, B ח, d ט, - י, < כ, p ל, P מ, q נ, w ס,
O ע, 6 פ, S צ, X ק, 9 ר, C ש, 7 ת. Variants: a (loop with long rising tail) = ת/ة, s and 8 = ר, e = ח, f = מ.
Observed habits: a dot under < marks خ (ידכ̇ל, כ̇לי); d (ט) and q (נ) are confused, and a final d/q after a vowel
ending writes tanwīn as נ (דמען, מקלתן); p/P are swapped in places (אל for אם, אמריח for אלריח).
