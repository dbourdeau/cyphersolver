# T-S NS J479, side 1: reading under the established key

Independent reading of `img/full1.jpg` (side 1), sign by sign, LEFT TO RIGHT, under the key given in the brief
(I א, U ב, E ג, A ד, N ה, 4 ו, c ז, B ח, d ט, - י, < כ, p ל, P/f מ, q נ, w ס, O ע, 6 פ, S צ, X ק, 9 ר,
C ש, 7 ת; variants: small loop with rising tail = ת, upright 8 = ר, e = ח, cursive B = ח, round ↄ = י).
`img/elbaum_annot.png` and the `ann_*` files were not opened. Enlargements were made with PIL in the session
scratch folder `j479_read1` (not kept).

Conventions: Hebrew is given in reading order (first sign written = first letter), word spaces as on the page.
`?` after a letter = doubtful; `*` = illegible or a sign outside the key; `[...]` = loss (hole).
"tr" = `tr_side1.md` (whose tokens run right to left; I reversed them before comparing).

General observations on the hand (they explain most departures from tr):

- **q and f/P are hardly separable in this hand.** Both are a stem with a loop on its left and a stroke running
  off to the right at the top. tr's q in S1L01 (2nd sign) and S1L02 ("N q B 9") must be מ (חמאמתי, רחמה);
  the q of אנא in S1L11 (with a top bar) is a real נ. I resolved q/f by context throughout and mark it `?`.
- **The loop with a long rising tail** (tr's "U long-tail", folded into U) is ת, as the key says: בתאע
  (S1L05), חמאמתי (S1L01). The plain U (loop hanging under a bar) is ב (כדב, זבי, רב).
- **b (thorn: stem rising above a mid-height loop)**, not in the key, reads ל in S1L04 (למ = لمّا) and is
  taken as ל? in S1L08. It may be a ל with ascender. In the S1L15 alphabet it stands after מ מ.
- **The upright 8** is ר (S1L02 קער, S1L13; it fills the ר slot in the S1L14 alphabet).
- **6 and E** have nearly the same outline (top stroke rising right, bowl at bottom left); 6 has an inward
  hook. tr's E in S1L08 is read here as 6, and tr's E? in S1L13 as the cursive ר (as in the parallels).
- The angular two-stroke N ("Λ") is ה (S1L11 אליהודי).
- A dot or dash under X marks ק; a dot under < marks כ = خ (S1L05 ידכל = يدخل).

## S1L01

## S1L02

1. Hebrew: **אל קער בתאע רחמ?ה יבכ? גואה**
2. Arabic: القعر بتاع رحمة يبكّ(؟) جوّاه

4. Notes: word break between אל and קער as on the page (tr: separate words, which I keep). The third sign

   must be מ (רחמה, as in S1L05, where it is a clear f). רחמה is taken as the woman's name Raḥma (رحمة);
   "her/its womb" (رحمه) is the alternative. Word 5: ↄ = י, then a loop under a bar (U = ב), then < with
   its tail = כ: יבכ. I take it as yabukk "presses, rams"; it does not read with certainty and may be the
   same verb as ויתכ? in S1L04 (see there). Word 6 E 4 I N = גואה, juwwāh "inside it" (tr has E < as two
   signs of one word with יב-; I put the word break after כ, at the small gap).
