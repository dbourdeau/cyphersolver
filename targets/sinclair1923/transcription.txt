== metapth1891750  H. F. Sinclair, Mexico DF, 29 Apr 1922 -> J. W. Zevely, 2029 Conn Avenue NW, Washington DC
== check 269WIW 42 VIA LAREDO JCT; received 1104 Conn. Ave., 6:02 PM; pencil 245
PAVILLION MONDAY ARROGANCE BLAMEFUL GLADSOMELY FOUR OCLOCK THURSDAY
ARROGANCE STOP HYGIENIC RECOMMIT ME THERE STOP IF FOR STONIG I SHOULD
NOT STOP GLADSOMELY ABITRATE ME HERE BEFORE FOUR OCLOCK MONDAY ARROGANCE
IT WILL BE RESEMBLE FOR ME TO DEMAGOGUE SUZARAIN OF CONQUER
H F SINCLAIR
# HYGIENIC: Y typed over I.

== metapth1891738  Wahlberg, New York, 1 Oct 1923, 4:45 PM -> Colonel J. W. Zevely care John W. Lacey, Cheyenne, Wyo.
== check 180V Q 96; red 193
FIBEL LEBAAL RATAPLAN FROM DLOUXELI TUBOLDNUMD ANZUNDET APTENTURED
ANOMALIDAD ANCHORA QUOTE THE PELUOE HAS UNDER COLLIGAREM THE ASEGURABAN
OF YOUR TEOBROMA RESUDINO YANTADORS YACIDO TO YACK INCLUSIVE PARAGRAPH
WITH VERLANDEN DELLBIRD YOUR TENTANT LEPIDOID FOR THESE YACCATRES AND
ALSO WITH VERLANDEN OF CLOSING THE CATCHFLYS SOOTHSAYER IT IS DEHESENLOD

WUOTAV DESNUDA UNQUOTE STALLION
WAHLBERG

EDW H CHANDLER
