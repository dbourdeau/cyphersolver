# sinclair1923 - reading sheet (14 Nov 2026)

Notation (Daniel's rule): sure / {probable} / [restored] / ‹unresolved›. Code book not identified (see codebook_screening.md), so no code word is decoded. The clear words are read; every code word is ‹unresolved›.

Word totals agree with the operators' word counts in the check of each telegram (42, 96, 30), which confirms the word division of the transcription.

## metapth1891750 - Sinclair, Mexico City, 29 Apr 1922, to Zevely, Washington (42 words, 15 code)
‹PAVILLION› MONDAY ‹ARROGANCE› ‹BLAMEFUL› ‹GLADSOMELY› FOUR OCLOCK THURSDAY ‹ARROGANCE› STOP ‹HYGIENIC› ‹RECOMMIT› ME THERE STOP IF FOR ‹STONIG› I SHOULD NOT STOP ‹GLADSOMELY› ‹ABITRATE› ME HERE BEFORE FOUR OCLOCK MONDAY ‹ARROGANCE› IT WILL BE ‹RESEMBLE› FOR ME TO ‹DEMAGOGUE› ‹SUZARAIN› OF ‹CONQUER›
- Ordinary English words used as code. ARROGANCE follows a day or an hour three times: by position a time word (a.m./p.m., "next", "afternoon"), not established. RECOMMIT could be plain ("recommit me there" makes no sense), counted as code.
- Read by sense: 27 of 42 (64%). Not read.

## metapth1891736 - G. T. S[tanford], New York, 21 Jan 1924, to Bert W. Henry, New Orleans (30 words, 18 code)
‹PYRR› ‹ANZIEHEN› LEFT ‹MINIMENT› FOR ‹ANZUNDET› RESPONSE ‹TOMALLINE› ‹CABI?NA› ‹KUBEL› ME STOP ‹ANOMADOR› ‹AOJOLAS› ‹M??DEREBBE› ‹SPUKTESTED› ‹TORVISCO› ‹TRICON› STOP LEAVING FOR ‹ANZUNDET› ‹ARDEN-TELLB› STOP CONSIDERING ‹SILVIGER› ‹GESTERN› ‹OPVANGE› SPACE MARK
- ANZUNDET follows FOR twice ("left ... for ANZUNDET", "leaving for ANZUNDET"): by position a place name (the same word in the 1923 telegram, "TUBOLDNUMD ANZUNDET APTENTURED"). {a place} is the only probable sense, the place itself unresolved. RESPONSE may be code (counted as clear).
- Read by sense: 12 of 30 (40%). Not read.
