# nanking1930: English translation

## p. 14: Chiang Kai-shek to the Dalai Lama

Kun Kanpu has come and handed me in person your letter and your replies on the eight points; all noted. I am also obliged for
the photograph and the precious gifts you sent, and for the kind words he conveyed; my joy and gratitude are beyond words.
China and Tibet have always been very closely bound. Formerly, because those who ruled the country did not deal with you in
sincerity, the hearts of the State Preceptor (國師, the Dalai Lama) and of the people of Ü-Tsang were lost; estrangement
remained and correspondence became rare. Now, happily, the State Preceptor, wise [?] and clear-minded, understands the times and
sincerely supports our central government. All that Kun Kanpu has reported to me in person shows still more the patriotic
sincerity that overflows your words. From now on China and Tibet are one family and unification is completed, to my great
satisfaction. Besides earnestly instructing Ma Fuxiang, Chairman of the Mongolian and Tibetan Affairs Commission, to handle the
matter with care, I first reply with my thanks and wish you good health. [?] Chiang Chung-cheng (Chiang Kai-shek). [13th.]

## p. 15: Ma Fuxiang to the Dalai Lama

We have been apart for over twenty years and I have often thought of you. I have just met Kun Kanpu and learned that Your
Holiness is in good health, to my deep satisfaction. The State Preceptor's devoted support of the central government has
earned Chairman Chiang's high praise; the details have been answered by the Chairman by telegram. All your affairs I, Fuxiang,
will handle with full devotion, so that public and private interests are both [served]. Please set your mind at rest. With
respectful good wishes. Ma Fuxiang. [8th.]

Note: 棍堪布 is Kun Kanpu (Könchok Jungné, the Dalai Lama's representative, also written 貢覺仲尼); 國師 "State Preceptor" is
the Dalai Lama.

Notes added at integration (12 Nov 2026)
- "wise [?]": the code as transcribed (4156) gives no sense; 4158 目 "eye", one digit away, is a candidate (慧目明心).
- "both [served]": the code as transcribed (5129) gives no sense; the set phrase 公私兩便 would need 0189, two digits away.
- Six words rest on codes emended by one digit: "all noted" (均), "correspondence" (音), "earnestly" (切), "Chiang" in "Chairman
  Chiang" (蔣), "praise" (許) and the second "devotion" (竭). See decryption.txt.
- Order of the two telegrams: Ma Fuxiang's, dated the 8th, says the Chairman has already answered by telegram; Chiang's is
  dated the 13th. Open.
- The identification of 棍堪布 (Kun Kanpu) with Könchok Jungné (貢覺仲尼) is the contributor's and was not checked here.
