# English translation of fol. 200r (22 July 1636)

## Block 1

We praise God for the good and courageous resolution you have taken to come to us without delay, to which we invite you again as far as we can, because every consideration presses.

## Block 2

Although we see some slight danger in your arrival, having been put off too long, it is nevertheless not possible to delay it any further, and it is better to risk something than to lose everything; but we hope that, acting in good order, boldly and courageously as you know how to do, and we on our side making the rest of our efforts to second you, all will go to our glory and to the confusion of our enemies.
