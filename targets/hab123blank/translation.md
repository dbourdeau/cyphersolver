# Catalogue 3807: HAB Cod. Guelf. 123 Blank., f. 59v: English translation

This translation follows the passages of reading.md.
- **Cipher passages:** translated as read. In alchemical usage, *luna* is silver, *saturnus* lead, *venus* copper and *mars* iron; *rex* is the regulus, the metal button left after smelting.
- **Uncertain words:** in [brackets].
- **Clear passages:** given only in outline, because the German clear text was not arbitrated.

1. **L01–L02.** First, [one weight] of silver, and two [weights] of the composition, and three of antimony and of copper, equal parts, … 16 …; and it went on badly.
2. **L03–L05.** … if I were to soak the [scoriae], the seal, the cupel and the cupellation shards in lead together, I nevertheless took, in place of the copper, lead. And because it did not go into the lead …
3. **L06–L08.** The washings of the seals and of the cupellation shards went in readily; but I did not find all the silver. … I had taken much … in a moderate quantity of silver, and
4. **L09–L11.** so … was … of this kind … with the reguli of the composition from the composition alone and antimony, without adding cupels of lead or of copper; likewise …
5. **L12–L14.** … with silver, antimony and … equal parts of each … I added some of the scoria, washed perfectly, … onto one [part] of silver … silver, up to the sixteenth [part] … abundantly …
6. **L15–L16, clear German.** 8 lot of hard [matter] … brick and shards … to 6 lot, and 42 lot of slag, and 1 lb, to be washed off.
7. **L16–L18.** All together, as much as you like, in proportion. I took half a lot and … with the scale of common iron and with litharge, equal parts, and I found a regulus from the lead in weight … so there will be less of the regulus from the lead … litharge.
8. **L19.** … added: per hundredweight, twenty-eight pounds and four lot.
9. **L20–L29, clear.** Weights in lot and pounds; slag, brick, shards, nitre.
10. **L30–L31.** To be considered: only wash the cupellation shards, throw away their washing water, and keep the [slime] [and] the slag.
11. **L35–L36.** … of lead and … one [part] of antimony, mixed …
12. **L38.** … of lead and of antimony, 2 … and 1 [weight] of iron …
13. **L40–L41.** … 1 [weight] of glass … 2 of litharge and 1 of scoria [clear] and 1 of glass …
14. **L42–L45.** … and with a very fine regulus … of this very fine regulus, 3 [weights] … and of the composition … with the usual silver …
