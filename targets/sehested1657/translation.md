# sehested1657 - English summary translation, letter by letter

Summaries of reading.md (Brice Dudout, with Claude Opus 5.5 agents, 11 Nov 2026); not a word-for-word translation. Identifications of
nomenclator symbols are those of key.tsv (certain / probable); persons: Kurz = Ferdinand Sigmund Kurz, imperial vice-chancellor;
La Fuente = Gaspar de Teves, marqués de La Fuente, Spanish ambassador in Vienna; Rebolledo = Bernardino de Rebolledo, Spanish envoy in
Copenhagen; the chancellor = Christen Thomesen Sehested; the Reichshofmeister = Joachim Gersdorf.

## L01 — to the chancellor, Vienna 17/27 Jan. 1657 (with P.S.)

Sehested refers to his letter to the king; Kurz, at first surprised that no one came with full powers, took the matter up seriously on 14/24 Jan after conferring with La Fuente and gave the enclosed answer; Sehested asks the chancellor for the promised papers. P.S.: a breach between the Emperor and France is expected; forced levies; the French army put at 50,000; Rákóczi already in Poland, for himself rather than for Sweden; rumour of a Saxon marriage for the young king; Kurz flatters Sehested and has attached Windischgrätz to him to sound him out.

## L02 — to the king, Vienna 17/27 Jan. 1657

To the king: Sehested greeted Kurz on the alliance and the 'secret'; Kurz took it ad referendum, then reported that the Emperor is not averse to the defensive alliance proposed by the king but expects the draft and conditions from Denmark; the 'secret' once mentioned to Castel Rodrigo no longer matters. On 14/24 Jan Kurz asked for haste and for the inclusion of the States General.

## L03 — to the king, Vienna 7/17 Feb. 1657 (date read 28/7; Gigas: 17 Feb.)

To the king: nothing new; spirits in Vienna raised by Rebolledo's favourable report on Danish armaments, passed to the Emperor by La Fuente on 2/12 Feb; passage through Friuli refused to the Turk; the Swiss rejected France's threefold proposal (Alsace); birth of an archduke on 1/11 Feb; Rákóczi advancing in Poland, Danzig seeking neutrality.

## L04 — to the chancellor, Vienna 7/17 Feb. 1657 (with P.S.)

To the chancellor: the ministers await the resolution and full powers; ciphering has worn out his eyes, so no duplicates; news as in the letter to the king; his reports to Kurz confirmed by Rebolledo. P.S.: talks in Paris with the cardinal; a Franco-Swedish offensive and defensive alliance is believed concluded.

## L05 — to the king, Vienna 14/24 Feb. 1657

To the king: the Emperor has consented orally to the cession of Bremen; should Sehested press for a written promise, and claim Bremen as a see or as a secularised duchy (better: hereditary)? The Turkish danger is played up; new patents for 7,000 men; an envoy is sent to [H].

## L06 — to the chancellor, Vienna 14/24 Feb. 1657 (glossed original; two cipher copies)

To the chancellor: Kurz would discuss Bremen only after the alliance; Sehested argues that Danish preparations are ready and that general peace is impossible while Sweden holds Pomerania, Mecklenburg and Bremen. On 11/21 Feb the Emperor, Kurz and La Fuente conferred: the king will be satisfied; the Danish minister at The Hague should block ratification of the Swedish-Dutch treaty and bring the States General into the alliance. Kurz then announced the Emperor's consent to the transfer of Bremen. P.S. at nine o'clock: the Emperor resolves on an offensive alliance and awaits the full powers urgently.

## L07 — proposition to Emperor Ferdinand III (audience of 18/28 Feb. 1657), copy

Proposition to Emperor Ferdinand III (audience of 18/28 Feb): Swedish possession of the duchies of Pomerania and Bremen endangers the Empire and Denmark and threatens the peace of Westphalia; if the king must carry his arms against Bremen, he hopes the Emperor will not oppose it and will support it with a diversion in Pomerania; he will wait for the Emperor's resolution and asks his advice and how far he may count on his help.

## L08 — to the chancellor, Vienna 21 Feb./3 Mar. 1657

To the chancellor: on 18/28 Feb he presented to the Emperor the points of his new instruction (copy enclosed); the cession of Bremen was already granted; an answer on the diversion and effective cooperation is promised; the proposition goes to the privy council. P.S.: inclusion of the States General pressed; La Fuente wavers.

## L09 — to the chancellor, Vienna 28 Feb./10 Mar. 1657 (mostly clear)

To the chancellor, mostly in clear: the king's full powers for an offensive and defensive alliance are eagerly awaited.

## L10 — to the chancellor, Vienna 4/14 Mar. 1657

To the chancellor: the Emperor has resolved to conclude the offensive and defensive alliance quickly; Bremen better in Danish hands; send the full powers at once. Colonel Garnier secretly seeks help for Poland against Rákóczi; a secret imperial envoy urges Rákóczi to withdraw; the Emperor will act this summer, where is not yet known.

## L11 — to the chancellor, Vienna 7/17 Mar. 1657

To the chancellor: Kurz says the Emperor now wants a general offensive and defensive alliance binding both sides; Sehested took it ad referendum. The States General should come later as an accessory. Questions: should Denmark insist on 'Königliche Majestät' instead of 'Königliche Würden', on a recredential, and on a written promise for Bremen?

## L12 — to the king, March 1657 (pencil 7/3)

To the king: the Emperor stands by his first answer: nothing further before the king sends absolute full powers and an instruction for an offensive and defensive alliance; Bremen preferred in the king's hands; renewed advice on the States General, Poland, Danzig and other princes.

## L13 — memorial handed to Kurz, ?/9 Mar. 1657

Memorial handed to Kurz (?/9 March): Denmark can hold the Baltic and act on land; the Emperor is asked to declare himself on general peace while Sweden holds Pomerania and Bremen, on transferring Bremen to the king after its occupation, and on effective cooperation. If Sweden made peace in Poland it would recoup itself from Pomerania and Bremen 'mit den spoliis des armen Teutschlandes'. Sweden's attack on the city of Bremen against the imperial avocatoria has nearly forfeited the fief.

## L14 — to the chancellor, Vienna 11/21 Mar. 1657 (with P.S.)

To the chancellor: under the new order he handed Kurz a detailed memorial yesterday; an answer is promised by the next post. P.S.: should he ask Kurz for written full powers, and is it enough that both sides show theirs and keep the originals?

## L15 — "Monsieur", March 1657 (pencil 14/3)

No answer yet; Vienna seems to wait until the king has actually broken with Sweden to bargain better; the king cannot keep his army long without acting.

## L16 — memoir to a prince ("Ewere fürstliche Gnaden"), 16/26 Mar. 1657

Memoir to a prince (Auersperg?): the king cannot decide until the Emperor declares himself; the Danish fleet can hold the Baltic and the army is ready; the king offers a treaty for the security of the Baltic and against the Swedish provinces, at first for one year, to be extended later with the States General; he asks the prince to further the matter.

## L17 — to the chancellor, Vienna 18/28 Mar. 1657 (mostly clear)

Mostly in clear; cipher: on his last instance the Emperor will send the king of Poland some thousand men under Hatzfeldt, already in Silesia.

## L18 — to the chancellor, Vienna 21/31 Mar. 1657

To the chancellor (cipher copy and clear of the time in the volume): Kurz explains that the Emperor renewed the family pacts with Poland and promised troops, solely to second Denmark against Sweden, and calls this a sufficient diversion; on Bremen he advises the king to attack Sweden itself first so that Sweden opens hostilities from Bremen; Sehested answers 'praevenire melius quam praeveniri'; Kurz: 'multa fiunt et cogitantur quae non dicuntur'. The Emperor is gravely ill; the privy council asked to see Sehested's full powers.

## L19 — to the chancellor, Vienna 25 Mar./4 Apr. 1657 (death of the Emperor)

To the chancellor: his hopes ended on Sunday with the Emperor's unexpected death; the help for Poland under Hatzfeldt is uncertain; his full powers and instruction expire; should he stay or leave? He asks for a recredential and the return of his written memorials.

## L20 — to the king, Vienna 28 Mar./7 Apr. 1657

To the king: his instructions and full powers expire with the Emperor's death; should he continue if Kurz, as a well-disposed imperial councillor told him, makes important proposals in the name of the new government?

## L21 — copy of a letter to the chancellor, Vienna 28 Mar./7 Apr. 1657

Copy to the chancellor: Kurz intends to make him an important overture once the new government is set up; he will take it ad referendum.

## L22 — to the chancellor, Vienna 4/14 Apr. 1657 (with P.S.)

To the chancellor: Hatzfeldt ordered into Poland with 12,000 men; his negotiation, which presupposed the late Emperor, is suspended; the news of 17/27 March was only meant to feed Denmark with light hopes, everything having long been concerted with Poland; an imperial councillor (Cridel or Lützow) may be sent secretly to Denmark to inspect the troops; 18,000 men decided for Poland. P.S. on the Swedish resident's insinuations.

## L23 — to the chancellor, Vienna 8/18 Apr. 1657

Partly in clear: the Swedish resident claims that Denmark and Sweden have settled and that the king will give Sweden 4,000 men; Sehested has reported otherwise.

## L24 — to the chancellor, Vienna 15/25 Apr. 1657

Partly in clear: his merchants have betrayed his name, mission and bills of exchange to the Swedish resident; the nuncio and the Spanish ambassador urge the young king to war on Sweden; the resident belittles Danish armaments and claims a Danish-Swedish treaty.

## L25 — to the chancellor, Vienna 18/28 Apr. 1657

To the chancellor: Hatzfeldt ordered to join the king of Poland with 15,000 men; the ministers want to carry out with Denmark the alliance begun under the late Emperor; Sehested's full powers have lapsed, so a draft will be made in Vienna and sent on.

## L26 — to the chancellor, Vienna 9/19 May 1657

Partly in clear: Vienna now wishes to conclude a defensive alliance quickly, offering help in money or troops if the king is attacked.

## L27 — to the chancellor, in French, Prague 3/13 Oct. 1657

French letter, Prague 3/13 Oct 1657, same cipher: the court will resolve either on full cooperation or on a strong diversion in Pomerania by the Poles, who will be given German troops after these are disbanded.
