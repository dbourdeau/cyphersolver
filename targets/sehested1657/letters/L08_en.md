# sehested1657, L08 — to the chancellor, Vienna 21 Feb./3 Mar. 1657 (img 136 d): English

Summary only, not a translation (Brice Dudout, 11 Nov 2026). No full English translation of this letter exists yet.

To the chancellor: on 18/28 Feb he presented to the Emperor the points of his new instruction (copy enclosed); the cession of Bremen was already granted; an answer on the diversion and effective cooperation is promised; the proposition goes to the privy council. P.S.: inclusion of the States General pressed; La Fuente wavers.
