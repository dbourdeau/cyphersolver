# sehested1657, L07 — proposition to Emperor Ferdinand III (audience of 18/28 Feb. 1657), copy (img 134 d, 135, 136 g): English

Summary only, not a translation (Brice Dudout, 11 Nov 2026). No full English translation of this letter exists yet.

Proposition to Emperor Ferdinand III (audience of 18/28 Feb): Swedish possession of the duchies of Pomerania and Bremen endangers the Empire and Denmark and threatens the peace of Westphalia; if the king must carry his arms against Bremen, he hopes the Emperor will not oppose it and will support it with a diversion in Pomerania; he will wait for the Emperor's resolution and asks his advice and how far he may count on his help.
