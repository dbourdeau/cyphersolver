# R8590: what is held of the letter of 15 Feb 1529 (key source)

BL Cotton MS Vespasian C IV f.315, Ghinucci to [Wolsey], Valladolid, 15 Feb 1529 (DECODE R8590). The cipher on the
page carries an interlinear decipherment of the time. This file holds the calendar entry that quotes it; the cipher
itself was not saved as a transcription (see `outcome.texts`, `transcription_missing`).

Source: Letters and Papers, Foreign and Domestic, Henry VIII, vol. iv (Brewer), no. 5300, as printed on British
History Online (`letters-papers-hen8/vol4/pp2313-2332`), opened 14 Nov 2026. In the calendar, italics mark the
words that are in cipher in the original; they are given here between asterisks.

> 5300. GHINUCCI to [WOLSEY]. [margin: 15 Feb. Vesp. C. IV. (315.) B. M.]
>
> Wrote three days ago jointly with Lee. It was perhaps not necessary to send the letters with such diligence. Had
> not time to read everything that was sent to them. "Legeram enim quod in meis particularibus literis cifris
> additum fuerat ubi dicebatur quod *non discederem ex Hispania quousque singula ab Urbe mihi et nuncio pontificis
> huc mittenda perbecta* (pervecta) *essent etc*.," which seemed to agree with the custom usual in such cases, for
> he thought such things *were done by the hands of the ambassadors*. Is in some perplexity, because in Wolsey's
> long letters to him it is supposed *that a new ambassador should be sent hither for this purpose*. It would not
> be much expence.
>
> Expects leave to go to the court in three days.
>
> Hopes to write something to his satisfaction about the matter mentioned in the long letters.
>
> *Wishes he had a copy of the bull and the briefs*. Valladolid, 15 Feb. 1529.
>
> Hol., Lat., p. 1. The cipher deciphered.
