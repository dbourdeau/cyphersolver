## Summary

An enclosed letter (`l'a[g]iunta lettera`) and divided sheet of cipher (`un [f]oglio di cifra`) to V.E., considering renouncing a priory (`il priorato`, `renuntia[r]gliel[o]`) to declare himself an independent servant and insisting on taking 4,000 scudi (`pigliare li quattro mila sc[u]di`) as provision to reimburse himself rather than serve as a salaried servant (`seruitore prouisionato`), written in strict confidence (`in tanta confidenza`).
