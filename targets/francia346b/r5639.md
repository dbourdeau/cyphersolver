## Summary

Companion letter to R5638 on the same priory and compensation settlement: denying self-interest (`[n]on per interes[s]e di ueruna sorte`), combining a 4,000-scudi payment (`quattro mila s[c]udi`) with the equivalent and remainder of the priory (`e l'equiualente del priorato, [e] il pi[g]liare il rima[n]ente`), and replying to news of the recipient's arrival (`in risposta l'au[u]iso [che] egli diede a me [de]l suo arriu[o] costà`).
