# Prior art: the Privy Council protocol of 1675 in print, and Historisk Tidsskrift 5. R. IV

Prepared 13 Nov 2026 for issue #424 at the maintainer's request. Quotations are copied from the archive.org OCR text of the two volumes (public-domain prints of 1882 and 1883), OCR errors kept as they stand; each is under 40 words. Not compared with the page images.

## Sources

1. *Aarsberetninger fra det Kongelige Geheimearchiv, indeholdende Bidrag til Dansk Historie af utrykte Kilder*, vol. VI ("Sjette Bind"), ed. C. F. Wegener, Kjøbenhavn: C. A. Reitzel, issued 1876-82. archive.org identifier `aarsberetninger02wegegoog` (https://archive.org/details/aarsberetninger02wegegoog; metadata: year 1882, Google scan from the University of Michigan, "NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT"). Section: the Privy Council (Gehejmeraad) protocol on foreign affairs, year 1675, printed pp. 116-126; the 1675 entries on Klingenberg stand on pp. 117-122. The protocol has no entry between 3 Aug 1675 and 13 Jan 1676 (p. 126: the King was with the army).
2. *Historisk Tidsskrift*, 5. Række, IV (1883-84), pp. 447-450: review ("Litteratur og Kritik") of Vaupell's *Rigskansler Grev Griffenfeld*. archive.org identifier `historisktidsskriftdk5s4` (https://archive.org/details/historisktidsskriftdk5s4; metadata: date 1883-1884, volume "5th s., 4", public-domain mark). Reviewer's signature not checked.

Page numbers are the printed page numbers in the OCR (each page number line precedes that page's text). Identifiers confirmed by one plain GET of `https://archive.org/metadata/<id>/metadata` on 13 Nov 2026; the OCR files are those downloaded by run b126 (12 Nov 2026).

## Historisk Tidsskrift 5. R. IV, pp. 447-450

Seen (OCR). The review names the protocol as a source and cites Aarsberetninger VI, 117 for the 14 January 1675 instruction; it prints no letter to Klingenberg and no cipher.

- p. 446: instructions "ofte i deres Chifferskrift bære Spor af den Hemmelighedsfuldhed, der skal omgive dem."
- p. 448: the protocol "gjengiver dog ikke Forhandlingerne, men kun Beslutningerne, særlig de vedtagne Svar til fremmede Diplomater. samt Ordrerne til Gesandterne."
- p. 448: "saaledes mangle næsten alle Breve fra Griffenfeld og Statssekretæren Conrad Biermann til Gesandterne i Stockholm."
- p. 449: "Kongen allerede den 14. Januar ... givet sit Samtykke til den Instrux for Klingenberg, som han sigter til, nemlig ,,under Skin og Prætext af Mediation"" (footnote: "Geheimearch.'s Aarsberetn. VI, 117").
