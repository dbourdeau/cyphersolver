Method
- Units = decode tokens as given (syllable, letter or whole word). '<struck>' and '{...}' removed, not counted.
- '[n]' and '[?n?]' code numbers with no value: 1 unit each, N.
- Each row segmented into words (judge_260195-41R.tsv: image, line, row, word_no, word, unit, label, gloss).
- Where a word is clearly identifiable and one unit is wrong: that unit E if a one-letter slip, N if worse; the other units of the word S.
- Period spellings accepted as S (seind, ursach, anderst, wurcklich, Chron, Zel, hette, aufgenomme).
- The final '.' in 42R 22-25 is a decoded token: counted as 1 unit, S.
- Counts by script (/tmp/judge_k41_s/j.py); unit joins checked against the decode for every row (no mismatch).
