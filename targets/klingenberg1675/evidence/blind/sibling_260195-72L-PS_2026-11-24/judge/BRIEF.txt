decode.txt: one mechanical decode (no edits) of the cipher runs of a short postscript in a German letter draft of 1675 (Danish-Swedish war period). Each row: image, line, then the decoded units separated by spaces. A unit is a syllable, letter or whole word as the code gives it. '[n]' (a number in brackets) = code number with no value in the key; '[?...]' or '<?...>' = unread or unvalued token; '<struck>' = a deleted group (not a unit); '{...}' = a drawn mark (not a unit); other bracketed marks such as [edge] or [gloss] are not units.
Task (strict judge, one pass, no second tries): join the units of each row into words, then label EVERY unit S (part of a word that reads as sense in 17th-c. German, period spellings and Latin/French loanwords allowed; proper names S only if well formed), E (wrong but plausibly a one-letter slip), N (no sense, and every '[n]' or unread token). Write judge.tsv with header: image, line, row, word_no, word, unit, label, gloss (one row per unit; 'row' = the row number in decode.txt counting from 1 after the header; gloss = your reading of the row, on its first unit only). Then JUDGE_NOTES.txt: unit rules, totals S, E, N and the strict share S/(S+E+N) with the arithmetic, recurring slips, and a 3-line gist. Write both files in this folder. Do not open any other file.
