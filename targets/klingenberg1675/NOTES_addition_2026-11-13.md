## Rule of the count (as in NOTES.md, applied to word units)

The first reader judged each decoded word once, after the single decode: a word unit is S if its decode equals the intended word letter for letter in Biermann's spelling, E if it differs (c for g and p for b count E; a code with no value in v4 counts E), N if it is a blot or unread group with no word to judge. Strict share = S / (S + E + N), in word units. The ledgers hold every unit, so the share can be recomputed by counting column 4. The blind second reader wrote `word` (S), `word:n` (E) and `?n` (no word given: a hole) in `_wordunits_blind.txt`; the blind share below counts `?` units as holes in the denominator, as the maintainer asked, and gives the share with the holes left out beside it.
