# Biermann to Klingenberg, P.S. of the draft of late July 1675 (unit 260195, images 72L-72R): English translation

Line for line with the German reading (`260195-72L-PS_klingenberg_reading.md`; manuscript line numbers given here), the same holes in place: `[n]` a code with no value, `<?>` units judged no sense, `[...]` words the English needs that the German line does not carry. The four E units are translated as the judge's intended words (Matrosen, geschaffet, Werbung, fort); that is an interpretation. "sie ... werde" is taken as "she/one obtains them", the subject not being in the cipher. Only the cipher runs are translated; the clear German between them is not transcribed, and the clear word before each run is given in brackets.

English by Claude (Anthropic) for Malcolm McBratney, 24 Nov 2026. UNCHECKED: not checked by a German reader or against the images.
