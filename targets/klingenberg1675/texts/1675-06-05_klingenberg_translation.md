## Translation

> My most honoured State Councillor's report of 8 June/29 May was read most humbly to His Royal Majesty today. Now as for

the [?Lüneburg] troops,

> he will already have seen sufficiently, and heard in detail, His Royal Majesty's most gracious intention from the dispatch of the 25th of last month, and with that, as also with what I was afterwards ordered to report concerning the

[...] [20] [...] in [202] [...],

> His Majesty most graciously lets the matter rest. For the rest, the Swedish Ambassador Extraordinary Count Niels Brahe, after he recently received a courier again, has

formally brought forward his king's [?proposal] with [116] [?er] [?er] [6],

> and

although [193] sought to decline entering into it

> in the

gentlest way,

> and

kept the ambassador waiting a considerable time with it, and prevented him from making his address any sooner, yet [103] one could not with good manners turn him away any [?longer],

> so that

His Majesty finally had the declaration made to him through the [193] that the King of Sweden's suit and request are not contrary to him,

> however

with the express rider that this marriage, should it [?make] progress, shall cause not the least prejudice to the treaties [?held] with the allies, nor hinder His Majesty's other

> actual

execution of them, as he is,

> as said before,

to comply with them and not to let himself be held back from it by any [136] considerations

> entirely resolved. His Excellency the Chancellor of the Realm has expressly given the Ambassador to understand this, and has since confirmed it both to M. Le Maire and to Chancellor Brandt; and so it will be necessary that my most honoured State Councillor, should he notice that

the States and the other allies take any umbrage at this,

> make the utmost efforts

to remove it from them, with the [?constant] [?assurance] that the effects will show [418] how they [?have] had no [?cause] for it at all,

> and

that His Majesty will on his high part give satisfaction to the said treaties in all [418].

> His Royal Majesty is most graciously resolved to set out next week on the journey to Holstein and to inspect in person his troops assembled at Rendsburg; he will also notify this to the Duke of Gottorp and propose a meeting to him.
