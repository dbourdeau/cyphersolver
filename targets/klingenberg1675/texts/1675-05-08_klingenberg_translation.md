## Translation

> Chancellor Brandt, however, arrived here a day before my most honoured State Councillor's report of 10 May/30 April came in, and has since handed over in writing the commission entrusted to him, aimed at what my most honoured State Councillor mentions in his report. The matter has not yet been taken into deliberation, but since he presses hard for a written resolution, I do not doubt that it will be given him very soon, and

all possible and [?reasonable] [?satisfaction] [?be granted] him.

> But that

His Majesty would enter into rupture with Sweden before the [?allies],
