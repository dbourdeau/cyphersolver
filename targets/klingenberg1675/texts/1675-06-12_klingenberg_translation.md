## Translation

> His Royal Majesty has had my most honoured State Councillor's report of 14/4 of this month read to him, and has most graciously seen from it what he, concerning the six points,

and [220] [...] which are contained in His Majesty's [377] of the [98] and [402] [...] May [?vor] [139] [?er] [299] [...].

> Now although

the [?declaration] of war in the name of [?all] the allies at The Hague does not

> take place, but

a [251] perform it in his own territory [143], yet it will nonetheless be needful that the said points, so far as they

> still

are not settled, be [?negotiated] and [?handled] [?in accordance with] the [?an] [?ge] [324] [384] [...] royal [377],

> as my most honoured State Councillor will of himself sufficiently judge how

much it [?matters] to His Majesty,

> especially

in the payment

> both

of all moneys still outstanding

> and

of the [233] [...] subsidies,

> since

His Majesty has his troops in the agreed number [190] more

> actually

together,

> and

[?de] large subsidies must

> unavoidably

be applied to [?their] subsistence.

> The Swedish Ambassador Extraordinary arrived here again yesterday and, as I understand, will have his farewell audience tomorrow, and as soon as His Royal Majesty has set out for Holstein, which will happen next Monday, he too will begin his return journey to Sweden. His Excellency the Chancellor of the Realm will go ahead tomorrow, as others have orders to follow. I do not yet know of any of the foreign ministers going along, as it is said that His Royal Majesty will be back here within 3 weeks, for which reason Her Majesty the Queen also stays here. For the rest His Royal Majesty wishes my most honoured State Councillor to tell M. Frantz Möller that concerning the

moneys received for the fleet he should, with Mr Guldensparre, and concerning the subsidies by land, with

> Mr von Stöcken, diligently and

by every post correspond [143],

> since both

the Admiralty and the General Commissariat

> must of necessity have correct information and knowledge of it, not only

when any moneys are paid, but also how much and [...] whom they are paid to.

> I remain
