## Translation

> His Excellency the Chancellor of the Realm has ordered me to convey to my most honoured State Councillor, on his report of 28/18 of this month, that His Royal Majesty could not see to what end the [377] given by the States General to their

ambassador destined for [?Moscow], concerning the peace [424] the [?Grand Duke] and the Turks, could serve, other than to confuse the [244] in his

> reportedly already

taken good resolutions,

> and very particularly since

[?de] peace,

> if it should even take place,

could have no other effect [246] [...] than that

> perhaps

the [258], once freed from war with the Turks, would at the [134]'s urging turn his arms against the [100].

> For which reason

His Majesty held it more advisable that one [292]

> should apply all diligence to this end

and [244] qvovismodo (Latin: by whatever means) against Sweden into action [41].

> Likewise it seems not a little strange to His Royal Majesty that

the States ask [?him] to contribute [?their] share to the present for the [244]'s [?chancellor],

> since he has already, for the common cause,

for the best on [?the same] [246] [...]

> spent rather considerable

[?costs], without anything having for long been made good to His Majesty by the allies.

> Therefore my most honoured State Councillor will know how best to decline this proposal too. For the rest I have already reported to him how the 16,000 men stipulated in the treaties stand quite ready, and His Royal Majesty would therefore gladly see their muster go ahead the sooner the better. His Excellency the Chancellor of the Realm had the enclosed copy made out to the Resident Le Maire for that reason only yesterday, and His Royal Majesty shall meanwhile hold himself fully assured that

the Crown of Spain and the States will [?pay] him the [233] [...] subsidies, to be reckoned from [...] [?requisition] [...] or [...] at least from the first [?die] [...] of the month when the troops were ready,

> without

[?longer] delay. The effects will shortly show that neither the marriage [?concluded] with the King of Sweden [?ship] any other

> considerations

would be [?capable] of holding him back from [?performing] that

> which

he has bound himself to towards the allies [143].

> The day before yesterday some deputies from Gottorp arrived here, whom, as is reported, the Duke together with his President of Government means to follow tomorrow. Whether one will on that side depart from the dangerous maxims held hitherto and set a safer course as the goal may perhaps be reported by the next post.
