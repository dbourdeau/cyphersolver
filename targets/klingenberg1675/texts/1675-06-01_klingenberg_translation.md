## Translation

> My most honoured State Councillor's report of 4 June/25 May was laid before His Royal Majesty in the Privy Council today by His Excellency the Chancellor of the Realm, and His Excellency thereupon graciously ordered me to send my most honoured State Councillor the list he asked for of the regiments and companies of the

stipulated [16] thousand men,

> and at the same time to report that His Royal Majesty is most graciously content that

the said

> three

thousand boatmen,

> as my most honoured State Councillor proposed,

[53] His Majesty's commissaries' billets and [363] [?-ly] pay be taken on,

> provided that, as far as possible, it be

of His Majesty's own subjects who

> still

free [?]

> may be. For the rest, concerning the Envoy Extraordinary Høeg's

mission to the Elector,

> as far as I know no final resolution has yet been taken, but I cannot gather otherwise than that it will go ahead. From Sweden there is news that 6,000 men still stand ready there, to be shipped over to Germany at the earliest.
