# Image 120 (right page): reading of the four upper cipher runs of the report "Unterthänigster Bericht"

HStAD D 4 Nr. 487/4, image 120, right page, headed "Unterthänigster Bericht" (marked "B R 12/5" at the top left). The
report runs on over image 121 and is signed by Jähring on 121, right page. Clear German with cipher runs. This file
covers the four runs in the upper half of 120 (120.1-120.4). The long run at the foot of 120 (120.5) and the cipher
block of image 121 are in reading_121.md. The clear text around the runs is an approximate working transcription.

<!-- 120.3  er uns nemlich in der messe eine casse von siebe{y/n} tausend gulden gemacht hat -->
<!-- 120.4  ihn sehe{y/n} ihm viel gn{u/a}diges zu s{u/a}gen -->

Status of these four runs: **read in part**. Each run is a clause of 4 to 14 words that completes a clear sentence.
Words: 29 verbatim from the signs, 7 with a marked change, 1 hole (37 words).

## The runs in their sentences

So bin ich glücklich genug, Ew. Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht zu melden, dass **wir die Messe glorreich geendigt haben,
anstatt, wie ich fürchtete, stecken** zu bleiben.

Einen **Hauptoperation habe ich [...] gemacht**, woraus ich den Resultat unterthänigst melde, dass **er uns nemlich in
der Messe eine Casse von sieben tausend Gulden gemacht hat**.

Und ich bitte Ew. Hochfürstliche Durchlaucht, wenn Sie **ihn sehen, ihm viel Gnädiges zu sagen**.

## Notes on the runs

- 120.1: the two letters at the end of the first line (gt, partly under a blot) give "xa"; "haben" needs y for g. The
  next line starts "vgg", giving "bnn"; "ben" needs y for the second g. Both are the g/y tail confusion.
- 120.2: nine letters (sitpaqsen) between "habe ich" and "gemacht" do not read; a name or a place may stand there.
- 120.3 and 120.4: the decryption gives y where n is wanted in "sieben" and "sehen" (one changed sign each).
  "Gnädiges" and "sagen" each have u where a is wanted.
