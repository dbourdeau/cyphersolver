Our building goes very slowly; not even the cellar vaults are finished. **The cash box is** for the most part **[...]**.

The **cash business** always gives me **terribly good** [the sense is unclear].

We have **pulled ourselves out of unbelievable difficulties**.

The snake always grows a new head. People try **to harm** our **credit**, and we keep **it all the same**.

But I **grow tired [...]**, **I lie** [...] Kuppel's Speyer [...] and our correspondents [...]. **[They] are well disposed.**

Here we have constant **withdrawals** [of credit]; but I do not write **out of mistrust**.

Jacobi has [...] me **a thousand guilders [...]**.
