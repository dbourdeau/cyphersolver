run is given in its sentence; the cipher words are in bold. The clear text around the runs is an approximate working
transcription, given only to place the runs (it was not read word by word).

Status: **not prose**. The cipher is a set of short runs (28 words in all) set into a letter in clear German. Each run
completes a clear sentence, but the cipher forms no sentence of its own. Words: 17 verbatim from the signs, 8 with a
marked change, 3 holes.

## The runs in their sentences

Unser Bau geht sehr langsam, noch nicht einmal die Kellergewölbe sind fertig. **Die Casse ist** grösstentheils **[...]**.

Die **Cassengeschäfte** geben mir immer **fürchterlich gut**.

Wir haben uns aus **unglaublichen Schwierigkeiten gezogen**.

Der Schlange wächst immer ein neuer Kopf. Man sucht unserem **Kredit zu schaden**, und wir erhalten **ihn doch**.

Aber ich **ermüde [...]**, **liege ich** Kuppels Speyer und stellen unsern Correspondenten. **Sind gut gestimmt.**

Hier haben wir beständige **Aufkündigungen**, ich schreib aber nicht **aus Miscredit**.

Jacobi hat mich durchgehen **tausend Gulden [...]**.

## Notes on the runs

- 119L.2 (eynlug): the mechanical decryption "sehult" does not read and is left as a hole. "schuld" would need two
  changed signs and is not taken.
- 119.2: "fürchterlich gut" is what the letters give. The sense in context is odd, and the clear words before it
  ("geben mir immer") were not read closely.
- 119.7: "ermude" is verbatim, but the six letters after it (flstjr) do not read; "liege ich" is verbatim. The clear words
  after the run ("Kuppels Speyer") are a working transcription. Kuppel is named again in cipher on image 121.
- 119.8: the writer left out one m of "gestimmt". The key runs on without a gap, so the m was never enciphered.
- 119.13: the last three letters (crd) do not read.
- Several changed letters are q where g or y gives the sense (119.3, 119.5, 119.10). In this hand q, g and y differ only
  in the tail, and the change always goes the same way. Each is marked, not silently changed.
