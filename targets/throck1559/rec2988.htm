    (Satoshi Tomokiyo, 17 September 2023) This is an extract of ciphered segments from a partially enciphered letter. The full letter not found in Patrick Forbes (1740), A Full View Of The Public Transactions In the Riegn of Q. Elisabeth (Google) around p.156. What I call "Throckmorton's Cipher 1" is used. The ciphertext in the margin (about half of the sheet) is unidentified.
