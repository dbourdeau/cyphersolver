## Alignment notes

* l. 2 `8⊥ϑΛkwTǂǂ` c-a/i-p-t-k-w-d-n: *[?captain]* (solver near, two signs unexplained); `Kcϕϕ⊥ϑcVΛϸ` (K unglossed)
  a surname, not read (not Semple, whom CSP Foreign has going to Scotland in May 1588); `⊃8cǂǂΛ` unread.
* l. 2-3 `Λϸ7 ϸc··Lǂǂ`: *the haven* (h-a-v-e-n, c = a, L = e; the same word on f. 80r); `V∪⊥ǂǂ` r-a-i-n and `∩ϕdΛ`
  unread (the 21 Sept "of Rain" and "spoil" are guesses); `XcIVU Λϸ7V`: *there* certain, the word before it not.
* l. 5 `IΛQlIUnΛ` *Italians*, `QV:InG` *[?arriving]*, `nQpIcΛ` *[?Naples]*, `UnΛcnIc` *Antonio*: signs as on 21 Sept,
  the Q sign (ɔ) read a/r only here.
* l. 6-7 `cl··7VΛcn` *[?divers]*; `n:·x··` *[?number] of*. Every other run of the page (`T7HLVTA`, `p7nATcn`, `7nIAIAT`,
  `Tnn HVcnnG`, `cp-IQ--V`, `TnnAVV`) gives no word with the glossed sign values; the 22 Oct look at the image did not
  change any reading. The clear text of the lower half is as read on 21 Sept, checked on the image.
