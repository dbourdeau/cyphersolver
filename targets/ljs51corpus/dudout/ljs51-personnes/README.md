# LJS 51: secondary people in the compiler's headings, and internal dating

Contribution by dudout (Brice Dudout; AI agent Claude Opus 5.5), 28 Nov 2026. It adds to
`targets/ljs51corpus/identity_ibn_durayhim.md` (Daniel Bourdeau, origin/main d8ac608093), whose sections 2 and 4 list these
people as "not identified (not checked)". Nothing in Daniel's files is changed.

## Method
- Each heading was re-read on the Penn IIIF images (2343 px = OPenn, public domain; "University of Pennsylvania, LJS 51,
  OPenn") at 1-2.5x, and the readings were frozen (SHA-256 `ffa417d7…6a2b`, 2026-11-28T14:23:35Z) before any biographical
  search, so that a candidate could not steer the reading.
- Criterion fixed in advance: **certain** = at least 3 independent matching elements (ism, laqab, father/grandfather, nisba,
  office, place, date) and none against; **probable** = 2; **not found** = fewer, or homonyms not separated.
- Sources: OpenITI texts of Safadi, *A'yan al-'asr* and *al-Wafi*; Ibn Hajar, *al-Durar al-kamina*; Maqrizi, *al-Suluk*,
  *al-Muqaffa*, *Durar al-'uqud*; Ibn Kathir, *al-Bidaya*; Ibn Taghribirdi, *al-Manhal*, *al-Nujum*; Ibn Rafi', *al-Wafayat*;
  Nu'aymi, *al-Daris*. Page numbers are the OpenITI page markers of the Shamela editions (VxxPyyy) and should be checked
  against the printed books before citation.
- No statistical measurement, so no held-out portion and no freeze entry on #1023.

## A reading correction: f. 45v l.11 (the mukus)
On the image I read «فابيت / الّا ان تبطل المكوس واستعفيت» (shadda on الّا; dot under the first tooth of تبطل): "I refused,
**unless the mukus were abolished**, and asked to be excused". The chain record has «الان بطل المكوس» (reading medium). So the
passage states a condition the compiler set, not an abolition that had happened, and there is no abolition event to date it by.
(Damascus abolitions found: the Nasiri survey, *Suluk* 3:313, year 741; Baydamur, 16 Sha'ban 762, *Bidaya* 18:630, after Ibn
al-Durayhim's death.)

## Dating
- **101r-102r: after 28 Safar 749** (the father is "al-marhum") **and before late 761** (death of Shihab al-Din), in Cairo, in
  'Ala' al-Din Ibn Fadl Allah's chancery. With 62r (after Safar 760) and 102v, this fits Ibn al-Durayhim's last Cairo stay
  (late 759/760-762).
- **99r**: "al-Sahib" points to 746 or later; before 764; at Alexandria, where Ibn Murajil was once nazir (date not found).
- **60r**: weakly, before 'Arafa 760 (the father is named without "al-marhum").
- **86r**: the recitation the compiler heard in Alexandria falls in 730-733, which matches Safadi's account of Ibn al-Durayhim
  first coming to Egypt as a merchant in 732 or 733.
- **24v**: in Cairo.
The range c. 1325-1360 in NOTES.md is confirmed and, for the last quires, narrowed to 749-761. Nothing found contradicts the
identification of the compiler; the people identified all belong to the circle of 'Ala' al-Din Ibn Fadl Allah's secretaries.
