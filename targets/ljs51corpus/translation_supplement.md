# LJS 51: English for the ciphers that the eight translation files do not cover

Made at write-up time (27 Nov 2026) by the model, from the plaintexts in `keys_eye/20r.8+47r.1.json`,
`keys_eye/46v.11+13r.1.json` and the chain record 49r.1 (`chains_all.json`). Not checked by a human Arabist.
Translation confidence is given per verse. The eight `translation_*.md` files translate the written copies as the chain
records gave them on 27 Nov; where a by-eye key shows that the cipher spells a word differently, the decryption files
(`texts/arabic_*.md`) follow the cipher and the key files list the differences.

Note on `translation_0006_0053.md`, section 20r.8: that section translates the rhymed prose of f. 21r. The later key
work showed that this prose is the plaintext of the cipher on ff. 48r-48v (with 21r l.1-5), which has its own page
(`targets/ljs51f48r`), and that 20r.8 enciphers riddle verses 1-5 instead (below).

## 20r.8+47r.1 (cipher 20r l.8-20v l.13 and 47r l.1-11; the riddle in clear 47r l.13-47v l.7)

One cipher of al-Jawlani's eight-verse riddle, split by the misbound quire 3. Verses 6-8 (the 47r part) are translated
in `translation_0078_0101.md`, section 47r.1. Verses 1-5 as enciphered:

فما وليد ربا في شهر مولده وعاد فيه قديم السن قد نحلا / وما ثياب وقد خيطت بلا ابر وما ادير لها سلك ولا فتلا /
وما فتى ان تهيا هيب جانبه ولا يهاب اذا في الملبس انتعلا / وما جبان لما عان في شرف ان ابطا ساعه مبناهما بطلا /
وما مساكن ترتاح القلوب لها يريدها القوم قد كانت لهم حللا

1. What is a newborn that grew up within the month of its birth, and in that month turned old in years and wasted
   away? (confidence medium; the new moon would answer it, not checked against the compiler's answer-verses)
2. And what are garments that were sewn without needles, with no thread passed through them and none twisted?
   (medium)
3. And what is a youth whose side is feared when he makes ready, but who is not feared when, dressed, he puts on his
   sandals? (low)
4. And what is a coward who, when he toils in a high place, if he is slow for an hour, their building is undone?
   (low; the syntax is a guess)
5. And what are dwellings in which hearts find rest, which the people seek, though they were once their garments (or:
   their halting places)? (low)

Metre: basit, as verses 6-8 (not scanned line by line here).

## 46v.11+13r.1 (cipher 46v l.11-13 and 13r l.1-4; plaintext 13r l.5-7)

قلت لنفسي حين مالت الى وزاره الشام وفعل الاثام / اين الحجى اين التقى تقنعي بقولهم هذا وزير الشام

I said to my soul, when it leaned towards the vizierate of al-Sham and the doing of sins: / Where is reason, where is
piety? Are you content that they should say, "This is the vizier of al-Sham"? (confidence high)

The compiler's note on 13r l.8-9 defends تقنعي for تقنعيني (the dropped nun is allowed). The theme is the vizierate he
was called to and refused (45v l.10-12; `identity_readings.md` section 6).

## 49r.1 (cipher 49r l.1-4; the verses in clear 49r l.6-7)

The block enciphers only the last twelve words of the two verses:

هتفن ضحى على بان بسلع سواجع كم سلين شج يرجع / سفكن دمي وما يدرين شوقا ولا يدرين يوما در دمع

At forenoon the cooing doves called on the ban-trees of Sal' - how often have they consoled a grieving man, who [then]
returns [to grief]? / They shed my blood, and they know nothing of longing, nor do they ever know the flow of tears.
(confidence medium; the enciphered part begins at شج يرجع)

The compiler says it "would not come apart except after much toil, because of its difficulty" (49r l.4-5).

## 63v.1 and 63v.trees (f. 63v; the couplet in the right margin), as corrected

`translation_0126_0149.md` translates the record's earlier reading (ابري ... الاحور ... بحور وثلثه). The sign cipher, the
tree script and the red letters over the trees all give four other words (`keys_eye/63v.1.json`, `63v.trees.json`):

ايري كبير والصغير يقول لي اطعن حشاي به وكن صنديدا / ناديت هذا لا يجوز فقال لي عندي يجوز فنكته تقليدا

My member is big, and the little one says to me: "Pierce my insides with it, and be a hero." / I cried, "This is not
allowed!", and he said to me, "With me it is allowed"; so I had him, by way of imitation (taqlid). (confidence medium
for the first verse, low for the point of تقليدا; metre kamil, not re-scanned)
