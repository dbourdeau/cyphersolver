# Penn LJS 51, differences between chain records and by-eye keys from f. 37r onwards

The record gives the earlier transcription. The key form follows the signs where they could be aligned. The cipher sometimes covers only part of a verse. Elsewhere a difference can reflect the spelling in the cipher, an omitted letter, or a correction to the earlier reading. Each linked key gives its evidence and uncertainties. The comparison ignores vowels, verse separators, uncertainty marks, alif variants, final ta marbuta versus ha, and alif maqsura versus ya. Word breaks are retained.
