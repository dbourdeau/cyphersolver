# Readings of the sibling telegrams of catalogue 1462 (20 Nov 2026)

Marks: `[?word]` uncertain; `[...]` one unread word; `[... n]` n unread words; `{x}` a letter supplied against the
key's output (slip); `*` a clear word of the telegram, not coded. Translations follow each reading. Decryption: `decryption.txt` (unedited output of
the key, `decode.py`). Counts are by words of the coded passages (README read bar).

## CMXV.15.1430.3-3: Hernández ("E H Leroy"), Presidio, 28 Mar 1911 (Hernández swap card)

> Total cie{n}to sesenta hombres. Diga si viene re{f}uerzo y cañón.
>
> Total one hundred and sixty men. Say whether reinforcements and the cannon are coming.

- The key's output: `total cieto sesenta hombres digas iviene reouerzo y canon`; two slips: "crnu" lacks the n of
  ciento, and "ninst" has s (plain o) where f is wanted (re-f-uerzo).
- **Confirmed by the period pencil gloss** at the head of the form, unnoticed by the catalogue: "Total 160 h[ombres] -
  Diga si viene refuerzo y [...]" (the end is under the CEHM watermark). So this telegram was in fact deciphered at
  the time.
- 10 of 10 words read (2 with a one-letter slip that the period gloss confirms). Complete.

## CMXV.15.1420.5-6: B. Hernández, Presidio, 27 Mar 1911, 9:35 am (Hernández swap card)

> [?Sánchez] tie[?ne] [...] {o}chenta hombres. Venga Villarreal [...] y tenga listo [?dan] cañón. Salgo por él mañana
> [?a mediodía].
>
> [?Sánchez] has [...] eighty men. Let Villarreal come [...] and have the cannon ready [?]. I leave to fetch it
> tomorrow [?at noon].

- Output: `fanch eztie aedie noche ntaho mdres benga billa realb oespj entey tenga listo dan danon salgo porel manan
  aieedi odia`.
- Sure: ochenta, hombres (`mdres`, d for b), venga (written "benga"), Villarreal ("billareal"), y, tenga, listo,
  cañón ("danon": cipher d for c; the card has CLESE = canon in 1420.3-6), salgo, por, él, mañana.
- Probable: Sánchez (`fanch`, F for S), tiene (`tie` + `ae`), a mediodía (`aieediodia`: "ie" where "am" is wanted).
- Unread: `aedie` before ochenta, `boespj` before `-ente y`, `dan` before cañón.
- It answers 1420.3-6 of the same day (Villarreal to leave Wednesday or Thursday with a cannon).
- 19 words: 11 sure, 4 probable, 1 sure-but-emended (hombres), 3 unread. Strict 63 %, 84 % with the probable. Read in part.

## CMXV.17.1695.1-2: J. González, Marfa, 5 May 1911 (Clave G.A. card)

> ... envíen facturas José Pin{e}da para reclamarlas ...

- `kopjg Ftskf Tlkap Lvnme`, joined and read in pairs with the Clave G.A. card: ko pj gF ts kf Tl ka pL vn me =
  j o s e p i n f d a. One pair (pL = f) is a slip for e. **The period pencil gloss "José Pineda"** stands over the
  groups, so this telegram was deciphered at the time; the card confirms the gloss. All 4 coded groups read (1 slip).
