# Readings of the sibling telegrams of catalogue 1462 (20 Nov 2026; revised after review, PR #831)

Marks: `[?word]` uncertain; `[...]` one unread word; `[... n]` n unread words; `{x}` a letter supplied against the
key's output (counted as a hole); `*` a clear word of the telegram, not coded. Translations follow each reading.
Decryption: `decryption.txt` (unedited output of the key, `decode.py`). Counts are by words, only sure words count
(README read bar). Classes: "read", "read in part" (coherent sentences under 95 %), "decoded, not read" (words but no
sentence).

## CMXV.15.1430.3-3: Hernández ("E H Leroy"), Presidio, 28 Mar 1911 (Hernández swap card). Read

> Total cie{n}to sesenta hombres. Diga si viene re{f}uerzo y cañón.
>
> Total one hundred and sixty men. Say whether reinforcements and the cannon are coming.

- The key's output: `total cieto sesenta hombres digas iviene reouerzo y canon`; two slips: "crnu" lacks the n of
  ciento, and "ninst" has s (plain o) where f is wanted (re-f-uerzo).
- **Confirmed by the period pencil gloss** at the head of the form ("Total 160 h - Diga si viene refuerzo y", the end
  under the CEHM watermark). So it was deciphered at the time, though it is not in the typed list 1795.1 (see below).
- 8 of 10 words sure from the key; ciento and refuerzo each carry one corrected letter, and the period gloss gives
  both words (160; refuerzo). Read.

## CMXV.15.1420.5-6: B. Hernández, Presidio, 27 Mar 1911, 9:35 am (Hernández swap card). Read in part

> [?Sánchez] [?tiene] [?cien] ochenta hom{b}res. Venga Villarreal [...] y tenga listo [...] [?cañón]. Salgo por él
> mañana [?a] [?mediodía].
>
> [?Sánchez] [?has] [?a hundred] eighty men. Let Villarreal come [...] and have the [?cannon] ready [...]. I leave to
> fetch it tomorrow [?at] [?noon].

- Output: `fanch eztie aedie noche ntaho mdres benga billa realb oespj entey tenga listo dan danon salgo porel manan
  aieedi odia`. The pencil strokes on the form fall after "fanchez" and "tieae", so the words are `fanchez tieae dien
  ochenta`; "dien" is perhaps cien, with d for c as in "danon" (reviewer's observation).
- Sure from the key (10): ochenta, venga (written "benga"), Villarreal ("billareal"), y, tenga, listo, salgo, por,
  él, mañana.
- Corrected or uncertain (holes): Sánchez (F for S), tiene (`tieae`), cien (`dien`), hombres (`homdres`, d for b),
  cañón (`danon`, d for c; cf. CLESE = canon in 1420.3-6), a mediodía (`aieediodia`, "ie" where "am" is wanted).
- Unread: `boespj` before `-ente y` (one word, extent estimated), `dan` before cañón.
- 19 words, 10 sure: **53 %**. It answers 1420.3-6 of the same day (Villarreal to leave Wednesday or Thursday with a cannon).

## CMXV.15.1420.6-6: "B. H. Leroy", Presidio, 27 Mar 1911, 3:08 pm (Hernández swap card). Decoded, not read

- Output: `nfsal gohoy man eand eande carta luque urdoe ctres isuae ctrar arreg loscapitul`.
- Words: salgo, hoy, carta, Luque, arreglos (5 of at least 10); `mle nled nledn` looks like a garbled "mande"
  repeated (struck through in pencil on the form); `urdoe ctres isuae ctrar` and `capitul` do not give a sentence.

## CMXV.15.1432.3-4: "E.H. Leroy", Presidio, 29 Mar 1911, 7:45 pm (Hernández card on the coded words). Decoded, not read

- Coded words with the card: usula = total, atqtn = Luque, neuin vroul = entre vista (3 of 13). The other groups
  (`osasnom aiamodon eloru irtan dedile dscse umons dtmen ouna anes`) do not read with the card; some may be clear words
  mangled in transmission. The clear words: tenemos, dícese, gobierno (`coverino`), concedió, [?mueocado], evítalo.

## CMXV.15.1447.1-3: Braulio Hernández, Presidio, 31 Mar 1911, 7:18 pm (mostly clear). Decoded, not read

- Coded words with the card: `revru Lorur Lichrh` = `invit asiti archih`, which reads **"invita sitiar Chih."**
  (reviewer's reading, PR #831): "[?Jacinto] Rubio invites [us] to besiege Chihuahua"; `Orgtn` = sigue. So 4 of 10
  coded groups read; `Kttns`, `chule edsase`, `esaan elltc`, `Mrule` do not.
- The clear part: "Presidente aliviado. Fecha 23 [?advs] correas de [?Jacinto] Rubio ... Orozco 1300 hombres ...
  Gobernador viene de Guerrero con 400. Franco sigue. Auxilio capitán Escudero ... Mándame de aquí auxilio ... Escaso
  [?Alanís vive]."

## CMXV.17.1695.1-2: J. González, Marfa, 5 May 1911 (Clave G.A. card). Existing decipherment

- The catalogue lists it as partly read, and the CEHM record prints "José Pineda". **The typed list of period
  decipherments CMXV.18.1795.1 has the whole telegram** (scan 21, entry 113): "Nemi informa mercancías enviadas capitán
  Lewis decomisadas. Envíen facturas José Pineda para reclamarlas. Conviene llamen Lewis. Esperen carta." So the clear
  words "Luis", "Etamen" of the form are Lewis and llamen.
- What this adds: the pencil gloss "José Pineda" covers all four coded groups (`kopjg Ftskf Tlkap Lvnme`), and the
  Clave G.A. card read in pairs gives the same (ko pj gF ts kf Tl ka pL vn me = j o s e p i n f d a; pL = f is a slip
  for e).

## CMXV.17.1695.2-2: "J. Gouzateo", Presidio, 5 May 1911 (Clave G.A. card, fragments). Not read

- `ykego ijkabji Gydyn birbh kukjg nkygf kbpap apgtf`: the card gives `aton` at the start (offset 1) and
  `...l..v..oucsenn?s` (offset 0), where the period pencil gloss also stops ("at ono ll e va oucpennos"). In 1795.1,
  entry 114, right after 1695.1-2 (entry 113), is left blank: perhaps the place kept for this telegram.

## Not read

- **CMXV.17.1648.2-3** (Gabriel, San Antonio, 27 Apr 1911): the Hernández swap and Clave G.A. cards do not read it,
  nor does a simple-substitution solver that recovers a control of the same length. The recipient wrote **"No se
  entiende"** across the form. In 1795.1, entry 103 (San Antonio, 1 May) is signed "Gabriel Madero" in full and entry 98
  (San Antonio, 30 April) "Adrían Gabriel", so the sender is perhaps Gabriel Madero; the 1795.1 page that would hold
  26-29 April (entries 94-97) is missing from the CEHM scans, so whether this telegram was typed there cannot be checked.
- **CMXV.34.3344.1** (Ávila to Villa, 15 Jun 1914, two-digit groups): the two-digit syllabary card of CMXV.16.1557.1
  scan 3 tried as is and with every additive digit offset (both orders, 200 variants): nothing reads.
- **CMXV.16.1561.10-13** is not in cipher. 1795.1 types it in clear (scan 12, entry 66): "Buhger necesita con urgencia
  otros cinco mil. B. Hernández."
