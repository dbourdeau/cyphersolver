## Notes
1. **Subject.** The fate of deserters from the Russian army ("moskowitsche") who came over to the Swedes in
   Pomerania: some sent to Sweden, the others put into the regiments for the time being, "so that they do not eat
   the bread uselessly", until they can be shipped safely to Sweden. Then news from Stralsund (Major General Mevius).
   This fits the Russian presence in Swedish Pomerania in 1712-1713; the year (1712 or 1714, by the archivist's
   pencil) is not settled by the text read.
2. **Language.** Early 18th-century chancery German ("Deserteurs", "krigen", "kan", "letzlich").
3. **Author and addressee.** Probably Johan August Meijerfeldt (Meyerfeldt), governor-general of Swedish Pomerania,
   to the King ("au Roy" on neighbouring pieces 143519 and 143531); the page seen has no signature.
