# diede1730 - English rendering, Hann. 92 Nr. 1149/1 aufn 0004, groups 1-160

P.S. ad Relat. 1, Regensburg, 2 January 1730. The passage begins and ends mid-sentence (it continues the postscript of aufn 0003).
Words in square brackets are supplied.

> ... whose good and confidential friendship, in the Electoral College as well as in the Evangelical Body, is, as at all
> times, so above all at present, of no small [importance], and it daily [gives] occasion ...

"Evangelical Body" = the Corpus Evangelicorum, the Protestant estates at the Imperial Diet in Regensburg. Whose friendship is
meant is stated in the part of the postscript before this page (aufn 0003), not translated here.
