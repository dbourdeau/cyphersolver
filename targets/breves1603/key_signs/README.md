# Key sign cut-outs

One strip per plaintext letter (the letter as written at the top, its cipher homophones below), cut from the
alphabet row of the original key, BnF fr. 3462 f. 103 (Gallica ark btv1b9058227h, canvas 109), via `key/alpha.jpg`.
Cut by hand at the midpoints between the columns, 11 Nov 2026; the O and P strips overlap a little because
P's first homophone is written under O. `et.png` is the & column. Shown at the top of docs/breves1603.html (docs/_keyfig.py).
