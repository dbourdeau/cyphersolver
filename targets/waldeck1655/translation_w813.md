# Translation: Waldeck to the Elector, Königsberg, 14 September 1655, the cipher passages (scans 813-814)

English of `reading_w813.md`. [51] is a number outside the letter alphabet, probably a code for a person (perhaps Your Electoral Highness); not resolved.

Weiher has also offered to assist [51]. At Marienwerder, Vistula boats have also been ordered. [...] Magnus came back from Thorn yesterday and reports that they have resolved there to defend themselves, and are also at work to hold a meeting with Danzig, Elbing and the three voivodes on the Vistula, in order to put themselves in a state of defence together; also that they are not unwilling to assist [51].
