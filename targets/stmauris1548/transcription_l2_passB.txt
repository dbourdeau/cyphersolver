# N-signs (unknown shapes)
N1   large compound sign (4r L20 start): stem with crossbar, big open loop hanging below, small ring at top right; ornate, like a looped q/h
N2   plain cross (+) with a small ring on top of the stem and a small loop at its foot
N3o  "m"/"ii" shape (two or three short minims) with a raised ring (4r L17, 4r L20, 3v L07)
N4   two upright minims run into a loop, like "ue"/"lle" (4r L20)
N5   small ring joined by a bar to a double-barred cross (o#)
N6   small epsilon / reversed-3 in light ink (4r L16)
N7   single small ring or loop standing alone (no bar, no tail); the commonest N-sign
N9   upright stem with two crossbars and a small ring/loop on top (like a double dagger with a loop head)
N10  small ring under a horizontal top stroke joined at the right, like a 7 or flat bar resting on a ring
N11  t- or f-like crossed stem followed by small u (or i) minims ("tu"/"fu"/"ti"); the f-form has a descending tail
N12  open triangle pointing down (nabla)
N13  ring + small cross + ring written as one sign (o+o)
N15  tall stem with a 3/z-shaped bowl attached at the right, like "t3" or a German sharp s (4r L02)
N16  w (double loop) with a dot above (3v L22)
N17  figure 4 whose stem ends in a closed loop at the foot (4 over a small b/6)
N18  dumbbell O with a small tick/accent above the bar (3v L17)
N21  y/gamma-like sign with a curled, hooked top and a long descending tail (sometimes with a dot above)
N24  open e-loop followed by a ring, the e often with a long curved underside (like "eo" or "Lo" as one sign); 3r L04 has a dark filled ring
N25  phi-like curl: open c-curve run into a p-like loop, light ink (3v L04, 3r L12)
N26  f-like stem with a y-like descending tail and a dot above ("fj"), 3r L17
N31  ring or 4-like head on a stem with a short horizontal bar near the foot (3r L03, 3r L14)
Not used: N3 without mark, N8, N14, N19, N20, N22, N23, N27-N30 (dropped or merged while working).
x covers both the crossing-stroke x and the back-to-back-curve form; xo is the crossing x with long tail and raised ring.
