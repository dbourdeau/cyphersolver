## English

(3r) However, the lord Cesare Pignatelli carried the [...] that one [...] that it was a pure reprisal, and was [...] beforehand your reply.

The said Dorsey sent a courier here express [...] [?Rome], who arrived on the sixteenth of December, and informed by him that you were truly making the enterprise of Mirandola [...] of monseigneur our prince; for which the count of the said Mirandola has done very well for himself, for his guard has been increased by a hundred arquebusiers, and in this [...] the said [...].

They feel it to their entrails, what has happened at [...], and fear that a castle may be built there; and I wish, and believe, that they were made to lose the desire for it.

They have sent from La Rochelle about fifteen hundred soldiers to Scotland to reinforce their men, who are much reduced on that side; and unless they are helped by the said Scotland they are on the way to being badly treated.

And from what one hears, the king is resolving to send a powerful army there again, of which I will inform you from time to time.

For the rest, monseigneur, I can assure you in confidence that people are not without hope of reaching some peace between these princes; so that in my view you will do very well to temporise your [...], if time might bring [...] some remedy; and I will not fail, monseigneur, to inform you from time to time as public affairs go.

Captain [?Sebastiano] of Finale [...] is with [...] to pray you to write [...] you, it has been entrusted to him.

## Notes

- "Cesa Pignatel": a member of the Neapolitan Pignatelli family; the gloss on 3r spells the same signs "...pignat...". Not identified further.
- "Dorsei": Dorsey, whose detention at Crescentino is the subject of the clear text above the cipher.
- The Mirandola enterprise and the count's new guard of a hundred arquebusiers: Galeotto II Pico, count of Mirandola,
  under French protection from 1548 (not verified).
- French troops sent from La Rochelle to Scotland: taken to be the 1548 expedition to Scotland; the port and date of
  sailing are not verified.
- "delibere d'i renvoie{+r}" (3v L20) reads since the barred 8 (`N15`, b) was split from the plain 8 (q).
- The hole at 4r L08 contains the letters "borbon" (two `N15`); it is not read.
- All historical notes here are checked only in outline and must be verified before publication.
