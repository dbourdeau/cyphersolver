## New shapes (N12 and up)
N12 = cross whose upright has a tall ascender curving up and right, joined at right to a 3-like bowl ("t3", looks like a B/13 written with a cross).
N13 = the same tall bent-stem cross with a small loop at its right on the line; the stem stops at the line (a "to" with a tall curved stem). Could be a short fs or a +o variant; kept apart.
N14 = two-loop chain: small open loop, long U-swing, closed (often inked-in) loop at right ("u-o"); eee has three loops.
N15 = 8-like sign with a horizontal stroke across or over its top, top loop open (like a German sharp s or an 8 with a bar).
N16 = small loop joined to two uprights crossed by one bar ("o#", a vt with a double cross).
N17 = A-like sign: long oblique stroke rising from lower left, short upright, small ring at top right, no crossbar (looks like a 4o whose diagonal runs on below the line).
N18 = long f-stem with a foot curling to the lower left, crossed by a bar, with a small n/r at the right ("fn").
N19 = cursive x whose right half closes into a ring, with a small curl at top left (like a 3 or x joined to an o).
Used from the given list: N3, N4, N6. 5o? = a 5 whose top closes into a ring joined to the stem (not a separate raised ring); written 5o? throughout because the mark is merged with the base.

## Least sure
- 5o? at 9r L01, 9r L18, 9v L01, 9v L03, 9v L15, 9v L19: ring joined to the 5 rather than raised; could be a plain 5 with a looped top.
- 3o variants with a closed lower bowl (9r L03 after 4o, 9v L17 after 4o): read as 3o, but the base looks partly like a 6 or delta.
- 9r L17 "3o?": the raised mark over the small 3 is a heavy filled dot, not a clear ring.
- 9r L08 "Le?": looped sign on a flat base followed by a short upright stroke (not a c); maybe Le, maybe Le plus 1. Similar pair in 9v L14 "z 1" (z followed by a short upright), kept as two tokens.
- 9r L09 last sign at the torn edge (N4?) is half lost.
- 9v L15 "x3?": the 3 is written onto the x (overwritten or heavy), not clearly above it.
- N4 vs d: small rings with a tiny tick were put under N4; only rings with a long incoming stroke are d.
- N13 vs fs vs +o, and n vs y (y used only for the gamma shape with a loop at the foot: 9r L02, 9v L16).
- Dots: 9r L14 has a dot below w (not transcribed); 9v L12 has a dot left of xo (not transcribed); 9v L19 "+o ." dot is under the ring; 9v L20 "3o" has an extra dot above the ring.
- 9v L02: the "/" before z, and a faint line under the z (ruling or stroke?).
