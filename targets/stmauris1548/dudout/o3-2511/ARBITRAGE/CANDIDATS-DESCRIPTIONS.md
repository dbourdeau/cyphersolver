# Shape descriptions of the candidate names (shapes only, no values)
From SIGNS.md / CONVENTIONS-SHAPES (c. 1r) and, for names added on this page, from the header of the pass that added them.
Names added by one pass are written with its letter: `A:N13` is pass A's N13, `B:N9` is pass B's N9 (they are different lists).

- `d`   = small loop with a tail rising to the right (reversed e with an upstroke); c. 1r convention: loop with a long oblique tail coming in from upper left (most common loop sign), not crossed.
- `u`   = small v-shaped sign with a tiny loop at its left foot (the "v" of vt); pass A: small v-shape with only a short left stroke.
- `sq`  = small square (plain closed square).
- `B:N12` = square whose right side runs below the base and whose bars protrude (box with legs), distinct from plain sq.
- `fs`  = f-like stem with a loop on the right (written fu / fv / fo / fa / fn; pass B: with or without crossbar).
- `A:N13` = loop at right with a long stem descending to lower left (as N7), crossed at its top-left by an arched/hooked stroke (looks like "fp" / "ɣ6").
- `B:N9`  = stem with top hooking to the right, small closed loop at lower right, tail descending to lower left, no crossbar.
- `A:N9`  = c/ς-like hook whose top carries a small ring at its upper right, with a long descending (slightly curved) stem; close to q but the ring is open/hooked and the stem curves.
- `q` / `qo` = 9-like: ring with a (straight) descending stem; `qo` = the same with a raised small ring.
- `1o`  = 1-like stroke with a raised small ring; `12` = 1-like stroke with a raised 2-like mark.
- `4o`  = figure four with a raised small ring; `Ro` = R-like sign (A-like sign with a closed loop at the top and a leg) with a raised small ring.
- `N7`  = p/rho-like: ring at upper right with a long stem descending to lower left.
- `a`   = alpha-like loop with a tail (α), open, not crossed.
- `B:N13` = alpha (a) crossed by an oblique stroke.
- `A:N12` = small loop at lower left with a curved stem rising from its right and a short hook at its upper left (horned, ȣ-like).
- `B:N14` = open-topped 8: closed lower loop with two arms rising.
