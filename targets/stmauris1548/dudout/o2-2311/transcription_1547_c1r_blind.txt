# Blind shape transcription of c. 1r (Saint-Mauris to Ferrante Gonzaga, 1547, Biblioteca Estense)
# Who/when: Claude (Opus 5.5) sub-agent, 2026-11-23. Blind: only scans/c01r.jpg and wainut-SIGNS.md were read.
# Method: PIL crops of each row in 3 overlapping thirds, upscaled 1.5x, plus 2-3x zooms on doubtful signs.
# Rows: 27 = 7 (opening block, L01 starts with "Monseigneur") + 20 (main block). Later glosses in other ink ignored.
# Conventions decided (by comparing several instances):
#   d   = loop with a long oblique tail coming in from upper left (most common loop sign), NOT crossed.
#   D/  = the same loop with an extra short oblique stroke crossing it (clearly distinct, rarer).
#   u   = small v-shaped sign with a tiny loop at its left foot (the "v" of vt); kept separate from d.
#   w   = two joined small loops (infinity-like / omega-like), incl. the open "ew" variant at start of L18 and in L20.
#   O   = dumbbell. In L08 the first O has a small dot above its bar (not transcribed separately).
#   #   = H with one or two crossbars (both forms merged).
#   8   = 8-like sign, often with a short flat stroke on top (all forms merged; distinct from & of L12/L20).
#   fs  = f-like stem with loop on the right (written fu / fv / fo / fa / fn; all merged).
#   y   = 7-like sign with descending loop, and the gamma-like crossed loop of L13 (which has a dot above).
#   q   = 9-like ring with a straight vertical descending stem.
#   b   = only the b/beta-like sign of L18.
#   12  = 1-like stroke with raised 2; 1o? in L16 = same base with a blotted mark above (2 or o).
#   "/" = long oblique page stroke between signs; "(" = page closing stroke.
# N-shapes introduced:
#   N1 = curly x whose right half closes into a loop (like x+d ligature), distinct from plain x (L01, L02, L03, L09, L26).
#   N2 = 8-like vertical curl crossed by a horizontal bar (L07, L15, L27).
#   N3 = 8/g-like loop joined to a b-like looped stem ("gb" ligature), followed by "." in L07 (L07, L16).
#   N4 = figure 4 whose stem ends in a closed loop (L10, L13).
#   N5 = small loop joined to a double-barred cross (like vt but with #) (L11, L14, L26).
#   N6 = isolated small ring on the line (start of L12).
#   N7 = p/rho-like: ring at upper right with a long stem descending to lower left (L01, L04, L06 x2, L21 upright form).
#   N8 = 6-like sign whose bowl extends into a rightward horizontal loop (L16).
# Uncertainties: L02 sign 4 read 5 (S-shape, no clear raised o); dots marked .? are faint/possibly specks (L08, L10);
#   L23 two blotted small signs read u?; L17 second sign blotted (5o); y vs z for 7-like forms; N7 vs q mapping;
#   u vs d split may differ from the reference naming.
