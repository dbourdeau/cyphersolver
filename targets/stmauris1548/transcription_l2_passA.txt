## N-signs (new shapes)
N1: q-like ring at the top of an upright stem, the stem crossed by one or two horizontal bars (like a 9 standing on a double cross). 3r L03, L14, L16; 3v L11; 4r L19.
N2: small t-like cross joined to a following u/n/i (reads like "tu", "ti", "fu"); often lighter ink. 3r L03, L06, L14; 4r L08, L10, L11, L14.
N3: downward-pointing triangle (a v closed by a bar on top). 3r L09, L14; 4r L10.
N4: small free-standing closed ring or D-shaped loop at line height, no tail (not the d with its long upstroke, not a raised o).
N5: figure 4 whose stem ends at the foot in a closed loop (4 over a b-bowl); in 3r L09 with a dot at its upper right. 3r L09; 3v L05, L16.
N6: small loop at the bottom, with a stroke rising from it to a horizontal top bar hooked to the left (like a 7 over an o, or a capped delta). 3r L09; 4r L02, L03, L14.
N7: m-shape (humps, no x and no descending tail), always with a raised o, written N7o. 3v L07; 4r L17, L20.
N8: t-like cross joined to a 3 (reads like "t3"/"tz"). 4r L02.
N9: small ring joined on the left of a # (like "o#"). 4r L16.
N10: large fs-like sign (cross-stroke, loop, long descender) with an extra looped stroke rising on top, like an fs and a b run together. 4r L20, first sign.
N11: upright cross with a small ring at the top of the stem and a closed loop at its foot. 4r L20.

## Naming choices used throughout (to map against the other reading)
- q = 9-like sign: ring at upper right, long stroke descending to the lower left, no m (looks like xo without the cross, or mx without the m). Another reader may call it xo or mx.
- y = the r-like / gamma-like sign with a hooked left start and a descending left leg, mostly in lighter ink (in 3r L03, L06, L12, L14 it looks like an n).
- w = both the cursive w and the closed "infinity" form; W = the capital W with crossing stroke.
- a = the alpha/fish sign (including the heavy form in 3r L01); vt = the v + t sign (the v sometimes closed or blotted).
- d = loop with a long upstroke to the upper left; dd = two loops under an arch.
- @ = large open curl with a small 2-like curl inside.
- x: also where both dots sit above the stroke (3v L06).
- ( = the large light-ink curve at paragraph ends (3r L07, 3v L03/L09/L18/L22, 4r L22).
