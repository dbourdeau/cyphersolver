# Jean du Bellay to Montmorency, London, 16 June 1529 (BnF fr. 3078 p. 21, DECODE R3688): the cipher block, English translation

Monsieur, if you approve the advice I sent you some time ago — to pretend that Madame [Louise of Savoy] had not gone in
order to conclude, and that once she was there Madame Marguerite had, by importunity, made her send for powers — you must
not forget to have them dated from that time and from the place where the King [will] be.
