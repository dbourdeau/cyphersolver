# Venceslau de Lima to King Manuel II, eight code telegrams of November-December 1909 (ANTT PT/TT/CAM/M09, catalogue 409)

Status: contributed by WAINUT (27 Oct 2026). The eight coded telegrams are read from the decodes of the time that the
receiver pencilled above the groups; nine of 409 code items stay open. One rule for all eight: a telegram is read
when at least 95% of its words read as sense. Counted on the words of the readings (14 Nov 2026, see "Read bar recount"
below), seven telegrams are at 96.3% or above and m0011 is at 93.7%, so seven are classed read and m0011 read in part,
and the target read in part; the earlier figure (each at 95.7% or above, 400 of 409, 0.978) was a share of code items.
The open items are listed under Remaining gaps. Pairing the glosses with the groups gives 261
values of a two-part (randomised) Portuguese code. The site page and the ledger rows are left to the maintainer.

Catalogue 409. Contributed by WAINUT (https://github.com/WAINUTAI). The work was done with AI language-model agents;
the transcriptions were made with a vision-capable language model from the DigitArq images. A reviewing agent re-read
six of the eight coded telegrams against the full-resolution scans (m0005, m0009, m0014 and m0018+m0016 group by group
with every gloss; m0011+m0012 and m0020 in part) and corrected five readings of the first pass (listed under 3); the
corrections are applied here. m0001 and m0007 have had one reading.

## 1. The unit

- **Source.** Arquivo Nacional Torre do Tombo, *Documentos de D. Carlos, D. Amélia e D. Manuel II*, maço 9,
  PT/TT/CAM/M09, [DigitArq record](https://digitarq.arquivos.pt/documentDetails/43db7e2e319d472a996dd926fcc6c5c1),
  21 images, downloadable through DigitArq's JSON backend. The images carry the ANTT watermark and are not copied here.
- **What is in it.** Telegrams of Venceslau de Lima, head of the government, to King Manuel II during the King's
  state visit of November-December 1909 (Madrid, Paris, London). Two are on Spanish palace forms ("Real Palacio",
  Lisbon -> Madrid, by hand): m0001, with four code groups, and m0003-m0004, all in clear (`related_clear.md`). Seven
  are on French receiving forms with printed strips (Lisbon -> Paris, Hotel Bristol); two of these run over two forms
  (m0011+m0012, m0018+m0016). So the unit holds 9 telegrams on 11 forms; the even-numbered images are mostly backs.
- **The decode.** The receiver wrote the meaning in pencil above nearly every group. He also marked his doubts: a
  circled group with "(? uma palavra indecifravel)" (m0007) and underlined groups that came through garbled.

The French form says that the first number after the place of origin is the serial number and the second the taxed
words, so every Paris strip runs Lisbon -> Paris (the first pass listed some as "Paris" telegrams). The year is not on
the strips; the stamps read 27 NOV 09, and the dates fit together: m0011 is dated the 27th, a Saturday in 1909; m0009
(30th) complains of no answer to "Saturday's telegram" and says the King leaves "the day after tomorrow" (2 Dec);
m0007 (2 Dec) thanks him for agreeing.

**Word count.** On four of the seven Paris forms (m0005, m0007, m0011, m0014), code items + clear words + a constant
9 for the address equal the taxed count of the header (`groups.py`, part 2). m0020 gives 83, the figure the receiver
pencilled over the header's 93. m0009 is one short, and the office noted "(Correction suivra pour mot qui manque)".
m0018 is one short although its 91 groups were checked one by one against the scan; the missing word is not found.
The address constant is the only free figure. So five transcriptions agree with the count, and m0009 and m0018 may
each lack one word, m0009's probably in transmission.

## 3. The readings

The full texts are in `m*_reading.md` (Portuguese) and `m*_en.md` (English), group by group in `m*_decryption.txt`.
In short:

- **m0001** (12 Nov, to Madrid): thanks for the welcome in Spain; "Tudo continua sem novidade. Escrevo Windsor."
- **m0011** (27 Nov): Lima, with Porto and Serpa, proposes the return route: the 7.40 pm night train from Paris to
  Medina del Campo, then a special train to Lisbon, so as to drop the stop at Luso, "incommoda e de mau effeito".
- **m0018** (28 Nov): he edits the King's reply toast for the President's dinner: "e amizade" after "sympathia",
  thanks for the welcome "na sua intellectual Paris", "maior estreitamento das suas relações".
- **m0014** (28 Nov): since the President has given the Grand Cross of the Legion of Honour to Bocage, the King
  should be friendly to Briand, with no inequality against what was done in England and Spain.
- **m0009** and **m0005** (30 Nov): no answer yet about the return trains; departure is the day after tomorrow;
  a stop at Bussaco would be awkward; Vasconcellos Porto presses for an answer.
- **m0020** (30 Nov): the King should go only to São Carlos on the evening of his arrival and receive at the station.
- **m0007** (2 Dec): arrival in Lisbon at 1.15 pm; greetings at Vilar Formoso; lunch between Santarém and Lisbon.

**Corrections to the first pass** (from the review, applied): m0011 opens "Em combinação [?] com o Porto e Serpa
assentámos", not "Em Coimbra, como Porto e Serpa" (margin gloss "Em combina[ção]" over 01748 78853; 68173 = "com
o"); m0014 93186 = "G.C. Legião"; m0018 69958 + 66931 = "tend-" + "-es", i.e. "tendes feito", the words of the King's
draft; m0020 51443 = "contar com" and 19864 = "isso" (one value, not two), 05068 "inconveniencia" [?], 19313 "posso"
or "pouco" [?]; m0011 48696 = "Hen.." (Hendaye?), and 43243 left open there (see 4). Our own addition in this pass:
10231 + 01021 are glossed "medi" + "na" and spell Medi-na in both places, so m0011 reads "vir até Medina, onde" and "em
Medina para o trem especial" (the first pass had "Medina, na onde").

## 4. The code (`code_values.tsv`, `python groups.py`, output in `groups_output.txt`)

- **261 groups, 409 code items**, values from the glosses. Five-figure groups, sent with leading zeros
  (00327, 04184), and two-figure punctuation groups: 06 = full stop, 04 = comma. Words, phrases ("com toda
  urgencia", "depois de amanhã", "dar ordens", "resposta ao telegramma"), and syllables or endings for words not in
  the book (insis- / -to, Bri- / -and, estreit- / -amento, chega- / -ria, tornar- / -á).
- **Two-part (randomised).** The rank correlation between group number and the alphabetical order of the value,
  over 227 cleanly glossed groups, is 0.068; 30% of 2000 shuffles reach it. Every first figure carries values from
  almost the whole alphabet. So the code is not alphabetical, and it cannot be extended by bracketing.
- **Repeated groups agree.** Seven groups recur with two forms of one word or phrase (Em/em, teve/tem, terá/teria,
  comboio/comboios, relação/relações, 1 hora/uma hora, Pres. Republica/do P. Republica). The one open point is 43243: "da" four times (m0005, m0007, m0009, m0020),
  but in m0011 line 7 its gloss looks like "dep.."; it is not read there.
- **Not the code of the sibling folders.** No group is shared with King Carlos's code of 1903
  (`targets/carlos1903`), Sabugosa's (`targets/sabugosa1910`), Manuel II's of August 1910 (`targets/manuel1910`) or
  Hintze Ribeiro's of 1903 (`targets/hintze1903`). In this unit 38% of a telegram's groups recur in the other seven,
  and the ten commonest groups (e, a, que, na, V. M., em, de, no, da, mais) alone make up 22% of the groups; a
  telegram of 54-106 groups in the same code would share some of them by chance with P(none) below 2e-6 for each
  folder. (Most of carlos1903's coded text is French, so the test is weaker there.) Manuel II's 1910 telegrams also
  put "06" between groups, as here; whether that is a shared punctuation convention or more is open.

## 5. Prior work

- The decodes of the time are on the forms, so the readings are not new. No printed decipherment was found in one web search
  (Venceslau de Lima, telegrams to Manuel II, the 1909 journey, Medina, Bussaco); editions of Manuel II's or Venceslau
  de Lima's papers were not searched.
  What is new here is the transcription, the dating and direction, the word-count check, the 261 code values, and
  the corrected readings.

## Corrections to catalogue entry 409

- "ten telegrams, some in cipher": nine telegrams on eleven forms; eight carry code (one of them only four groups),
  one is in clear.
- "no decipherment noted": the receiver's pencil decode stands above nearly every group.
- "Government-to-king telegrams in the 1909 cabinet crisis": they concern the King's state visit to Spain, France and
  England of November-December 1909 and his return journey (route, toast, decorations, reception in Lisbon).
- Place "Lisbon": Lisbon -> Madrid (Palacio Real) and Lisbon -> Paris (Hotel Bristol).

## Read bar recount and check of PR #570 (14 Nov 2026): m0011 read in part

Brice Dudout (`VERIFY_dudout_20261114.md`, `verify_dudout_20261114.py`) re-ran the decode from `code_values.tsv` and the
eight transcriptions: 409 code items in the order of the decryption files, 409 of 409 values equal to the glosses.
Re-run here: the same.

He also showed that the `read_share` values recorded on 27 Oct 2026 (0.957 to 1.0) were shares of code items with a
gloss (m0011: 97 of 101), not shares of words, and that the pass which applied the README read bar to every reading
was never run on this target. `research/reading_audit/v10_relabel.py` on the reading files gives 0.868 to 0.973, but it
counts as holes the 31 words the readings supply in brackets for readability and which stand for no code group:
articles and prepositions the telegraphic style leaves out (`[o]`, `[a]`, `[da]`, `[vá]`), the signature place `[Lima]`,
and the expansions of abbreviated code values (`Pres[idente da]`, `G[rã-]C[ruz da]`, `[de Honra]`; the code book has
"Pres. Republica" and "G. C. Legião"). Those are not words of the telegrams, so they are left out of both the words
and the holes. What remains as holes are the unread groups and the doubt marks `[?]`:
