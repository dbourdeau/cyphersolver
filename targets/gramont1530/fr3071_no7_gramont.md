# BnF fr. 3071 no. 7, f. 17 — Gabriel, cardinal de Gramont (bishop of Tarbes), Rome, to Montmorency, 21 July [1530]

Image: Gallica btv1b9060315f, canvas 30 (= f. 17r). 7 lines clear, then 14 lines of cipher, then clear.

Key: "Gramont's cipher (1530)" (Lasry 2023, cryptiana GL.htm `GL/BnF_fr3071_f17.png`; Tomokiyo's table from
fr. 3019 f. 20, `img/francisGramont.png`). Substitution with 2-7 homophones per letter, LL/RR/SS signs, and
COM/CON signs. Lasry's table as transcribed:

Readings on the leaf settle three of Lasry's five unknown signs from context: **8Δ = PAPE**
("de la part du 8Δ", "8Δ trouvé bon"), **key-ring sign = ROY** ("congratuler avec le ⚷ de la venue de la
royne"), and **ʃʃ-on-a-stem is a null in word-initial position** (line 1). g serves for B, V and E: context decides.

## In modern French

... [le pape a] toujours bonne volonté et continue plus que jamais ... Parce que les Impériaux pourraient
concevoir un plus grand soupçon si j'y allais [moi-même], le pape a trouvé bon dès le commencement que ledit
Francisque y aille ..., et je pense qu'il n'y a là que du bien, d'autant qu'en restant ici je pourrai parer à
tout ce qu'on pourrait lui mettre en avant pour l'en détourner; à quoi je vous assure qu'on fait toute la
diligence possible. L'évêque de Faenza, neveu du comte de Carpi, partira dans trois ou quatre jours pour aller,
de la part du pape, féliciter le roi de la venue de la reine et de messeigneurs [les enfants de France].

## Date

"Rome, 21 July", no year. The congratulation on "la venue de la royne et de messeigneurs" is the return of
the Dauphin and Henri, hostages in Spain since 1526, with Queen Eleanor, handed over at the Bidassoa on
1 July 1530; the "bonne nouvelle" of the clear opening is the same. Rodolfo Pio, bishop of Faenza and nephew of
Alberto Pio, count of Carpi, did go to France as nuncio in summer 1530. So **21 July 1530**, and Gramont
(cardinal since June 1530) is in Rome. This fits Lasry's name for the key, "Gramont's cipher (1530)".
The "Francisque" sent in Gramont's place is presumably the same agent as in the clear text; the matter (a
marriage, or the Pope's meeting with the King) is not named in the cipher that has been read.

Gaps: the ends of lines 1-2 and the middle of lines 5-7 were read at lower confidence; about 85 % of the
cipher is read.
