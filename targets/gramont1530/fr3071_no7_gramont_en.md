## Translation

Monseigneur, I wrote to you the other day how it went with the Pope and with all others over here regarding the
good news you have let them know, which is such, and of so great effect, that everyone takes good hope from it that
things will be restored to their entirety; and little praise is given you for the perfection of the work. As I hope
to let you know more fully within three or four days by an express messenger, who will carry the full resolution
from here as to the matter of the coming of the sa[id] Francisque ...

⟨The [ʃʃ] [Z̄] will is still good and continues more than ever. [#] I am working to have it put into
du[▣] execution. And because the Imperialists might conceive greater suspicion if I went there, for this beginning
[the Pope] finds it good that the said Francisque should go there alone to learn what is being done there, and it
seems to me that there is nothing in it, the more so as, being here, I shall be able to forestall all that might be
put before him to turn him aside. As to which I can assure you that all possible diligence is being used. Monsieur
the bishop of Faenza, nephew of the Count of Carpi, will leave within three or four days to go, on behalf of [the
Pope], to rejoice with [the King] at the coming of the Queen and of my lords.⟩

I do not wish to forget [?to tell you] ... of good sums of promise ... and good ... that fourteen months ago ...
