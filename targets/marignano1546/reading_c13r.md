# Marignano to Ferrante Gonzaga, Ratisbon, 12 June 1546, c. 13r: reading of the cipher (Italian)

The cipher passage of c. 13r as the signs give it. Clear words that the writer mixed into the cipher are in *italics*.

Files: `reading_marked_c13r.txt` (line by line, checked by `scripts/check_marks.py`), `translation_c13r.md` (English),
`clear_c14r.txt` (the contemporary clear translation on c. 14r).

Marks: `(x)` = a letter with no sign, since the cipher writes double letters once. `[?x]` = a letter on a doubtful sign.
`{b}` = read b where the sign as named gives s. Null signs and the dotted-vowel groups are left out here.
*V. Ecc.a* expands the code `eti`.

## Italian

*Qua* la cossa di [?l]a guer(r)a contra luterani è per resolta, si actende al(l)e provisione, et io non li manco di quanto
posso, e ne ho più di parte. *Hora* si cognoscerà *meglio* V. Ecc.a. *L'aparechio* è grando, e per il primo ne mandarò
parti*cular* aviso [?a] V. Ecc.a. Qua si fa professione di tenir *questa cosa* oc(c)ulta, quale già è pu{b}lica. Però
V. Ecc.a non mi ne fac(c)ia me auctore.

## Notes on the text

- Spellings are the writer's and are kept: *cossa*, *di la*, *actende*, *grando*, *mandarò*, *auctore*. The translation
  on c. 14r has *della*, *grande* and *autore*.
- Double letters are written once in the cipher: *guera*, *ale*, *oculta*, *facia*.
- *pu{b}lica*: the third sign is a doubtful o-like sign (`o?`). Elsewhere `o` is s or m. The word rests on the sense and
  on c. 14r.
- *di [?l]a*: the l is a looped script l with a descender. It occurs once and is doubtful on the image. c. 14r has *della*.
- *aviso [?a]*: the sign is the hooked d (a) with an inner dot or stroke, which is doubtful.
- *per il primo*: by the first courier. c. 14r abbreviates it as "p.o".
- The last sentence on c. 14r reads "però V. Ecc.a no' [?mi ne] [?faccia] me autore". Some letters there are small and
  doubtful.
- An earlier rough reading of that line, "no' mancarà me avisare", was wrong.
