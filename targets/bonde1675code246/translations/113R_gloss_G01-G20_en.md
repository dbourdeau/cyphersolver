## Translation

Herr Chancery Councillor, (I have) most kindly to request: as he showed me his affection during my presence in
Stockholm [?ever] [?quietly], [?indeed] [?in fact], (for which) I find myself most highly obliged, that he would be
pleased [?still], and also (with me) away, to take it to heart and [?with] His Majesty to put forward my humble request,
(that) I may be [...] allowed here in this place by [?Christen], from this term's subsidies [?falling due] in January,
to take the four thousand riksdaler which His Majesty was most graciously pleased to grant me for my [?maintenance] here
in this place.

[...] to devote still to His Majesty's service, provided that I need not see myself so unfairly excluded from His Royal
