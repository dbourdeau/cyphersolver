# Carl Bonde, Paris, 13 Nov 1675, in the second code (1-246): the period gloss of image 113 right, gloss lines G01-G20

Source: the chancery's ink gloss written between the cipher lines of image 113 right (Riksarkivet R0003797_00113),
read from the images by Claude Fable 5.2 (gloss/113R_G01-G20_fable.tsv), blind-checked on a sample by Claude Opus 5.5
(gloss/113R_G05-G20_blind_check_log.tsv), normalised here: first option of each doubtful letter unless marked,
punctuation added, the period spelling kept. `[?word]` = doubtful word, `[...]` = unread word.

The gloss runs ahead of the cipher (about 2.2 letters of gloss for each number below it): G01 to the start of G19 is the
clear of the 33 cipher lines of 113 right (the draft-key decode of 114 left opens `at beklaga mig`, in G19), and the
rest of G19 to G33 with 114 left lines 1-4 is the clear of the cipher of 114 left. This
file covers G01-G20 only; G21-G33 and the gloss of 114 left are in the second entry (decoded, not read).

## Notes on the holes

- G02 `[?ält] [?skilla]`: Fable's shapes; the draft-key decode of the cipher at this point gives `altsom ste...`, so the
  gloss words are probably misread or abbreviated.
- G03 `[?sig] [?realiter]`: Fable's letters (`thy snälitas`, with alternatives) do not make a word; `realiter` is
  Fable's own suggestion. The cipher here decodes `och affection för hvilckes`, so the gloss may differ from the cipher.
- G05 `[?hoos]`: Fable's second options; the cipher decodes `och hoo[s] hans`.
- G07 `må [...]`: two letters unread; G07 `[?Christen]`: last word not resolved (Fable `Christon` / `Christen`).
- G08 `[?infallande]`: a large loop before `fallande`, read as a prefix.
- G10 `[?underhålld]`: one letter or flourish after `-lld`.
- G14 `min [...]`: word not understood by Fable (`rvit` / `egit`).
- G17 `[...]`: an unread ending after `ministrer`. G19 `[?orsak]` and `[...]`: very doubtful. G20 `[?aftal]`: first word doubtful.
- The sentence that ends G20 runs on into G21 (`[R]adiga förtröst[ning]ar ...`), read only by sub-agents.
