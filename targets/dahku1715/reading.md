- e05 ‹kodzarzyc› (V = z; with V = g "kodgarzyc"): a place name is expected after "przeprawić z"; not identified.

- e06 ‹a›: after the measurement we noticed that this isolated `+` is probably the clear word "to" (+o); not corrected.
