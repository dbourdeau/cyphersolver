Context (to be checked by a historian): autumn 1715, the run-up to the Confederation of Tarnogród (November 1715), when the
Sandomierz nobility rose against the Saxon troops of Augustus II. The unit card dates the letter 21 Sep 1715 ("1715, IX,
21"), and the cipher speaks of 9 October as still to come; our first sorting note (21 May) is probably wrong. The date line
of p. 1 was not re-read for this file.
