# c. 44r: marked reading, line by line

Diego Hurtado de Mendoza to Ferrante Gonzaga, enclosure of the letter dated Siena, 3 April 1549 (Biblioteca
Estense, Autografoteca Campori, Hurtado de Mendoza, Diego, sfasc. I, c. 44r). Read from the signs with key.tsv.
The contemporary decipherment on c. 42r was used to build the key; where the cipher and c. 42r differ, the reading
follows the cipher (NOTES.md). Code groups: `chu` tiempo, `dis` emperador, `qre` vuestra.

This file gives the reading of `reading_44r.md` line by line, with every departure from the mechanical decryption
(`decryption_44r.txt`, key.tsv) marked in place. `reading_44r.md` gives the same text as sentences, with the marks
left out and with editorial punctuation and accents. Built by `scripts/reading.py build`, checked by
`scripts/check_marks.py`.
