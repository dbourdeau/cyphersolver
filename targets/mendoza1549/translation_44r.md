# c. 44r: English translation

Translation of reading_44r.md. The reading has no holes; the 28 marked repairs are repairs of single sign values,
not guesses of words, and the translation carries them without marks.

And when we have given the authority to the Eight: if we order them to vote in secret, as they do now, the
government remains a republic; and if we order them to vote openly and by voice, it remains a principate of the
few; and if we give them a Captain of the People who has authority, and time, before whom the vote is held
openly, it remains a duchy of Florence. For this reason I would not make the Captain of the People for two months
before the Eight were named, and I made him for four the first time, to test how the brigata [the company; probably the
faction] would bear the time. This last I beg Your Excellency not to make known at court, nor to anyone in the world, because
they will say that one of us wants to make himself a tyrant; and I here have deceived Juan Gallego in it, so that
the Emperor is left with the door open if he wants to enter with an appearance of reason.
