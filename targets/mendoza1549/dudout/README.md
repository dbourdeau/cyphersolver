# mendoza1549, c. 44r: shape splits of x and o' checked blind (proposal)

The target is lccompany's (WAINUT, #519). Its NOTES name the shape splits as the next step: `x` stands for de (8
places) and no (6), and `o'` for ga (3) and ta (2). This folder records a blind check of those two splits, plus a
transcription and a marked reading that apply them. It is offered for the owners to take, change or decline.

## How
Brice Dudout (human reviewer) answered a blind sign sheet built by Claude on 23 Nov 2026. Each question showed one boxed
sign next to unlabelled examples of the two shapes from the same page, with no value or reading. There were 13 targets
and 2 controls (omega, mo), and both controls passed. The rule was written and hashed before any crop was made:
`sign_check_2311/REGLE-SELECTION.md` (in French), `REGLE-SELECTION.sha256`. The answers are in
`answers-2311-mendoza.json` and their meaning is in `results.tsv`. The images stay private (Biblioteca Estense): no crop
or derivative is included. AI analysis was not raised with the archive; the project's method was not presented to it
at the time.

## Result
- **o'**: the small round form and the flat form separate cleanly, and all 3 answers agree with the alignment to c. 42r.
  Proposed names: `op` (small round) = ga, at L06 s6, L15 s3 and L15 s13; `of` (flat) = ta, at L16 s7 and L16 s12.
  L11 s16 is also flat, but there the alignment wants ti ("ultimo"). It is labelled `of` and left as a repair; that
  `of` may stand for t + vowel is noted, not adopted.
- **x**: decoded with the sheet key (`sign_check_2311/sheet-2311-key.json`), the looped x agrees with de at 5 of 5
  places and the round x with no at 4 of 4. **Erratum (25 Nov)**: the first version of this folder decoded q01 (L13 s25)
  and q12 (L14 s1) as looped, "against the alignment", and held them back; the key gives form 2 = round x for both, so
  they agreed. The second sheet below asked them again in tight frames, with the same answer. Labels `xb` / `xr` are
  marked `!` where answered blind. One place (L05 s22) is a third shape, an "elongated 8" with a closed left loop and a rising stroke that does not cross it. It is labelled
  `x8`, value de by the alignment; the word was already read.
- Words that now read from the signs: L12 "no comunique" (round x = no), L16 "puerta" and "abierta" (flat o' = ta). No
  value comes from the sense alone.

Count as first applied (rule of #728, words read from the signs, 154 words; with the two places above wrongly held
back): 122 -> 125 (79.2% -> 81.2%), repairs 28 -> 25, code words 4. Still well under 95%: read in part.

## Second sheet (25 Nov 2026): x split agrees at every x answered blind
A second blind sheet (7 questions: 4 targets, 3 controls, all 3 passed; rule hashed first, `sign_check_2511/`) asked
the two "against" x places again with tight frames, and two odd-looking q' forms.
- **x**: L13 s25 and L14 s1 are both **round** in tight frames, as on the first sheet once decoded with the key. Over
  the two sheets, 10 of the 14 x of the page were answered blind: looped `xb` = de 5 of 5, round `xr` = no 4 of 4 (L12 s9
  also passed as a control), `x8` = de (1). The other four are the example places of the sheets (L03 s14 and s15, L05 s4,
  L08 s4; the rules call the L03 pair s13 and s14). Their form was labelled by Claude's eye when the sheets were built,
  not by the reviewer: they carry `xb` / `xr` without `!`, and the two words that change on them are counted probable
  (below). The values are the key's own (x: de 8, no 6), not the sense.
  Check made afterwards, not pre-registered: the split learned on L01-L10 alone (xr = no 2/2, xb = de 5/5) gives the
  right value at the 6 held-out x places of L11-L17, 6 of 6; unsplit x = de gives 2 of 6.
- **q'**: the open-horned q' of L04 is the closed dotted q' (transcription confirmed, "{ha/a} voz" stays repaired). The
  q' of L13 "diran" is a **g'** (key ra): the word reads di{-ga}ran, one mark instead of two (`o:`, ga in the key, is not
  explained there), still repaired.
- Word that now reads from the signs: L13 "uno". L03 "govierno" and L08 "no" rest on example places (round x seen by
  the model only): probable, so holes under the owner's rule. "noso{mo/tro}s" (L14) loses its x mark but keeps the
  lo = tro repair.

Count (rule of #728, 154 words): read from the signs 125 -> 126 (81.2% -> 81.8%), code words 4, repairs 25 -> 22,
