# mendoza1549, c. 44r: shape splits of x and o' checked blind (proposal)

The target is lccompany's (WAINUT, #519). Its NOTES name the shape splits as the next step: `x` stands for de (8
places) and no (6), and `o'` for ga (3) and ta (2). This folder records a blind check of those two splits, plus a
transcription and a marked reading that apply them. It is offered for the owners to take, change or decline.

## How
Brice Dudout (human reviewer) answered a blind sign sheet built by Claude on 23 Nov 2026. Each question showed one boxed
sign next to unlabelled examples of the two shapes from the same page, with no value or reading. There were 13 targets
and 2 controls (omega, mo), and both controls passed. The rule was written and hashed before any crop was made:
`sign_check_2311/REGLE-SELECTION.md` (in French), `REGLE-SELECTION.sha256`. The answers are in
`answers-2311-mendoza.json` and their meaning is in `results.tsv`. The images stay private (Biblioteca Estense): no crop
or derivative is included. AI analysis was not raised with the archive; the project's method was not presented to it
at the time.

## Result
- **o'**: the small round form and the flat form separate cleanly, and all 3 answers agree with the alignment to c. 42r.
  Proposed names: `op` (small round) = ga, at L06 s6, L15 s3 and L15 s13; `of` (flat) = ta, at L16 s7 and L16 s12.
  L11 s16 is also flat, but there the alignment wants ti ("ultimo"). It is labelled `of` and left as a repair; that
  `of` may stand for t + vowel is noted, not adopted.
- **x**: the looped x agrees with de at 5 of 5 places; the round x agrees with no at 2 of 4. Two no places (L13 s25,
  L14 s1) were seen as looped, against the alignment. So the split is only partly supported, and we do not propose
  renaming x as a whole. Labels `xb` / `xr` are given only where the answer agrees, marked `!`. One place (L05 s22) is a
  third shape, an "elongated 8" with a closed left loop and a rising stroke that does not cross it. It is labelled
  `x8`, value de by the alignment; the word was already read.
- Words that now read from the signs: L12 "no comunique" (round x = no), L16 "puerta" and "abierta" (flat o' = ta). No
  value comes from the sense alone.

Count (rule of #728, words read from the signs, 154 words): 122 -> 125 (79.2% -> 81.2%), repairs 28 -> 25, code words
4. Still well under 95%: read in part.
