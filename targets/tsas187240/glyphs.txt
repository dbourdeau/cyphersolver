## Layout (native px of n2.jpg)
Rule R1 at y~2130 (x 2900-4800); rule R2 at y~3320 (x 2770-4800); text below R2 = unit 2 (torn at the bottom edge).
Unit 1 (R1-R2): block A (y 2150-2780, partly torn/stained: lines A1, A2, A3) and block B (y 2700-3300: lines B1, B2).
Unit 2 (below R2): lines C1, C2.
B1 and C1 start with the goblet G01 and repeat each other glyph for glyph (fibre loss in C1 hides 1-2 glyphs).
A1 also carries the same sequence from a blotted goblet to at least G09, so the formula line stands THREE times (A1, B1, C1).
The second lines (A2/A3, B2, C2) are a longer, more cursive row of different signs; B2 and C2 repeat in part (start G15 G16, end G22-G27).
Small interlinear marks sit above line 2 of both units (unit 1 at abs ~3130-3340, y 2930-3050; unit 2 at ~3220-3340, y 3520-3620).

## Counts and alignment with the Hebrew formula
B1: 16 signs (G01-G14, G03 twice), 8 words including the goblet (7 without).
Hebrew formula: 9 dot-groups (לכ עב קו וח כך לס נן מעט נך). The 7 words of B1 do not line up one to one with the 9 groups.
Splitting G07 | G08 and G09 | G03 G11 would give 9 words without the goblet, but the scribe's spacing does not support those splits.
So no 1:1 alignment is shown. The second lines B2/C2 have 8-9 words, also unrelated in count.
The Hebrew formula lines vary in length (5, 9, 10-11 groups) while the glyph formula line repeats exactly, so it is not a
group-for-group transliteration of the Hebrew lines (open: could still be a code of the same formula, with the goblet as a line marker).
