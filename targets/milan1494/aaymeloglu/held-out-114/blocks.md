## Masking notes

- Gloss ink and its trailing rules are replaced by flat paper colour inside the interlinear band over the cipher span; cipher ascenders, descenders from the line above, raised dots and raised small signs are kept. Where gloss touched a cipher stroke the stroke was kept and the gloss cut away, leaving small notches or short stubs.
- A small raised two-sign mark occurs three times on c259v (lines 4, 13, 16, right half). It could not be decided whether it is a cipher superscript or a decipherer's mark; it was left unmasked and is flagged in manifest.tsv.
- Crops are 3x LANCZOS of the raw pixel box in manifest.tsv; a/b halves overlap by about 60 raw px (x 640-700).
