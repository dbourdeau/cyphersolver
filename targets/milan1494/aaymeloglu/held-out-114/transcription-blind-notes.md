## Conventions

- Units follow visible gaps; division is approximate. `kind=null` only for a unit that is a single ###, ##, 9n, Z°, Z°x or F. B, ET, TH, RC standing alone are left as `word`.
- "9n" is used for a lone g-like 9 with a sweeping tail, as in the exemplars; "9" inside words.
- The long lead-in underline of `oo` is not counted as a separate N. Where I first counted one (c259v L15, twice) the third look removed it.
- Method: pass 1 from 2x slices of stitched lines (c258v L14 to c259v L21); pass 2 from the original half-crops in reverse line order, written to a separate file without re-printing pass 1. Limits: both passes were made by the same reader in one session, so pass 1 was not truly out of mind (c259v L21 and L20 were re-read directly after their pass-1 entries). The two passes were diffed by script: 19 lines differed, almost all in primary/alternative order or a hedge; count differences were c259v L3 (the three opening signs), L7 and L9 (raised hook), L15 (two N), L16 (pale dashes), c258v L20 (one tick). Third looks on fresh zooms were made for c258v L16, L19, L20, L23, c259v L3, L5 end, L7, L9, L13, L14, L15, L16, L17, L18, L21; the remaining swaps (e.g. c259v L8 Q/9, L11 6/pb, L19 9/PHI, L5 Z/4) were settled from the pass views only and keep both labels.
- The Z / 5 / pb hedges (about 20 signs) were not re-checked side by side against exemplar cut-outs; the crib for them comes from 112r lines 13, 14, 17.
- Block boundaries were checked against blocks.md on the crops; all agree except c259v L3, where the three opening signs sit at x~400-445 against a stated start of x~420 (see L3).

## Hardest distinctions

- Z vs 5 vs pb: Z = open hook with top bar; 5 = closed bowl with crossed stem; pb = bowl with bar at the very top of the ascender. Many Z in c259v have the stem poking through the bar; I kept Z and gave Zx or 5 only where the form was marked.
- 3 vs X3: tails vary continuously; 3 first, X3 alt where the tail is long.
- 9 / 4 / Q: by crossbar (4) and looped or crossed descender (Q).
- N: often a bare dash or arc with one dot or none; all dash-like strokes on the line were read N unless clearly an oo underline.
