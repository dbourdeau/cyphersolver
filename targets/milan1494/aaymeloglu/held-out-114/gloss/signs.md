# Glossed non-alphabetic signs, ASF MAP LXXIV doc. 114

Raw-pixel coordinates in raw/c258v.jpg and raw/c259v.jpg (1272 px wide). x is the centre of the cipher sign, good to about +/-10 px; y is the cipher baseline region of that line.

**How sign and gloss were paired.** The gloss is NOT written over the sign it explains. It is a compact run of small cursive that starts near the left end of the cipher on each line and stops (usually with a horizontal rule) well before the cipher line ends, because gloss letters are about half as wide as cipher signs. Pairing is therefore by sequence: counting gloss letters against cipher signs from the nearest spelled-out words on both sides. Each row has a confidence for that pairing. The key file and the transcription files in the parent folder were not opened.

## 3. The Re and Duca signs compared (c259v L3-4)

Crops: sign_re_c259v_L03.png, sign_duca_c259v_L03.png, sign_duca_c259v_L04.png, and context_c259v_L03-04_re_duca.png (both lines, 4x).

- They are **different signs built on the same base**. The base is the "2 with a tail to the right". The tail length varies freely (Re has a short tail on L3 and a long one on L16 and L20), so tail length does not separate them.
- What separates them is the **raised letter above the tail**: Re has a raised **c** (5 occurrences: L3, L8, L12, L16, L20; flat-topped on L3 and L12, plain c on L8 and L20, small and faint on L16). Duca on L3 has a raised **3**.
- The second Duca (L4) has the same wavy 2 with long tail, but its raised sign looks like an **h** rather than a 3. Two readings are possible and the image cannot decide between them: (a) it is a raised 3 whose top is joined by an unrelated stroke from the gloss word "scusa" directly above, so the sign is the same as on L3; (b) it is a genuinely different raised letter (h or b). The gloss treats both as the Duke ("Duca", "D."). Treat L3 = L4 as probable, not certain.
- The plain base sign, with no raised letter, is an ordinary letter-sign: it is the **b** of "bona" (c259v L13 x~190, L17 x~255), of "buono/buona" (c259v L13, L20; c258v L21, L23) and of "Isabella" (L17). Crops: sign_b_bona_c259v_L13.png, sign_b_bona_c259v_L14.png, sign_b_bona_c259v_L17.png. On L14 (x~250) a loop appears above this sign, but it is the descender of the g of the gloss word "patrigno" on the line above, not a raised cipher letter.
- A 9-shaped sign with a long descender stands next to the Re/Duca sign three times (after Duca on L3, before Duca on L4, after Re on L16) and has no gloss equivalent. It is absent beside Re on L3, L8, L12 and L20.
