# Image: rot_85_f67.jpg (3292x2890). Crops in crops_C/ (sips, 3x; doubtful signs 5-6x).
# Labels = shape names from sign_legend.md; @C.. = shapes absent from the legend (see end).
# "?" = doubtful sign. Brackets = notes, not signs. No decoding attempted.
# Conventions used throughout:
#  - b6 = T-bar over a 6/b (could also be read "ss"; one shape, labelled b6 everywhere).
#  - rb = b/5-like sign whose bowl ends in a hook to the lower left (often with a descender); rb? = plainer b-form.
#  - oc = circle with a bar to its right; here the bar usually ends in a short downward hook (o-ᒣ).
#  - dd = ⊣/+ cross with dots above and below the left arm (not the legend's upper-angle tt form); always marked dd?.
#  - o3 includes the leaning gamma with a tick written with a lead-in stroke (~ɣ).
#  - VEN = two circles on a bar (o-o-); CHE = three circles on a bar; g = bar-o-bar.
#  - COSIMO = any frame (Π / ⊓) with an inner I, several variants.
#  - IN = short comb (2-3 prongs) standing on a bar.
# Totals: 12 runs, 464 signs, 45 marked doubtful, 10 new shapes (@C01-@C10).

## New shapes
@C01: `<` (left-pointing angle) with a small closed loop at the end of each arm and a dot between the arms (`<̈` with looped ends). Frequent (10x).
@C02: small v/r-like hook joined by a horizontal bar to a tall vertical stem on its right (`ᵛ⊣`, "r|").
@C03: large angular meander: top bar running right then down, left stem down, bottom bar right then up (square S/Z, `⊐` over `⊏`). Once (L3).
@C04: backslash with a dot at upper right and one at lower left (`·\·`, mirror image of the legend's u). L6, L10, L17, L21; always right after s (⊢) or i/8.
@C05: upright triangle/A whose base or crossbar is prolonged as a long bar to the right (`A—`).
@C06: reversed c (`ↄ`), a single open curve opening to the left.
@C07: π-shape: a horizontal bar with two legs hanging from it (comb under a bar; could be IN inverted).
@C08: circle and small loop joined by a long horizontal bar, with a short upright stem rising from the middle of the bar and a dot below it (L10 only).
@C09: loop on top with two legs below that cross or spread (8 open at the bottom, `ꝭ`-like); L11, L15, L20(?), L21.
@C10: diamond with a dot inside, its top corner carrying a bar to the right (L21 only).
