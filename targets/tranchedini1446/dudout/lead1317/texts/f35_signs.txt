## New shapes
@N01: '<' whose two arm-ends close in small loops, with one or two dots between the arms (= B @B01 = C @C01)
@N02: lead-in minims 'ui' + tall loop (δ-like) with a dot and a long descender crossing down-left; L1, L6 (= B @B03 = C m8?)
@N03: circle on a straight stem going down from its bottom (ϙ), distinct from the 9 'l' (= B @B04 = C l)
@N04: stem rising on the right, bar to the left from its foot, the left end hooking down (⅃-like); L2, L15, both times before iI (= B @B11 = C re?)
@N05: backslash with a dot upper right and a dot lower left (·\·, mirror of u) (= B @B15 = C @C04)
@N06: small r-hook joined by a bar to a tall stem on its right (r⊣) (= B @B07 = C @C02)
@N07: large square S / meander spanning the line height; L3 (= B @B12 = C @C03)
@N08: circle - bar - stem going down below the line, the bar overshooting slightly as a ⊤ (on L4, L10 the stem rises above the bar with a short top bar to the right); L1, L4, L8, L10, L22 (= B @B02 + @B13 = C oc); distinct from plain oc (L14, L15)
@N09: loop on top with two legs below that cross or spread (8 open at the bottom); L11, L15, L20 (?), L21 (= C @C09 = B li?/i/o3)
@N10: bar then circle (-o), no right bar; L6 (= B @B16 = C g?)
@N11: heart: two loops forming the top, V below with a short tail, no apex; L2, L6, L12; possibly a variant of ma (= B @B06 = C ma)
@N12: reversed c (open to the left) with a bar to its right (ɔ-); L8 (= B @B17 = C oc?)
@N13: triangle with its base/side prolonged as a bar to the right (∆-); L9, L15, L17, L19 (= B @B18 = C @C05)
@N14: reversed c alone (ɔ); L9, L14 (= B @B19 = C @C06)
@N15: circle - long bar - loop with a short stem up and a dot below; L10 (= B @B20 = C @C08)
@N16: π: a bar with two straight legs; L10, L17 (= B @B22 = C @C07)
@N17: dumbbell: small loop, straight waist, larger loop, no stem through; L12 (= B @B24 = C lo?); compare i (8) and lo
@N18: composite on L17 (5th sign): ss-like b under a long top bar, crossed by a diagonal from upper left to lower right ending in a curl, with a ⊥-like stroke on its right; one sign width, probably a correction; could be 2 signs (ss + r) (= B @B27? = C b6? + r?)
@N19: diamond with a dot inside, its top corner carrying a bar to the right; L21 (= B @B35 = C @C10)
