## New shapes
@N01: a small circle on a straight stem whose foot ends in a horizontal bar (lolli standing on a foot bar, ♀ with ⊥ foot, no cross-bar); L1 (first sign), L20 (3rd sign).
@N02: a standing 8 (two stacked loops) with a bar running to the RIGHT from its lower loop (8—); compare ce (lying oo-) and i; L5b.
@N03: a C/ς-shaped bowl on the LEFT joined to a tall straight stem on its right; the stem rises above the bowl and runs down below the line (like q with an ascender, "Ϥ"). Not the small r-hook of rstem, not the curved tail of l, bowl bigger than h. L5b (1), L6 (1), L20 (2 + 1 damaged '?'), L23 (1).
@N04: three joined loops (ooo) followed by a bar that crosses a tall stem going down below the line (ooo-+ / ooo-⊤); could also be read as two signs (three loops + e) — the bar looks continuous; L6 (5th sign).
@N05: a CLOSED inverted triangle ∇ (top side drawn) with a dot inside and a short stem rising from the middle of its top side; compare ta (open V with dot); L11 (alone between clear words; a gloss "el S. de [R]agona" above it).
@N06: a tilted 8 (bigger loop lower-left, smaller loop upper-right) crossed by two parallel diagonal strokes from upper-left to lower-right (&/ℵ-like); maybe a variant of oo+; L20 (first sign).
@N07: reversed c ɔ (open to the left) with a dot inside; L22.
@N08: a bar running into a square bracket ⊏ (vertical with short bars to the right at top and bottom, open to the right): -⊏ ; L22.
@N09: a c/ς-curve open to the RIGHT with a dot inside, its lower end curling down and back to the left (ς with dot); L23.
@N10: three small pointed 'a'-shaped loops (like the clear letter a) standing on ONE baseline bar (CHE-like but the loops are a-shaped, pointed at the top); L24, immediately followed by an ordinary CHE (round loops).

## Notes
- Margin mark (NOT counted): L1, left margin — a bar with two loops crossed by a stem (-oo- with + , oo+-like). No other margin marks on L1-L24.
- L1: RCAP? = a lead-in bar followed by a cursive R-like shape (loop + leg); gloss "Cosmo" above the COSIMO frame.
- Glosses (later hand) seen above: L1 (one word), L5b (many: above most of the line), L6 (many, e.g. above and between signs), L11 (above @N05). None on L20, L22-L24.
- Clear-hand line fillers/abbreviation tildes, NOT counted as cipher: "la J. S. ~." (L5), "qsta ~ Coita" (L5), "qsta —Coita" (L4), "qsta ~ S.ia" (L17).
- L5b: first sign si? = two stems joined by one bar that overshoots on both sides (‡‡/H-like), followed by a separate small circle (asm); maybe one sign "H-o".
- L5b: CHE? = a bar with three loops on it, the LEFT one filled/blotted.
- L5b: i? = an 8 whose top loop is overwritten by a gloss; bslash? = a backslash with only one clear dot (right side).
- L5b/L6/L23: rb / rb? = 5-like b sign; in L6 and L23 it has a dot at its lower left and the top is a short upright, not clearly hooked.
- L5b and L22: o3? = an arch running right and then down, crossing itself in a small loop at the bottom right (lying gamma).
- L5b pa = four loops in a diamond; L6 CLOVER? and L23 CLOVER = clusters of only THREE loops (two above, one below, no stem). L20 pa = four loops.
- L6: o2? (3rd sign) has two dots at its left (dotted o2); L20 and L23 also have o2 with one dot at the left (written o2).
- L6: o2? (8th sign) is followed by a heavy oblique stroke touching it (part of the sign, or the end of a gloss mark?).
- L6: lo? = an 8 hanging from a tall stem that rises from its top only (the stem does not visibly go through the lower loop); VEN? = o-o (two circles joined by a bar, no outer bars). The last sign of L6 (-o-o) is a clear VEN.
- L6: re? = a ∂-shape (small bowl at bottom left, stem rising and curving left at the top), no bar through it (not ET).
- L20: @N03? = sign partly covered by a paper repair/damage (only the c-curve top and a long descender visible).
- L22: tu? = λ-shape (Λ with the left leg longer), no dot; te? (near line end) = the same λ-shape with a dot between the legs.
- L22: e? = a raised cross standing above the left bar of the following g (a thin gloss pen-stroke curls into it); I count it as a separate sign.
- L22: ET has thin strokes across it and a small ~ under it (later marks?); i? (L22, L24) = 8 whose lower loop lies sideways.
- L23: fa3? = bar + three loops, the middle loop very small; X3? = three parallel bars crossed by one stem, all slanted ~45°, with two dots on the left; rstem? = a ʒ/3-shaped hook joined by a bar to a tall stem on its right.
- L24: "ha dicto a" — the isolated 'a' before @N10 looks like the clear letter (kept in the frame, not counted).
- L22 "ch la tua uera" and L23 "ptc" are copied as seen (abbreviations unexpanded).
