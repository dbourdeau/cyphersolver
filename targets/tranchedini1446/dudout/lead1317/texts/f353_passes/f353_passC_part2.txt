## New shapes
@N01: c open to the RIGHT (Є / ε-like) with a dot inside, sometimes a short inner bar; L29, L30.
@N02: cross whose two dots are both on the RIGHT side (one above, one below the right arm: +÷), mirror of tt; L29 (x~1930).
@N03: dotted o2: reversed c joined to a loop (ɔo) with a dot inside the ɔ; L29 (x2), L30, L31, L34, L35 (x2), L37, L38, L40 (x2).
@N04: bar - two joined loops - bar (-oo-), i.e. fa with a trailing bar / ce with a leading bar; L30 (x~3080).
@N05: three joined loops lying in a row (ooo) with NO bar (cf. fa3 which has a bar); L31 (x~2410), L40 (x~2130).
@N06: 4-shape (small loop on a stem) whose bar runs right through a cross and ends in a circle (4—+—o), written as one stroke; L34 (x~1250-1380). Could also be split CON/@N10 + e + baro.
@N07: three lying loops (ɑ ∂ o, the middle one with an ascender/stem rising from it), sometimes a short bar crossing that stem; L26 (with crossbar, x~1850), L34 (x~2730), L35, L36 (x3), L38, L39 (x2, one with crossbar), L40. Compare oo+ / CLOVER.
@N08: stem crossed by TWO horizontal bars at the top, ending below in a 6/б-like bowl or downward triangle (б with doubled top bar); L35 (x~860).
@N09: vertical stem passing through a single small circle (ϕ), no bar (cf. ro which has a bar, lo which has two loops); L35 (x~1310).
@N10: closed 4 / ϙ- : small loop on a stem with a bar to the right and NO dot (cf. n with dot, CON open 4); L27 (x~2470), L31 (x~1210).
@N11: lying Y: two curled arms opening to the LEFT and meeting in a bar that runs right (⊱-); L38 (first sign, x~180).
@N12: tall Π / open rectangle with a stem rising from the middle of its top bar, no inner stroke; L40 (x~1100).
@N13: A-shape whose crossbar is crossed by a vertical stroke with a small loop/dot at the crossing (A with #); L40 (x~1200).
@N14: short bold wavy horizontal stroke (~) standing alone between lo and @N03; L40 (x~2700). Doubtful: may be a stray stroke.

## Notes
- Line centres from the brief were usable; ink of each line sits ~0-60 px BELOW the given centre (crops taken y-50..y+75).
- L32, L33 and L41 are entirely clear (no cipher). L41: "fteze / e sempre me recomando ala .J.V.S. / Florentie .14. octubris .1447. ..." (first word read uncertainly).
- Margin marks (not counted): L34 left margin a looped sign with a dot (li-like, x~60-130); L39 left margin three vertical strokes (|||).
- L34 x~720: a plain slash "/" (no dots) between g and a, taken as a punctuation virgule and NOT counted; if it is a cipher sign, R106 has 16.
- Doubtful clear/cipher boundaries: L26 end, the "m"-shape with a long descender before "grandissima" taken as clear "in" (could be mplain); L39 same shape before the run taken as clear "in". L28 "tu re" just before "omne" and L30 "re asm" just before "volentieri" kept as cipher, but "re asm" might be clear "ho". L34 first sign of R107 (∂ with bar, ET?) directly after "famegho" might be the clear abbreviation of "de".
- L25 x~1415 ET?: faint/smudged ∂-with-bar, partly erased.
- L26 x~420 nshape?: a top bar over an N/ᴎ zigzag (different from the plain heavy N of L29 x~1450 and L34 x~2800).
- L26 x~2280 te?: Λ/λ fork with one dot between the legs and a second dot left outside.
- NON is used throughout for a Π with a doubled (rectangular) top bar and a short middle leg (L25 x3, L26, L40); COSIMO for an open frame with an I-beam inside (L26, L28, L31, L36, L40). At L26 x~760 the NON middle leg continues down into a zigzag (perhaps a descender from L27).
- e5 = ʕ-shaped curl with the top hooked left and a long tail down-left (L25 x~600, L27, L29, L35, L40); re = 5-shape with a straight top stem and a bowl/tail (many lines). rb and htall not used.
- hookl is used for the frequent "c-hook joined at its top to an upright stem that pokes above" (ς⊣), read as the ⅃+ form of the legend.
- L39 x~1330 fa?: bar + two loops + a blotted mass (possibly a third loop -> fa3).
- L40 x~140 and x~570 q?: ">"-like with a flat horizontal upper arm (7-shaped); L40 end rstem?: a ">" (not an r) joined by a bar to a tall stem.
- L29 x~1730 / L38 x~1650 / L40: "8" with a tick on the top loop given as m8; plain 8 as i.
- Glosses (thin later hand, ignored) seen above/among signs on L25, L26, L27, L28, L29, L30, L31, L35, L37, L38, L39, L40; a gloss stroke crosses @N07 on L39 and ni on L39.
- A library stamp overlaps the end of L40/L41 (x~2600-2900) without hiding signs.
