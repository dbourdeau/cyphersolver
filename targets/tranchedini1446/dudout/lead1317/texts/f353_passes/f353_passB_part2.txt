## Notes
- L25 begins directly with cipher (R101 may continue a run from L24).
- "8+" (CLOVER then a separate ⊣ cross with left arm) occurs in L25, L26, L28, L29, L40: transcribed as two signs CLOVER iI (trefoil = 3 loops, the cross-like stroke has no right arm, so iI).
- "a-a-a" (three cursive a's joined on a base line) in L25, L30, L36: labelled CHE? (three small loops on a bar); in L28/L29 the same sign is written as clear o-o-o on a bar (CHE, certain).
- L26: the "2" right after ss before "trasforire" is taken as the clear-hand "e" (the clear hand writes e/et as 2, e.g. L30 "2 fari"); if cipher it would be `a` (R101 = 49 signs).
