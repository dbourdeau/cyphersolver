## New shapes
@N01: a bar from the left into a circle pierced by a vertical stem, then a second (oblique) stem, then a circle ending a bar (-ϕ/-o; like VEN with stems through it); L25 (3rd sign).
@N02: a struck-through sign: a W/crown-like shape with a small loop, cancelled by two long oblique strokes; L25 (12th sign). Underlying shape unreadable.
@N03: a circle on a stem going down (lolli) joined by a horizontal bar to a second, free circle on its right (ϙ-o); L28.
@N04: two circles joined by one straight bar, with NO bar outside them (o-o; VEN has bars outside: -o-o-); L30, L37.
@N05: a long oblique stroke rising from lower left to upper right, ending at the top in a small closed loop (ſ / ρ-like, no circle at the foot); L32, L34 (both times right after a tt).
@N06: a loop at the foot of a stem that rises up-left (δ / ∂ shape), with a bar running out to the right from the loop (δ—). Not ET (no bar THROUGH a d); very frequent: L26 (2), L27, L30, L31, L32, L33, L34 (2), L35, L37, L38, L39, L40 (2).
@N08: a backslash with a small closed triangle at its upper (left) end and a dot beside it (Δ\·); L34 (after i). A fainter small λ-like stroke under the preceding 8 seems to be a later hand (not counted).
@N09: a stem passing through an 8 (as lo) with a horizontal bar at its top AND a horizontal foot bar (lo between two bars); L36.
@N10: an oblique stroke with a short bar on top (a slanted T / 7) with a dot on each side (·T·), cf. u ·/· but with a top bar; L36.
@N11: a short upright stem with a bar to the right ending in a small filled loop, from which a long oblique tail runs down-left (like nu but a stem instead of the circle; cf. @N05); L41.
(@N07 not used.)

- L24 'blot?': a heavily blotted sign (cross with filled loop, two vertical strokes) after ro.
- L25 @N02 is struck through (see New shapes).
- Doubtful/conventions: CHE used for 3-4 circles joined by a running bar (vs ooo = loops touching with no bar, L30/L30); 'NON' for П with a shorter inner leg; COSIMO for a frame with a stroke inside (L26 ΠI, L31 rectangle with inner ⊥ - doubtful); DEL? for bold triangles with an inner base line (L35 x2, L41); tribar for the 4-like triangle with a bar to the right; o3 for the small ɤ/γ with a tick (many); rb for the ƅ/ʒ/h-like hooked sign (many, includes ʒ forms).
- L38 last sign and L40 'stack3': tall 8 with three loops (L38 one doubtful, partly at the window edge).
- L41 last sign 'a?': a very small low 2/v-shape after the ribbon, could be a flourish ending the cipher text.
- L39 'Ap?': light cursive A with lead-in.
