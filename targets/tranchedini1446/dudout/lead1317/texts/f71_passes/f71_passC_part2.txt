- L24 (x~1650-1800) and L25 (x~1500-1600): a sign struck through / blotted with several strokes — labelled `blot?`, true shape unreadable.
- L24 first sign `m8?`: 8 with a lead-in stroke at the top left. L24 frame sign (x~610, and the same frame in L24, L27, L28, L30, L35, L37, L39, L40):
  top bar + inner bar under it + three legs (middle one short) — labelled NON (ПП); could be COSIMO. The open frame with an inner I on a foot
  (L26 x~1850, L31 x~2050) is labelled COSIMO.
- htall vs rb: the h-like sign with a tall left stem and a hump curling left at the bottom is written htall; rb only where the stem is short with
  a top flag (5/ƅ-shape). The boundary is uncertain (L30 x~1440 `rb?`, L30 x~2200 `htall?`, L40 `htall?`).
- CHE vs ooo: CHE where three circles are joined by a visible bar along their foot; ooo where loops touch directly (L22, L30). L25 last sign `CHE?`.
- fa vs fa3 counted loop by loop; L22 x~1250 `fa3?` (third loop may be the bar end). L27 x~1500 `ce?`: lead-in hook + 2-3 loops + bar.
- L31 x~2790 / L34 x~2850: small marks under the 8 (tiny "x", "^") look like later interlinear marks: not counted.
- L35: bold plain triangle = do; triangle with inner base line = DEL (L35, L41 x2); L38 A with crossbar on a foot bar = `DEL?`.
- L36 x~1160 `pe?`: cluster of 3-4 loops with a stray dot to its right (dot not counted). L36 x~3260 `t?`: slanted stem with short top bar and dots both sides.
- L41 x~1350 `q?`: 7-shape with a heavy ink blob at its upper right. L41 ends with a small "v" flourish after the ribbon: line-end flourish, not counted.
- L40 x~2080 and L41 x~1900: a (2) with a long foot running right (one sign each).
