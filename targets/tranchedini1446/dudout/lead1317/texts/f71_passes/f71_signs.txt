## Notes
- The page is ONE cipher run, L1-L41, between "~ Post dat~" (left of L1) and the clear date line L42. Part 1 and part 2 were arbitrated separately; this file only makes them consistent.
- Cross-half conventions decided on the image (montages in arb/crops_merge/), and why:
  1. δ— (small closed loop on the line, ascender rising up-left from its right side, a bar running RIGHT from the foot of the bowl): part 1 = ET (16x), part 2 = @N02 (17x). Side by side (m_ET: L1, L5, L21 vs L26, L31, L32) the shapes are identical. Decision: ET everywhere (33x). Why: the legend's ET is the only '∂ with a bar' sign; no ∂ with a bar crossing the ascender occurs anywhere on the page, so this is the hand's form of that sign, with the bar starting at the bowl instead of crossing it; BRIEF-arb says a pass's @N that is a legend shape gets the legend label. It is clearly different from tribar (pointed ∆ with bar, '4' form), which both halves keep. Part 2 changed: 17 signs.
  2. Small gamma ɤ vs o3: part 1 split @N01 (small, leaning, tick top-left, loop at mid-height, tail below) from o3 (large symmetric Ɣ, loop at the foot); part 2 labelled both o3. Montages m_o3_A/B/C (all 20 part-2 o3 next to the part-1 references of L1) show 17 small ɤ and 3 large Ɣ. Decision: keep the part-1 split (the two forms are visibly different and both occur in the same lines, e.g. L1, L28): 17 part-2 o3 -> @N01; o3 kept at L28 2nd ('rc o3 co') and L32 (x2).
  3. Loops on a plain stem: part-2 @N01 (two/three loops in a row with ONE stem going DOWN from under them) is the same shape as part-1 'de' (e.g. L1 after @N01, L5 'CON de s'; z_L05_de_N05 vs z_L29_N01). The legend de 'qpo' = loops whose shared stem goes down between them, which fits. Decision: de everywhere; part 2 changed 12x.
  4. Part-1 @N05 (o∂o: loops in a row with ONE stem RISING from the middle loop) is the same shape as part-2 'da' (z_L05_de_N05 vs z_L29_da; also L28). Legend da = 'ab', ∂o∂ (ascender), the mirror of de. Decision: da everywhere; part 1 changed 8x.
  5. б with bar right: part-1 @N03 (Ƃ—, bar attached to the bowl) vs part 2 'ss' for all б. Montage m_ss/m_ss3 of the 17 part-2 ss: 9 have the attached right bar (L22, L26 x2, L30 x2, L33 1st, L38 2nd, L39, L41) -> @N03; 8 are plain б (L24, L25, L33 2nd, L35, L36, L37, L38 1st, L40) -> ss kept (in L24 and L33 2nd the bar that follows is the separate first stroke of the next sign ro, with a gap).
  6. Small round/rhomboid loop pierced by a stem above and below: part 1 @N04 (6x) vs part 2 diamstem; montage m_phi (L20 x2 vs L22 x2, L25, L28, L32) shows the same sign, in the same contexts ('X a u e'). Decision: diamstem everywhere (legend: plain ◇ pierced by a stem above and below, no inner loops; at this size the hand's ◇ is a small rounded rhomb, several show the pointed corners). Part 1 changed 6x.
  7. o—o (two loops joined by one straight bar, no outer bars, no stem): part 1 @N14 (L17, L20) vs part 2 dumb (L30, L37); windows w17/w20/w30/w37 show the same sign. Decision: dumb everywhere (legend dumb = loop, straight waist, loop, 'lying or standing'); part 1 changed 2x. The standing dumb of part 1 (L1, L7) stays dumb.
  8. ρ after tt: part-1 @N02 = part-2 @N06 (z_L01_ttN02b vs z_L32_ttN06: identical stroke and identical context 'ribbon tt X e'); merged as @N02, still counted as a separate sign from tt in both halves.
  9. ꝗ-o: part-1 @N10 (L11) = part-2 @N05 (L28) (m_N10, same shape and same context 'bslash ro X'); merged as @N08.
  Checked and kept apart: part-1 @N16 (L21, bar at the FOOT of a tall stem, open loop) vs part-2 @N11 (L41, bar at mid-stem, filled blob) -> @N13 / @N22. No other @N of one half matched a legend label or an @N of the other half.
