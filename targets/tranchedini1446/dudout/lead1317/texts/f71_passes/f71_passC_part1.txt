- Conventions I applied (state them so the arbiter can map): ET = loop with an ascender curving up-left + a bar running right from the bowl (bar not 'through'; frequent); da? = two or three joined loops with an ascender rising from the middle (d-o-d), marked ? each time; re = open c-hook OR closed 9-bowl joined by a short bar to a tall stem (4 open at top / q-like); rb = stem on top coming into a z/5-bowl open to the left, NO descender; ri = the same with a descender going on below the bowl (h-like); n0 = closed 4 (triangular loop left of stem, bar right, stem down), no dot; n = circle with bar right and stem down from the circle, one dot under the bar; to = inverted A, including a variant whose crossbar is low and the left arm thicker (L12, L20); i = 8, also when the lower loop is triangular / tilted (L8, L11, L13, L18, L21); i2? once in L14 (top loop open).
