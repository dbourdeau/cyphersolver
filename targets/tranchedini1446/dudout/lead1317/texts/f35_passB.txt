## New shapes
@B01: '<' with small loops at both arm ends, usually with a dot (or two) to the right
@B02: circle - bar - stem down (o⊤)
@B03: minims 'ui' + tall loop with a dot (δ-like); could be clear text, kept as a sign
@B04: circle on a descending stem
@B06: two loops over a V (heart shape)
@B07: r-hook joined to a tall stem (r⊣)
@B10: ⊢ without flag (stem with a bar to the right, no flag)
@B11: ⊥ whose left arm hooks down
@B12: large squared S spanning the line height (L3)
@B13: circle - bar - Γ (stem up with top bar right, plus stem down)
@B14: ⊔ (U) on a central stem (Ч-like)
@B15: backslash with dots ·\·
@B16: bar then circle (-o)
@B17: ɔ with a bar to the right
@B18: triangle with a bar extending to the right (4-like / ∆-)
@B19: ɔ alone
@B20: circle - bar - loop with small stem up and a dot below
@B22: π (bar with two legs)
@B24: dumbbell (small loop, stem, larger loop)
@B26: ⊤ whose left arm hooks up
@B27: composite: ⊤ + diagonal + cross enclosing a small triangle; possibly a correction (L17)
@B29: ∃-like: top bar running right into a stem down, long middle crossbar through the stem (mirror-like of uu) (L19)
@B34: two stems each topped by a small loop, joined by a bottom bar (⊔ with looped tips) (L21 x2)
@B35: diamond with a dot inside and a bar going right from its top (L21)
@B36: circle - bar - circle - bar, joined (o-o-); might be two oc (L21)
Other non-legend variants written with '?': n? (stem with loop, two dots and bar right: L20/L21, best match legend n), i? (three stacked loops, L21),
 ma? (diamond outline enclosing two loops, L20), ni? (loop with ascending stem plus superscript zigzag and long hairline, end of L20),
 VEN?/COSIMO? (variant forms of those shapes), b6d? (ss-like with dots), lo? (stem through loops, unclear).
