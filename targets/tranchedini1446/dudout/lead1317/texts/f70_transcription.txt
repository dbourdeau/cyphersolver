## Notes
- L1 {fyhola?}: letters f-y-h-o-l-a fairly clear (sense unclear; possibly a variant of a word meaning "letter/note"); "de quista lra" written as one block "dequistalra" with abbreviation stroke over lra (= lettera, left abbreviated).
- L2 "scaua"? first word read "scaua/scava" (left as "scaua"; could be the end of a cipher-word); "possati" vs "possan" (final abbreviation unclear).
- L3 {mo?}: "mo" with no visible stroke (mio?). "vida" clear (for vede/veda).
- L5 struck "de" at line end (repeated at start of L6 sense "piu de / fiate" — actually "fiate" follows directly; struck stroke may be a flourish). Decorated first Et with bar is also heavier here.
- L6 {quindicimilia?}: reads "quindicamilia"/"quindicimilia"; number word uncertain.
- L7 {contra?}: word looks like "uma/cima" with a long overline; "contra" by sense.
- L8 {desfaza?} {Comune?}: "de sfaza lo" then a word with big looped initial + "-iare/-icare"; not resolved.
- L9 {voglierlo?}: "ad voglierlo" or "ad sogliolo" – uncertain.
- L12: after "de" a single letter (l or S) struck through, then "o pfato" (prefato).
- L13: first word struck (fa/sa?); after "sta" a paper hole removes 1 short word (d.. visible) [—].
- L14 interlinear "de" over magiore and "costoro" (with caret) before "se demonstrano"; {indrieto?} reads "indruto/indreto"; {tristo?} reads "tesho/tristo".
- L17 {possettava?} reads "possttua(a)"; {pegio?} reads "pegio/peglio" (meglio?).
- L18 {fa?} "fr" with abbreviation stroke.
- L20 {aspectavano?}: "aspectava" + blotted ending; {poi?} reads "por".
- L21 interlinear small letter over "no" (a/e?); struck word before "vedo" blotted (Et?).
- L22 {se cura?} reads "seccura"/"se cura".
- L23 {deliberara?} "delibzaa" with stroke.
- L25 {ad e?} "ao e/" – opening of line unclear.
- L26: "parole" written above struck "prome" (= promesse?); after "uno" a short flourish/word not read [—].
- L27 {mo?} "ino/mo" with tilde; {tractarano?} "trotarano/tractarano" (an overline from the tilde runs across it; not taken as a deletion).
- L31 {via?}: word with large C-flourish + "dia" (Cdia) – maybe not "via".
