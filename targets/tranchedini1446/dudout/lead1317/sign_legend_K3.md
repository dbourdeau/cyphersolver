# Sign labels for the Tranchedini cipher (Nicodemo, 1446-1449) — legend K3 (shapes only, NO values)

A label is a NAME FOR A SHAPE. Nothing here tells what a sign means; do not try to decipher. Copy the label of the shape you see.
Lower-case labels are small signs (letters/syllables); UPPER-CASE labels are single signs that stand for a whole word.
If a shape is not in this legend, invent `@N01`, `@N02`, ... and describe it under `## New shapes`. Mark a doubtful sign with `?`
after its label (e.g. `fa?`). A sign that is struck through or smudged: give the label you see + `?`, and say so in a note.

## 2. Word signs (one sign = one word)

| label | shape |
|---|---|
| CHE | three or four small circles on one bar |
| ET | `∂` / d with a bar through it |
| NON | double comb (П with a short inner bar, ПП) |
| CON | 4-shaped sign (open 4: a U on a central stem) |
| GDA | a 7-stroke joined to an N/ᴎ zigzag under a long top bar (one ligature), or Ϻ |
| IN | Ш with THREE prongs on a bar, a stem below (comb on a bar) — compare zz (two prongs) |
| COSIMO | a frame with an I inside (ΓIΓ, open or closed rectangle with an inner stroke) |
| FIOR | inverted triangle / V with a small circle on EACH top corner |
| SFZ | U whose two arms end in small circles or loops |
| VEN | bar-o-bar-o `-o-0-` (two circles on a bar) |
| COLL | a stem with three bars across it (to the left), under a long bar |
| oo+ | two or three loops in a row FOLLOWED by a cross on their right (ooo+), the cross standing on the line |
| FED | a small cross standing ON TOP of two or three loops in a row (the stem of the cross touches the loops from above) |
| MALA | two loops (ꝏ) on top of a cross (like ♀ with a double loop) |
| X3 | three bars crossed by a stem `≡+` (Ǝ with a stem) |
| MIL | cross followed by an I with bars (`+I`) — PREFER writing it as two signs `e sI` |
| LTL | `<` (left-pointing angle) whose two arm-ends close in small loops or circles, with one or two dots between the arms |
| MINL | cursive minims `ui`/`m` + a tall loop with a dot and a long descender crossing down-left |
| MEANDER | large square S / meander (┘┌) spanning the line height |
| OBLOOP | circle - long bar - a loop with a short stem up and a dot below |
| DIAMDOT | diamond with a dot inside, a bar running right from its top corner |
| RCAP | a capital R written like the R of the clear hand |
| UDOT | U with a dot inside, its left arm hooking to the left, its right arm running on as a long top bar |
| CLOVER | a trefoil (three loops) standing ON A CROSS: a stem going down from the lowest loop with a crossbar on it (♣ on +). Without the cross it is pe |
| DEL | (see §1: bold triangle with inner triangle) |

## 3. Conventions settled from earlier disagreements (f. 28, f. 30, f. 35)
- Count loops: `fa` (bar + 2 loops) vs `fa3` (bar + 3 loops); `pe` (trefoil / ꝏ, nothing under) vs `CLOVER` (trefoil ON A CROSS);
  `i` (8) vs `stack3` (3 stacked loops) vs `dumb` (dumbbell) vs `lo` (stem through the 8).
- Count prongs: `zz` (2 prongs on a bar) vs `IN` (3 prongs, Ш).
- Dots: `n` (dot below the bar) vs `ne` (dots above and below); `li` (dot under the loop) vs `ribbon` (no dot); `tt` (both dots left)
  vs `dd` (dots on both sides); `te` (Λ with dot) vs `tu` (no dot); `ta` (V with dot) vs `pi` (V with hook/stroke); `u` `·/·` vs `bslash` `·\·`.
- Circle + bar family: `oc` (o-) / `otee` (o- with a stem DOWN) / `baro` (-o) / `g` (-o-) / `VEN` (-o-o-) / `rc` (ɔ) / `rcb` (ɔ-).
- Triangles: `do` (plain outline) / `DEL` (bold with inner triangle) / `tribar` (∆ with bar right) / `mi` (-▷) / `tri` (▷).
- `ma` (diamond WITH apex spike) vs `heart` (no apex spike) vs `diamstem` (plain ◇ on a stem).
- Stems: `s` ⊢ (bar right) / `iI` ⊣ (bar left) / `rstem` (r-hook + stem) / `se` (r + 9) / `hookl` (⅃ alone) / `cbar` (ȼ: c crossed at the top; `cbar` + ⅃ = two signs) / `re` (ς-hook + tall stem) / `rb` (ƅ, stem on top of a left-open bowl) / `Z7`.
- Loops + cross: `oo+` (cross AFTER the loops) / `FED` (cross ON TOP of the loops) / `MALA` (loops on top of a cross) / `CLOVER` (trefoil on a cross).
- Circle on stem: `lolli` (no foot) / `SIGNORE` (stem stands on a foot bar).
- A-forms: `Abold` (plain A) vs `Ap` (cursive A with tail); `to` = ∀ (upside down).
- b-forms: `ss` (б, long top bar) / `co` (+ dot left of stem) / `b6d` (bar + dots) / `ff` (plain 6) / `rb` (ƅ, top hooked left) / `ri` (ħ).
- A sign written as one ligature counts as ONE sign; two separate signs touching count as two.
