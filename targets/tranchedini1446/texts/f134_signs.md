# f. 134 (R7905 P1): cipher runs sign by sign, with the contemporary glosses (5 Nov 2026, Claude Sonnet 5.5)

Crops `crops/s134_*.png` (2.6x-6x, git-ignored). Signs named after Domnina Fig. 5 / Lasry's table where the shape matches; `?` = not sure.
The gloss is a small hand between the lines; its words sit over spans of signs, not over single signs, and my span marks are approximate.
