## English gist
Starhemberg asks Riva (in London) for news of the South Sea affair (the directors' payment of 7 millions said to be deferred), and whether an opera company will be formed this year and what it would pay an excellent singer. In cipher: Borosini is in Vienna, an excellent singer "who sings tenor and has no equal in all Europe"; he would like to join the company, but he must be asked for expressly in the name of the King, or at least of the company, and then leave would not be in doubt. A letter of request sent to Starhemberg would do, so that he can put the proposal in Vienna; he will gladly serve "the tenor Borosini". Then: a certain Mr Colman has lately arrived to serve under St Saphorin; Starhemberg suspects he is the son of "Hoffman", who bears almost the same name, and wants a straight answer, promising no harm to Hoffman. Last, he wants all the talk of court and nation in London about the conduct of the Vienna court concerning Mr Knight, and whether both are content.
