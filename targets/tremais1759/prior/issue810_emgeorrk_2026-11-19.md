### Reading and English translation

On f. 346r Trémais describes widespread abuses that an official failed to stop and argues against holding him alone responsible for the colony’s soaring expenditure. The context points to the intendant François Bigot. The recovered passage itself uses a pronoun, and some intervening words remain open.

| Folio and lines | Groups | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 346r, 7 | `440 241 219 227 108 197 231 262 156` | *mon instruction* |
| 346r, 21–22 | `298 385 362/262 8 407 246 240 681`, then `8 176 80 275 466` | *résulté une infinité d’abus* |
| 346r, 22 | `309 298 8 15 247` | *arrêté le cours* |
| 346r, 26–28 | After clear *ni d’avoir seul donné*, see the aligned lines below | *lieu à la prodigieuse augmentation des dépenses de cette colonie* |
| 346v, 22 | `227 108 100 8 162` | *traitement* |
| 347v, 3 | `92 497 74 219` | *mépris* |

On f. 346r, ll. 21–22 the cipher follows clear *d’où il est* and mixes with clear *dont* and *n’a point*. The result is “… from which arose innumerable abuses, which he failed to stop.” Later the passage says “… nor [can he be accused] of having caused, by himself, the enormous increase in expenditure in this colony.” The final *s* in *dépenses* is supplied by context.

The 49 high-confidence rows below contain 296 group positions, about 22% of 1,327 transcribed cipher slots. This counts the positions in the checked fragments, including isolated words and positions with uncertain digit shapes. The fraction of words read remains undetermined. The longest gaps are on ff. 346v–347r, and the binding hides parts of several lines. A bare gap mark has unknown extent.

### Key and method

The September ciphertext was aligned with its contemporary clear copy. The resulting values were tested on November, then extended using context and recurring groups. September *augmentation* anchors `262 156` (*ti-on*), which recurs in November *instruction* and *administration*. September *mérité*, *place* and *dépositaires* supply all four groups of the November control *mépris*. The working key includes possible alternatives and entries inferred from November. Null values remain unconfirmed.

Brice Dudout had already identified the September clear copy and reported a partial key test on held-out September material. [Prior contribution #273](https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/pull/273)
