Although I have reason to expect that you will have received the letter I had the honour to write to you by M. de Joannes,
aide-major of the Languedoc regiment, I have nevertheless thought it my duty to take the liberty of sending you a copy herewith.
Everything in it, My Lord, shows the haste with which I was obliged to write it; but on comparing it since with what **my
instruction** prescribes, it seemed to me that, being unable for the present **to procure you clarifications** such **as you
desire them**, I had nothing to change in the general account it contains. I shall only add that whatever difficulties I may
meet *[in?]* **my inquiries**, I shall nevertheless push them as far as my feeble lights can lead them, and I dare assure you
that I shall omit none of the means that **decency** will allow me to use to bring **the abuses to light**. But, My Lord, your
love of truth and the confidence with which you honour me seeming to lay on me the law of concealing nothing from you, I believe
I must not hesitate to take the liberty of repeating to you,

That *[however?]* **reprehensible M. Bigot may be**, whether for having given, by carrying on **an immense trade, a pernicious
example** from which **an infinity of abuses resulted** whose **course he** did not **stop**, or for **the** other **faults**
with which one may **reproach his administration**, one could not nevertheless without **injustice accuse him** of having
**alone** caused all the **ills that have afflicted Canada**, nor of having alone given **rise to the prodigious increase** of
the **expenses** of this **colony**: the **disorders** have had many other **sources**. One need only examine the substance of
things with some attention to be convinced of it: the **memoirs** without number that have come out of **this country** all
*[breathe?]* the **passion that dictated them**. You know better than I, My Lord, that *[interest?]*, which unites **men**,

*[crime?]* in **others**. Allow me, My Lord, to enter here into some detail on this, without claiming **to justify anyone**.
