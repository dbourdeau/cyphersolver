# Grasse and Bouillé 1779: an enciphered war plan for the defence of the French West Indies

Status: read (Brice Dudout, 28 Oct 2026; fol. 196, Bouillé's code, text and tables: 1,252 of 1,266 words read in their sense, 1,194 certain, 12 probable, 46 restored, 14 unresolved; fol. 191, cipher of 1777, text and tables: 1299 of 1326 words read in their sense, 1243 certain, 36 probable, 20 restored, 27 unresolved; Daniel Bourdeau's 95% rule).

Contributed by Brice Dudout (Europe chiffrée), 28 Oct 2026. Not in the catalogue. Write-up prepared for https://bdudout.github.io/europe-chiffree/en/documents.html#grasse-bouille-1779 (published).

## Document

Archives nationales d'outre-mer, COL C8 A 76 fol. 196, images FRANOM23_C8A_076_0309-0317 (IREL record https://recherche-anom.culture.gouv.fr/ark:/61561/zn401ysttruo; images not included, no reuse licence found). Martinique, 4 August 1779, duplicate. The inventory calls it "Autre chiffre préparé par MM. de Bouillé et de Grasse"; it is in fact a memorandum entirely in cipher, with the enciphered title "Projets remis par M. le comte de Grasse et M. le marquis de Bouillé sur les moyens les plus sûrs de protéger les colonies françoises de l'Amérique et d'attaquer celles des Anglois", signed in autograph "Le comte de Grasse" (image 0313). Images 0314-0317 are the enciphered tables announced in the text (artillery 142,944 livres; park and ammunition, total 1,045,994 livres; landing troops 2,600; squadron 9,220 men); the sums of 0314, 0316 and 0317 check, 0315 is 2,000 livres short of its carry-over (probably one misread value). At the foot of 0317, in clear, in Bouillé's hand: "le 4 août 1779 Bouillé" and "M. de Grasse n'a pu signer ce mémoire, étant absent". No gloss and no contemporary decipherment.

## Cipher and key

Bouillé's numerical code ("chiffre du 25 avril 1778", about 850 elements, homophones, nulls 163, 586, 257), the same as in targets/bouille1778 (PR #171). Key K6 of that folder (490 values from glossed letters of 1779-1780) was extended by aligning three contemporary cipher/plain pairs from ANOM C8 A 79-81: the return of stores of 1 Oct 1780 (fols. 3/5), the garrison return of 4 Apr 1782 (fols. 212-213) and letter no. 624 of 4 Mar 1782 (fols. 55-56). New values were taken only where both neighbouring groups aligned with cost <= 1. Result K7, 509 values, frozen (SHA-256 23248c6cd6a5fc3661ba8821546158736ee94396a6b971cc6c21f455398699aa). `key.tsv` is K8 = K7 + the held-out pair, added after validation.

## Validation

Protocol written before any computation; held-out pair fixed in advance: Bouillé's "Mémoire sur la campagne prochaine" (Martinique, 14 Sept 1781, C8 A 80 fols. 113-115), whose cipher original was transcribed and frozen before the contemporary "Déchiffrement" was opened. 485 groups; frozen K7 gives 0.825 of words and 0.889 of characters against the Déchiffrement (passages left in clear in the original removed from both sides); 200 permuted control keys (seed 20261028) reach at most 0.149 and 0.089. Criterion fixed in advance (characters >= 0.85, words >= 0.75, above the controls): passed.

## Reading

First published decipherment, to our knowledge. Daniel Bourdeau's rule (95% of words read in their sense): text 794 words (759 certain, 9 probable, 22 restored, 4 unresolved; 99.5%), tables 472 words (435 certain, 3 probable, 24 restored, 10 unresolved; 97.9%); together 1,266 words, 1,194 certain (94.3%), 12 probable (0.9%), 46 restored (3.6%), 14 unresolved (1.1%) -> 98.9%.

## Notes

- Content. A month after the capture of Grenada, Grasse and Bouillé propose a small mobile squadron (8 ships of the line, 4 frigates, 2 corvettes, 3 storeships, 2,500-2,600 men, artillery, six months' provisions) to leave France by the end of October, based at Martinique: it would guard the Windward Islands in the winter season and strike the British elsewhere (Leeward Islands, New England, Florida with St Augustine and Pensacola, the Newfoundland banks and fishery), serving the Spanish ally; Bouillé to command on land, Grasse at sea.

## The same plan in the "cipher of 1777": ANOM COL C8 A 76 fol. 191 (31 March 1779) — isologue

Added by Brice Dudout, 28 Oct 2026.

Fol. 191 (images FRANOM23_C8A_076_0300-0308; IREL https://recherche-anom.culture.gouv.fr/ark:/61561/zn401eyzzxyw; images not included, no reuse licence found) is the duplicate of 31 March 1779 of the same memorandum, in another code: the margin reads "Chiffre de 1777 — La Martinique" and "C'est le duplicata du projet de MM. de Grasse et de Bouillé". Fol. 196 (4 August 1779) is the same text re-enciphered in Bouillé's code. The two keys share no value, so the pair is an isologue: plain text identical apart from small edits ("Projet remis à la cour par" / "Projets remis par"; "des entreprises des Anglois" / "des entreprises angloises"). Fol. 191 also keeps the returns announced in the text: troops (2,600), siege and field artillery with weights in clear (sums 142,464; 669,094; 1,034,494 pounds, all exact), and the composition of the squadron (17 vessels, 9,330 men, numbers spelled out in cipher; A + B = C and C × ships = D check on every row).

Key ("cipher of 1777", code M, about 850 groups; 688 = de, 656 = le, 508 = s): K2, 267 values learned only from the minister's dispatches glossed at the time (C8 A 77 fol. 31, 20 March 1778, images 0049-0051; fol. 43, image 0072); K4 = K2 + 81 values from the numbers and sums of the tables of fol. 191, each attested at least twice; K6 = K4 + 56 values from aligning fol. 191 with the reading of fol. 196 (certain words only), and one correction (119 = "o"). 404 values, frozen before the test (SHA-256 f6b50eea74d91b08d45fb64ea1588da24f7e90be256674e5f8960c32ac15a1b2): key_codeM_K6.tsv.

Validation on a held-out text fixed in advance: last glossed page of the dispatch of 20 March 1778 (fol. 31, image 0052, 71 groups), never used for learning. K6: words 88.7%, characters 87.2%; 200 control keys (permutations, seed 20261028) at most 2.8% and 18.5%; criterion (both ≥ 85%, above controls) passed. Caveats: third test on the same page (K2 77.5/70.8 and K4 85.9/79.0 failed and are recorded); three values that make it pass (370 "mu", 788 "ac", 670 "circonstances") were already known to the analyst from the two earlier tests; they were admitted only because they align with certain words of fol. 196, but without them the words score would be about 84.5%, below the threshold. Four numbers of the test page (314, 837, 315, 678) are still missing from the table.

Files: f191_codeM_transcription.txt, f191_codeM_reading.txt ({x} probable, [x] restored by sense, ‹n› unresolved, one word per number), key_codeM_K6.tsv. Justification of every probable and restored word: see the French write-up (section "Le même projet en « chiffre de 1777 »").
