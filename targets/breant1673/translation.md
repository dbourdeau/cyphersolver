# Bréant to M. de Réveillon, Cologne, 26 December 1673 and 8 January 1674: English translation

Translation of `reading.md`. The ciphered passages are in italics; `{x}` marks the same probable words; "(…)" marks clear text not transcribed. "M. de Cologne" is the Elector of Cologne (Maximilian Henry of Bavaria); "our ambassadors" are the French plenipotentiaries at the Cologne peace congress; Kaiserswerth is the Rhine fortress of the Elector held by Réveillon.

## 26 December 1673

Our ambassadors need to know whether, with *the troops you have at Kaiserswerth*, you are *in a state to withstand an approach of the enemy, and whether you are* (…) *the Germans who are inside; it is not without* reason *that they ask you* this. They have asked me to write to you so as to know *exactly* what you *have, whether of French or of Swiss*, and whether with what you have you *can be master of the place*; please take the trouble to let me know, so that if *M. de Cologne {were} to turn {his coat}* (…) for it is important that we should always be *masters of his best places*. (…) *that he may be willing to listen to the proposals* that are *made to him on behalf of the Emperor*. I beg you that this remain between *M. de Chamilly and you* and me (…). Bréant.

P.S. If *by chance M. de Cologne came to terms with the enemy, you* would be *warned of it*, and I *beg you to make no stir about this; you will know everything that happens here*.

## 8 January 1674

I have received the letter you did me the honour of writing to me of the 30th of last month and the 2nd of this. I showed your letter of the 2nd to our Ambassadors, who have charged me to tell you *to be assured that you will be informed of everything that happens, and that they have the word of M. de Cologne that he will listen to no* proposals *on behalf of the Emperor until he has* an answer *to a Letter he has written to the King, which he will send* tomorrow *by a gentleman of his chamber*. So, Sir, *be at rest and be* persuaded *that you will not* be *caught for want of being warned, and that you will be warned* at once (…). Bréant.
