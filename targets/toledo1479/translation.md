Italics translate provisional clear handwriting. Question marks remain substantive;
unread words and code groups are preserved. Subject, tense, and antecedent are left
open where the Italian does not settle them. Editorial expansions are not extra
decoded signs. Consult reading.md and the literal decryption alongside this version.

This edition does not identify the sender or any coded person. Santiglana is kept

handwriting in lines 19–20, 28, and 33–35 prevents a continuous translation there.
