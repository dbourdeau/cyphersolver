## Notes on the count

- All six parts: 844 words, 26 holes, 818 read, 0.969. MS pp. 1-6 (parts 1-4): 587 words, 17 holes, 570 read,
  0.971. MS pp. 14-18 (parts 5 and 6): 257 words, 9 holes, 0.965. MS p. 5, the lowest page: 173 words, 8 holes,
  0.954. `python we028.py count` prints the parts and the pages. (Until the claims review of 24 Nov 2026 the
  figures were 23 holes and 0.973: [the], ind[u]strious and des{+cri}b[i]ng were then counted as read.)
- [than]: the last digit is heavily inked on the frame and was read 8 by two blind AI readers, 3 by two of the three
  transcribers; the clerk has "than". The doubt is not settled, and 1383 and 1388 are both whole words, so the word
  is counted as a hole. If it were read the total would be 819 of 844.
- [the] in "the correct principles" (p. 5, 114R.03, sixth group) is counted as a hole on the same ground as [than]:
  the third digit is blotted (1385 or 1345, the film check split), 1345 is *boa*, and both are whole words. Brice
  Dudout's transcription and the clerk have 1385 and "the"; the claims reviewer's look at frame 0114 (an AI model,
  not blind) leans 1345. If it were read the total would be 819 of 844. The collated transcription writes the group
  1385 without a query; the doubt is carried by the bracket in the reading.
- ind[u]strious and des{+cri}b[i]ng: the tick under 800 stands under the first 0 and the tick under 1359 under the 3
  (frame 0114, looked at in the claims review of 24 Nov 2026 by an AI model that knew the candidates). By the
  printed rule they withdraw u (dos) and i (brng); the words want o and r withdrawn. The letter is supplied from the
  word, so both are holes.
- "as I observe in a former letter" (p. 3) is kept as written: the group has no caret, and the clerk has "observe".
- The two words with no group ([under], [is]) are counted as words and as holes. "cannot" is one word for two
  groups; "M." is counted as a word each time (group 601).
- The 13 words listed under Spelling are counted as read, as in `../jqa` (suc-ces for success, il-ness for
  illness): every group is read from the key and the word is not in doubt. Eight of them have a letter in the
  ordinary spelling that no group gives (committed, beginning, especially, merely, epoch, liberality, overthrow,
  attributed). If those eight were counted as holes the six parts would come to 810 of 844 (0.960); if all 13,
  to 805 of 844 (0.954), with part 6 at 192 of 203 (0.946), MS p. 5 at 162 of 173 (0.936) and MS p. 18 at 85 of 91
  (0.934).
- If the four one-digit slips still counted as read were holes the total would be 814 of 844 (0.964) and MS p. 2,
  which has 35 words, would come to 33 of 35 (0.943).
- If both the 13 spellings and the four slips were holes the total would be 801 of 844 (0.949), MS pp. 1-6 557 of
  587 (0.949) and MS pp. 14-18 244 of 257 (0.949), under the 95% bar. The class therefore depends on counting
  those 17 words as read under the README rules for spellings and for one-digit slips.
- Every difference between the key output and this reading was listed by machine on 24 Nov 2026 (letters of the
  decryption against letters of the reading), and again by an independent AI reader the same day: each is a mark
  applied by the sheet's rules, one of the holes, one of the five slips or one of the 13 spellings.
- The clerk has the word, or a correction to it, at 21 of the 26 holes; not at th[at] ("the"), [under] (line end
  lost), Minister[s] ("Minister"), en[able] ("en") and [is]. At [ten]or he wrote "Ton" above "or", reading tone, so
  the bracketed letters there are not his.
