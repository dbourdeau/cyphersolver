JUDGE BRIEF: cpp10a132 (one decode, frozen key, no emendation)

You are given judge/decode.txt: the decoded letters of a cipher text, line by line, exactly as one mechanical pass
produced them. Language expected: Latin written by a north Italian hand about 1500-1550 (spellings such as "santam"
for sanctam, "paradixi", "misa", single for double consonants are period spelling, not errors, if the word is
recognisable). Words run across line ends (e.g. "sant / os").

Score EVERY decoded letter (a-z only) with one of:
  S = the letter stands in a word that reads as sense in the language
  E = the letter is a one-letter slip in an otherwise readable word (score the slip letter E, the rest of the word S)
  N = no sense
Do not score: clear numerals in { }, the scribe's points . and /, and the marks # (a drawn sign the key gives no value
for), _ (one illegible letter), ~ (text lost in a blot or the gutter). A word broken by # _ or ~ is scored on the
letters that are there: S if the word is still recognisable, N if not.
The single letters between points on p76r l.1 and l.3 (p e f e s / d n i x) are initials; judge whether they make
sense as initials in context and score them S or N accordingly.
Do not emend, do not re-divide beyond obvious line-end joins, do not consult any other reading.

Report: per line S/E/N counts; totals for each page (p75r, p76r, p76v); the HELD-OUT figure (p76v alone, the key was
built without it); and the full figure. Percentage read = S / (S+E+N). Our line is 65%.
