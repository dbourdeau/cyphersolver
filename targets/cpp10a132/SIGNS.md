# SIGNS: cpp10a132 (College of Physicians of Philadelphia, Ms. 10a 132, pp. 75, 76, 76 verso)

The cipher is written with ordinary letters of the scribe's own cursive hand, so the transcription uses the
letter each shape is, not invented labels. Only two drawn signs needed neutral labels (S01, S02).

Images: OPenn 6207_0112 (page "75", TEI surface 75r), 6207_0114 (page "76", TEI 76r), 6207_0115 (verso of 76, TEI 76v).
The book is paginated in ink ("75", "76"); OPenn's TEI treats the numbers as folios.

## Conventions in TRANSCRIPTION.tsv
- one row per manuscript line; tokens are the word groups as spaced by the scribe
- `x?`  the letter before `?` is a doubtful reading
- `[.]` one illegible letter; `[...]` illegible or lost (on p76v: line ends run into the gutter of the scan)
- `{ }` clear text (only numerals occur: 12, iiij, iij, xx.iiij, 10, xij?, iij)
- `.` and `/` are the scribe's own points and strokes
- capitals D (p75r l.1, p76r l.1) and N (p75r l.2) are enlarged forms of d and n

## Letter shapes
| label | shape |
|---|---|
| b | b with tall ascender |
| c | open c, flat top, no tongue |
| d | round-backed d (uncial), upright in D |
| e | c-shape closed by a tongue or loop; can be close to c (doubtful cases marked) |
| f | f with cross-bar, sits on the line |
| g | g with a large open loop below |
| h | h with the limb curling under the line |
| i | short i, dotted in places |
| k | k with tall ascender and a kicked leg |
| m | three minims, n two minims |
| o | small round o |
| p | short p, small bowl, short descender |
| q | q with straight descender |
| r | short r with a hook to the right, no descender |
| s | long s (always the long form; no round s seen) |
| t | t with a long cross-bar, often running into the next letter |
| u | u; written v-shaped once at a word start (p75r l.1 "uf") |
| x | x made as a cross whose down-stroke runs to a long tail curling left under the line (the commonest tailed shape) |
| y | small v-shape with a short tail to the left: p76r l.3 (standing alone between points) and l.8 |
| S01 | a diagonal stroke through the descender of q (p76r l.5, once): the usual scribal bar that makes "pro"/"par" from p |
| S02 | a 2-shaped stroke crossed by a looped diagonal (p76r l.5, once): the shape of the common Latin "-rum" abbreviation |

No letters a, l, z occur in the cipher text. Numerals are in clear, in Arabic (12, 10) and Roman forms.
