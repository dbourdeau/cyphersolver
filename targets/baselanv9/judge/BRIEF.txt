Clavicula Salomonis (conjurations, pentacles, planetary seals). Spellings such as "e" for "ae", single for double

instructions and "virtues"; H3 = two rim inscriptions of pentacles.

Score EVERY decoded letter (a-z only) with one of:
  S = the letter stands in a word that reads as sense in Latin

that are there: S if the word is still recognisable, N if not.

the Clavicula literature, or any other reading of this manuscript. Judge only from the decode.

Report: per line S/E/N counts; totals for H1, H2, H3; the total over all three; and the count of '?'.
