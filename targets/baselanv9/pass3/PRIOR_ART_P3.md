# Prior-art look for the rim texts (pass 3), 25 Nov 2026
- Two web searches on the rim formulas (queries in RUN_RECORD row 7) returned no page carrying the Basel wording and no mention of a Basel cipher copy [measured: result lists read, titles only].
- Genre parallel [sourced]: Clarck Drieshen, "Magical seals in a medieval English book of hours", British Library Digitised Manuscripts blog, 18 Jul 2019 (read in the brewminate.com reprint): London BL Stowe MS 16 f. 151r "Hoc signum Salamonis qui super se portaverit nec manu in bello erit captus", f. 152r "Hoc signum fer [te]cum contra omnes inimicos"; Royal MS 17 B XLVIII f. 1r "Quando audieris tonitruum respice hoc signum et liberaberis". These are captions of the late-medieval pseudo-Solomonic amulet seals, the same form as the Basel rims ("hoc signum ...", "qui hoc signum portat ...").
- Scripture in the rims [derived: recognised from the decode, not checked against an edition here]: S2-04 Luke 4:30 (Vulgate "ipse autem transiens per medium illorum ibat"; the rim has iesus ... ipsorum abiit); S2-06 Luke 1:52 (Magnificat); pass 2's S1-102 Ps 90(91):13.
