Clear copy, f. 167r, for comparison: "The seal of Jupiter is made of the best English tin, having on one side the second figure, arranged with its numbers and cells as appears in the frontispiece; on the other face is to be put the image or type of the planet, namely a priestly man and a philosopher looking into a book and reading; above his head a certain star shall be marked with this name, Jupiter, as in the earlier design of Saturn. Then, having watched for some day of Jupiter when the Moon enters the first degree of Libra and Jupiter is well placed among the other planets, cast and stamp your seal and keep it in some sky-blue silk cloth, etc. Its virtues are: this sign, carried, wins love, grace, friendship and favour with all men; wherever it is put, it wonderfully increases fortune and substance. Whoever carries this sign with him will obtain his wish in all his affairs and will be free of fear."
