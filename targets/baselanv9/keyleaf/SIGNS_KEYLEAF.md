# SIGNS_KEYLEAF: labels added for the second pass (25 Nov 2026)

Written before any pentacle inscription was transcribed. The first-pass labels (`../SIGNS.md`) are kept unchanged. The
labels below name shapes that appear on the key leaf f. 99r (canvas 205, page id 5353424) and were not met on the
first-pass leaves. A label names a shape; its value is in `KEY_LEAF.tsv`, taken from the leaf only.

## New base labels
| label | shape (as drawn on f. 99r) |
|---|---|
| `Kz` | six-shape with a zig-zag stroke standing on its head |
| `6o` | six-shape under a large loop arching over it from the left |
| `Wd` | W-shape followed by a dot |
| `Bt` | box (Bx) whose right upright runs on into a hooked tail |
| `V` | V-shape (used with `@`: the left stroke runs on into a big loop) |
| `Tq` | dash with a short down-tick at its right end (like ¬) |
| `Hkb` | hook-sign (Hk) with a small bar or cross stroke on its stem |
| `Hkd` | hook-sign closed into a delta-like bowl |
| `Hkf` | hook-sign with a cross stroke through its descender (like ʒ with a bar) |
| `5c` | five-shape joined to an e-like loop on its right |
| `2o` | two-shape joined to a small closed loop or ring on its right |
| `2z` | two-shape with a ʒ-like tail |
| `Ec` and others from `../SIGNS.md` keep their first-pass meaning |

## New compounds of an old base with a mark (attached, not split)
| label | shape |
|---|---|
| `Lm4` | lambda joined to a 4-shape on its right |
| `X4` | x-shape whose right stroke forms a 4-like cross |
| `Bx4` | box whose right upright forms a 4-like cross |
| `X@`, `Bx@`, `V@`, `Pe@`, `Dl@`, `Tk@` | the sign with a big loop (`@` as in `../SIGNS.md`) |
| `Bxs`, `Dls` | box, delta, crossed by a slanting stroke |
| `6d` | six-shape crossed by a horizontal dash |
| `Xz` | x-shape with a ʒ-like tail from its lower right stroke |
| `Xo` | x-shape joined to a six-like loop on its right |
| `Xcz`, `Xco` | the same on the crossed x (`Xc`) |
| `Oe^` | oe-sign with one dot above (attached to the oe-sign, not split as a dot above) |
| `Tk@^` | tack with a big loop and a dot above (attached) |

## New mark
| label | mark |
|---|---|
| `*` | a dot inside the body of the sign (lambda, box, x, ...), appended to the base like the other marks, e.g. `Lm*`, `X*`, `Bx*`. On the first pass this dot was not recorded (`../SIGNS.md`); on the second pass it is recorded wherever it is seen, because the key leaf's two-dot rule refers to it. |
