I received only the day before yesterday, my dear Sir, the letter you did me the honour to write on the 31st of last month. I think exactly as Mr Leerding does about the Stadholder [507] [501] [...] Stadholder, and I am persuaded that the "friend of the heart" is still the soul of this whole merry band.
What vexes me in all this is the indiscretion of V. Vort in parading his intimacy with the respectable Mr Leerding; and he prides himself at court on his skill and his successes, which he owes only to Monsieur the Pensionary of the Amsterdammers.
Besides, these connections give the "friend of the heart" pretexts, and I am convinced that our partner's business would have gone much better if this intimacy had always stayed secret. But with a fellow, a vain little fool like V. Vort, one had to expect that he would sacrifice the good of the thing to his vanity.
