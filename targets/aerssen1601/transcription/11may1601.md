# Aerssen -> Oldenbarnevelt, Paris 11 May 1601 (NA 3.01.14 inv. 2019 scans 50-51) - cipher passages with contemporary interlinear decipherment (fork trE)
Conventions: CONVENTIONS.md (+UPDATE). Extra sign names used here:
- `8`  = delta-shaped sign, ascender rising to the RIGHT, open (δ) = la.  `d` = closed-loop sign with ascender curling LEFT over the body (∂) = ra.
  The two are distinct when seen side by side (scan 51 'la crainte': `8 1 d 26`), but easy to confuse at low zoom.
- `d+` = d with cross-stroke (đ) = pa.  `theta` = 8/θ-like sign with a stroke through it = f.  `oo` = two small circles joined by a stroke = o.
- `ib` = '1' followed by a closed-loop sign (looks like 'ıɮ' / 'ı' + looped 's'), only in 33^ ib 4 18 'doibt' (scan 50) = i. Not the ordinary 15 ('ıs').
- `l`  = tall-loop sign ℓ = li.  `l2` = ℓ with a large descending curl (ℓ+c) = ru.  `pi` = π = li.  `psi` = ψ = je.
- `gq` = g-like sign whose descender curls LEFT in an open hook (ℊ); value qui. Plain `g` (closed lower loop) = que; `g^` (barred) = no.
- `qT` = q with a stroke through the descender (ꝗ) = qui (scan 51 right; probably the same sign as gq).
- `c^` = c-shaped sign with bar = pa.  `eps^` = ε with bar = ma.  `omega^` = round ω with bar = ne (distinct from `w^` = ϖ-like w with bar = na).
- `X` = X-like sign = a.  `K` = capital K (scan 51 right), value not supported.  `3'`, `2'`, `48'`, `96'` = token with grave tick above (code words).
- Bars: `^` = bar above. Several bars coincide exactly with underlines in the decipherer's gloss (the gloss underline sits on the cipher bar); where only the gloss underline is visible the token is marked `^?`.
