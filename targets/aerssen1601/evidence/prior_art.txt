PRIOR ART — Aerssen cipher letters to Oldenbarnevelt (research notes, 2026-10-04)

1. De Leeuw thesis (UvA 2000) — ONLINE: https://dare.uva.nl/id/2d5163aa-e69b-4c90-8624-1bd4b5a04d0d
   full PDF https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/3074957/12760_Thesis.pdf
   Full-text grep: NO mention of Aerssen/Aarssens/Buzanval. Early-17th-c. discussion (pp.13-15 of ch.1):
   Marnix, d'Alaume (codebreaker hired by Oldenbarnevelt 1605), Huygens; Dutch codes 1572-1648 "show remarkably
   little variation"; first detailed examples = Boetzelaer (Paris 1615; ~80-item 2-digit nomenclator + ~30-group
   cipher; cites Veenendaal RGP III ix-xiii) and Haga (Constantinople 1620; nomenclator 1-116). Aerssen 1598-1614 not discussed.

2. Haak/Veenendaal, Johan van Oldenbarnevelt. Bescheiden (RGP GS 80 [1934, Haak, 1570-1601]; GS 108 [1962, 1602-1613]; GS 121 [1967, 1614-1620])
   Online OCR: https://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/retroboeken/oldenbarnevelt  (search endpoint searchText/index_html)
   Vol.1 Inleiding p.VII: Aerssen correspondence printed abridged ("Soms is bekorting wenschelijk gebleken").
   p.X: copies in Legatie-archief S.G. 613-619; ~250 originals Aerssen->Oldenbarnevelt 1598-1609 in archief Staten van Holland H. 2632
        (ex Graaf van Limburg Stirum, Noordwijk); 1933: 7 letters 1598 + 8 letters 1601 given to Rijksarchief Arnhem (Wassenaer Nederhemert).
   Vol.1 prints ~34 Aerssen->JvO letters, nos. 214-339 (2 May 1598 - 30 Dec 1601).
   Cipher handling in vol.1:
     p.397 n.2 (no.222, 29 May 1598): Leg. 611 IV orig + copy; "origineel gedeeltelijk in cijferschrift. In de copie is het cijfer opgelost. De sleutel is in hetzelfde dossier aanwezig."
     p.398 n.3: word wrongly deciphered in copy, key gives correct word.
     p.446 (no.230 instructie Aerssen 26 Apr 1598): "Sal tot naeder advis in secrete saecken gebruycken den cyfer" n.2 "Te vinden in Leg. Arch. 611, IV."
     p.474 n.5: spaced words in cipher; solution in hand of griffier (Cornelis) Aerssen attached to MS followed.
     p.497 n.3-4 (no.240, 20 Oct 1598): cipher passage solved; "Dit cijfer is niet opgelost."
     p.538 n.2: "Deze namen in cijferschrift."
     p.555 n.4: Villeroy — "Zie den sleutel in Leg. 611 IV."
     p.584 n.2: "In den tekst stond: 21, boven welk cijfer de oplossing geschreven was."
     p.599 (no.318, 23 Jun 1601): Aerssen PS: "Je vous envoyé cy-joinct le chiffre qu'avez désiré..." (new cipher 1601)
     p.605 n.1 "Zie cijfer in Leg. 611, IV"; n.4 "De oplossing van dit cijfer is mij niet bekend. Waarschijnlijk wordt De Buzanval bedoeld." (code 95 printed raw)
     p.625 n.2: cipher number argued = Buzanval.
   Vol.2 intro p.VII/huygens page: Aerssen correspondence NOT included in vols 2-3 (planned separate edition, never realised). p.XIII: only one cipher doc in vol.2 (nr.92), unsolved, key not found.
   Vol.3 intro pp.IX-XIII (Veenendaal 1967): full reconstruction of Boetzelaer (Paris 1615-) number code (<100, dots/bars/strokes modify meaning) + letter/digit cipher (solved by M.A. Koolen); partial key table printed.

   CORRECTION of RGP numbers: Deel 1 1570-1601 = GS 80 (Haak 1934); Deel 2 1602-1613 = GS 108 (Veenendaal 1962, Haak d.1937); Deel 3 1614-1620 = GS 121 (Veenendaal 1967). (Not 91/106.)
   Vol.2 Inleiding pp.VII-VIII (Veenendaal): Aerssen letters to JvO to 1613 preserved "zeer uitvoerig, talrijk, volledig" — orig. Holland 2632, copies Legatie-archief;
     Haak printed them "volledig" in vol.1 (1598-1601) but did not plan to continue; Veenendaal omitted them entirely for a future separate edition (never made);
     only quotations in notes; JvO's (few) letters to Aerssen ARE printed in vol.2.
   Haak vol.1 cipher-annotated Aerssen items: nos. 222 (29.5.1598), 235 (Sep 1598, p.474 solution in hand of griffier Aerssen), 240 (20.10.1598), 265 (29.5.1599),
     277 (JvO->Aerssen 1600, minute H.2583; "Villeroy – zie den sleutel in Leg. 611 IV"), 304 (10.4.1601), 323 (18.8.1601: "25 (Monsr de Villeroy)", 95 unsolved), 335 (23.11.1601), 318 (23.6.1601 new chiffre sent).
     Convention: numbers printed with solution in parentheses / solved text in spaced type.

3. Van Deventer, Gedenkstukken (3 vols 1860-65). archive.org: gedenkstukkenva00unkngoog (vol.2, 1593-1602, 1862); gedenkstukkenva00devegoog (vols 2+3 bound; vol.3 1604-1609, 1865).
   Vol.2 preface: Aerssen->JvO originals lent by Graaf van Limburg Stirum-Noordwijk. No. CXIX pp.248-250: Aerssen->JvO 29 May 1598 printed "(uit het cijfer)" [= the deciphered copy].
     Other Aerssen items vol.2: CXLV, CXLVII, CLI (to JvO), CXXXIX, CXL, CXLII, CL (to Valcke), CXXX (Villeroy to Aerssen), CXLVIII memoire.
   Vol.3 preface: Aerssen->JvO letters 1603-1611 recently bought by Rijksarchief, used selectively (CLVII, ~CLXI, CLXXIV, CLXXVII, CLXXXVIII, CLXXXIX, CXCII, CXCV...). No cipher notes at all; no raw code numbers found in OCR. Whether ciphered passages were silently printed from interlinear decipherments: unverified.
   Vreede 1846 (archive.org lettresetngoci00choa): Lettres et négociations de Buzanval et d'Aerssen (1598-1599): Aerssen letters to the STATES-GENERAL (Oct 1598-1599, mostly from Utrecht copies), not to JvO; only cipher mention = instruction clause "in secrète saecke gebruyckende den cyfer".
   Nouaillac 1908 (archive.org unenvoyhollanda00nouagoog): Lettres inédites de François d'Aerssen à Jacques Valcke (1599-1603), 121 letters, Legatie-archief 612.
     Only cipher = letter 11 May 1601 (pp.407-410): printed with raw number groups (e.g. "59, 36, 9, 30, 33..." "p. 13" "ff. 22, h. 1") + marginal/interlinear decodings in notes;
     "Nous n'avons pas la clef... impossible de déchiffrer le reste, un même nombre désignant des lettres ou des mots différents." -> printed but UNSOLVED in 1908.
   Groen van Prinsterer Archives 2e sér. t.1-2: no Aerssen->JvO letters; nothing on Aerssen cipher.
   Kernkamp & Van Heijst, BMHG 76 (1962): Buzanval letters to Daniel van der Meulen 1595-99; no cipher discussion.
   Motley Barneveld: no cipher discussion of Aerssen letters.
