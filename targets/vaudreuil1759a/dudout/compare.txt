Comparison of the two separately produced transcriptions and key tables (Vaudreuil 1388-1390), 5 Nov 2026.
Dan's side: targets/vaudreuil1759{a,b,c}/transcription.txt and key/key274.json + key/overrides.json.
Brice Dudout's side (PR #268): dudout/transcription_<n>.txt and dudout/key.tsv (v2).
Method: the cipher groups are pulled out of each file (digits only; [..] and {..} brackets, '~' line ends and '|' marks
dropped; clear words and the written ordinals skipped) and the two number sequences are aligned with difflib.SequenceMatcher.
Groups cut by the binding are written with fewer digits in one file ('47~', '[47]'), so they appear as differences.
The comparison finds where the two readings of the images differ; it does not say which one is right. Nothing was reconciled
and neither transcription was changed. The PR was public before the 1388-1390 readings here were committed, so this is a
cross-check between two readings, not a test of independence.
