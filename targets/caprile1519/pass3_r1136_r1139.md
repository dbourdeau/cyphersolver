# Pass 6 (3 Oct 2026): pen shapes of the look-alike signs, failed

`shapes21.py` cut each R1136/R1139 line into ink blobs and aligned them to the tokens (one blob per token where the
widths agree); contact sheets `shapes21/sheet_<label>.png`, features `shapes21/features.tsv`, LM choices per QB
occurrence `shapes21/qb_values.txt` (`shapes21_vals.py`). V1920, V1921 and R1138 were not cut (their 8s are in the
EM counts already).

- The automatic alignment gives a correct crop for only about half of the QB occurrences (13 of 31 checked by eye) and
  almost none for EL (1 of 8) or 8 (0 of 3): the rest are neighbouring signs.
- QB in the correct crops has two pen forms: a closed figure-8 (9 crops) and a loop on a stem with a cross-bar, ♀-like
  (4 crops). The LM's choice per occurrence does not follow the form: figure-8 e, t, t, e, o, o, e, o, o; ♀-form d, o,
  t, o. No split by shape.
- Plain c is a distinct form (open c, no hook; 3 clean crops), but its LM values were already inconsistent (o 5, e 4,
  c 3 in pass 5), so the shape does not give it a value.
- AMP crops show one form (α/&-like, 4 clean); EL could not be cropped reliably, so AMP vs EL is not settled by shape.

Verdict: shape separation does not help. No re-decode was run with shape classes because no class carries a
consistent value. Percentages unchanged: R1139 89.7 %, R1136 66.9 %, V1921 97.0 %. Stopped here as instructed.
