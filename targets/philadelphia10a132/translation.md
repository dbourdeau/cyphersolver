### 76r

> [I conjure] you, rota, in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. By holy Mary, [mother] of our Lord Jesus Christ; by the twelve apostles; by the four evangelists; by the [three?] patriarchs; by the [twenty-eight?] prophets; by the ten choirs of angels and archangels; by the cherubim and seraphim; by the sun and moon; by the stars; by all the saints of paradise; by all the heavens; by the air and earth; by the incarnation of the Son of God; by his birth; by his baptism; by his holy fasting; by his holy Passion; by his holy cross, for which he [deigned] to die; by his holy [Ascension]; by his holy [Resurrection]; by all the words spoken in the holy Mass; by all the good things of the world: through these, you, rota, have the power and strength to do and fulfil everything I shall wish. > Again [I conjure] you, rota, by the evil spirits, by all those who have ...

The literal reading is “digntus est mori”; [dignatus] / [deigned] is an editorial repair supplying the missing a, not a silent change to the ciphertext.

### 76v

> ... the power to fulfil my will, by which I bind you, so that the person whom I touch [ending supplied], so that I make a name [the naming/touching clause is uncertain: approximately, “whatever person I touch with you, rota, or cause to be touched”], you, rota, do not let that person go, nor stand, nor drin[k], nor eat, nor sleep, nor [enjoy] carnal pleasure with any lover except me, nor love any person except [me]; and let that person love only me, [?wherever] I carry you, rota, upon me. And do this through [your] great power. When the [conjuration] is completed [final letter supplied], “colige ipsas rota” [collect/bind the rota; number and syntax unresolved] in the [na]me of the person by whom you wish to be loved.
>
> And let it be said [seven or three?] times.

The feminine grammatical forms follow persona, a feminine noun; they do not by themselves establish the sex of the intended beloved. [Enjoy] supplies a verb missing before the ablative phrase about carnal pleasure.
