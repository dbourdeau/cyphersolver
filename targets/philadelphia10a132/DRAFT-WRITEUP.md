# A ciphered Latin love-binding conjuration

Circa 1550 · College of Physicians of Philadelphia, MS 10a 132, ff. 75r, 76r–v

The recto and verso of f. 76 give a Latin binding conjuration. The cancelled opening on f. 75r contains a second version of part of the formula.

## How the text was obtained

A shift back by one position in the manuscript’s 23-letter alphabet gives the literal output. A new replay reproduced all 39 submitted lines, including the cancelled opening. Clear numerals are kept outside the cipher count.

## What remains open

The cancelled opening gives 14 sure words of 20. The main text has a difficult clause on f. 76v, provisional rota/ruta forms, and an uncertain closing numeral and word. The literal output preserves the initials. The edited reading supplies proposed expansions.
