# V/134 — Dieci di Balìa to Machiavelli, Florence, 17 Aug 1510 — single pass, decoded in part

Source: BNCF, Autografi Palatini, Carte Machiavelli V/134, f. 1r (old f. 230), Teca image of about 900 px
(images are not reproduced here: BNCF condition). Transcription: one pass only (`transcription/R1_V134.txt`, labels of
`key/KEY.tsv`); mechanical decode: `reading/V134_decoded_raw.txt`. Far below 95 % of words: **read in part**.

Notation: plain = clear on the sheet; *italic* = decoded by sense; [..] = restored; ‹...› = unread stretch.

Clear (lines 1-2): "Spectabilis vir etc. Dopo l'ultima de x mandatati per mano del Pandolphino comparse"
+ cipher (line 2) *Giovanni Girolami* (gi-o-ua-n-ni gi-ro-la-mi, clean) + clear "et di nuovo non haviamo dipoi che dirti salvo"
+ cipher *come intendemo* (come-i-n-te-n-[de]-mo).

Cipher, lines 3-13, by sense where it holds:
- l. 3: *le [galee] viniziane* ‹...› *da l'altro* ‹...› *in porto et da Geno[va]*
- l. 4: *ciò che l'armata* ‹...›
- l. 5: *che* ‹...› *[galee] viniziane furono* ‹...› *San* ‹...›
- l. 6: ‹...› *dipoi* ‹...› *che* ‹...›
- l. 7: ‹...› *da Roma* ‹...› *intendiamo che*
- l. 8: ‹...› *[...]mila lance et tremila cinque[cento]* ‹...›
- l. 9: ‹...› *in quello luogo* ‹...› *tornato in sul[la]* ‹...›
- l. 10-11: ‹...› *et mostra altra* ‹...› (twice) ‹...›
- l. 11-12: *[Ma]rcantonio et* ‹...› *gente et* ‹...›
- l. 13 + clear: "Bene vale. Ex Palatio florentino die xvij Augusti 1510."

English: after your last of the 10th, sent through Pandolfini, Giovanni Girolami arrived, and since then we have nothing to tell
you except that we hear that the Venetian galleys ... in port and from Genoa ... what the fleet ... from Rome ... we hear that
... lances and 3,500 [foot?] ... Marcantonio [Colonna] and ... troops ...

What the reading lacks: a second blind pass and the glyph pairs the 900 px images do not separate (su/ta bb/66, la/ri theta,
ga/pa, to/s, so/a+a). Not checked against the ASF Dieci di Balìa missive register (a clear minute of 17 Aug 1510 probably exists).
