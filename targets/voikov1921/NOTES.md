## Open points

- `de?iatg`: one letter lost, so девять or десять. The next item has десять, which slightly favours девять here.
- `chetyre shjima`: not read. The word before "by six inches" is probably a unit or size for the saws, and "arshin" would be a guess.
- `kieszi` read as клещи (pincers) from context. It could be another tool word, though "for sharpening saws" follows.
- `ce` before `de?iatg` is unexplained.
- `dlia tochki pil` may belong with what follows, as a separate item for saw files, rather than with the pincers.
- Several `s` ("with") are missing before `polovinoi`. The clerk may have dropped it, and it is bracketed each time.

## Translation

For the goods-exchange fund with the countryside it is necessary to buy immediately hardware, colon: tyre iron, horseshoe
iron; wire nails from two and a half to seven inches; horseshoe nails of middle sizes; shovels for earthwork, square,
nine [or ten] by eleven and three-quarter inches, pointed, ten by eleven and a half inches; rakes; forks for hay and
manure; carpenters' axes weighing two and a half to three pounds; cross-cut saws, three and a half, four [...] by six
inches; joiners' pincers of eight and ten inches; for sharpening saws; mortise chisels up to three-quarters of an inch;
flat paring chisels from a half to one and a quarter inches; half-round from a half to one inch. Continuation follows.

## Context

A buying order from Moscow's foreign-trade side to B. S. Stomoniakov in Berlin. Secondary notes to Lenin's collected works
tie the Politburo's decision on "Comrade Kopp's proposal" of 16 April 1921 to goods exchange and to Stomoniakov, but the
decision's text has not been seen, so the link is by subject only.
