For the goods-exchange fund with the countryside it is necessary to buy immediately hardware, colon:
tyre iron, horseshoe iron;
wire nails from two and a half to seven inches;
horseshoe nails of middle sizes;
shovels for earthwork, square, nine [or ten] by eleven and three-quarter inches,
pointed, ten by eleven and a half inches;
rakes; forks for hay and manure;
carpenters' axes weighing two and a half to three pounds;
cross-cut saws, three and a half, four [...] by six inches;
joiners' pincers of eight and ten inches;
for sharpening saws;
mortise chisels up to three-quarters of an inch;
flat paring chisels from a half to one and a quarter inches;
half-round from a half to one inch.
