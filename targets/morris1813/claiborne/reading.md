# Reading

Content already read in 1805-1806 (Wagner's interlinear decipherments, printed in PJM-SS and Rowland): nothing unpublished on the content side. Our alignment (328 words) reads the three NARA pieces word by word against those decipherments, 89 values sure, 145 probable, 27 doubtful (key.tsv). No new translation is offered.

Historical note from the printed sources: Claiborne asked for a cipher on 26 March 1805; Wagner sent it on 8 July 1805, received 12 Aug 1805 (Rowland 3:165). The code called "Weber 419-30" that Graham used on 2 Jan 1806 is the one given to Governor Sargent of Mississippi, which Claiborne had kept in his office (Claiborne to Madison, 9 Jan 1806, PJM-SS 11:169): a different code from Claiborne's.
