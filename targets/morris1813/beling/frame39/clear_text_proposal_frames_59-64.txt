The democratic Government of the States under the administration of the [Frenchified; adjacent corrected/overwritten word unclear] Jefferson and Madison not only during the existing War but also before that epocha has given the most strong and repeated proofs of its desire and intentions to effectuate the separation of the Spanish americas from the Mother Country. In the summer of the year1810 broke out the insurrection of West Florida instigated by the Anglo-american Government whose inordinate and despicable ambition stimulated it to profit of the injuries and difficulties which then threatened to overwhelm the Peninsula and to possess itself of the district—that of Baton Rouge—which was the Theatre of the insurrection. The futile pretext of this spoliation as alleged by the President Madison was that the Territory thus plundered appertained to Louisiana notwithstanding that the same territory had been denominated and governed by H.B.M. and H.C.M. successively as an integral part of Florida from the year1763 until the month of August1813 [as written; earlier paragraph says1810] the date of the insurrection mentioned. In the month of June1811 in compliance to his importunity was conceded to Mr Madison by the

60L
Congress authority to annex to the States the entire province of both Floridas altho under certain conditions involving the corruption and treason of the Governors of the said Floridas. The Ambassadors of the revolutionary Government of Caracas received from Mr Madison and his Ministers declarations the most expressive of their deep interest in the success of the revolution of the said place and of similar results in all the other ultramarine provinces for whose inspection was given to the said ambassadors a form and example of a democratic model of the political constitutions recommended for the general adoption under the suggestion persuasion and seduction of which the same President had formed purposely and intentionally the model referred to from the abundance of his philanthropy in order to better the sons of Spanish America. Emissaries & democratic agents have been sent by the President into all the Spanish Provinces to excite and organize the insurrection of their inhabitants and as soon as any temporary advantage was obtained by the insurgents there was sent immediately certain

60R
special revolutionists called Consuls with the intention to animate those insurgents by the recognition of their authority, among the numerous messengers can be mentioned with entire precision the names of Matthews Shaler, Robinson, Kingston Lowry and Poinsett destined to Florida Mexico Havana, Caracas and Chile. In the present year the Government of the said States encouraged by its former impunity in respect to [treacherous?] possession of Baton Rouge has taken with violence the Mobile and all territory East of the River Perdido; Before the present War and during the Presidency of Mr Jefferson a military officer named Pike penetrated into the internal Provinces of Mexico with the motive now avowed by the same emissary of collecting all the statistical and Topographical information possible in order to facilitate to his Govt the extension of its territory to the Rio Grande also including on the West in Louisiana the entire Province of Texas and part of those of New Santander New Leon Cohavilla and Northern New Mexico. If any evidence of the insidious and hostile attempts of that Emissary is wanting such evidence will be found in the memoir of Don Pedro Baptist Pino deputy in the Cortes from New Mexico, from exact and indubitable

61L
information it is known that an assemblage of vagabonds and Malefactors instigated and protected by the American Government had taken possession of the Territory between Louisiana and the Rio Grande calling it the State of Texas, thus indicating its confederation and new parentage with the other States of the North. By these facts and [others?] it is evinced [interlinear: without doubt?] indisputably by the conduct of the American Government that there can not exist the least doubt that its firm and unalterable intention is to weaken and finally to annihilate the Spanish Government in America and by means of hidden fraud and open force to promote its own aggrandizement by the iniquitous sacrifice of the legitimate Rights and Interests of the Spanish Monarchy. The means and resources which that Government has [severally?] to attain the accomplishment of its unjust and atrocious designs are numerous and powerful especially at New Spain on account of its proximity. The establishment and triumph of

61R
democracy and its companion atheism having been effected in the immense regions of that Province their impious and destructive domination in the other Colonies can [scarcely?] be long retarded; but as there are times when divine providence in the spirit of its goodness and beneficence resolves to furnish the means competent to moderate and even more to restrain the great calamities which threaten the dissolution of societies thus also in the present [grievous?] situation of Spanish America exposed to be deluged by the poisonous lava from the volcano of the twin demons of democracy and atheism there are certain resources and expedients whose employment by Spain might conduct to the establishment of an impassable and insuperable barrier in the defence and preservation of the possessions thus so much threatened. Louisiana by treaty of St Ildefonso of the year

62L
1800 was retroceded by Spain to France on certain conditions in favor of the Queen of Etruria and under the terminatory stipulation that the said Province should not be transferred to any other Power whatever. Napoleon notwithstanding this stipulation always disposed to violate and to treat with contempt his most solemn engagements sold to President Jefferson in the year1803 the territory named without having executed the condition specified on the fulfilment of which depended his Right according to Justice to the said Territory. Of the stipulation of the Treaty thus violated by Napoleon by his sale of Louisiana Mr Jefferson was well informed and he also well knew that his purchase of Louisiana involved equally the most flagrant fraud in respect to Spain and the violation of the constitution of his own Country. The

62R
American Government has therefore only the mere possession of Louisiana divested of all legitimacy while the right belongs to the Spanish Monarchy. So have asserted and still assert according to their opinions the Citizens of the States most distinguished for virtue and talents. From my residence for more than three years in Louisiana after its cession to the American Govt, the exercise of the faculty of a Counsellor of Law and in Military Command I was enabled to collect the most exact information of the circumstances of the Country and of the opinions of the inhabitants respecting their incorporation with the States and with entire truth I am authorized by my knowledge thus collected and my subsequent correspondence with certain individuals of the greatest influence in that Country to declare that there is not in the world any important Project susceptible of so easy an execution as that of the separation of Louisiana from those States. The principal motives which incline the Louisianians to that disposition may be

63L
reduced to1st the continual display of the vulgarity and excess of unbridled democracy.2d The scandalous degradation of the ancient Religion of the Country—the Catholic apostolic and Roman Church being subject to the authority of the civil tribunals which may always be presided by the most violent and inveterate Jews and Heretics.3d The prohibition of the introduction of slaves for the cultivation of sugar Cotton Indigo and Tobacco—the culture of which will have to be abandoned unless that introduction is obtained. The produce already so much diminished in quantity is reduced in value to the most extreme and ruinous depreciation by the impossibility of the least exportation in consequence of the constant blockade of the Mississippi by the English Squadrons. From hence in the united causes of disgust and discontent arises the ardent desire of the Louisianians to separate and divorce themselves from their incestuous connection with the Democratic anti-religious and anti-commercial Government in whose [embrace?] they are forced and violated by means of the combinations and intrigues of both [Napo-; prefix clipped]

63R
-leon & Jefferson. Since then that Government thus has only mere possession of Louisiana devoid of all justice and legitimacy and makes use of the Territory thus fraudulently and deceitfully acquired as a focal point where the renegade officers and soldiery of usurpation and disorganization may collect to form and mature their plans it clearly appears that moral justice and sound and true policy on the part of the Spanish Government conspire in favor of the project of converting Louisiana from its present condition so prejudicial and injurious into an impassable and impregnable barrier in the defence and protection of the adjacent possessions of the Spanish Monarchy. This translation [alteration substituted?] of Louisiana inimical and destructive into Louisiana friendly and protective is a project whose effects cannot be more important and beneficial than its accomplishment

But to give to this project the force requisite and adequate to the end of placing limits thus impassable and impregnable and to form an insuperable counterpoise to the intrusive enterprises of the American Government, not less ambitious of the extension of its dominions than ancient Rome and modern France—Florida and the Territory between Louisiana and the Rio Grande already [in?] the possession of the Renegados cited would [be?] of the greatest utility and efficacy in being incorporated with the new Kingdom in case [that?] the Spanish Govt should cede them [for?] the end mentioned and under the guaranty

A new Government thus formed from the union of these Territories and organized on the principle of a limited Monarchy sheltered and protected by the guaranty specified might immediately like Hercules in infancy strangle the serpent of Democratic usurpation and restrain with the most perfect efficacy its future efforts and enterprises in the northern Regions of Spanish America. Thus then may Spain have on the frontier—a [point?] so accessible and vulnerable—of Mexico an anti-Democratical ally whose fidelity and justice would be secured by the most efficient securities in place of the present neighbouring power whose Government without ceasing has endeavoured and still endeavours per fas aut nefas to despoil that Monarchy of its legitimate territory and to occasion it the irreparable loss of all its ultramarine possessions.
