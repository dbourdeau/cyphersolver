## Coded runs as sentences

1. I have proceeded this far on my route to Madrid and am now detained by [?occurrences] which render it insecure to proceed and which [?threaten] materially to change the form and views of the Spanish government and politicks.
2. The king having delayed his arrival at the capital for an unexpected length of time and preceding his departure from Valencia by marching troops from various points toward Madrid has occasioned apprehensions that [?he] will not take the oath required by the new constitution.
3. These have been encreased by the [?occurrences] in Valencia ...
4. This however is not the [?true] one: it was effected by the usual means of bribery and corruption by the priesthood, the nobility and the rich individuals whose influence is more felt in this than in any other City of Spain ...
5. It is my duty to mention that the failure of the liberation of our [?citizens] in Algiers is I fear attributable to the agency in it being committed to the author of the memoir forwarded to you relative to [?Lou]isianna, [?who] certainly ought not to be admitted to any participation in the confidence of those to whom the interests of the United States are in any shape entrusted.
6. Signature: Anthony Morris.
