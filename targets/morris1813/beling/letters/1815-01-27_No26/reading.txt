Morris to Monroe, Madrid, 27 January 1815, headed No 26 Triplicate. NARA M31 roll 14, frame 75.

Summary and context: Morris reports that a messenger has left for Onis, the Spanish minister in the United States, and that the peace with Britain has changed Spain's position.

The messenger has at length departed with dispatches for Mr Onis, as intimated in my late letters, but I have grounds to believe much modified by the new situation in which Spain finds herself with the [?United States] in consequence of the peace with [?Great Britain]. Anthony Morris
