### Collation with 88L

I read the 88L block directly on M31-014-0088.jpg, x 0 to 2400, y 1260 to 1880. Line breaks differ from 84, and some groups at the line ends run into the gutter (968 1069 at the end of the first line, 554 1065 at the end of the fourth) but remain legible enough to match. Group for group, 88L agrees with my reading of 84 in the body, including 1060, both 1069 after 1063 and 1321, 963, 510, 146 and all four "<" marks. The only differences are that 88L has no 370 after the final 364 (it runs on as "364. - I annex") and no numeral signature. 88L shares both April non-fits (510, 146), so it does not help with them and is likely a copy of the same exemplar rather than independent evidence.

### Where it does not read

- 510 = cape, seen as 510 in both witnesses. "sour-cape" is not a word. 516 = ce would give source, but I do not see a 6: this hand's 6 has a tall stem, and this figure has none. Proposal, not a reading.
- 146 = jur, seen as 146 in both witnesses, and the tall stem is there. 140 = it would give visit. The frozen 140 gave the right sense but is not what the page shows. Proposal, not a reading.
- 370 twice, once after the last sentence and once before the signature. The key cell is an unidentified z-like glyph, so there is no value to apply. It follows 364 each time, and april4 has "364 370 101 359" in the same position, so it may be a sentence-level sign rather than a word. That is a guess.

### Where it does not read

- 1569 = December, and the page clearly shows 1569. "with the December" does not read. 1549 = United States would fit and 1549 occurs in april4, but I see a 6 with a tall stem, not a 4. The key entry for 69 is flagged alpha_adjudicated_faint_numeric, so the key cell is also a possible source of the error. Proposal, not a reading.
- "much modified" has no expressed noun: what is modified is not stated, and I am not supplying one. The dispatches or their content are the natural sense, but the text does not say so.
- 101 = I in "but I have": the first figure is heavily inked and joined, so it could be taken for 601 (oot). 101 is kept and the group stays K, but this is the softest K in the letter.

## Bias notes

- 1086 and 1138 were not blind. My first look gave 1080 and 1133. After seeing 282 = th, I already expected length, and I settled on 1086 and 1138 under zoom with that in mind.
