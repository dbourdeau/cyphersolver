Morris to Monroe, Cadiz, 11 April 1814, No. 13 Duplicate. NARA M31 roll 14, frames 84 and 88L.

Summary and context: Morris, at Cadiz, tells Monroe he will go to Madrid to be nearer reliable information, and that he has had no word from Washington since reaching Europe.

In order to be nearer the sour[?ce] of authentick information and to avail myself of the best means of promoting the objects of my vis[?it] I intend in a short time to proceed to Madrid. Not having had the satisfaction of hearing from you since my arrival in Europe I have been left to the exercise of a discretion on this subject which determines me under present circumstances to proceed. [370] . [370] Anthony Morris
