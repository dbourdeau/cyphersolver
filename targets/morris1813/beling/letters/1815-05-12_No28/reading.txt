Morris to Monroe, Madrid, 12 May 1815, headed No 28 Duplicate (number and date in clear). NARA M31 roll 14: frame 0209 right page (Duplicate, the base text) and frame 0210 right page (Triplicate). No contemporary decipherment on these copies. One reader.

Summary and context: Morris writes that things stand as in his letter of 24 March; that with no communication with the government since Erving's letters he has had no talks with the secretary of state, but the result of the expedition against New Orleans must have changed the court's view. In clear he encloses the government's only public document on the return of the "Ex-emperor", says nothing has followed from it but some military appointments (listed at the foot in clear) and that Spain will probably make no move of importance until England's course is known, and notes that he has not heard from Erving since 17 March.

Conventions: *italic* = Morris's own words in clear. `[?word]` = inference.

(No 28 Duplicate) Madrid May 12th 1815.

The situation of things here remains apparently as stated in mine of the twenty-fourth of March last. Although the actual non-communication with this government since the letters *referred to* from Mr Erving have prevented any *conversations with the* secretary of state, yet I cannot *doubt that the* glorious result of the expedition *against N.* Orleans *must have produced different* views on the part of this court.

*I enclose the only publick document issued by the Govt. here relative to the return of the Ex-emperor.* *(The clear paragraph goes on: nothing has yet been done on it but the appointment of the officers noted below, and probably nothing of importance will be done by Spain until England's course and the help she will give are known.)*

*I have not had the pleasure of hearing from* Mr E. *since March 17th,* the date of his last letter from Paris. Anthony Morris

*(Appointments, in clear, follow.)*
