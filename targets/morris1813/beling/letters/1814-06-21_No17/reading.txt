Morris to Monroe, Seville, 21 June 1814, headed No 17 duplicate. NARA M31 roll 14, frames 0107-0111: nine pages in clear, with the date in code at the head of 0107 and three coded lines and the coded signature at the end, on 0111. Read 12 Nov 2026 at the integration of PR #415 with the same key and decoder as the other letters. No contemporary decipherment on the frame. One reader. Only the coded lines are given here; the clear text of the letter is not transcribed.

Summary and context: After a long account in clear of the king's return and the means by which his authority was secured, Morris ends in code: things being as they are, he will stay quiet until he hears from Monroe. The coded heading reads "Seville, June twenty-first, 1814" (read at reduced size).

Conventions: `[370]` = the unidentified sign that follows full stops. `[?word]` = inference.

Seville, June twenty-first, 1814.

*(Nine pages in clear, ending: "... overturn the mighty mass of corruption by which it is kept down.")*

In the [?present] most unexpected state of things, therefore, I have thought it most [?prudent] to remain quiet until I shall have the satisfaction of hearing from you. [370] Anthony Morris

Notes.
- "[?present]": the third digit of the group is blotted; 1310 (present) fits the sense, 1320 (probab) does not.
- "[?prudent]": 1306.1159.393, pr + ved with a stroke + ent; 1159 does not give "ud" by its key value.
- 1018 with the triangle (ect) is "expected"; 295 with the angle (thing) is "things".
