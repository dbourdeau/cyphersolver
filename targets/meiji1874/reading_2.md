# Established text, telegram 2 (JACAR C04025783500 frame 1811 + C04025783600 frame 1812), 8 Nov 1874

First deciphered by Ohno Tetsuya (2010, his no. 4027). The continuation on C04025783600 frame 1812 was also read by Satoshi Tomokiyo (personal communication, 30 Oct 2026). This is an independent re-reading, made before we knew of these readings.

支那ノ談判{既ニ}破レタル趣、諸新聞紙ニ記載シアレドモ、未ダ政府{エ}ハ報知‹ケ›ナシ、尚確報アラバ[速ヤカニ]{申シ}{達ス}ベキニ付キ、浮説等信用スベカラズ。

## State of the reading (Daniel Bourdeau's rule: 95% of words read in their sense)

29 words: 23 certain, 4 probable ({既ニ}, {エ}, {申シ}, {達ス}), 1 restored by sense ([速ヤカニ]), 1 unresolved ‹ケ› → 28/29 = 96.6%.

## Justifications

- Frames 1811 and 1812 run on: 1811 stops at 尚確報, 1812 (filed by JACAR in the next item) resumes with アラバ and bears the date and signature.
- {既ニ}: 67 written for 76 (transposition by the clerk). {エ}: pair 56; エ is a common phonetic spelling of the particle ヘ. {申シ}, {達ス}: one cursive 2/3 digit each read by sense (43, 35), not reviewed.
- [速ヤカニ]: a single 6 on the copy where スミヤカニ needs 66 (Brice Dudout confirms a single sign, plate C09 q11): one digit restored.
- ‹ケ›: pair 53 in 報知‹ケ›ナシ, probably the clerk's slip.
- Targeted reviews by Brice Dudout (shapes only, no plaintext shown): plate C02 10/10; plate C03 3/3 controls; plate C09 all 10 shape answers agree with the reading by sense (control included; in q03 the frame took in two signs, a plate error, and Brice read both correctly).
