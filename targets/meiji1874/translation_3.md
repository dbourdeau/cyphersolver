# English translation, telegram 3 (JACAR C10071951300)

"Consul Shinagawa says in his telegram of the 20th: I have just received a letter from Beijing: the Chinese government [...] to Plenipotentiary Okubo [...] must answer by the 20th of this month; after which he will probably withdraw." Okubo was threatening to break off and leave Beijing; the agreement was signed on 31 Oct 1874.
