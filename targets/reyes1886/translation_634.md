# DLI.4.634.1: Bernardo Reyes to Ignacio Revueltas, Monterrey, 1 April 1886 (English)

Words that were in cipher are in **bold**; a word in which one letter is a context fill is marked with `*`.

From Monterrey to Mexico City, 1 April 1886.
General Ignacio Revueltas.

My dear comrade and friend,
I refer to your kind letter of the 20th of last month.

Since Mr Garza Galán arrived he has taken steps towards a reconciliation with General Cervantes, and this has shown
me how **useful*** **his** **journey*** to the **capital*** was, and I have been glad that he made it. But in any case,
since I am more **afraid** of a ~~scoundrel~~ **rancher*** than of a politician, I shall keep **watch*** on the
**conduct** of **that*** **gentleman***, without **ceasing**, for **that** reason, to draw our relations closer, as I
have done.

You know [closing formula].

Notes: "ranchero" (here "rancher", a country landowner or rural strongman) is the deciphered word that replaced the
struck clear word. "su viaje" can also be "your journey"; the context (Garza Galán's arrival in Mexico City and his
steps after it) points to "his". "pendiente de" is "watchful of, keeping an eye on".
