# Sign conventions, DCXXI.3.323.1 ([F.] Sánchez to "José González", 12 Jan 1922)

The conventions followed in `transcription_DCXXI.3.323.1.txt` (set down from its header at packaging, 13 Nov 2026).
Shapes only, no values.

- The cipher words are typed on the same machine as the clear Spanish, in lower case; a capital is typed where a
  proper name starts. Each sign is recorded as the letter it is, with its case; `ñ` (with tilde) is a sign of its own.
- A cipher run is a stretch of such words in the clear text. Runs are numbered W01-W11 in page order.
- Commas, full stops, `#` and figures between cipher words are kept as typed; they are not enciphered.
- A letter overstruck by another sign before W09 (a typed V) is noted and not transcribed.
- Pen strokes over letters (W07: over `xf`, `uu`, `lw`, `ut`) are noted; the typed letters underneath are transcribed.
- Where the full page and a crop disagree, the crop is followed and the disagreement noted (W07).
