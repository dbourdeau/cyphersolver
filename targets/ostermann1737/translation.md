## l. 21, cipher lines 1 to 20

... to which nothing further is found [to give] you by way of resolution; only the former decrees are confirmed(?). And as to what has since passed here with the Persian(?) ambassador in the conferences, the protocols of them are enclosed herewith, according to which(?) you are to act in case(?) of need. But since the Persian(?) ambassador more than once pressed our vice-chancellor that he should write[l] to the Shah from himself, and although no need for it was seen, nevertheless, to please the ambassador, the vice-chancellor did so by our order; a copy of it is communicated to you herewith, where you will see that in everything reference is made(?) to him, the ambassador, and to you. You are(?) to send that letter to the Shah in a fitting manner, and for the rest to watch vigilantly and diligently to find out[ть] what is going on[т] between the Shah and the Turks, and how and with what his ambassador Abdul Baki Khan has returned from Constantinople(?), with whom, as is known here, the Turkish ambassador too has gone to the Shah.
