# Translation of ASF MAP CXXXVII doc. 666: Francesco Pandolfini to the Otto di Pratica, Vercelli, 6 and 8 Sept 1515 (copy)

English, line by line after `reading_666.md`, with the same holes. `[...]` = not read; `[?word]` = conjecture;
`[15]`, `[22]` = numbers of the nomenclator not in the key. Words in parentheses are added for the English only.

## c. 674r

(Clear: "... with resolution until I have particular advice from Your Lordships,")
r01: in fact (I shall do) what
r02: the pope's orator does, who is not going to go forward nor towards the Most Christian (King) until the pope changes his resolution; who,
r03: one sees, until the 30th of last month persevered in the resolution made.
r04: I can give Your Lordships little news, and that not very reliable, since I am away
r05: from the camp; and I [?am] persuaded that Your Lordships will be able to learn
r06: the truth better by way of Parma and Piacenza.
r07: The camp of the Most Christian (King) for now is withdrawing this way: yesterday it was four or five miles from
r08: Buffalora, and it was said [?to be going] towards Biagrasso [Abbiategrasso]; the lord [...] up to the three (?).
r09: It seems that [...] with a certain number of men-at-arms and foot, up to Milan, [?has quarters]
r10: at San Cristofano in the borgo of Porta Tesinese, and having [?shown himself] towards them. As is
r11: said, some troops of Milan went back [?again]; some believe (so) as not to [...] show among
r12: the people of Milan that ready disposition towards the Most Christian (King) that His Lordship hoped for. While the King was
r13: at Buffalora, some envoys of the city of Milan came to His Majesty, making very great offers
r14: to His Majesty and promising to give them victuals [...]; and that disposition towards the Most Christian (King) not having [?yet] been confirmed,
r15: I gather that His Majesty has been surprised and angered by it.
r16: The army of the Swiss is said to be at [?a] place not far from Gallarate; and yesterday
r17: the Duke of Savoy, who has declared himself entirely for [?France], went in the company of the Bastard
r18: of Savoy and Monsignor de Lautrec
r19ins: and the general who was of Milan (identified by the contributor as Sebastiano Ferrier)
r18b: to that place, to the camp of the Swiss, to have the answer
## c. 674v
v01: on the agreement discussed in recent days in this city; and to have the final conclusion, where
v02: [...] today one should learn what has followed from it; and if the matter goes any longer,
v03: one may believe it will have no [?effect]. The conditions that were being discussed, and that [...], as

v06: revenue, a pension of [?twenty] thousand francs, the command of a hundred lances, and a wife of his own blood;
v07: that the Most Christian (King) pay the Swiss [?nine hundred] thousand scudi, that is two hundred (thousand) from today and the rest within [...] six
v08: years in various instalments; that he give them forty thousand scudi a year as a pension for [...]
v09: [...] of them in his [...]; on condition that he be bound to keep of them [?always] in his pay [...]
v10: in peace and war. The terms the Most Christian (King) offers them are very large, and [...] many are persuaded that the agreement

v12: and what number in the camp, Your Lordships can learn more reliably by way of
v13: Milan, (than) I by myself, who find myself away.
