# Sign conventions for 2393 (shape names only, no values)

Written before the first transcription pass of c. 674r and extended while transcribing; follows the token legend of
Brice Dudout's pandolfini1514 (frozen/LEGENDE.md) where the shapes are the same, so his key applies by name.

One token = one sign: `shape[^superscript]`. The superscript is the digit or digits written above the sign, noted on the
sign it stands over (in this copy it is often shifted by one sign; the transcription keeps the position as seen).

- Arabic figures as written: `0` (small round), `2` (a 2 or a curled ʒ; not told apart), `3`, `4`, `5`, `6`, `7`, `8`, `9` (q-like).
- Latin-like letters: `c`, `d` (round uncial d), `r` (long r / 2-like, also the long ʄ form), `t` (barred t), `x`, `y`,
  `S` (large S with hook), `L` (square), `D` (reversed D / ꝺ), `F` (barred F), `M`, `E` (square E), `T`, `B`, `p` (p-like).
- Others: `W` (ϖ / w), `O` (ɔ, reversed c), `Q` (φ, barred circle), `Φ` (larger looped φ, different from Q), `Z` (stroke
  followed by a lying 2, "-2-"), `A` (triangle Δ), `δ` (closed delta), `J` (long ʃ / ʄ), `V` (∧, mostly with a superscript 0),
  `α` (alpha with a cross stroke), `H` (two looped strokes crossed by a bar, "ℓℓ"), `qu` (a "qu"-like ligature).
- Doubled forms: a sign followed by `#` is the sign crossed by the double bar (`t#`, `n#`, `c#`, `δ#`).
- `^n`, `^m`: a small n or m written above (`D^n`, `T^m`, `F^n`).
- `.n.` = a number written between points (nomenclator). `x x x` on 674r r03 are struck roman numerals written in the cipher line.
- `END` = the closing marks of a cipher block (`Θ . d .`, `. D d .`, `. 0 . d .`, and similar).
- `/` = a slash written in the line. `{INS}` = the mark 0/10 in 674r r18, keyed to the line at the foot of the page (`r19ins`).
- `?` after a token = doubtful shape; `?` alone = an unidentified shape.
