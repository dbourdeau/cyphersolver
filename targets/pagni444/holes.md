## 6. Clear text

- `[?intendrà]`: "intendra" with a long final stroke; intenderà or intenderan. One reader.
- "raguagliandolo": the ending is read -lo; issue 426 printed "ragguagliandola".
- Dateline `El dj xx. m[...]`: one letter m under a bar that runs from the first x. No further letters in the writing ink. The month is not read.
