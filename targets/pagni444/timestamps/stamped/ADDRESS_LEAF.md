# Task 5: is c.318 the conjugate address leaf of c.315? (images: dl/asf444/, ASF iipsrv mdp_carteggio_universale/444/0634..0645)

Verdict: PROBABLE, not proved [measured on images; codicological inference derived].

Evidence for
1. Nesting pattern [measured]: the neighbours form nested bifolia. c.314r (Rasponi letter, Ravenna) pairs with c.319v (address to the Duke + chancery docket "Dal Cap.no Lodovico Rasponi da Ravenna l'ult.o di marzo 1555"); c.316r (Scrigni letter) pairs with c.317v (docket "Da Jac.o Scrigni Com.rio di Suvereto dell'ultimo di marzo 1554"). c.315 and c.318 sit in the matching middle position (314 / 315 / 316-317 / 318 / 319). Crops: crops/c314_319_sheet.jpg, crops/c317v_endorse.jpg, crops/c319v_addr.jpg.
2. Letter layout [measured]: c.315r-v carries all the text; c.318r is blank; c.318v carries only an outside address on the packet panel. That is the normal 1r-1v text / 2r blank / 2v address layout of a single bifolium letter.
3. Address [measured]: c.318v reads "Al my mag.co s.r il segr.o my / cristiano pagni my s.r / in florentia", with a paper-covered seal remnant on the right of the panel and two horizontal packet folds bounding it (crease rows near y 888-933 and 1472-1489 px). A stain on c.318r sits in the mirror position (seal through the leaf). Crops: crops/c318v_panel.jpg, crops/c318v_address.jpg.
4. No chancery docket on c.318v, unlike 317v and 319v: consistent with an anonymous cipher letter to the secretary (Pagni) rather than to the Duke [derived].
5. Hand [measured, weak]: the address and the plain opening line of c.315r ("Scrissi laltro hierj a v.s. ragguagliandola di quel che passava per", crops/c315r_plainline.jpg) are both small italic cursive with long-descender p; compatible, not proved the same hand. Spelling in the address ("my", "florentia") and the dateline ("el dj") is non-Tuscan, consistent with the cipher plaintext's "essare, intendare, biu, sabere" [measured spellings; no attribution drawn].
6. Foliation [measured]: both the old ink foliation (.311.-.316.) and the modern pencil foliation (314-319) run straight through, so neither series shows a missing or inserted leaf (crops/foliation_314_319.jpg).

Not established
- Physical conjugacy: the images are of single leaves; no image shows c.315 and c.318 joined at a fold or the binding guards. Creases on c.315 cannot be measured because the page is covered in text (scripts/folds.py gave only text-line minima).
- Paper: no watermark or chain-line match visible in these images.
- Test that would settle it: an archive check of the gathering (is 315/318 one sheet?), or a transmitted-light image of both leaves.
