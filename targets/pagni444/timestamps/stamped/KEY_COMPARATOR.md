# Task 6: key comparator, ASF Cifrari (Chiavi delle cifre del Carteggio di Cosimo I dal 1536 al 1573, F.III)

Verdict: the key register is NOT online; no contemporary key matching ours was found. Prior-art risk on the key stays OPEN [measured searches; sourced inventory notes].

1. Inventory wording [sourced: ASF inventory VII PDF, dl/inv/inv7.pdf, pdftotext]: filza 444 notes cite "A.S.F., Cifrari I Serie, Chiavi delle cifre del Carteggio di Cosimo I dal 1536 al 1573, F. III" for Francesco Babbi's cipher (letter of 18 Mar 1555 from Ferrara, c.113, partly in cipher with interlinear decipherment). Other notes in the same volume cite the same register as "Cifrari II serie" (filza 443 note 21: F. II, a cipher that does not match the one attributed; filza 445 note 52: F. III again for Babbi, decipherment at c.367). The series name is inconsistent inside the inventory.
2. Online status [measured]: ASF "Archivi digitali" fonds list (archiviodistatofirenze.cultura.gov.it/archividigitali/fondi/) holds, for Mediceo del Principato, only "Carteggio universale" and "Minute di lettere". No Cifrari series. One web search for the register title found no digitisation. An earlier Track B note (sweep5) already recorded "Key in ASF Cifrari II F. III, not online".
3. Practical comparator done instead [measured]: Babbi's own cipher leaves in the same filza are online and were downloaded (dl/babbi/: 444 c.113r-v, c.126r-v, c.127r-v). c.113r has a contemporary interlinear decipherment. Its system differs from ours: dots are written BELOW the digits, 0 is a frequent sign, and "questa notte" sits over "1 8 6 1 9 5 0 1 6 3 ..." (crops/babbi113_z1.jpg). Under our key "questa" would be 8 x 3 9 9' 1. So Babbi's F.III cipher is not our key.
4. Other published comparators (from the review, not re-checked here): Thomas Smith's dotted numerals and Argenti's 1585 dot-distinguished pairs are mechanism parallels only, not this key.

Recording rule: a contemporary key, if later found in F.III or elsewhere, would not make the plaintext reading NOT FIRST, but must be recorded as prior key material. Not checked: the F.III register itself (offline), Cifrari other fascicles, DECODE, MAP/BIA.
