# MdP 444 c.315r-v  clear-text portions (blind reading, letter by letter; (..) = expanded abbreviation, [..] = comment)

RECTO line 1 (wholly clear):
  scrissi laltro hieri a v. s. raguagliandolo di quel ch(e) passava por
  [letter notes: "ragua|gliandolo" written continuously, gl could be read "gh"; "ch" has a stroke through the h ascender = che;
   last word is p + "or" with p-stroke, i.e. por/per; folio numbers "315" (stamp) and ",312." (ink) stand above the line end]

RECTO line 2, clear opening before the cipher:
  acca~ co(n) esta~ inte(n)dra~ como
  [acca followed by a long terminal flourish; "co" with a tick above o; "esta" ends in a long drawn-out stroke;
   "intendra" has a suspension/long final stroke, so intendra(n)/intendera(n); then cipher starts with the d-like sign]

VERSO line 11, dateline (clear, below last cipher line, left side, x 440-640, y 1280-1350):
  e l d j   x x .  m~
  [letter by letter: e ; l with a foot ; open d (delta-form; could be an open 8) ; long j/i stroke ;
   x ; x ; low point ; m. A long horizontal bar starts above the first x and runs right over the m,
   and the last minim of m sweeps down to the right in a long tail. The month is therefore a single letter m
   with an abbreviation bar: no further month letters are written. Reading: "el dj xx m(...)" = day 20 of a month beginning with m.
   No year, no place, no signature visible on either image. Faint writing right of the m is show-through.]

Other clear marks: verso lower half is blank apart from show-through of the recto.
