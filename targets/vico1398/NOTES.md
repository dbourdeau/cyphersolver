# Fr. Pedro de Vico to Guillem Carbonell, 4 December 1398: one cipher line "de clave desconocida" (Puig y Puig 1920, app. XIX)

Status: attempted, open: the line was never printed and is not imaged; nothing to attack.

Split on 14 Oct 2026 from `benedict1399` under the one-key rule (George Lasry, 14 Oct 2026). Until then this line was
listed among the gaps of Benedict XIII's cipher letters to Climent, but Puig says it is in an unknown key, and it is not
Benedict's letter. Benedict XIII's letters are [`targets/benedict1399/`](../benedict1399/NOTES.md) (a different cipher,
with its own page). The work below was done in the benedict1399 sessions of 23 Sept 2026 and is carried over from its
NOTES.

## The document

- Arxiu Capitular de Barcelona, Climent's Schism papers (Puig's "Documentos inéditos del obispo Çapera"), not
  digitised; some of these papers were not returned after Puig studied them at home in 1920-30 (Baucells 1978).
- Fr. Pedro de Vico, Avignon, to Guillem Carbonell, canon of Barcelona, 4 Dec 1398. Puig y Puig, *Pedro de Luna*
  (1920), appendix XIX, p. 461, note 3: "Sigue una línea cifrada, de clave desconocida" (a ciphered line follows, in an
  unknown key). Puig did not print the line. The benedict1399 write-up linked it to secret instructions written on the
  back of the letter to look like a page of a missal.
