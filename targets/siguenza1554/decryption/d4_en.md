Things here are getting tighter, at least if what the Venetian ambassador writes comes true; but I do not know how they could come without a formed army in this kingdom.

(As for what Your Lordship tells me concerning) peace, there is nothing to say. … It is true. … to settle the matter of England; and what is most to be feared is the arriv[…].

For seventy thousand ducats a month will now be spent providing for Siena and for here, there being no way to assign a single ducat any more; (but nevertheless) I shall strive as much as I can to fulfil it.

If these excesses could be reduced it would be very good, and His Majesty ought not to look at what they deserve but at what their reduction could matter.

That he should need anoth[…]; he alone needs nobody.
