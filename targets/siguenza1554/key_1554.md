# Catalogue 855 (siguenza1554): the rebuilt key

The key is rebuilt from the decipherments of the time on letters 61/1-61/4 and 61/6, aligned sign by sign with
the cipher (transcriptions in `transcription/`). One key serves all five letters. The evidence for letter 6 is two lines only.

## How the cipher works

It is a mixed syllabary of the Spanish chancery type. There are four kinds of sign:

1. **A consonant sign plus a vowel mark.** The marks:

   This is a model that fits most signs, not a table found in the source. Several signs do not follow it.

   | mark | vowel |
   |---|---|
   | tail `ꝶ`, or `ſ` (`8ſ` ca, `5ſ` ba) | *a* |
   | bar through the sign, or bars on both sides | *e* |
   | dot above, or bar before | *i* |
   | plain, or tail below | *o* |
   | small circle `°` | *u* |

2. **Signs for a whole syllable or short word:** `c` *que*, `o-o` *no*, `ʒ` *yo*, `A` *po*, `◻` *tra*, `ẋ` *pli*,
   `ċ`/`g` *qui*.
3. **Single letters:** `tt` *e*, `Ω`/`oʒ` *n*, `h` *r*, `b`/`t`/`ȯ` *s*, `M` *a*, `4` *y*.
4. **Code groups** for frequent words, which take a plural *-s*: `3z` *dinero*, `43` *ducado*, `43b` *ducados*,
   `ιos` *su Mag.d* and `ιoz` *su Santidad*, and numbers (`42` *duque*, `36` *duque de Florencia*, `25`
   *(ca)vallos*) in the sibling letters of the Real Biblioteca.

Dots set off groups; they are not word dividers.

## Values

Status:
- **confirmed**: three or more places in at least two letters;
- **seen twice**: two places;
- **single**: one place;
- **ambiguous**: the sign as transcribed takes two or more values. These are probably look-alikes that the
  online scan does not separate.

the reading, which comes from the decipherments of the time.

## Evidence from the sibling letters

Pacheco's letters of January-March 1554 in the Real Biblioteca (II/2251; `siblings/rb2251/`) use the same key. Before
they were read, the key built from the BNE letters gave a value to 86% of the first 590 of their 626 cipher signs. Those decodes
followed the decipherments of the time over long runs. The values marked "siblings" above come from them. With
these values the key gives a value to 97% of the sibling signs (608 of 626).

## Note on the a-tail

In the person's check against the image (letter 2, C1-C3; `profile.json` `outcome.human_check`), the tail of `∇ꝶ`
(*ha*) looked f-like (ſ) rather than p-like. The *a* tail is written `ꝶ` in some names here and `ſ` in others (`8ſ`
*ca*, `5ſ` *ba*). These are one mark under two conventional names, and the values are unchanged. `aꝶ̶` (*ll*) is a
struck p.

## Signs without a value

`f`, `ffoꝶ`, `f̈`, `m`, `ova`, `°`, `ŧ`, `ʳ`, `⊳`, `ꝗ̈`, `ꝶ̃`: 14 tokens of 887. Each is met once or twice where the
alignment with the decipherment does not fix a value. `°` (start of A1) may be a null (the `ı` of A2 turned out to
be a comma); `ŧ` stands where *de* is needed in E2 (*hay de podello*) but where nothing is needed in L1 and L2.
