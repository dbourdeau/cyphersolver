# Translation (CMXV.17.1695.1-2, J. González, Marfa, 5 May 1911)

Nemi reports the goods sent [by] Captain Lewis seized. Send the invoices [with] José Pineda to claim them back. You should call Lewis. Await a letter.

J. González.

The Spanish is the decipherment of the time, typed in the list CMXV.18.1795.1, entry 113 (CEHM scan 21): "Nemi informa mercancías enviadas capitán Lewis decomisadas. Envíen facturas José Pineda para reclamarlas. Conviene llamen Lewis. Esperen carta." The four coded groups are the name José Pineda (the pencil gloss on the form; the Clave G.A. card gives josepinfda). The telegram leaves out its prepositions; the two in brackets follow the CEHM record 12896 ("enviadas por [?] Lewis", "facturas con José Pineda").
