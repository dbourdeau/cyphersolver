# Translation (CMXV.16.1561.7-13, 19 April 1911)

Dr wants Elías to come too. While the crisis lasts, go on using the old key, since it is quicker. Send funds and ammunition of all kinds at once.

González Garza.

"Parque" is the Mexican military word for ammunition and supplies. "The old key" is set against the new key of 18 April (CMXV.16.1551.3-4, `reading_grille.md`): the turning grille is slower to work than the Clave G.A. card.
