# Targeted scan of ff. 119r, 119v, 120r (canvases 249–251) for the opening's context values

What was checked: every line of all three pages, read in half-page bands at full resolution. I searched only for the
20 numbers whose values in the opening were fixed from context, plus the structural values and nulls. I did not
transcribe the pages.

## Structure and nulls

- **Structure:** 36 = p (f. 119v), which fits the descending alphabet 28–50. 377 = pi (f. 120r), which fits the
  p-block 375–379.
- **Nulls:** 199, 200, 205, 207, 209, 210, 225, 226, 229, 230, 232 and 250 are unglossed wherever they occur. That
  includes 207 and 230, which the opening treats as nulls.

## Date of the mémoire

- The last line of f. 120r is `337 543 519` with the gloss "le 5me mars 1649". **No. 43 is dated 5 March 1649.**
  That is the day after the Rueil conference opened (4 March), which fits the opening's reference to "la
  conférence que le parlement a accordée".
- In the same way, `560 518` on f. 119v is glossed "xxij feburier" (22 February). So the 500s are a date and
  number series.

## Result

- Three of the 20 context values are now confirmed independently by the contemporary decipherer, and none is
  contradicted.
- The other 17 do not occur on ff. 117v–120r.
- The structural values and the null range held wherever they could be tested.
