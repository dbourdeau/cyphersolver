# Who are "Ludouic" (nos. 24, 60) and "Launan/Launay" (no. 60)? (11 Nov 2026)

Result: **not identified.** Four web searches (standard mode) found no "sieur/capitaine Ludovic" or "sieur de Launay"
tied to Sens, Balagny, Champagne or Nevers in 1589-90. A negative web result says little for names this common.

What the letters themselves give [V on our transcription]:
- No. 24 l. 8-9: "comme il ne seroit possible d'avoir la capitulation non entretenue a Sens, et la verité du faict de Ludouic".
  So the Ludouic affair is linked to a capitulation at Sens that was not kept.
- No. 60 l. 1: "Ce que je vous ay escript du s[ieu]r Ludouic … est vray": the writer had already reported on him.
- No. 60 l. 5: "en est aussi de Launan", gloss possibly "-ay".

Context [I, from search results, not from primary sources]: the royal army besieged Sens after Ivry (March-April 1590),
and Sens stayed with the League until 1594. A "capitulation" there in spring 1590 would fit that siege.

Leads for a later pass (not done): L'Estoile's journal and the Mémoires de Nevers for April-June 1590; local histories of
the siege of Sens in 1590; the Nevers papers in fr. 3623-3625 around nos. 24-60 for other mentions of Ludovic.
The name could also be a code word chosen to hide someone; nothing here decides that.
