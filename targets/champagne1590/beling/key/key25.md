# Nevers key no. 25 (BnF fr. 3995 f. 51r, endorsed October 1589), as transcribed by Michael Brackx (issue #96, 24 Oct 2026)

Tomokiyo, cryptiana nevers.htm: "no.25 (fol.50) (October 1589) ... Substitution by two-digit figures. Some homophones. Nulls ... Arabic figures 1-99 with an overbar represent names and words." f. 50r is the numbered wrapper, f. 51r the sheet. Only the alphabet, the null list and six barred entries are transcribed; the rest of the nomenclator is not.

## The table

The alphabet is homophonic: several different signs may give the same letter. Each listed sign has a fixed value.
The key writes the u/v class as u. Letter case, punctuation and word division in the reading are editorial.

Only six selected barred entries are transcribed here; this is not the complete nomenclator.
In particular, plain 12, 19 and 89 are nulls. The key does **not** support assigning de to 12.
The [de] in the edition's union clause is an explicitly marked supply at the clear/cipher junction.
