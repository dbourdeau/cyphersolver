BLIND TRANSCRIPTION: Gerlach, Occulta scripta chymica (Wellcome MS.309), four pages

You are given 4 page images (scan0131.jpg, scan0132.jpg, scan0133.jpg, scan0143.jpg; record the page number the scribe wrote
at the top corner) and SHEET.md, a sheet of sign labels with shape descriptions. The sheet deliberately gives no
letter values. Do not look anything up, and do not try to decipher, guess words or "correct" signs to make sense.
Write what is on the page, sign by sign.

TASK
Transcribe every cipher line on every page into one tab-separated file, BLIND.tsv, using only the labels in
SHEET.md and the conventions in its "How to write them" section.

BLIND.tsv FORMAT (UTF-8, tab-separated, one manuscript line per row, header row first)
page	line	tokens	clear_before	notes
- page: the scribe's page number as written on the page.
- line: L01, L02 ... counted from the top of the written block on that page; count each written line, including
  short or partly blank ones.
- tokens: the labels for that line, separated by single spaces; word divider written " , "; "=" at line end for a
  run-on word; "?" after a doubtful label; "#" for a sign not on the sheet; "[...]" for illegible or damaged parts.
- clear_before: any ordinary-script (Latin-letter) word, initials or number the scribe wrote just before or beside
  this line (headings, margin numbers such as 1, 2, 3, initials), copied exactly as written; empty if none.
  Clear text inside the line goes in tokens within { }.
- notes: optional; describe any "#" sign, damage, larger-hand headings, or a line you are unsure belongs to the block.

Example row (made-up labels, not from these pages):
138	L01	SQ DOT , 4R? LAM PHI , FL =	{1}	heading in larger hand

RULES
- Work page by page, line by line; do not skip a line. If a page is short or has no cipher, say so in notes.
- Zoom in on the figure-4 signs and the s-like signs; they are easily confused.
- Do not edit BLIND.tsv after you hand it over. Corrections, if any, go in a separate file, listed one by one.
- Return BLIND.tsv and nothing else (plus a one-line note of anything you could not do).

Write BLIND.tsv in this folder (/tmp/blind_ger_fresh). Report its md5.
