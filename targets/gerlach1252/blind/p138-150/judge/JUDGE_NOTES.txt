GISTS (recipe level only)
p.138: Continues a distilling recipe. The spirit is left to settle, the receiver taken off and the water drawn into
  a flask under a still-head in a water bath. The watery part is driven off until the liquid shows sharpness, then
  it goes into a long-necked flask.
p.139: Ends the recipe. Sal ammoniac goes into the water, the glass is sealed and left to stand. The text says it
  improves with age, and that this makes 'aqua regis'. A new heading follows: dissolving gold (Hungarian gold) in
  aqua regis.
p.140: Gold-refining recipe: purifying gold with lead on a cupel in the fire until the gold is clean and bright,
  then taking it off the cupel. The text breaks off as the next step starts.
p.150: Ends a recipe for a 'precipitate' made with spirit of wine, called a noble medicine. A new heading follows:
  how to multiply this precipitate, by grinding it together with 'mercurius solis' (mercury of gold).
