UNIT RULES
- 1 unit = 1 decoded letter. '?' (doubtful mark) is not a unit; the letter before it is judged as written.
- '#' or '_' = 1 unit, always N.
- Text in { } (B A M D etc.) is clear script: excluded.
- ';' '.' ',' ':' '=' and spaces are not units. ':' and '=' at line end are taken as word continuation; split words are judged as one word, each fragment's letters counted on its own line.
- S = letter in a word that reads as sense in 16th-c. German (period spellings accepted: u for v, ck, tz, doubled consonants, syncopated g- for ge-, gfes, golt, lem, mol, holttz, achtt) or Latin chemical term.
- E = wrong letter where the word is still clear with a one-letter slip. A needed letter that is missing is charged as E on the letter next to the gap. Extra inserted letter = E.
- Strictness cap: a word gets S/E credit only if its slips are <=1 (words of 1-4 letters), <=2 (5-9 letters), <=3 (10+ letters). Above the cap, the whole word is N (e.g. coce/coren 'roren' 4 letters 2 slips = N; ferc 'feur' = N; nrc 'nur' = N; cerecbecir = N; walgnbrc|gisch = N).
- Unreadable fragments (doc, gcg, gc, nen, zirgeuhs) = N.

RECURRING SLIPS (decoded letter in E positions, code tally, total 263)
c 126, r 60, g 29, n 9, l 9, e 8, u 6, s 5, a 4, o/i/w/t/d/b/m 1 each.
- c for r (126): the dominant slip, nearly always on a c marked '?': dec(n)=der(n), andecn=andern, gcad=grad, reuecbecir=reverberir, core/coce=rore, ferc=feur. Looks like one cipher sign assigned c that should be r. Fixing it alone would lift most E and much N.
- r for u (60): arf=auf, mrs=mus, rmb=umb, zrndet=zundet, ars=aus, qrecksilber=quecksilber, trt=tut, nrn=nun. Second key-level confusion (u read as r).
- g for d/k (29): gn/gm=den/dem, galck/golben/gonnet/gomnet=kalck/kolben/konnet/kommet, gein/glein=kein/klein. The g? sign is overloaded (g, d, k).
- smaller: n for m (gomnet), l for u/g/k (daralf, lelet, lleinen), r for v (ron, rom, reuec... initial), e for p (seiritus, efen).
Most N is caused by c/r and r/u slips stacking in short words (coce, ferc, nrc, arc, arr), not by unreadable stretches. Only two '#' units in the whole set (p.170 L02).
