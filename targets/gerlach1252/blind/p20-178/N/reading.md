## Conventions

- Margin: page.line (e.g. 161.01). Line breaks as in the decode.
- A word split at a line end (decode mark `=` or `:`, or no mark where the two fragments plainly make one word) is joined and printed on the first line; the footnote gives the decoded fragments where it matters. Splits after a stop (`.`) are left as decoded.
- ⟨word⟩ = corrected word. Corrected only where every judged slip in the word is one the judge's notes name (c for r; r for u or v; g for d or k; n for m; l for u, g or k; e for p) and the correction is obvious. The decoded form is in the page's footnotes.
- A word with a judged slip that is not a named slip, or not obvious, is kept as decoded and footnoted.
- [?] = word judged no sense (all letters N) or containing an unknown sign (#). Decoded letters in the footnote.
- {...} {clear} = clear-script capitals, kept as written.
- Period spelling kept (u for v, ck, tz, gfes, golt, mol, lem, achtt).
- Footnote markers are [1], [2] ... after the word.
