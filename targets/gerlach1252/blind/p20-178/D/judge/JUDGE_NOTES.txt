UNIT RULES
- Unit = one decoded sign. Letters a-z count 1 each; '#' and '_' count 1 each and are always N.
- '?' is a doubtful flag on the preceding letter: the letter is judged, the '?' itself is not counted.
- Punctuation ( = ; . , ) and spaces are not counted. Nothing in { } occurs in this decode (0 units excluded).
- Words broken over a line (with or without '=') are judged as the joined word; each fragment scored on its own line.
- S: letter of a word that reads as 16th-c. German / Latin chemical sense, period spellings allowed
  (u for v, ck, th, dt, nim, golt, hot = hat, schisen, kule, ampos, granalir, olitet, aquositet, semisch, gerederten = sieved).
  Run-together words (undlas, nimobge, merckdas ...) are split and count S; dittographies (der der, mit mit) count S.
- E: a letter that is wrong where the target word is clear: a single substitution (q for w, d for z ...),
  or an inserted letter (uenedisch[n]em, grad[b], du[e], grad[a][s]). An omission has no letter to mark,
  so one adjacent letter of the word is charged E (allerhoch[s]en, s[e]iget, ich[s], ha[s]u, dur[c], sub[i]le, [a]us);
  two omissions charge two letters (hinder[s][e]lige, uiui[c][i]ret).
- N: unknown signs, and every letter of a word whose target is not clear or which has 3+ defects:
  !drnme (p.20 L07, 5), !uol (p.23 L15, 3), !dun?amen (p.24 L24, 7).
- judge.tsv shows each line in markup: [x] = E letter, !word = all letters N, #/_ = N, all other letters S.
  The script asserted that stripping the markup gives back exactly the signs of decode.txt on every one of 244 lines.
