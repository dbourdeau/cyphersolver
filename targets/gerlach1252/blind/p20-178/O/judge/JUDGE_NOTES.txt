UNIT RULES
- One unit = one decoded letter. '#' or '_' = one unit, always N.
- '?' after a letter: the letter is judged; '?' itself is not a unit.
- Not units: spaces, ',', ';', '.', ':', '=' (line-break hyphen), and anything in { } (clear script BAMD signatures; 6 rows carry them).
- Words broken across lines ('sp=' / 'iritus') are judged as the whole word; each fragment's letters are counted on its own row.
- S = letter in a word that reads as sense in 16th-c. German (period spellings, dialect vowels such as hot/domit/molen, doubled
  consonants, Latin chemical terms such as spiritibus, balneum marie, caput mortuum, putrefaction, oleum vitrioli allowed).
- E = letter that is wrong against the evident word but plausibly a one-letter slip (e.g. 'gommen' for kommen: g = E).
  Two swapped letters = 2 E (uire for vier). An extra letter that is not a period spelling = E (komptt, furr, entt).
- N = letter with no sense (only the two unknown '#' signs).
- Dropped letters cannot be marked (no letter present); the letters present were judged S. Listed below so the
  reader can see what was let through: flesig (fleissig), wei (weis), renen x2 (reinen), auh (auch), besamen (beisamen),
  zippele (zipperle), wasersuct (wassersucht), gebrauhen (gebrauchen), g=machten (gemachten), ode (oder), draf (drauf),
  gle=nen (kleinen: g = E, missing i not counted), filtra= at the foot of p.175 run 1 (verb ending not on the page).
- Page 175 appears twice (scribe's numbering). judge.tsv keeps both runs in decode.txt order; totals below call the
  second run 'p.175(2nd)' (decode.txt rows 136-158, after p.174a).
- Every row's annotated letters were checked by code to equal the decode letters exactly (assert passed for all 207 rows).
  Segmented reading column: word|MARKS gives per-letter marks for any word that is not all S.
