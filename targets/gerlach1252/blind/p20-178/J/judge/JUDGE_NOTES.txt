UNIT RULES
- 1 unit = 1 decoded letter a-z, or 1 unknown sign '_'/'#' (always N). '?' dropped, letter judged. No { } clear text occurred.
- Divider/hyphen marks (',' spaces, '=', ';', '.', ':') are not units. Words split across lines ('=' / '-') judged as the whole word.
- S = letter in a word that reads as sense (16th-c. German or Latin; period spellings allowed: hot=hat, dorfet, uerterben,
  drot, iha=ja, gelucg, procedir, geleichwol, zue, u/v and i/j interchange, y for i).
- 'cg' is taken as the transcriber's rendering of ck (wercg, mercget, wenigcg, bestendigcg) and scored S.
- A letter missing (gewich, fila, cra, penetran, einzugehe, dmit, sclagen, uollentem, leisigste) does not by itself make
  the letters present wrong: the present letters are S. Only a wrong letter costs a unit.
- E = one wrong letter inside an otherwise sensible word (substitution or one inserted letter, e.g. ala[n]s, spa[r]girum).
- N = letters with no sense, or words needing two or more corrections at once.
- Notation in judge.tsv: [x] = E letter, <x> = N letters, all other letters S.
- Catchword 'ge' at foot of p.93 (followed by 'geleichwol' on p.94) scored S.
