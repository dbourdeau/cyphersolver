## p. 120

Take this oil or wax, put it into a vial [*uiola*: a glass flask], and pour on it, four fingers' breadth deep above it, some *spiritus vini* [spirit of wine, alcohol] that has been sharpened three times with calcined and [?] [decoded *geflo#enen*, probably *geflossenen*, "melted"] tartar [*weinstein*: wine-stone, crude cream of tartar].

Note with great care that you always fill your vials only one quarter full, so that three parts of the vial always stay empty. So you must fill all vials only to the fourth part with all the things you want to putrefy [*putrificiren*: digest by slow rotting in gentle heat], and not go beyond the fourth part, or the vials will all burst apart on you. So do it in such a way that three parts of the vial always stay empty.

Latin: *spiritu uini* (spirit of wine)

## p. 121

*Sigillum Hermetis* [the seal of Hermes; heading]

That is, how to seal a vial at the top by melting, to set it in putrefaction. Now take the vial and set it on a little ring [*krentzlein*: a ring stand], and let the neck of the vial pass up through a potsherd or crucible. Lay a little piece of glass on top over the small opening of the vial, so that no spark from the fire can fall into the vial. Make, quite gently, a circle of fire round the tip of the neck of the vial. Then increase the fire, all gently, until the neck of the vial begins to melt. Then have ready a glowing pair of shears with which to press the neck

Latin: *sigillum hermetis* (seal of Hermes)
