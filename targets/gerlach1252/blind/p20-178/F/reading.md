## Conventions

- Line numbers in the margin are page.line of the decode. Line breaks are kept. A word broken over a line end is joined and given in full on the line where it starts.
- ⟨word⟩ = a letter slip corrected. Only slips the judge's notes name (as a recurring slip, a listed word or a tallied single slip) and whose correction is obvious are corrected. Each correction is listed under its page with the decoded form.
- [?] = an unknown sign (# or _ in the decode) or letters judged to make no sense; the decoded letters are in the notes.

- {…}{clear} = text written in clear script in the manuscript, not in cipher.
- The decode's doubtful-sign flags (?) and word-divider punctuation are not shown; the letter is printed as decoded. Period spelling is kept (u for v, ck, dt, th, single and double consonants).
