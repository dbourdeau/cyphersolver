## Conventions

- Faithful rather than smooth. Words in square brackets are ours: either a word the sense needs, or a short note. Figures in round brackets are the German line numbers.
- [?] = the German is unread or unsure at that point (see the footnotes in reading.md). Where a word was printed as decoded with a judged slip, the probable sense is translated and the decoded form is given in brackets.
- Latin passages are translated in the running text; the Latin itself is given in italics in the notes under each page, as it stands in reading.md (with the corrections marked there).
- "Sol" and "solis" mean gold, as usual in alchemy. "Mercurius solis" = the mercury of gold.

## p.56

(56.01-56.20) operation of this art, namely sublimation, that is the destruction, which happens by putrefaction: from a corrupted and unsightly thing, that is from a body or ash which now cannot move, but lies as if as nothing and as dead, to make a more glorious being and thing, which is then more fitted for the tincture than its body [would be] if it remained undestroyed in its coarseness.
