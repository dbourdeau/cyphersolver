Write what is on the page, sign by sign. You may make zoomed crops of the images for yourself (e.g. with Python PIL)

TASK
Transcribe every cipher line on every page into one tab-separated file, BLIND.tsv, using only the labels in
SHEET.md and the conventions in its "How to write them" section.

BLIND.tsv FORMAT (UTF-8, tab-separated, one manuscript line per row, header row first)
page	line	tokens	clear_before	notes
- page: the scribe's page number as written at the top corner of the page. Some pages carry a small letter after the
  number (e.g. "148a"): copy it exactly, with the letter.
- line: L01, L02 ... counted from the top of the written block on that page; count each written line, including
  short or partly blank ones.
- tokens: the labels for that line, separated by single spaces; word divider written " , "; "=" at line end for a
  run-on word; "?" after a doubtful label; "#" for a sign not on the sheet; "[...]" for illegible or damaged parts.
- clear_before: any ordinary-script (Latin-letter) word, initials or number the scribe wrote just before or beside
  this line (headings, margin numbers or notes, initials), copied exactly as written; empty if none.
  Clear text inside the line goes in tokens within { }.
- notes: optional; describe any "#" sign, damage, larger-hand headings, or a line you are unsure belongs to the block.
  Ignore writing that shows through from the facing page at the image edges.

Example row (made-up labels, not from these pages):
20	L01	SQ DOT , 4R? LAM PHI , FL =	{1}	heading in larger hand
