UNIT RULES
- One unit = one decoded letter a-z, or one '_' / '#' (unknown sign, always N).
- '?' after a letter: '?' itself not counted; the letter is judged on its merits.
- Text in { } (clear-script B A M D) excluded. Punctuation (; . : =) not counted.
- Words re-segmented by the judge; words split across lines are judged as whole words, letters counted on their own line.
- S = letter in a word that reads as 16th-c. German / Latin chemical term (period spellings allowed: u for v, ck/gk endings,
  gemolet/gemalet, mol, oell, uir, ihar, erbeiten, bodem, lem, nim).
- E = the wrong letter in an otherwise sense word (substitution or one extra letter). Two wrong letters in one word = 2 E.
- N = letter with no sense. Missing letters (renes, greiniget, a-lenthalben, m-chen, ren, wer-g-n) have no unit to score
  and are not counted; they are listed below.
- Long-s convention: the decode writes 'f' where the word needs s / ss / sz (difem, weifen, lafet, daf, wafer, gifet, mufet,
  dafelbige, frefen, schlifet, ingresuf, ingrfus ...). It is consistent across all pages, so it is scored S as the
  decode's rendering of long-s / sz. 30 such letters. Sensitivity figure given below.
- Page-opening fragment 'lewege' (p.151a L01, tail of allewege) scored S.
- Marking in judge.tsv column 3: [x] = E letter, <x> = N unit, ^f = f read as long-s (S).
