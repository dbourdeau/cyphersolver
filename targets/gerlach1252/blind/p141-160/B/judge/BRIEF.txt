decode.txt: one decode (no edits) of 9 cipher pages (pp.151, 152, 153, 154, 155, 157a, 148a, 149a, 150a) of Johann Gerlach's 'Occulta scripta chymica' (Wellcome MS.309), 16th-century Early New High German alchemical recipes. Each row: page and line, then decoded letters; word breaks are the transcriber's ',' dividers turned to spaces, so approximate; '?' after a letter = doubtful sign (judge the letter); '_' or '#' = unknown sign (1 unit, N); text in { } is clear script, not cipher (exclude it from the counts).
Task (strict judge, one pass, no second tries): segment into words and mark each letter S (part of a word that reads as sense in 16th-c. German, period spellings and Latin chemical terms allowed), E (wrong but plausibly a one-letter slip), N (no sense). Write judge.tsv (page, line, segmented reading, S, E, N). Totals PER PAGE and overall, strict share S/(S+E+N) with arithmetic. Then a 3-line gist per page, describing recipes only at the level 'a recipe for X' (no quantities, no how-to). Write judge.tsv and JUDGE_NOTES.txt (unit rules, totals, recurring slips, gists) in this folder. Do not open any other file.
