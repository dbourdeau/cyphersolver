GISTS (recipe level only)
p.124: Close of a preceding mercury operation with thanks to God and "ewigkeit", BAMD sign-off. Then the "first process" on mercury of the sun, beginning with sal ammoniac. Refers to the "great book".
p.125: Continues the sal ammoniac preparation for use in aqua regis and putrefaction. Then a recipe for spiritus vini (spirit of wine) by distillation. Mentions flask, helm and receiver.
p.126: Continues the spirit of wine distillation: separating the true spirit from brandy-wine. Repeated distillations. Collecting the spirit.
p.127: Spirit of wine finished. Then a recipe for sharpening spirit of wine with calcined tartar (weinstein). Preparing the calcined tartar.
p.128: Tartar and spirit sealed in a long-necked viola and set to putrefy. Description of a furnace with holes in its base. Continues to p.129.
p.129: Further furnace construction detail (holes in the corners). Short page. No new recipe.
p.130: Construction of a kettle with a step, pipes and nested lids. Water-bath apparatus. Lids with finger-sized holes.
p.131: An oak cask fitted over the kettle. Iron-bound, with a perforated bottom. Kettle filled with water.
p.132: Cask seated on kettle; hay softened in warm water and packed in. A tripod set in the cask. Sealed viola placed on it.
p.133: Viola packed round with wet hay, cask closed with copper lids. Seal-and-stand instruction. Furnace fired.
p.134: Fire regime over days via the furnace holes. Cooling, opening the viola, pouring off the spirit. Fresh tartar added.
p.135: Putrefaction repeated three times with fresh tartar. Spirit sealed in a clean glass. Kept for the work.
p.136: The finished spirit is thick and brown "like oil"; the process is called balneum roris. Spent tartar discarded or turned into oil of tartar. BAMD sign-off.
p.137: Definition of aqua regis (dissolves gold) and aqua fortis (dissolves silver). Recipe for aqua regis from saltpetre and calcined Hungarian vitriol. Distilled as in the great book.
