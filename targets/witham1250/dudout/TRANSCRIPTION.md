## Witham, BM 1858,1116.5 — one face, gold wire

Images: two independent British Museum photographs, (a) the inscription detail reproduced on the BL blog
3 Aug 2015 ("image courtesy of the British Museum", 1186x761) and (b) BM collection image 00296785 (1000x760).

Positions numbered on the corrected string NDXOXCHWDNCHDXORVI: #10 = N, #11 = C (in the BL string they are
the R and G at the same places). Letter forms #3, #4, #6, #10, #11 are those of the Fyris 74 / Karlstad /
W 1830 / St-Omer workshop group described by Wagner et al. 2009 (pp. 24, 30-32, fn 74, 81) — a palaeographic
link already suggested by van Hasselt (BL 2015) for X and the IS ligature, here extended to O, C and round N.

Effect: every reading that uses "R(e)g(em)" or "DRGHD" (Winter 2016 "D(efendit) R(e)g(em) H(onorem)", the
"DRoGHeDa", "Schwert-träger" and Wodan proposals) depends on two misread letters.

## Alphen, Provincial Depot Zuid-Holland 8888 — two faces, silver

Only images reachable: Madoc 2014 afb. 1 (72 ppi, 332 px wide strip) and Worley & Wagner 2013 picture 1
(details of SCS, DIC, BENE). Both far too small to re-read the 79 letters; they confirm the SCS with a
curled abbreviation mark over C, DIC and BENE. Daniel's text (van Hasselt's drawing) is kept unchanged:

A  +BENEDOXOFTISSCSDRRISCDICECMTINIUSCSDNI+
B  +DIOXMTINIUSESDIOMTINIUSCSDICCCMTDICII7ISI+   (7 = Z-like sign)

Uncertain (from van Hasselt's own remarks and the forum variants): the signs carrying abbreviation marks
(SCS, possibly DNI at the end of A), the "7", the IS ligatures (I and S may be one sign: IS = Iesus).
A re-reading needs the Restaura report or the Provincial Depot photographs (not online).
