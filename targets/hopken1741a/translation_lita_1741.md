# Translation: "Lit. A", French memorandum on the last Russo-Ottoman treaty, 1741

English of reading_lita_1741.md (notes as there).

The more one looks at and examines the last treaty of peace made between the Porte and the Muscovites, the more one
finds in it not only conditions onerous and unbearable for the Sultan, but also (the cipher has "couvre" for
"découvre") manifest contradictions, and that every means was sought to deceive the Ottoman Porte, with which the
Muscovites dealt, with the Sultan, not as with an equal prince but as with an inferior prince, and on whom they meant
to impose laws.
