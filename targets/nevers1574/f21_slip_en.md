# BnF fr. 3315 f. 21r, the cipher slip over Birago's letter: English translation

Translated from `f21_slip_reading.md`, sentence for sentence. 14 Nov 2026, Claude (session of 14 Nov 2026).

You shall also be told that it is the sieur Camille Fera who told me that the King had heard that you had given the warning of this little league to the Queen, and that he had it from a very good source, and above all did not wish to be named, which he begged of me very earnestly.

I must also tell you that when I said to the Queen that, when governments came to fall vacant, Monsieur de Nemours would have the first, she told me no, and that he must not count on it, because the King and she had resolved that you should have the first one vacant, whichever it might be.

And as far as I can tell, they fear to displease you. Ask boldly for whatever you want: there is no doubt that you will carry it, whatever it may be, since they foresee well that they cannot do without you.
