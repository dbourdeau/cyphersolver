# BnF fr. 3315 f. 21r, the cipher slip over Birago's letter: decryption

Decoded sign for sign from `f21_slip_signs.tsv` with the alphabet of `f15_key.md` (the cipher of the duchess of
Nevers's letter on ff. 15-16), 14 Nov 2026, Claude (session of 14 Nov 2026). One word per cipher word, line for line.
Where the sign is ambiguous (`+` = f or m, `9` = q or s) the letter is the one the contemporary French interlinear
decipherment under the cipher line gives. `[Roy]`, `[Royne]` are the name signs; `{sur tout}` is written in clear on
the cipher line. `?` marks a word with a doubtful sign. The text is French, not Birago's Italian: the slip is a
separate cipher note in the duchess's cipher, pasted on f. 21.

## Decryption

1	vous serez aussi adverty que cest le sieur camille
2	fera qui ma adverti que le Roy avoit entendu que vous
3	aviez donne ladvertissement de ceste petite ligue ala
4	Royne et quil le tenoit de fort bonne part et sur tout
5	ne voulloit estre allegue dont il me pria bien fort
6	il fault que ie vous die aussi que lors que ie dit
7	ala Royne que quant il viendroit des gondernemens a
8	vacquer monsieur de nemours auroit le premier
9	elle me dit que non et quil ne failloit pas quil si a
10	attendit parce que le Roy et elle avoient resoluz
11	que vous auriez le premier vaccant quel quil
12	fust et ace que ie peulz cognoistre ilz craignent
13	de vous mescontanter advisez hardiment de deman
14	der ce que vous vouldez il ni a nulle doubte que
15	vous ne lemportez quoy que ce soit prevoiant
16	bien quilz ne se peuvent passer de vous

Doubtful signs: l. 4 `F4?` (et), l. 7 `bF9?` and `...F29?` (des gouvernemens; the last sign of each could be `4`),
l. 12 `9?` in congnoistre and `7?` in craignent, l. 14 the middle of vouldrez (signs struck through and rewritten).
None changes a word against the interlinear decipherment.
l. 7 `082bFT2F+F29` decodes letter for letter as "gondernemens"; the interlinear has gouvernemen(t/s), and the same
spelling of the signs stands for gouvernement on f. 15 (`f15_key.md`), so it is the writer's habit, kept as written.
l. 9 ends with an `a` (doubtful: cipher sign or clear letter) and l. 10 begins `144F2b74` attendit, so the a is
written twice or belongs to the interlinear; the reading has one.
