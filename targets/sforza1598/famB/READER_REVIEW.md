Deciphered here with one key recovered from the ciphertext alone (`keyB2.json`); see `../NOTES.md`.

**What the reviewer is asked to judge:** whether each text reads as sentences, and whether the English matches the Italian.
Mark anything that does not. The project counts a letter as read when 95% of its words read as sense.

**How to read the Italian below**
- The letters are the decryption exactly as it comes out, with one exception: `u` and `v` are one sign in the cipher, written `v`
  in the decryption and `u` or `v` here as the word needs.
- Word division, capitals and punctuation are added here. The cipher has none.
- `[?]` marks letters that do not read. `[...]` marks text lost to paper damage. `{ }` marks a reading I am unsure of.
- The English follows the Italian closely. Glosses in ( ) are mine.

Spelling is the writer's: Firensa/Firense (Firenze), cum (con), scripto (scritto), sarvane (?), Sarsane (Sarzana), and so on.

## R5562 (cart. 1598 no. 88), 5 December

> Fui a Firensa de prosimo, et avisai Nicodemo de tuto quelo che intesi per tenire al signore. Trovai Iovan Galeasmo in quela cita,
> venuto a cercare partiti cum Fiorentini, facendo soldati, o cum lo signore; le dimande sue sono grande, e cum lui era uno
> {ma}miglio di l'arcivescovo mandato per sentire di qua. {Si s'gain} desiderio et espectatione di cose nove; ma se pur al signor
> parese di prendere alcun partito cum meser Ludovic{o}, advertite che sua signoria el facia obligare per Tomasino, el quale intende
> temp{er}are le cose de Corsica in li primi movimenti de importantia chi a{c}cadano. A Firensa trovai le cose molto inviluppate;
> non ne scrivo altro, essendo la Nicodemo, como e il quale ancho avisai de uno ambasiatore venitiano chi secundo hebi da Iovan
> Galeasmo doveva venir a far pratiche a Firanse e poi andare a Roma. Sar{vane}, die quinto decembris, etcetera.

*I was in Florence recently, and I informed Nicodemo of everything I heard, to pass on to the lord. I found Iovan Galeasmo in that
city, come to look for arrangements with the Florentines, raising soldiers, or with the lord; his demands are great, and with him
was a {servant} of the archbishop, sent to sound things out here. [?] desire and expectation of new things; but if the lord should
nevertheless see fit to make some arrangement with messer Ludovico, take care that his lordship has it guaranteed through Tomasino,
who means to temper the affairs of Corsica at the first important movements that occur. In Florence I found things very much
tangled; I write nothing more of it, since Nicodemo is there, whom I also informed of a Venetian ambassador who, according to what I
had from Iovan Galeasmo, was to come to conduct negotiations in Florence and then go to Rome. [Place?], 5 December, etc.*

Resolved 15 Nov by the confirmed look-alike pairs (5/S, M/N, c/D): "Fui a", "inviluppate", "hebi da", "far". Open: "{mamiglio}" (probably famiglio), "Si s'gain", and the place "sarvane" (probably Sarsane).

## R5576 (cart. 1598 no. 102), 7 June

> Non vi ho scripto de la venuta de Iohan Galeasmo in questa terra, perche non era al fine. Ma como ho inteso, per vivere cum manco
> spesa, meser Ludovico ha avuto da Firensa {pro} e contra circa il suo dover esser del Consiglio ducale. Mi pare che, posendolo
> haver cum utile e stasi di qua, se ne contentaria. Uno doctore mio parente, venuto heri da Firensa, mi diceva haver inteso da uno
> residente in quela cita per il duca di Modena che el conte Guido Rangone per venir presto di qua, mandato dal suo duca predicto,
> dubito de qualche trama. Sarsane, die septe iunii, etcetera.

*I have not written to you about the arrival of Iohan Galeasmo in this place, because it was not concluded. But as I have heard, in
order to live at less expense, messer Ludovico has had from Florence {arguments} for and against his being of the ducal Council. It
seems to me that, if he could have it with profit and stay here, he would be content. A doctor, a relative of mine, who came from
Florence yesterday, told me he had heard from a resident in that city for the duke of Modena that count Guido Rangone [is] to come
here soon, sent by his said duke; I suspect some plot. Sarzana, 7 June, etc.*

## R5577 (cart. 1598 no. 103), 17 July

> De qua non acade altro al presente, se non che meser Ludovico e Tomasino sono in molte diferentie, et ogni volta piu cresce l'odio e
> la difidentia fra loro. El podesta de Ponsano, el meso del quale io usava in mandare le letere al comisario, non le vole piu mandare,
> si che, se non vi ho scripto, peso non vi miraviglate. Io andero questo septembre a Volterra per observare la fede, ma finito l'oficio
> tornero; che cosi vo promesso a meser Ludovico. Io bisogno de ducati quaranta per espedire certi mei strecti bisogni; vogliate pregare
> Madona et el Signore che io ne sia servito, e quando non se podesse, ad ogni modo intendo essere bon servidore de le vostre
> illustrisime signorie. Sarsane, die decimoseptimo iulii, etcetera.

*Nothing else happens here at present, except that messer Ludovico and Tomasino are at great odds, and every time the hatred and
distrust between them grows. The podestà of Ponsano, whose messenger I used to send the letters to the commissioner, will not send
them any more, so if I have not written to you, do not be surprised. I shall go to Volterra this September to keep faith, but when the
office is done I shall come back; for so I have promised messer Ludovico. I need forty ducats to settle certain pressing needs of mine;
please ask Madonna and the Lord that I be granted it, and if it cannot be, in any case I mean to be a good servant of your most
illustrious lordships. Sarzana, 17 July, etc.*

Resolved: the digit-like signs 6, 10 are null marks before a doubled letter ("Volterra", "podesse"). Open: "Ponsano" not identified.

## R5575 (cart. 1598 no. 101), 20 February

> Respondo a la vostra ricevuta heri. Nicodemo ha sempre confortato meser Ludovico circa la specialita del signore, ma ben gli ha
> scripto e mi ha mandato a dire che certi mesani chi sono tra el signore e lui sono casone che le sue cose non {mas}cano bene. Meser
> Ludovico per tali avisi e stato molti di in suspecto de li soi, e spesso mi mostrava la letera de Nicodemo; il perche, vedendolo io in
> tal fantasia, ordinai che mi fusse mandata la electione di la cancelaria di Volterra, la quale acceptai non per voglia che io ne havesse,
> ma per estinguere de mi ogni sinistro umbramento; e cosi e succeduto. El tempo non comincia fino a octobre, ne ancho anora gli andero, se
> me ne potero valere; tratanto seguiro l'ordine nostro cum Nicodemo. Non mi pare habiate a fare altra provisione, perche mi pare haver
> tolta via ogni rugine et a meser Ludovico; e mi incresce che io habia acceptato dicto oficio; nondimeno fate como vi pare meglio.
> Iovan Galeaso mi disse questi di a Firensa haver havuto letera da Iovan Coscia che volese mandare uno de li soi al duca Iovani per bona
> casone, e che gli voleva mandare uno Piero del Cerreto, famiglio di l'arcivescovo, {coqesum}, intrinsico e pratico di la lingua e del
> paese. Sarsane, die vigesimo februarii, etcetera.

*I reply to yours received yesterday. Nicodemo has always encouraged messer Ludovico concerning the lord's favour, but he has {written}
to him, and sent word to me, that certain go-betweens who stand between the lord and him are the reason his affairs do not go well.
Because of such reports messer Ludovico has for many days been suspicious of his own people, and he often showed me Nicodemo's
letter; so, seeing him in such a mood, I arranged for the appointment to the chancellorship of Volterra to be sent to me, which I
accepted not because I wanted it, but to remove from myself every hint of suspicion; and so it has turned out. The term does not
begin until October, nor shall I go there even then, if I can avoid it; meanwhile I shall follow our arrangement with Nicodemo. I do
not think you need make any other provision, because I think I have removed every rancour, also with messer Ludovico; and I regret
having accepted the said office; nevertheless do as you think best. Iovan Galeaso told me these days in Florence that he had had a
letter from Iovan Coscia, who wished to send one of his men to duke Iovani for a good reason, and that he wanted to send one Piero del
Cerreto, a servant of the archbishop, {?}, familiar with and practised in the language and the country. Sarzana, 20 February, etc.*

Open: "{mas}cano", "{coqesum}". ("ha scripto" resolved by the 5/S and c/D pairs.)

## Doubling marks (15 Nov 2026)
The sign o-with-cross (written `o +`) and the digit-like signs 6 and 10 are nulls set between doubled letters, the "dupple" habit of
Sforza keys: "el mes[o+]so", "Volter[6 10]ra", "Volter[o+]ra", "dis[o+]se", "podes[10 6]se". Removed from the decryption.

## Historical check (15 Nov 2026, not complete)
- Ludovico Sforza (il Moro) was exiled to Pisa, in Florentine territory, after the failed coup of May 1477, and returned to Milan in
  September 1479. Bona of Savoy governed for her son Gian Galeazzo; the letters' "Madona" and "el Signore" fit them.
- "messer Ieronimo" whose death is mentioned (R5586) fits Girolamo Olgiati, executed in January 1477 for the murder of Galeazzo Maria
  Sforza (not checked in the text beyond the name).
- Tommasino (da Campo)fregoso was lord of Sarzana and, later (1484), governor of Corsica; Milan held Corsica 1464-1481.
- Nicodemo Tranchedini wrote from Genoa in 1478 (DBI); a Nicodemo handling Genoese and Corsican affairs fits him.
- So the letters most likely date from June 1477 to March 1479, written by a Sforza informer at Sarzana close to Ludovico in exile.
  Not checked against the Milanese registers or Musso, "Il dominio sforzesco in Corsica (1464-1481)".
