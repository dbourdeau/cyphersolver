# R5523: decoded from ciphertext

- **DECODE:** record R5523, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5523.
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598, no. 49.
- **Ledger entry:** catalogue 2632, sign family E.
- **Pages:** page 1.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode is the plain output of my key on my rows. It has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands yet.
- **How the key was obtained:** from ciphertext. The key came from the cipher text alone. No key sheet and no clear text checks it.
- **Year, as a lead:** 1465 or before; most likely between 1455 and 1458. The rows give no year; `NOTES.md` gives the reason and the source.
- **One doubt:** On 34 of the 482 signs where my frozen read shows a letter (7.1%), my key gives another letter. 9.6% of the signs show no letter in my frozen read.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `transcription/`.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project; M. Héder and B. Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.
