- **DECODE:** record R5488, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5488. The key sheet: DECODE record R5347, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5347.
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598, no. 14.
- **Ledger entry:** catalogue 2628, sign family A.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode is the plain output of the key sheet's values on my rows. It has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands yet.
- **How the key was obtained:** from key: the archive's key sheet R5347. I read each value off the sheet and joined each sign of the page to a sign of the sheet. `key/` gives each value, its place on the sheet and my confidence.
- **One doubt:** I mark 7 of the 28 joined labels of page 1 as holding two written signs of the sheet. They carry 38.7% of its signs, so a sign of these labels can take the wrong one of two values.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `transcription/`.
