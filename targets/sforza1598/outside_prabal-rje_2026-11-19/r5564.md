- **DECODE:** record R5564, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5564.
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598, no. 90.
- **Ledger entry:** catalogue 2639, sign family L.
- **Pages:** page 1.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode is the plain output of my key on my rows. It has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands yet.
- **How the key was obtained:** from ciphertext. The key came from the cipher text alone. No key sheet and no clear text checks it.
- **Key not confirmed:** this page has, on average, under 9 signs for each label (503 signs, 64 labels). No key sheet and no clear passage checks its key.
- **Read together:** one key reads this page with R5565 page 1, R5582 page 1, R5583 page 1, R5584 page 1. My public page gives them as one text; `key/` files each page apart.
- **One doubt:** On 70 of the 426 signs where my frozen read shows a letter (16.4%), my key gives another letter. 15.3% of the signs show no letter in my frozen read. My public page marks this reading as less sure.
- **Rows:** a transcription of the DECODE images, in `transcription/`.
