- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598, no. 105.

- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode is the plain output of the key sheet's values on my rows. It has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands yet.

- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `transcription/`.
