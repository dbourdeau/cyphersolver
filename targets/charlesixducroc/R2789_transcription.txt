# Sign labels: the cipher is graphic signs, so each sign is written with a label taken from George Lasry's key table
#   of 25 May 2022 (image GL_CharlesIX.png on S. Tomokiyo's cryptiana GL.htm): the letter the sign stands for plus
#   its row in that letter's column (A1 A2 A3, B1 B2, ... top row = 1); CC FF LL MM NN RR SS = the doubled-letter
#   signs; JE QUE QUI QUEL VOUS DE ET POUR PAR TRES = his word signs; ESC = "La Royne d'Escosse?", ANGL = "La Royne
#   d'Angleterre", ROY = "Royaulme?", JACQUES = "Jacques?" (the square); U1..U9 = his nine "Unknown" signs, left to
#   right (U5 "fon", U7 "fur" as they look). The labels name signs; they are not a new reading of the key.
# '|' = a tall vertical stroke between signs (it mostly sets off a word sign). The small ticks above and below the
#   line are not recorded. '?' after a label = doubtful sign.
# Limits, stated plainly: some pairs of the key's signs could not be told apart by eye in this facsimile
#   (D1/O3, P1/F1/N2, E2/QUE, V4/LL/POUR, B1/S1, E3 and the stroke '|'); for these the label follows the one that
#   makes sense in the line, so those labels are not independent of the plaintext. Where a sign has the shape of D2
#   but the sense wants E it is written D2?. Strokes may be missed or misplaced.
