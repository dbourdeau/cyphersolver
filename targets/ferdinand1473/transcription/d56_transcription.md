# BNE MSS/20211/56, Ferdinand to John II, Dueñas 12 Nov 1470: transcription of the cipher (pass 1)

Source: BNE Digital bdh0000186631 (viewer id 2fba5fe4-9dcc-4ea9-a483-a3cbc6f03a7c), image 1 of 3, the "high"
PDF (1117 x 1485 px), read in the pdf.js viewer at 4x, checked at 5x and 6x. The cipher is the lower third of
the recto: 15 lines, each opening with a long dash, after clear text ending "... mia". Lines numbered C01-C15.

**Not the cipher of the 1473 letter (/64).** This one mixes one- and two-digit numbers (8-37, 90-93) with about
35 signs (planet signs, Greek letters, dotted letters). The 1473 cipher has no numbers.

A faint **pencil decipherment** (modern, grey) is written between the lines, one syllable or letter over or
under each sign (*ra*, *ve*, *ta*, *pre*, *de*, *lo*, *e*, *a*, *pne e* ...). At 1117 px it is legible only in
places, and is not transcribed here yet. A few **ink** corrections or notes are written over signs (C01 *a* and *e*
over `V s`; C04 *a* over the second `B`; C13 ...); they are marked `{...}`.
