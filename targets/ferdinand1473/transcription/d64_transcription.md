# BNE MSS/20211/64, Ferdinand to John II, Hostalric 5 June 1473: transcription of the cipher lines (pass 3: L03-L11 re-read at 8x, L07-L10 details at 12x)

Source: BNE Digital, bdh0000186643, image 1 (the "high" PDF, 1085 x 1187 px), read in the pdf.js viewer at 4x and 6x.
Lines are numbered as on the page (L01 = "Esta noche de pascua cinquagesma ..."). Clear words are in quotes;
`/` is a stroke the writer put between cipher words. `?` after a sign = uncertain reading.

Sign labels:

| label | shape |
|---|---|
| `p` | p with a long straight descender and a closed bowl (L03 *certificar*, L07 *cavallo*) |
| `po` | p with an open bowl and a hook or loop at the top left (L08 *algunos*, L09 *todos*, at 12x). The p of *ochocientos* (L10), *ciento* (L11) and *mios* (L04) looks plain at 12x, so the hook may be a variant of writing, not a separate sign |
| `1` | short upright stroke |
| `b` | b-like, bow open to the right |
| `m` | m |
| `h` | h-like, hooked (ƕ) |
| `q` | q / 9 with loop at the top and a tail |
| `qo` | q joined by a stroke to a small o (L04 *trayo* at 12x: what pass 1 read as `q o`) |
| `Q` | q with a straight descender and no loop (L03, *certificar*) |
| `b.` | b with a dot and a short stroke after it, one sign (L03) |
| `9` | open 9 / g with the tail curling left (distinct from `q`; checked at 8x on L10-L11) |
| `P` | ϙ: a closed ring on a long stem (L10-L11) |
| `Y` | open 4 / ч (L10, in *comigo*) |
| `0` | small squarish o (L10) |
| `-o-` | o on a horizontal stroke through it (L09 *viene*, L10 *iran*; read at 12x) |
| `o-` | o with a stroke to the right only (L10) |
| `ooo` | three rings joined (L10, *cavallo*) |
| `OO` | two filled rings on a bar (L11) |
| `B` | plain 3 without the tail of `3` (L11) |
| `C` | open c with a long tail down to the left (L05 only; read at 12x, earlier taken for `6`) |
| `3-` | ʒ with a stroke through the top (L04 *trayo*, L07 *ya*; read at 12x) |

| `FI` | long s and i joined, with a cross-bar (L08; one sign) |

| `T` | 6-like bowl with one cross-bar on the stem (ŧ) |
| `TT` | the same with two cross-bars |

The interlinear notes (another hand) are of two kinds: words written above the cipher (`W:`), and single
letters on a ruled guideline just above the cipher line (`G:`). Their horizontal placement is approximate.

```
L02 "p a V.S. e porque agora me puede mandar lo que acerca su servicio tengo"
L03 "de fazer acorde delo" p 1 b. m h Q a. p o T 4 d + 6 q 8 F m 1 / "que"
    W: certificar | dela | gente        G: ... a . s . d e . l . a . c(g) n t e
L04 "comigo" m T 2 3- qo / x O 1 / 3 oo T o "entre Los" a 6? p TT / p qo F
    W: trayo (over m T 2 3) | que (over x) | hora (over 3 oo) | e (over T) | mios? (over a 6 p TT; 6 blotted) | continos (over p qo F, with a stroke to L05)
L05 m h F 9 oo TT? / "e los que" 4 d / P 6 TT m a. + + p / P H C qo "viene"
    G (above m..T): . n . o . l l .      W: de (over 4) | castilla (over P 6 TT m) | mios? (over p H 6 qo, faint)
    G (below, above L06): a e a a n ... n n e n t a
L06 "e los" 4 8 2 T o q oo F a 6 TT 4 1 x # h F a. d F m p
    W: de aragon (over 4 8 2 T o q oo) | mas (over a 6 TT) | de quinientos (over 4 1 x # h F)
    G (above F a. d F m p): n i e n t o
L07 TT 4 8 p 2 O o + + qo? "Los valencianos" TT? oo F / 3- 6 o + + q
    W: de cavallo (over TT 4 8 p 2 O) | son (over T oo F) | ya algunos (over 3 6 o + + q, runs into L08)
L08 9 F po TT . "dellos llegados" 1 F FI / "e todos los otros" 3 h TT d T 2 F / "por toda" 8 TT m o
    W: en (over 1 F) | barçna (over FI: one sign, long s + i with a cross-bar; the note is the usual abbreviation of *Barcelona*, read at 12x) | hi seran (over 3 h TT d T 2 F) | juntos (L09, written under por toda)
L09 TT 1 H o F 2 / x O d / 9 -o- 8 F 1 / "en manera que" | m oo 4 po TT 6 # F m qo TT "esti..."
    W: semana (over TT 1 # o F 2) | que (over x) | viene (over O d / q o 8 F) | todos (over m oo 4 p)
L10 "mo fan los que" P oo a Y q oo / -o- T 2 F / p P 3 qo P h d F m oo TT 4 8 P 0 9 o- ooo oo
    W: comigo (over P oo a Y) | a struck word, then yran? (over -o- T 2 F) | ... | de cavallo (over 4 8 P 0 9)
L11 1 F x O d / 3 2 9 T o / P OO 8 F m p / 3 qo H B T 1 TT 4 d 2 T a o TT / "vea V.S. la for..."
    W: en | que | hauran | ... cent(struck) ... hombres | darmas
```

`TT?` = read with one cross-bar, but the sense needs *s*, which every other `TT` gives; the bars are at
the limit of the image.

Uncertain: whether `1`/`i`, `q`/`9`, `T`/`TT` and `o`/`0` are each one sign or two; the gloss placement
over L04 right and L06 right.
