# BNE MSS/20211/73 (1474): key aligned on the clear paragraph

`align1474.py` aligns the 310 signs of `transcription/d73_transcription.md` with the 325 letters of the clear
paragraph (`decryption/d73_clear.md`), each sign standing for 0, 1 or 2 letters, starting from a 19-sign seed
key read from the interlinear letters, and re-estimates the key until it stops changing (2 passes). Counts per
sign are in `decryption/d73_alignment.txt`. `dec1474.py` holds the resulting key.
