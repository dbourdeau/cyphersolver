# Lx   = the "ls" ligature with a horizontal bar crossing its looped stem (L02 after o^5; L07 with ^3; L11 last sign, doubtful).
# rs   = r whose stroke runs on into a long s-like tail below the line (L02, L17, L22, L24).
# Os   = theta-like oval (O) joined to a long s-like tail; its bar sticks out to the left (L09, L11, L12, L18, L22). Not struck through.
# ;    = small semicolon-like mark on the line (dot over a short tick), L01 after q^2.
# .7 / .3 = a dot standing at mid-height/baseline immediately LEFT of the sign (L01 .7, L02 .3); x. = dot under the x.
# d    = used for the b/6-shaped sign (round bowl, ascender rising to the left); I read all such signs as the table's d.
# O    = used for every theta-like oval (bar inside, not sticking out); O? where it may be a plain o.

# [?]  = L14: a heavy ink blot with a short tail on the line, between ms and L; may be a blotted sign.
