A person can only be considered a spy when acting clandestinely or on false pretences he obtains or endeavors to obtain information in the zone of operations of a belligerent with the intention of communicating it to a hostile party. Thus soldiers not wearing a disguise who having penetrated into the zone of operations of the hostile army for the purpose of obtaining information are not considered spies.
