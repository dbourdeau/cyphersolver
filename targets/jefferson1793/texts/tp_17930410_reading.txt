# Thomas Pinckney to Jefferson, London, 10 Apr 1793: the coded stretch as George Taylor Jr. deciphered it between the
# lines (M30 roll 2 frame 0083); clear words in {..}. Wording as in PTJ 25 (Jefferson/01-25-02-0487).
# Open point (NOTES.md): "will be again allotted" stands over four groups, 1257 583 956 1198. Taylor first wrote "will again
# allotted" and added "be" with a caret; 1257 583 is "will be" in the 5 Apr letter, and 956 (with a mark) is "all" there,
# so the code may say "will be allotted" and "again" may be Taylor's. Not settled; Taylor's word is kept.

{... as they contain intelligence of some importance.} The armament of this country both by sea and land continues with spirit. A division of the dominions of France is talked of in which the French W. Indies will be again allotted to Great Britain.
