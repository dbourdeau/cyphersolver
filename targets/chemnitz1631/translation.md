# Translation (English) — Chemnitz to Gustav II Adolf, 1631, cipher passages of images 55 and 56

Image 55: "...[the contribution ... he] should pay. From this town one could keep the enemy strongly on the alert, and now
and then also beat up one or another of the enemy's quarters; but the new troops are used neither to hard service nor
to the enemy, therefore nobody will advise that one should risk anything with them."

Image 56 (Nuremberg, 7 Dec. 1631): "At Ansbach there is said still to be a fair store of arms and fine guns; the enemy
will surely carry them away unless one forestalls him. That would however have to be done by force; for if one asked
the [Margrave's] government for it amicably, it would no doubt be betrayed to the enemy by disloyal servants."
