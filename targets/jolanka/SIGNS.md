# SIGNS: jolanka series, neutral shape labels

Labels describe shape only. Written before any solving. Suffix `+a` = one dot or short tick above the sign; `+aa` = two dots above.
`?` after a label = doubtful reading; `[.]` = one sign struck out, blotted or illegible; `[...]` = more than one; `{ }` = plain writing (letters or digits as written).

| label | shape | working shorthand in tx_short/ |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | one short horizontal bar | - |
| H2 | two stacked horizontal bars | = |
| H3 | three stacked horizontal bars | E |
| V1 | one upright stroke | 1 |
| V2 | two upright strokes side by side | 2 |
| V3 | three upright strokes side by side | 3 |
| GAM | upright with a bar to the right at the top (Greek capital gamma) | G |
| EFF | capital F | F |
| RC | half-circle open to the left (reversed C) | C |
| LC | small half-circle open to the right | c |
| ZIG | zigzag like a 5 or a small final sigma | Z |
| YOG | y-like or yogh-like sign with a tail below the line | y |
| TIL | horizontal wave (tilde) | N |
| EX | plain x | X |
| EXS | x with a long tail or a stroke through it (reads like a crossed x) | K |
| TEE | T, often lying on its side or upside down | T |
| ESS | S | S |
| ONE | caret or figure 1 with a long flag | A |
| LOOP | looped sign like a round d or a partial-derivative sign | D |
| SIX | figure 6 | 6 |
| EIGHT | figure 8 | 8 |
| OVAL | two facing arcs, like ( ) | O |
| CROSS | upright cross, like a plus or a small t | + |

Known weak points of this transcription (one reader, images at 3000 px width, no second pass):
- H2 against H3, and V2 against V3, are often hard to tell apart when written fast.
- `V1+a` covers a stroke with a short tick beside or above it; it may be two marks in places.
- TEE and CROSS may be one sign written two ways. YOG and YOG+a likewise differ only by the dot.
- Adjacent bar groups (for example V3 V2) are split by spacing alone.
