## 04r (Rome, 10 May)

Salviati has told me that his niece, wife of the Count of Santa Fiora, has written to him: if Your {Excellency} were thinking of marrying Donna Ippolita to the lord of Piombino, she would have the heart to conclude the match. Then Salviati told me that he would be glad if it were done, because it seems to him that this would be the preservation of that state of Piombino. I answered that I did not know whether Your {Excellency} had other plans in hand. So I let it pass without [giving him] any intention of writing of it to Your Excellency. Still, Salviati will hold for certain that I have written to you of it; it may be that {within} a few days he will ask me what you say of it. So I should like to know what I am to answer, if I am asked.
