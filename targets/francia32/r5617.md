# R5617: Filippo Sega, Paris, March 1591

My own transcription of the DECODE images. The decode uses A. Meister's printed key no. 65 (1906), as this ledger's `targets/sega1590/key.tsv` gives it. State: decoded, not read.

- **DECODE:** record R5617, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5617
- **Archive:** Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 32.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project.
  Héder and Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.
- **Date:** March 1591, in clear on f. 108v; the day and the last digit of the year are doubtful in my transcription.
- **Before this folder:** No reading of these pages is in this ledger.
- **What this page adds:** The rows and the decode of ff. 106v and 107r.
- **Cipher pages:** f. 106v (1 row, `transcription/R5617_f106v_digits.txt`); f. 107r (8 rows, `transcription/R5617_f107r_digits.txt`).
- **Counts** (this folder): 321 digit signs, of which 14 doubtful.
- **Units with a value in the key** (as counted on 17 November; `check.md`, section 2, gives the rule and its faults): .91 (145 of 160); by page: f. 106v .91, f. 107r .91.
- **Counts by the word rule:** not counted. The decode has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands. `README.md`, "The counts", says why.

## Summary

Words that seem to stand in the decode of f. 107r (my guess, in English): governed, merchants.

**Doubt:** F. 106v holds one row of 23 digit signs. It is too short to judge.
