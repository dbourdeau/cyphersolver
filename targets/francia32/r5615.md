# R5615: Filippo Sega, Paris, early January 1591 (the duplicate)

My own transcription of the DECODE images. The decode uses A. Meister's printed key no. 65 (1906), as this ledger's `targets/sega1590/key.tsv` gives it. State: decoded, not read.

- **DECODE:** record R5615, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5615
- **Archive:** Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 32.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project.
  Héder and Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.

- **Before this folder:** Brice Dudout read this letter in part, before me (`targets/sega1591b`, with the same key). His count: 1,319 cipher words, 1,091 read in their sense.
- **What this page adds:** A second transcription of the 11 cipher pages, made apart from his, and its decode. It adds no reading to his.

- **Units with a value in the key** (as counted on 17 November; `check.md`, section 2, gives the rule and its faults): .91 (4,193 of 4,612); by page: f. 49v .80, f. 50r .91, f. 50v .95, f. 51r .90, f. 51v .96, f. 52r .81, f. 52v .87, f. 53r .90, f. 53v .94, f. 54r .96, f. 54v .92.
- **Counts by the word rule:** not counted. The decode has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands. `README.md`, "The counts", says why.

## Summary

The duplicate of a long letter (ff. 49v to 54v), almost all in cipher.

**Doubt:** On f. 49v the transcription writes some vowel signs as raised marks; the decode shows `?` there. F. 49v gives the lowest share of units with a value (.80).
