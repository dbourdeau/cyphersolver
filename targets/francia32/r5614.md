# R5614: Filippo Sega, Paris, 9 January 1591

My own transcription of the DECODE images. The decode uses A. Meister's printed key no. 65 (1906), as this ledger's `targets/sega1590/key.tsv` gives it. State: decoded, not read.

- **DECODE:** record R5614, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5614
- **Archive:** Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 32.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project.
  Héder and Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.
- **Date:** 9 January 1591, in clear on f. 48v.
- **Before this folder:** No reading of these pages is in this ledger.
- **What this page adds:** The rows and the decode of ff. 47r and 48r.
- **Cipher pages:** f. 47r (12 rows, `transcription/R5614_f047r_digits.txt`); f. 48r (6 rows, `transcription/R5614_f048r_digits.txt`).
- **Counts** (this folder): 694 digit signs, of which 23 doubtful.
- **Units with a value in the key** (as counted on 17 November; `check.md`, section 2, gives the rule and its faults): .92 (344 of 374); by page: f. 47r .87, f. 48r .99.
- **Counts by the word rule:** not counted. The decode has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands. `README.md`, "The counts", says why.

## Summary

Words that seem to stand in the decode (my guess, in English): confusion, the soldiery, discredit, the politiques, the death of a chevalier, a return, Saint-Denis.

**Doubt:** On f. 47r, 28 of 211 units have no value in the key (.87), against 2 of 163 on f. 48r.
