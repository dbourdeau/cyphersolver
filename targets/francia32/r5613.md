My own transcription of the DECODE images. The decode uses A. Meister's printed key no. 65 (1906), as this ledger's `targets/sega1590/key.tsv` gives it. State: decoded, not read.

## Summary

Lignerac goes, on the order of Umena (Mayenne), to Poitou and Brittany. This content comes from the decipherment of the time (f. 46r) and from George Lasry's reading of the Paris copy. The decode alone does not read as sentences.

**Doubt:** Row C03 decodes to letters that form no Italian word for most of its length. The measure .854 counts matching letters; it does not test word sense.
