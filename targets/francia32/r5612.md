Transcription of the DECODE images. The decode uses A. Meister's printed key no. 65 (1906), as this ledger's `targets/sega1590/key.tsv` gives it. State: decoded, not read; cipher words not counted.

  Héder and Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.

- **What this page adds:** A second transcription of the same cipher rows, made apart from his, and its decode. It adds no reading to his.
- **Cipher pages:** f. 17r (14 rows, `transcription/R5612_f017r_digits.txt`); f. 17v (1 row, `transcription/R5612_f017v_digits.txt`).
- **Counts** (this folder): 573 digit signs, of which 16 doubtful.
- **Units with a value in the key** (as counted on 17 November; `check.md`, section 2, gives the rule and its faults): .97 (312 of 323); by page: f. 17r .97, f. 17v .90.
- **Counts by the word rule:** not counted. The decode has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands. `README.md`, "The counts", says why.

## Summary

The imprisoned bishop leans to the opposite party (Navarre's). Sega will temporise. This agrees with Brice Dudout's reading; the decode shows parts of it.

**Doubt:** Where Brice Dudout's rows give the code letter for 'della' (his row L03), my row C03 describes a P-shaped loop that the key does not hold.
