# R5621: Filippo Sega, Paris, 21 December 1590

My own transcription of the DECODE images. The decode uses A. Meister's printed key no. 65 (1906), as this ledger's `targets/sega1590/key.tsv` gives it. State: decoded, not read.

- **DECODE:** record R5621, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5621
- **Archive:** Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 32.

  Héder and Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.

- **Before this folder:** Brice Dudout made the held-out test of key no. 65 on this letter (`targets/sega1590`, 29 October 2026). In his test, 268 of 287 characters and 56 of 65 words agree with the decipherment of the time on f. 230r.
- **What this page adds:** A second transcription of the cipher, made apart from his, its decode, and a second measure on the same letter (`check.md`: .848).
- **Cipher pages:** f. 229v (8 rows, `transcription/R5621_f229v_digits.txt`); f. 231r (5 rows, `transcription/R5621_f231r_digits.txt`); f. 231v (1 row, `transcription/R5621_f231v_digits.txt`).
- **Counts** (this folder): 558 digit signs, of which 34 doubtful.
- **Units with a value in the key** (as counted on 17 November; `check.md`, section 2, gives the rule and its faults): .88 (263 of 298); by page: f. 229v .91, f. 231r .86, f. 231v .79.
- **Counts by the word rule:** not counted. The decode has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands. `README.md`, "The counts", says why.

## Summary

- The agreement at Tours may fail.
- La Châtre withdraws, on the pretext that Umena (Mayenne) did not keep his promises.
- The Duchess of Guise is angry about her son's release.

This content comes from the decipherment of the time on f. 230r, dated 26 March 1591. The decode alone does not read as sentences.

**Doubt:** The ledger names ff. 228v and 231 for this cipher. My page labels are 229v, 231r and 231v; they follow the image labels on DECODE.
