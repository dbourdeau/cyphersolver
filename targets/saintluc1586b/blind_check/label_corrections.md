# Label corrections from the blind plates of 25 Nov 2026 (f. 260r-v)

Added at the merge of PR #992 (integration review, for the maintainers). Michael Brackx's files are not edited:
`brackx2026/f260_cipher.txt`, `brackx2026/f260_decryption_lines.md`, `brackx2026/key_r3.json` and
`brackx2026/label_legend.md` keep his labels. Since 25 Nov 2026 the readings in `texts/` follow the plate answers at the
places below, so at these places the reading departs from his transcription and decode. This list is the record of it.

Places as in `README.md` here: r. = f. 260r, v. = f. 260v, line number, then the sign's rank in the line.

## Relabelled: the [?] mark dropped in the reading (plate A)

| place | word | old label (Brackx) | new label | plate answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r. 1 r7 | hier | `z` (decoded m) | `zc` (h) | plate A q02: `zc` |
| v. 20 r42 | leuee | `z3` (m) | `z` (u) | plate A q12: `z` |
| v. 20 r46 | du | `z3` (m) | `z` (u) | plate A q06: `z` |
| v. 23 r32 | guerre | `3` (m) | `z` (u) | plate A q08: `z` |
| v. 24 r47 | uos | `z3` (m) | `z` (u) | plate A q10: `z` |

`z` has four values in the key (u / v / h / m, `label_legend.md`). The plates chose the shape `z`; the value u among the
four is chosen by sense, as everywhere else in this reading. `zc` has the one value h. So the shape is settled by the plate,
and for the four `z` places the letter u is settled by sense. The README sentence "each with a single value" does not hold
for `z`.
