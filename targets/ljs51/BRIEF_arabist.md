# LJS 51 f. 106v: a brief for a reader of Mamluk Arabic verse

**What we need:** a reading of two lines of ṭawīl, recovered from a cipher. Most letters are known. The open
question is which words give sense and scan.

## Context

- **The manuscript.** University of Pennsylvania, LJS 51, a 14th-century Mamluk secretary's book of cipher verses
  exchanged with colleagues. It belongs to the circle of the Ibn Faḍl Allāh chancery, about 1330-1370.
- **The image.** f. 106v is the last page:
  https://openn.library.upenn.edu/Data/0001/ljs51/data/master/0084_0217.tif
- **What comes just before the cipher.** The compiler gives the plaintext of the previous cipher: «أفلج خصمي واحتج
  بحجتي / أفلحت في حجتي وخاب المحتج». He then adds: «والصواب أفلجت بجيم وليست أفلحت بالحاء المهملة وإنما تصحّف»,
  i.e. a jīm misread as ḥāʾ.
- **The cipher.** «وكتب لي ابن القبابي [؟] فيه» ("Ibn al-Qubābī wrote to me about it"), followed by the cipher. The
  compiler's solution was on the next leaf, which is lost.
- **So the text is a reply on that taṣḥīf.** Letters of one shape (ج ح خ) are «أمثال», look-alikes. On f. 17r
  (image 0033) the compiler describes a «تصحيفي» cipher in which one sign covers all the letters of one shape.

## The text as recovered (two bayts, ṭawīl, rhyme ‑ḍā)

Firm letters are written out. `?` is an unknown letter. `ا/ل` is one sign that stands for alif or lam (the compiler:
«جعل اللام ألفًا»). Word dividers can be one sign off.

```
bayt 1:  و ل/ا ل/ا ? ? ?   ? م ث ا ل   ك ا   م ي ل   ا/ل ل   ? و ا/ل   ه ? ? ?
         ه م ?   ك ا ن و ا   و م ا   ك ل ه ا   ا ي ض ا
bayt 2:  ? ه ي ?   و ا م ث ا ل ?   ه م ا/ل م   و ل ا   ا/ل ل ا
         و ل ا/ل ? ?   و ل ا   ل ا/ل ه ? ا   و ا م ث ا ل ه ا   ا ي ض ا
```

**Candidates that scan:**

> وَلِلْحُسْنِ (أو: وَلِلنّاسِ) أَمْثالٌ كَأَمْيَلَ لِلْهَوى … هَوانٌ هُما كانُوا وَما كُلُّها أَيْضا
> رَهِينٌ وَأَمْثالُهْ هُمامٌ وَلا إِلى … وَلانَتْ وَلا لَهْجًا (لِلْهِجا، لِلْهُدى) وَأَمْثالُها أَيْضا

- **Reads as sense:** «هما كانوا وما كلها أيضا», «وأمثالها أيضا», and the letter-play on أمثال and هوى/هوان.
- **Unread:** the opening half of each bayt, and «ولانت/ولاحت ولا لهجا».
- **The metre problem.** Bayt 2 has one syllable too many for ṭawīl unless one alif is a null. This circle used nulls
  («زاد واوا وألفا في الآخر لئلا يتضح»).

## Questions

1. Does a known reply verse on a jīm/ḥāʾ taṣḥīf, or on letters as «أمثال», fit this skeleton? Possible sources:
   Ibn al-Qubābī, al-Ṣafadī's taṣḥīf literature, or the Mamluk «ألغاز».
2. What is the best reading of the opening halves and of «و ل ا/ل ? ? | و ل ا | ل ا/ل ه ? ا» within ṭawīl?
3. Is «للهجا» (the alphabet) or «للهدى» likely in this context?
