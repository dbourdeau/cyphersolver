# Sign 2 in MAP LXXII doc. 192: shape criterion (written before the survey)

Gabbrielli's key no. 120 gives two 2-like consonant signs: b is a plain 2, c is "a z-like 2 with an angular foot".
Each occurrence of a 2-like sign carrying a superscript is put in one of three classes from its shape alone:

- Z: the top is a straight, roughly horizontal bar that meets the diagonal at a sharp corner, as in a printed Z or z.
- R: the top is a curved head, hook or arch with no corner at the upper right, as in a printed 2; the base may be a
  long flat or wavy stroke.
- D: doubtful. The top is blotted, cut, too small to judge, or two readers of the same crop disagree.

The class is taken from the top of the sign only. The length of the base, the superscript and the neighbouring signs
are not used. Key rule fixed in advance: R = b + vowel, Z = c + vowel, D keeps the token 2 and the key's value c.
A token is renamed only if both blind readers give R.
