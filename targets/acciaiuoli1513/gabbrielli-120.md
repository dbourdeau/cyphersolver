# Gabbrielli's key no. 120: Roberto Acciaioli, 1512–14

Transcribed on 20 November 2026 from DECODE record R5807 (`ASFi_SIVol1_120`, two images), accessed with the DECODE
project's permission; the images are not redistributed. The source is Pietro Domenico Gabbrielli's nineteenth-century
compilation of cipher alphabets for the correspondence of the Dieci di Balia, vol. I, Archivio di Stato di Firenze. The
page is headed *An. 1512-13-14. Cifra. 120. Acciaioli (de) Roberto. Carteg. de' X di Balìa, Filze n. 107. 108. 110. 113.
114. 115. 116. 117.* It is Gabbrielli's reconstruction from deciphered letters in those filze, not a chancery key sheet,
and the letter of 23 October 1513 lies outside the filze he lists. DECODE numbers the volume's keys R(5686 + N) by
catalogue order, but the images run one leaf behind: R5806 shows key 119 (Francesco Guicciardini) and R5807 shows key 120.

Signs are described in words where they cannot be typed. A raised figure is the vowel: 1 u, 2 o, 3 i, 4 e, 5 a.
