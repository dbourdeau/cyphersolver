# either by an enlarged crop of the sign itself or by a blind pass that read the sign that way without the key (among

# verify.py counts them. After the Codex review (codex-review.md) I checked on crops and adopted: facto and the
# double-g of passaggio at R04 and V04, the uncrossed Q (Qp) at R11, the crossed one at V14, the struck signs after
# M^n at R19, and the slashed circle (Ø) at V03, V04 and V06. Departures made only
# to get a word are not here: they are in 192-repairs.tsv. The independent blind passes are in blind/.
