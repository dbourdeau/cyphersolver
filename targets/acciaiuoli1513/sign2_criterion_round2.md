# Sign 2 in MAP LXXII doc. 192: second criterion (written 21 Nov 2026 after the first survey was joined to the readings)

The first criterion (criterion.md, top of the sign only, tight crops) did not separate the readings: both readers
gave R at 7 of 14 b-words and at 10 of 53 c-words. This second one uses the whole sign and its neighbours for scale.
It is written with the first result known, so it is not a first look. Bar fixed before the second survey: a token is
renamed only if both new readers give S, and only if both give S at no more than 3 of the 53 places read c. Otherwise
no token is renamed.

- S (squat 2): a low sign, clearly shorter than the figures beside it, with a small hooked or curled head and a long
  base stroke along the line; wider than it is tall.
- T (tall z): about as tall as the figures beside it, with a flat top bar, a diagonal and a base; a printed Z.
- D: doubtful.
