## The cipher (from the images, 20 Sept 2026)

Each group is a letter followed by one or two figures (`Z6 n54 m56 g42 n24 m62 Z11 n23 g62 m12 641 633 …`), in
numbered lines. The codebreaker wrote syllables above the groups, as on the other intercepts of the file (the crop
`rot/r704_a.jpg` of R704, used on the page, shows *dal … to ge … me … sa …*, *che me … por ta to … el propri*,
*do di … lo ti ri ma ne ra* over the groups; read off the image 14 Oct 2026, not aligned), so the plaintext is
Italian and his partial solution is on the page. Group counts are DECODE's, not measured here.
