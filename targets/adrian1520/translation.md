# English translation

“… and all the more {perhaps} this will give ‹…› to these [men], who rise not {only} {against} [Your] Majesty {but} also against all the grandees, from whom they {intend} {first} to {take away} all the {alcabalas}, {and then} all the {towns} {and} places which {lately} {were} of the royal {Crown}.”

## Justification of the probable, restored and unresolved words

- dara (certain): o̥o = d, 9 = a, ꞡ = r, cf = a, all firm values of key K7; “esto dará”.
- {solamente}: ded. = “sola”, a single attestation, followed by mud. = -mente (certain).
- {contra} (l. 2): vuh. = con and ε̥ = t certain, then a ⊥ with o below that is not in the key, and 4 = a: “cont‹?›a”.
- {mas}: nah. = mas, a single attestation.
- {entienden}: zu. = “entende”, a single attestation (PTR,LEG,2,1,73, “da allá en que entender”), + & = n.
- {primeramente}: p r i ‹?› e r a + mud. (-mente); the 4th sign (ʆ) is r or t in the key, not m.
- {alcavalas}: 8 of 9 signs have firm values; the 3rd (z with a stroke above) is not attested for c.
- {despues}: su. = des (firm), then gad. = pues, a single attestation.
- {y} (l. 5): the sign n = y has a single attestation.
- {poco} {tiempo}: hag. and cu., one attestation each, in the letter of 15 March 1521 (PTR,LEG,2,1,68) and its contemporary transcription (2,1,69): “hay pocos o ningunos que en este tiempo quieran comprar”.
- {fueron}: qec. = “fue”, a single attestation (2,1,73, “ha dias que fue a Burgos”), + r, o, n.
- {corona}: 5 of 6 signs certain; the key gives e for the 1st (ð), read c (shape close to θ = c); sense fixed by “real”.
- [tales]: “a estos ‹?›ales”; -ales certain, “estos tales” a common phrase; the first sign is an uninventoried shape.
- [vuestra]: “contra ‹q m.› mu.”; mu. = magestad (certain), elsewhere always preceded by a “vuestra” group (cim.); the two signs before it are outside the key.
- {Quiça} (9 Nov 2026, key K7b): the first word, tt Fx m mo th 4. In the cipher sheet fol. 434 (PTR,LEG,2,1,100) the pair tt + Fx (tt followed by the looped long s with a small o) is q six times (quitome p. 1 l. 21, quise p. 2 l. 17, quitaria p. 2 l. 36, quiero p. 2 l. 38, quatro p. 3 l. 4, aqui p. 3 l. 20), against Danvila’s printed plain text (MHE XXXVIII pp. 547-549); the shape was compared on the images (2,1,19 l. 1 against “quatro”). m = u, mo = i, th = c (firm in K7), 4 = a. Probable, not certain: the 5th sign is θ/ð, and ð = e would give “quiea”. “quanto mas quiça esto dara …” (“all the more perhaps this will give …”).
- {quitar} (9 Nov 2026, key K7b; replaces [juntar]): Qx dho Z q 4 gr. The 1st sign (“an odd g” in Brice Dudout’s re-reading) has the same shape as the barred q with an S-shaped tail of “quitaciones” in fol. 434 (p. 1 l. 26, one attestation: “1x:q” in K7b). dho = u (one attestation in K7), q = t, 4 = a, gr = r (as in the 2 Nov labels). Z is n in K7, but i in fol. 434 (“diga”, p. 1 l. 2), and the shape is ambiguous i/c/n in the sign atlas; “q u n t a r” is no word. “primeramente quitar todas las alcavalas y despues todas las villas”: to take from the grandees the alcabalas and the towns, the Comuneros’ demand, fits better than “juntar” (“gather”).
- {villas} (9 Nov 2026, key K7b): oto ‹ti› b g9 p (line 5 of transcription.txt). The sign transcribed “ti” is the Ꝉ-shaped Li = i (firm in K7; the same shape as in “publicamente”, fol. 434 p. 1 l. 2). The plain b has the shape of the b = ll of “allego” (fol. 434 p. 1 l. 16, one attestation); the barred b (ƀ) of fol. 434 is z (haziendas, four times), a different sign, so K7’s b = z is split in K7b. u i ll a s = “villas” (u for v), with “villas y lugares”.
- Unresolved: the group fud., without value in K7 and absent from fol. 434 (where ful. = secreta is attested twice; fud. is not). The phrase “de poco tiempo en fueron” is awkward; the group zi. = en is certain.
