# Sign conventions: fuerstenberg1659 (W 004 Nr. 52, images 85-86)

The cipher passages are written in ordinary lower-case Latin script letters (a round, upright hand, set apart from
the German current hand of the plain text). Each cipher sign is transcribed as the Latin letter it looks like.
No values are implied by these names.

- `a b c d e f g h i k l m n o p q r s t u w x y z`: the letter shapes as written.
- `f`: a long-stem letter with a cross-bar (looks like an italic f; not a long s).
- `s`: the short round s; no long s was seen in the cipher runs.
- `k`: an upright k with a looped bow (written like a capital K reduced to letter size).
- `w`: the double-v shape; `v` is not used for it.
- `i`: a short stroke with a dot; `j` was not seen.
- Spaces in the transcription are line breaks or the writer's visible gaps only; they carry no meaning.
- `?` after a letter: uncertain reading of that letter. `[?]`: a sign not read.
- Struck or corrected signs: the final intended letter is given; corrections are noted in brackets.
- Line labels: `85.1` = image 85, first cipher line, and so on.
- The marginal marks (crosses and double strokes) beside the cipher runs are not part of the cipher; they flag the runs.
