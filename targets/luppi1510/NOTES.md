# "Luppius tuus" (Florentine chancery) -> Niccolo Machiavelli at the French court, letter wholly in cipher, [14 August] 1510 (catalogue 1549)

Status: no write-up (found already solved: printed deciphered by Fachard, Edizione Nazionale, Legazioni VI (2011); nothing added here beyond the identification of the print and the corrections of sender and date)

## What the document is
BNCF, Carte Machiavelli I/3, 2 ff. ("La lettera e interamente in cifra"): a letter to Machiavelli, then on his
third mission to France, from a Florentine chancery correspondent who signs "Luppius tuus". The date line in clear
gives the day and the year only (Florentiae xiiij 1510). [*Lettere*, II, p. 815, on the mission](https://machiavelli.sputnikdns.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lettere_Tomo-2.pdf#page=255)

## What is known (20 Nov 2026)
- **Printed deciphered.** Denis Fachard, ed., *Legazioni. Commissarie. Scritti di governo*, VI (Rome, 2011),
  document 269, pp. 490-492, prints the Italian and cites I/3, c. 1r and its attached copy. The note on p. 571 explains
  the asterisks around passages the editors deciphered. The edition supplies the month: "[14 agosto 1510]" (p. 492
  n. 28), so **the month is editorial**: the manuscript has day 14 and the year. Tommasini (1911) had guessed July.
  Paragraph 19 keeps two possible readings in the edition's note.
- **Sender.** Tommasini called the writer a chancery clerk. In the 2011 commentary, Emanuele Cutinelli-Rèndina
  linked the signature to the writer of 17 August, who signed “Vester amicus”, and asked whether he also wrote
  the letter of 29 August signed “Compater vester”. He gave no name. [Doc. 269, pp. 492–493, n. 29](https://portalemachiavelli.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Legazioni-Scritti-di-governo_Tomo-6.pdf#page=501)
- **Published attribution.** Marcello Simonetta identifies the hand of the 17 August letter (no. 203) as that of
  **ser Giovanni di Salvatore da Poppi** by comparison with his signed letter no. 178. In no. 203 Giovanni says he
  wrote three days earlier. His report about the pope's claim to have an agreement at his disposal echoes I/3.
  Simonetta therefore identifies I/3 as the likely earlier letter and suggests that its “Luppius” was a cipher error
  for “Puppius”. Plates 6–8 show the comparison manuscripts for nos. 178, 203 and 208. [Simonetta in Bausi, ed., *Lettere* (2022), II, pp. 834–836, no. 203, nn. 1, 5](https://machiavelli.sputnikdns.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lettere_Tomo-2.pdf#page=274) · [Plates identified in III, p. 2010](https://machiavelli.sputnikdns.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lettere_Tomo-3.pdf#page=718) · [Editorial credits, I, p. cxxix](https://machiavelli.sputnikdns.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lettere_Tomo-1.pdf#page=131)
- **Where to read I/3.** Its writer says the Dieci lacked time to write before the courier left. Simonetta calls
  it an official letter and refers readers to Fachard's 2011 text. The 2022 index lists no separate letter for
  14 August. [Fachard, p. 490, §4 and n. 3](https://portalemachiavelli.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Legazioni-Scritti-di-governo_Tomo-6.pdf#page=499) · [*Lettere*, II, p. 848](https://machiavelli.sputnikdns.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lettere_Tomo-2.pdf#page=288) · [III, p. 2001](https://machiavelli.sputnikdns.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lettere_Tomo-3.pdf#page=709)
- **Earlier notes.** Tommasini, *La vita e gli scritti di Niccolo Machiavelli*, II (1911), Appendice, pp. 1352-1353:
  "tutta in cifra, senza decifrato", with quoted passages (Andrew Aymeloglu found and posted these, issue #590, 14 Nov
  2026). The Fachard edition was identified by Andrew Beling (PR #804, 19 Nov 2026); his own account says AI agents did
  the work and no person has checked it. The 19 November record relied on the PR's citation and the catalogue. On 20 November,
  AI agents compared the two printed editions for Andrew Beling.
- **What this project did.** Vetted the lead on the BNCF images (29 Oct - 2 Nov 2026, one sheet per side, about 26
  and 6 lines of cipher, no gloss on the leaf), corrected the sender (14 Nov 2026), and removed the entry from the
  catalogue (19 Nov 2026). No transcription of the cipher, no key, no decipherment of ours.

## Not done, and why
No key was recovered and the printed text was not aligned with the signs: the project's priorities put a target whose
work would be transcribing an existing decipherment last (README Conventions), and the print covers the whole letter.
The sibling letters of 1510 (IV/111, V/61, V/67, V/123, I/81) are glossed in the same collection.

## Remaining gaps
- Alignment of Fachard's text with the signs of I/3 (and with the attached copy he cites): not attempted; the edition's
  asterisked passages show where the editors deciphered rather than read a gloss. Blocker: none outside the project's
  priorities; reuse terms of the BNCF images not checked.
- Giovanni da Poppi is the published candidate for the writer of I/3. The proposed error in the signature remains conjectural.
