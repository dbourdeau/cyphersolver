# "Luppius tuus" (Florentine chancery) -> Niccolo Machiavelli at the French court, letter wholly in cipher, [14 August] 1510 (catalogue 1549)

Status: no write-up (found already solved: printed deciphered by Fachard, Edizione Nazionale, Legazioni VI (2011); nothing added here beyond the identification of the print and the corrections of sender and date)

## What the document is
BNCF, Carte Machiavelli I/3, 2 ff. ("La lettera e interamente in cifra"): a private letter to Machiavelli, then on his
second mission to Louis XII, from a Florentine chancery correspondent who signs "Luppius tuus". The date line in clear
gives the day and the year only (Florentiae xiiij 1510).

## What is known (19 Nov 2026)
- **Printed deciphered.** Denis Fachard, ed., *Legazioni. Commissarie. Scritti di governo*, VI (Rome, 2011),
  document 269, pp. 490-492, prints the Italian and cites I/3, c. 1r and its attached copy. The note on p. 571 explains
  the asterisks around passages the editors deciphered. The edition supplies the month: "[14 agosto 1510]" (p. 492
  n. 28), so **the month is editorial**: the manuscript has day 14 and the year. Tommasini (1911) had guessed July.
  Paragraph 19 keeps two possible readings in the edition's note.
- **Sender.** Not the Dieci di Balia, as the BNCF record first suggested, and not identified beyond the signature
  "Luppius tuus": Tommasini calls him a clerk of the chancery ("addetto alla Cancelleria") of whom nothing else
  survives; Fachard leaves the correspondent unidentified.
- **Earlier notes.** Tommasini, *La vita e gli scritti di Niccolo Machiavelli*, II (1911), Appendice, pp. 1352-1353:
  "tutta in cifra, senza decifrato", with quoted passages (Andrew Aymeloglu found and posted these, issue #590, 14 Nov
  2026). The Fachard edition was identified by Andrew Beling (PR #804, 19 Nov 2026); his own account says AI agents did
  the work and no person has checked it. Checked here only against the PR's citation and the catalogue record, not on
  the printed pages (the edition PDF at portalemachiavelli.it was not read in this session).
- **What this project did.** Vetted the lead on the BNCF images (29 Oct - 2 Nov 2026, one sheet per side, about 26
  and 6 lines of cipher, no gloss on the leaf), corrected the sender (14 Nov 2026), and removed the entry from the
  catalogue (19 Nov 2026). No transcription of the cipher, no key, no decipherment of ours.

## Not done, and why
No key was recovered and the printed text was not aligned with the signs: the project's priorities put a target whose
work would be transcribing an existing decipherment last (README Conventions), and the print covers the whole letter.
The sibling letters of 1510 (IV/111, V/61, V/67, V/123, I/81) are glossed in the same collection.

## Remaining gaps
- Alignment of Fachard's text with the signs of I/3 (and with the attached copy he cites): not attempted; the edition's
  asterisked passages show where the editors deciphered rather than read a gloss. Blocker: none outside the project's
  priorities; reuse terms of the BNCF images not checked.
- Identity of "Luppius tuus": open in the edition itself.

## Escalation
Not run: the target is closed as printed; the escalation steps of the writeup skill (siblings, known keys, key
rebuild) would only reproduce a reading that is in print.
