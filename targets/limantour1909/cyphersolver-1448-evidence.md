This packet responds to Daniel Bourdeau's request for the two 1909 telegram plaintexts and the **numbered transcriptions** of the two letters. It supplements, rather than replaces, the earlier GitHub attachments (TRANSCRIPTION.md, READING.md, WORKLOG.md).

Contents:

**Source records:** CEHM 118140, 118185, 118144, 118230 (links inside individual files). Original source scans are **not redistributed**, to respect the archival holder's terms; the text is based on research transcriptions, not a new original-image examination.

# Two plaintext-control telegrams (15 and 23 July 1909)

**Researcher:** Alberto Garvía García. **Target:** Cyphersolver catalogue 1448 / GitHub issue #245.
**Published research:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23934776

This file transcribes the **plaintext readings**, not a complete diplomatic transcription of the cipher lines. It is based on the supplied reproducible Work research report; the original CEHM images should be consulted for paleographic verification. Numbers and letter alignments below are copied from that report, not generated by guessing missing groups.

## Telegram of 15 July 1909

- **Archive:** CEHM, Fondo DLI, **DLI.39.7677.1**, catalogue record **118140**.
- **Source:** https://www.cehm.org.mx/Buscador/FichaBasica/118140?Tipo=A
- **Evidence:** Cipher with a handwritten plaintext expansion beneath.

**Transcribed plaintext (as reported):**

> Sr. Presidente enemigo declarado aplaza aclaración. Ruégote esperes carta mía.

The initial code `79` is faint and provisionally aligned to «Sr. Presidente». The cipher for ENEMIGO is less legible and was **not counted** as one of the strict 28 direct control mappings.

## Telegram of 23 July 1909

- **Archive:** CEHM, Fondo DLI, **DLI.39.7696.1**, catalogue record **118185**.
- **Source:** https://www.cehm.org.mx/Buscador/FichaBasica/118185?Tipo=A
- **Evidence:** Cipher with a typed plaintext transcription beneath.

**Transcribed typed plaintext (as reported):**

> Indispensable Dr. Gómez vaya; hémoslo resuelto. Tiene graves cosas que comunicarte. Avisaré salida. Sírvete suspender hasta entonces contestaciones á Clubs, colectivas ó separadas.

**Source discrepancy:** the handwritten wording before `SALIDA` appears to read «anunciaré», while the typed reading says «Avisaré». The two are **not asserted to be a verbatim diplomatic match**. For the strict correspondence count, only the clearer numeric sequences below were used. The VAYA/AYA segment was treated conservatively as provisional because of a watermark: the readable suffix `15 60 17` gives `AYA`, but its unique assignment `60=Y` is excluded from the strict correspondence count.

# Main letter of 15 July 1909: diplomatic transcription and numbered cipher tokens

**Archive:** CEHM DLI.39.7680.2, record 118144.
**Source:** https://www.cehm.org.mx/Buscador/FichaBasica/118144?Tipo=A
**Researcher:** Alberto Garvía García. **Publication:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23934776

The diplomatic transcription below reproduces the work report's revised reading. **It is not a certified paleographic transcription.** Numbers in the original are separated by punctuation; the `(A)`–`(L)` labels and global token numbers used later are editorial, not written by the historical author.

## Transcription, original letters and cipher numbers

```text

HABITACION:
5ª Calle del Naranjo 123.

Sr. Gral. D. Bernardo Reyes,

Alternative readings for a handful of other digits are recorded in the research data and do not justify silently changing the diplomatic text.

The 77 total numeric tokens comprise 75 alphabetic-position tokens plus two special codes, 77 and 79.

# Related note of 29 July 1909: diplomatic transcription and numbered cipher tokens

**Archive:** CEHM DLI.39.7712.1, record 118230.
**Source:** https://www.cehm.org.mx/Buscador/FichaBasica/118230?Tipo=A
**Researcher:** Alberto Garvía García. **Publication:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23934776

The handwritten prose is faint. Unreadable text stays **explicitly unreadable**; this is a partial transcription and not a claim that every letter has been resolved.

## Transcription

```text
[Cabecera muy tenue: México; fecha según catálogo: julio 29 de 1909]
[Dirección: Gral. Reyes; Galeana, lectura parcial]
[Foliación o número de pieza: 7712, repetido]

[Primera línea manuscrita de lectura insegura]
[...] círculos. [...]
Presidente y continúas [¿perdiendo?]

## Reading and open questions

- `31–19–40–16–27–62–44–56–37` → **LIMANTOUR** under the reconstructed letter key.
- `24–46–42–1–31` → **C[46][42]EL** using independently supported values, not unconditionally CREEL. The Work report suggests `46=O` from a 1895 **structural** template, which is **not an observed 1909 value**; the third code `42` is also unresolved.
- The clear typed note at bottom reads: «Retira de tu ánimo preocupaciones, y lee mi respuesta con atención.» Its author is not independently established.
- The third digit of the last coded group was tentatively confused with `72` in the initial visual work; `42` is the adopted reading.
