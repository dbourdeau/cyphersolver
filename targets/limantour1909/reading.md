# Reading of the cipher letter of 15 July 1909 (CEHM DLI.39.7680.2) and of the note of 29 July 1909 (DLI.39.7712.1)

Key: `key.tsv` (Alberto Garvía García's 28 strict values, plus values fixed on the telegram of 15 July and on the letter's own words;
see VERIFICATION.md). Cipher words are given in normal spelling; `[...]` = group not read; `[79]`, `[77]` = special codes; `?` = doubtful.
Clear words of the typed letter are in italics.

## 15 July 1909, José López Portillo y Rojas to General Bernardo Reyes (Galeana, N. L.)

*Muy estimado amigo: He sabido de buena fuente que* [79] *habla de tí como de un* enemigo, *queja,* [28 23 24 54 2: n-d-(c)-s-e = n-d-o-s-e, *quejándose*, with the 24 read as a slip for an o code; tentative, proposed by A. Garvía García, 6 Nov 2026]
*de que no* te pronuncies *de una vez. Me inspira* temor *tú* situación. *Hay que tener* calma *y* prudencia. *Cuídate mucho para
que sanes pronto.* [77] s oficios s *desencadenados. Otros* clubs *de* Guadalajara *te escribirán. Hoy te dirijo un mensaje
suplicatorio. Con memorias afectuosas para todos los tuyos, me repito tu viejo servidor y leal amigo q. b. t. m.*

Notes: the typed 48 in *situación* stands where an o code (44 or 45) is expected; 48 is p elsewhere, so it is read as a typing slip.
The group 28 23 24 54 2 has the same shape: 24 is c everywhere else (*Clubs*, *comunicarte*, *calma*) but an o code is wanted for
*quejándose*, so one number is taken as a slip. This is the contributor's proposal, checked on the clearer images of 6 Nov 2026 (the
group is printed as given, after "queja,"); it rests on assuming one error and stays tentative. On the clearer images the character
after 77 is a typed **s** (as in the contributor's file), not an e as the phone screenshots suggested; the last s after 54 looks the same.

Count (counted by hand from key.tsv and decryption_7680.txt): 75 alphabetic cipher groups, all 75 read as letters of words
(100%), 10 of them certain (temor, calma: every value in the strict 28); two groups are read only by assuming a one-number slip
(24 in *quejándose*, 48 in *situación*); the codes 79 and 77 are not read.

## 29 July 1909 (note)

*... Hay ruptura entre* Limantour *y* [c r? e? e l: Creel?; 24 46 42 1 31 on the clearer images]

The typed line below reads: *Retira de tu ánimo preocupaciones, y lee mi respuesta con atención.* The handwritten plaintext above the
cipher is faint and is not transcribed here. LIMANTOUR (9 groups) is read; the second name is tentative.
