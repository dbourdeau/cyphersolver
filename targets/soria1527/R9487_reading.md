Rests on: the contemporary decipherment ("Claro", f. 34r, `claro.md`) for sections A, C and the closing paragraph,
and for B (and the torn start of C) on the Claro's surviving half-lines **plus** the intact cipher block of f. 31v (8 lines) and the f. 33r block of C (5 lines), transcribed on 23 Oct 2026

from the figures, with the code groups and signs listed in `R9487_reread_log.md`. `[xad]` = a code group with no
value; `[?word]` = a word partly carried (one sign or code uncertain, or a code valued by sense and by its place in the

Revised 14 Nov 2026 (second pass, `R9487_reread_log.md`, section "14 Nov 2026"): the last two cipher lines of C were

## Reading

A. Luego fize pagar al Señor de Monago los tres mil ducados que V. Magestad me mando por su carta, y havian quedado en poder de los mercantes, obrado otros hazer mil, no embargante que les haya fecho quitanças de toda la suma de los cambios que esta se hizo por buen respecto. Mandara V. Magestad lo que tengo de fazer de los y de las joyas que fueron de Borbon que estavan empeñadas en Genova.

B. Ayer recebi una letra de Andrea de Burgo fecha en Ferrara antea[yer] […], y me dize que los obstajes que estavan presos en Roma en [soc] de los [zu]s se avian soltado y huido a Narni, que si es cierto seria [?gran]de inconviniente. […] no es de maravillar de qualquiera desconcierto que a[?ya] en el dicho [?exercito], [pib] esta la gente sin obediencia y con algunas diuisiones [yod] [ync]s. V. Magestad lo [?provea] como conbiene a su servicio. Alla era arrivado el principe [?de Orange] [pur] [?li] estavan el marques del Guasto, y dize que los dos venian con el [?exercito].

C. Los dichos duques y marques dizen que [?servidores] de V. Magestad; esto [?podria] ser, [?pero] no dexan de usar desacatamiento en [tal]rse a ser contra V. Magestad y hazer matrimonio con Francia, y bien se [?puede] creer que vi[+ud]endo el [?exercito] seran con V. Magestad por no recebir daño, que son gentes que saben navegar con todos vientos.

Todos los enemigos se retirayeron de cabe Milan, de suerte que ahora es señor de la campaña Antonio de Leyva y corre por do quiere; pero dentro de Milan hay muy pocas vituallas y lo de fuera ha [?ronuezino] e [?sprann] y destruydo, de manera que tienen necessidad de proveydo para que provea [a] bastecer la ciudad.

## Notes

- A and the closing paragraph are the Claro's text; their cipher (the torn lines at the foot of ff. 31r and 33r) is not transcribed. In A the Claro's *obrado otros hazer mil* and *de los y de las joyas* are given as transcribed on 21 Sept 2026; the sense of the first is not reached.
- *estavan presos en Roma*, *se avian soltado y huido a Narni*, *es de maravillar*, *esta la gente sin obediencia*, *como conbiene a su servicio*, *el marques del Guasto y dize que los* are in the Claro and in the figures (the figures spell *conbiene* with the group *zed* = bien, and *del Vasto*).
- *[?exercito]* is the group *yal*, three times (*en el dicho yal*; *venian con el yal*; *el yal seran con V. Magestad*). Key B of 1523 has *yal* = ella, which does not fit after *el dicho*; the Claro's word at the third place was read "el Emper[ado]r(?)" in September, with a query. *Exercito* fits all three places and the code order (the y-groups run from Di to Es), but no text gives it: sense only.
- *[?gran]de*: the group *xad* followed by *zog* = de; *a[?ya]*: the sign a followed by the group *pur*; *[tal]rse*: the group *tal* followed by r and *pu* = se (a verb in M-Pa by the code order, such as *mostra-*; not adopted). *[soc]* is *rey* in key B, which does not fit *en [soc] de los*; left open. *[zu]s* is the name of those who held the hostages.
- *[?provea]*: the figures give p-o-v-e-a. *[?de Orange]*: the figures give d-o-a-n-r-e. *[?servidores]*: the figures give servi-do-s-e-s. *[?podria]*, *[?pero]*: the figures give a-o-d-r-i-a and a-e-r-a-o, read with p (ɑ+) for the transcribed a (m+); the Claro has "[…]dra que, pero no dexan de" here. *[?puede]*: the group *sab*, by sense and code order.
- *dexan* follows the Claro (the figures were read d-e-v-a-n on 23 Oct); *desacatamiento* is *zog* = de + s-a-c-a-t-a-m-i-e-n-t-o; *los dos* is *xip* = lo + s, d-o-s.
- Where the Claro and the figures differ in C (Claro *por […] fazer*, *pues son gentes*; figures *contra V. Magestad y hazer*, *que son gentes*), the reading follows the figures.
