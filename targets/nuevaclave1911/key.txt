# The "nueva clave" of 18 April 1911: a turning grille (Fleissner) of 8 x 8 cells with 16 holes.
# Proposed by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic) in one API run from the cipher of CMXV.16.1551.3-4, 1561.5-13 and 1569.3-10;
# checked by us with an independent decoder. Not validated: the preregistered measure on a held-out telegram failed (VALIDATION_grille.md).
#
# Deciphering: cut the cipher into blocks of 64 letters; write each block row by row into an 8 x 8 square; lay the grille
# on it in position 1 and read the 16 letters under the holes row by row, left to right; turn the grille a quarter turn
# clockwise and read again; four positions = 64 letters of clear. The last block is filled up with nulls (x y z, and once a b c ... j).
#
# Grille in position 1 (O = hole), rows from the top:
...O....
O....O.O
..O...O.
.O.O.O..
.......O
...O....
.O..O...
..O..O.O
#
# Holes in position 1 as (row, column), counted from 0 at the top left:
# (0,3) (1,0) (1,5) (1,7) (2,2) (2,6) (3,1) (3,3) (3,5) (4,7) (5,3) (6,1) (6,4) (7,2) (7,5) (7,7)
# Source of the holes: the 17 nulls X/Z of the second block of 1551.3-4 fall in the cells of the fourth position, one per
# rotation orbit (one orbit had two X; the reading decided it).
