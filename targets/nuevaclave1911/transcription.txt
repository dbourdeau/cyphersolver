## CMXV.16.1551.3-4: Adolfo González (San Antonio) to W. Olliphant, Alberta Hotel Room 16, El Paso, 18 April 1911.
## Texas Telegraph Company, received at El Paso; check "30d c 30". Two small pencil letters "v e" above ESSFP (not explained).
## The 13th group is ERDEO: the typed letter is an o; a pencil stroke runs down its left side (read as P, and p or b, in the first transcription; corrected at the check of 13 Nov 2026 against the CEHM image and by issue #502).
ocurreseme que ESSFP TCEUI REBEC RIOZQ ORAUM SAPGO EUCAU ONNID PQFRO UROEI LNAND OOONR ERDEO XNSXT NZRSZ RUEAM FZIZR ITZII CDIOX EXNOX AXVST IXTES CXEEM XNAXE SXDOX RCA nueva clave
Adolfo Gonzalez

## CMXV.16.1569.3-10: Adolfo González (San Antonio) to W. Olliphant, Alberta Hotel, El Paso, 20 April 1911.
## Texas Telegraph Company, received 7:07 pm; check "155: D: Rb. J. 64/32". The 22nd group is IRRXNO (two typed r's, a pencil stroke between them; every group on the form has six letters, 192 in all).
