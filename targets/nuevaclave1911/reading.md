# Four telegrams of 18-20 April 1911 in the turning grille, the text read (Spanish)

Deciphered words in capitals; (x>y) = letter given by the grille x, read y; [x] = letter restored; the clear words of the telegram in lower case. Read in part (`VALIDATION.md`).

## CMXV.16.1551.3-4, Adolfo González to Olliphant, 18 April 1911

ocurréseme que FUERZAS PODRÍAN RECIBIR AGUA POR NORDESTE. COMUNÍQUELO. ESPERO QUE CON FONDOS REMITIDOS TENDRÁN SUFICIENTE. CASO CONTRARIO AVÍSEME. nueva clave.

17 words, all 17 sure: the 13th group is ERDEO (the typed letter is an o, with a pencil stroke on its left side; the first transcription read it as P, which made *contrario* a probable word; corrected 13 Nov 2026, issue #502 and the CEHM image). Nulls: 12 x, 5 z.

## CMXV.16.1561.5-13, González Garza to Adolfo González, 19 April 1911

CLAVE S. DR. GOTERAS JUÁREZ, L(c>i)STO ATA(e>c)AR. COMUNICADO CON EL S. TERRAZAS. NU(a>e)STRO M(l>e)NSAGERO: PROBABLE ARMI(y>s)TICIO. FONDOS insufientes.

17 words: 9 sure (Dr, goteras, Juárez, comunicado, con, el, Terrazas, probable, fondos), 6 probable (clave: the word cut is uncertain; listo, atacar, nuestro, mensagero, armisticio: one letter corrected each), 2 unresolved (the two single S, perhaps "señor"). The second block needs the declared deletion of one letter. "Goteras" is the Mexican word for the outskirts of a town.

## CMXV.16.1569.3-10, Adolfo González to Olliphant, 20 April 1911

OPINA OFICIAL AMERICANO INCONVENIENTE ASALTO JUÁREZ (t>p)OR ESTAR MINADO. CONV(v)ENDRÍA MÁS ATACAR INCESANTEMENTE, DIVIDIENDO EJÉRCITO TRES PARADAS OCHO HORAS CADA UNA. ASÍ LOGRARÁSE RENDIR GUARNICIÓN CANSANCIO.

26 words: 24 sure, 2 probable (por: the grille gives t; convendría: v doubled across the block boundary). With the group IRRXNO (192 letters) no letter is lost and nothing is restored: *guarnición* reads as it stands, and *por* before *cansancio* is not added. The typed copy BNM AMA Ms.M/T.2260 c.21 (an outside document, see `VALIDATION.md`) has *por estar*, one v in *convendría* and *lograriase*; the grille gives *lograrase*. Nulls: xxxzzz then a b c d e f g h i j.

## CMXV.16.1561.3-13, Adolfo González to Olliphant, 19 April 1911 (read by SeldomPhooey, issue #502; checked 13 Nov 2026)

OPINO ARMISTICIO EQUIVALDRÍA DESTRUCCIÓN FUERZAS INSURGENTES. DEBEN ATACAR JUÁREZ SIN TAR(b>d)ANZA SI HAY PROBABI(e>l)IDADES [de] ÉXITO. [?PARRAS] ATACADO.

17 words: 14 sure, 2 probable (tardanza and probabilidades: one letter corrected each, both falling in the last group BEA), 1 unresolved (parras: the six letters are certain as decrypted, their meaning is not; perhaps the place Parras). Block 1 reads with no repair. Block 2 needs one placeholder at index 96 (inside YPXB, falling in the filler after *atacado*), chosen by reading among the positions 94-98 (95 breaks *hay*, 97 breaks *probabilidades*). [de] is a telegraphic omission, not counted as a word. Nulls: ? and bnaonxx.
