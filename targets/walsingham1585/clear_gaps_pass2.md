# Walsingham to Wotton 1585: gap readings from the DECODE images

Grades: H certain, C probable, M possible, illegible (reason). Spelling as written where it matters.

## R4840 (21 Aug 1585), f. 163r = IMG_R4840_I28147_P1.jpg

1. l.12 "40 did greatly [..] in his [..] whilst he was here" -> **wauer** (waver). H.
2. same line, "in his [..] whilst" -> **wordes** (words). H. ("whyles he was here")
3. l.13 "27 was always [..] of late devoted unto 11" -> **vntle** / untill (until): v-n-t-l-e, last letters at the paper edge. C.
   Sense: "27 was always, until of late, devoted unto 11".
4. l.14 "for they are all [..]" -> **borne**. H.
5. l.14 "and all become [..]" -> the transcription is wrong here: the line reads
   **"for they are all borne under one planett"**. "and all become" is not on the page; it is **vnder one**
   (H) + **planett** (C: p-l-?-net with a cross-stroke through the p/l).
   So gaps 4+5 together: "they are all borne under one planet."
6. l.14 "it were not amiss [..] you acquainted 39" -> the page has "amysse" + a small e-like stroke + "doe" + a
   z-shaped y (the same y as in "they" on the line above) + "you acquainted". Letters seen: **"doe y[e]"**? M.
   Not certain of the syntax; "doe" itself is clear (H for the letters d-o-e).
7. l.15 "with the [..] of such suspicion" -> **groundes** (grounds). H.
   (Interlinear insertion above "proceeding" at the line end, apparently "the auctour of" or similar; not a gap
   in the transcription, not checked further.)
8. l.16 "suspicion, [..] in my judgement" -> **can**. H.
9. l.16 "in my judgement [..], but I leave it" -> **bee no grevance** (be no grievance): "bee no" H at line end,
   "grevance" C at the start of the next line.
   Transcription correction: after it the page reads "**and yet doe I leave** yt to your best judgement"
   ("and" abbreviated, then "yet doe I"), not "but I leave it".

## R4840, f. 164r(v) = IMG_R4840_I28147_P2.jpg

10. l.18 "the stranger [..] is fully resolved" -> **K.** (King), written as an abbreviated capital K with a
    point. H. Transcription correction: "stranger" is **ffrenche** (French; secretary ff = F) - C.
    So: "the French K. is fully resolved".
11. l.18 "fully resolved to [..] that realm" -> **sende an Amb. into** ("to send an Amb[assador] into that
    realm"). "sende an" H; "Amb." C (looped capital, then -mb with a point of abbreviation); "into" H.
    Fits "but the party is not yet known".
12. l.19 "it is easily [..] what end" -> **gessed** (guessed), with **to** written above the line before "what".
    H. ("easyly gessed to what end")
13. l.19 "he shall be [..]" -> **sente** (sent). C.
    Transcription correction: the next word is not "specially" but **consideringe** (con- abbreviation, then
    -syderinge). C.
14. l.19 "the [..] proceedings in France" -> **p(re)sent** ("the present proceedinges in ffraunce"). C.
15. l.21 "your request made for the [..] of 24" -> **r..t..ng/-nt**: starts "re", ends in "-t" with a long
    stroke over it; read as **restraint** M (letters not all distinct; long overline from the line above).
16. l.21 "10 desireth [..] may be delivered" -> a short word after "desyreth", written **he**? M. The last
    word of the line is "may" (partly at the edge). Words are run together here.
17. l.22 "19 is more esteemed than [..]." -> "esteemed the(n) / her:" - the last word, after a long descender
    from the line above, looks like **her** (or hers) with a colon. M.
18. l.23 "that the more [..] the day of" -> **forwardinge** (forwarding), f-o-r-w-a-r-d-i-n-g with a long tail.
    C. The word the transcription reads "more" looks like "mely/rely" on the image; not re-read (doubtful).
19. l.23 "the day of the [..] meeting" -> an abbreviated word ending "...comm.." at the line end, probably
    **commissioners** M; the first part (r/w-o-t crossed + long s) is illegible (cramped, crossed by descenders
    from the line above).
20. l.23 "meeting [..] the King" -> **yf** (if). C. ("meetinge yf the K. shall assent therunto")

### Postscript (left margin of f. 164, below the text)
21. "[postscript] [..] we had news" -> **yesterday** (ʒesterdai). C.
22. "news of the [..] of [..]" -> **rendringe** (rendering, i.e. surrender). H.
23. "of [..] which may breed" -> **Antwarpe** (Antwerp). H. Antwerp surrendered to Parma on 17 Aug 1585.

R4840 note: the brief said 12 gaps per letter; the R4840 file actually has 23 [..] places (9 on f.163, 11 on
f.164, 3 in the postscript). All 23 are listed above.

## R4844 (10 Sep 1585), f. 194r = IMG_R4844_I28167_P1.jpg

1. l.6 "13 and 14 is only [..] therein" -> **vsed** (used). C (v, long s, e, d; the s is crossed by a
   descender).
2. l.7 "This day the [..] will be taken" -> **full resolu-|tion** ("the full resolu" at the line end, "tion"
   at the start of the next). "resolution" H, "full" C.
3. l.7 "will be taken [..] and if" -> a word **struck through** in the ink of the text, under the stroke
   "therin" (therein). C that it is deleted; the transcription should mark it as a deletion, not a gap.
4. l.7 "and if there [..] be as well" -> "and yf there" ends the line; the next line opens with a word
   **f/s-o-r-g?-e** (long s or f, o, r, a g-like letter, e) and then **"as well"**: word not identified
   (the same word-shape recurs at R4844 f.194r l.17 "so ... forge"). Illegible as a word (letters clear, word
   not identified); no "be" is written: the page reads "... as well".
5. l.7 "as well [..] as 39 content[ment]" -> **falle out to** ("as well falle out to 39 content|ment").
   "falle" C, "out" C, "to" C. Transcription correction: "as 39" should be **to 39**.
6. l.10 "The Border of the [..] will [..] 19" -> first gap **Marches** C ("The Border of the | Marches");
   second gap two words: a short word read **fight** M (ε-like f, long s/i, g, h, t) and **agaynst** M
   (looks v-ſſ-e-r-s; could also be another verb). Only "Marches" is C.
7. l.12 "(as I guess, deferred with [..])" -> a word p(x-shaped)-e-a-d-d + a long final stroke, before the
   closing bracket. Illegible as a word (letters clear but not resolved; perhaps abbreviated).
8. l.12 "proceeded wherein 13 [is] absent from [..]" -> **court** ("from court."). H.
   Transcription correction: "wherein 13 [is]" is **thorowe 13** (through 13 [being] absent) - C; no "is".
9. l.14 "letters by the [..] have [..]" -> after "by the" a w with a superscript (w^ch or w^t) M; then
   "ha." (have, abbreviated) H; then a short word "dyd"? and **stayed** C (long s, t, a, y, e, d, colon).
   So probably "by the w[ay] have [been] stayed" - only "stayed" C.
10. l.14 "I would not [..] of any" -> **here** (hear) C (h-e-r-e, run together).
11. l.15 "do well therefore to [..] him" -> **comforte** (comfort). H.
12. l.15 "until the [..] come" -> one word, w/v-e-ſſ-l-e-r-s (or -ers) at the line start, before "come".
    Illegible as a word (letters partly clear, word not identified). "come." H.
13. l.16 "I have not been [..] of [..]" -> **vnmindfull** (unmindful) H; "of" + **your** H + a word at the line
    end "ce-bord-" / "re-ward-" with a hyphen: M **rewarde**, letters not certain.
14. l.16 "but [..] would of us [..] of it" -> **her Ma(ies)tie** (her Majesty, "her m^a^lie" with superscripts)
    C; "would of us" then **haue a-|ssurance** (have assurance) C ("haue a" at the line end, "ssurance of
    yt" on the next line).
15. l.16 "I have not [..] that I shall so [..]" -> transcription correction: the page reads **"I hope
    notwithstanding that I shall so ..."** (hope H; notw^t standyng C). So "I have not [..]" = "I hope
    notwithstanding".
16. l.16 "I shall so [..] as you shall receive" -> four short words after "so": f/s-m, f/s-o-r-g?-e (the same
    word as gap 4), a looped d/ð, -ozd. Illegible (letters clear, words not resolved).
17. l.17 "direction to [..] to the King" -> **repaire** (repair) M-C: r-e-p(x-shaped)-a-i-r-e at the line end.
    C for "re-", M for the whole word.

## R4844 f. 194v = IMG_R4844_I28167_P2.jpg

18. l.17 "[..] to confer with him" -> after "to the K." a looped capital + point, then **withall** (w-t-h-a-ll,
    the h z-shaped). M: "& withall to confer wth him".
19. l.17 "the proceedings in [..]" -> **the commission** ("in the" at the line end with a struck word after
    it, "comission" at the start of the next line, con- abbreviation). C.
20. postscript l.24 "of the [..] father [..] knowledge" -> after the sign words the page has **y(t) he hathe**
    (that he hath) - "hathe" H, "yt he" C - so the transcription's "of the [..]" is "that he hath".
21. same, "father" -> reads **farther / further** (f/s-o?-t-h-e-r) C; then **[..]** before "knowledge": a word
    b/s + minims + d, crossed by a long descender from the line above. Read "bound/sound" M only; treat as
    illegible.
22. postscript l.26 "My letters out of 32 [..] I am advertised" -> nothing is written between "32" and "I am"
    on the line. The only extra word is a **struck-out interlinear word** under "letters" ("Comp..."?),
    blotted. No gap here: "My letters out of 32. I am advertised that 23 ...". ("out" C: o-u-t, could be "ont".)
    Also: above "37" there is a struck interlinear word ("Scot:"? blotted) not in the transcription.
23. postscript l.27 "I think the following [..] 19 [..]" -> "the followinge | wilbe" (**wilbe** C, w-l-b with a
    point), then a word p/x-e + a faded word under a stain ("...hm"), illegible (stain, crossed by a
    descender); after 19 **dyrectly** (directly) M (d-y-r-e-c-t-l-y, "ct" unclear).
