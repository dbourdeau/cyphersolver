# R4838 and R4839 (28 Jul and 7 Aug 1585): what is held

BL Add MS 32657 ff. 125-127 (DECODE R4838, Nonsuch, 28 Jul 1585) and ff. 140-141 (DECODE R4839, the Court,
7 Aug 1585), Walsingham to Edward Wotton. Both are clear English letters in which the only cipher is a handful of
two-digit name numbers.

No transcription of either letter was made. On 21 Sept 2026 the pages were viewed on the DECODE images and compared
with the abstracts in the Calendar of State Papers, Scotland, vol. viii (Boyd, 1914), nos. 40 and 65, which are
calendared from Walsingham's drafts with the names in clear (NOTES.md sections 2 and 5: "left to CSP nos. 40 and
65, checked against the images (they match)"). The folder holds neither the letters' text nor the calendar's.

## Why the texts are not here (14 Nov 2026)

- DECODE serves the full images of R4838 and R4839 only to a logged-in session (an anonymous request returns the
  placeholder "Insufficient permissions to see the full image").
- CSP Scotland viii nos. 40 and 65 could not be copied: British History Online gives the volume as premium content,
  the HathiTrust text view (miun.abe1726.0008.001) answers with a Cloudflare bot check, and the archive.org scan
  (calendarofstatep0008vari) is lending-only (HTTP 401 on the text file).
