JUDGE NOTES  -  Magdeburg 1647 cipher passages, strict one-pass judge (16 Nov 2026)

Method
- One pass per file, decode as given, no edits. Words segmented across rows where a word runs on
  (e.g. fran|ckisofe, prin|cews, le|gato, stega|nographice, reter|uati); each half is marked against
  the whole word, and the reading column shows the join.
- Per letter: S = fits a 17th-c. German or Latin word (period spellings allowed: u/v, y for u-umlaut,
  undt, hette, were, komen, desen, newe, uiel, -irte forms); E = wrong letter in an otherwise clear
  word, plausibly a one-letter slip (substitution, extra letter or transposed pair); N = no sense.
- A missing letter has no unit, so it is not penalised (gelegenh[e]it, erha[l]ten, mo[g]lich): these
  are flagged in the reading column with [ ]. This is the one place the judge is lenient.
- [S] and [blot] = 1 unit N each, as the brief says. '?' inside a token = 1 unit N.
- <struck> rows (writer's deletion, no letters) = 0 units, listed but not counted (fr252: 1, no29: 9).
- Words needing three or more slips, or with no clear target, are all N.
- Names accepted as S: Axel Lil[l]ie (one E), Helm Wrangel, Georg Wilhelm, Rorte (Baron de Rorte),
  Torgaw, Magdeburg, Brandenburg, Oldenburg (one E), Regenspurg (two E). Mylonius and Wurdenhagen
  accepted as names, not verified. Steinacker taken as the name (one E).
- Recurring slips (all scored E, not normalised): o for c (moohte, oontra, traotat-), c for o (scnst,
  kcmen), f for n at word end (werdef, einef), q for d (qas, qiesen, wieqan), t for l (uielteicht,
  helte), k for o (hiebeukrn = hiebevorn). Read as a system, much of the E would become S; the strict
  judge does not do that.
- Columns in judge_*.tsv: image, line, decode, marks (one letter per unit), S, E, N, reading. Last row
  TOTAL with the strict share. specs_*.txt hold the per-token judgments the TSVs were built from.

Totals (strict share = S/(S+E+N))
- letter_fr186:   S 41,   E 4,   N 17   -> 41/62     = 66.1%
- letter_fr203:   S 134,  E 17,  N 38   -> 134/189   = 70.9%   (3 [S] units)

Gists
- fr186: fragment. "diario ... ein ... fyrfallet; periodus post ... in beyden gesetzt": something that
  came up in a diary; a period or post set in both. Too short to say more.
- fr203: someone is to go "noch diesen winter" to make report (umb relation) and fetch a new instruction
  on the treaties; this gives a good chance to recommend "dero negotia undt desideria" better.
- fr252: mostly symbols. Names the Margrave of Brandenburg (Marchio Brandenburgensis), the Archbishopric
  (Erzstift) of Magdeburg, something Franconian, a princeps; the rest unread.
- slip fr281: a requeste concerning Obrist Steinacker; Mylonius strongly disapproved (per discursum) that
  the Evangelical estates sometimes met at Frankfurt; Electoral Saxony had forbidden its legate to attend
  such conventicles of the Evangelicals, which made him suspect to the Emperor; expressly forbidden at
  Aachen; mention of the diary and of Baron de Rorte's former German secretary.
- no29 fr244-245: long report of talks. Postulation of the administrator of the Archbishopric
  (Georg Wilhelm named; capitulation, marriage terms, Augsburg Confession in Latin passages); Swedish
  side (Axel Lillie, Helm Wrangel) on withdrawing troops, the garrison, Torgau; Regensburg, the Count of
  Oldenburg, Gluckstadt, the Danube; how deputies should negotiate (per deputatos, coniunctim, by
  memorial, even steganographically).
