## What can presently be read

Item 2 supplies the strongest sustained narrative: a report about submitting a principal proposition, professions of intention, and an undertaking not to alter counsel or delay the proposition even if a projected enterprise succeeded. The subsequent curse/wish of punishment if fortune led to unjust demands is recoverable in outline, with damaged words. “Peace” is plausible for Φ from *[Φ]ficam intentionem*, *den lieben [Φ]* and the wider corpus; **the sign is not mechanically decoded or newly identified here**.

Item 3 appears to criticize convening an Imperial Diet and sending a representative or party, as a way to obstruct negotiations or set grievances aside in favor of negotiations with foreign powers. The syntax and several crucial verbs remain repaired. Item 4 appears to contrast a prince's professed intentions with restraint by *reverentia paterna*; the father/title words and persons are unresolved. This is a research lead, not a secure identification of a named person. Item 1's *superficiaria mediatione* is a plausible line-joined Latin phrase, not a clean unbroken literal reading. Gamma mainly confirms the accompanying copy of a report; this short reading was already recognized in prior work and is not claimed as new.

**Priority:** recover the actors from the separate symbol evidence and collate the strongest clauses in item 2 against an edition. Retain the literal numeral **92.** at n21_2 L01; do not promote it to a date.
