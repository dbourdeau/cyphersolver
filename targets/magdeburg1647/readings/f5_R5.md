Conventions: plain words are the decode as it stands (period spelling; word division, capitals and punctuation are the editor's); `[?word]` = a word the decode gives with one or two wrong letters (the hand's look-alikes: c/e/o, g/q/o, t/l, f/n, d/e, r/i) or that the draft supplies; `[xxxx]` in plain brackets = the decode as it stands, not read; `[sign]` = a name sign of the Catalogus, not valued here; `{...}` = clear (unenciphered) text of the slip, as far as this reader could read the German hand, `{...}` with dots = clear text not read.
