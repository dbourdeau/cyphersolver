# No. 31 — proposed complete source-assisted annex reading

12 November 2026. This is a normalized working edition based on the historical clear witness, **LASA A 1 Nr. 540 Bd. 1, f. 585r–v, frames 0633–0634**, collated against the existing cipher transcriptions for **Nr. 530, f. 212r–v, frames 0242–0243**. All eight paragraphs α–θ are represented; θ's last three cipher lines are on the verso. The preceding diary ends “Signatum Osnabrück den 12. Martii Ao 1645” on frame 0632, f. 584r. Its dorse No. 31 and the annex heading No. 31 are visible on frame 0633. This dates the matching packet, not a separately dated cipher page. Earlier suggestions of 1646–47 based on subject matter are superseded for this particular pair.

The full inherited **38 cipher records** and exact unchanged-key literals are retained in `records.tsv`, with line-to-paragraph references and uncertainty notes. The worksheet key is unchanged. These are prior project transcriptions, not a new blind test. Names and expanded symbols in the text below come from the clear witness; they are not established as universal codebook values. `[?word]` is uncertain; `[...]` is an unread span. Capitalization, spacing, punctuation and abbreviated titles are expanded lightly. Historical reported plans and accusations are reported claims, not independently verified events.

## α

Praesertim cum sint Status Imperii atque inter eos plurimi Evangelici, quibus per Gallos non multum sperare liceat.

## β

Vielleicht aus der Ursachen, weil aus Wolffenbüttel noch wöchentlich cum Imperatore correspondiret würde, dahero solches suspect sein und dem fürstl. Haus Zell und Calenberg oder auch D. Lampadio praejudicirlich sein oder verantworttung zuziehen möchte, wie dann solches auch Lampadius selbst nicht obscure zu verstehen gegeben his circiter verbis: Wie käme ich oder meine Herren darzu, daß wir Hertzog Augusti zu Wolffenbüttel consilia verantwortten solten.

The clear copy expands the cipher's described signs into the named houses and Lampadius. Its *weil aus Wolffenbüttel* differs from the old cipher reading *wegen [W]*. No new glyph resolution is claimed for that connective.

## γ

Neben deme contestirte er nochmals sein hertzlich desiderium nach des Herrn Ertzbischoffs zu Magdeburg anhero, sintemahl Ihre fürstl. Durchl., zumahl do die ChurSächsische Neutralität zum effect käme und ihr reverentia paterna nicht mehr im wege lege, beym Evangelischen Wesen sehr viel thun könte, auch sonder zweifel thun würde.

The wording *desiderium nach ... anhero* is retained as written rather than silently inserting *Ankunft*. The archbishop's coming here is the inferred sense. The speaker's identity is not supplied from this paragraph alone.

## δ

Hielte darbey gewiß dafür, wann das Treffen und ruin der Kayserl. armee continuirte, daß solches nicht allein die Friedenshandlung ins gemein befördern, sondern auch ChurSachsen desto mehr ursach zur Neutralität geben würde.

No particular battle is named in this sentence. Earlier contextual dating from the military subject alone was insufficient.

## ε

Welches auch umb allen Verdacht zu meiden unter andern eine ursach wäre, warumb er noch zur Zeit cum Suecis nicht viel conversiret. Repetirte auch darneben antecedentia lit. δ.

The final cross-reference is ordinary clear writing in the cipher witness too, not newly deciphered plaintext.

## ζ

Der Mecklenburgische Legat aber referirte ex relatione [?Kleins] (:Regis Daniae Secretarii:):

1. Ad evitandum majus malum Regem Daniae satisfacturum Hollandis.
2. Cranium (:Legatum Caesarianum:) exprobrasse [?Cleino], quod [?Danus] contra pactum cum Imperatore cum Suecis pacem meditaretur; cui iste respondisset: (α) Wären solches nur tractaten ohne gewissen schluß oder verbündnus gewesen. (β) Hätte ja der Kayserliche Succurs unter [?Gallas] seinem Könige vielmehr geschadet als gefrommet. (γ) Könten doch die Kayserlichen dem ChurBayern nicht wehren, daß er zu conservation seiner Lande allerhand expedientia gebrauchte und inconsulto Imperatore mit einem oder dem andern sich in tractaten oder confœderation einließe.
3. Gallos inter alia Instructionis suae capita hoc quoque habere, ut lente utrobique festinetur.
4. Eosdem (:quod pro secretissimo secreto mihi referebat:) hoc meditari, ut successionem in Imperio Germanico domui Austriacae eripiant, Bavaro vero hanc dignitatem conferant. Bavarum quidem propter decrepitam aetatem eam vix suscepturum, sed quid si eo negotium perducerent, ut filius vel agnatus quispiam in Regem Romanum constitueretur.

The secretary's spelling remains provisional: the clear copy looks like *Kleins* in German and *Cleino* in Latin. A contextual identification with Johann Leonhard Klein has surfaced in modern local historical material, but is not promoted into a securely read full name here. In item 2 *Danus* is a tentative Latin form referring, in context, to the Danish king. Item 3's *lente festinetur* is intentionally paradoxical: make haste slowly. Item 4 is reported secret intelligence, not proof that France adopted or enacted the scheme.

## η

Salvius hätte bey neulichster Visite gedacht, daß Torstensohn cum Rusticis Austriacis sich conjungiren und dieselbe armiren, demnechst recta in Steuermarck gehen, deßgleichen Olmütz entsetzen und verhoffentlich die ChurSächsische Neutralität nunmehr gewiß für sich sehen würde.

This is the clear witness's account of a projected course of action. *Deßgleichen Olmütz entsetzen* is source-assisted; the exact cipher segment and possible connective difference remain in the alignment note.

## θ

Wenn sie nur die clausulam generalem, daß durch solch hauptproposition der ReichsStände particular interesse und desideriis privatis nichts benommen noch praejudiciret sein solte, annectiren möchten, gestalt er sich tam erga Legatum Hassiacum quam Megapolitanum dahin incliniret hätte.

The clear copy cancels a phrase before *ReichsStände*. The cipher begins its verso continuation with a triangle-like sign before *der [Estates sign]*, so the precise treatment of that cancelled phrase is a witness variant, not silently resolved. The final parties are **Hessian and Mecklenburg envoys** in the clear copy; older guesses based on a W-like sign are not retained as established identities.
