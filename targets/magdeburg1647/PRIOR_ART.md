# Prior art: LASA A 1 Nr. 530 (Erzstift Magdeburg, envoys at Osnabrück, 1646-47), catalogue row 1639

Checked 12 Nov 2026 (sessions b160, b162) and 13 Nov 2026 (b189). The date of each check is given.

## Found

1. **The clear drafts in the envoys' diary, LASA A 1 Nr. 529** ("Tagebuch der erzstiftischen Gesandten, 1645-1647", catalogue ID 80462, film 57). Malcolm McBratney's second checker (Perplexity) gave the lead; b189 verified it from the images on 13 Nov 2026. Details and frames are in `nr529/README.md`.
   - Frame 875 right, fol. 801r, headed "1647 ad Diarium No. 31": the clear text of slip f.5, enciphered passages underlined ("die jüngst überschickte beyde schreiben").
   - Frames 881 right and 882 left, fol. 807r-v, headed "ad Diarium No. 32": the clear text of slips f.6 and f.7 ("praesertim in pari gradu constituti legati").
   - Frame 532 right, fol. 484r, headed "ad Diarium No. 54": the clear text of slip f.101 ("als der Venetianische hierunter").
   - **Consequence: the content of f.5, f.6, f.7 and f.101 is not first.** The key was recovered from ciphertext alone before we knew of these drafts. The drafts confirm it (`nr529/COMPARISON.txt`).
2. **The file's own key material** (Nr. 530 itself, read 12 Nov 2026):
   - frame 3, a Catalogus of name signs with clear glosses;
   - frame 51, a postscript with interlinear glosses;
   - frame 168, the key worksheet, keyword "AMPHICTYONES" over "zxwurqlkgfdb".
   The writers and readers of 1647 had the key. A contemporary decipherment of other slips may survive elsewhere.

## Not found, as far as checked

- No decipherment of the bare slips f.102, f.104 and f.204 was found in the Nr. 529 frames we searched (listed in `nr529/README.md`). Priority for their content is **not established**: most of Nr. 529 was sampled, not read page by page.
- Cyphersolver catalogue row 1639: open, no decipherment (repository check, 12 Nov 2026, b162).
- Web searches on 12 Nov (b160, b162) and 13 Nov (b189) found no edition or decipherment of these slips or of the Magdeburg envoys' cipher. Terms: the shelfmarks, "Magdeburg Gesandte Osnabrück 1647 Chiffre / Diarium", "Krull Osnabrück 1647". The Landesarchiv catalogue entries for Nr. 527 and Nr. 529 name no edition or literature, and they do not mention cipher.
