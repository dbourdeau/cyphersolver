# Juan Claver to the Catholic Monarchs, Milan, 19 June 1497

Status: read.

Alberto Garvía García matched the cipher to Ivan Parisi’s published reconstruction for the Ferdinand–Escrivà correspondence. All 98 units of alignment replay across 11 rows. The transcription uses 437 signs, of which 390 are printed-form matches, 44 have provisional graphical matches, and three are unresolved. Prior maintainers counted 100 of 104 sure cipher words with the provisional forms accepted on combined evidence. The 2020 Villanueva Morte and Fernández de Córdova edition supplies the originally clear passages. The complete surviving letter is in `full-spanish-letter.md`: a manuscript-based transcription of the clear passages, with the separately credited cipher reading inserted. The CC BY-NC-ND scholarly edition is linked as a separate source. [Manuscript PDF](https://opacaraimagenes.aragon.es/b4/11/AHPZ_C_CRAR_000040.pdf) · [Contributor report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24408633)
