# Juan Claver to the Catholic Monarchs, Milan, 19 June 1497

Status: in progress. Source files assembled for the write-up and reading review.

Alberto Garvía García matched the cipher to Ivan Parisi’s published reconstruction for the Ferdinand–Escrivà correspondence. All 98 units of alignment replay across 11 rows. The transcription uses 437 signs, of which 390 are printed-form matches, 44 have provisional graphical matches, and three are unresolved. Prior maintainers counted 100 of 104 sure cipher words with the provisional forms accepted on combined evidence. The 2020 Villanueva Morte and Fernández de Córdova edition supplies the originally clear passages. The edition should be linked and its reuse terms checked before preparing the complete continuous letter.
