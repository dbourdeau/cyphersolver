# Juan Claver’s cipher letter from Milan

19 June 1497 · Archivo Histórico Provincial de Zaragoza, Colección Zurita, C_CRAR/000001/0040

Claver reports on relations among the French king, the archduke and the King of the Romans. He relays reports of a withdrawal from Burgundy after the archduke sought French support.

## How the text was obtained

Garvía García matched the letter to Parisi’s published reconstruction of the Ferdinand–Escrivà key. A replay checked the submitted output across 11 lines and 437 signs. The contribution includes a sign-by-sign account of provisional and unresolved forms.

## What remains open

The tear and three unresolved signs remain visible in the transcription. The companion documentary edition prints the clear passages. The scholarly edition is linked separately under its CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license.
