# Francesco d'Este to Ferrante Gonzaga, 13 Apr 1546: c. 42r, the cipher passage in context

The cipher text below is the contemporary interlinear decipherment (`clear_c42r.txt`), checked unit by unit against the
signs in `reading_marked_c42r.txt` by `scripts/check_marks.py`, which also requires the letters of the reading to equal
the letters of the decipherment line by line. Clear text of the letter is in plain type; it was read from the image only
around the cipher, as a first transcription. The cipher passages are in **bold**.

Marks:
- `[?]` a group of cipher signs with no decipherment above it. All but part of the first stand under the writer's small
  superscript digits 6 or 4, which the decipherer copied instead of writing a word. Their meaning is open.
- `°` after a word: at least one letter of that word is marked in `reading_marked_c42r.txt` (a sign value seen only once
  on this page, a letter with no sign, or a doubtful sign). The word itself is the decipherer's.

Words split across lines are joined. `S.M.tà`, `V.Ex.` and `Imp.re` are abbreviations as written above the signs.

## Italian

... Saperà dunque V.Ex. **[?] che circa° le cose° di Sicilia [?]**, delle quali a' q[uesti] dì, doppo l'esservi messa la
morte del Marchese del Guasto, **[?] scrissi alcun° mio particolare° a V.Ex.° S.M.tà° ha risoluto non° volere° per°
trattarne° altramente, ma expettarne° la consulta di Spagna°**, e questo senza che **[?] da me né da Monsignor di
Granvela [?]** gli sia stato fatto motto alcuno **[?] de l'animo mio [?]**. Il che però non si restarà di far **[?]
attesa la promessa° che mi ha fatta° sua signoria di fare° ogni opera che le sarà possibile° per mio beneficio° [?]**.
E che ben mi dispiace che **[?] le cose s'habbiano° a consultare° in Spagna°**; **[?]** me ne allegro però, perchè fra
tanto **[?] venendo V.Ex.° senza° la quale° l'Imp.re° ha detto non volerne° determinare° cosa alcuna, haverò da
expettarmi° da° lei tutto** ...

## Notes

- "Monsignor di Granvela" is Nicolas Perrenot de Granvelle; "sua signoria" in the next sentence refers to him. The
  Marchese del Vasto (del Guasto), Alfonso d'Avalos, died on 31 Mar 1546. Both are context, not from the document.
- The line drawn through the signs under "a V.Ex. S.M.tà" (L02) and through part of the signs of the first "Spagna" (L04)
  may be a deletion or an abbreviation or title bar. That is open; the signs are read in place.
- 28 of the 85 words carry at least one mark. Most are values seen once on this page, which 42v-43r would test, and the
  abbreviations, where the decipherer wrote letters the cipher does not spell (V.Ex., S.M.tà, Imp.re).
