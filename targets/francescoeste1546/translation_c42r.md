# English of c. 42r (Francesco d'Este to Ferrante Gonzaga, 13 Apr 1546), the cipher passage in context

Same passage, same holes and marks as `reading_c42r.md`. `[?]` is a group of signs under the writer's 6 or 4 with no
decipherment above it. The cipher passages are in **bold**; the clear text around them is a first transcription.

... Your Excellency shall know, then, **[?] that concerning the affairs of Sicily [?]**, about which in these days, after
the death of the Marchese del Vasto had been reported there, **[?] I wrote something of my own to Your Excellency, His
Majesty has resolved not to want to deal with them otherwise for now, but to await the consultation in Spain**; and this
without **[?] either I or Monsignor de Granvelle [?]** having said a word to him **[?] of my mind [?]**. This, however,
will not fail to be done, **[?] given the promise His Lordship has made me to do every service that will be possible for
him for my benefit [?]**. And I am indeed sorry that **[?] the matters have to be consulted in Spain**; **[?]** yet I am
glad of it, because in the meantime, **[?] if Your Excellency comes, without whom the Emperor has said he will decide
nothing, I shall have everything to expect from you** ...

Notes: "His Majesty" and "the Emperor" are Charles V. "His Lordship" is Granvelle. The sense of "doppo l'esservi messa la
morte" is rendered loosely ("had been reported there"); the clear text there is a first transcription.
