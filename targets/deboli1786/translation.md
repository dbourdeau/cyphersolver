# Translation (English summary): Deboli to King Stanisław August, St Petersburg, 12 Sep 1786, first page in table 3865

Deboli, Polish envoy at St Petersburg, tells the King that, as announced in his last despatch, he has handed Vice-Chancellor
Ostermann his written observations on Prince Czartoryski's memorandum (the Branicki-Czartoryski affair, and the threat to take it to
the Diet), and sends the King the full text as he handed it in. He deliberately added a passage aimed at Ambassador Stackelberg, so
that Stackelberg sees that the matter can be well received in St Petersburg only if Deboli's ideas agree with his own, or with the
corrections he will make to them. Deboli only "picked the memorandum apart", so that nobody would be misled by it. Sedler presented
it to the Vice-Chancellor only on the Sunday, and Deboli had been watching for him ... (the page ends here).
