# Doc 2 - JACAR C10072242900 (catalogue 2573), (暗号文) 琉球出張兵の小屋建設に及ばずの件, table no. 1

Contemporary plaintext (large kana; the cipher is in small kana to the right of each sign), with kanji added here.

リユウキウヘ シユツチヨウヘイタイノタメ コヤハタツルニヲヨバズ。コレラノコトハ {マ}スミツタイイヘモ マウシツカハス。

琉球ヘ出張兵隊ノ為、小屋ハ建ツルニ及バズ。是等ノ事ハ{益満}大尉ヘモ申シ遣ハス。

- {マ}: the plaintext sign looks like テ or a cursive マ; the cipher sign is レ, which table no. 1 deciphers as マ (テ would be enciphered ヲ). Read マスミツ = 益満, a captain's name ({probable}; we take him to be the officer commanding the detachment in Ryukyu, not checked in a roster).
- Words: 20 counted, 19 sure, 1 probable = 20/20 = 100 %.
- Key check: table no. 1 gives the plaintext's cipher for 45 of 47 evaluable signs (one エ/ユ confusion; the テ/マ sign above).
