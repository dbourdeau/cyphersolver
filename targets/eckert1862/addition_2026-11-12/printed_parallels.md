## Three-witness collation of the McPhail report

| Point | mssEC19 / Book2 | OR I/32 II p394 | OR I/33 p559 |
|---|---|---|---|
| After No troops left | Java = Johnston, two key copies | Johnston | Beauregard |
| Name with 120 men | Greers/Greys uncertain | Greer’s [?] | Green’s |
| Hanover Junction | 700 infantry | 780 Maryland Line | 780 Maryland Line |
| Brigade / 1700 | no Hoke; Brigade 1700 | cavalry brigade. Seventeen hundred left... | Hoke’s brigade (1700) |
| Current strength wording | Has now 35000 | Has now 35,000 thousand [printed redundancy] | has now 35,000 |

The p394 witness is closer to the ledger on Johnston, the uncertain Greer name and absence of Hoke. It still differs materially at Hanover Junction and punctuation of brigade. These agreements do not establish which manuscript each OR editor used or prove the underlying troop estimates. The page explicitly says forwarded to Generals Grant and Schofield on February 15, fitting the ledger wrapper.

The p394 print confirms 46,000, 6,000, 1,600 and 640: OCR versions 40,000/0,000/1,000/040 are recognition errors, not additional witness variants.
