# McPhail: notes and boundaries


Operator heading: S. H. Beckwith, book 2, Washington, 15 February 1864. Complete quoted report, wrappers and forwarding request are included above; no body excerpt is substituted for the full reading. Conjectures each remain holes: whims has no established value; Prove may abbreviate provost; Greers/Greys is uncertain; Allen may be numeral Alton/one; Dusty may be Rusty/forage. All five original forms remain in the cipher and literal. "had ... had" is the literal repetition, not repaired to match print. The name McPhail is spelled differently in different parts of the source. Johnston is the key's explicit Java value in two copies; OR prints Beauregard. OR also prints 780 Maryland Line instead of 700 infantry, Hoke's before brigade, and Green's instead of the uncertain name. Full source-specific collation: ../McPhail_reading.md. Prior clear report: OR I/33 p559. These are witnesses to intelligence reporting, not independent verification of troop strengths.
