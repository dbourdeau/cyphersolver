# Clear minute (ASMi Sforzesco 642) vs our decrypt of DL 46150

Clear-text references are to clear.txt. Decrypt references are to the page sections of latin.md (D p1 to p4). reading.md line numbers were not mapped one by one.
"Plain difference" means a word that both sides read clearly and that differs. A gap ([...]) in the decrypt is not counted as a difference.

## Wording and additions
1. f1r L01 "ad vigesimamquartam diem": the decrypt (D p1) has "ad uigesimam diem" with no "quartam". Either the cipher leaves it out or the decrypt skipped a word. Check this.
2. f1r L09–10 "Excepit ... Excellentia audivitque": the decrypt (D p1) has "excepi ... audiit que". The verb forms differ: excepi vs excepit, and audiit vs audivit.
3. f1r L13: the insertion "precipue inclinare" and the deletion of the second "precipue" ARE enciphered. The decrypt reads "quam quam ad eam precipue inclinare debeat", so the cipher follows the corrected text. In the decrypt, "et ... et regni beneficio" stands where the minute has "Ser(enissi)mi D(omini) Regis sui nec non Regni beneficio".
4. f1r L18: the minute has "amabat [?ho] mediocriter". The decrypt (D p1) has "amabat mediocriter", so the doubtful extra word is either not enciphered or not seen.
5. f1r L19: the insertion "bonis operibus" IS enciphered (D p1 "bonis operibus comprobatus est").
6. f1r L23: the minute adds "Maiestatem suam" after "benivolentia". The decrypt (D p2) has "prosecuta sit" directly; "M. suam" is not visible there.
7. f1r L27 to f1v L02 "persuadebit quando hec affinitas procedat in ea que prius cepta fuerat continuasse, non nova inivisse": the decrypt (D p2) has "persuadebit quando hec procedat in ea que prius cepta fuerat continua[...]". "affinitas" is missing in the decrypt.
8. f1v L03–05: the deleted passage "[ne tam] immodica petitio alio tenderet quam ostenderetur" is not in the decrypt. The decrypt goes "suspensione [...] durum enim", so the deletion was not enciphered, as expected.
9. f1v L06: the insertions "summa" and "expeti" ARE enciphered (D p2 "ea summa a se expeti debeas"). The decrypt's "debeas" matches the minute's doubtful "de...ret", which may be "deberet".
10. f1v L07: "quam[tumvis]libet" was corrected to "quamlibet", and the decrypt has "quamlibet". "dotis nomine accepit, imo videat quod ambobus haberi possit": the decrypt (D p2) has "dotis nomine [...] neque [...] quibus haberi possit". The decrypt's "neque/quibus" differ from the minute's "imo videat / ambobus". This is probably a misreading in the decrypt; check it.
11. f1v L09 "et ipsa et vos alias michi affirmastis": the decrypt has "quemadmodum et ipsa [...] michi[?] affirmastis". This matches.
12. f1v L11: the deleted "bona hab(ere)" does not appear in the decrypt, so the deletion was not enciphered.
13. f1v L13: the insertion "centum" (caret) is behind the decrypt's "ad [...] ducatorum". That gap is the place to look for "centum quinquaginta milia".
14. f1v L19–20 "ut magna est ita quisque dignissimus ... condecentem existimare": the decrypt (D p3) has "ut magna est ita quisque [...] dignissimus ... debet eam sibi [...] existimare". This matches.
15. f1v L22–23 "ita liberaliter procedit et descendit ad tam honestas conditiones": the decrypt (D p3) has "ita liberaliter procedit et [...] honestas condiciones". The spelling differs (conditiones vs condiciones). It is unclear whether "tam" is enciphered.
16. The marginal addition (f1v lower left, keyed to L23) "et eo minus quoniam plus in bonitate eius Excellentie quam in summa dotis reponere potestis, ipsa quemadmodum sanguine ita et fortunam cum affinibus communem habere solet" IS enciphered. The decrypt (D p3) has "...plus in bonitate eius excellentie quam in summa dotis reponere potestis ipsa quemadmodum [...] ita et fortunam cum affinibus communem habere solet". The decrypt places it after "merito", which matches the reading order: L24 "merito reiici possint" + the marginal addition + "Quas conditiones".
17. f1v L24–25 "Quas {conditiones} si Maiestas sua et vos {ut spero}": the decrypt (D p3) has "quas conditiones si et uos ut spero". Both insertions ("conditiones" and "ut spero") ARE enciphered. "Maiestas sua" is not in the decrypt; it may be in a gap or may have been left out.
18. f1v L26–28 and f2r: "et aut operabitur q(uod) ... oratores ... mittantur, aut providebit": the decrypt (D p3–4) has "operabitur [...] cum opportuno mandato sine mora [...] aut prouidebit hinc nostri". "oratores" and "mittantur" fall in decrypt gaps.
19. f2r L01: the minute has "omnino debebitis"; the decrypt (D p4) has "omnino debebit". It is singular in the decrypt, against the minute's "debebitis".
20. f2r L02: the minute has "Ser(enissi)mi D(omini) Regis v(est)ri voluntatem"; the decrypt (D p4) has "uestri uoluntatem". "Ser. D. Regis" is in a gap or was left out. Vestigia's explicit, "Regis vestre", supports "Regis vestri".

## Summary on insertions and marginal additions
All of these are enciphered in DL 46150:
- the interlinear insertions: precipue inclinare, bonis operibus, summa/expeti, conditiones, ut spero
- the long marginal addition
- "centum": probably, but it falls in a gap

None of the deleted passages appears in the decrypt. This shows that the cipher was made from the corrected minute.
