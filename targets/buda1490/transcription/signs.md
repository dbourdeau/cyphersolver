# Sign inventory, DL 46150 (all four pages, final transcription p1-p4.txt)

Labels are ASCII names for the shapes; they say nothing about value. A label with a capital suffix (qA/qB, gA/gB,
ooA/ooB, hA/hB, 8A/8B/8C, 6A/6B, 3G, jB, Tx, wL ...) is a split made on the images by shape alone, before any decrypt
of that page was consulted (rounds q and split-2), or a new label added during the image repairs of rounds 5-6.
"?" in a transcription file marks a token whose reading is doubtful; it is counted under its base label below.
`??` = a sign lost to damage (hole, blot, crease). Value = key.json (key from the clear minute).

Pair units used by the decoder: `Ho` = p, `oH` = q (an `o` beside the free-standing `H`), `nq`/`ni` (`n` before a
u-sign = q, else i), `LL` = `6`/`6A` + `gA` = ll, `Ga` and `Gp` (codes), `ve` (code), `Om` = m.

## Split criteria (from the per-page logs; images: Hungaricana charter 228061)

- **q -> qA / qB / 4.** qA: closed bowl whose top stroke runs on as a flat bar RIGHT past the bowl, long straight
  descender, often ending in a dot. qB: round "9" bowl closed into the stem, no projecting bar (stem as long as qA's
  on pp. 2-4: the bar is the only reliable test). 4: small, often triangular or open bowl with a crossbar at mid-height
  through it, shorter stem ending in a dot. Confidence qA/qB about 70-75% per token on p.1 (joining strokes can mimic
  a bar). Result: qA = s, qB = u/s/f (polyphonic), 4 = i.
- **g -> gA / gB.** gB: the "9" bowl and left-curling tail plus a flat or rising stroke leaving the bowl top to the
  right (often joining the next sign); gA: no such stroke. Found on p.3, applied to p.4; on pp. 1-2 the first pass saw
  one family, and the later image repairs relabelled by the p.3 criterion. Moderate confidence: the stroke may in part
  be a ligature. Result: gB = t, gA = o (t twice).
- **oo -> ooA / ooB.** ooA: two small flat o's spaced and joined by a hairline, reached by a lead-in from the left;
  ooB: larger round o's touching, free-standing. Caveat (p.1 log): ooB mostly follows signs ending without a rightward
  stroke, so the split may be partly ligature-conditioned. Result: ooB = s (52/53), ooA = u (61) / s (7).
- **h -> hA / hB.** hA: crossed ascender with a closed round bowl (b-shape); hB: open bowl hooking down-left. hA = l,
  hB = f.
- **8 -> 8A / 8B / 8C.** 8A small closed figure-eight at x-height (= h); 8B tall ss-like, big upper loop over a bowl
  (= x); 8C open top, two arms crossing into a lower loop (4 tokens, value open: i, x, ii; see NOTES).
- **6 -> 6A / 6B.** 6A curved ascender (in 6A + gA = ll); 6B straight-stemmed closed b without a cross (one token, c).
- **3 -> 3 / 3G.** 3G has a flat z-like top and a tail running below the baseline (= g); plain 3 = n (g six times,
  probably 3G misread as 3; see suspects.md).
- **j -> j / jB.** jB has a second closed loop or blob at the foot (= x); plain j = f.
- **T -> T / Tx.** Tx is a free "+" with a centred crossbar and a foot dot (code: Maiestas sua); T has a top bar (= b).
- **w -> w / wL.** wL's last minim drops into a long descender (= tt).
- **B -> B / Bd.** Bd is a dumbbell of two loops on a short stem, no ascender (= x, once).
- **a -> a / al / aN.** al: alpha with a long tail at clause ends (null); aN: alpha whose exit stroke becomes a crossed
  descending tail (null).
- **f o -> fo** (P3L02): the o is small and raised (f°), one sign: code for 150,000 (centum quinquaginta milia).
