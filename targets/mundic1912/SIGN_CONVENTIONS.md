# Sign conventions: X-1.3.207.1 and X-1.3.239.1 (written before any key was applied)

The cipher is written by the Paris receiving clerks in ordinary French cursive lower-case letters on the telegram form.
Each sign is transcribed as the Latin letter it is written as. No values are implied.

- Groups are separated as the clerk spaced them. 239 has two groups run together on the last line (dodbxwlfrb); kept.
- The clear signature is written `=mundic=` (clear text, not cipher). The telegraph sign "=" before it is written `=`.
- Shapes that need care in this hand:
  - `u` / `n` / `w`: u has two arches open at the top; n one arch open at the foot; w a u with a looped last stroke.
  - `b` / `h`: b closes its bowl; h ends with an open hump and an exit stroke.
  - `z` / `r` / `x`: z is the looped z with a descender (like a 3); r is the peaked or "2"-shaped r on the line;
    x is two crossing strokes.
  - `f`: tall loop with a descender; in 239 group 4 one f has its stroke across the foot (read f, flagged as weakest).
  - `t`: short stem with the bar high (efcxt).
  - `l`: tall single loop without a hump (lxzrx, wlfrb).
- A doubtful sign is written as its likeliest letter and discussed in the header of transcription_<doc>.txt.
