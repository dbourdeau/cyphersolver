# X-1.3.239.1: "Mundic" to Luis León de la Barra, Mexico to Paris, telegram of 15 March 1912 (reading)

CEHM-Carso, Fondo X-1, X-1.3.239.1, image 0001 (received form, Paris Bourse stamp 15 Mars 12, "VIA ANGLO"; Mexico no.
103, 18 words). The text is 15 groups of five letters, then "=" and the signature "Mundic" in clear.

Marks as in reading_207.md. Line breaks fall inside words, as the five-letter groups do.

```marked
L1 | «H» aclaraciones bu-
L2 | ena impresión. Situació-
L3 | n incierta, tendencia general
L4 | vivo anhelo de paz. = Mundic.
```

Spanish, normalised: [H...] aclaraciones, buena impresión. Situación incierta; tendencia general, vivo anhelo de paz.
Mundic.

Notes
- The first letter, l = h (as in anhelo), stands before "aclaraciones" and is not read. It could be the end of a word
  cut off by the encoder (for instance "Hechas aclaraciones"), but nothing on the form supports one choice.
- "buena" rests on f read for the first letter of the fourth group (see transcription_239.txt); this is the weakest
  sign of the telegram.
- Every other word is verbatim from the signs with the frozen key; n stands for both t and u (situación has both).

Words from the cipher: 12 (the unread h counted as one): 11 verbatim, 0 repairs, 0 guesses, 1 not read, 0 holes,
0 from a gloss; plus the clear signature. (scripts/check_marks.py counts the halves of bu-ena and situació-n as
separate words, so it prints 15 with 14 verbatim.)
