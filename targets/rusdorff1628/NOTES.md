## The documents

DECODE R4333-R4337, "Riksarkivet_Rusdorff_Oxenstierna_1628-1" to "-5" (4, 3, 3, 2 and 3 phone photographs,
2448 x 3264, public records; not committed). The volume is Oxenstierna af Södermöre, Ser. B, E 701 (spine on R4333
image 2). DECODE: 1628, no sender, receiver or language, Non-decrypted; symbol set "Numerical" (R4333, R4336, R4337) and
"Alphabet" (R4334, R4335). Johann Joachim von Rusdorff, the Palatine diplomat, wrote from The Hague. The archive's
description says the letters are "in many cases annotated as unsolved cipher".

R4333 image 1 carries a short German sentence with a run of figures: *… 120. 65. 47. 83. 56. 103. 61. 77. …*
(two- and three-digit numbers separated by dots, among clear words).
