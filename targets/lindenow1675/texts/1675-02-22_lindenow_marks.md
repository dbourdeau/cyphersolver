# Biermann to Lindenow, 22 February 1675: the count and what each mark rests on

Companion to `1675-02-22_lindenow_reading.md`.

## The count

The four cipher runs hold 84 words (196 groups). One is unread (`[607]`, three groups: 607, a sign read r, and the sign G18). 29 are marked `[?word]`. 54 of 84 are plain: 0.643. This is the count Daniel Bourdeau made on the first submission (issue #679: 54 of 84 with whole-word codes and single values read from context counted as holes; 72 of 84 by the twelve marks of that submission alone), reproduced here word by word. The 29 are: 23 words with a value seen once and read from context; four with a value seen once whose only support is the fitted reading of 12 April (*que*, *avec*, *faire*, *malheur*); *les* before *quartiers*, because the clause is provisional as a whole; and *mettent*, for a disputed reading of the scan. The seven words whose single value rests on the clear letter of 20 February stay plain (*tout*, *entier*, *l'avantage*, *sans*, *y*, *moindre*, *realité*); counting them too would give 47.
