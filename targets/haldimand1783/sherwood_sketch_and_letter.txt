## Copy p.84 (old fo. 62). Margin, later pencil: "See pp. 345-347 infra"
A Sketch of what I understand from the Cyphers

No 1 "A member of the House of Newhampshire informs me officially, accounts then[?]
"he was there, came to Council, afterwards he was below Boston, and saw the French
"fleet & army sail for the West Indies. Reports say 800 to carry & man the 74 Gun
"Ship, building by Congress at Portsmouth. --
Something is said in the Cypher of damage done in March by thunder to the Ships but
I cannot make any sense of it.

No 2. seems to be an advertisement but I cannot find head nor tail to it --

No 3 Acknowledges my letter in Eight days, promises to observe its Contents, and
seems to recommend Clemency, gives Encouragement that Mr Taylor may be brou[gh]t into
the plan propos'd. Says Judge Alcot[?] will undertake with Gen.l Bull[?] or Lamentis[?]
leaving some letters at St Johns, & desires they may be forwarded. but I cannot guess
what letters he has in view. thus Concludes with his Respects to Major Holland, who is
acquainted with his manner of writing by Eu--es Gr--2[?]

No 4 "Wednesday last 500 Sleighs 5 men to a Sleigh march'd from Albany towards
"Oswego, Vermont post from Albany alarm'd them, they expect[ed?] the force was destin'd
"towards the reduction of the new State. Col. Stroey[?] reported this in Council, at
"Windsor, in my hearing

"on Saturday; other reports say 500 men only. A movement there certainly is
" Feby 16th 1783
                                   J Sherwood   {archivist: A.D.S.}

## Copy p.105 (old fo. 77), Sherwood to Capt. Mathews. Margin: "1. See pp. 84-85 supra"
Loyal Block House 2d March 1783
Sir
   Last Evening Mr Joseph White jun.r return'd with the Enclos'd letters from Mess.rs
Sum--r and Cos--t. I have not time to decypher them properly, Especially as they are
wrote in a very blundering manner and frequent mistakes made in numbering the pages.
I have, however, look'd them over, and inclos'd a Sketch of what I understand from them.
   I think Mr Cos--t's letter scarce leaves a doubt of Tom Johnsons duplicity, when
join'd to the initial letters of his name, plac'd in the Blanks of Edgars Affidavit;
which was done by Mr Sum--r. I have enclos'd this paper with four others to Gen.l Riedesel.
   I beg you will not forget to speak to Mr Dey[?] about Spruce Beer for this post.
          I am with great Respect / Sir Your most obedient and / most Humble Servant
                                   J Sherwood   {archivist: A.L.S.}
Captain Mathews
