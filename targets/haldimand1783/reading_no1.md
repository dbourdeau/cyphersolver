## Reading limit
The first source/account/council clause remains a partial, raw reconstruction. Its connecting syntax and the later logistics guesses have not been securely restored. The normalized shipping and thunder clauses should be read with their brackets intact.

## Summary
Intelligence about French ships and troops sailing from Boston toward the West Indies, a seventy-four-gun ship at Portsmouth, and thunder damage to one of thirteen ships. The source and logistics clauses are incomplete. Sherwood understood most of the first portion in 1783 but explicitly said he could not make sense of the thunder passage.
