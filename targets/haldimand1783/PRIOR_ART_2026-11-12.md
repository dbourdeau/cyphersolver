## Archival follow-up completed: Mathews to Sherwood,9January1783

The complete cited letter was acquired on Canadiana H-1737 frames595–602 and visually read, corresponding to B179-2 transcript pp.162–169. The copy labels BL AddMS21839 ff.366–367v, with endorsement at368v and368blank. Page162 itself is headed HeadQuarters,9January1783; p169 ends B:M and CaptainSherwood. The transcript is on lined paper in the later copyist's hand. **Correction to the earlier thumbnail survey:** at least this B179-2 portion is a later transcript, not photography of the eighteenth-century original. The manuscript folios are marginal references and must not be confused with transcript page numbers.

Page164 approves Summer's private proposal of corresponding with Sherwood, encourages the discovery sought through his brother-in-law, and authorizes20guineas for each dispatch-and-answer service, with more depending on value. Pages164–165 emphasize concealment of the people involved. Page167 rejects stationing a Canadian agent on the Connecticut River and is unfavorable to Summer's choice of Pritchard. Page168 recommends a fixed payment per courier trip to Wright/Hawley and others instead of reliance on daily allowance. The other pages discuss Cossit's/Enos's proposals, Vermont policy, Sullivan, settlement, and several agents.

No Entick, dictionary title, page-line rule or numerical key occurs in this eight-page letter as read here. The private-correspondence approval is useful historical context but does not prove that the February messages used an already specified book. A fresh full diplomatic transcription has not been made; the report distinguishes this complete image reading from a transcribed edition. Source URLs and raw image hashes are in the readiness manifest; the letter starts at https://heritage.canadiana.ca/view/oocihm.lac_reel_h1737/595 .

Also inspected: January2letter ending on transcriptp159/frame592, approving precautions with Cossit and Summers; May7,1782p10/frame440 and the partII title/frame430 were locator checks, not full correspondence surveys.

## Sherwood journal instalment narrowed

The publisher's complete extracted text of Sherwood's Journal partI, VermontHistory24(1956), pp.101–109, was read at https://vermonthistory.org/journal/misc/JustusSherwood1.pdf . It covers his October–November1780 mission and does not print the February1783 numerical messages or identify Entick. Editorial introduction credits JeromeA.Johnson with obtaining the copy from the BritishMuseum; it does not establish the intended Hoyt bibliography. PartII was then acquired and its complete publisher-extracted text read: July1956pp.211–219 (the publisher index says211–220, but this ten-page PDF includes a cover and ends at219), covering May1781negotiations. It mentions a signed messenger-recognition signal onp219, not a printed cipher key or the1783messages. It likewise supplies no Entick identification. Both PDFs are preserved with source URLs/hashes. OCR garbling in the introduction is retained as an extraction limitation, not silently converted into verified quotations.
