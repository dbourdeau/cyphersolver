## Summary
Reports a march from Albany toward Oswego and the Vermont government's fear that the expedition was intended to subdue the new state. Colonel Strong reported it in council at Windsor. A competing report gives only five hundred men. Sherwood read almost all this in 1783; his closing sentence, "A movement there certainly is", remains unaccounted for in the final two code groups.
