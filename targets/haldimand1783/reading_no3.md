## Summary
The intelligible opening acknowledges a delivered letter; the text mentions clemency, visits and a judge. The probable continuation asks for letters left at St Johns to be forwarded and closes with Holland. The middle is not yet coherent. Sherwood's references to Mr Taylor, Judge Alcot and General Bull or Lamentis are uncertain readings of his separate sketch and have not been recovered from these number groups.
