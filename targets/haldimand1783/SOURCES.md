# Sources and witness distinctions

All manuscript readings here are from the Library and Archives Canada B.178 hand transcript, photographed on reel H-1737 or H-1736. “Original” written on a transcript describes the exemplar; it does not make the photograph an image of eighteenth-century paper. The original is British Library Add MS 21838.

Prior-art searches recorded in the research pass of 12 November 2026:

- [Report on Canadian Archives 1888](https://archive.org/details/reportoncanadian1888publuoft): calendar lists the cipher messages brought by “Summer”, copy pp. 345–347, and Sherwood's complaint about blundering encipherment; no full decipherment printed there.
- Wilbur, *Ira Allen* (1928), [vol.1](https://archive.org/details/iraallenfoundero01wilb), [vol.2](https://archive.org/details/iraallenfoundero02wilb): searched full text; no reading of these messages found.
- *Collections of the Vermont Historical Society* (1871), [vol.2](https://archive.org/details/collectionsofver02vermuoft), and [vol.1](https://archive.org/details/collectionsofver01verm): no reading of these messages found in the search.
- Waite, [*History of Claremont*1895](https://archive.org/details/cu31924096961069): background for Ranna Cossit and Benjamin Sumner; no cipher reading found.
- Fryer, [*Buckskin Pimpernel*1981](https://archive.org/details/buckskinpimperne0000frye) and [*King's Men*1980](https://archive.org/details/kingsmensoldierf0000frye): located (lending-only online; catalogue and library copies found), but the relevant pages were not accessible, so their contents are not checked. H. M. Jackson's 1958 Sherwood study and Ryan L. Wagner's 2026 thesis (403) are unchecked, and the intended Hoyt citation is unresolved. These are **not negative prior-art findings**.
- Pemberton's 1972 thesis (all 373 pages screened), his 1976 *Vermont History* article, both 1956 "Sherwood's Journal" instalments and Mathews to Sherwood, 9 January 1783, were checked: no reading of these messages and no identification of Entick. See PRIOR_ART_2026-11-12.md.

This package does not claim an exhaustive literature search or a first modern reading. Sherwood's earlier partial reading was known and used from the beginning.
