# No. 2 — decoded, not read as a complete notice

The physical description is intelligible; the clauses after New Haven are not securely connected. Supplied wording and uncertain readings remain bracketed. Do not quote the connecting grammar as recovered plaintext.

## Reading
Advertisement. Thirty [?years]; five feet two inch[es]; freckled [?face]; light or sandy [?hair]; [?] [?make]. Belong[s] to New Haven; slipped through ye [?] spy, rebel, York. Will [in] May next trade [?fur], sell hemp; [?name] unknown.

## Summary
Apparently a notice describing a thirty-year-old man, five feet two inches tall, freckled, with light or sandy hair, associated with New Haven. The later words include spy, rebel, York, trade and hemp; their syntax and the man's identity remain unresolved. Sherwood in 1783 recognised an advertisement but said he could not find head or tail to it.
