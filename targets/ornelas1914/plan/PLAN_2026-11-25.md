## Transcription rules

1. Each telegram is transcribed twice by this agent. Pass A is saved (`transcription/<id>_passA.tsv`) and hashed.
   Pass B is made later from a fresh crop of the image without opening pass A, saved, then the two are compared by
   script (`scripts/compare_passes.py`). Every disagreement is settled on the image and recorded in
   `transcription/<id>_settled.tsv` with the reason. Both passes are by the same AI agent, so they are not blind.
2. Numeric groups: one row per group, in the order written, with line number, the value, and a doubt column. A digit
   written over another: the final (top) digit is the value; the under-digit goes in the doubt column. An unreadable
   digit is written `?`. Clear words between coded runs are rows of type `clear`.
3. Letter groups: as typed, lower case, one row per typed group.
4. Nothing is corrected at transcription. Check marks, pencil notes and form fields are recorded in a notes file.
5. Clear copies (3433.2-2) are transcribed once, then checked once against the image.

## Keys (all fixed now; transcriptions in `keys/`)

## What counts as a key "fitting"

- **Telegram with a period clear copy (T1).** The coded groups are aligned with the letters of the clear copy (only
  where the number of groups in a coded run equals the number of letters of the clear stretch it replaces, counting CH,
  LL, RR either as one or as two signs, whichever makes the counts equal; runs that cannot be aligned are listed and
  left out). Agreement A = share of aligned groups that the key decodes to the clear letter. A key fits if A >= 0.80
  and A is above every one of 1,000 controls (the key's values permuted among its defined groups; seed 1462).
- **Telegram without a clear copy (T2, T4, T5, T6).** W = share of decoded letters covered by the best segmentation into
  Spanish words of two or more letters (upstream `lang/lexicons/es.json.gz`, words seen at least 3 times, plus the names
  of the telegram's clear frame and context: Ornelas, Ávila, Villa, Juárez, Chihuahua, González, Garza, Torreón,
  Carranza, Madero, Bonilla, Luque, Ojinaga, Díaz); undefined groups count as uncovered. A key fits if W >= 0.80 and W is
  above every one of 1,000 controls (key values permuted among defined groups, seed 1462).
- **Short texts.** T4 (10 groups) and T5 (44 numbers): declared now that no reading of them can be validated; a key can
  at most "apply", which needs every group (T4) or 80% of numbers (T5) to have a value, W >= 0.80 (T4) or a phrase that
  fits the clear frame in at least half of the coded stretches (T5, the agent's own judgement, stated as such), and the
  control condition above. T6 follows Dudout's declaration (76 letters, cannot be validated).
- A missed key test does not rule the key out (transmission and copying errors); it is reported as a miss.

## Steps after the key tests

- **T1-R (key from the clear copy).** Build the table group -> letter from the aligned runs (method: key recovered from
  adjacent plaintext). Report conflicts (a group given two letters), and test whether the table has the three-row form
  (tens digits fall into row families; within a family the units digit fixes the letter). Read count for T1: a cipher
  word is read if its groups agree with the clear copy (reading from existing decipherment); separately, a
  leave-one-out count: each word decoded with the table built from all other words; words whose every group is then
  defined and correct are "key-supported"; the rest are holes of that count. If the recovered table has the form
  structure, it becomes K-DIEG for T2.
- **T2-P (pencil line).** Test whether the pencil sentence under the signature of 3492.2-3 can be the decipherment of
  any stretch of the groups: slide it over the groups and count positions where no group is given two letters and the
  pattern of repeats agrees. Descriptive only; if a consistent position exists, report it as a lead, not a reading.
- **T2-S (cryptanalysis, labelled as such).** If no key fits T2: hill-climb on the three-row model (tens digits
  assigned to three rows, a column permutation shared by the rows) for the two layouts (K-ALLEN-X's alphabet and Clave
  No. 0's), Spanish letter-trigram score from the upstream lexicon counts. The result is a model reading with no witness
  and goes under Remaining gaps, not into any count, unless a later check outside this text (another telegram in the
  same key) confirms it.
- **T3-C (catalogue).** Look at the CEHM catalogue records next to 13242 (3464.1) for a decipherment or the code's
  name; no decoding.

## Read bar and counts

- Cipher words: the words of the reading written wholly or partly in cipher; word division from the reading. Clear
  words are not counted. A word is read if every group decodes to its letters without emendation; a word with a
  supplied, changed or undefined sign, or a digit in doubt that matters, is a hole. Read bar: 95% of cipher words.
- Counts per telegram: cipher words, read, holes, and the list of holes.
