## What the larger text adds [I]
- **Addressee in England.** "dessa república" (the Commonwealth) as the source of mediation, "o senhor Protector"
  to be moved "dessa banda", Bordeaux (French ambassador in London) and letters sent "por via de Vossa Excelência":
  the addressee is in London. Francisco de Melo (later Sande), whose papers these are, was Portugal's envoy in London
  in these years; he fits. Not proven.
- **Date.** A Dutch armada on the Portuguese coast that should not "return in the spring", France offering mediation,
  the Protector alive, de Thou and Cominges in post: autumn-winter 1657 (with the m0268 "30 Nov 165[7]") fits best.
- **Writer.** An agent at or near The Hague, not an accredited envoy he trusts the States with: he has no safe way to
  write to the King, his letters are opened, he fears expulsion. Names in his network: Duarte da Silva, António Correia
  Br[avo?], Jacob Aba[?], Francisco Mendes (clear). Not identified.
- The writer reports that the Dutch "têm cifras que andam decifrando" (m0272.32-33).
