# c. 53r, 22 July 1634: English translation

The matter that Secretary Cioli raised with you is of great weight. You answered prudently in saying that you had no instructions on it. If the approaches are renewed, as can easily happen, repeat the answers you gave before: that you are not informed, but that you will write to us to learn our mind. If Cioli himself asks whether you have told us of his talk or its subject, reply that he did not say you should do so, so you went no further, but that you will oblige him whenever His Highness there wishes it. Do not open any talk on this of your own accord. Wait for the approaches to come from Cioli himself, or from others by order of the [Grand Duke?].

Cioli is Andrea Cioli, secretary of state of the Grand Duke of Tuscany. Forni is the Modenese agent in Florence.
