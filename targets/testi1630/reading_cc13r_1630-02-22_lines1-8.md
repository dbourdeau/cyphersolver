# c. 13r, 22 Feb 1630, first 8 cipher lines only. Held-out passage. Later decipherment at c. 15: a transcription job, not a break. READ IN PART

Plain text was written in cipher. *Italics* were written in clear. Marks: `[x]` a letter damaged, lost or changed by the editor (each change is listed under Editorial notes); `[?word]` doubtful; `[...]` hole; `[deleted]` ink struck out by the writer. Writer's spelling is kept in the cipher. Word division, accents and punctuation are added. Abbreviations in the clear text are expanded. The clear text was read from the images and is less tested than the cipher. The letter-for-letter text is in the decryption file.

## Italian

[Clear text before this passage, and the rest of c. 13r, c. 13v and c. 14r, not transcribed.]

Per agevolare l'esi[to] delle sue negoziazioni ha fatte a cotesti ministri et all'imperatore stesso offerte grandissime della persona nostra e de' nostri stati, e ch'egli è fi[n g]iunto ad esibire e promettere a *[?fermo]* in queste turbolenze d'Italia quindecimila fanti e quattrocento cavalli. *Supponiamo che* la mente del marchese fosse rettissima, ma non può negarsi che questo

## Editorial notes

- Only this passage was transcribed. c. 15, in a later hand, covers the rest. The catalogue marks the addressee of cc. 13-15 as uncertain.
- l. 5, [?fermo]: a clear word, fe + minims + o; c. 15 leaves it out. "Promettere a fermo", to promise firmly, fits better than the earlier "a ferro", which is withdrawn.
