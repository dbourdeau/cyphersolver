# Mantua 1524 manuscript recheck, 17 November 2026

## The messenger in f. 68r

The surname reads **Borromei**. In line 8 the first minim group after the second crossed 9 has three downstrokes, like the m of the following mi. Compare the two-stroke n on lines 1 and 5. Under the published key, changing that one transcription from no to mo gives the following outputs. | Reading of the units | Key output |
|---|---|
| b 9+ 8 bu 9+ no 2 mi | borronei |
| b 9+ 8 bu 9+ mo 2 mi | borromei |

The surname is graded forced. Initial b had one supporting word in the original fit, and bu and mo were weakly supported. The preceding e+ yields m, consistent with M[esser]. The given name yields cchilte. Reading Achille requires the initial c to stand for a and the t to stand for l. The second discrepancy also occurs in cavallo and canceller.

An extract in this same volume names Achille Borromeo in its heading, dated at Bagnolo on 26 August 1524. The Florence archive indexes correspondence from Mantua with the Duke of Urbino in 1522 under “Borromei Achille” (Classe I, Div. G, f. CCXLI). Together they support the probable identification. Evidence for the January mission itself comes from f. 68r. ## News of Prospero’s death in f. 298r

Line 7 ends in clear *in-* and line 8 begins with clear *teso*. The cipher resumes with *la morte del S. Prosper(o)*. The comparison is on line 14 of the same leaf, where *hora s’era inteso* is again followed by the cipher for *la morte*. Both refer to news of Prospero’s death reaching the court. The former transcription treated the beginning of line 8 as H 3 o and read the clear words on line 14 as *dora sera messo*. [End of line 7](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiifimage/MSS_Vat.lat.8214/Vat.lat.8214_0703_fa_0298r.jp2/1140,880,598,118/full/0/default.jpg) · [Start of line 8](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiifimage/MSS_Vat.lat.8214/Vat.lat.8214_0703_fa_0298r.jp2/299,990,803,120/full/0/default.jpg) · [Start of line 14](https://digi.vatlib.it/iiifimage/MSS_Vat.lat.8214/Vat.lat.8214_0703_fa_0298r.jp2/282,1532,835,123/full/0/default.jpg)
