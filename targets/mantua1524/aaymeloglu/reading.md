# Reading: Vat. lat. 8214, ff. 298r-v, 68r, 198r (Mantua, 1524)

Key: `key.json` (all values grade S, fitted and hand-read; no key sheet or chancery decipherment is known). Segment tiers: `reading-words.tsv` (strict / forced / lenient / doubt / unread, as in milan-1494). Controls and method: [README.md](README.md).

Conventions: plain = clear text; **bold** = deciphered cipher; `raw → read` = the key output and the reading where the reading departs from the key; (x) = letter supplied; [..] or [SIGN] = unread; ? = doubtful. u/v normalised in the readings.

## f. 298r-v: extract of a letter of G.B. Suardino from the Imperial court

**Heading.** Ex litteris **Suardini** datis **Victorie?** 26 **Februarii**
"From the letter of Suardino, dated at Vitoria (?), 26 February."
- *Suardini*: forced (q = a occurs only here). *Februarii*: strict. *Victorie*: doubt. The key gives `uf[n]fctorie`; Victorie needs the minim cluster after p# to be a single i, and the image shows three minims (a dotted i plus a two-minim u/n), not the four of `m i`. `-ctorie` is secure; the place is a guess from it and the date. "26" looks like clear numerals in the same forms as the cipher signs 2 and 6.
- Charles V stayed at Vitoria from 5 January to 6 March 1524 ([EFE, 30 Dec 2023](https://efe.com/pais-vasco/2023-12-30/carlos-v-vitoria/); the Treaty of Vitoria with Portugal was signed there on 19 February, [Gasteizhoy](https://www.gasteizhoy.com/carlos-v-vitoria/)). So "Vitoria, 26 February [1524]" fits. This makes Victorie plausible; it does not repair the minim cluster.

**r01-07.** **[..] .S. `canceltere → cancellere`** havendoli communicato tutto lo soprascritte cose fu molto contento in intenderle, et Maestà **il star?** di **[IZ]**, dicendomi como **[H o q+]** anchora n[ost]ra molto alegra, et che se trovava tanto **bene servita** quanto di alcuno altro che **in Italia li** havesse fatto servitio, et che lui continuo era disposto ogni volta che soccorresse li occasion di operarse a beneficio suo di bona voluntà...
"The lord Chancellor [Gattinara?], when I had told him all the above, was very pleased to hear it, and His Majesty [..] of [IZ]; telling me that [..] still [..] very glad, and that he found himself as well served as by anyone else who had done him service in Italy, and that he was always ready, whenever occasions arose, to work for his benefit with good will..."
- `b+ a ss#` unread (Il? Al?); `. a .` = `.s.` = S[ignor] (same form as clear ". s ." at r21). In cancellere the reading substitutes l for the decoded t of `lo` (see open problems). "il star" is doubt. IZ and `H o q+` are unread.

**r07-10.** ...o allora essendosi in-**[H g o?] la morte del S. Prosper(o)** per questo ultimo, ancora che per **Sua S[ignori]a** me fosse resposto nel caso di **Carpi**, quanto per l'altro mio già molti giorni io scritto, mi parso replicarlo,
"...and now, the death of Signor Prospero having become known (?) by this last [post], although His Lordship had answered me in the matter of Carpi, as I wrote in my other letter many days ago, I thought it right to raise it again,"
- *la morte* strict. *del S. Prosper(o)*: the stem is clear; the final `) )` are two anomalous tall strokes, read o. *Carpi* here is forced (`g 1` = a, one word); Carpi is strict at r12, v02, v06.
- Prospero Colonna died at Milan in late December 1523 (sources differ: 28, 30 or 31 December; Petrucci's DBI life gives 31 December, [Petrucci, DBI 27](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/prospero-colonna_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/)). News reaching Spain by February 1524 fits.

**r11-17.** dicendo como per **Sua** la **(S[igno]ria)** me fu detto **[BOX] haver donato** **al?** **S. Prospero Carpi** `ului → per lui` et per li **heredi**: ma che non li dava **[p s r o]** **fatto la investitura**: et perché d'ora serà messo **la morte** che porebbe alla volta **[BOX]** **volendosene prevaler** di qualche **summa di** `dfnari → denari` **in questo bisogno di Italia?**, et **recompensar poi li heredi del S. Prospe(r)o** in quel che **altra cosa**...
"saying that His Lordship had told me that [BOX] had given Carpi to Signor Prospero, for him and his heirs, but that he had not yet [?] made the investiture; and because ... the death ... could towards [BOX] ... wishing to draw some sum of money from it in this need in Italy, and then to compensate the heirs of Signor Prospero with something else..."
- Carpi had been granted to Prospero Colonna but the investiture had not been issued; this is already in Petrucci's DBI life and is not a new finding. The letter adds the arguments of the day: the heirs, the grant turned to money for the Emperor's needs in Italy, compensation of the heirs otherwise. Whether these details are already published was not checked.
- The clause around r14 ("dora sera messo la morte") does not construe as read. *denari* lenient (raw `dfnari`: S = d is one word, and i must read e against its secure f); *Italia* lenient (`r n i` cluster); *per lui* forced (`p+` must be the clear p[er] sign here, u elsewhere); *summa* forced (`mo` = m is weakly supported).

**r18-23** (clear): ...et ogni volta che questo messo ad essere [da] Sua S[ignori]a saperia quanto io ali giorni passati li dava parlato: et serebbe ragionevole che **[BOX]** fosse preposto ad ogni altro: per la qual cosa dimando ad essere così io per gratia sua .s. che volesse porger il suo favore in questo negocio appresso **[BOX]** ogni volta che io li ... dovessi parlato, che così teneva commission di parlarne. Dal predicto .S. **Can-** | (298v) `-celter → -celler`
"...it would be reasonable that [BOX] be preferred to anyone else; so I ask His Lordship as a favour to lend his support in this business with [BOX] whenever I speak to him, as I had a commission to speak of it. From the said lord Chancellor..."

**298v 01-08.** **Canceller** me fu resposto, como quelli giorni passati me dava ditto quello che stava stabilito, cioè che **[BOX] havea donato Carpi al S. Prospero** et per suoi **heredi**: et così non se mutarebbe questo `ulamorte → per la morte`, ancora che non fosse **fatto la inve|stitura**. Vero era che anchora non era stato cognoscuto se **di ragione Carpi** era stato **tolto** o no; et ogni volta che se sarebbe cognoscuto che de non, porebbe esser che se ne farebbe poi **altro a volontà di qualche uno**.
"The Chancellor answered me, as he had told me in past days, what had been settled: namely that [BOX] had given Carpi to Signor Prospero and his heirs, and this would not change because of his death, even though the investiture had not been made. True, it had not yet been determined whether Carpi had been taken away by right or not; and if it were found that it had not, something else might then be done with it at someone's pleasure."
- The best-read passage: nearly every segment is strict. `per la morte` is lenient (p# gives u; the reading takes it as the per abbreviation).
- Carpi chronology: Alberto Pio was first expelled on 3 January 1523, when Prospero commanded the imperial army, and recovered Carpi through his brother in August 1523; the renewed imperial occupation and forfeiture came on 9 March 1525, after Pavia ([Forner, DBI 84](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/alberto-pio_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/)). In February 1524 the legal question was open, which is what "se di ragione Carpi era stato tolto o no" says.
- **BOX**: in "[BOX] havea donato Carpi" and "appresso [BOX]" it is the Emperor (the grantor); in "che [BOX] fosse preposto ad ogni altro" it reads more naturally as the Marquis Federico. Unresolved (one sign or two).

## f. 68r: a letter in the first person plural (among Milan items of January 1524)

The leaf is a "we" who has been told not to ride out and feigns illness, with an urine deception. Gino Benzoni's DBI life of Federico II Gonzaga describes this episode: Federico showed physicians blood-dyed urine and admitted the deception to Baldassarre Castiglione, with the phrase "sano et gagliardo" ([Benzoni, DBI 45](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/federico-ii-gonzaga-duca-di-mantova-e-marchese-del-monferrato_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/)). So 68r is plausibly Federico writing to Castiglione, his ambassador in Rome. That is inference from the match; no name or address is on the leaf. Benzoni cites Vat. lat. 8214, so the episode is already known to scholarship, and his account is published corroboration rather than an independent witness. Benzoni gives the lack of money from Rome and Florence as the reason.

**01-06.** **[p@ o ss#] havendo [ved..] quanto ne haveti scr|itto?** per una [pollica?] [v(ost)ra?] **in** [..] come salve **desi|deraria che non** `caualasssimo → cavalcassimo`, **se ben?** **quan|to sia per la infirmità potessimo ca|valcare, ma che in Milano? non se sapes|se** [..]. Ca[..]r dal **altro canto, essendo sti|mulato tanto da** o passi? per littere et per **[e+] [7 7 4 m i ss# lo 2] | [borronei]** **mandato da Sua S[ignori]a et dal Duca di Mi|lano** per littere ic et ultimamente per **Grossino** il quale sapevamo `douecuenire`,
"Having seen what you wrote to us by a note of yours (?) ... how [salve] would wish that we should not ride out, even though, as far as the illness goes, we could ride, but that it should not be known in Milan ... On the other hand, being so much urged by letters and by [an unread name] sent by His Lordship and by the Duke of Milan, by letters, and lately by Grossino, who we knew [unread: dovea venire?],"
- *salve* is clear text both times (also 68r 10); a code name in clear or an abbreviation. Not interpreted. *bollica / ura* (68r 02) look like clear text ("per una polizza vostra"?).
- `desideraria` depends on `b u` = r, a post-hoc unit decision (forced). The name in 07.6-08.1 is unread: Achille and Borbone/Borrone are not established by the signs. *se ben* (`H i` = b) and *Milano* (raw `milauo`, blotted p+) are lenient. `douecuenire` is doubt: the extra c is visible and unexplained.

**10-16.** **volendo pur satisfare a** salve, **ha|vemo** **cavalcato** **non heri** `lattro → l'altro` **un** `caualto → cavallo`, `mostraando → mostrando` **la sera che'l ne havesse stancato alquanto, et heri** **maneggiassimo?** **un** **gian-|netto?** **et mostrassimo haverne sinistrato** [in l b n i] **schina** **indebilita?** **per lo male pas|sa(t)o?**
"wishing nevertheless to satisfy [salve], we rode a horse the day before yesterday, showing in the evening that it had tired us somewhat; and yesterday we schooled a jennet and made a show of having hurt [our ..] back, weakened by the past illness (?)"
- *cavalcato* is forced (transcriber alternative `r{c}?` in the `g r` unit). l'altro and cavallo substitute l for `lo` = t (lenient). *gian-netto* reads `mo` as n (lenient).

**16-19.** **et havemo questa matina** **mandato** **fori** `alti → alli` **medici in sala? orine fatte tingere a posta con sangue [CROSS]** `fccendo → facendo` **uscire voce che havemo [9 9]** `orincto → orinato` **sangue, et ve lo** [..]
"and this morning we sent out to the physicians urine dyed on purpose with blood, letting the word go round that we have passed blood; and we have [..]"
- *orine fatte tingere a posta con sangue* is strict sign for sign. *medici* and *uscire* read cleanly after image re-reads (7 for 9 in medici; one 7, not two, in uscire). *facendo* and *orinato* are lenient: one sign each disagrees with the key. *mandato* forced (`m{n}?`); *sangue* forced (`gr` weak); *et ve lo* forced (`bb` = o is one word).

**20-23** (reserved control lines; see README.md, Controls, for why this is not a held-out test). **fatto** `scruerbs → scriver(e)` **in questa littera da poter mostrare al [l?] Duca di Sessa et a chi ve parirà, ma per la verità siamo** `sa[8+]o → sano` **et** `ga[H]li|ardo → gagliardo` **como mai fossimo. Tutto fareti inten|[dere]**
"...had it written in this letter so that it can be shown to the Duke of Sessa and to whomever you see fit; but in truth we are as sound and vigorous as we ever were. You will make all this understood..."
- "ve parirà" (as you see fit) is the reading proposed in the Codex review; it keeps every decoded sign (`uepa | rira`). The Duke of Sessa, Luis Fernández de Córdoba, was Charles V's ambassador in Rome in 1524 ([Real Academia de la Historia](https://historia-hispanica.rah.es/biografias/15460-luis-fernandez-de-cordoba); [Cal. State Papers Spain, July 1524](https://www.british-history.ac.uk/cal-state-papers/spain/vol2/pp647-650)). "Sano et gagliardo" matches Benzoni's quotation.

## f. 198r: extract of a letter of Capino de Capo, Robbio, 8 April 1524

...prima la tregua di Spagna per il maneggio del Arcivescovo di Capua **se retirerà** `[C@]uesto → questo` `enercito → exercito` **de fiume in fiume, et poi in Pavia, Cremona et Milano**, secondo dice **Barbon**.
"...[if] the Spanish truce [has not arrived] first through the negotiation of the Archbishop of Capua, this army will withdraw from river to river, and then into Pavia, Cremona and Milan, according to what Bourbon says."
- Strict except *questo* (forced: the looped che-shaped sign C@ is null in the key; q is a conjecture), *exercito* (lenient: x must stay x, c = e is one word) and *Barbon* (forced: b = b rests on this name alone). The cipher gives **Cremona**, not Tortona (`7 8 2 e+ 9+ x 9`, all frequent signs).
- This is a conditional, prospective retreat (`se retirerà`, inside a condition about the truce), not a report of an itinerary followed. The three towns may be fallback destinations rather than a sequential march. "Questo exercito" most naturally means the writer's own force, against "li inimici" in Novara; that is an interpretation. Schönberg, Archbishop of Capua, was negotiating with Charles V in April 1524 (arrived at Burgos 14 April: [Cal. State Papers Venice, April 1524](https://www.british-history.ac.uk/cal-state-papers/venice/vol3/pp359-364)). Bourbon served the Emperor in this campaign.

## Main open problems
- **lo** gives t securely in many words; in five places the reading wants l (canceltere twice, lattro, caualto, alti). No graphic rule separating the two was found, so those readings are substitutions against the key (lenient). A doubling device is speculation: lattro does not have lo right after `ss#`.
- **Minim clusters** (`i n i` in the heading, `n i`, `r n i`): Victorie (doubt) and Italia (lenient) depend on them.
- **Exceptions to a single substitution:** `p+` and `p#` must read per in per lui and per la morte; x must stay x in exercito; i must read e in denari; H would need incompatible treatments in se ben, indebilita, maneggiassimo. Some may be copying or transcription errors; that is not established case by case.
- **Unread:** H, BOX (no single referent), IZ, CROSS, `p@`, `b+`, `8+`, `) )`, lone `n`, `r`, `p`, `u`.
- **One-word values:** q, o, bb, c, S, b, g1, p+; weak units: mo, gr, bu (DECISIONS.md).
- The 68r transcription is second-checked on 5 of 23 lines, plus the review's checks; 68r 01-02 is blotted and partly clear text.
