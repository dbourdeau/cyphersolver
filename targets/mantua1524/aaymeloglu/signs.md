# Sign inventory: Vat. lat. 8214 cipher, ff. 298r-298v, 68r, 198r

Total 1465 signs, 43 labels (counts approximate to within a few signs). Points (`.`) between signs are not counted. A doubtful reading `x{y}?` is counted under x. Locations: r = 298r, v = 298v, then the line number (H1/H2 = heading).

Most cipher signs are written like the hand's own minuscules and digits. Sign pairs that look similar but are kept apart: `m` (3 minims) vs `n` (2) vs `i` (1, usually dotted). In this hand **`m i` is almost always written together** (4 minims, dot over the last), so read it as two signs.

## Not counted as cipher (clear-text abbreviations)
- **Looped c** (a large loop around a small c, with a stroke out at the top right) = `ch[e]`. It fits every context ("che se trouaua", "et che lui continuo", "ogni uolta che"). Transcribed as clear.
- **Single-barred p standing alone among clear words** = `p[er]` ("p[er] questo", "et p[er] li", "p[er] la"). Inside a run, a single-barred p is kept as `p+`. The case at r12 run 3 (`p+ ss# p# m i`) is doubtful: its first p may be p[er].
- **Superscript "ra"** over S = `S[ignori]a` (r18). **"pto"** = `p[redi]cto` (r23).

## Shape decisions to watch
- 7 vs 9: the rule is the top. Flat or angular = `7`, curved = `9`. Pass 2 moved 5 signs from 7 to 9. Borderline forms remain at r01 (`7 9 x 7`) and r09.
- `a` vs `1`: treated as one sign. The only separately labelled `1` is the small apostrophe-like stroke in r09 (`7 g 1 8 I m i`).
- `9+` vs `q` vs `q+`: crossed loop-descender = 9+; uncrossed straight = q (once); crossed with a B-loop tail = q+ (once).
- `IZ` (r03) vs `BOX` (r11, r14, r20, r22, v02): open versus closed square. They may be variants of one sign.
- `I` vs `I=`: the extra middle bar is clear at 3x in r08, r12, r13, r16, r17.
- `H` at r03 and r08 may be two signs (crossed t t).

## Pair behaviour across leaves (counts from the TSVs)
- **m + i:** 298: 31 of 32 m are followed by i (exception r15 `e+ m o 9`). 68r: 58 of 62 (exceptions: all `m o`, at 68r 13, 16, 19, 23). 198r: 8 of 8.
- **o:** almost never stands alone. It occurs doubled (`o o`) or right after l or n (`l o`, `n o`). 298: 36 of 51 o in `o o`, 15 in `l o`/`n o` etc. 68r: 58 of 114 in `o o`, 29 in `l o`, 19 in `n o`. Tripled `o o o` occurs (298v 04, 08; 68r 11, 20).
- **c:** usually doubled (`c c`): 298 14 of 14; 68r 8 of 10; 198r 0 of 1 (`2 x c 8`).
- **d:** usually doubled: 298 8 of 10; 68r 4 of 5.
- **b:** paired `b b` occurs only on 68r (19, 20), 6 of 16.
