## Notes

- The read core of the leaf, all strict: "fori ... medici in sala orine fatte tingere a posta con sangue" (urine
  dyed on purpose with blood) and, on the reserved lines, "in questa littera da poter ... al [...] duca di Sessa et a
  chi ve parirà ma per la verità siamo ... et ... como ... fossimo".
- The episode matches G. Benzoni, "Federico II Gonzaga", DBI 45 (1995): Federico showed the physicians urine dyed
  with blood and admitted the deception to Castiglione, "sano et gagliardo". The leaf names neither writer nor
  addressee; Federico to Castiglione, his ambassador at Rome, is an inference from that match. In the cipher "sano"
  and "gagliardo" are lenient (`sa[8+]o`, `ga[H]liardo`: one sign each with no value), so they are holes here.
- The Duke of Sessa, Luis Fernandez de Cordoba, was Charles V's ambassador at Rome in 1524.
- Lines 20-23 ("fatto scrivere ... fareti inten-") are the reserved control lines (NOTES.md, Controls).
- At lines 07-08 the key gives *m cchilte borromei*. The surname fits the corrected transcription. The given name requires two substitutions. A letter from Bagnolo in the same volume names Achille Borromeo. [Manuscript comparisons](audit/recheck-2026-11-17.md)
