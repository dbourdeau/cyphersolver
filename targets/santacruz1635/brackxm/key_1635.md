# Philip IV → Santa Cruz, 1635 cipher: working key (28 Oct 2026, from D.182/183 and D.196/197)

D.196 (images 63-65, Madrid 25 Oct 1635) aligned with D.197 (images 66-67) paragraph 1; D.182 partly.

## System

A syllabary. Each consonant+vowel syllable has a code; single letters and frequent words have their own codes;
names and some nouns are three-digit nomenclator numbers; numerals in clear carry a small "8" above them.

**Two-letter codes, vowel kept, consonant replaced** (consonant + same vowel):

| plain consonant | code letter | attested |
|---|---|---|
| l | x | xa la, xe le, xi li, xo lo, xu lu |
| d | n | ne de, ni di, no do, na da |
| p | l | le pe, li pi, lo po, lu pu |
| b/v | Z | Za va/ba, Ze ve, Zi vi/bi, Zo vo, Zu bu |

**Vowel-first codes** (vowel + code letter): `Vr` = s+V (er se, or so, ur su, ir si; also iv si);
`Vt` = g+V (at ga, et ge).

**Number codes in blocks of five (a e i o u):**

| numbers | syllables | attested |
|---|---|---|
| 18-22 | ? (22 = xo in *dixo*) | 22 |
| 28-32 | ca ce ci co cu | 28 ca (*cargo*, *embarcar*), 30 ci/zi (*recivo*, *tercio*, *deziis*), 31 co (*con*, *contrario*) |
| 43-47 | ma me mi mo mu | 43 ma (*mas*, *tomado*), 44 me, 45 mi (*mil*, *mia*, *remito*) |
| 48-52 | ra re ri ro ru | 48 ra?, 49 re (*otubre*, *dispusiere*) |
| 53-57 | ta te ti to tu | 54 te (*deste*, *tercio*), 55 ti (*advertido*), 56 to (*todo*, *remito*, *tomado*), 57 tu (*otubre*) |

**Single letters:** h = s, d = s; g = e; f, v, p = a; e = i; C = l; L = n; y = o (and e?); b = o; 6 = r.

**Words:** 13 and 15 = y (and); 68 and 69 = que; en = el; 260 = para.

**Nomenclator:** 178 gente, 217 Marqués (de Leganés), 218 Milán, 233 Nápoles, 229 Villanueva, 46 D. Gerónimo?,
228 navíos?, 144(l) españo(les), Cr infante(s).

## Fourth pair: D.185 (cipher, image 34, Madrid 8 Oct 1635) / D.186 (decipherment, image 36)
