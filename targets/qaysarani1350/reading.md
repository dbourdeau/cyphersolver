# Reading: Penn LJS 51, f. 106v, the cipher note of Ibn al-Qabāqibī

Two verses in the ṭawīl metre, rhyme -ḍā. Twenty of the 21 words are read; the first word of the second hemistich is
damaged and not read. Corrected on 23 Nov 2026 after Alan Elbaum's eight corrections (NOTES.md).

## As enciphered (letter for letter, `python decode.py`)

وناعسة ^ا^لاجفان قد جعل الهوا [..ه..] مهجتي مثوا وخدي لها ارضا
سهرت واجفاني صحاح ولم انم ونامت ولم تسهر واجفانها مرضا

(^ا^ is a small plain alif written above the line before the lam-alif sign. `decode.py` prints لها for the damaged
group, from the three signs transcribed there; that is not a reading.)

## In normal spelling, vocalised

وناعسةِ الأجفانِ قد جعلَ الهوى / [..ه..] مهجتي مثوًى وخدّي لها أرضا
سهرتُ وأجفاني صِحاحٌ ولم أنمْ / ونامتْ ولم تسهرْ وأجفانُها مرضى

## Notes

- The cipher writes final alif maqṣūra as alif (الهوا، مثوا، مرضا), no hamza, no shadda, and "الأجفان" as a small
  alif above the line, one lam-alif sign, then جفان.
- Doubtful signs, read from sense and metre: the last sign of وناعسة (abraded; ة), the lam-alif sign (abraded), and the
  last sign of line 6 (broken in the middle; ر).

## Not read

- **[..ه..]** (line 2, before مهجتي): a damaged patch. The ه is whole; before it a short stroke and a small loop below
  the baseline survive, after it a sign and a tall stroke on a lifted flap. It was first read لها, from the parallel
  لها ارضا and the metre. Elbaum: at least two more letters stand there than لها accounts for; he has no reading
  either. لها is what the metre allows, not what the page shows.

## Withdrawn (23 Nov 2026)

- صحاح was marked as an emendation of صحاج. It is not: the last sign's foot turns to the right of the stem, as in the
  ح before it; the ج sign has its loop under or left of the stem. The word is صحاح as written.
- ولم تسهد is ولم تسهر: the last sign of line 6 is the ر sign, broken in the middle.

Seven of the 22 signs rest on the sense of one word each (ح, ص, ث, خ, ق, ة, لا).

## A parallel for the second verse

Ibn al-ʿAdīm's *Bughyat al-ṭalab fī taʾrīkh Ḥalab* preserves the second verse in a quotation attributed to al-Agharr ibn Sinān al-Ḥalabī. In the Suhayl Zakkār edition, the passage can be found by its opening سهرت وأجفاني صحاح ولم أنم. Ibn al-ʿAdīm has **صحاح** and **تسهر**, and so does the manuscript as corrected on 23 Nov 2026 (the cipher was first read تسهد here, wrongly), so the witness and the page agree letter for letter in this verse. The following verse in the history differs from the other verse on the Penn leaf, so the witness verifies only the shared verse. The attribution concerns the quoted poetry; it does not identify the correspondent named in Penn's prose. [Ibn al-ʿAdīm, text and transmission statement](https://www.islamarchive.cc/ketab_content/563/p-1881)
