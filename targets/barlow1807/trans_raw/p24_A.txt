The system of our external Relations
supported by the power and ascendancy of the British Go-
vernment in India, and maintained in its original
purity by a strict adherence on our part to the principles
on which those Relations were originally founded, continues
to preserve the primary States of India, in a Condition
of peacefull inactivity and secure Repose, whilst the
Minor States and those with which we are unconnected
by the Obligations of Alliance engaged in mutual
dissentions, or in the prosecution of the objects of their
respective views and conflicting Interests have neither
leisure to meditate and Combine, nor power to carry into
effect any feasible projects of Hostility or Ambition
against the British Government & its Allies.
