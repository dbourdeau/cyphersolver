Fort William the 15th June 1807.
Sir,
The Dispatches of this Government
to the Honorable the Court of Directors & to the Secret
Committee forwarded by the Extra Ship Indus, contain
ample Details of Communication upon every Subject of
Importance connected with the Political Interests of the
British Government in India, which has engaged our
attention since the Departure of the homeward bound
fleet in the Month of February. Your access to those Dis-
-patches precludes the necessity of describing to you in a
separate Address the Course of our proceedings in the
conduct of Political Affairs. You will have the Satis-
-faction to observe that the same Prospect of durable
Tranquility which we announced in our last
[catchword Dispatches]
The Right Honorable
George Tierney
&c. &c. &c.
