# Avenel III, the other pieces in the same cipher (May 1629, March 1630): reading

<!-- measure: cipher_markup=⟦⟧ -->

Seven pieces that Avenel prints with their cipher figures (Lettres de Richelieu III, 1858, nos. CLVI, CLXXIV, CCCXII,
CCCXIV, CCCXVI, CCCXVII, CCCXXXII), read with the key frozen at fbc611f8 (`key.md`; PREREG2,
`../avenel3_others_check/`). Transcription: `avenel3_others_figures.txt`; unedited decryption:
`avenel3_others_decryption.txt`; notes and count: `avenel3_others_reading.md`; translation: `avenel3_others_en.md`.
Written 20 Nov 2026 from the word-by-word table `../avenel3_others_check/word_count.tsv` (Brice Dudout, with Claude
Opus 5.5). The manuscripts were not seen.

Conventions:

- The clear text is Avenel's, as transcribed (abridged with …); his note calls are left out. Words written in cipher
  are between ⟦ ⟧, normalised (word division, accents); nomenclator figures are named by the key table.
- `[?x]` = a word the key does not give as such, counted as a hole: a code valued from Avenel's gloss at the place
  (53, 59, 89), 42 read *le/les* (the key has *la*), a corrected figure or a missing letter. `‹…›` = unread (63 in
  CCCXII; the run of CCCXVII n. 4).

## CLVI — Béthune 9331 fol. 44, original; [1er mai 1629], [à M. Bouthillier]; Avenel p. 297-298

C'est pourquoy le sieur Pancrace escrira, de la part de ⟦le Roi⟧ ⟦[?et]⟧ ⟦la Reine mère⟧, qu'au cas qu'on luy apporte advis ⟦de⟧ ⟦l'Angleterre⟧ qu'on envoye 2 ⟦ambassadeeurs⟧ ⟦la Reine mère⟧ advertisse, s'il luy plaist, promptement ⟦Elbeuf⟧ qu'il ⟦parte⟧ aussy tost.
