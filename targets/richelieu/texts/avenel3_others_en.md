# Avenel III, the other pieces in the same cipher (May 1629, March 1630): English translation

Translation of the cipher passages of the seven pieces, as read with the key (reading `avenel3_others_fr_reading.md`, unedited
decryption `avenel3_others_decryption.txt`, word-by-word table `../avenel3_others_check/word_count.tsv`). The clear text
around them is Avenel's (1858), abridged with … as in the transcription. Words that were in cipher are between ⟦ ⟧;
`[?x]` = a word the key does not give as such and that is counted as a hole (a code valued from the gloss at the place,
42 read *le/les*, a corrected figure); `‹…›` = unread. Translated 20 Nov 2026 (Brice Dudout, with Claude Opus 5.5).

## CLVI — Béthune 9331 fol. 44, [1 May 1629], to Bouthillier (doublet of CLV)

Pancrace [Bouthillier] will know that the ambassador ⟦of Venice⟧ doubts whether ⟦England⟧ will want two
⟦ambassadors, one great and one⟧ other, by sending two ⟦in [?exchange]⟧. If two are ⟦needed⟧, ⟦the King⟧ will think it
good, as first planned, to send ⟦Elbeuf and [?Châteauneuf]⟧. The ambassador's courier will bring word whether they ⟦will
send⟧ two ⟦from England⟧. So Pancrace will write, on behalf of ⟦the King [?and] the Queen Mother⟧, that if word comes
⟦from England⟧ that two ⟦ambassadors⟧ are sent, ⟦the Queen Mother⟧ should at once tell ⟦Elbeuf⟧ to ⟦leave⟧ immediately.
He will further write to ⟦the Queen Mother⟧ that if the courier who left ⟦from England⟧ on the 1st ⟦of May⟧ brings news
⟦from Contarini⟧ that he has in hand ⟦the signed articles of the peace⟧, ⟦the Queen Mother⟧ should have it ⟦published in
Paris on the [?20th of] May⟧.

## CLXXIV — Béthune 9331 fol. 46, [May 1629], to Bouthillier (?)

⟦The King⟧ writes to ⟦the Queen Mother⟧ a letter on the Spanish ambassador's publishing of the peace. … The gentleman
from ⟦Lorraine⟧ who goes to see ⟦Mme de Chevreuse⟧ goes no doubt to assure her of the solicitations ⟦Lorraine⟧ made
for ⟦Mme de Chevreuse⟧ on his journey. Although it is said he has changed ⟦mistress⟧, he spoke of it to me with great
affection. I referred him, as I wrote to you, to the true ⟦aversion of the King⟧, who will in no way have ⟦Mme de
Chevreuse⟧ ⟦[?near] [?the reigning Queen]⟧.

## CCCXII — AE Turin XI fol. 136, 5 March 1630

When the 10th of this month comes, on which ⟦[?the] six thousand⟧ loads of corn are to be supplied, I shall leave here
with the army. But only ⟦God and Nostradamus⟧ know what will happen. … have ⟦de Halier⟧ or another sent to the Pont de
Grezin … It is good to tell ⟦Chalot⟧ not yet to pay ⟦‹63›⟧ (Avenel: perhaps Marillac or La Force).

## CCCXIV — AE Turin XI fols. 187, 298, 8 March 1630, offers made to the Prince of Piedmont

… the six thousand sacks of corn that were to be in ⟦Casal⟧ on the 9th … As a token of great trust he proposed the
marriage ⟦of the [?cardinal] of [?Savoy]⟧ with the daughter of ⟦[?the Duke of Mantua]⟧, meaning, I think, to gain new
rights over ⟦[?the] [?Montferrat]⟧. Yet it would be a good occasion ⟦to [?marry] her⟧ if the affair of ⟦Monsieur⟧ were
settled. He told me His Highness wished ⟦to attack [?the] Genevese⟧, or that they should be reconciled with ⟦him⟧, which he
knows to be impossible, because ⟦Spinola⟧ will not allow it … that it was not reasonable that they ⟦should enter [?the]
Milanese⟧ before that agreement was made, because ⟦[?the] Genoese⟧ might ⟦attack [?them]⟧. I answered that we could get
a promise ⟦from them⟧ that they would not. … He then said that ⟦[?the] Venetians⟧ were bound to give him 100 ⟦thousand
crowns⟧ a month when he ⟦entered the Milanese⟧ … I did not wish to answer that the obligation of ⟦this treaty⟧ had lapsed,
having been made only for a time when they were jointly at ⟦war with Spain⟧ … the difficulties he was preparing for his
⟦entry into the Milanese⟧ … and his cannon would be in the ⟦plain of Piedmont⟧.

## CCCXVI — AE Turin XI fols. 300, 317, 9-10 March 1630, to the King

… the enterprise ⟦of Saint-Luc⟧ must be put off … (the cipher is quoted in Avenel's note).

## CCCXVII — AE Turin XI fol. 323, 10 March 1630, entries into Savoy

… Briançonnet, which is between the Tarentaise and ⟦the Morane⟧ (Avenel: read *la Maurienne*). … There is an enterprise
on the castle of Montmélian, made by ⟦‹l· d i g u i e r e p e r i n›⟧, is the leader of it (unread: Avenel gives the

## CCCXXXII — AE Turin XI fol. 359, 31 March 1630, memorandum for the King

If the King wants to make progress in Italy, he must of necessity ⟦attack Savoy⟧ without delay. To that end I have
written several times that two thousand men must be sent from ⟦[?Champagne]⟧ and 300 horse … There is no store of
munitions ⟦at Lyon⟧, nor on the frontier, unless there is some in ⟦Burgundy⟧; the guns needed are ready ⟦at Lyon⟧. …
Money must be sent for the upkeep of this army ⟦of Savoy⟧. … The King's army is in fact only ⟦twenty-one thousand⟧ men.
… So that we shall not have ⟦[?eigh]teen thousand men⟧ left (figures *cis huit*, 10 = c where *dix* wants d: the *dix* is the hole), from whom we must still
take a thousand … and what the King sends from ⟦[?Champagne]⟧ make seven thousand effective men …
