## Result

The survey found **no ciphered passage in the Bishop of Rennes' court cipher, deciphered or not**, in Colbert 391,
394 or 395. In particular:

- No Bourdin-countersigned 1562-63 letter with cipher. The single Bourdin 1563 item (395 p. 93, 20 Apr 1563) is
  all clear text.
- No Catherine 1563 letter in the fr. 3181 f.55/57 hand. Catherine's letters are all in vol. I (Colbert 390),
  which was already harvested.
- The only two ciphers found belong to other correspondents (Tournon 1557, Boistaillé 1561), and neither has a
  decipherment.

## Not surveyed

- **Vol. VIII (btv1b100339488, 135 views): 23 Bourdin letters, 1557-61.** These are pre-1562 but are the best
  remaining source for Bourdin-office hands and for any early Bourdin cipher with margin glosses. It is the next
  volume to sweep. bourdin1561_glossed.md may already cover some of them.
- Vol. IV (393, btv1b10033960h): 1554-59 Swiss embassy, less likely to be relevant.
- BnF fr. 3180 and 3182 were not checked.

Limitation: at 800 px per view, a one-word cipher group or a faint margin gloss could be missed. The pages
listed above were checked at full resolution.
