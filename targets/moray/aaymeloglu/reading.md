# Moray to Wood, 13 July 1568, cipher postscript: reading and translation (issue #553, aaymeloglu, CC0)

Scots reading (contributor's edition; holes marked here by the strict rule, 14 Nov 2026):

> The suffering of [Q] remane in Carlyle, and sending haym of the Lord Fleming, hes done greit evil. Gif the [Q2] cummis
> haom thair, la[?]is na mair to s[p]il the haill e[?] oure freindis [seven signs unresolved].

English (contributor's):

> The allowing of [the Queen] to remain in Carlisle, and the sending home of Lord Fleming, has done great harm. If the [Queen]
> comes home there, [...] no more to ruin the whole [...] our friends [...].

Conjectured ending, not established (contributor): "lakkis na mair to spil the haill es oure freindis knauis" ("nothing more
is needed to ruin the whole, as our friends know").

(word-sign inferred from position), Carlyle (c = r graded M, the second c read through as a slip), la??is, spil (s = p fixed by
