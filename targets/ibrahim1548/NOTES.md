# Sigismund August, Frater Georg Martinuzzi and Queen Isabella to Ibrahim Pasha, 1548 (catalogue 885)

Status: not applicable (item could not be reached). Key candidate identified; no transcription made.

## Documents
HHStA Wien, StAbt Türkei I:
- 7-4-4 (AIS 7040827), fol. 28-30: Sigismund August to Ibrahim Pasha. 2 cipher copies, 1 Latin copy. Beilage zu 7-4-3.
- 7-4-5 (AIS 7040824), fol. 31-34: Frater Georg to Ibrahim Pasha, 6 Jun - 2 Jul 1548. 3 cipher copies, 1 Latin copy. Beilage zu 7-4-3.
- 7-4-9 (AIS 7040845), fol. 51-59: Malvezzi to Ferdinand I, with two enclosed cipher copies.

## Why it is blocked
All three AIS records (checked 29 Oct 2026) read:
"Erforderliche Bewilligung: Kein Zugang (Reservatbestand)"; "Physische Benützbarkeit: Nicht möglich";
"Zugänglichkeit: Ausgewählte Archivmitarbeiter/-innen". A digital copy exists ("Reproduktion vorhanden:
Digitale Aufnahme"), but the public image view (bildansicht.aspx) shows nothing to an anonymous user.
The images need a request to the HHStA.

## Key candidate
The cipher copies were made in Malvezzi's office in 1548. The Ferdinand–Malvezzi key of 1548
(DECODE R367, R386; applied in targets/malvezzi1548) has code words for exactly these persons:
Vb = rex Poloniae, zos = Frater Georgius, zes = Regina Isabella, zis = Joannes Sigismundus,
Sis = Ibrahim Passa. First test once images arrive: apply that key. The Latin exemplar filed with
each letter is probably the plaintext, so it gives a direct check (method would then be
"external key matched", or "key from adjacent plaintext" if the key differs).
