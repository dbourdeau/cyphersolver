LABEL SHEET (names only, no values)
Cipher signs in these letters look like Latin letters or digits, sometimes with a small digit written above/after (superscript).
1. Label each base sign by the Latin letter or digit it most resembles, case-sensitive, from this set: 8 C D K Ko L L2 N P Ri T W X a b bt c d e f g h j m o p q r s sg t u v w x y z
2. If a sign carries a superscript digit (1,3,5,7,9 or other), write label+digit with no space, e.g. b3, K7.
3. Number groups written as numerals standing for a word: write #nn (e.g. #1 #11 #12 #13 #14 #15 ...).
4. Other marks seen in this system: A R/ ~ E/ GR . (write as shown). A full stop/point between groups: '.'.
5. Anything not covered: NEW1, NEW2 ... and describe it in notes. Doubtful: trailing '?'.
