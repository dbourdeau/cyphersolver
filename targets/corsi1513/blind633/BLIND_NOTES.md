## Conventions and difficulties
- Numerals parsed against the sheet's list: "1" alone = #1; "1"+"2"-shape = #12; "1"+"ʒ"-shape = #13; "15" = #15; "14" = #14; "1814" = #1 8 #14; "1519" = #15 #19? (doubtful).
- z = the tailed ʒ shape. NEW1 = a flat "2"-shaped sign (looks like the digit 2 / round r), frequent before z ("2ʒ" pair), o, a, 8, and "1".
- NEW2 = boxed/barred triple-stroke sign like "III" with a top and bottom bar (L12, once).
- R/ = the ℞-like sign (R with crossed tail), L7 and L11-L12.
- E/? (L9) = E with a vertical/cross stroke rising above; doubtful whether this is the sheet's E/.
- bt = b with crossbar through the ascender (L8, L10).
- g = the "8"-like sign with open lower loop in the frequent pair "q g"; 8 = closed figure-8.
- L9 ".?" = a light virgule-like stroke after "q5 u", rendered as a point with doubt.
- Superscript attribution is the hardest part: superscripts often sit between two signs; assigned to the sign below or to the left. Superscript 2 occurs (c2, q2, y2, L? none).
- L8 "81?" = small 8/ϵ-like sign with superscript 1; L12 "b?" could be a digit 6 (#16).
