## P10 = f.218v, address leaf (written sideways), two red seal traces:
{Illmo et Exmo Dno Dno Mediolani Duci, Dno suo semper Colmo [colendissimo]  --  Cremone}
 -> ADDRESSEE = the Duke of Milan, i.e. Francesco II Sforza, then at Cremona (Milan itself was held by the imperial
    army; Sforza held Cremona from 1526). So "Illmo et Exmo unico mio Signore" on P1 = Francesco II Sforza, and the
    writer "el Gilino" is a Milanese (Sforza) agent at the imperial court in Castile.
 Also faint dorse marks at left near the seals: illegible ("Gil..."?); top: faint offset "Hispania 1527" bleed-through.

## Summary of clues in the clear text
- Writer at Paredes de Nava (imperial court in Palencia area), 9-15 Sept 1527; Sforza agent.
- Names in clear: Gran Cancelliere (Gattinara) and Gio. Bartolomeo di Gattinara; Mons. de Bourbon (Martino Centurione
  his "general in herba"?); French ambassadors; English ambassadors; Palencia; Portofino/Genoa/Alessandria (Lautrec's
  campaign, Aug 1527); capitoli di Madrid; Ludovico Catti (Ferrarese ambassador); Mons. di Tarbes (Gabriel de
  Gramont); conte di Caiazzo; conte di Monteleone, vicere di Sicilia; Marco Pio (of Carpi?).
- Recurring cipher group "a++ Hvv P.25" / "vv a++ Hvv P.25" (P1 l.1, l.8; P2 l.9; P5 l.9; P6 l.14; P7 l.11; P9 l.1-2,
  l.10) and P2 l.15 "... vv P.25 {vra Extia}" suggest "Vra Extia"/"V. Extia" or the like is often enciphered too;
  the clear "vra Extia" inside cipher (P2 l.15) is right after "vv P.25".
