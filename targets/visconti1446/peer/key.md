# Key: "Filippo Maria Duca di Milano" (DECODE R1857, image P17)

Source: ASMi, Potenze Sovrane cart. 1592 "Cifrari", an 18th-century copy of the duke's cipher, on DECODE as
R1857 ("Sovereign Powers 1592 Cifrari", 24 images, key record, Authentication required), image 17
(`IMG_R1857_I8470_P17.png`, 2220x3308). Read 28 Oct 2026 from that image (crops `k17_a1.jpg`, `k17_a2.jpg`,
`k17_b0..b3.jpg`, `k17_c.jpg`; images git-ignored). Sign descriptions are mine; `?` marks a form I could not
fix on the copy. The copyist's blots (several cells) are listed as "blot".

Related page: R1857 P11, "Marcolinus de Barbavara et Symon Arrigonus - Petro de Pusterla", a sibling alphabet of
the same family (Tomokiyo: the key of BnF ital. 1584 f. 69, 1447). It is NOT the alphabet of the duke's slips
(f. 5 reads with P17; with P11, r, s, t, i would be wrong).
