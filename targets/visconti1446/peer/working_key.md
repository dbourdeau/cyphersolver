# Working key: the signs as used on the slips ff. 5 and 8 (BnF ital. 1584)

Calibrated 28 Oct 2026 against the contemporary translations ff. 6 and 9. Source transcriptions:
`texts/f005_transcription.tsv` (689 tokens) and `texts/f008_transcription.tsv` (647 tokens); readings and
alignment in `texts/f005_reading.md`, `texts/f008_reading.md`. Counts below are from those files (f. 5 + f. 8).
P17 = DECODE R1857 image 17, as read in `key.md`. "conf." = value confirmed by the translation.

## Counts

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| f. 5 | 689 | 631 | 35 | 23 |

| both | 1336 | 1239 | 67 | 30 |

Classification rule (applied by script to the notes): *unresolved* = an unread run, the filler tail after the
signature, or a sign marked unresolved; *corrected* = a sign not in P17 whose value comes from the alignment, the
S sign used as the conjunction *e*, II used for *vu* of *vuy*, ∿ð# as the Mantua name, the doubtful CON stroke;
*agree* = everything else (spelling differences of the original, *Pedro* for *Piero*, *u* = *v*, *i* = *y/j*, are
not counted as disagreements). Nulls that are P17 nulls count as agreeing.

## Corrections and additions to key.md

1. **c-like sign with a stroke on top (ꞇ / ɛ̄-like) = i.** 20 tokens on the two slips (f. 5: 11, f. 8: 9; one of the f. 5 tokens is in the unread tail), always
   where the translation has *i*: *ringratiamo* 5/3/43, *cossi* 5/4/21, *advisi* 5/4/35, *via* 5/6/27, *gli* 5/8/3,
   *faciamo* 5/9/2, *habiamo* 5/11/22, *Abiate* 5/13/38, *confortiamo* 8/1/36, *vostri* 8/2/31, *piu* 8/8/32, *sia*
   8/9/11, 8/14/6, *die* 8/14/35 ... P17's i column (b, 5 with bar, 6, 3, ı) does not have it: a homophone the
   18th-century copy omits (or one of its blots). Close to the ⊢ of e; the two are told apart by the top stroke.
   Two ⊢-like tokens at 8/16/11 and 8/16/17 (*Papie*, *Anglerie*) and 5/14/8, 8/5/20, 8/13/10 also read i.
2. **S (P17 f#1) is also the conjunction *e/et*.** 26 tokens (f. 5: 15, f. 8: 11): standing alone between words it is always *e*
   (*e de Piero* 5/1/10, *e quanto* 5/2/7, *e concluso* 5/2/33, *e pregandove* 5/5/8, *Et* 5/5/46 ...). Inside words it
   is f (*facto* 5/7/13, 5/11/25, *informa* 8/4/25, *Filipus* 8/15/30). Probably a word sign ET written like the f,
   not in the copy's word list. Counted as corrected where it is *e*.
3. **II (P17 g#1) followed by 3 or ı = *vuy*.** 8 tokens of II before i (5/2/12, 8/3/9, 8/4/3, 8/5/6, 8/5/36,
   8/10/6, 8/11/37, 8/14/24): each time f. 6/9 has *vuy*. Elsewhere II is g (*gli* 5/8/1, *Anglus* 5/14/40,
   *Anglerieque* 8/16/14). So II3 is a *vuy* group (II = *vu*), or II is also a *v* homophone; the slips do not decide.
4. **∿ð# = Marchese di Mantova** (P17: a name sign under a blank heading). 5/6/18, f. 6 *el Marchexe de Mantoa*.
5. **Duca di Milano** written ∿ + ɑ-like mark on f. 5 (5/14/42), ∿∞ on f. 8 (8/16/3): the signature *Dux*.
6. **"per" (letters) = `or`, "par" = `os`** as in P17 (conf. 9 and 19 tokens: *confortandove*, *forma*, *ancora*,
   *vostre*, *posto*, *cossi*).
7. P17 doubtful syllable signs, now confirmed: `ad` = 9 with a stroke (*adesso* 5/8/12, 5/11/34); `af` = 9# (*afare*
   8/4/39); `ap` = ꝑ with + (8 tokens, *saputo*, *sapere*, *Papie*); `ot` = ꝑ with a stroke (*poteremo* 5/13/27);
   `ag` = q/g with ÷ and dots (*agitato* 5/2/30); `as` = 5 (*quaranta septe*, *pasato*); `ud` = ∞+ (*indutto*
   5/11/1); `ur` = struck m/y (*securo*, *pur*, 4 tokens); `un` = ɱ-like (9 tokens, *alcuna*, *uno*, *undecimo*);
   `ip` = ¬z / ⅂ (*Filipus*, 2); `od` = p with a stroke (*modo* 8/2/7); `ir` = w (*avenire*, *tenire*, *aparira*,
   *dire*, 4).
8. **ı with a dot = n** (5/1/53, *ne*), one token; **6-like sign = e** (5/4/31, *del*), one token, maybe P17's
   blotted fourth e; **8/S-like sign = f** (*faremo* 5/8/4, *Filipus* 5/14/30), two tokens.
9. **Word signs as used:** PER (q-bar+o, 8), QUE (q with ꝛ, 12; also inside *questo*), CHE (q÷ with dots, 19; also
   inside *rechiedemo*), CON (6 with tilde, 10; also *con-tra*, *con-sentimento*, *con-tentissimi*), NON (q≠, 7),
   QUANTO (3), PERCHE (q with + above, 5), COME (go, 6), QUI (q+o, 2), QUA (q with +, 4: *qua-trocento*,
   *qua-ranta*), PERO (1). All conf.
10. **q with + above and two dots** (5/5/47) stands where f. 6 has *poi*: a word sign not in the copy (doubtful,
    one token).

## Nulls in practice

P17 nulls seen (19 tokens): + (8: f. 5 6, f. 8 2; between words and at line ends), π (6), oto (2), ♀ with side
strokes (5/15/30), ffl/ff+ (8/16/32), 3 with ring above (8/3/1). Also null in practice, not in the copy: the small ɔ before QUANTO at 5/4/8
(no counterpart; doubtful). The ♀ sign is also b (*bon*, *ben*, 8/11/30, 8/14/8): as b it stands alone, as null
it carries side strokes.
