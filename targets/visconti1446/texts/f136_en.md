# BnF ital. 1583 f. 136 (DECODE R7907): English translation. Filippo Maria Visconti to Francesco Sforza, Milan, 29 November [1446]

Translated from `f136_text.md` (the cipher reading). The single unread signs all fall inside words whose sense is
clear; the words they complete are given in square brackets.

On the fifteenth of last month we received a brief of yours; having understood it, immediately on the following day
we sent to you Zohanne (Giovanni) del Maino, with the widest mandate and power to do whatever we ourselves could have
done.

Yet up to now we have seen no effects at all, except that all your men-at-arms who were in Cremona and in the
Cremonese have now gone off and joined the camp of our enemies.

We therefore remind you, being the man you are, to think well and consider that it would be better and more
honourable to win as one ought, than to keep us waiting and feed us hopes; since we then have no reason to make
provision for our own affairs, [this would be] the cause of putting them in danger.

This we have wished to remind you of, because it does not seem to us that such revenge on one's own enemies is a
thing for men such as you.

Given at Milan on the twenty-ninth day of November.

Philippus Maria Anglus, Duke of Milan etc., Count of Pavia and Angera and Lord of Genoa.
