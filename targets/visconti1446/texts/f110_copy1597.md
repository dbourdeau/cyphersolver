# BnF italien 1597 ff. 123r-123v: 19th-century fair copy of the clear text of ital. 1583 f. 110

Source: BnF italien 1597, "Copie moderne di documenti", Gallica ark:/12148/btv1b10037396j, canvas 126 (right page,
folio 123, numbered "(41.)") and canvas 127 (left page = 123v). Fetched 29 Oct 2026 at full resolution
(8588x6190) through the BnF IIIF image API; strips cropped and read by eye (images in `img/c1597/`, git-ignored).
The copy ends on 123v with the date and title; 124r (canvas 127 right page) is a different letter, "(42.)
1446 7 9bre Milan".

Heading: `1446 1er 9bre.` (left), `Au Cte François Sforza.` (centre), `123 (41.)` (top right), BnF stamp.

Line by line as on the page. `[?]` = word I am unsure of; `[blank]` = a space the copyist left open.
Spelling as written (the copyist keeps *gi* = vui/voi, *za* = qua, *zoè* = cioè).

## Notes

- Two blanks marked `V+` (ll. 26, 40) where the copyist could not render a sign: both stand where a name is
  needed (*dicemo de li [Fiorentini?]*; *che ve dano li [Fiorentini?]*: the people who pay Sforza a provision, set
  against the Venetians). The cipher has a name sign at both places (see `f110_reading.md`).
- l. 12 *miclo* (copy: "meclo"/"miclo"): a crumb; the cipher has *miclo* (*micolo*?); see the reading.
- l. 45 *faresemo*: last letters cut by the binding; read *fareseno*/*faresemo*.
- l. 51 *que* is struck through by the copyist.
- The copy was made from a decipherment of the cipher, not from a clear original: *gi* for *vui* (ll. 23, 35, 37,
  41, 43) reproduces the cipher group II + 3 letter for letter, *za* reproduces the z sign + a, and the two "V+"
  blanks fall exactly on the cipher's name sign ∿ð+. See `f110_reading.md`.
- l. 33 *Tanaro*: the cipher has ∴ (p), i.e. *Panaro*.
