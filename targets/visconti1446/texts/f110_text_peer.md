Conventions: letters in square brackets are letters the cipher does not write, supplied from the context ([c]osi,
[p]otese, [p]osati); [?] one unread sign, [..] an unread run of several signs (on the verso up to 16 signs; the
verso runs are signs I could not segment on the binarized DECODE image, not signs missing from the key). [∿ð+] is
the name sign left blank in the P17 key (the 1597 copy leaves it blank too). *gi* is the cipher's group II + 3,
read *vuy* (you) in the working key; it is kept as written, as the copy also writes it. Consonantal u is printed v;
nulls are dropped; word division and punctuation are editorial. The recto reads 96.5% of its non-null signs, the
verso 69.6%.
