## Summary

Milan, 29 November 1446, the duke to Sforza.
- On the 15th of last month the duke received Sforza's breve. The next day he sent Zohanne del Maino to him, with
  the widest mandate and power to do whatever the duke himself could have done.
- So far no effect has been seen. On the contrary, all of Sforza's men-at-arms who were in Cremona and the
  Cremonese have now gone over and joined the camp of the duke's enemies.
- The duke reminds him, as the man he is, to consider that it would be better and more honourable to win as one
  should, than to keep the duke waiting and feed him hopes. That way the duke has no reason to make provision for his
  own affairs, and that could put them in danger.
- He says such revenge on one's own enemies is not fitting for men like Sforza.

Names: Zohanne del Maino (of the family of Agnese del Maino, the duke's mistress and Sforza's mother-in-law),
Cremona, the Cremonese.

## Open
