# Prior art (supplied research, not a new online search)

K. M. M. de Leeuw, *Cryptology and statecraft in the Dutch Republic* (University of Amsterdam doctoral thesis, 2000), describes Fagel codebooks but does not discuss Battier. No edition of these letters was found in the supplied research. The letters are not in DECODE.

Thesis: https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/3074957/12760_Thesis.pdf (citation only; PDF not copied).

DECODE R2794 holds the key but cites inv. nr. 5345, absent from the current NA 1.10.29 inventory. The matching key is inv. 1209, headed “Voor den Heer Extraord. Envoye Battier”.

The States General set, NA 1.01.02 inv. 12588.120–125, is not digitised. No claim of universal absence of an unpublished decipherment is made.
