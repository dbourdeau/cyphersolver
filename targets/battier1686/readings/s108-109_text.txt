109
Want hy merckt wel, dat als den Grand Maistre offe een creatuer van Oropesa na Vlaanderen komt, de Nederlandse toestemminge(?), gelyk in tyt van Grana's geschiedt, door syn handen niet veel sullen passeren. [Ick blyve met respect ...]

English gist: By the last post the Grand Master [Ludwig Anton] ordered his agent to assure the Queen Mother that the Emperor's letters would arrive within 14 days; Melgar is still soliciting [the Netherlands government]. No money will go to the Netherlands as long as Gastañaga remains governor. A project (by "Holstein"?) to maintain 30,000 men
in the Netherlands from local revenues plus 75,000 pieces of eight a month was handed to Oropesa. A sworn party is forming against
Oropesa - the Constable of Castile, Osuna(?) and Monterey are plotting together. A creditor threatened Monterey he would complain to
Oropesa; Monterey became so angry he could hardly leave his house; he now fancies the presidency of (the Council of) Italy, or that of
Flanders, which is more profitable - for he sees that if the Grand Master [Ludwig Anton of Pfalz-Neuburg] or a creature of Oropesa goes
to Flanders, little of the Netherlands business will pass through his hands, as happened in Grana's time.
