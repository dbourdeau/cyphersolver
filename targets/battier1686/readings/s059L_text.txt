[Ick blyve met respect, Wel Edele Gestrenge Heer, U Wel Ed. Gestr. Ootmoedigste en gehoorsaemste Dienaar P. Battier. Madrit den 24 April 1687]

English gist: The Imperial ambassador (Mansfeld) moves heaven and earth to regain the Queen's favour, but has no chance at all, as
with the King; he employs a nun close to the Queen, courts the confessor and ministers, and has renewed friendship with the Countess
of Soissons, hoping through her (who stands very well with the Queen) to obtain good offices; but it seems the Queen will never forget
the harm the ambassador did her.
