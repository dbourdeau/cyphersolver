English gist: The Spanish government of the Netherlands is to be decided (Melgar or Leganés; Gastañaga then viceroy of Catalonia),
all uncertain. Someone ("Monsieur Nouelli"?) is sounding things out for the son of the Elector Palatine (the Grand Master); but the
Palatine side will make no claim for fear it would gain nothing, while Vienna works to make the Grand Master president of the war council.
Madrid will not press the matter: there is still time enough, and one wants to give the Palatinate time to think it over well.
