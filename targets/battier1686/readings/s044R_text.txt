[... over de twintigh-jaarige treves] in een vrede te con[senteren], en dat men het achter ruchs [rugge] van den Keyser wilde doen.

accommodement van [11:846 Havana?; decipherer "Philippe Ville"] is.

English gist: The Imperial ambassador suspects a plan to turn the Twenty Years' Truce into a peace behind the Emperor's back and
asked what the States General thought; Battier answered they want only general peace and their allies' satisfaction, and that if
the Emperor made peace with the Turks France would hardly act. The ambassador believes even the Emperor's friends begrudge his
victories and would like him forced to end the Turkish war; an express courier from Brussels alarmed him, but it only concerned the
Philippeville arrangement.
