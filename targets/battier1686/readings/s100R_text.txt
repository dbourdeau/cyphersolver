English gist: [The claim that someone wanted the title of Prince of Asturias with an allowance as heir presumptive is] without
foundation: Mansfeld had it put about in order to bring the Imperial prince to Madrid, which cannot be done. The Grand Master
(Ludwig Anton of Pfalz-Neuburg), [suspected?] in the last letters, was to consult his father (the Elector Palatine) how best to have
this business sent by the Emperor to the Council of the Netherlands - it now seems serious, and the Imperial letters are expected
by the first post. (Context: candidature of the Grand Master for the governorship of the Spanish Netherlands, spring 1688.)
