NA 3.01.18 inv. 401, scan 92 right page: end of the letter, "Mad. den 4 Decemb. 1687", signed "Ootmoedighsten ongehoorsaemsten
Dienaar Battier". Helper hD. NO CIPHER on this page (entirely clear Dutch): Leganés wants service and lodging money for 14 men
(where before he had no 4 men in effective service); the Prince of Parma arrived with a train of 140 persons and receives 1000 pistoles
a month plus the charge of prince de la mer; Maria de la O.'s husband given a post in Granada; Valenzuela enjoys more freedom and
better treatment; "men arbeit continuellement aan 't redresseeren van de Monarchie" without much progress.
