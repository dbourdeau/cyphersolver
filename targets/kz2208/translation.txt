1. Suggest to the commander of the High Seas Fleet that a submarine accompany operations against the Firth of Forth or be deployed in support.
2. For the commander of the High Seas Fleet. The aim is to capture Dunkirk in the next few days and Boulogne later. Enemy naval forces are expected to take part in the defense. Keep submarines ready for use there as soon as the submarines currently in the English Channel return.
3. I also believe the submarines in the Baltic are British. His Majesty the Kaiser agrees to the use of the VIII Flotilla in the Baltic until the submarine threat has passed. The later arrangements under point 5 of the telephone message of 19 October at 3:30 p.m. are approved. Notify the commander of the High Seas Fleet and the Baltic commander. Signed, von Pohl.
4. The Army needs the anti-balloon guns stationed at Namur. Please establish immediately whether and when we can protect the airship hangars there.
