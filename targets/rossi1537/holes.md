# Holes, supplied letters and doubtful words in the readings

Marks used in `reading_A.md`, `reading_B.md` and the translations: `[...]` signs not read; `[?word]` a word proposed and doubtful; a bracket inside a word (`ha[v]erà`) is a letter supplied that the sheet does not have. Word division, capitals, accents and punctuation are added. u and v are one sign; v is printed where modern spelling has it. Roman numerals and the Latin word *fuit* are in clear on the sheet. Lines beginning `>` in the readings are the clear close in the writer's own hand, not cipher.

"Units" is the number of cipher units counted unread (N) in `units.tsv`, the ledger stamped on 11 Nov 2026. A word marked `[?word]` with 0 units is one whose letters the key gives but whose sense is doubtful, or which needs a letter supplied.
