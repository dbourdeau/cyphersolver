# NYCD X omission check, repeated 12 November 2026

Source: *Documents relative to the Colonial History of the State of New-York*, X (1858), pp. 669–671; Internet Archive identifier `documentsrelativ10newyuoft`. [Digitized volume](https://archive.org/details/documentsrelativ10newyuoft).

The local OCR file was independently re-opened during packaging, rather than relying only on the earlier research conclusion. At local lines 42183 and 42255 it explicitly marks the omitted cipher paragraphs:

> Here follows, in the original, a paragraph in cipher, which is omitted in the French text.

> Here [is] omitted another paragraph in cipher.

The second OCR reads “U” for “is”; the brackets above are an editorial normalization, not a fresh page-image reading. The letter itself mentions the separate cipher sheet twice: after the inspection of the North Shore, and at its close where Montcalm refers to the next campaign. The printed letter ends without that sheet’s text. This checks absence from this edition; it does not establish that the cipher passages were never published elsewhere.
