# Meza Salinas to Bernardo Reyes, 12 October 1901

Status: in progress. Source files assembled for the write-up and reading review.

Anna Beck and Larry Beck, with ChatGPT, reconstructed 46 values from the Spanish context beside the cipher. Their account says the separate period decipherment was unused. It was recorded by Brice Dudout in the repository snapshot they consulted, and the original browsing sequence is unavailable. The earlier review reproduced 142 groups in 23 runs and counted 21 of 24 cipher words from the key and three from the period sheet. Keep FLORSE, INSULTADS and PRESIDNNTN as literal slips where they occur. The received text of the typed sheet needs a separate transcription before filing.
