# Meza Salinas to Bernardo Reyes, 12 October 1901

Status: read.

Anna Beck and Larry Beck, with ChatGPT, reconstructed 46 values from the Spanish context beside the cipher. Their account says the separate period decipherment was unused. It was recorded by Brice Dudout in the repository snapshot they consulted, and the original browsing sequence is unavailable. The earlier review reproduced 142 groups in 23 runs and counted 21 of 24 cipher words from the key and three from the period sheet. Keep FLORSE, INSULTADS and PRESIDNNTN as literal slips where they occur. The linked period witness, DLI.34.6703.2, attests the three repaired words. The full visible text is transcribed in `contemporary-typescript.txt`. The typescript omits “de este mes”, inserts “Sr.” and has “alguno” for the key’s “alguien”. It signs T. Meza y Salinas. [CEHM original DLI.34.6704.1](https://www.cehm.org.mx/Buscador/FichaBasica/120053?Tipo=A)

Source submission and review: https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/issues/791

## Transcription doubts

- Written runs C01, C09, C10 - read: florse, insultads, presidnntn; checked: image as described by the contributor or prior review, decode; The original review checked the manuscript and cited the period typescript for Flores, insultados and presidente. The literal file preserves the cipher output.
- Signature flourish - read: initial uncertain; checked: image as described by the contributor or prior review, decode; The contributor left the flourish open in the transcription. The later clear letter signs T. Meza y Salinas.
