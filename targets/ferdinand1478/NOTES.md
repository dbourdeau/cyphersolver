## Documents

Archivo Histórico Provincial de Zaragoza (AHPZ, *Archivo del Reino de Aragón*, *Colección Zurita* / *Alacena de Zurita*):

R. de Castilla 1478"*; damaged address *"[A la] maiestat del Rey mi / [senyor e pad]re muy reduptable"*). Bears a partial contemporary interlinear gloss **`y su bi vn gia`** above `L21` tokens 9-15; otherwise unglossed in the manuscript.
- **Contemporary Sibling Witness (`C_CRAR/000001/0022`, Medina del Campo, 15 August 1477):** Previously described in Ansorena 2017, lot 503, item 22, noting its contemporary interlinear decipherment; located two folders away in the same file during a 16-document sweep of `AHPZ C_CRAR/000001/`. Written ten months earlier by the same correspondents and countersigned by `J. Arinyo secr.`, its **24 physical cipher lines** on the recto (labeled `L01-L21, L22a-L22c`) carry a **syllable-by-syllable contemporary interlinear decipherment** in John II's chancery (`transcript_crar22.txt`, `contemporary_crar22.txt`, `crar22_witness_alignment.txt`). ## The system and the key (`key_ferdinand1478.tsv`)

The 1477-1478 Ferdinand-John II royal cipher family was already published in Satoshi Tomokiyo (2018, §2.4, on nine BNE letters chiefly of 9 April 1478), María Josep Bertomeu Masiá (pp. 212-213, grid slots `3..12`), and Mikel García Larragan's 16 July 2026 blog decipherment of `#2854`. Our table `key_ferdinand1478.tsv` was independently rebuilt from the contemporary interlinear decipherment of sibling letter `C_CRAR/000001/0022` in the same file:
- **Regular 75-slot CV-syllabary (`3-77`):** 15 consonants (`b, c, d, g, f, l, n, m, p, s, r, t, x, z, y`) x 5 vowels (`-a, -e, -i, -o, -u`) numbered consecutively `3..77` (`57/75` directly witnessed in `CRAR_22`, `10` additional grid slots in `#2854` incl. `L03 45=pi`, plus `20=gi` confirmed by both the grid and `#2854 L21`'s gloss `gia`).
- **Homophonic single letters and digraphs:** Vowels (`q_bar/q_dbl/93 = a`, `tt/tt_dot/t_a = e`, `ct/ct_dot/c_tail/c_dots = i`, `e_dot/f = o`, `4/L_dot = u/v`, `0/n/n_br = y`) and consonants (`b_bar = s` shifted from `h/g` in 1477 while retaining `1x g` in `L57 peligro`; `b_dbl/b_bar_dot = h`; `e_tail = n`; `gt/gt_dot/gtt = r`; `b/1 = s`; `b_tail/k_bar = t`; `d_bar = l/m`; `B_bar/f_dbl = c`; `ff/ftt = p`; `g = q`; `long_s = b`; `t_dot = d`; `m = x`; `B = ya`).
- **Systematic two-letter function-word codes (`9` entries):** Witness-attested `de = como`, `di = porque`, `do = por`, `du = que`, `fu = del`, `q_d = al`, `p_bar = es`; plus `f+vowel` codes inferred from `#2854` alone (`fa = quanto` `3x`, where Larragan reads *carta*; `fi = qual` `2x`; `fo = quel` `2x` — all 7 resulting words are bracketed as inferred in `reading_2854.md`).
- **Royal and territorial nomenclators (`8` entries, total `17` word-code/nomenclator entries):** Witness-attested `gi = Vuestra Alteza` (`16x` in `#2854`), `gam2 (82) = Rey` (`CRAR_22 L05` glosses *Reyna*; transferred to masculine *Rey* `8x` on `#2854` + `L11 [reyna]` + `L57` slip), `gam4 (84) = Conde` (`3x`), `gam6 (86) = Reyno` (`CRAR_22 L21` `rey[no]`, `#2854 L54` + `L06` slip), `95 = Sicilia` (`3x`: `L38, L39, L41`, matched by AI model crop check to `CRAR_22 L02/L06/L19`), plus codes inferred from `#2854` alone (`96 = Cerdenya` `4x` on `L39, L40, L45, L46`, distinct from syllabic `L38 Cerdenya`; `99 = Francia` `4x` on `L13, L16, L25, L29` — all 8 resulting words are bracketed as inferred in `reading_2854.md`) and `1` unresolved token `{oo?}` (`L40`; Larragan reads *aquí*).

## Validation (`validate.py`, `validation.txt`)

The packaged validator (`validate.py`) uses `cyphersolver_repo`'s built-in order-5 `DenseLM` (`es-golden-age-nospace`, zero overlap with `#2854` or held-out `ferdinand1473`) and unboosted `lang/lexicons/es.json.gz` (+ `ca.json.gz` at `0.1` weight, with zero custom boosts). `validate.py` computes PASS/FAIL dynamically (`z_lm >= 5.0` and `p <= 0.01` on both 200-shuffle LM controls) and exits `1` on failure (verified on all-`'e'` and all-`'a'` keys):
1. **Strict zero-choice fixed-key 200-shuffle controls (`validate.py` -> `validation.txt`):** On `transcript_2854.txt` (`1,915` tokens, `1,914` mapped = `99.95%`, `3,323` decoded chars, `894` Viterbi words):
   - Real text: `LM/ch = -2.1317`, `WordHit = 0.9452`, `Len3Hit = 0.9270`, `Cov>=4 = 0.5429`, `Cov>=5 = 0.4550`.
   - 200 shuffled keys: `LM/ch = -4.0639 ± 0.2469` (`max = -3.3823`, **z = +7.82**), `Cov>=4` **z = +5.36**, `Cov>=5` **z = +5.44** (`0/200`, `p = 0.0050`).
   - 200 shuffled streams: `LM/ch = -3.6751 ± 0.0340` (`max = -3.5759`, **z = +45.38**), `Cov>=4` **z = +26.47**, `Cov>=5` **z = +40.11** (`0/200`, `p = 0.0050`).

## Historical verification (Seville, 9 June 1478)

- **Roussillon and Cerdagne escrow plan (`L01-L33`):** Reveals secret three-way diplomacy in June 1478 whereby King Ferrante I of Naples (*el Rey don Ferrando*) and Ferdinand's sister Queen Juana (*la Reyna mi hermana*, married Sept 1477) proposed placing French-occupied Roussillon and Cerdagne under Ferrante's third-party custody (*intervenidor*) while auditing Louis XI's debt claims, even as Ferdinand pursued parallel direct talks with France that culminated in the Treaty of Saint-Jean-de-Luz (9 October 1478).
- **Lieutenancy of the Infante (`L33-L36`):** Ferdinand endorses the *lugartinencia del Infante* (Enrique de Aragón y Pimentel, *el Infante Fortuna*).
- **Sardinia, Sicily, and Don Leonardo de Alagón (`L36-L63`):** Three weeks after the Battle of Macomer (19 May 1478), Ferdinand questions his 79-year-old father John II on recalling the Count of Cardona (*Joan Ramon Folch III de Cardona*) from Sardinia to Sicily, names `mossen Pachs` and `Micer Uilanoua` (`L46`), and warns that Viceroy Nicolás Carroz de Arborea is secretly negotiating to marry his daughter to rebel Marquis Leonardo de Alagón's eldest son (*"que los dos queden senyores de [aquel] reyno"*).
