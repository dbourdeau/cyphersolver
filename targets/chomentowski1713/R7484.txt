R7484 (DECODE "Krakow_UNK_3") -- Stanisław Chomentowski, wojewoda mazowiecki, Polish envoy to the Porte, to Hetman
Adam Mikołaj Sieniawski, Adrianople, 9 May 1713. Not in Kaczka (Kaczka cites only Brâncoveanu's letters TO
Chomentowski). Archive foliation 111-114; old no. "Nr 124 116".
Images: P1 = p. 111 (superscription "Monseigneur", then the letter: clear Polish with a 3-digit syllabic code);
P4 = pp. 112-113 (letter continued, mostly code, signed, dated "w Adrynopolu 9 Maja 1713"); P2 = p. 114, address
leaf, docket "9 Maj 1713 / Chomentowskiego do Sieniawskiego z Andrynopola / Listów 2 / Nr 124 116"; P3 = modern
archive wrapper: "Nr 116 / Chomentowski Stanisław / 1713 / Poseł na Porcie ... / 2 Listy Chomentowskiego do Sieniaw-
skiego. Pierwszy z Adrynopola 1713 cyfrowany pisany, drugi bez daty / II / Autogr. I / A.Dz. 24/11" (approx.).
So R7484 and R7485 are the two letters of ADzied 24/11 no. 116 (not 26/32), R7485 being "drugi bez daty".

Summary: Chomentowski, Polish envoy at the Porte, writes from Adrianople on 9 May 1713. The Porte held a public divan
on 24 April at which war was declared "sub ignoto nomine"; the envoys feared being sent to the Yedikule; the Turks
wanted to force the Swedish king to recognise Stanisław or elect a third candidate, not wishing Augustus out of fear of
the German and Muscovite alliance; someone "being with the Sultan" (wezyr ... u Cesarza) was thrown down and strangled (reading of p. 113 L1-L2 uncertain); nothing is
settled, so the Hetman must be on guard; the Muscovite troops should not be in Poland and the King should not leave
Poland.
