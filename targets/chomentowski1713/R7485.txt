R7485 (DECODE "Krakow_UNK_4") -- Stanisław Chomentowski, wojewoda mazowiecki, Polish envoy to the Porte, to Hetman
Adam Mikołaj Sieniawski, no date (1713/14, on his embassy; Bender, seraskier, khan, hospodar of Wallachia).
Archive foliation 115-118 (pencil, top right), old no. "Nr 116" (address leaf, p. 118: "Od JMci Wojewody
Mazowieckiego Chomentowskiego bez daty / Nr 124 116"). Not in Kaczka.
Images: P1 = p. 115 (letter, cipher at foot); P3 = pp. 116-117 (clear text, signed "najniższy sługa / Chomentowski"
(reading approx.), very faint, show-through); P2 = p. 118 (address/docket).
Crops: img/crop/sien_R7485_p1_a.jpg ... _d.jpg (native resolution).

Hands/inks: letter body in pale brown ink (Chomentowski's secretary) with the cipher in the same pale ink; numbers are a
3-digit syllabic code (values up to ~331) mixed with clear words. A second, darker ink has (1) written the decipherment
of the whole cipher passage out in clear at the head of p. 115 (under the salutation) and (2) glossed the numbers
syllable by syllable between the lines. The interlinear gloss and the head-of-page clear text agree.

== Head of p. 115, clear decipherment in the darker ink (as written) ==
Jasnie Oswiecony M. Dobrodzieiu  [salutation, pale ink]
Seraskierowi Turcy wierzyc nie chcą y Hanowi y dla tego mnie prosto wołaią. Seraskier pisał do Hospodara
Wołoskiego, aby mi perswadował zebym na Bender iechał. Juz tedy z łaski Bozey pierwszą zasadzkę miiam. Ho-
spodar Wołoski nam przychylny, ale

Reading of L8-L9 (not in the head-of-page clear text, which stops at "ale"): "...[si]ę boią zli[?] wię[?], powiadaią,
że go Han [u]daie do Porty. Hospodar JMC wierny ..." -- i.e. the Khan denounces the hospodar to the Porte.

Counts: 96 cipher tokens; 95 carry a written interlinear gloss (99%; 161 has none, "ny" supplied from the
head-of-page clear text); L1-L7 (78 tokens) are also deciphered in clear at the head of the page. Values are collected in sien/syllabary.tsv.
Conflicts inside this page: 191 glossed "o" (Hanowi, L2) but "p" (prosto L3, przychylny L7); 140 glossed "ł"
(iechał L5) and "le" (ale L7); 6 and 8 both "a" (homophones); 49 "da" (L8) and "uda"/"da" (L9).

Summary: Chomentowski, on his way to or at Bender/Adrianople, reports that the Turks will not believe the seraskier
or the Khan and therefore summon him directly; the seraskier wrote to the hospodar of Wallachia to persuade him
to go to Bender; he has escaped the first trap; the hospodar is friendly to the Poles but the Khan denounces him to
the Porte. Undated, but on the 1712-13 embassy (Chomentowski entered Turkey 1712; R7484, the same code, is dated
Adrianople 9 May 1713).
