# Thomas Pinckney to the Secretary of State: the coded parts of his letters of 21 July and 11 August 1795, re-enciphered in "Cypher 2d"
# and enclosed on 27 August 1795, and the coded lines of his letter of 1 October 1795 (Code No. 16), NARA M31 roll 7 frames 0018-0021.
# The words are George Taylor Jr.'s, written over the groups. Each paragraph is one coded passage; the clear sentences they belong to are in the letters themselves and are not repeated in the enclosure.
# [...] = the start of the line is lost in the binding; [?word] = doubtful.

The cyphered part of the letter of 21 July 1795 (frame 0019 left, then 0018):

That they are still anxious for further delay, which is to them equivalent to a cession of our rights so long as we shall acquiesce therein, they being in possession of the object of controversy.
not guarantee the possessions of Spain in America.
Stipulations on this subject in our treaty with france should be observed.
Their accomodation with france.
Establish a triple alliance between france, Spain and ourselves.
an alliance.
Intentions of the french government with respect to our negotiation here.
French commissioners have complied with their instructions.
[...] minister at paris offered an accommodation between Spain and france unless our rights were previously acknowledged by the former.
This court means to proceed in their system of delay, or whether they will yield to us our rights.
alliance and guarantee.

[...] the guarantee.
