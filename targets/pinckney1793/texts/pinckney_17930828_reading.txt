# Thomas Pinckney to Thomas Jefferson, London, 28 August 1793, the coded passage (Code No. 16), NARA M30 roll 2 frame 0140.
# 447 = open (the editors printed 497). [?55]ats: the editors conjectured [Dunda]s, but 668 is "at" in Eaton 1801, Pinckney 1795 and the Bey's letter of 1802, so the
# name is ?55 + at + s and stays open. time (234) and secret (501) are attested in Lear's letter of 1813 (beling/, PR #399).

447 regiments are to embark in a short time on a secret expedition. They are to be commanded by Sir Charles Grey with Generals Stuart and [?55]ats under him. Their destination is generally thought to be for the West Indies.
