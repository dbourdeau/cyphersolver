# M30-002-0178 (NARA M30 roll 2, frame 0178)

Thomas Pinckney, London, November 1793. Transcribed blind from the frame image only (no key, no other transcription, no other frame).

## What is on the frame

- Left page: blank apart from the docket in the upper right corner and a stray dot. No other clear text.
- Docket (two lines): "Pinckney T. London Nov. 2[?]" and "recd. feby. 18. 94." The day after "Nov. 2" is cut off by the edge of the right-hand leaf. The "18" could also be "15".
- Right page: numeric code groups separated by dots, 21 lines, no interlinear words, no clear text. Lines 2, 12 and 16 are indented. Lines 11 and 15 end short, which looks like paragraph ends.

## Counts

- 21 lines, 353 rows in groups.tsv.
- 40 rows carry a `?` (doubtful digits).
- 7 rows are whole-group placeholders (`___`): four at the gutter (lines 14, 17, 18, 20) and three on line 21. The first placeholder on line 21 stands for an unknown run of groups (about 7 to 8 by spacing), so the true group count on the page is roughly 360.

- The crosses under 526 (line 3), 623 (line 5) and 1258 (line 12) are heavily inked. They are recorded as single crosses but could be double.

- Line 19 begins "409" right at the edge of the gutter. A leading digit may be hidden there.
