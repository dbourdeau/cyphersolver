# M30 roll 2, frame 0208: blind transcription

I transcribed this blind, from the image `sources/raw/code16_pinckney_2026-11-11/M30-002-0208.jpg` (4224 x 2832 px) and my own sips crops of it, and nothing else. I did not attempt any decipherment.

## What is on the frame

- **Left page:** the end of Thomas Pinckney's letter (London, 26 Feb 1794), the signature "Thomas Pinckney", and below it 4 lines of numeric code. The groups are separated by dots, and a clerk wrote interlinear words above them. Under the code is a clear note (quoted below), then a short line at the lower left that reads "[..] 26 Feby." (its start is blotted), and an upside-down docket at the foot of the page.
- **Right page:** a note from Lord Grenville dated Downing Street, 17 March 1794. It is not code and is not transcribed.

## Counts

- 4 lines of code, with 55 groups: 16 in line 1, 17 in line 2, 16 in line 3, 6 in line 4.
- 17 groups have at least one doubtful (?) or hidden (_) digit.
- Line 1 opens with a reference mark (+) before 194.
- Crosses written after groups: 1196+ (line 1) and 586++ (line 2).

## What covers the code

- **Light vertical patch** (about x 440-605 px). This is translucent paper, and the ink shows through only faintly. It covers the 3rd group of line 1 (1?2?0?6?), the last digit of 149(5?) and the group 5?1?5? in line 2, and most of 52?5? and 1?27 in line 3. It also covers parts of the interlinear words "con", "which", "h[..]" and "cu[..]", and the faint word over 1035.
- **Dark vertical repair strip** (about x 1050-1230 px). It runs the full height of the page and has ink blots. It affects 139?4? (line 1), 1005 / 885 (line 2, where the dot dividing them is uncertain) and 620? (line 3).
- **Overlapping slip** (about x 1815-2185 px), with a black band beyond it. It covers the right ends of lines 1-3:
  - Line 1: 3[5?4?], 1490?, 1328?
  - Line 2: 1[8?9?], 1515?, 8?_
  - Line 3: 2[7?3?], 1146?, 241?

  These digits show only faintly through the slip. At the end of line 2, all of the last group except one digit is lost at the slip edge. Line 4 ends at 373 and is clear of the slip.
