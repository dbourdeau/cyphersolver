# Copy 2 = M23 roll 10: 8 Nov frame 0072 (a clerk's copy); 31 Dec "(Copy)" frames 0075-0082 (the copy Founders Online prints).
# Three transcriptions went into this file: reader 1 forward on copy 1 and from the end on copy 2 (copy1_roll09_reader1_forward.txt,
# copy2_roll10_reader1_from_end.txt); reader 2 blind on copy 1 with every doubtful group and P2-P7 checked on copy 2
# (copy1_and_2_reader2_blind.txt). Reader 2 read before opening reader 1's files.
# The two copies agree group for group except in two places:
#   P4 group 3: reader 1 read copy 1 as 980 or 986; copy 2 is plainly 980 (reader 2 and Founders agree): 980.
#   P6 near the end: copy 2 has 663 before 374 ("197 616 663 374"); copy 1 has no 663. Kept (gives "on the first favorable").
# ^ = a small "3"-shaped mark under the group in both copies (under 972 and under the last 6 of 606 in P6). Read as Code No. 16's
#   caret, which doubles the last letter (972 al, 972^ all), as with Eaton's caret in the Tunis letters. It is not a group.
#   Founders Online prints one such mark in P6 as a group "3." between 856 and 356; group 3 is "greatbritain" in key v5, so
#   decoding it would put Great Britain into a sentence where Lear did not write it.
