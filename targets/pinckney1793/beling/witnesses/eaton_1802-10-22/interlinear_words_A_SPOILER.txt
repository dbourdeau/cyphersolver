Frame T303-02-0320 (Eaton to Madison, 22 Oct 1802, page 1).

A second hand has written English words above the code groups: a contemporary
interlinear decipherment. This file is my rough reading of those words, kept out of
transcription_A.tsv and README_A.txt so that the numeral transcription stays blind.

Status of this reading: quick and approximate. The words are tiny, several are blotted,
and I did not double-check them the way I did the numerals. Positions ("above ...") are
by eye. I did not use these words to choose any digit, and I did not try to derive or
apply any code values from them.
