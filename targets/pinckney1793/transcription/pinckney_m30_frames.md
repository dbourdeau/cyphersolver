# Pinckney code frames, NARA M30 roll 2

Coded frames found on the contact sheets: 0102, 0103, 0106, 0107, 0135, 0136, 0140, 0148, 0178, 0180, 0183, 0196, 0208, 0305, 0317.

## 0140
- Letter London 28 Aug 1793 (to Secretary of State), right page, 5 coded lines (58 groups incl. gutter fragments).
- Decoded: no interlinear decipherment. Only a faint pencil note above line 2 reading "7?? so far only".
- First group read 447 (both middle strokes look like the 4 in 1465; 497 not supported by shape). Line-initial groups of lines 2-5 lose leading digits in the gutter (visible: ..10, ..39, ..55, ..3).

## 0180
- Letter London 27 Nov 1793, "(private)", to Mr. Jefferson. 11 coded lines, 170 groups.
- Decoded: yes, full interlinear (TJ's hand?) word by word; alignment per group is approximate for multi-group words.
- Number words: 6 = 1244 (leading 1 partly in gutter), 8 = 531 ("6 or 8 months in France"). "posts" = 597+. "+" = plural/-s marker (rights 589+, treaties 550+, months 1316+).
- Line 3: a blotted group (1447 or 1497?) with "433." inserted above by caret.

## 0102 + 0103
- Letter London 20 June 1793 to Secretary of State; one coded line at foot of 0102 continuing three lines at top of 0103 (13 + 37 = 50 groups).
- Decoded: yes, interlinear throughout ("but I have no hope that this government will accede to that principle of the armed neutrality that free ships shall make free goods since Russia, Denmark and Sweden do not insist upon it for the present war").
- No number words; no 497.

## 0106 + 0107
- Letter begun on 0105 (London 5 July 1793, to Secretary of State). Two coded passages on 0106 right page (6 lines + 6 lines) and 6 lines at top of 0107 (continuation). 0106: 157 groups; 0107: 95 groups.
- Decoded: no interlinear decipherment.
- Line ends of 0106 and line starts/ends of 0107 are cut by the page/frame edges (leaf larger than film field or tightly bound): partial groups marked ?.
- Contains 753.175 (I thought) and 433.589+ (neutral rights). 0107 line 6 has 496 (clear 6), the nearest to 497. Marks: + and # below some groups, a second digit written below in 972/3, 779/7, 737/3.

## 0135 + 0136
- Letter London 15 Aug 1793 to Secretary of State, almost entirely "in cypher" (0135: 27 lines after a 3-line clear opening, 413 groups; 0136 left page 32 lines + right page 9 lines, 607 groups). Total ~1020 groups, the longest Pinckney code text on the roll.
- Decoded: no interlinear words. But almost every group carries a small check-mark (tick) below it, in a second ink, i.e. someone worked through it group by group (decoding checks?). Many groups also carry + (plural), # , u-mark, or a "3" written below (marks of the code; same as 0106/0107).
- Group notes: 0135 line 10 has a clear 1497 (515.1497.1565); 0135 line 18 ends 1?97+ (2nd digit 4 or 8, at edge); 0136 L24 has 159? (7 or 9) + and ends 149? (last digit in gutter); 0136 L29 has 597 (unmarked).
- Gutter/edge losses: several line ends on 0136 left page.

## 0148
- Letter London 25 Sept 1793 to TJ (docket "recd Nov. 28"), left page: 7 coded lines with interlinear decipherment (Morris's expenses; a confidential person "for six thousand livres expenses"; "to assist him to emerge from captivity"; "I paid the bill from the contingent fund but wish for particular instructions specifying what money may be advanced to him and his family ... for that purposes"), ~86 groups; plus one undecoded coded line at the foot below the signature (14 groups).
- Number words: "six thousand livres" over 1226.1491.63.435.1064 (for = 1226; six ~1491; thousand ~63/435; per-group alignment approximate). Line ends lost at page edge.

## 0178 (+ 0179 Explication)
- Letter London 25 Nov 1793 (docket "Pinckney T. London Nov. 25, recd Feb. 15. 94"), right page all code: 21 lines, ~352 groups (leading digits of most lines hidden in the gutter; bottom line mostly lost at the fold).
- Not interlined, but frame 0179 carries "Mr Pinckney's letter of 25 Nov. 1793. Explication of the Cyphers" (clerk's clear text). Its 2nd-4th paragraphs are the plaintext of 0178 ("That the real intention of this court towards us might be ascertained ... He was then proceeding to another point ... they would relinquish the posts to us ... had deferred the accomplishment of their part of the obligation for nine years ..."; "You will see by their last treaties ..."; "In case I should be directed by you to withdraw from hence ... retire to France ... a frigate to carry me & my family to America on account of the Algerines ..."). Its first paragraph ("He produced to me a letter from la Fayette desiring me to furnish him with money from the thousand pounds sent for him by the President") belongs to a coded passage not on 0178 (earlier page).
- Groups hand-aligned to the explication are marked in the note column ("explication (0179) aligned"). Number words: nine = 383, years = 659 (7 below) in line 9 "1226.383.659" = "for nine years".
- In 0178, "posts" seems to be 393.924+ (not 597+ as on 0180); alignment of that stretch is uncertain.

## 0183
- Letter London 17 Dec 1793 (docket "Pinckney Thomas 17 Decr 1793 recd 1 May 1794"), postscript-like coded passage of 9 lines (~140 groups) on the left leaf, after "I remain &c". Right line-ends hidden under the overlapping 26 Dec leaf.
- Decoded: yes, interlinear throughout ("But the most important part of this conversation consists in my asking in direct terms whether if we should fulfil the treaty ... they would relinquish the posts ... answered that if one party to a treaty ... for nine years whereby it could not be completed ... neither justice nor the law of nations would exact compliance from the other party. This removal added to the general [tenor] of their conduct to us appears to me under the present circumstances to bear war, but of this opinion I give no intimation here").
- 597+ = "posts" again (cf. 0180). Number word: nine = 6?4 (2nd digit under an ink stroke), years = 659+. Note 0178 reads 1226.383.659 for "for nine years": 383 may be a second homophone for nine, or my alignment of 0178 is off.

## 0196
- Letter London 28 Jan 1794 to Secretary of State (right leaf), 12 coded lines (~235 groups).
- Decoded: partly. Interlinear on lines 1-4 ("In addition to my letter of the 10th I have to add that I had no [?] notice in obtaining the revocation of the instruction of the 6th of Nov. ... procrastination ... an interview with me until the cabinet had determined on the measure. The") and lines 11-12 ("Admiral McBride should be des[tined] ... ad... ro... n & some"). Lines 5-10 not interlined.
- Number/date words: 1156 = "10th", 631 = "6th". No 497.

## 0208
- End of a letter (Pinckney to Secretary of State, ca. Feb 1794; docket upside-down at foot, date not certain), 4 coded lines after the signature (~48 groups), partly under a paper repair strip and an overlapping slip.
- Decoded: yes, interlinear ("This plan consists in sending a confidential person to Silesia to [?] place Fayette is removed to establish a communication with him by letter and to take advantage of any favourable [occasion] to procure his evasion"). Clear note below: "In the above I have made use of the last Cypher received - I mention this circumstance because I find you have used the first".
- No number words; no 497.

## 0317
- Letter London 3 April 1795 ("My dear Sir"), one coded sentence of 13 groups after "A friend here who is generally well informed says that".
- Decoded: no interlinear. 3 = G.B. (from 0180) so probably "...and Great Britain..."; no 497.

## 0305
- Letter (date not on this frame; early 1795, before 3 April; about leaving London for Spain, route through France), ~16 coded lines in three passages (~180 groups) across both pages.
- Decoded: no interlinear. Line 11 had a pre-code clear phrase ending "this Route 78?" at the margin (partly cut). No 497.
