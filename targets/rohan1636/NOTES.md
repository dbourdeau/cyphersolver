## Documents and access

- BnF Français 5190 nos. 214–215: Victor Amadeus I of Savoy and Canisy to Rohan, 27 May 1636, received 6 June. Their
  text is printed deciphered in *Mémoires et lettres de Henri duc de Rohan sur la guerre de la Valteline* (1758), III,
  pp. 76–79, under headings that call them "en chiffres". The Gallica reproduction btv1b9059471x of the 208-folio
  volume has 90 views and ends at manuscript p. 80; requests for later views return the same last image. No
  manuscript ciphertext in hand, so no paired cipher/plaintext sample.

## Reading result

The printed text (transcribed in the report): Savoy asks Rohan to lodge 2,000 foot and 200 horse in the Val Mesolcina
to block the Germans' passage, and to threaten the Francesca post; the 6,000 men Canisy was to bring are sent to Rohan
instead. Canisy confirms the king's and Savoy's intentions and a march towards the Ticino. No cipher sign has a value.
