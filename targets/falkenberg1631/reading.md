# Reading

Letter of 28 March 1631 (SE/RA/1133/1133.07/~/10, image R0000785_00133, fol. 101): provisional reading with K2 before the clear copy was found: sure 92 (53.5 %), probable 20 (11.6 %), restored 25 (14.5 %), unresolved 35 (20.3 %) of 172 cipher tokens, i.e. about 79.7 % read by sense (below the 95 % bar); a first count with K2 gave 84.3 % (sure 104, probable 21, restored 20, unresolved 27).
Then, on the next images (R0000785_00134 right page, fol. 102, "D. 28 Mar 1631", signed "D. v. F.", and 00135, fol. 103), two clear copies of the time of the same letter were found: the content is existing (decryption.txt is our quick reading of the clear copy). Of the 25 restorations made before the discovery, 21 are confirmed by the clear copy (angekommen, Regiment, Ross, Constantia, bald, anders, ueber, Ostern, Schiffbruecke, Schoenebeck, Munition x2, uebrig, lassen, Ew. x2, unterthaenigster, gehorsamster, Diener, Garnison, passagi) and 4 are wrong (3757 werden = wehren; 2311 Magistrat = mahlen; 317 Artillerie = arrestiren; 3777 welcher = wenigst). Codes identified from the clear copy: 3729 Tilli, 1807 Herr, 3961 zween, 2311 mahlen, 317 arrestiren, 501 bereit, 3783 1/2 werck, 3757 wehren, 3770 weit, ff = nun, nn = so.

Provisional text (part 1): Omissis omittendis ... E. K. Mt. und ... nach Sparenbergs verreysten hat sich hier ziemlich {veraendert} ... Daher dann nicht rathsam klein truppen zu schicken, sonderlich general Secours ist nothwendig ... Proviant mangel gross ... Wirdt also E. K. Mt. [bald] Rath schaffen ... und koennen lang [ueber] [Ostern] nicht halten, und der Feind ... in massen und durch [Schiffbruecke] bey [Schoenebeck] ... Chur Sachsen ... gibt {Chemnitius} fuer ... gut Schwedisch ... Part 2: the cities Garleben and Saltwedel, no garrison, well affected; on the other side much wood and passages, little to eat; E. K. Mt. can come over the Elbe.
Cross-check: part 2 follows the copy "D. v. F." paraphrased by Dittmar (no. 21), part 1 the next letter (Dittmar no. 34: "Lang ueber Ostern koennen wir uns nicht halten"). Dittmar's note that the letter was "nicht aufgeloest" (no. 33) was wrong; Kirchhoff was right.
