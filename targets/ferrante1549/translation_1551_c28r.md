# Philip to Ferrante Gonzaga, Madrid, 24 Nov 1551, c. 28r, passages D and E: English

For the good order which you write you have given to the guard of Nice we give you thanks, [?and] so we ask that care be [?always] taken of that [?stronghold], since you see what it matters.

As to the hope you have of a good outcome in the business of Siena, and from what the bishop of Arras and Don Diego de Mendoza write to you, it seems to be on a good road.

Passage C (from the margin): you are to inform us particularly and continually both of the enterprise of Parma and of that of the Mirandola, and of all the rest.
