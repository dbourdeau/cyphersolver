# Philip to Ferrante Gonzaga, Valladolid, 4 Aug 1548, c. 10r: English

We have seen what you write about the affairs of Genoa, which seems to us very prudently considered, and there is nothing to answer beyond what you will gather from what the Duke [?of Alba] writes to Ambassador Figueroa.

As for what you learn there of the King of France and of his coming down into Italy, we ask you to take very particular care to inform us, and if it should be a certain matter and of a quality that calls for it, and [?it being so], you will send a courier in haste with it.
