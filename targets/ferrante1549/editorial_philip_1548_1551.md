# Philip to Ferrante Gonzaga, 1548 and 1551: notes on the readings

## 4 Aug 1548, c. 10r

No decipherment exists on the leaves. One pass; a second reader read the signs at half scale and differs on 20.3% of them. Status: read in part.

### Notes

- A1 `genoui`, A5 `figueraa`, B4 `qualidado`: repairs. `y:s` gives ui and must be ua in Genoua and d'Alua.
- `D3` is du, so A4 gives `el duque` as written. It was `do` in the earlier file, made before the key changed. The next signs give `d al ui`. "d'Alua" rests on one occurrence.
- B4 `y ser la`: not understood.

Context: Henry II went to Piedmont in the summer of 1548, which fits the concern about his descent into Italy. General knowledge, not checked against a source.


## 24 Nov 1551, c. 28r

One pass, no second reader. Passage C has a contemporary marginal decipherment that the catalogue does not mention, so C is a transcription job and stands apart from the count. Status of D and E: read in part.

### Passage C, from the marginal decipherment

nos auiseys particular y continuamente assy de la empresa de Parma, como de la Mirandola y de todo lo de mas

The cipher gives the same text, with `la de` after `como` that the margin omits. `D3` gives `randula` under the current key; the margin shows `dola`, so `D3` was do here.

### Notes

- D2 `dimosas`: damos, with `as` dropped. D3 `buassy`, D3-D4 `siendecuydado`, D4 `paca` are the proposals.
- E3 `de rras`, `encamana`: repairs. `parecr` is taken as a misread `ce`, so "parece" is a proposal.

Summary: Nice is guarded; Siena's business seems to go well, by the word of the bishop of Arras and Don Diego de Mendoza.


## 24 Nov 1551, c. 26r

No decipherment seen. One pass, no second reader. Status: read in part. The vicariate passage is unsafe.

### Notes

The signs give `capa a`, `misuta`, `disuleri o`, `luicaraato`, `entiar pu`, `criui ho`. The vicariate reading is the most interesting claim on this leaf and it rests on those broken words. It needs a second pass before anyone relies on it.

Summary: the Germans sent by the Emperor should help finish matters; Ferrante is thanked for sending a paper on Siena, which may concern a vicariate.
