# Poinsett 1811, Buenos Aires reports "in cipher" (catalogue 640)

Joel R. Poinsett to the Secretary of State, Buenos Aires: 10 May, 25 Jul, 30 Jul 1811.

## What the document is
Not a ciphertext in hand. What exists online is the typed list of Poinsett's official reports
(NARA M37 roll 2, frames 0020-0022, https://catalog.archives.gov/id/213803757). Frame 0022 marks three entries:
- 10 May, from B.A.: England recognizes Gen. Elio and tries to mediate between B.A. and Montevideo. "Part in cipher."
- 25 Jul, from B.A.: defeat of the B.A. army in Upper Peru, negotiations with Brazil. "Parts in cipher."
- 30 Jul, from B.A.: conflict with Brazil expected, revolution in Arequipa. "Parts in cipher."
A handwritten marginal word next to the 16 June entry (not one of the three) may read "Deciphered"; not legible enough to be sure.

## Correction to the catalogue brief
The list's own heading says "All these reports are bound in vol. 1 of the collection" (HSP Poinsett Papers),
not vol. 4. The catalogue's "vol. 4" should be rechecked.

## Key, reading
None. No ciphertext, no key. The list gives summaries only, no cipher groups.

## Remaining gaps
All three. Blocked from outside: the cipher passages are in HSP Poinsett Papers vol. 1 (Gilpin collection),
not digitised that I could find; Manning 1925 says the Department copies went back to Poinsett in 1818.
