# Pass B: Cosimo III's cipher signs, Codex 533 (reference for passB_backward.tsv)

Reader: pass B (backward pass, 70v to 1r), 13-14 Nov 2026. Reference sheet: crops/B_ref_signs.jpg (one example of each sign, leaf and position printed on each cell; the "4" cell is mis-cropped, see crops/B_19r_5514.jpg for a clean 4). Examples come from clear places in the cipher runs and from the plain numerals on 19r ("5514", "del 14").

| sign | how Cosimo writes it | confusable with | how pass B decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | single upright, often slightly curved, sometimes a small foot or blob head | 7 (when the head has a hook) | no flat top bar = 1 |
| 2 | small hook or small closed loop at top, diagonal, then a long flat base stroke to the right | 7, 9 | a flat base = 2 |
| 3 | z-shape: short top bar, a zig in the middle, long descender curving left | 7 | any middle zig = 3 |
| 4 | a small (often filled) triangular loop on the left, a horizontal crossbar, and a vertical stem through it | c+diagonal w | crossbar and vertical stem both visible = 4 |
| 5 | open hooked head (curl does not close), descender turning left: like a Greek final sigma | 9 | head stays open = 5 |
| 6 | oval body with a tall ascender rising from the right and arching over to the right, often over the next sign | 0 | ascender present = 6 |
| 7 | flat (sometimes slightly hooked) top bar and a straight or slanting stem, no zig, no base | 3, 2, 1 | flat top, no zig, no base = 7 |
| 8 | two stacked loops, no separate c before it; sometimes the upper loop is an open hook over a closed lower loop | c+8 form of w | no detached c to its left = 8 |
| 9 | closed round head (an a- or q-shape) with a descender; a second form is a small closed loop with a very long diagonal descender sweeping down-left | 5 | head closed = 9 |
| 0 | plain oval, often leaning, no ascender | 6 | no ascender = 0 |
| w | an open c followed, usually joined, by a loop. Forms seen: c+6 (loop with tall ascender), c+8 (loop over a second loop, or an a-shaped closed loop over an 8-loop), c+small round loop, and c+heavy diagonal (the loop collapsed into a thick slanting stroke, sometimes with a white fleck inside) | 6, 8, 9, 4 | any detached-looking open c joined to the following loop is one sign w, never c + digit |

Hardest decisions, in order: 5 against 9 (the head curl is often half closed; pass B gives its preferred reading first in [5/9] brackets), the c+diagonal form of w against 9 or 4, and the c+8 form of w against a plain 8. Pass B never split a c+loop into two signs.
