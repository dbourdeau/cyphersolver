## Folios used

- **Codex 533 (cipher in Cosimo's hand):** 1r, 1v, 3r, 19r (doubtful), 21v, 22r, 25r, 26r, 28v, 35r, 35v, 36r, 53r, 56r,
  57r, 63r, 70v; date lines on 1v, 3r, 19v, 22r, 25v, 27r, 29v, 35v, 37r, 55r, 57r, 63v, 72r
  (`transcriptions/dates_codex533.tsv`). Every leaf was paged; no decipherment anywhere in the volume.
- **Codex 534, interlined (key source):** f. 14r (Dall'Ambrogiana, 30 Oct. 1690), f. 15r (Firenze, 20 Nov. 1690), f. 21r
  (Firenze, 5 Dec. 1690; the held-out letter), f. 396v (line 2).
- **Codex 534, bare (read here):** ff. 114r-114v (Firenze, 24 Apr. 1694), f. 115r ([?] May 1694), ff. 116r-116v (15 May
  1694).
- **Codex 531, bare (read here):** f. 4r (Firenze, 17 July 1694).
- **Codex 534, other ciphers (not this system):** ff. 117r-118r (interlined, small digit groups); 251r-253r ("other-1");
  about 129 leaves of ff. 268r-454r and 517v-539v ("other-2", mostly 1696, many interlined). Separate targets if worked.
