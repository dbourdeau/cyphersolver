# P1: Codex 534 ff. 114r-114v, secretariat letter for Cosimo III to Montauti, Firenze 24 April 1694. Cipher in CAPITALS, normalized; clear text lower case as transcribed around the runs; [... n] = n unread words (Holes are counted by words, 14 Nov 2026), counted from the decode: where the hole covers cipher letters with no word division, n is the least number of words they can make, an estimate; where it covers clear words transcribed in bare/P1_decode.txt but not carried into this reading, n is their number. A bare [..] standing alone is of unknown extent: extent unknown, the one [..] between SAVOIA and D'ACCASARE (no group and no transcribed word stands there in P1_decode.txt; the mark is the reader's). [..] inside a word is part of that one word. [n] = code number without a value. One reading pass by one agent; lines 2, 4, 8 and 14 only partly read.
