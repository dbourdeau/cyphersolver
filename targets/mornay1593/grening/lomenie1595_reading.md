# Loménie to Duplessis, Paris, 24 February 1595: reading and translation

*Mémoires et correspondance de Duplessis-Mornay*, vol. 6 (Paris, 1824), letter CVII, pp. 179-181.
Cipher words are in **bold**. Marks: `[?word]` a doubtful reading, `[...]` not read, `[25]` a name figure left
as printed. The clear text is the 1824 print's; only the sentences that carry cipher are given.

## French

1. (p. 179) Il a la **province de Bretaigne**, en **de Camor**.
2. Mais pour **les despesches** tous deux consentent; feut la resolution du sieur **de Camor**.
3. (pp. 179-180) Mais pour ce que nous faisant le service **de Vennes** il se declareroit que l'on trouve meilleur
   qu'il exempte le reste, comme plus important, s'il ce faict, de lui promettre telle **ville de seureté de
   Bretaigne** et vaudra pour l'estat de colonnel de la cavallerie legere dudict pays.
4. (p. 180) C'est une pitié, et **la necessité** des affaires **du [?roy]**, car elle estoit aveneue, et on ne rud.;
   et **d'edicts**.
5. J'estime que vous aurés veu M. Cellier, ce qui s'est enquis de moi, si vous ne m'aviés rien mandé de
   **l'assamblée de Saumur**, mesmes si ces messieurs contendans depuis que l'edict de 77 a esté publié.
6. Enfin les deputés de **Bearn** mesmes pour **la messe** sont ici.
7. (pp. 180-181) Ung se promest qu'il **de Boisdauphin**, et M. **le marquis de Belisle** seront ses serviteurs, et
   en bref, M. de Bouillon est marié il y a huict jours.
8. (p. 181) **[... 1] querelles**.

## English

1. He has the **province of Brittany**, in **de Camor**. (The sense of "en" before the name is not clear to me.)
2. But as for **the dispatches** both agree; such was the resolution of the sieur **de Camor**.
3. But because, in doing us the service **of Vannes**, he would declare himself, it is thought better that he carry
   out the rest, as the more important, and, if it is done, to promise him such **town of surety in Brittany** [as
   he likes], and it will count towards the post of colonel of the light cavalry of the said country.
4. It is a pity, and **the necessity** of the affairs **of the [?king]**, for it had come about, and [two words of the
   print not understood: "on ne rud."]; and **of edicts**.
5. I think you will have seen M. Cellier, who asked me whether you had sent me nothing about **the assembly of
   Saumur**, even if these gentlemen [have been] disputing since the edict of 1577 was published.
6. Lastly the deputies of **Béarn** are here, on the very matter of **the mass**.
7. It is expected that **de Boisdauphin** and M. **the marquis de Belle-Île** will be his servants, and in short,
   M. de Bouillon was married a week ago.
8. **[... 1] quarrels**.

## Notes on the reading

- "[?roy]" (L8): the print has 87 410 8, a clear 8 on the page image. d u r o are certain; the last figure is d or
  p by the attested values, which gives no word. "du roy" fits the sentence, and y occurs nowhere else in the
  letter, so either y = 8 in this key, or the pattern holds (y = 3) and the 8 is a slip of the clerk or the
  printer. Nothing in the print decides between them, so the word is marked doubtful.
- "[... 1] querelles" (L15): 67 104 109 910 2 gives "querelles" exactly. The two groups before it, 100 76, are not
  read. 100 holds the only 0 in the letter that is not part of a 10. My guess is that 100 is a name figure from
  the same list as the 98 and 99 that stand in the clear text of this letter ("le voyage de 98", "aller à 99"),
  which would leave 76 as two letters (i/u/v, then c/q) or another name figure. It is a guess. The hole is counted
  as one word; that is an estimate.
- "Vennes" is the spelling the figures give (8 10 7 10 2 2 10 2); "Vannes" would need 2 for the fourth figure. The
  king's letter of the same day, printed in clear just above (CVI, pp. 178-179), has "ceulx de Vannes" and "tant
  pour le regard de Vannes".
- "exempte" in sentence 3 is the print's word; the sense wants "execute" (cf. CVI, "que le dernier s'execute"). It
  is clear text, left as printed.
- "l'edict de 77" is clear text: the year, not cipher.
- The single figures used as names in the clear text (3, 11, 14, 16, 25, 26, 98, 99) are not read. They are a
  separate list and this key says nothing about them.
- Every figure stands for two or three letters, so each reading is a choice among the letters the key allows. The
  choices are listed figure by figure in `lomenie1595_decryption.txt`.
