## Literature on the same events (not the text of the telegrams)
- A. Nowak, "The Road to Rapallo: Germany in the Soviet Russia's Policy in the Light of Viktor L. Kopp's Reports for Georgy V. Chicherin from 1920-1921" (https://rcin.org.pl/ihpan/Content/248163): Kopp was recalled at the end of April 1921, Lutovinov replaced him as diplomatic representative and Stomonyakov became trade representative. Kopp's and Lutovinov's reports are said to be in RGASPI f. 5, op. 1, d. 2137.
- Lenin's letter to Lutovinov of 30 May 1921 and the documents of June 1921 in vol. 52 of the Collected Works touch on the abuses in the Berlin mission and on Kopp's position.
