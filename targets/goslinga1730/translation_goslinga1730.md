# Translation: Goslinga to Reinhard van Reede-Ginkel, Compiègne 23 July 1730 (catalogue 2534)

English translation of the Dutch coded passages (see `reading_goslinga1730.md`). Square brackets: a word or name
group, its value from the contemporary decipherment slip. `[#158]`, `[#478]`: groups the slip does not render; they
stay holes here as in the reading.

The information that [Seckendorff] has given to [the King of Prussia] has been [#158] true.

The answer of [the Emperor] is in keeping with it and contains in substance the following points. (The points follow
in French, in clear: a general guarantee of the Pragmatic Sanction by France, Great Britain and the States General;
the Ostend affair to be settled amicably; Spanish garrisons refused outright, other means offered to secure Don
Carlos's succession; all on condition of satisfaction for the Emperor's allies.)

At the same time a plan of war and of operations has been drawn up (the French sentence goes on: "which we sent at
the same time to The Hague").

(The French sentence "but I dare assure you" goes on:) that if it is [#478] settled and carried out in full harmony,
[the Emperor] will in all likelihood listen to reason.

But I very much fear that the jealousy between [His Most Christian Majesty] and [the King of Great Britain], and a
letter of [the King of Great Britain] to that of [Spain], followed by another from this court to [His ... Majesty] of
England about the expedition to [Sicily], may very easily make all these salutary measures vanish in smoke.

[The Keeper of the Seals] told us in the last conference of the incident between [the minister of Great Britain] and
[the King of Prussia].

If this is so, it could also hinder our affairs.

I beg you to let me know whether this is true.
