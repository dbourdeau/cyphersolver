# Contucio to Francesco Sforza, Venice, 7 Dec 1446 (BnF italien 1583 f. 148, DECODE R7911): the cipher passages read

Deciphered with ASMi Carteggio Sforzesco cart. 1597 no. 14 (DECODE R5314), `key.md`; sign by sign in
`transcription.txt`, decoder output `decryption.txt`. Cipher in **bold**, clear text in roman around it, word
division and modern punctuation added. [?] after a word = one sign of uncertain shape; [utinam] = code word with no
value. u/v rendered by sense.

## f. 148r lines 22-30

The Signoria will send its own ambassador to Sforza, Ser Pasquale [Malipiero]; the Florentine ambassador says
Florence too will send another ambassador; they are to meet with the Count. Then:

> ... li quali si abbiano a trovare insieme con la S. V. Et con la prefata V. S. pratticare e intendere quello che sia
> da fare **tanto per la desfatione del Duca de Milano quanto de la vostra perdeta, et del vostro danno, et de lo
> restoro d'essi.** Et precipue mi pare che vorieno **se facesse la pace con [utinam], la quale fatta che fosse,**
> pocho stimeria el male che potesse fare **lo Re de Ragona[?].** Et vorriano poi che la S. V. **personalmente se
> trovasse a la disfatione del Duca de Milano.** Et sequira altro non so, voglo havere advisato la S. V. di quello che
> sento.

English:

> ... who are to meet together with Your Lordship, and with Your Lordship discuss and learn what is to be done, **both
> for the destruction of the Duke of Milan and about your loss and your damage, and the making good of them.** And
> above all it seems to me they would want **peace made with [utinam]; once it was made,** they would think little of
> the harm **the King of Aragon** could do. And then they would want Your Lordship **to be present in person at the
> destruction of the Duke of Milan.** What else will follow I do not know; I wanted to have told Your Lordship what I
> hear.

## f. 148r lines 48-51

Contucio has reported the Count's speech to the Signoria: the Count would abandon the little he has left for the
Duke's ruin, and do it again "as he has done the other times when it was needed for the state of Your Serenity and
of the League". Then:

> ... quando e bisognato per lo stato dela V. Ser.ta e dela lega. **Questa tale oferta parve[?] a Cosimo che** in ogni
> modo **se devesse fare.**

English:

> ... when it was needed for the state of Your Serenity and of the League. **This offer, Cosimo [de' Medici] thought,
> should** in any case **be made.**

## f. 148v lines 4-7

The Count asks the Signoria to say what it wishes him to do in the coming season, whether in winter quarters, with
a lord or with another person, and he will do it gladly. Then:

> Essendone advisato fara di buona voglia ogni cosa che debbia essere stato e di piacere ala V. Ser.ta. Ancora:
> **questa parte parve a Cosimo se devesse dire.**

English:

> Once informed, he will gladly do anything that is to be the state's and Your Serenity's pleasure. Further: **this
> part too, Cosimo thought, should be said.**

A hand of the time wrote a decipherment above these two lines: *questa* · *parte* · *parve* · *a cosimo* · *se
devesse* · *dire*. It agrees with the key word for word and gives the code word *pec* = Cosimo.
