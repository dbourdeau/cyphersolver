# Key: ASMi Carteggio Sforzesco cart. 1597 no. 14 (DECODE R5314), "Angelus cum cifra Vincentij"

An 18th-century copy of a 15th-century key. Heading: *Angelus cum cifra Vincentij*; at the right, the users:
*Johannes de Stavolis, Augustinus, Baptista, Matheus, et Vincentius*, i.e. Giovanni Stavoli, Agostino (da Narni),
Battista, Matteo (Giordani) and Vincenzo (Amidani), the Sforza envoys at Venice in 1446. Contucio, who wrote f. 148,
signs the joint letters of 7 and 14 Dec 1446 with them. Image: DECODE R5314 (`IMG_R5314_I30290_P`, not public
domain, not committed; a copy is in `targets/birago1448/keys/`, git-ignored).

Transcribed here from the image (6 Nov 2026). Sign shapes are described in words; where the copyist's shape is
unclear the description says so.
