# Contucio to Francesco Sforza, Venice, 7 Dec 1446 (BnF italien 1583 f. 148): English of the cipher passages

Deciphered passages in **bold**, the clear text around them in roman. Italian in `reading.md`.

**f. 148r, lines 25-30.** ... who are to meet together with Your Lordship, and with Your Lordship discuss and learn
what is to be done, **both for the destruction of the Duke of Milan and about your loss and your damage, and the
making good of them.** And above all it seems to me they would want **peace made with [code word *utinam*, not
identified]; once it was made,** they would think little of the harm **the King of Aragon** could do. And then they
would want Your Lordship **to be present in person at the destruction of the Duke of Milan.** What else will follow
I do not know; I wanted to have told Your Lordship what I hear.

**f. 148r, lines 49-51.** ... as he has done the other times when it was needed for the state of Your Serenity and of
the League. **This offer, Cosimo [de' Medici] thought, should** in any case **be made.**

**f. 148v, lines 5-7.** Once informed, he will gladly do anything that is to be the state's and Your Serenity's
pleasure. Further: **this part too, Cosimo thought, should be said.**
