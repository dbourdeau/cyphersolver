L’ABBÉ TO NEVERS, 1577 — DECIPHERMENT

BnF français 3198: Prague, 2 March (no. 30), and Breslau, 2 May
(nos. 37–38). Both letters use the same numerical substitution.

The breakthrough was guessing that three similar strings meant l’empereur
or empereur. Matching their figures to the letters revealed one- and two-digit
codes, with several codes for some letters (homophones). Decoding other
passages supplied the rest of the key and showed that 6 separates words.
The same key decodes both letters.
