# The Gowna police telegram

12 May 1920 ? Irish Military Archives, IE-MA-CP-03-21, pp. 11?13

The three copies preserve a police telegram describing two vehicles connected to the burning of Gowna barracks. The collated body describes a dark open Ford and a covered grey van. Separate correspondence from GHQ describes a plan involving whiskey stores. The surviving file does not explain the difference.

## How the text was obtained

Beck recovered the reciprocal letter table from the damaged sheet. The keyword INTERVAL generates the table, but the issued keyword remains unknown. The method used already known examples of keyed reciprocal alphabets. A new replay reproduces all 19 submitted rows.

## What remains open

The date word and opening and closing groups remain open. The collation gives 42 of 43 body words, or 42 of 45 units when the edge groups are included.
