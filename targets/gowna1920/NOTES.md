# The Gowna police telegram, 12 May 1920

Status: in progress. Source files assembled for the write-up and reading review.

Larry Beck, working with ChatGPT, recovered a reciprocal substitution from the damaged sheet. INTERVAL reproduces the alphabet after omitting J, but the issued keyword is unknown. Historical examples were known before the search. The 19 revised rows decode exactly with the filed key. The collation gives 42 sure words of 43 body words, or 42 of 45 counting the unknown edge groups. The day remains doubtful. The telegram describes vehicles connected to the Gowna barracks fire. The separate GHQ reply describes a whiskey-store plan. Both records should be shown together. [Military Archives, IE-MA-CP-03-21](https://www.militaryarchives.ie/uploads/MAC-002/IE_MA_CP_03_21.pdf#page=9)
