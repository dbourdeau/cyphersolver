## Look-alike families (a sign read as the value of a similar sign)

- **T (heavy-top v) read as b (tau)**: *bresa*, *bisogno* x2, *buon*, *buone*, *ben* x3, *benche* x2, *debba*, *monstrerebe*,
  *ambasator* x2, *nobilita*, *dovrebero* x2, *sarebe*, *potrebe*, *Abruzzo* x2 — written `b` in the sign file where the
  sign was judged the small tau, but size alone does not settle it in this hand.
- **pi (n) read as e (n with tail)**: *meter* x2, *provisione*, *render*, *ofendere*, *confutarle*... where a word needs e.
- **fork Y (i) vs gamma-tick (v)**: as in no. 66.
- **Daniel's "B" sign (looped, b-shaped)**: here it is **z** in every context (*Abruzzo* x2, *excelenza* x3,
  *importanza*, *sicurezza*, *speranza*); Daniel's no. 66 log gives it **rr** (*ridurre*, *Ferrara*,
  *l'errore*). Either two signs are being conflated, or the sign is polyphonic; not settled here.
