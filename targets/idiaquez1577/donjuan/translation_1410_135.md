# Translation of AGS Estado 1410,135 (Don John of Austria to the ambassadors in Genoa, castle of Namur, 7 August 1577)

English translation of `reading_1410_135.md`, sentence for sentence. Square brackets: a word the decode does not give, taken
from the contemporary decipherment AGS Estado 1410,110 (`plaintext_1410_110.txt`).

I understand that Charlemont, which is an important stronghold on this frontier of France, is in danger of falling into the
hands of the States, which would be very harmful, because there is much powder and artillery in it; and so I am now
negotiating to take it by agreement within [three] days, and if the undertaking turns out well I shall go and wait there for
whatever may come, leaving a garrison of Germans here, unless those of the land of Luxembourg oblige me to something else,
for from one hour to the next there is no certainty and no firm resolution, but one has to keep changing according to the
new turns that arise every day.
