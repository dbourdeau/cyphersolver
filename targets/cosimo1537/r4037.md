## What the cipher passages say (my words)

The two papal tithes on Florence. Cosimo writes that he was given to understand that he should proceed in this business as he sees "the prince … Doria" and the city of Genoa proceed. A proposal: to be content with half of the two tithes, one raised now and one a year later, by a Florentine commissioner whom His Holiness appoints. Church censures and an interdict are named.
