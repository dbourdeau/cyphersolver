## What the cipher passages say (my words)

The two papal tithes on Florence. Cosimo will act as Prince Doria acts for the city of Genoa. A proposal: to be content with half of the two tithes, one raised now and one a year later, by a Florentine commissioner whom His Holiness appoints. Church censures and an interdict are named.
