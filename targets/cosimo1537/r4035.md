- **DECODE:** record R4035, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4035. DECODE's image name: 214_1_05.
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project, record R4035; the archive above; M. Héder and B. Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.
- **State:** from key: the archive's key table, DECODE record R4033 (https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4033), as I read it (`key/key-table-R4033.tsv`). Decoded, not read.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R4035_digits.txt`, 7 rows.
- **Code tokens:** 136. The table gives 121 of them a value with no doubt (0.890). 14 hold a code that the table, as I read it, does not hold. 1 are doubtful: a doubtful digit, or a cut that the table does not force.
- **Counts by the word rule:** no reading. 2 words that a word sign or word code gives with no doubt are sure. The number of cipher words and of holes is not known: the decode has no word cuts. Decoded, not read.
- **One doubt:** 14 code tokens hold a code that the table, as I read it, does not hold. They show as `[nn]`.

## What the cipher passages say (my words)

The pieces that show: His Holiness; growth out of the ruin of others; to proceed in this business as the prince does at Genoa.

## Reading

Decoded, not read. No reading in sentences is given beyond the decode.
