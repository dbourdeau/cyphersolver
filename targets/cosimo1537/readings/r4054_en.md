# R4054: English translation

Biagio della Campana [...] what his nature and his quality have always been [...] always [...] the sons of Bartolomeo Valori [...] he was arrested two years ago [...] and examined; so many crimes were found in him that he deserved every punishment [...] by the Eight of the Balìa he had been condemned, so that to save him from death [...] he was sent to the fortress of Pisa [...] freed many other men held in prison for their misdeeds [...] to Florence where he has always [...] been well received [...] as an enemy [...] and offender [...] my mother at the suggestion of Giovan Francesco da Mantova [...] de' Marzi, of all three together [...] to save [...] Cybo [...] a promise of his safety given to him in very great secret, how by the Mantovano [...] de' Marzi he had been asked to poison [...] his most reverend lordship [...] Giulio, natural son of Duke Alessandro [...] without my knowledge [...] by his most reverend lordship, that the said Biagio be sought [...] and held; he had left the city to go to Rome [...] the officer [...] had come beforehand [...] he is found in prison [...] seeming [...] the favor of [...] his most reverend lordship concerning the capture [...] of this man [...] that he be released [...] his men [...] before [...] reverence [...] respect [...] always [...] shown [...] I bear [...]
