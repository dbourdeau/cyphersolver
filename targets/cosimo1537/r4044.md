# R4044: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 8 November 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4044, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4044. DECODE's image name: 214_1_72.
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project, record R4044; the archive above; M. Héder and B. Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.
- **State:** from ciphertext: my own key of the main system (`key/key-main.tsv`). It is not checked against a key. Decoded, not read.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R4044_digits.txt`, 6 rows.
- **Code tokens:** 50. The key shows a value for 42 of them (0.840). 2 of these hold a doubtful digit.
- **Counts by the word rule:** no reading. 0 words that a word sign or word code gives with no doubt are sure. The number of cipher words and of holes is not known: the decode has no word cuts. Decoded, not read.
- **One doubt:** No reading: the decode does not build sentences. At image 1, C01, the frozen read shows the value of a two-digit code on both codes of its cut; the decode gives the key's values (see `check.md`, section 4).

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows a few letters and short words only. The cipher passages of this letter are short, and many codes stay hidden. No content is claimed.

## Reading

Decoded, not read. No reading in sentences is given beyond the decode.
