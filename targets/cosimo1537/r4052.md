# R4052: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 22 May 1539

- **DECODE:** record R4052, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4052. DECODE's image name: 214_1_129.
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project, record R4052; the archive above; M. Héder and B. Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.
- **State:** from ciphertext: my own key of the main system (`key/key-main.tsv`). It is not checked against a key. Decoded, not read.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images: `transcription/R4052_digits.txt`, 9 rows.
- **Code tokens:** 115. The key shows a value for 80 of them (0.696). 4 of these hold a doubtful digit.
- **Counts by the word rule:** no reading. 2 words that a word sign or word code gives with no doubt are sure. The number of cipher words and of holes is not known: the decode has no word cuts. Decoded, not read.
- **One doubt:** No reading: the decode does not build sentences. At image 3, C04, the frozen read shows the value of a two-digit code on both codes of its cut; the decode gives the key's values (see `check.md`, section 4).

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows a few letters and short words only. The cipher passages of this letter are short, and many codes stay hidden. No content is claimed. The rows show "grande" and "acidente" ("great", "accident"; not sure).

## Reading

Decoded, not read. No reading in sentences is given beyond the decode.
