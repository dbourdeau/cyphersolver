## What the cipher passages say (my words)

The tithes. Cosimo writes that he begged someone not to burden a party, at present so exhausted, with such a levy; the code for that party has no value. His Majesty's aid is named. A doubtful word sign that the table gives as the Emperor stands at four places: his favour, his mind, his coming near Italy. After "il prefat", a doubtful barred sign gives the table's King of France; cut otherwise, it gives "Arciuescouo" (archbishop). Niccolini is to draw the matter out as long as he can, with no offer, because time can bring much advantage. The passage probably names the case of "Altopaso" (perhaps Altopascio).
