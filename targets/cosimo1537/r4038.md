## What the cipher passages say (my words)

Niccolini is to accept the extension "se[c]ondo Clemente" (perhaps "as under Pope Clement"), as they have said, and have it issued as in the copy that is sent to him. If they knew of it, they would not want it so broad, or would want better terms. Any further advantage is left to him.
