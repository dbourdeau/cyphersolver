# R5453: decoded from ciphertext

- **DECODE:** record R5453, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5453.
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1597, no. 153.
- **Ledger entry:** catalogue 2407, sign family I.
- **Pages:** page 1, page 2.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode is the plain output of my key on my rows. It has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands yet.
- **How the key was obtained:** from ciphertext. The key came from the cipher text alone. No key sheet and no clear text checks it.
- **Read together:** one key reads both pages. My public page gives them as one text; `key/` files each page apart.
- **Year, as a lead:** most likely 30 November 1491. The rows give no year; `NOTES.md` gives the reason and the source.
- **One doubt:** Page 1: On 19 of the 502 signs where my frozen read shows a letter (3.8%), my key gives another letter. 4.6% of the signs show no letter in my frozen read.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `transcription/`.
- **Credit:** DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project; M. Héder and B. Megyesi (2022), "The DECODE Database of Historical Ciphers and Keys: Version 2", HistoCrypt, https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188397. No DECODE image is published.

## Summary

My public page gives a gloss of this letter as far as my frozen read carries it, and an English text. The gloss is not a reading, and this folder does not repeat it: https://github.com/prabal-rje/branicki-1757/blob/main/sforza-160-records/records/r5453.md
