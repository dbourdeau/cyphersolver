# R5416: English translation

### Page 1

You told me that if Messer Ludovico wishes to make an agreement with the Lord, the Florentines will be content with it. Girolamo da Forlì, upon your advice, has reported to Messer Ludovico on behalf of Diotesalvi that if he wishes to place Aulla and Rissana under the guard of his community, he will see that they are accepted, and that if he wishes to transfer the rest he will arrange good terms for him with the Lord's consent; so that these things and other hopes [...] in Italy keep our friend in suspense, all the more since he is strongly urged by Tomasino and his mother to bide his time. He refuses to understand the danger of Tomasino, who is poor, spirited, and eager to follow, knowing little of his avarice, instability, and scant show of affection; he trusts in the eldest son and so judges the timing badly. The Ferrarese negotiations in my view are dormant. I have nothing else to write at present except that matters do not stand well in this way. From Sarzana, [...] April [...]
