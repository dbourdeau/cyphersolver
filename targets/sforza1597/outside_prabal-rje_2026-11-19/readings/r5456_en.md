# R5456: English translation

### Page 1

Giovanni da Folo, whom I sent to Genoa to give news to the governor, informs me that he has won over to his side and service a citizen named Nicolosio de' Leonardi, whom he has found to be a shrewd man and already greatly inflamed with love for the Archbishop. The said Nicolosio says he is confident [...] the Lord of ten thousand ducats without scandal [...] if you wish to understand his design. If it seems good to the Lord, he may send Messer Angelo Acciaiuoli to speak with you [...] he has urged Messer Ludovico to go to Florence in person in case the restitution of the citadel is delayed any further. I do not know whether it would be well done, in order to please the [...] men of La Meglia who are in the wrong, to displease those of Sarzana who are in the right. It does not seem the right time to me now. At Sarzana, 11 September, etc.
