# The transcription

## What it is

My own transcription of the DECODE images, one line for each cipher row. Each sign becomes a label of two letters. A label names one sign shape of one page. `?` is a sign that I do not read. Clear text never enters a row.

## Known challenges in the source

Some signs differ only by a small stroke or dot, so two labels can be one sign, or one label two signs. The record pages give the share of signs that my frozen read leaves without a letter.

## Where the rows are

`transcription/<record>_p<page>_signs.txt`.
