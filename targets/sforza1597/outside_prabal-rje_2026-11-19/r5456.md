- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1597, no. 156.
- **Ledger entry:** catalogue 2402, sign family D.
- **Pages:** page 1.
- **State:** decoded, not read. The decode is the plain output of my key on my rows. It has no word division, and no reading in sentences stands yet.
- **How the key was obtained:** from ciphertext. The key came from the cipher text alone. No key sheet and no clear text checks it.
- **One doubt:** On 33 of the 571 signs where my frozen read shows a letter (5.8%), my key gives another letter. 9.1% of the signs show no letter in my frozen read. My public page marks this reading as less sure.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `transcription/`.
