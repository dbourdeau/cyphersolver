Notes on the Italian:

- *cot.*: as written; probably *cotesta*. No gloss seen over it.
- *compendo*: as written, c-o-m-p-e-n-d-o, and the gloss has the same. The sense needs *comprendo* (with an r supplied)
  or *compiendo* (with an i supplied). Not settled.
- *[?mas]sime*: 19P is *ma* from the gloss on L05; here it is proposed, with the double s written once: *massime*.
- *listu[?zione]*: the groups give l i s t u, and the gloss over 183 ends "zione". The gloss over the letter groups reads
  more like "sistu-". *l'istruzione* would need an r that is not in the cipher. Not settled.
- *[52 ?]*: the V06 line ends in a sign pair written like "Sʀ.". It may be code 52 or clear "Sp.". A gloss word, perhaps
  "Caggio", stands beside it.
- *min(i)stro*: the fourth group is blotted; it fits 16 = i.
