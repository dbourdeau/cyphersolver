## Documents

Archivio di Stato di Firenze (ASFi), *Mediceo avanti il Principato* (MAP):
- **Cat. 2574 (`MAP XXXVIII, doc. 418`, old c. 428; viewer pos. 913-914):** Autograph letter of Pierfilippo Pandolfini (*"Rome orator"*) to Lorenzo de' Medici, dated *"Die xxvij Martij 1482"* (verso endorsement *"1482 Da Pierphilippo pandolfini da Roma ad xxvij di marzo, recev. a di 31 detto"*). BNCF *Autografi Palatini* VI/51-53 (`BNCF0003865786-5800`) and Stella Fletcher (pp. 71-72 n. 38) support re-dating it to **27 March 1483** as a proposed 2-day Florentine New Year (*ab Incarnatione*, 25 March) slip (see Historical verification below). Clear opening (`L01-L04`), 27 lines interleaving `1,114` cipher symbols (`73` types) with `18` clear Italian anchors (`L05-L31`), and clear close/signature (`L32`). Zero interlinear or marginal glosses.
- **Cat. 2559 (`MAP LIII, doc. 13`; viewer pos. 36, id 65099):** Seventeen continuous unspaced lines (`L01-L17`, `863` cipher symbols across `64` types) in the identical hand, ink, and symbol inventory, with zero clear words and zero glosses, dated April 1483.

Unified audited stream (`transcript_2574.txt`, `transcript_2559.txt`, `pandolfini_diplomatic.txt`): `1,977` cipher tokens across `75` types (`67` core cipher symbols + `8` atomic cursive Latin cover words). Core IC: `#2574` `0.02980`, `#2559` `0.03951`, unified `0.03335`.

## The system and the key (`key_pandolfini1482.tsv`)

Both letters encipher continuous unspaced Quattrocento Florentine Italian with a **polyphonic homophonic substitution cipher**, dedicated cipher nulls, embedded cursive Latin *nihil importantes* / cover words, and four compact diplomatic nomenclators:
- **Five audited Grade-1 polyphonic (`2-to-1`) symbols:** `II` (`187×`) = `a / o` (`-339.3` nats on drop), `9` (`115×`) = `e / l` (`+118.3` nats), `sig` (`91×`) = `t / d` (`+57.0` nats), `pi` (`35×`) = `t / b` (`-56.1` nats), `d` (`27×`) = `c / g` (`-147.6` nats).
- **43 audited Grade-1 monophonic symbols:** `m, 4 = e`; `xi = o`; `c, o_base = a`; `6, 7, 8, t, trid = i`; `n = u/v`; `e, c_plus, Q = r`; `f, p_bar, d_bar = n`; `o_plus, w_bar, T = s`; `3, eps = d`; `rev_c, 1, b = c`; `bar_dot, a = p`; `5, g, q_dbl, V = l` (with standalone `V = t` in Grade-2 refinement); `o, theta = m`; `p, eq, y = h`; `nab, 2 = b`; `SL2 = f`; `u = g`; `f_dbl = z`; `o_dot, dot_bar = q`. Single consonants routinely stand for geminates (*esere, potrebe, doverebe, tropa, apicata, bola, benedeta, coligati, ocupazione, mezo*).
- **Six Grade-1 confirmed cipher nulls (`''`, recorded as `system.nulls.count = 6`):** `b_bar` (`29×`), `h_bar` (`26×`), `alp` (`21×`), `div` (`21×`), `x` (`19×`), `wavy` (`7×`). Separately, `Mx` (`15×`) and `Rx` (`12×`, after audit rejected `Rx = che` at `-35.4` raw nats) remain **Grade-2 provisional nulls**: `decrypt.py` emits `[Rx]` explicitly (`9×` in `#2574`, `3×` in `#2559`), and `#2574 L09 [debia]` is bracketed because its final vowel depends on provisional `Rx = a`. `tf_lig` (`10×`) has chancery *per*-ligature morphology (`+10.2` raw nats as `per`) and emits `[tf_lig]` in `decrypt.py`.
- **Paleographic / Grade-2 refinements:** `b = {c, f}` (*Faenza, Ferara, fondamento*), `o_base = {a, d}` (*di Faenza, da fare, fondamento*), `y = {h, g}` (*bisogno, compagno, gli*), `D = {c, b}` (*ocupazione, apicata* vs. *darebe*), `H = p` (*tropa fede, apicata*), standalone `V = t` (*benedeta bola*), and high-resolution crop confirmation of `1 p 9 = che` (`5×`), `#2559 L03` `a m e 1 p 9 = perché`, `#2559 L06` `et da Milano`, and `#2559 L14` `o_dot = q` (`in questo`). Every word depending on Grade-2 refinements, off-table spellings (`#2559` `[praticha]`, `[pocha]`, `[questa]`; `#2574` `[suo]`), or mechanical departures (`#2574` `[pensieri]`/`pensier(a/o)`, `[debia]`/`debi[Rx]`, `[dicono]`/`dic(t/d)(a/o)no`, `[desiderio]`/`desiedrio`; `#2559 L10 [?tr]` from `V e`) is bracketed as a hole in `reading_2574.md` and `reading_2559.md` (and three unbracketed shorthand/orthographic variants on `#2574 L25, L26, L30` are excluded from sure words in `sure_words.tsv`).
- **Embedded cursive Latin cover/null words (matching Laurentian key sheets `MAP LXXII 178, 181, 185`, `CXIX 125`):** `LAT_nam` (`4×`), `LAT_Si` (`3×`), `V t = {LAT:ut}` (`8×`: `5×` in `#2574`, `3×` in `#2559`), `V 3 = {LAT:vz.}` (`2×`), `LAT_iterum` (`2×`), `LAT_quotiens` (`1×`), `LAT_dumnon` (`1×`), plus unresolved `{?ocusa}` (`1×`), `{?grandis}` (`1×`), and `{?grandes}` (`1×`, occupying a subject slot before *pigliassi* on `#2574 L24`).
- **Four open diplomatic nomenclators:** `o o` (`{oo?}`, `3×`), `B` (`{B?}`, `5×`), `invT` (`{invT?}`, `2×`), and `H_cr` (`{Hcr?}`, `1×`).

## Validation (`validate.py`, `validation.txt`)

All validation uses a leak-free order-5 early-Italian nospace `DenseLM` trained on `1,368,968` chars of repository pre-1650 Italian strictly excluding `{pandolfini1482, rucellai1482, mazzinghi1478, toledo1479}`, and unboosted `lang/lexicons/it.json.gz` (zero target lexicon boosts). `validate.py` loads both monophonic and polyphonic entries directly from `key_pandolfini1482.tsv`, computes PASS/FAIL dynamically (`z >= 5.0` and `p <= 0.01` on all four 200-shuffle LM controls), and exits non-zero on failure (verified against both all-`'e'` and all-`'a'` constant negative-control keys):
1. **Strict zero-choice monophonic baseline (`M1`, zero beam disambiguation):**
   - Unified (`1,820` decoded chars): `LM/ch = -2.9150`, `Cov>=4 = 0.5527`, `Cov>=5 = 0.3527` (`#2574`: `-3.0358 / 0.3427`; `#2559`: `-2.7704 / 0.3647`).
   - 200 shuffled keys: `LM/ch = -6.4021 ± 0.4242` (`max = -5.2982`, **z = +8.22**, `p = 0.0050`); `Cov>=5` **z = +33.92**.
   - 200 shuffled streams: `LM/ch = -5.2049 ± 0.0558` (`max = -5.0458`, **z = +41.06**, `p = 0.0050`); `Cov>=5` **z = +31.00**.
2. **Audited 5-polyphonic beam (`P5`, identical `beam_w = 8` on real text and all 200 controls):**
   - Unified (`1,820` decoded chars): `LM/ch = -2.5123`, `Cov>=4 = 0.5885`, `Cov>=5 = 0.3929` (`#2574`: `-2.6851 / 0.3730`; `#2559`: `-2.3051 / 0.4167`).
   - 200 shuffled keys (randomly assigning both monophonic values and which 5 symbols carry the 5 polyphonic pairs): `LM/ch = -6.2878 ± 0.4005` (**z = +9.43**, `p = 0.0050`); `Cov>=5` **z = +34.43**.
   - 200 shuffled streams: `LM/ch = -4.9144 ± 0.0537` (**z = +44.73**, `p = 0.0050`); `Cov>=5` **z = +28.48**.
3. **Separate blind `#2559` experiment, held-out synthetic control, and independent review check (`PR #1018`):**
   - Equal-restart blind KL-SA on pure-cipher `#2559` alone (`12` restarts x `20k` steps + polish, zero clear anchors): `-2.8226` vs 10 shuffled streams `-3.4738 ± 0.0377` (**z = +17.27**). (The final joint key used `#2574`'s inline clear anchors, so `outcome.method` is classified as `key recovered based on adjacent plaintext`, and no untouched historical held-out letter in this key is yet tested.)
   - Held-out Quattrocento synthetic homophonic+null control (`rucellai1482 + toledo1479`, `1,704` chars, excluded from LM): `85.0%` character accuracy blind (`0` anchors) and `95.3%` with `8` anchors (`z = +18.54` vs 8 equal-restart controls).
   - Independent review check by Brice Dudout (`@bdudout`, `PR #1018` / `#1023`, manifest `5fa5a920...`): two blind transcriptions of `#2574` and `#2559` agreed with ours on `0.889` (`#2574`) and `0.925` (`#2559`) of tokens, yielded `0.703` lexical coverage on `#2559` under our `value_m1` key (`0.464` best of 200 controls), and confirmed 21 of 22 sign-shape targets across two valid blind shape plates (`controls 3/3`).

## Historical verification (27 March and April 1483, War of Ferrara)

- **Catalogue date analysis for `#2574` (`27 March 1482` = `27 March 1483`) with BNCF digital IDs:** Inspection of Lorenzo de' Medici's original signed outgoing letters to Pandolfini in Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze, *Autografi Palatini* VI (`BNCF0003865717-BNCF0003865905`):
  - **`Autogr. Pal. VI/51`** (`BNCF0003865786-5788`, 20 Mar 1481 [= 1482]): addressed `Urbini`.
  - **`Autogr. Pal. VI/52`** (`BNCF0003865790-5794`, 28 Mar 1482): addressed `In Foro Sempronii` (Fossombrone).
  - **`Autogr. Pal. VI/53`** (`BNCF0003865796-5800`, 2 Apr 1482, `Urbini`): opens *"Se bene nelle de 26, 28 et 29 del passato si contengono molte cose..."*, placing Pandolfini in Urbino and Fossombrone on 26, 28, and 29 March 1482 rather than as resident *Rome orator* (followed by `VI/54-55`, `BNCF0003865802-5808`, 6 and 10 Apr 1482, `Urbini`).
  Independently, Stella Fletcher (pp. 71-72 n. 38) cites Pandolfini's dispatches from Rome on 29 March and 2 April 1483. Because the Florentine year (*ab Incarnatione*) turned on 25 March, reading *"Die xxvij Martij 1482"* as a proposed 2-day New Year slip for **27 March 1483** is strongly supported by this itinerary.
- **War of Ferrara convergence:** Re-dating `#2574` to 27 March 1483 places both letters days or weeks apart in Rome right after the **Diet of Cremona** (late Feb - early Mar 1483, `#2574 L22` `alla dieta`). `#2574`'s *"indugiare la publicatione"* (`L16`) and `#2559`'s *"publicare [questa] [benedeta] bola contro a {B?}, che [darebe] [a'] coligati più sicurtà"* (`L15-L16`) match Pope Sixtus IV's papal bull of excommunication and interdict against Venice (finalized 24 May and published 25 May 1483; cf. Fletcher pp. 71-72 n. 38), delayed while Count Girolamo Riario (`{oo?}`) pursued **Faenza** (`speranza di Faenza`, `#2574 L15-L19`) and while Archbishop Andrea Jamometić's Basel council schism (*"il fatto dell'arcivescovo"*, `#2574 L15, L17`) alarmed Rome.
