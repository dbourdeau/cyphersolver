## Historical Notes

- **Date and diplomatic context:** April 1483, during the War of Ferrara (1482–1484) after Pope Sixtus IV switched sides (December 1482) to join the *Santissima Lega* (*coligati*) against Venice. The *"benedeta bola contro a {B?}"* refers to Sixtus IV's papal bull of excommunication and interdict against Venice, agreed upon at the Diet of Cremona (Feb–Mar 1483), delayed across March–April 1483, finalized on 24 May and published on 25 May 1483 (compare Stella Fletcher, pp. 71–72 n. 38, and Eleonora Plebani's study of Pandolfini's Roman embassy, `iris.uniroma1.it/handle/11573/952345`).
- **Open nomenclator candidates:** `{oo?}` (2× in `#2559`, 1× in `#2574`) contextually fits Count Girolamo Riario, Sixtus IV's nephew and lord of Imola and Forlì; `{B?}` (3× in `#2559`, 2× in `#2574`, grammatically plural *"con loro"*) contextually fits the Venetians (*i Viniziani*); `{Hcr?}` (1×, closing L17 *"pratiche di {Hcr?}"*, token `H_cr`) is a candidate for *pace* (peace).
