este1585 - tests of the key on BnF Cinq Cents de Colbert 338 (contributor's protocols, not docs/_validate.py)

The key is Satoshi Tomokiyo's table "Cardinal d'Este's Cipher (1584-1586)" (Cryptiana, built on BnF fr. 16041-16042), with our
shape names and a few values fixed on pages of Colbert 338 that carry a contemporary gloss. Each protocol and amendment was
hashed (SHA-256) before the computation it governs.

   Why: the glosses of this volume are not literal (summaries, other wording, abbreviations such as "maral"), and some are cut
   in the gutter. The cipher passages read as French with every key from K-T2 on, but the formal test was never met.

B. Probable words and a single held-out page (5 Nov 2026; protocol PROTOCOLE-CRIBS 933401e4...)
   - Probable words, fixed in a list before any trial, placed on the pages already seen (A) only. One value changed:
     alpha with a descending tail = f ("differer / difficultes", 4 places). Key K-C01 frozen (SHA-256 002d38d9...).
   - H = p. 523 (view 275, right page), a whole page in cipher with no gloss, never transcribed or decoded before; transcribed by
     shapes after the freeze and hashed before decoding (08a3f8a0...). The transcriber knew the table but applied no tool to H.
   - Metric M5: share of decoded letters covered by French words of 5+ letters (the repository's lang/lexicons/fr.json.gz,
     frequency >= 5, accents removed, j->i, v->u, y->i), dynamic programming over the whole page; nulls give nothing; a sign
     with no value counts as an uncovered letter. Controls: 200 keys shuffling the letter values of K-C01, seed 523.
   - Thresholds fixed in advance: VALIDATED M5 >= 0.60 and >= best control + 0.30; PARTIAL if the gap holds without 0.60.
     Calibration declared before the measurement: the contemporary glosses themselves score 0.50-0.73 on M5 (0.62 on the two
     longest), so 0.60 sits near the ceiling.
   - Result (one measurement): 777 signs, 766 with a value (0.986); M5 = 0.457; controls max 0.078, median 0.014; gap 0.379.
     DECISION: PARTIAL.
