Pre-registration PREREG-KC02 (SHA-256 18bf6d3dfaf7b16e79a76dd12e5f1fba4a4ae1af25cac817bb8acce055613221), hashed at 21:48 CET before any

M5 >= 0.45 AND >= best of 200 shuffled keys + 0.30. Why 0.45 and not 0.60 (declared before): the contemporary clear texts of the volume

already bring M5 to a median of 0.35 (max 0.43), so M5 >= 0.45 means fewer than about 5% wrong tokens.
Result (one run, seed 517): 979 signs, 969 with a value (0.990); M5 = 0.509; best control 0.067 (median 0.025); gap 0.442. VALIDATED.
The block is used up. Found by reading after the measurement (not part of the test): the Venus sign is o in p. 517 L1 (K-C01's value)
and a in L28, against K-C02's [faire]; the barred dumbbell behaves as a null in L1 and L29 (Tomokiyo), as on p. 523 L22.
