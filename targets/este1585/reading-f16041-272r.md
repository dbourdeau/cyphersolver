## Content (summary)
The writer fears that his words to a man he knows to be "so cold" will be thrown to the wind, but will strive to make him believe him. News: the King of Spain, beyond his eighty (ottante) galleys,
wants to bring them up to two hundred, and has ordered at Naples, Sicily and in all his other places where timber can be had that they work at it with all diligence. Asked by the writer
whether he knew anything certain of "the marriage of him and the daughter of Spain", Cardinal Madruzzo (Madrusi), brother of the Emperor's ambassador, who handles this lord's affairs,
showed rather that he had word that the business... (continued on f. 272 v). "Ce seigneur" is not named on this page; in April 1584 the match in question is most likely that of
Charles Emmanuel of Savoy with the Infanta Catalina Micaela (married March 1585): an inference, not a reading.
