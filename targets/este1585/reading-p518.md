## Why each probable word is probable (decoded letters -> reading)

- L1 `estredel?` -> **de la**: the sign first named `6` (a closed o with a short upward tick) was shown on a blind shape sheet
  (14 Nov 2026; shape thumbnails only, no values, two controls answered right): Brice Dudout chose the plain o (= d), the same tick
  being seen on certain o's ("de", p. 518 L11; p. 513 L10). "de" + "l" + a sign lost in the gutter -> "de la [nature]"; both probable.
- L1 `as?maieste` -> **a sa maieste**: the looped cross `dag` (no value in K-C02) taken as a (one place).
- L2-3 `deb|ir` -> **debvoir**: the line ends in the gutter; "vo" lost or omitted.
- L4-5 `dudi|` -> **du dict**: "ct" lost in the gutter at the end of L4 (pass B: last sign legible at the edge).
- L5 `ma?reeschea l` -> **mareschal**: the new shape `sdot` and the two barred dumbbells (oob) read as nulls (as on p. 517 L1, L29).
- L5 `c?me` -> **come**, `l[faire]n` -> **l'on**: the X with a curved stroke (`Xc`, noted as a different X by the blind pass) as o, the barred dumbbell here as e, the Venus sign as o (as on p. 517 L1).
- L6 `iu[fait]ques` -> **iusques**: the "bold lambda" is a plain lambda (= s) crossed by the bars of the neighbouring #, not the barred A.
- L7-8 `sapr|hoit` -> **s'aprochoit**: "oc" omitted by the cipher clerk.
- L10 `a?aires` -> **afaires**: `feq` (f crossed by a double =) as f.
- L13 `to?t` -> **tout**: `fnb` (plain long s, no bar) as u, a variant of f (= u). `poul?` -> **pourrai**: p-o-u certain, the rest in the gutter.
- L14 `toutesor` -> **toute sorte**: "te" lost in the gutter.
- L15 `montmorenc?` -> **Montmorenci**: last sign in the gutter (as spelled on p. 517).
- L16 `a?aiser` -> **apaiser**: `ssi` (three tight loops) as p.
