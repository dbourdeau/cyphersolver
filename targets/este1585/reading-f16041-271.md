## Probable words (why)
- {d'Espagne}: code y = [Espagne] (given as "Espaigne" by the contemporary decipherment of Colbert 338 p. 513), here after "le Roy" and a null.
- {dict}: decrypts "aict"; "ce que dict ung chacun" by sense (one letter short).
- {puisqu'}: "puisi" + a sign cut in the gutter. {retombe}, {maladies}: "rtolbe", "laladie" with g8 = m (learned on this page).
- {dernierement}: "de rnier" + a sign without value (iloop) + "ed"; {on}: Xc (o) + null, before "eut advis".
- {indisposition}: "indisposifio" + a cut sign (f for t). {aux}: "aum" before "champs".
- {qu'en}: "quen" before "doulceur". {procedé}: "proceg" (g for d). {capable}: "[ca]pable" across the line end.
- {n'auroict}: "na" + cut sign / "oict". {voioit}: "uoioic" + cut sign. {discours}: "disco" at the line end.
- {predecesseurs}: "prede" / "eurs" across the line, a group lost between. {coustumiers}: "co<new3>stu.mi.e" with new3 = u and two nulls.
