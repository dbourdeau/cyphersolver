# este1585 - English translation of the cipher block of p. 513 (letter of 2 January 1585)

Read in part (91.5%); confirmed by the contemporary decipherment in the margin. ‹…› = unresolved.

"‹…› that in [Spain] - so the sieur de Longlée informs me - they are scheming, should the Church change its shepherd [i.e. should the
pope die], strengthening their own designs and weakening those of the others; and that express orders have been sent here. And since
this is a matter of such importance for the King's service, …"

Context (not checked in sources): Pierre de Ségusson, sieur de Longlée, was Henri III's agent at the court of Philip II; Gregory XIII
was 83 and died on 10 April 1585, three months after this letter; the conclave elected Sixtus V on 24 April.
