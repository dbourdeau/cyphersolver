# este1585 - English translation of BnF fr. 16041 f. 271 (cipher page of Cardinal Luigi d'Este's letter to Villeroy, April 1584)

Translated from reading-f16041-271.md; [ ] = restored, ‹ › = unresolved, ( ) = added for sense.

"... ‹...› the King of Spain ... ‹...› If what everyone here says is true, he [the Pope] cannot last long, since he falls back so often into his
usual illnesses; and again lately we had word of his indisposition, with some danger, although since then he has had the same (news) of his
recovery. His Holiness is in the country, and it is believed he will not be back for eight days; at that time I hope to go there too, and not
to fail to speak to him as of myself, in keeping with your [letter], about the Duke of Montmorency, and with what gentleness His Majesty has
proceeded with him. I believe His Holiness will still be capable of this, and that this duke would not have such boldness against the commands
of the King his master if he did not see himself encouraged by another greater than (himself). And if His Holiness enters into the matter,
I will remind him well that his predecessors were accustomed ..."
