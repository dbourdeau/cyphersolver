# cardona1517: reading of the cipher passages (draft, 4 Nov 2026)

**Status: decoded, not read.** The sign alphabet and the frequent code groups are secure. Two historical names
confirm the key: *Burgès de Petrucis* (Borghese Petrucci) in 1517 and *Ian Paulo Basionio* (Giampaolo Baglioni) in
1520. But most sentences still have holes, so only the passages that build sense are given as sentences.

The full line-by-line decryption is `decode.txt`; transcription line numbers are in brackets.

Notation:
- `[...]` unread, `[?word]` doubtful, `[word]` supplied;
- spelling as decoded (*u* for *v*, *z* for *ç*).

## Document 1: Ramón de Cardona to Charles I, Naples, 5 October 1517 (MSS/20210/11/1)

**Summary.** Cardona reports to the young king on the troops in the kingdom of Naples and on Rome:
- the men cannot be kept or paid; he asked the Pope for a loan to keep the infantry, and the cost requires
  discharging fifty men;
- the affairs of Siena after Leo X crushed the cardinals' plot of 1517. Cardinal Alfonso Petrucci was executed that
  summer; his brother Borghese, the former lord of Siena, wishes to live and die under the Pope's protection.

**Context in clear.**
- Before block A: the 260 lances Cardona could not bring.
- After A: the count of Potenza's men want their back pay.
- Before C: the infantry coming to the kingdom sacked Fabriano, in the March of Ancona; Cardona sent Don Ramón de
  Perellós to the ambassador Don Pedro de Urrea, asking the Pope to lend *dineros para entretener la dicha
  ynfanteria*.

## Document 2: Ramón de Cardona to Charles V, Naples, 18 June 1520 (MSS/20210/11/4)

**Summary.**
- **Rome, June 1520.** Leo X had Giampaolo Baglioni, lord of Perugia, lured to Rome and beheaded in Castel
  Sant'Angelo; the cipher speaks of the proceedings, the sentence and the cardinals.
- **Naples.** A *parlamento* (presumably the Neapolitan parliament), and the pay of the troops.

**Context in clear.**
- Before the block: defence against the Turkish fleet on the coast of Apulia, money for the castles and pay, ending
  *de lo que ocurriere a la hora daré aviso a v. al.*
- After it: the 200 houses to be built on the *monte de Gaeta*.
