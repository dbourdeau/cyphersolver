# Sign inventory (two blind passes)

Two AI language-model agents transcribed both letters independently from the photographs, each with its own codes
(pass A: transcription_*_passA.txt, 24 codes; pass B: transcription_*_passB.txt, 22 codes). The correspondence is in
key.tsv (columns 1-2). Differences of method: pass A writes the stroke-plus-loop sign as two codes (Le), pass B as one (E);
pass A separates small o-variants (a, E) that pass B merges into o; both write the W-shape as VV.

Agreement: after mapping the codes, the two passes give the same decryption for most words of both letters; the
disagreements are concentrated on the vertical strokes (L / I / F, i.e. d / b), on a few blotted or struck signs, and on
r18, r27, v04 (see the diff of decryption_120_passA.txt and decryption_120_passB.txt). The plain stroke I (b) and the
stroke with foot L (d) are not kept apart reliably by either pass; the reading therefore takes L as d or b by context.
