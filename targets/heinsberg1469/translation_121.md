Dear faithful one. Please be at Aachen, you and Hucking the [?rent-master], on Friday evening or Saturday morning, with
the money and all that is ready; and if there are [?any] letters there that one must have, bring them with you. And tell
Krumel that he too should come there. We intend, God willing, to have our envoy there at the said time; know how to
conduct yourselves accordingly. And order Heinsberg well, so that no harm happens during the absence. If [...] is there,
tell him to ride with you.
