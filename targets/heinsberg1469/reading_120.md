## Notes

- Spellings are those of the signs, with L read d or b and P read h or ch by context (geschriben, bliben, besserunge;
  hast, hette, nacht). "nest" (nehest), "sese" (sehs), "ale/alle" are the writer's.
- [?hinder] (r01, P-i-n-d-e-r): "hinder" (hindrance) fits "aller hinder an dem gelde"; probable.
- [?mant] (r07): "ein mant oder sese wochen", a month or six weeks; probable.
- [?inbrag] (r10): "sonder inbrag" = without prejudice (Eintrag); probable.
- [?hant] (r15, P-g-B-9): "bi hant", close at hand; probable.
- [...|3rude] and [...|sen] (r18): the first sign (3) occurs only here; "brude"/"frunde" and "sin"/"den" both possible;
  unresolved.
- [...|gedin] (r35): unresolved ("gedinge"?).
- [?halten] (r36): decryption "chaiten" (pass A) / "chaiten" (B): probable "halten" with one misread stroke.
- [?Flodrop] (v07): last sign unreadable on both passes; Godart von Flodorp, a Guelders/Limburg family (a Wilhelm van
  Flodorp is addressed in fol. 115 of the same file).
- r10: an isolated blotted sign after "inbrag" is left out; r06: a repeat of r05 ("2C LAo 8oLo") is struck on the page.
- r27 "niemt" = niemand. "binen" = binnen, "versorknis" = versorgnis: the writer's spellings.
- "des foits wegen von Remunde": the Vogt (bailiff) of Roermond.
