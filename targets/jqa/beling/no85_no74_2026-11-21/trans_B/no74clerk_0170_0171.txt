NOTES:
- The three numbers left in figures are on P1 (frame 0170): 387.1431.1101, written in a larger, upright hand between "How long this is" and "Russia". Over them the clerk wrote a short word (I read "sort", not sure) and drew a long line ending in a cross (+); the same cross stands on the short rule above "Error in Cypher." at the foot of the page, so the foot note is keyed to these figures.
- Marks on 0170: P4 an apostrophe-like stroke after "Commerce" and a vertical stroke before "more"; P8 an apostrophe before "have" and a vertical stroke after it; P14 "(will)" in round brackets; the heading word "Decyphered" struck through.
- Marks on 0171 left: Q12 "(they)" and Q13 "in)" carry the clerk's brackets as shown (opening bracket before "they", closing bracket after "they" and again after "in"). Q3 last word struck out and illegible. Q16 the place name looks like "Plangen" (Q3 has "Polangen").
- Spellings that may be my misreading: Q12 "Dantzic" (first letters unclear), P10 "Papinburg", P8 "succesful" (one s visible), Q17 "Radsiwiloff".
