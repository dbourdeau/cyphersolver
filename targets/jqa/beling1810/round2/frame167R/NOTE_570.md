# Frame 167R: the group before "ultimately" in No. 25-A (for the open 570 in No. 88)

Andrew Beling, with AI agents, 22 Nov 2026. Not checked by a person.

## Why this frame was looked at

Upstream's notes on No. 88 leave group 570 open at rem88 l. 11 ("it tol he said"). The image there shows 570. The sheet gives *tol* / *toe*. The sense wants *was*, which is 500.

In the first round of the 1810 test, our first transcription of No. 25 had 570 on frame 167R, at "but [570] ultimately rejected". There the period decipherment reads *was*. If the film showed 570 there, it would be a second place where Adams wrote 570 for *was*.

## Result

None of the four new readings gives 570 as its main reading. The two transcribers read 500, which is *was* in the table and on the sheet, and fits the decipherment's *was*. The two crop readers split between 500 and 510. Helper 2 names 570 as its main alternative: the thin dash above the middle stroke could be a 7's top bar. Reading the dash as the 5's flag is an interpretation, though both helpers judge a 7 less likely, since this hand's 7s drop below the line.

So this frame gives no clear second example of 570 for *was*, and our earlier 570 was most likely a misreading of ours. The frame does not exclude 570. The open 570 in No. 88 is not affected either way.

The crop readers were told which group to read but not why, and were given no plaintext, table or candidates. (Wording revised on 23 Nov 2026 after a review by a separate AI agent of ours, Claude Opus 5.5, that had not built this package.)
