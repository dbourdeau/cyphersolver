- 0384 L4: isolated "85." on its own line between two lines, under the drawn curve, no gloss (probably overflow of the previous cipher line).
- Clear words inside cipher runs are often the scribe's own re-spelling of a ciphered word (e.g. {pendant}, {catcherif}, {contraire}, {Veritable}, {intention}, {ordonne}, {donner}, {actuell...}): they appear in clear just after/before the cipher and the decipherer repeats them in the gloss.
- Many 5s are written with a flourish on the top stroke (look like 5'); not treated as a mark.
- Gloss transcription: 17th-c. spelling kept; final -s often looks like -x/-r in the hand and was normalised to s; doubtful words marked ?. Glosses are written in continuous script with word breaks partly arbitrary (e.g. "cetteresolution"); here separated where the hand shows a break.
