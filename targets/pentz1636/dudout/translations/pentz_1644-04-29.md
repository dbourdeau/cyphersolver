# Translation: Pentz to Christian IV, Glückstadt, 29 April 1644 (cipher passages in capitals)
"… The enemy is now beginning to burn hard; within a few days he has laid nine villages in the district of Ottersberg, and some in the parish of Zellinghausen, in ashes. I CANNOT, however gladly I would, DO THE ENEMY from here SUCH DAMAGE any more as was done to him before by MY RAIDING PARTIES, as everyone here knows, because THE GARRISON HERE has been so REDUCED for ME and THE TROOPS taken out, so that at times I could keep NO MORE than FOUR MEN in the {RAVELIN} before the KREMPE GATE as an OUTPOST. Nor does one hear that, since those troops MARCHED OUT FROM HERE and took their QUARTERS in DITHMARSCHEN, they have attempted or achieved anything against the ENEMY. …"
