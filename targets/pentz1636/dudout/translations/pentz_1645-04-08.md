# Pentz to the Chancellor, 8 Apr 1645 - English
As to the Spanish loan: I do not remember writing about it to His Majesty myself, but I may have held out hopes for [M]ay or some other such term. What one may expect from the Spanish [311, 1000] ... a year ago, when their ‹…› troops had joined the Imperial ones [269] ... it was requested that a paymaster [bring] a considerable sum [305] here ... not unwilling. But {at that time} ... treated with ... [81] I {no} longer stand in such understanding ... so easily kept, unless there were hope that after the peace is made ... the disbanding ... to hand over {some} troops to him. The Chancellor writes that provisions were badly handled; from this one may gather that His Majesty has once again been falsely informed.
