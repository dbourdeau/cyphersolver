# Pentz to the Chancellor, 1645 (pencil 4/5) - English
I have received your note of 24 April ... how this loss suffered has set back the peace ... ‹…› ... Rendsburg now in the greatest danger; great [is the guilt of] whoever is the cause of it ... [101] the remaining troops, [of which] we have lost many in a short time ... I represented that this fortress is far from being provided with men as {necessity} requires. But all in vain.
