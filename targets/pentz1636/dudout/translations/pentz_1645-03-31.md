# Pentz to the Chancellor, 31 Mar 1645 - English (cipher passages in bold in the reading)
... In your letter of 8 March you desire to learn the state of this fortress [1009, 306 ...]. As reported in my last, great damage has been done to this fortress, especially on the Reithofel, and to the breastworks as well as the main rampart [470], {also} at both ‹…› houses ... of the Reithofel, which in case of need was too extensive to man; I had to say {that} I am now at work drawing that post in more tightly and so securing it, so that the fortress will hopefully be in no danger from it. The Chancellor will kindly see that for the repair of the other [306] {ruined} works, which now must be done without fail, orders and money be sent as soon as possible; the commissary can {pay it out} and render account. Glückstadt, 31 March 1645.
