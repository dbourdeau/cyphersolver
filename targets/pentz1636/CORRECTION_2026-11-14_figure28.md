# Correction, 14 Nov 2026: "28 = o" withdrawn (Glückstadt letter)

**Our error.** `NOTES.md` (lines 42 and 84 in PR #522) suggested that figure **28** was a homophone of **o** outside the Vienna table, in andwzrt, bekzme and anherkzmen. **Withdrawn.**

## Finding
A Fable re-read (b193, 13 Nov 2026, 10:46 UTC) compared the glyphs on the image:
- The writer's **1** has a hooked form, like a 2 without its base stroke.
- The three "28"s where o is wanted are **18 by form**. They use the same glyph as the 1 in the adjacent 16 (= m).
- **18 = o is the table value.**
- The one 28 with a based, z-form 2 (line 17) gives **z** ("zur"), as the table says.

So no extra homophone is needed. The reading was a misreading shared by our first reader and Perplexity's reader. Perplexity's annex independently gives the hooked-1 reading for four tokens: image 2, line 6 groups 11 and 15, line 9 group 4; image 3, line 5 group 13.

## Limits
- This finding is **not blind**. The b193 reader knew the rough table shape and the 28 = o suspicion before reading (disclosed in its log at 10:31 UTC).
- A fresh, blind, shuffled digit-crop test is still open.

## What changes
- Nothing in any filed decode or count. 28 = o was marked "suspected; not applied" in PR #522.
- Read the two NOTES lines above as withdrawn.
