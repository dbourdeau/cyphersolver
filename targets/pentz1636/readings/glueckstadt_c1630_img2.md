## Notes

- Figure 28 is z in table 1 but stands where o is wanted in `andwzrt`, `bekzme`, `anherkzmen`: probably a second value for o (a homophone not in the Vienna table). Suspected only. Not applied to any count or decode.
- `hamburg ist nur drei tausent anher kommen` is ambiguous: perhaps "only three thousand [men] have come here (from Hamburg?)". The sense depends on the codes before it.
- The date c.1629-30 rests on the content (Hamburg, an admiral, a fortress to be manned) and on Pentz's appointment as governor of Glückstadt about January 1630 (Bricka and Fridericia II, p. 242 n. 1). Proposed, not proved.
