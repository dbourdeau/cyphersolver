## English (same holes)

I am glad that the King there has such good [feelings] towards us, and we shall see the effects at the [congress]

[minister] … the fury of this nation … imaginary … Count Scotti … an appointment so out of proportion to his birth
… what his ability may be … valid assistance from the King there and the [139 ?handling] of … with [this] daughter of the Regent … Stair … to insinuate into the King's mind …
