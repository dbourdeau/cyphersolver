# Selvatico -> Riva, Paris, 27 Oct 1719 (Autografoteca Campori, Selvatico Estense, cc. 12-13): reading of the cipher passages

Held-out letter. The Estense record says "decifrata in interlinea", but on the scans no running interlinear
decipherment was found: only a few small superscript letters by the writer (ra, e, Cno, Duo, ta, i, cio). It was
read with the key frozen from the 2 Oct letter, unchanged. Marks as in reading_1719_1002.md.

This letter is read in part and the reading is fragmentary: some phrases carry sense (the succession of
Tuscany, Lorraine, Monferrato, "in mancanza delle linee viventi", Mademoiselle di Valois, "seminar confusione"),
but many lines do not read, and unmarked fragments ("d", "far", "li le e se-") are counted as words by
check_marks.py. The continuous text below is a string of fragments joined by ellipses, not a reading of the
letter. "Queen mother" and "Duchess of Berry" are conjectures from "la [17]i madre" and "be[ivi]", nothing more.
