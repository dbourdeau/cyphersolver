## Reading (whole cipher passage, with the clear text around it)
y adjoustant seulement, que Mons. le marquis de Vandy m'asseure que c[?]e[?] Madamoiselle de Montpensier, l'[313] desespere du [285] de Si[v]oye a cause du refus que Sa Majeste [le Roy T.C.] fait d'y consentir. Je laisse a Vostre Excellence a juger des consequences, elle le peut beaucoup mieux que moy.

## English
...adding only that the Marquis de Vandy assures me that [?] Mademoiselle de Montpensier, [one?] despairs of the [marriage?] of Savoy because of the refusal that His Majesty [the Most Christian King] makes to consent to it. I leave Your Excellency to judge the consequences; you can do so much better than I. (Square brackets: unknown code 313 and 285, two doubtful signs, and 354 written where Savoye needs 352; '[marriage?]' and '[one?]' are guesses, not decoded.)
