English [derived]: The King's Grand Treasurer [Hannibal Sehested] has satisfied me on the point of the [346] and the guarantee. He tells me the Most Christian King wishes to see me and that I should therefore go to [135], for which I have no order from the one who alone disposes of my person. I think this [journey] would do no harm to Your Majesty's interests at that court; I wish I could guess the [good] pleasure of His Majesty [my master]. Those here threaten [us] too. It seems to me that the French king, the States General and Your Majesty could help towards a [peace] between [108] and the Muscovite, to humble Sweden a little, at no cost to those allies; or if the French king would only give the means to raise six thousand [men] for Your Majesty, Sweden would spin a softer thread. I do not know whether Mr [Juel]'s reports to you match what he sends me; I see from his letters that Coventry and the Swedes must be working together to feed him the reports best fitted to make [us] take false measures.
