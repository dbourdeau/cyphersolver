# R99 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher, summer 1568)

Same holes as R99_reading.md. Modern spelling; codes as names.

And His Holiness has resolved to make trial of this order of St Lazarus, and had instructed the Grand Master to
provide himself with two galleys so that he could keep to these seas and run them for the safety of navigators;
and he already hoped to have found them from Signor Gian Andrea Doria, who had been asked for them by His Holiness;
but on hearing this, Don John made [?a little] noise about it with His Holiness, opposing in this the plan of the said Grand Master [?and] chiefly
His Holiness's will, and the matter has not passed without some ill feeling. Your Lordship should know that His
Holiness answered him as Don John deserved and as befitted His Holiness's person, who is resolved, however, that
others be found if those of Doria cannot be had, because if these knights of St Lazarus do not then do what they must,
they will have no greater enemy than His Holiness; but first he wants to try them. Of which it is well that Your
Lordship be informed, to be able to answer in case you hear it spoken of.

The Duke of Alburquerque has lately done in Milan an act of his own authority in prejudice of the archbishop's jurisdiction, having
told his vicar [with] threatening words on the eve of Corpus Domini that he must not bring the armed household to the
procession; this having been done at the instigation of the Senate, His Holiness intends to proceed in this cause
against them with the spiritual arms that the Lord God has given him. For Your Lordship's information.
