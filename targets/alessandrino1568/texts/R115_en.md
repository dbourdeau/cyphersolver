# R115 — English (Cardinal Alessandrino to the nuncio, cipher, [January 1569; probably January 1572, see R115_reading.md])

Same holes as R115_reading.md. Modern spelling; codes as names.

I wrote to the Most Reverend Monsignor of Alessandria [Cardinal Alessandrino] two months ago, on the last day of
November, that he should ask the King in His Holiness's name for the nomination to the church of Monreale in the person
of Don [?Francisco] de Reinoso, private steward of His Holiness, and also the consent to be able to transfer the
pensions that he had laid on the archdeaconry of Toledo onto the same church, informing me [?also] of the true value of
it; and as I have had no answer to this so far, His Holiness wished me to give Your Lordship the same commission, so
that you do not fail to speak of it with the King as soon as you can have a good occasion, pressing him for the speedy
dispatch, so that the matter does not drag on for ever, and then to let me know by your next letters of his decision.
As to the business of [?…], since the King is dealing with it there, His Holiness will [?…] be satisfied, even if it is
pursued by those means that seem best to the King.
