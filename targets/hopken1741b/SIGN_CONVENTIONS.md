# Sign conventions (catalogue 2245-2248, Höpken and Carlson, Constantinople, 1735-1741)

Set at the start of transcription (they head each pass file) and applied to all transcriptions in this folder.
Shapes only; no values.

- The cipher is written in Arabic digits, in four-digit groups separated by spaces (sometimes run close together; a
  group is always four digits). Transcribed as written, one row of the page per line, labelled L01.., M01.., H01..
  (L = image R0003636_00049, M = image R0003636_00050 left page, H = image R0003635_00147).
- A short stroke written above a group (an acute-like tick or a raised comma) is recorded as `'` after the group
  (e.g. `1006'`). Its meaning was not assumed while transcribing.
- Points, commas and semicolons written between groups are recorded as `.`, `,`, `;` where they stand. A short dash
  at the end of a row (=) is recorded as `.` with this note: on these pages it is a line-end hyphen.
- Plain (unenciphered) words inside or between cipher rows are recorded as `[clear: ...]`.
- Underlining of the cipher rows (Lit. A page) and the strike-through of groups are not recorded.
- `?` after a group: one digit could not be settled on the image (the group is given in its likelier reading; the
  alternative is named in the file header).
- Digits that look alike in this hand and were checked on 2x crops: 3/8 (closed upper loop = 8), 0/8 in a blotted
  group, 5/6, 2/7 (open lower stroke), 7/1.
- Interlinear French (Lit. A only) is not transcribed into the cipher rows; it was read separately onto
  gloss_lita_1741.tsv, after the digits.
