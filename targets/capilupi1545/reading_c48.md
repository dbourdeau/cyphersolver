# c. 48r, Camillo Capilupi to Ippolito Capilupi, 23 Feb 1546: not read

Clear text as seen on enlarged crops, cipher left as `[digits]`. Readings of the clear hand are a first pass.

Car.mo fratello potrete avisar l'Ill.mo S.r Ferrando che hor hora dopo chiuse le lettere che sua Ecc.tia scrive drizzate a voi le quali ho trovate gia nel mazzo per altra posta donde scrivo ho parlato a [digits] et poi disse no so che stra se ma le sue parole intrisi [digits] che mi occorre d'altro a voi mi racc.do.

English: Dearest brother, you can tell Don Ferrante that just now, after closing the letters His Excellency writes addressed to you, which I found already in the bundle for another post from where I write, I have spoken to [digits], and then he said I know not what [...], but his words [...] [digits] whatever else occurs to me, I commend myself to you.
