# Catalogue 1565: Camillo Capilupi to Ferrante Gonzaga, Worms, 8 May 1545 (cc. 15-16)

Status: read in part. The letter mixes clear Italian and cipher inside sentences. `edit_c15_16.txt` shows which words are which; the counts below are the cipher words only.

Marks: `[...]` signs not read. `[?word]` proposal, not proved, or a word that keeps a doubtful value. `[x]` inside a word: a letter restored or changed by the editor. `word°`: surplus signs dropped. `[di]`: a word supplied, no sign behind it. `{IMA}` code word, value in NOTES.md. `<null>` an ordered string of null signs, left out of the count. Every departure from the signs is in the table below; `scripts/check_marks.py` stops on an unmarked one. The decryption without repairs is `decryption_c15_16.txt`.

Summary. The Cardinal (code Quan, very likely the Cardinal of Augsburg, Otto Truchsess; context only) called Capilupi and dictated news for Ferrante Gonzaga: the Marchese del Vasto's instability; Prince Doria and Genoa; the marriage of Ottavio Farnese; Farnese coming to court with words alone; a complaint meant to reach "mi" through Lus; what Ferrante should do if there is or is not war this year; the priory; the Cardinal's wish to have Ferrante at court to urge the Emperor against the Lutherans.
