# Translation: extract of the letter of the Khan of Crimea to the King of Sweden (enclosure "Lit. A", May 1742)

English translation of `reading_khan1742.md`; the same notes apply. Words in «...» are clear in the original.

Extract of the translation of the letter of the Khan of Crimea to H. M. the King of Sweden.

And although the Sublime Porte «has at all» times made it an inviolable law to observe «with» exactness the treaties of
friendship, and although «everyone recognises in this» the sincerity of its good intentions and wishes, «and therefore
one can hardly» attribute (1) the delay, of which His M. «speaks» to us «in his letters», in the execution of the
defensive (2) alliance treaty and of good friendship that it concluded with him, «to anything but considerations and
legitimate causes», we had nevertheless resolved to confer about it «and» to learn the true motives «at the time of» our
arrival at the Porte.

«So, having come there and having had the honour of presenting» our respects to the Majesty of the August Emperor, «and
that of being» received with every kind of goodwill, «having raised» this matter «with» his Supreme Minister, we learnt
that this delay came only from the painful necessity in which the Porte found itself of fortifying «and placing» in
safety its frontiers (3) and borders on the side of Persia.

«And as consequently the things» most pressing must come first, «and as moreover» the aid promised to friends (4) is
conditional and subject to the circumstances of the times «and» of affairs, one (5) cannot «therefore» for these reasons
grant aid to Sweden without «much» difficulty.

However, as the Ottoman Empire has at all times set particular value on a friendship as sincere «and as» constant as
«that» of the court of Sweden towards it, «and as» in this consideration it could hardly refuse Y. M. anything, it will
not fail «also» to grant him «on» this occasion all the aid that may depend on it «in» the present circumstances, and
Your Majesty will certainly soon (6) receive the real effects of its friendship and attachment.

This «having been» notified to us, we did not (7) wish to fail «etc. etc. etc.»

At the beginning of the month of May «1742».
