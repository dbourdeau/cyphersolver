Jan-Mar 1866. AHN, DIVERSOS-COLECCIONES, 193, N.99 (PARES 5928106).

Marks: clear words of the copybook are given only as context in ⟨ ⟩ (not counted). In the cipher: {x} = probable (a
one-number slip of the cipherer or copyist, corrected by sense; the justification follows each passage); [x] = restored;
‹…› = unresolved. There are no restored and no unresolved words in this unit.

corrected by sense. The key itself is **partial** by our own protocol (held-out margin 0.277, under our 0.30 rule; see
validation.txt), so we classify the unit "read in part" although every cipher word is read by sense.

## Report 21 (view 26, Jalet, about 30 Jan 1866): S03-S10

ahora ⟨que⟩ son las ocho de la noche se me presenta el cuñado de Saguete con el pliego ⟨de V.E.⟩ fecha ⟨de ayer⟩ hemos
⟨quedando⟩ entendi{d}os ⟨y espero⟩ su aviso ⟨para⟩ acudir al sitio ⟨que⟩ me diga.

- entendi{d}os: the cipher gives 81 (= c) "entendicos" where 61 (= d) is required.

## Report 22 (view 27, Jalet, 31 Jan 1866): S12-S17

⟨Los⟩ más afirman ⟨que⟩ Sagueta n{o} está ⟨en el⟩ país ⟨creyendo⟩ muchos de ellos ⟨marchó⟩ a Madrid o Barcelona ⟨con
objeto de⟩ obtener el indulto.

- n{o}: the cipher gives 15 (= ñ, as in cuñado, mañana) "nñ" where an o (36/64) is required; "no está en el país" is
  imposed by the sense ("most claim that Sagueta is not in the country, many believing he went to Madrid or Barcelona").

## Telegram (views 52-53; Reus, 7 Mar 1866, forwarded to colonel Taulet)

⟨El Excmo. Sor. General Comandante General de esta división, en telegrama que acabo de recibir ahora que son las 10 y
cuarto de la noche, me dice = "Traslade V.S.⟩ al coronel Taulet ⟨por parejas de Guardia Civil, lo siguiente =⟩ mañana
nueve mar{c}he ⟨V.S.⟩ a Cambrils con su primer batallón, dé cuenta de haberse alojado." ⟨Dios gue. a V.S. m. a. Reus 7
Marzo de 1866.⟩

- mar{c}he (S32): the cipher gives 21 (= a) "marahe" where a c (81/82) is required.
- "mañana nueve": either "tomorrow at nine" or "tomorrow, the ninth"; left as written.
- S33 ("a Cambrils con su primer batallón dé cuenta de haberse alojado", 11 words) is the held-out run: it was decoded
  only once, by the frozen key, in the single measurement. All 11 words are certain.
- A clear telegram of the same day (view 51, Tarragona, general Manso de Zúñiga) orders Taulet's battalion to Vilaseca,
  "donde quedará alojado": a neighbouring order with another destination. It was not used for the key or the measurement.
