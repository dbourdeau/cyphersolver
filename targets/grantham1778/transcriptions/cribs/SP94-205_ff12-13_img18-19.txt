# despatch: N.o 2. ; Madrid, 8.th Jan.ry 1778. ; ff. 12r-13r ; SP94-205_ff12-13_img18-19.pdf
# hand/notes: The heading reads "N.o 2." and "8.th Jan.ry", not No. 3. Complete despatch: begins at the top of f.12r, ends with signature on f.13r.
# hand/notes: Clear text in a clerk's copperplate hand; numbers in the same clerk's hand; decipherment between the lines in a larger, darker second hand (London). Closing formula and signature "Grantham" in a third (Grantham's own) hand.
# hand/notes: Left half of page 1 (f.11v) does not belong to this despatch: it carries only a sideways endorsement of an earlier enclosure, "In Gov.r Burt's[?] / 24:th October 1777."
# hand/notes: The endorsement of this despatch is on f.13v, which is not in this PDF but shows as the left half of page 1 of SP94-205_ff14-15_img20-21.pdf: "Madrid 8 Jan.ry 1778 / Lord Grantham / (N.o 2.) / R 28.th" - it confirms the number 2 and the date.
# hand/notes: f.12v has a pencil bracket in the left margin beside "Licence was granted ... of it." On f.13r the decipherer wrote no words above the last two lines of numbers and drew ruled dashes there instead (the numbers are presumably nulls filling out the paragraph).
# hand/notes: In this hand 0 is a plain oval (sometimes with a bar inside), 8 is an oval with a tick or tail rising at the top right; the two are the main source of doubt.

C: N.o 2.                                   Madrid, 8.th Jan.ry 1778.
C: My Lord,
C: In a Conversation which I have
C: had with M. Florida blanca since I had the
C: Honour of writing last to your Lordship, he shewed
C: me a Letter which he had just received from Don
C: Felix O'Neile, now Commandant at Coruña. It
C: appeared from this Letter that M. O'Neile had
C: received fresh Orders to prevent the Sale of Prizes, or
C: at least to have the Value of their Cargoes, if sold,
C: deposited; That he had likewise received the Instructions
C: mentioned in mine, N.o 87. of the last Year, to your
C: Lordship, to forbid Cunningham's Entrance into
C: any Port of Spain, and to do Justice to such Sailors
C: as might claim to be released. – With regard to
C: what had passed M. O'Neile offered the most trivial
C: Excuses, pretending an Ignorance of the Facts, which
C: is as improbable as it was unsatisfactory to me, and
C: I may add, to the Minister to whom they were
C: addressed. All the Documents relative to Prizes
C: at the Ferrol and at Coruña, as well as at other
C: Ports on the northern Coasts of Spain are ordered to
C: be sent here, and M. Florida blanca has promised
C: to give the fullest Attention to them.
C: I took occasion to mention to
C: him the Notification, which the House of Guadoqui
C: had given to their Correspondent in America; He
C: repeated to me the Terms of the Licence which had
C: been granted to that House. I know a Person
C: [foot, left] The Lord Viscount Weymouth, / &c.a &c.a &c.a
C: [catchword] who

C: artfull Use made of it by the Guadoquis, absolutely
C: disavowed the Extent to which, they pretend, the
C: Licence was granted, and the Formality with
C: which they have drawn up their Communication
C: of it.

C: to mention, as from myself, the Subject of an

C: Appointment not being yet made was owing to
C: Reasons which he would likewise explain to me
