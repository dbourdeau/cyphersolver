C: Madrid, 2d. March 1778.
C: My Lord,
C: Besides the Advices which I have
C: lately communicated to your Lordship from Cadiz

C: to Cadiz: -- And that a very considerable Quantity
C: of Cannon is expected at Malaga from Barcelona
C: and Carthagena with an Intent, it is said, to strengthen
C: the African Garrison, & the Forts on the Coasts of
C: Spain; and that the Activity in preparing

C: succeed Prince Masserano has taken place, and is made
C: publick, and Count Fernan Nuñez is appointed Ambassador
C: from this Court to that of Portugal.
C: I have not much personal Acquaintance
C: with Count Almodovar, having only seen him during a
C: short Stay which he made, upon a Leave of Absence in
C: the Year 1774. It was at the Time that the Disputes between
C: Spain and Portugal began to grow serious, and I found
C: him well informed on that Subject, & very temperate in
C: his Expressions. I believe, and hope that according
C: to      (catchword)

C: to M. Florida blanca's late Assurances, he will be immediately
C: sent for, and pressed to go to England without Loss of
C: Time.
C: The Embassies to Vienna and Turin are not yet
C: filled up. It is however probable that Count Aguilar,
C: now at Turin, will go to Vienna. The Duke of Villa=
C: =hermosa does not go to Turin, but there are several
C: Competitors for that Embassy.
C: By Advices from Buenos Ayres
C: Accounts are received of the Spaniards having taken
C: a Portugueze Fort in the inland part of the Country,
C: before the News of the Suspension of Hostilities could
C: reach that part of their Army.
C: I have the Honour to be with
C: the greatest Truth & Regard
C: My Lord.
C: Your Lordship's
C: most obedient
C: & most humble Servant
C: Grantham.
