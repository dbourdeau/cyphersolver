## page 1 (image 90) right half, folio 73r
C: No. 8.
C: 9.
C: Madrid 16th. Febry. 1778.
C: My Lord,
C: The Advices, which I have received
C: by this Days Post from Cadiz, bring Accounts of
C: great Activity in the Preparations making
C: there. They state, that the Squadron fitting out
C: consists of eighteen capital Ships; That the
C: Phenix of eighty Guns, and the St. Nicholas of
C: ninety with two Bomb ketches are expected from
C: Carthagena; (the former sailed from thence with
C: a Frigate on the 4th.) That three seventy Gun Ships
C: are expected from Ferrol; That the Frigate Sta.
C: Rosa, and a Snow, lately purchased, are fitting out
C: as Fire Ships; That three millions of Rations are
C: preparing for Sea Service; and the first and
C: second Pilots were to attend at the War Office by
C: an Order issued on the 8th. And it is added, that
C: Orders have reached Andalucia for all the Regiments
C: in that Province to be ready to march at the
C: shortest Notice.
C: Mr. Hardy acquaints me, that
C: he has communicated this Intelligence to your
C: The Lord Viscount Weymouth,
C: &ca. &ca. &ca.
C: Lordship      (catchword)
