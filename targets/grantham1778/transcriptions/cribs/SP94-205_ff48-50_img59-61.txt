# despatch: N.o 6. (with "A." beneath the number) ; Madrid, 29.th Jan.ry 1778. ; ff. 48r-49v (f.50r blank) ; SP94-205_ff48-50_img59-61.pdf
# hand/notes: Headed "N.o 6." with a separate "A." below it in the left margin; date "Madrid, 29.th Jan.ry 1778." Complete despatch: begins at the top of f.48r, ends with signature and postscript on f.49v. The right half of page 3 (f.50r) is blank; any endorsement would be on f.50v, not in this PDF.
# hand/notes: The despatch opens directly in numbers. Numbers and the clear paragraphs in a clerk's hand; decipherment between the lines in a larger second hand; closing formula, signature and a five-line postscript (lower left of f.49v) in Grantham's own hand.
# hand/notes: Left half of page 1 (f.47v) does not belong to this despatch: sideways endorsement of another paper, "Cadiz 23 Jan.ry 1778 / Consul Hardy / (N.o 2.) / R 16 Feb.ry".
# hand/notes: The lines of numbers on f.48r and f.48v run right up to (and on f.48v into) the gutter: the last number of f.48v lines 7, 8 and 9 is partly hidden in the binding. The number lines on f.48r slope and the last numbers are squeezed small.
# hand/notes: The decipherment runs ahead of the numbers: at the foot of f.48v and of f.49r there is a line of English with no numbers under it, and on f.49r two lines of English stand together above the last line of numbers.
# hand/notes: 0 is a plain oval (sometimes with a bar inside, sometimes joined to the next figure by a short stroke); 8 is an oval with a tick, tail or crossing loop rising at the top right. This pair accounts for nearly all the doubtful figures. The point after a number is often a short dash.
