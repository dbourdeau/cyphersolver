# hand/notes: Headed "N.o 3." with "Separate." underlined beneath it; date 8.th Jan.ry 1778. Complete despatch: begins at the top of f.14r, ends with signature on f.15r.
# hand/notes: Clear text and numbers in a clerk's copperplate hand; decipherment between the lines in a larger, darker second hand; closing formula and signature in Grantham's own hand.
# hand/notes: Left half of page 1 (f.13v) is the endorsement of the PREVIOUS despatch, written sideways: "Madrid 8 Jan.ry 1778 / Lord Grantham / (N.o 2.) / R 28.th" - not part of this despatch.
# hand/notes: The final 5 of 535 and 1375 has a detached top stroke that sits over the point and looks like a colon; recorded here as a point. In the decipherment "provocations" has a letter written over another.
# hand/notes: 0 is a plain oval (sometimes with a bar inside), 8 an oval with a tick or tail rising at the top right.

## page 1 (image 20) right half, folio 14r
C: N.o 3.                                   Madrid 8.th Jan.ry 1778.
C: Separate.   [underlined]
C: My Lord,
C: In my late Conference with
C: M. Floridablanca, I observed to him that I was
C: informed of a greater Degree of Armament at
C: Cadiz than was naturally to be accounted for.
C: This Observation I made as from myself. He did
C: not disown it, but alledged, that the Increase
C: of our naval Establishment was alarming, and
C: required such Precaution. I pointed out to him,
C: that the Necessity of all our Preparations was
C: obvious, and repeated His Majesty's Declaration,
C: That He would not be the first to disturb the
C: publick Tranquility; That the fullest Confidence
C: was really due to these Sentiments, and that
C: it was absolutely necessary to keep pace with
C: them by such a Conduct as should give no Umbrage,
C: nor just Cause of Offence.
C: I had no reason to be dissatisfied
C: with the Answers which he gave me on this Subject,
C: nor with the Assurances, which he repeated, of the
C: King of Spain's and his own Desire for the
G: (none)
F: preservation of Peace. 3042. 1845. 1504. 728. 11.
