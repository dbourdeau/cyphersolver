# Guastalla, 24 September 1717: reading

Antonio Ferdinando Gonzaga, Duke of Guastalla, to a correspondent who is not named. Biblioteca Estense Universitaria, Autografoteca Campori, Lettera A, "Antonio Ferdinando, duca di Guastalla", cc. 15-16.

Italian: in the clear at the start and the end, and in cipher between them.

**Marks.**

- A code group is shown with its number, such as [2]. Where context gives a sense, the sense follows after "=".
- `[?]` marks a doubtful word or letter.
- Words in brackets are restored from context. The key did not give them.
- The reading keeps the writer's spelling where the key gives it.
- The writer's spaces do not always fall between words. Word division and punctuation are mine.

## Clear closing

- Intendo con molto dispiacere il mal stato di salute del Sig. Contino Lei figlio,
- comprendendo l'agitazione d'animo che Lei ne prova,
- e prego il Signore ridonare allo stesso la primiera sanità, ed a Lei ogni vero bene.
- Guastalla 24 7bre 1717.
- Ant. Ferd. Gonzaga.

## Notes

- "Trauston" and "Starhemberg" read as names. They match ministers at the imperial court in 1717: Prince Trautson and a Count Starhemberg. I have not checked this in print.
- The code groups for names and titles stay conjectures.
- Doubtful passages: page 1 line 15 ("r[?]aie"), page 3 line 4 ("riflesic[14]e i [1] risc[14]na e re [12]"), and page 4 line 9 ("incororga").
