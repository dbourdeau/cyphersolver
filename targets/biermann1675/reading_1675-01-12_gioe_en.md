# Biermann to Marcus Gioë (England), Copenhagen 12 January 1675: cipher passage, English translation

Translated from the French reading in `reading_1675-01-12_gioe.md` (cipher of p. 12, Arkivalieronline image 015;
key: TKUA "Chiffer" 1670-1709, image 55). Marks as in the reading: `[123]`, `[488]` a code with no value;
`[?word]` a word that holds a group with no value on the sheet or a letter that is not written. Words in [ ]
without a question mark or a number are in clear in the copybook. Translation by OpenAI GPT-6 (Codex) for Tomáš
Pavuk, revised to these marks by Claude Fable 5.2.

[It is nevertheless to be hoped that,] with the States General doing what they can to give satisfaction to [123],
he will not wish to give rise [?through] a resolution of this kind to a new [488], which [?he] will have
difficulty getting approved by his Parliament, which [apparently will always be more inclined to contribute to]
the [?weakening] of France than to its enlargement.

Notes

- "[123]": a code that the key sheet does not carry. `new_values.tsv` has it from the Høeg letter of 9 January
  1675 as England or the King of England; "his Parliament" here fits the King. The value is not adopted and the
  mark is kept.
- "[488]": a code that the key sheet does not carry and that is not met elsewhere in the folder; in the French a
  feminine noun.
- "[?he]": the French is `qui[sq] aura`; the sign after `qui` is the open square, for which `new_values.tsv`
  proposes l (*qu'il*).
- "[?through]": the cipher gives `pa une`; the r of *par* is not written.
- "[?weakening]": the cipher gives `fo-i-b-li-31-se-me-n-t`; 31 is not on the sheet, and `new_values.tsv`
  proposes s from the Høeg letter of the same day.
- "not wish" and "difficulty": the cipher has `pos` for *pas* (579 where the sense wants 576) and `477-i-ne` for
  *peine* (477 where the sense wants 577), two one-digit slips of the copyist, kept as written in the French.
- The States General are code 162 and France code 212 on the sheet.
