# Batch 5: interlined code pages, King despatches (NARA M30 roll 6)

Output: `batch5_spans.tsv` (columns: frame, page, line, span_no, groups, interlinear, placement, note).

Conventions
- One row per span, in reading order. `line` counts every written line of the page from the first line that carries code (frame 0063: page line numbers, with clear-only lines omitted).
- Words King wrote in clear on the line are rows whose `groups` field is in braces; they have no placement. They are included only to fix position and were read quickly: do not rely on their wording.
- A row with several groups and one word means the clerk's word stands over those groups. Placement is `clear` when the word visibly spans exactly those groups, `ambiguous` when its extent is uncertain (usually: the word covers the first groups and only reaches toward the last). Groups after a word with nothing above them are separate rows with an empty interlinear and a note 'bare; follows ...'. Whether such bare groups belong to the preceding word was NOT decided.
- Drawn rules are rare in these frames: the only one is 0028 R1. Notes say `rule` there; elsewhere 'no rule'.
- `?` after a group = at least one digit doubtful, alternative in the note; `_` = digit hidden in the binding; `^` = caret-like mark after the group.
- No key or word list was consulted or built. Repeated sequences on the same page were sometimes cited in notes ('cf. line N') as a reading aid for placement only; where the pen wrote something different from the parallel passage it was kept as written (0153 L18 672; 0158 L24/R1 1547; 0177 R6 1232).
- Much interlinear writing is smudged or lightly struck (it looks like a second pass over the clerk's words); the notes say 'smudged or struck' without deciding which.
