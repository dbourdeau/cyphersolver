I have understood everything in yours. I shall answer [it] orally [when I come?]. Since these matters may be of very great weight and bring great consequences: [here is my] answer to Your Honour’s request concerning passports here. Jealousy is beginning between the [106] and the [114] [105] envoys; both have left here. I know [113]’s whole intention, what he has in mind. Well, I have also kept a close watch on him. He has gone to the [114]. I rest neither night nor day, whether I am ill or not, and shrink from no danger. I have received Your Honour’s two letters of 23 February and 13 March, about a necessary matter. I am staying on here a while longer, which matters.
