# lilienthal1665 - English rendering of the measured portion (image 45, left page, lines 1-10)

From our decode of the cipher (the gloss differs in places, see `decryption.txt`). The clear German written between the runs
and the line before L01 are not transcribed, so the passage starts and ends in mid-sentence.

> ... but [he] holds firmly to his instruction and [secret] propositions. Also some of the great boyars, such as Dolgoruka,
> Ilya Danilovich, Almaz, [...] yesterday after the conference, discussing it together, let it be known with ...

Notes: "Dolgoruka" is a Prince Dolgorukov; "Ilia Danielewitz" is probably Ilya Danilovich Miloslavsky; "Almasi" is probably
Almaz Ivanov, head of the Ambassadorial Office (Posolsky prikaz). These identifications are ours and were not checked against
the clear text of the letter.
