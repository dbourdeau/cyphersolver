# Translation (English): Coahuila code telegrams, 25 Jun - 27 Jul 1906

Clear parts as rendered by the Senate's translator in 1907; the decoded words put back into English. Unread words are shown
as ‹…›; words restored from a faulty copy are in [brackets]. Sense of the Spanish only; see `reading.md` for the counts.

**T35 - Múzquiz, 25 June 1906, L. A. Guajardo (Jefe Político) to Governor Miguel Cárdenas, Saltillo.** Bentley and ‹…› are
all accused criminally by an Indian of having forced him, at night and beating him, three of them, to sign an unknown
document. The accusation is supported by the American commissioner. Bought suit: Bentley ‹…› ‹…›. Ludlow [is] for removing
the whole Indian camp, to withdraw it from all investigation. If the American commissioners ask for the Indians to stay in
order to complete the investigation, I ask you to advise me whether I shall assist them.

**T36 - Saltillo, 25 June 1906, Governor Cárdenas to Guajardo.** It is advisable that you act with all prudence, to avoid
well-founded complaints from either side, proceeding with full justification and impartiality, bearing in mind that the
recommendations in favour of the commissioners of the American government are in legal terms.

**T53 - Múzquiz, 30 June 1906, Guajardo to Cárdenas.** The Bentley affair has entered a quiet period thanks to the prudent
intervention of the Juez de Letras, who arrested Bentley; he was dangerous for some distinguished persons, who manoeuvred,
as you know. The American commissioner told the judge he needs to continue ...

**T54 - Múzquiz, 30 June 1906, Judge F. Valdés Llano to Cárdenas.** Today I arrived here and intervened in the Kickapoo
matter. There does not exist ‹14 groups in another code, not read›. Will write tomorrow.

**T67 (2 July) and T82 (11 July, a second copy) - Múzquiz, Guajardo to Cárdenas.** A group of known thieves stole a
considerable lot of mules from here, taking them towards the Río Bravo six days ago. The chief thieves are still here,
watching the ‹…›. They hide the mule herd in the river flat of the thieves; one of the thieves has a ranch there and is the
protector of the rest. ... immediate pursuit as far as the river, sending one competent man as chief of the party, because
it is probable that they will have to be fought if they feel pursued. Two Kickapoo thieves go with Mexican thieves.

**T99 - Múzquiz (to Monterrey), 17 July 1906, R. C. Conine to Guajardo (English).** Your council and [advice] needed before
proceeding farther. When will you come?

**T104 - Múzquiz, 19 July 1906, Guajardo to Cárdenas.** ... desires by all means to take. I believe that the ones abuse as
much as the others. The commissioner [is] demanding. Bentley's wife [is] insolent, [she] can cause some difficulty.

**T124 - Sabinas, 27 July 1906, Guajardo to Ignacio E. Rivas.** You can issue permits for the Indians to leave, taking note
of [who] asks [for it], and issue passes stating the names of the Indians.
