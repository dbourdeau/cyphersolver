# Lope de Soria to Charles V, Genoa, 13 August 1523 (R9497-R9498, key B): English translation

Andrea Doria and the Prince of Salerno are very discontented with the King, because he had taken from them the enterprise of this city and had given it to the Bastard of Savoy; and one called Jeronimo Doria, who is one of the principal men of this city, has [?spoken] to me on behalf of the said Andrea Doria; and they say that if Your Majesty is willing to accept the said Andrea Doria into your service with his four galleys, he will serve you with very good will; and he has told me the same on behalf of the Prince of Salerno. I have pressed Antoniotto Adorno to have this republic pay what they owe Your Majesty under the capitulation made in the [?time] of Pope Leo, and he has told me that he has charged Martin Centurion to speak to Your Majesty about it, and that until his [?answer] comes nothing can be discussed.
