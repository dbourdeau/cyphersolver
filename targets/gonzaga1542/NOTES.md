# gonzaga1542: Charles V to Ferrante Gonzaga, viceroy of Sicily, Monzón, 11 August 1542

Status: read in part (7 Nov 2026). Contributed by Prabal Gupta (issue #318; repository `prabal-rje/branicki-1757`, folder `charles-v-1542`, CC BY 4.0): a cipher of consonant signs with a small digit above each that adds the vowel, found from the sign statistics of the letter alone and read as Spanish. Checked here against its own files only (`VERIFICATION.md`); the Estense scans are not reachable from the project, so no transcription was compared with a scan.

## The document

Biblioteca Estense Universitaria, Modena, Autografoteca Campori, Lettera K, Karl V, sfasc. IV, cc. 109-110 (Lodovico Media Library record c710a32c-42a7-4159-8abc-472aed58f7c9; scans licensed CC BY-NC-SA, not copied here). Catalogue 1560 (the other leaf of that entry, the undated cipher letter at cc. 131-132, is a different matter and stays in the catalogue). A letter of the Emperor, signed "Yo el Rey" and countersigned "Idiáquez", to Ferrante Gonzaga as viceroy of Sicily (the catalogue's "governor of Milan" is his office from 1546). Part of it is in clear Spanish: the heading, the opening, the closing and several links between the cipher passages. The cipher fills most of the body.

`reading.md` and `translation.md` are the contributor's, with the cipher in bold; `transcription.txt` the cipher as written with the clear text between double angle brackets (the sign shapes are given by labels, a label names a shape and not a value), `decryption.txt` the line-by-line decryption, `key.tsv` the table, `contributor_notes.md` the contributor's own notes (how it was solved, what stays open).

## What it says

Sicily cannot pay for its infantry, its galleys and the fort of La Goleta without help; the Emperor's other kingdoms are as short, so no help can come; La Goleta must be provided for because of the Turkish fleet; the spies report that the main French effort is coming to the frontier of Perpignan and that the French threaten Navarre; the count of Novellara's pay and the Viscount Cicala's galleys must be paid, because someone is soliciting Cicala for another contract.

## The cipher

A base sign is a consonant and a small digit above it adds the vowel (1 to 5 and 6 to 0 both run a, e, i, o, u); a sign with no digit is a single letter; a further sign marks a doubled or palatal consonant. See `key.tsv`.

## Remaining gaps

- About one sign in fifteen, mostly rare forms, fixed by sense and not by the table (listed in `decryption.txt` only), and three occurrences of "caber" read as "haver" - blocker: illegible; rare shapes seen once or twice
- The name "Novellara" (a guess for the string "niuilara") - blocker: open-codes; a Gonzaga branch, not checked in a list of the family
