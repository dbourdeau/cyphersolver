# Charles V to Ferrante Gonzaga, 11 August 1542: every place where the reading leaves the key

Checked on 7 November 2026 on the Estense scans at full size (3,000 by 4,200 pixels), enlarged two to four times.

Each row gives the place, what the signs give under `key.tsv`, what was tried, and the result. The letter model is an order-5 letter model built from *Don Quijote*. It scores the letters around the place, and it knows nothing of grammar. So it is a weak test, and the rows say when it disagrees with the sense.

## Counts

The cipher passages hold 246 words. Six are marked: [g]randes, [?allí], t[a]n, e[x]pedientes, [?screbir] and amena[ça]van. So 240 of 246 words read, 97.6%.

The value g with 6 = "ha" is an inference from its uses. Without it, the four "haver" would be marked as well. Then 236 of 246 words read, 95.9%.
