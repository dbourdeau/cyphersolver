LoC magbell.04100117, image 1 (folio 1 recto), lines 13-14 of the text, quoted in the letter of 27 Aug 1900 (SS Ulunda, on the Thames):

"Roboro, Diallage, Siquando, Adalligo,
 [Sigma struck out; Signinus written below the line], Promulgo, Bert,"

Token order as sent: Roboro Diallage Siquando Adalligo Signinus Promulgo Bert
(Bell first wrote Sigma = "Weather very fine", struck it out and put Signinus = "Weather very fine, sea quite smooth".)
The By the People transcription gives "Siquando ... Promulgo Bert" and a "Signinas" signature; the image shows Signinus is an
insertion in the middle of the message, not a signature.
