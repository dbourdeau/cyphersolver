# Reading: second enclosure of 2/13 December 1739 (image 257, right page, first lines)

Headed "Bil. till Höpkens o. Carlsons dep. d. 2/13 Dec. 1739", marked "N 2". Only the four cipher runs at the top were
transcribed; the rest of the page seen is clear French (the Ottoman peace terms for Russia: "Azov doit être démoli, les
frontières des Russes éloignées, les Russes ne peuvent tenir ni flotte ni flotille dans le Palus Meotis ..."). Transcription job.
