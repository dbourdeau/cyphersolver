# Postscript in cipher, image 263 (Swedish despatch, received 20 Feb. 1740; "P.S. 4 Dec.")

No gloss. 55 groups in four runs, the last two around the clear Swedish word "angående" (concerning). Decoded with the
final key.tsv (not the frozen key); **decoded, not read**. Recognisable pieces: "Drogueman" (1639 1209 1548, as in 255 r01), "Grand Vizir" (1104),
"...fensive" after 1020 (as in "défensive"), "commi-ss-" (1741 1324 1615). 15 of 55 groups are not in the key, and many values
are single-attestation. The language of the postscript (French or Swedish in a French code) is not settled.
A small superscript "o" stands over the second digit of 1363 (run p02); it may be a correction or a flag and is recorded
in bare_263.tsv. The clear Swedish word in the postscript makes the French-syllable fragments above weak evidence.
