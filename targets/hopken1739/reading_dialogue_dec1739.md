# Reading: enclosure to Höpken and Carlson's despatch of 2/13 December 1739 (images 255-257, left page of 257)

Riksarkivet, Diplomatica Turcica, SE/RA/2119/2119.1/I/9/21, images R0003582_00255 to _00257. Image 255 is headed
"Doupplett", received 10 Feb. 1740, "Bil. till Höpkens och Carlsons und. dep. d. 2/13 Dec. 1739". It is a conversation
written out as a dialogue between two speakers tagged in the margin "A 1440" and "B 1529". The tags are themselves code
groups, never glossed. From what B says (B calls the peace with Russia "mon ouvrage" and says the peace with the Emperor
was already concluded), B is very probably the French ambassador, the marquis de Villeneuve, mediator of the Belgrade
peace of September 1739; A is the Swedish side. This identification is ours, from the content, and is not in the gloss.

**This is a transcription job**: every cipher run on these pages carries the contemporary French decipherment written over
it. The key below was rebuilt from these same glosses, so the reading confirms the transcription, not a break.

## The opening in context (image 255; clear text as written, cipher runs in italics)

A [1440]. *Le Drogueman de la Porte* aura infailliblement rendu conte à V. Excell. ce matin *du succès de nos negociations*.
B [1529]. Il m'a dit seulement *qu'elles prenoient bon train, et qu'elles avançoient beaucoup*. Mais comme je crus qu'il ne
me convenoit pas d'être trop curieux, je n'ai pas voulû le questionner plus là dessus.
A [1440]. Il peut avoir dit *qu'elles avancent*, mais pourtant nous nous appercevons très bien *par les doutes dont il vient
s'éclaircir, une* [the gloss leaves a blank] à la fois, que l'on *cherche à gagner du tems, et trainer la negotiation en
longueur*, ce qui ne sauroit *être agréable à notre Cour, ni honorable* pour nous mêmes *qui avons apporté* tant de facilité
*en tout, et déclaré que nous étions* [256:] en état de contenter *le Ministère* promptement *sur tous les éclaircissemens*
dont il croiroit avoir besoin. ...

A [1440]. The Dragoman of the Porte will surely have reported to Your Excellency this morning on the success of our
negotiations. B [1529]. He only told me that they were going well and advancing a great deal; but as I thought it did not
become me to be too curious, I did not wish to question him further on it. A [1440]. He may have said that they are
advancing, but we see very well, from the doubts he comes to have cleared up one [thing] at a time, that they are seeking to
gain time and drag the negotiation out, which cannot be agreeable to our Court, nor honourable for ourselves, who have
shown such readiness in everything and declared that we were in a position to satisfy the Ministry promptly on all the
clarifications it might think it needed. ...

The rest of the dialogue (images 256-257) runs on the same lines: A complains that the Porte itself first asked for Swedish
full powers and now delays; B answers with "le flegme des Turcs et la gravité avec laquelle ils agissent", says they wait to
see whether the Russian ratification comes, and that the Swedes should not "déranger ce plan pacifique"; A replies that
Sweden only wishes "de seconder les propres vues de la Porte et de resserrer les noeuds d'amitié entre les deux Puissances
pour leur sureté et leur défense mutuelle à l'avenir contre un voisin incommode, turbulent et redoutable" (Russia). On 257
B: "Vous avez tort d'appeller la paix avec la Russie mon ouvrage ... La paix avec l'Empereur étoit déjà conclue sans qu'il
fût stipulé de la Russie." The page breaks off in mid-sentence; its continuation was not located in this pass.
