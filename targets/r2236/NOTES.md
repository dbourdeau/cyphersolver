# DECODE R2236: "Copie No 1" and "Copie No 2", Dutch number-cipher copies in the Hereditary Prince file (KHA, Prins Willem V inv. 337)

Status: attempted, open: viewed, not transcribed; numbers beyond the word list that reads its sibling R2239, and Dutch clear words; no list for it found.

Split on 14 Oct 2026 from `hereditary1796` under the one-key rule (George Lasry, 14 Oct 2026). Until then R2236 was a
document of the Hereditary Prince's letter of 12 March 1796 (R2239), filed with it in the same Huisarchief file and
listed with it as catalogue item 218. R2239 is read with a French numbered word list rebuilt from R2237; R2236 runs
past that list and is in Dutch, so it is probably another list. R2239 is
[`targets/hereditary1796/`](../hereditary1796/NOTES.md) (a different cipher, with its own page). The observations
below were made in the hereditary1796 session of 21 Sept 2026 and are carried over from its NOTES; the images were
fetched again with the DECODE cookie on 15 Oct 2026 for the site crop (kept out of git, in the session scratchpad).

Catalogue item 218 (narrowed to R2236 when R2239 was read, outcome "attempted, open").

## The record

- Koninklijk Huisarchief, The Hague, A31 Prins Willem V, inv. nr. 337; DECODE R2236 ("Non-decrypted", 4 pp.,
  images `IMG_R2236_I15513-I15516`, photographs of photocopies, the pages turned 90 degrees). DECODE gives the sender as
  Willem Frederik, the Hereditary Prince, at Berlin, to William V; the record itself was not checked for sender or
  date here.
- "Copie No 1" and "Copie No 2": Dutch letters with clear Dutch words between runs of numbers up to about 390
  (copy No 1 opens "215, 2, 6, 309, 1, 1, 69, 72, 6, 250, 331, op losse gronden 22, 93, 203, ..."; clear words such
  as "Echter schynt het als of", "met een van beiden", "tot een bestendig", "De minister ... stelling aan"), plus a
  page of numbers up to 2835 (bleeding through R2237 p. 1).

## What was tried (21 Sept 2026)

- Viewed against the system of R2237/R2239 (a numbered word list of about 250 entries; a group is a whole entry,
  chosen letters of an entry, or an entry cut by k letters at either end). R2236's numbers run past the rebuilt list
  (to about 390, and to 2835 on one page), and its clear words are Dutch where R2237/R2239 are French. Not read; not
  transcribed.
- The other Orange ciphers of 1795 (R1892, R2242) use a 6x6 digit square, a different system.

## Remaining gaps
- Copie No 1 and Copie No 2, and the page of numbers to 2835 - blocker: no-key-material; a different list (or lists) from the French one rebuilt from R2237, no list or decipherment on the record, in the file or in print found; the numbers run past the known list, so nothing of the R2239 key applies
