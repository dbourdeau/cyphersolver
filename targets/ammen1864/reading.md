System: Union route cipher (Stager family): two grids of 6 columns (8 rows for Sherman's relayed dispatch, words 1-54, hence the "54" at the foot of the sheet; 7 rows for Schofield's own message, words 55-107), same route in both: column 6 up, 1 down, 5 up, 2 down, 4 up, 3 down; "Friend" = commencement word; one or two null words per column (now, men, send, news, freedom, quiet, how, G, Odd, I, not, hell, well, newer, are); code words for proper names.

## Clear text (notation: {x} probable, [x] restored from sense and the printed text, ‹…› unresolved)
1. "I have just rec[eive]d the following: [Nashville] (ink), Tenn., Sept. the twenty-eight. To Capt. Dickson, A.A.G.: Recall General Burbridge with his {forces} (was) to this city or the nearest convenient point on the L. & N. rail road. ‹rig› W. T. [Sherman] (muscle), J. D. Webster, Brig. General."
2. "You will forward the above dispatch to General Burbridge if possible and acknowledge receipt and report as soon as General Burbridge has received it. I do not know that he has this cypher. By command of [Maj. Gen. Schofield] (maxim), G. M. Bascom, A. A. [G.]"
