## Contextual & Historical Notes

- **Plural group candidate (`{98e=^?}`):** Occurs `2×` in Item 6 alongside Joyeuse and Nevers; *franceses* remains an unverified contextual hypothesis and is kept open as `{98e=^?}`.
- **Montmorency negotiations & reported offer:** Joan Davies (*Historical Journal*, n. 23) identifies the September 1583 *Propositión y respuesta de Memoransi* signed by **Alessandro Constantino** and **Ferrante Pagano** enclosed with Terranova's 25 September 1583 dispatch (`AGS Estado 1258, ff. 165–166`). In Item 16 p. 2 L25 the mechanical text reads `[100~] mil acados de rrenta`: `{100~?}` + `mil` supports the figure, `[ducados]` depends on contextual `26=du`, and "annual income" interprets *renta* — treated here as a **reported offer**, not an independently verified payment.
- **Tier-A closed-class nomenclator candidates:** `{ful?}` = *para*, `{sa?}` = *más* (*de más*), `{95?}` = *como*, `{xol?}` = *lo que / quanto*, `{xa?}` = *muy / tan*, `{Si?}` = *-mente* adverbial suffix.
- **Tier-B diplomatic entity candidates:** `{sim?}` = *V. S.* (*Vuestra Señoría*, Baron Paolo Sfondrati); `{hom?}` = *S. A.* (Duke Charles Emmanuel I of Savoy in `#2077`; referent ambiguous between *S. A.* and *S. M.* in `#2518`); `{him?}` = *S.
