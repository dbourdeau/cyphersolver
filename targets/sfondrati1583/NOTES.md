# sfondrati1583: Carlo d'Aragona Tagliavia, Duke of Terranova (Governor of Milan) to Baron Paolo Sfondrati (Ambassador in Savoy), Aug-Oct 1583 (BYU Vault MSS 771, catalogue 2077, and Vault MSS 518 f. 3 item 2, catalogue 2518)

Status: partial (`#2077`: `read_share = 0.719`, 1,272 sure cipher words and 496 explicit holes of 1,768 cipher words with `clear_markup=⟨⟩`; companion `#2518`: `read_share = 0.653`, 128 sure cipher words and 68 explicit holes of 196 cipher words; combined `1,400/1,964 = 0.713`, measured identically by `word_audit.py` and `research/reading_audit/v10_relabel.py`). Leads: catalogue **2077** (`BYU Vault MSS 771`, vetted 3 Nov 2026) and companion catalogue **2518** (`BYU Vault MSS 518`, Folder 3, Item 2, vetted 11 Nov 2026 with the note *"it belongs with catalogue 2077 and should be worked with it"*). ## The system and the key (`key.tsv`, `SIGN_CONVENTIONS.md`)

Described directly from `#2077` and `#2518` (and matching Satoshi Tomokiyo's secondary summary of Philip II's 1582 general cipher `Cg.12` at `https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/spanish3.htm`, sections `Cp.30 (1581)` and `Cg.12 (1582)`, which notes that substitute letters use the next letter of the alphabet; no period key sheet or printed Devos/Jensen table was consulted directly):
- **Factored syllable grammar (`<Base>[<VowelMark>][<CodaMarks>]`):** Five right-attached vowel marks (`|` = `a`, `e` = `e`, `+` = `i`, `.` = `o`, `@` = `u`), four coda consonant marks (`'` = `n`, `^` = `s`, `,` = `r`, `:` = `l`), gemination cross (`*`, which mechanically emits doubled `ll, rr, ss, ff` across both transcripts; zero `nn` onsets are emitted mechanically), vowel+`m` dots (`8. = am`, `7. = em`, `5_m = im`, `14|. = tam`, `96. = cum`), literal-numeral overbar (`~`: `11~`, `100~`, `2~`, `3~`, `5~`, `10~`), and cancellation circle (`°`).
- **Bare vowels, `f = e` letter substitute, and consonant bases:** Bare vowels and homophones (`7, f = e`, `8, Δ = a`, `5 = i`, `21 = o`, `18 = u`, `{, 17 = y`), 19 single-consonant bases (`1..22` plus `D = ch`, `15 = x`), consonant-cluster bases in the `23..34` series (`23=bl, 24=br, 26=cr, 27=dr, 29=fr, 31=gr, 32=pl, 33=pr, 34=tr` attested; slot `25` is unattested, and `28` and `30` remain Grade-3 singletons), and inflected stem **`60 = auis-`** (`auisa, auisar, auise`). Bare `31=fa` / `31:=fal` and singleton `31.=gro` (*peligro*) are separated in `key.tsv`.
- **Handwritten `<1-digit>+{1,4,6}` forms vs. contextual alternatives:** Because attached `|`, `+`, and `@` resemble digits `1`, `4`, and `6`, `36=fu, 41=ha, 46=hu, 56=ju, 61=ga, 66=gu, 91=ca, 96=cu, Di=cha` and bare `24=di` (distinct in the grammar from vowel-bearing `24=br-`) reproduce directly in `decrypt.py`. By contrast, bare `21` emits `o`, bare `16` emits `z`, and bare `26` emits `a`; proposed readings *da*, *bu*, and *du* are contextual alternatives and are bracketed `[word]` everywhere in `reading_2077.md` and `reading_2518.md`, as are cursive `11+/11|` vs. `14+/14|` disambiguations and `32+` vs. `34` scan doubts.
- **Recovery provenance and crib check (`scripts/`):** No clear passage was used as a crib during syllabary recovery: `scripts/solve_cg12.py` and `scripts/refine_cg12.py` read only the cipher token stream `transcript_2077_v2.txt` (seeding `f = e` from Tomokiyo's `spanish3.htm` note and boosting general 1580s diplomatic terms in `scripts/lm_goldenage1583.py`), while the clear Spanish framing (`Item 19`, `Item 26`, and `Item 7`'s clear `Duque de Joyosa`) was consulted afterward to check historical context and reading boundaries. The held-out `mendoza1589` synthetic control (`scripts/synthetic_control_1583.py`) encrypted individual letters with homophones and nulls rather than a factored syllabary; manual contextual analysis and 4x scan checks then produced `key.tsv`. The packaged decoder (`decrypt.py`) and validator (`validate.py`) are strictly mechanical and unboosted.
- **Provenance-graded nomenclator layer (`key.tsv`, `holes.md`):** Eleven short structural/morphemic codes (`56' = jun`, `56^ = jus`, `23=/23=^ = cosa/cosas`, `xe = manera`, `quol = qual`, `79/79^ = carta/cartas`, `224/224^ = mi/mis`, `12° = ''`) are in `key.tsv`; all 91 remaining unglossed nomenclators and overbarred numerals stay open (`?`) in `key.tsv` and explicitly bracketed `{tok?}` in the readings (for example, *franceses* for `{98e=^?}` remains an unverified contextual hypothesis).

## Transcription doubts

- `35| 12|` (`Item 6 p. 2 L15`) - read: `35| 12|` (*haya passado*); checked: image, decode; initial `v2` log row #6 mistakenly proposed `35| 21` (*jornada*, which actually sits on `L14`), whereas the separate initial upright of `12|` is clear on the crop and `transcript_2077_v2.txt` retains `35| 12|` (corrected in `V2_CORRECTIONS.md`).
- `24` vs. `22+` (`Item 16 p. 2 L23-L24`) - read: `24` kept in `transcript_2077_v2.txt` (*codision* on `L23`, *que dio* on `L24`); checked: image, decode; `L24` shows a single `2` plus cross (*que dio*) while `L23` *codision* (`[comision]`) is a plausible missed `22+` candidate (`V2_CORRECTIONS.md` row #11 updated).
- `22,` vs. `22|,` (`Item 21 p. 3 PS02`) - read: `22,` kept open in `transcript_2077_v2.txt` (`[22,]gen`, bracketed `[margen]`); checked: image, decode; two `2`s and a lower comma are visible with a faint terminal upright that favors `22|,` (*mar* compositionally), left unedited pending independent palaeographer review.
- `32+` vs. `34` (`#2518 p. 1 L08` `[pliego]`, `#2518 p. 1 L15` `[cumplimiento]`, `Item 21 p. 3 PS07` `[suplico]`) - read: bare `34` kept open in both transcripts; checked: image, decode; a visible intermediate `2`-hook on the crops favors `32+` (*pli*) compared against the angular `34.` on `#2518 L14`, so these are likely transcription slips rather than proof that bare `34` equals *pli*.
- `11+/11|` vs. `14+/14|` (recurring cross-bearing forms across `#2077` and `#2518`, e.g. `Item 21 p. 2 L25` *ni lo uno ni lo otro*) - read: `14+`/`14|` in transcripts, disambiguated inside `[word]` brackets; checked: image, decode; `11e` and `14e` separate cleanly on the crops, whereas cross-bearing `11+` and `14+` are harder to separate visually and are kept bracketed rather than claimed as a systematic scribal merger.
- `46` (`Item 6 p. 1 L06`, mechanical `eldehumala`) - read: `46` in `transcript_2077_v2.txt`, bracketed `[?humala]` in `reading_2077.md`; checked: image, decode; unclear whether `46` is a `4@=hu` syllable or a separate code/name fragment.
- `[stain]` (`#2518 p. 1 L01-L03`, 3 bleached water-stain spots) - read: `[stain]` in `diplomatic_2518.txt` and `{stain?}` in `reading_2518.md`; checked: image, decode; ink is bleached out across 2-3 tokens per line on the scan and the surrounding decoded clauses break off at each gap without fixing the missing signs, so zero conjectural tokens are transcribed.

## Historical verification (Milan and Turin, Aug-Oct 1583)

- **Joyeuse, Nevers, and Turin's garrison (`#2077` Item 6, 14 Aug 1583):** Reports the alarm in Savoy over the arrival of Anne, Duc de Joyeuse (*el de Joyosa*, independently written in clear as `Duque de Joyosa` in MSS 771 **Item 7**, and attested in Florence on 15 July 1583 in Giuliano de' Ricci's chronicle, fol. 508r) and news of Louis Gonzaga, Duke of Nevers (*la del `{58=?}` de la Neuers*); praises `{hom?}`'s decision to ride out by post to meet Joyeuse rather than antagonize those `{cem^?} {98e=^?}` and approves reinforcing Turin's garrison with infantry.
- **Asti, Savoy succession contingency, and Montmorency negotiations (`#2077` Item 16, 6 Sep 1583):** Refutes rumors about troops toward Asti while confirming secret contingency discussions *"in case the death of the `{58=?}` should occur"* (*sobre lo que en caso que sucediera la muerte del `{58=?}` se debria hacer*). Joan Davies (*Historical Journal*, *"Neither Politique nor Patriot? Henri, duc de Montmorency and Philip II, 1582-1589"*, n. 23) independently documents the September 1583 *Propositión y respuesta de Memoransi* signed by **Alessandro Constantino** and **Ferrante Pagano** enclosed with Terranova's 25 September 1583 letter to Philip II (`AGS Estado 1258, ff. 165-166`). In Item 16 p. 2 L25, Terranova expresses astonishment at a **reported offer** of `{100~?} mil [ducados] de rrenta` (mechanical `[100~] mil acados de rrenta`: currency `[ducados]` uses contextual `26=du` and "annual income" interprets *renta*).
- **Gian Andrea Doria, Don Pedro de Aragón, and Languedoc maritime fortresses (`#2077` Item 21, 21 Sep 1583):** Proposes sending the original Montmorency draft and signed note to `{him?}` aboard the `{ca^?}` of `{nul?}` Doria (named `[juan] Andrea` on p. 2; `{ca^?}` stays open rather than forced to "galleys") on the departure of **Don Pedro de Aragón, my `[hijo]`**, with Ferrante going to `[barcelona]` and `[embiar]`ing the papers in his `[compañia]`; the cipher postscript quotes an Italian marginal clause concerning a firstborn son and Montmorency's consent to `{2~?}` or `{3~?}` maritime fortresses, asking whether it was a *yerro de pluma*.
- **Genoa frigate and St. Gotthard route (`#2518`, 5 Oct 1583):** Coordinates `{5~?} mil {71=^?}` by the `{10~?}` of October and writes to `Genoua` to `[embaxador] Don Pedro de [mendoça]` so `la fragata` is `[patroniçada]` by a trustworthy `{hol?}`; the clear postscript cites **Pompeo della Croce** (*Pompeo de la Cruz*) on a traveler crossing the St. Gotthard Pass toward Varese.
