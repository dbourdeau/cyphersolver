## Historical & Out-of-Sample Notes

- **Pre-revision transfer vs. revised joint edition:** Evaluated separately in `validate.py`, the pre-revision `#2077` transfer key (without `14|.=tam` and `96.=cum`, `286/323` mapped) independently confirms `29 = fr` (*offrescido*, *fragata*), `60 = auis-` (*auisar*), `Di = cha` (*mucha*), and literal-numeral overbars (`5~ mil`, `10~ deste`); the revised joint `key.tsv` (`288/323` mapped) adds `14|. = tam` (*tampoco*) and `96. = cum` (*cumplimiento*) from joint inspection across both letters (`96.` occurs `0×` in `#2077`, `1×` in `#2518`).
- **Ambassador in Genoa & editorial repairs:** **Don Pedro de Mendoza** served as Spanish Ambassador to the Republic of Genoa in 1583, coordinating Mediterranean frigate dispatches (`Genoua`, `Don Pedro`, and `fragata` decode verbatim; `[embaxador]`, `[mendoça]`, and `[patroniçada]` carry whole-word editorial brackets).
- **Referent of `{hom?}`:** Kept open across both letters (`{hom?}` aligns with *Su Alteza*, Duke Charles Emmanuel I of Savoy, in `#2077`; in `#2518` its referent could be *Su Alteza* or *Su Majestad* and is left unforced).
- **Postscript:** **Pompeo della Croce** (*Pompeo de la Cruz*) was Philip II's Milanese diplomatic agent to the Swiss Catholic cantons; the traveler crosses the **St. Gotthard Pass** toward **Varese** in the Duchy of Milan.
