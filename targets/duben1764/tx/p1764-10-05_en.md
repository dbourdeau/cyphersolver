# 5 October 1764 (St Petersburg), Düben to the Crown ("Sire Votre Majesté"): English translation

## English translation

5 October. Sire, Your Majesty will deign, I hope, to do me justice and to believe that since the moment of my arrival
I have neglected no means I thought proper to carry out Your intentions and to manage[?] usefully the interests You
have been pleased to entrust to me. My repeated instances on a matter which [?] so close to [Your] heart having[?] so
far produced only very sweeping assurances of the good dispositions in which the Empress finds herself, I thought I
should put off sending news until I was able to report something positive and real. As that moment keeps being delayed
from one moment to the next, I did not wish to fail to have the honour of informing Your Majesty of the cause of my
silence, and of the little progress I have been able to make so far, which must be put down partly to the affairs of
Poland, which have absorbed all the Empress's attention, and partly to the news that reaches her from Sweden. This
Princess has nevertheless had me assured that she will not lose sight of Your Majesty's interests and will support them
effectively at the coming Diet. I tried to find out how she meant to go about it, and above all to obtain the most
effective help to that end, but they were content to tell me that Stachieff will soon be sent back to Sweden and will
explain the Empress's intentions at greater length. Meanwhile I need not set out how indispensable it will be to handle
this Princess's dispositions with care, so that on the one hand she is given no umbrage about the aims one may have,
and on the other one does not confide in people whose [?good] intentions she has every reason to suspect of being
contrary to her views. For my part I shall not cease to do all that depends on me to obtain a happy outcome, of which I
venture to beg Your Majesty to be persuaded, as well as of the respectful zeal and the profound submission[?] with
which I have the honour to be, Sire, Your Majesty's —
