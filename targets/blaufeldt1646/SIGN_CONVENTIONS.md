# Sign conventions (inv. 24854 and the glossed sibling letters 24852, 24853)

Written before any key was applied to 24854; shapes only, no values.

- The cipher is written in Arabic figures in continuous runs inside the Italian plain text. Each run is read in
  two-figure groups from its first figure. A run starts after plain script, a comma or a gap.
- Figure shapes in this hand: 1 is a short upright stroke, sometimes with a small hook; 2 has a flat foot and is
  often joined to the next figure; 3 has two bowls and can stand lower than the line; 0 is a small round loop that can
  look like o; 9 has a long tail below the line (like g); 5 has a flat top bar (it can look like s); 7 has a straight
  top and a slanting stem, and a 7 with a cross-stroke can look like 4; 6 has a tall looped ascender; 8 is a closed
  double loop.
- A bar over a group (seen over some groups on 24852 and 24854) is copied as `^` after the group; its meaning is not
  assumed.
- Tokens in the transcription files: `NN` one two-figure group; ` | ` a gap, comma or point between runs; `<...>`
  plain script copied through; `=` the run breaks at the line end; `NN?` a group whose shape is uncertain;
  `NN/MM` a group with two possible readings (the first is used); `[?word]` inside `<...>` a plain word not
  certain on the image (added after the blind review of 12 Nov 2026).
- Image limit: the frames are photographs of a microfilm-reader screen (Vlnas 2017 appendix), about 9 px per figure
  height. Many figures are at the edge of legibility; `?` marks are frequent and are honest.
- Glosses (on 24852 and 24853 only) are written over the runs in a smaller hand; 24854 has none.
