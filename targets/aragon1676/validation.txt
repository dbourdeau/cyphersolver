Method (the same in every cycle, fixed in hashed protocols before each measurement):
- Key learned only on training pairs (cipher with its contemporary clear: marginal clears on views 0138-0139 and 0484-0485,
  and the separate gathering "Descifrado" of views 0356-0368 paired with ten letters of views 0318-0355), by EM alignment,
  then frozen by SHA-256.
- One held-out page per key, opened only after the freeze: cipher transcribed and hashed before its margin clear was read.
- Score = longest common subsequence of decryption and margin clear / the longer length, with a head cut t in [0, 40]
  (the margin may begin before the cipher block). Controls: 200 keys made by shuffling the key's values (seed 20261104);
  each control key gets its own best head cut; control = the best of the 200. The same 200 keys give the M5 control
  (share of decoded letters covered by Spanish words of at least 5 letters, lexicon lang/lexicons/es.json.gz).
- Thresholds: VALIDATED if characters >= 0.85 and >= control + 0.30 (or M5 >= 0.60 and >= M5 control + 0.30);
  PARTIAL if the margin over the control is >= 0.30 without the absolute threshold; otherwise FAILED.
