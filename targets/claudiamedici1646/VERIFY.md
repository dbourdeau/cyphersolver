## Verdict

**Confirm, with small fixes.** The sign copy is good. The key values hold in the glossed pairs the verifier checked
and agree with Vlnas's printed table. The mark check works. The statuses stand: 24793 **read** (80 of 83 words
verbatim from the signs, 96.4%) and 24800 **read in part** (28 of 33, 84.8%). The 24793 margin over 95% is thin (see
section 6). The held-out test is real in kind but small, and its transcription drifted toward the gloss in one place.
24784, 24786 and 24796 carry a contemporary decipherment and are rightly claimed only as key source and test.

- **Vlnas 2017, ch. 4.3, table 5** (PDF p. 39) prints: 2 a, 5 c, £ d, r e, 3 f, l g, ß h, g i, o i, c- k, c l, 4 m,

## 4. Held-out test (24786)

- Genuine in kind: the test is graded against the contemporary interlinear decipherment, not against the solver's
  reading. The verifier's blind copy of the same three passages matches the solver's except at "doppo".
- Weaknesses: (1) it is small (3 partial lines, about 13 words); (2) the gloss sits right above the signs and the
  solver "covered it mentally", so the copy is not blind to the gloss, and the dropped Q in "doppo" shows that the
  gloss pulled the copy once; (3) the freeze cannot be proven (no commit or hash; `key_frozen.tsv` is dated 15:07,
  `heldout_24786_transcription.txt` 15:15).
- On the signs as written, every sign decrypts to the gloss letter or to a spelling variant. 10 of 13 words agree
  letter for letter (communica, doppo and col are the spelling variants), not 11. "No sign failed" still holds.
- The stronger evidence is that the frozen key alone turns both bare frames into fluent Italian, and that Vlnas's
  separate decipherment of 24790 uses the same values.

## 5. Mark check

- `python scripts/check_marks.py`: **exit 0, PASS**. It prints "decryption_24793.txt: rebuilt, identical",
  "decryption_24800.txt: rebuilt, identical", "24793: 83 cipher words: 80 verbatim from the signs, 3 with a marked
  repair, 0 guesses, 0 holes; verbatim share 96.4%" and "24800: 33 cipher words: 28 verbatim from the signs, 5 with a
  marked repair ... 84.8%".
- Tamper tests on a scratch copy each gave **exit 1**: (a) `{e/c}on` changed to `con` without the mark gives "FAIL
  24793 L02: unmarked departure"; (b) a transcription sign changed without rebuilding gives "FAIL decryption_24793.txt
  is not what decrypt.py gives"; (c) the `I` in "forze" set to `?` and the decryption rebuilt gives "FAIL 24793 L03:
  unmarked departure"; (d) `a{+d}dosso` changed to `addosso` gives "FAIL 24800 L07". So every departure inside the
  marked blocks is marked, and the check catches unmarked ones.
- Every repair that the verifier could check on the image is a real departure and is marked: 24793 L02 r for c; L04
  and L08 g without a cross-bar for D (the verifier's blind copy also has `g r` in L04); 24800 L01 2/Q; L05 y and c in
  "indisciplin-ate gen-ti"; L07 + for 4 (the verifier also saw a clear cross) and the added d.
- Not covered by the check: the Italian prose and the English of both readings give the normalised text without marks
  (con, de', dall', per, prudentemente, addosso, indisciplinate). "[?genti]" is flagged there, but "indisciplinate" is
  not, although it holds one of the two changed signs. Minor.
- The counts include code groups as cipher words (4 in 24793, 2 in 24800). Their values come from glosses on sibling
  letters, so counting them is fair, but NOTES.md should say so.
