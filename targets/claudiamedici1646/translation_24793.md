# English translation, inv. 24793: Claudia de' Medici to Ottavio Piccolomini, Innsbruck, 1646

Translation of the Italian in `reading_24793.md`. Square brackets at the start and end: the plain-script context,
read roughly, not letter by letter. "forces" (`forze`) rests on one doubtful sign shape (see the reading).

[Your Excellency's letter ... has arrived ...; the good outcome, for the reasons] you mention, depends very much on
acting in concert and with prompt resolution, while the forces of the Catholic arms balance those of the enemy. So
it seems to me that the opportunity should not be lost, also on the side of the Duke of Lorraine, unless His
Highness perhaps has some other hidden aims, ruled by his passions or by his inconstancy. The King has done well,
and prudently, in ordering Your Excellency to stay this year too in Flanders, since it may be believed that the
French will make every effort to pursue enterprises, against which your valiant resistance will be more than
necessary; in which may God our Lord grant you complete victory. [I thank Your Excellency for your most kind news ...]
