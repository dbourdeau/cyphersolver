# English translation, inv. 24800: Claudia de' Medici to Ottavio Piccolomini, Innsbruck, 25 September 1646

Translation of the Italian in `reading_24800.md`, with the same doubtful words in `[?...]`. The plain-script
passages around the cipher are read for context, not letter by letter.

With Your Excellency's letter of the 7th I receive the continuation of the news from those parts, which, although
not very good for the interests of the King, I nevertheless hold in due esteem, since they come to me from Your
Excellency's natural courtesy, for which I give you due thanks. It grieves me not a little to hear that affairs in
Flanders go so badly, and that a great part of the cause lies in the disorders of the [?undisciplined] [?troops] of the
Duke of Lorraine; for they could, as Your Excellency prudently observes, bring even greater ruin upon themselves.
May God our Lord be pleased to provide a timely remedy ... Innsbruck, 25 September 1646.
