# delcarretto1576 - English translation of the cipher

Translation of reading.md (Brice Dudout, with a Claude Opus 5.5 agent, 6 Nov 2026). Probable words are not marked here
(see reading.md); the torn margin is left out except for its legible words.

> Beccaria has said, among other things, that there is no one who could carry out His Majesty's orders better or as
> well as Count Espinula (Spinola), and he assures us that he will not fail to carry them out, as long as it is left to
> him alone; then let Signor Pietro judge the rest and the quality of the man. But be sure that we have not failed, nor
> shall we fail in any point, to make the truth known, as much for the honour of Your Lordship as for the service of His
> Majesty, and ours too, although it brings some difficulty that this man was approved in the way he was by Your
> Lordship, who sent him to His Majesty as a trustworthy person, and that he has behaved rather well than badly.
> So let Your Lordship rest easy and attend to doing your duty on that side, as we have done and shall do on this one;
> for since this man is known to His Majesty and to his ministers better perhaps than is believed, we trust that neither
> he nor the one for whom he acts will achieve their aim.

Margin (torn): "… al … … things … …".

Context (to be checked by a historian): Alfonso II del Carretto, marquis of Finale, at the Diet of Regensburg (1576),
writes to his agents in Milan, Lodovico Allaria and Paolo Fenocchio, about a man recommended to Philip II and about
Count Spinola; Finale was then contested between its marquis, its subjects, Genoa, Spain and the Empire. "Signor
Pietro" and "Beccaria" are not identified.
