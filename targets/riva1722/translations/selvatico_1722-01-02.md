# Selvatico Estense (Modena) to Giuseppe Riva (London), "2 Genaro" [1722], cc.2-3: English

Same holes as the reading, in the same places. Text between asterisks is from cipher.

## Translation

Modena, 2 January. We are *by this post without [?letters] from [347], but we do not fail to give him [211] of [366] matters and of the return of the [?Most Serene] [?Princes], who together with the [?Cardinal] de Rohan came from Rome to [?Modena] and, after [?having] stopped only for dinner, went on [...] Reggio.* There stays *the [?Cardinal], and no one knows [?when] he leaves, having been troubled by gout; and it is said there that he is [50_] to leave for [?France]. It seems that [259] [?Most Serene] wishes to dispose of the person of [347] elsewhere, and so he [?will learn] [?from] his [?letter] the [?more] [?exact] detail.*
