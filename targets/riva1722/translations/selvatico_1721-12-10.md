# Selvatico Estense (Modena) to Giuseppe Riva (London), "10 Xbre" [1721?], cc.14-15: English

Same holes as the reading, in the same places. Text between asterisks is from cipher.

## Translation

Modena, 10 December. The agent Tori(?) has passed me a letter of yours, and I thank you for what it says, assuring you that I answer it with all the esteem your merit deserves. For my part I do not believe the Congress will ever be held, and it is certainly not by my advice that Marchese Rangoni is in Paris: I wrote from there that he should not be sent, but the Master judged otherwise, and since he left it has cost us close to 6,000 Spanish doubloons. Massi(?) asks me to put in a word(?) for his return home. How it pains me *[?to] have to tell you the [?contrary], because he will soon go on either to Paris or to [297]. From the [?letters] of [259] [?Most Serene] you will [?perhaps] have, either [?this] evening or* by the next post, some precise order; and keeping myself for some happier occasion to serve you, I shall have for you everywhere the esteem you deserve and I owe you, and remain meanwhile with respect [clear text not transcribed here].
