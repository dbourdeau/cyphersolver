# Selvatico Estense (Vienna) to Giuseppe Riva (London), 10 June 1722, cc.21-22: English

Same holes as the reading, in the same places. Text between asterisks is from cipher.

## Translation

I acknowledge your first letter, from which I learned many things concerning *that [?court]*. I should however have wished for other news, and in particular what is so necessary to know and what I can hope for only from you, namely *to know [?whether] that King's mind has been brought round for the [188] of [287]. [?These] [196] make known the need for that power to [186] in [?favour] of the [?Most Serene] [133], to carry and assist the cause of [287] at the congress*, but at the same time they make one fear *that it will not [?wish] to commit itself*. This *[...] opinion, which they hold as certain, is at odds* entirely with what is written to me from Modena, and with what *[?Sanseforino]* told me on this matter. Please therefore take the trouble to inform me exactly, as I am impatient for sure and certain news on this point, so as to persuade *[?this] [196]* if it is favourable, and to warn *[287]*, so that he *[...] hope so much*, if it is contrary to the hopes he has formed. I beg the greatest speed, as we are given to hope *that the time of the congress is very near*.
