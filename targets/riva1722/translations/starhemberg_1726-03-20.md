# [Conrad Sigmund von] Starhemberg (Vienna) to Giuseppe Riva (London), 20 March 1726, Campori S, Starhemberg cc.21-22: English

Same holes as the reading, in the same places. Text between asterisks is from cipher.

## Translation

Dear Riva, *I have received three of your [?letters] of* 19 and 22 February *and* 6 March; *I thank you for the [108] they contain; I have not* failed *to make use of them*, but I must confess to you that *we already had the greater part, and [?earlier], from other correspondents*, and so I beg you *to [?contrive]* as far as possible *to [276] me [?punctually] and to send me everything [?that] may ever concern [?this] [?court] or the* general *[?affairs] [140] of the modern [?constitution]*; in that case *I do not despair that*, if you are willing, *to go on letting him [?have]* every year *the [100]* pounds sterling asked *by [?you] from [?Emperor]*, without *ever [...]*, and you may trust entirely as regards *the [274]*. I am only sorry that my private affairs do not allow me to *give of* my*self [252]* of this *consequence*; otherwise *I would not hesitate to send them to him* at once, but as these are all matters *that touch the [269] of [254] [255]*, and still to him *[?will not give] the rewards [?but]*; both *[?you] and the friend in [?Holland] may be assured that, continuing [?this] year with their [108], they will [?have] between them* at least a couple *of hundred [...]* of *fees for the expense and the trouble*, and this in any case *I [?shall give] also from my own*; but if *[108] of consequence* come to me, *[...] I shall certainly make them [?count] with [254] [255] and have them rewarded either with more [136] or with* some *other [?Imperial] favour or distinction more [?welcome] to them. Let [?you] explain yourself* on this *point and continue*, in case this *sincere opening* of mine is welcome. *The [?Most Serene] [?Master] [143]* will always find *his [?account] in it if he and the [...] [198] continue to [?be] good [?Imperials]. To Frencs* I beg you to pay a thousand compliments on my behalf [clear text not transcribed here] for the coming lottery; I hope *Frencs* will meanwhile be willing to make the payment for me [clear text not transcribed here].
