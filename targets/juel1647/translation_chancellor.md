# Translation: Juel to the chancellor, 20 Nov 1647 (register p. 5, entry No. 4)

[On the audience with the Queen ... that] a strong [war] council is held, and they would gladly have 124 [...] general in
158. He could [...] become General of the Realm (Rigens Feltherre).

Then it went with 129 [...] with 123 ... from ... for Sparre ... that ... will [...] [quarters] in Holstein. Then ... for ...
who would [follow] ... [...] with him.

Holes as in the reading: `[...]` unread, `[word]` guess. 123, 124, 129, 158 are unidentified codes; the signs TT, Θ, ∞, Ψ,
Δ and Ш are unidentified.
