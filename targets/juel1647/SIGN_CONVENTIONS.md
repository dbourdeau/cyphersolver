# Sign conventions (shapes only, no values)

Register TKUA Sverige, Peder Juel, Ekstraktprotokol 1647-49 (Arkivalieronline unit 261585), images 5, 6, 61.

- Cipher groups are Arabic numerals in the secretary's hand, one to three digits, separated by dots (sometimes a colon or
  a comma). Transcribed as written, one number per token, separated by spaces.
- A number written heavily over another number is transcribed as the upper (visible) number and marked `!` in pass files,
  e.g. `33!`.
- A number whose shape is ambiguous is written with both readings, `50|56`, and settled in the reconciled file on the
  image only (shape comparison with other instances on the same page).
- Clear Danish words inside a cipher run are given in braces, `{kunde hand}`.
- Non-numeral signs inside the runs are named by shape:
  - `#X` : vertical double-barred cross before "83" (p. 5, line 6 of the right page);
  - `#PI` : two-legged sign like a Greek pi (p. 5, after "123");
  - `#THETA` : circle with a bar (p. 5, after "fra");
  - `#EIGHTLOOP` : a large looped figure like an 8 before "Will" (p. 5);
  - `#PSI` : trident (p. 5, after "da");
  - `#DELTA` : triangle with a small ring on top (p. 5, after "for");
  - `#SHA` : three-stroke comb like Cyrillic sha (p. 5, before "10");
  - `#TBAR` : cross with a ring, p. 6 line 1 (before "8").
- Numbers 83, 84, 86, 123, 124, 129, 158, 208, 212, 277, 339, 351, 401, 412, 426, 442, 446 (three-digit groups) are
  transcribed like any other number; they are nomenclator codes and are not given letter values.
- Line breaks of the register are kept as `/` in the reconciled files.

Glossed sibling letters (unit 261575, images 117, 118, 121): the plaintext of the time is written in small letters over
the numbers. In `pairs_*.tsv` each pair is number, gloss letter as seen, and a confidence (`c` clear, `p` probable,
`?` the gloss sits between two numbers).
