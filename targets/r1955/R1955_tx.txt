# NOTE hand: one upright copyist's hand throughout (heading, cipher, date line). Groups separated by full stops. 1 = plain stroke (sometimes small top serif); 2 = rounded/looped top and long flat foot; 3 has two forms: round-topped, and FLAT-topped with a closed lower bowl that looks like 'B'/'8' (e.g. 3255, 3159, 3120, 3013, 2313, 3312, 3222, 3294, p2 931/3193) - all read 3: real 8s in this hand are figure-8s, mostly with a hairline flourish rising to the right from the top (868, 1008, 1820, 1828), and 4-digit groups never begin with 7/8/9 in the Michell letters (lead digits 1-6), so 8255/8120/8159 are excluded; 4 = open angular or closed-top 'A'-like (2148, 1245, 247, p2 413); 5 = 's'-shape with a separate flag tick, sometimes the flag fused into a closed top loop (3356, 275, 495); 6 = tall looped ascender; 7 has two forms: 'Z'-like (flat top bar starting with a blob, diagonal, short foot: 715, 77, 707, 2175, 1573, 247) and a small hooked '>' form (720, 2071, 2497, 3317, 224072398) - told from 2 by the flat, unlooped top; 9 = closed bowl with long descender curling left; 0 = small oval. Every 3/8, 2/7, 5/9 and 1/4 decision was checked at 3-7x on contrast-stretched crops; every line was read twice (2-line half-width strips at 0.7 and 3-line third-width strips at 0.8, plus per-line 1.2x crops) and the readings reconciled.
