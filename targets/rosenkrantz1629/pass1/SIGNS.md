# Sign labels, Rosenkrantz sign cipher (letters No. 12-14, 20-23, Feb-Apr 1629)

Neutral labels given while transcribing from the page images at about 1.7x native. No values are implied.
Digits written as digits are transcribed as digits (2 3 4 5 6 7 9 and 1? once); two-digit numbers set off by stops
(33, 68, 79, 84, 98) are transcribed as numbers. `.` is a written stop. `{ }` clear Danish. `?` doubtful reading.

| Label | Shape |
|---|---|
| D8 | tall closed figure eight |
| OU | small ring below with an open v or u on top (an 8 with open top); easily confused with D8 |
| TH | figure eight or theta with a cross-stroke through the waist |
| B6 | 6 / b shape, plain |
| BX | stem with a ring at the foot and a flag or cross-stroke at the top (b with stroke; P with ring foot) |
| PF | stem with a closed box flag at top right (P) |
| GM | gamma: stem with open flag at top right |
| AR | short stem with a small hook or arrowhead at top (r-like, arrow-like) |
| PI | pi: two legs under a bar |
| MM | three legs under a bar (m-like pi) |
| FK | open square fork, three prongs up on a base (Cyrillic sha) |
| LD | ladder: two or three closed boxes side by side, often slanted |
| LH | two small flat boxes on the line (horizontal ladder); seen once |
| SQ | square (once a flat rectangle) |
| TG | triangle (once with a tail, twice bold with a bar) |
| TV | open triangle point down |
| OO | plain circle |
| CD | circle or cup with an inner mark: a dot, a ring, a smile or a small w |
| CU | open cup or C without inner mark |
| U | open cup or u without inner mark, sometimes like a broad omega |
| TB | circle with a horizontal bar through it |
| OX | circle with a cross (X) in it |
| RH | ring on a stem (rho, q-like) |
| FM | ring on a stem with a cross-bar (Venus sign) |
| FO | large ring, stem, small ring at the foot, sometimes barred |
| OM | omega, w-like, small |
| OMb | omega with a bar across the top |
| DB | dumbbell: two (or three) small rings joined on the line |
| DD | double delta: two looped d or 6 shapes joined; also the ampersand-like looped form |
| V | V |
| VR | V with a small ring on the left arm |
| RG | small raised ring after a sign (written as its own token) |
| W | W |
| + | plain cross |
| PM | plus over a bar (plus-minus sign) |
| X4 | 4 or cross with a second bar across the stem (double-barred) |
| X3 | cross with three bars, small |
| XA | X4 with a caret or arc above |
| XX | large long cross with three bars; stands alone inside clear syntax ("som XX kand bekommis") |
| HB | double cross: two uprights, two bars (hash-like) |
| HH | h or H-like sign with a tail |
| F7 | 7 or F with a cross-bar on the stem |
| TR | trident (psi-like), sometimes with a bar on the stem |
| YY | Y-like sign |
| AD | a or d with a cross-stroke through the ascender |
| AA | large looped A-like sign |
| J | small j-like stroke; seen once, doubtful |
| MOON | large crescent open to the left; stands alone inside clear syntax ("att MOON haffuer", "att MOON kand") |
| TBL | bar over a small box with dots beside the stem; stands alone ("Ellers kunde TBL ingen ...") |

Known weak distinctions (same sign possibly written two ways): D8 / OU / TH; PF / BX / GM; X4 / X3 / XA / + / 4;
CD / CU / U / OU; RH / FM; OM / OMb; DD two forms; LD / FK. They are kept apart here and merged only in
named solver runs.
