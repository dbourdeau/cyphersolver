## Key comparison (unit 260610, "Chiffer" packet no. 112)
Rosenkrantz sign cipher, working description before comparison: 40+ distinct drawn signs (so several signs per letter), among them a three-pronged fork on a base (Ш), an arrow/flagged stroke, square, gamma, circle, circle-on-stem (ρ), circled dot, double loop (oo/8 on its side), a "ladder" of three joined boxes, double-barred cross, barred a, theta, plus digits 2 3 4 5 6 9 used as signs, and two-digit numbers (33, 68, 79, 84, 98) that behave like code words.
- 145-146 "Chiffre du Roy" (German nomenclator; names Fagel, Printz Georg: later 17th c.): ONE sign and one number 1-28 per letter (A X, B Γ, C L, D ∂, E ⊐, F +, G ⊥, H T, I I, K H, L comb, M barred a, N barred t, O double-barred cross, P barred ff, Q barred q, R barred f, S ρ, T square, V α, W <, X barred 4, Y λ, Z ω, ch, sch), syllables and words 30-700. No fork, no ladder, no double loop, no circled dot; no homophones. Applied to Rosenkrantz's recurring word (8 Γ 4 U Ш ρ ω): only 3 of 7 signs exist in the key and give "?B???SZ". DOES NOT FIT.
- 123: key signed by Krag, 26 July 1659 (vowels a e i o u = 9 6 3 7 8, capital letters for names, 5 signs for ambassadors/fleet). Wrong period, no sign alphabet. DOES NOT FIT.
- 124-126: Erik Krag index key (three number alphabets). Numbers only. NOT a sign cipher.
- 127 R-128: the Danish key for France 1629 (homophones 44-98 in a 5x5 grid, words 200-240). Numbers only, no signs. Rosenkrantz's sign cipher uses only 33, 68, 79, 84, 98 (+ 5.9?) as two-digit numbers, isolated between sign runs: it is not a 44-98 homophone text. His number code A (3-1687) has most values under 44 or between 100 and 200 (e.g. 87.5.85.155.157.35.181.137.164.96.14.60): outside 44-98/200-240 design. DOES NOT FIT either system.
- 129-130: "Duplicat" number-cipher letter, a ciphertext not a key. 131-132: letter with interlinear decipherment dated 1658 (not a key for 1629).
- 133-134 ("DSM" sheets): Danish word list (Dania, Norvegia, Holsatia, Mare Balticum, Øsel ... Stralsund, Stettin, Wismar ... Tilli, Wallenstein: late 1620s) headed by a two-row alphabet "D a n u s b c e f g h i / k l m o p q r t x y z ö" (a reciprocal letter-pair alphabet: D-K, A-L, N-M, U-O, S-P, B-Q, C-R, E-T, F-X, G-Y, H-Z, I-Ö); 133 R: signs for weekdays, for the digits 1-0, for 100/1000 and for the months (O Jan, triangle Feb, square Mar, barred circle Apr, ... three-box ladder Jun, gamma Jul, F-combs Aug-Dec). This matches the edition's description of Christian IV's "Nye Characterer paa Danske" sent to Chancellor Friis WITH PALLE ROSENKRANTZ on 30 May 1627 and the pair-alphabet rule of 15 June 1627 (Bricka/Fridericia II nos. 70, 74: "Huilcken baagstaue der fiindis y den øffuerste rey, den betiider den, som staar der nedenfor"). The word list carries NO sign or number per word on these sheets (list positions only), and the system is letters-for-letters, not drawn signs. Shapes shared with Rosenkrantz's cipher (circle, square, gamma, ladder) are here month signs. NOT a key to the sign cipher as it stands. To be tested mechanically after transcription (letter-pair rule cannot apply: his text is signs).
- 135 L: small Latin key (seven triangle variants for a e i o u + nulls, F-comb family for consonants, capitals for countries, + Γ □ = equites pedites naves). Rosenkrantz has no triangle family. DOES NOT FIT.
- 135 R: Latin key with capital letters for places and digits for words (11 Decemb.). No sign alphabet.
- 137: "DSM" sign alphabet, one sign per letter (A barred-M, b ω, c Ɔ, d 6, e Ⅎ, f Ψ-like, g, h Ж, i arrow, k Ψ, l λ, m 4-like, n δ, o Э, ø circle-on-stem, p У, q B, r P, s Π, ss ↕, t ⊣, u Γ/F, w Λ, x, y, z ⊙; 4 "mutae"; note "r et s nulla interim litera fere ..."). Several shapes recur in Rosenkrantz (ω, 6, P, Π, Γ, ⊙, δ, ρ) so it is the closest candidate; but his most frequent signs (8, V, W, U, Ш, oo, +, 4-barred) are absent from it.
