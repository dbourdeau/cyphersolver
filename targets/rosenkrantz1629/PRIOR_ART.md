# PRIOR_ART rosenkrantz1629 (24 Nov 2026)

Target: Palle Rosenkrantz, London, to Christian IV, the Statholder (Frands Rantzau) and the Chancellor (Christian Friis),
Feb-Apr 1629. Rigsarkivet, TKUA England A II 13, unit 261258, images 263-293.

## Result
No decipherment, key or printed reading of THESE letters found. One unresolved risk (item 3): a manuscript key by
Temler to Christian IV's own sign cipher of 1625-27 exists in Copenhagen; whether Rosenkrantz's sign cipher is that
alphabet could not be checked online.

## Checked

   (GATE_20.md row 16; zobel_leads/decode_search.txt). [sourced]
2. The pages themselves: all 31 images viewed at 2000 px, cipher lines at about 1.7x native. No interlinear or

   The writer names his systems: 266R, the king's letter is "y Zifre"; 281L PS, "Ziffre" sent with Christen Thomesen;
   289L margin, "NB. dette er effter dj sidste Zifre som jeg sende med Christen Clemetsen" (the bigger number code was
   Rosenkrantz's own, sent home in 1629). [measured; readings of clear Kurrent at this scale, not collated]
3. Bricka and Fridericia, Kong Christian den Fjerdes egenhaendige Breve, vols 1-7 (archive.org OCR,
   kongchristianden01..07chriuoft; dl/c4_0N.txt), searched for Rosenkrands/Palle and Chif*. [sourced]
   - No letter of Rosenkrantz from London 1629 is printed or quoted (the edition prints the king's letters only).
     Letters TO Rosenkrantz in vol. 2 are of 1631 (nos. 318-330), in clear.
   - Vol. 1 p. 408 note: the king's letters to Chancellor Friis of 1625-27 are largely in cipher, "dels Tegn for
     enkelte Bogstaver, dels Tegn eller Tal for Ord"; the letter signs "fuldstaendig have kunnet tydes ved den af
     Etatsraad Temler dannede Nogle" (Kgl. Bibl., Kallske Saml. 4to 436), used by Molbech (1848).
     So a key to the KING'S sign cipher with Friis exists in manuscript. The system type (signs for letters, signs or
     numbers for words) is the same type as Rosenkrantz's cipher with the king. Same alphabet or not: NOT CHECKED.
     Temler's key is not online; Molbech's edition (archive.org kongchristianden01molb, Fraktur OCR) prints the
     plaintext and notes ("Dechiffreret af mig efter den af Temler udfundne Nogle") but, as far as the OCR shows,
     no sign table. The page images of Molbech were not paged for a plate.
   - Vol. 2 no. 70 (30 May 1627): the king sends Friis a cipher list headed "Dii Nye Characterer paa Danske, Som
     Chanseleren tilskiickit er med Palli Rossenkrandtz den 30 Maij Anno 1627". Rosenkrantz was the carrier.
     No. 74 (15 June 1627): a two-row letter alphabet for it, followed by "en Raekke uloselige Chifre".
   - Vol. 3 appendix "Dechifrerede Breve": the 1632 key for Frederik Gunther (numbers for letters; German). Not this.
   - Vol. 1 p. 186 note: a key sheet "Peder galtyss Baagstauer" (Stockholm resident, 1620s). Not this.
4. Rigsarkivet "Chiffer" packet, unit 260610, images 123-200 and 305-309 fetched at full size and compared (see
   WORKLOG.md, "Key comparison"). No key fits either Rosenkrantz system. The "DSM" sheets 133-134 carry the two-row
   alphabet "Danus bcefghi / klmopqrtxyzo" and a late-1620s word list (Tilli, Wallenstein): this is the 1627 system of
   item 3 (nos. 70, 74). Sheet 137 (also "DSM") is a one-sign-per-letter alphabet; a trial on Rosenkrantz's recurring
   seven-sign word gives no Danish, and his commonest signs are not on it. The trial was by eye on one word and a few
   lines, not a full mechanical decode. [measured]
5. Web: 4 searches (Rosenkrantz 1629 England cipher; Tomokiyo/Cryptiana Danish ciphers; Fridericia). Hits: SSNE entry
   1193 for Rosenkrantz (no cipher), TNA catalogue entries, HistoCrypt proceedings, Klaus Schmeh on Biermann's Danish
