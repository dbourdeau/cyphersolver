# Ercole Gonzaga’s outgoing register

**Letter B was written at Mantua on 9 January 1544.** The cardinal’s confidential letter-book preserves a contemporary version in plain Italian, including the part beyond the break in the Modena ciphertext. The secretary heads it *Al detto signor in zifra, et fuvi la minuta di man propria*. The previous entry is addressed to Ferrante. The letter runs from f. 5v through the date on f. 8v, followed by a postscript on f. 9r whose day is written with two upright strokes, apparently 11 January. [Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana, Barb. lat. 5792, f. 5v](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0012) · [f. 8v](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0018) · [f. 9r](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0019).

The report of the Pope's protest, the Emperor's reply, Farnese's mission, the journey to England, the seizure of the cardinals' revenues and the account from Mirandola follow the same order in the two witnesses. They often phrase the same information differently. For instance, the register gives *beneficii* where the ciphertext gives *frutti*, and *con poca perdita et iattura … restarne con vittoria* where the cipher puts victory before the small loss. The register can establish the identity of a person or the subject of a clause even where its wording cannot restore the cipher letters.

### The register on Salviati

Codex transcribed the Vatican manuscript from the library’s digitized page on 25 November 2026. The following reading of f. 8r expands abbreviations and adds punctuation. The first words continue the preceding page’s account of the seizure of La Baume’s property in Savoy. The name at the right edge of the preceding page reads *Monsignor della Baulma*.

> trova in Savoia, era venuta commissione di S. Maestà al signor Marchese del Vasto di sequestrar anchor lui tutti i frutti de’ beneficii di cardinali servitori del Re nello stato di Milano. Et m’ha pregato che, poi ch’io sapevo l’animo suo, vedessi di far opera che non venesse trattato da partiale. Così ne scrissi al Marchese, toccandogli una parola della pratica ch’io tenevo già con V. Eccellenza intorno alle cose d’esso cardinale. Et S. Eccellenza m’ha risposto che ’l sequestro era ordinato, però che non mancheria di far cosa che mi fosse grata. Et acciò che meglio mi possa compiacer, le ho replicato che V. Eccellenza, prima che partisse per Inghilterra, me n’haveva data buona speranza dal canto suo. M’è parso farla ne avertita, con pregarla che, se di ciò udirà parlar, voglia aiutar la materia per servitio del cardinale sopradetto, qual è tanto obligato a monsignor di Granvela et a V. Eccellenza insieme per gli ufficii ch’hanno fatti in favor d’esso con S. Maestà, che non potrei dirlo. Egli scrive al detto Granvela ringratiandolo molto, etc. Et io ne ringratio medesimamente S. S. et V. Eccellenza, con pregarle ambedue di cuore che si degnino continuar nell’opera buona cominciata, perché certo al giuditio mio fanno piacer a signor tale che lo merita quant’altro forse che lo possa meritar del grado suo.
>
> Ho mandato al conte della Mirandola quel che V. Eccellenza m’ha scritto sopra i casi d’esso, quale m’ha risposto …

The cipher’s corresponding line retains *me pregaua* and its own changes of tense and diction. The two proper names are identifiable even with those differences. [Vatican manuscript, f. 8r](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0017).

## Letter A on 4 May 1544

A letter to Ferrante in the same register, dated 4 May at Mantua, discusses his criticism of the orders given to the master of the horse when sent to Casale. Ercole explains why they initially acted on their own. The marquis had too few forces to help. When his strength recovered, they sent an offer of Casale and sought his aid. It also names the Abbadino. Ercole refers to the marquis simply as *il Marchese* here. His own letter to the *signor Marchese del Vasto* a few pages earlier refers to the same intelligence from Mirandola, Pietro Strozzi and the count’s troops. The commander represented by L30 in letter A can thus be identified with high probability as Alfonso d’Ávalos, marchese del Vasto. These documents give parallel accounts in different words. [4 May, ff. 49r–50v](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0099) · [the offer of Casale to the marquis, f. 49v](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0100) · [the appeal for help, f. 50r](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0101) · [letter to del Vasto, ff. 46v–47r](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0094).

The register’s next dispatch on Strozzi, dated 5 May, begins by recalling what Ercole wrote to Ferrante the previous day in cipher. It reports that Strozzi has now passed the Po, that the count of Mirandola’s men mean to cross at Luzzara, and that their artillery is to accompany them. This answers precisely the question still open at A79r 3–6. [ff. 51v–52r](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Barb.lat.5792/0104).

Novi Ligure’s municipal inventory records a six-leaf dossier on the subsequent defeat of Strozzi. Its historical note says he had assembled six thousand infantry around Mirandola. This independently supports the report of *s[e]i milla* at A78v 18, where the middle cipher sign lies under a blot. [Dimitri Brunetti, *L’archivio storico del Comune di Novi Ligure*, p. 125, no. 40.8](https://www.comuni-servizi.it/noviligure/upload/noviligure_ecm8/gestionedocumentale/libro%20archivio%20Novi%20-%20inventario_784_4485.pdf?ID=4485).

## Printed sources checked, 25 November 2026

- Arturo Segre, *Un registro di lettere del cardinale Ercole Gonzaga*, in *Miscellanea di storia italiana*, 3rd series, XVI (1913), pp. 273–458. The register he edits covers 1535–36. His appendix ranges more widely, but its chronology jumps directly from 11 December 1537 (no. 111) to 3 October 1548 (no. 112). The Internet Archive OCR was inspected at the document headings. Neither target occurs there. No. 111 and its note also identify an *Abbatino* who served the duke of Mantua as secretary and was sent to Milan. [Segre’s edition](https://archive.org/details/miscellaneadistoriaitaliana47).
- Giampiero Brunelli’s entry on Ercole quotes another letter to Ferrante, 6 May 1544, on his policy toward the Emperor. The entry names del Vasto as the commander to whom Ercole refused quarters. The quotation is from *Mantova. La storia*, II, p. 316. Only the quotation and reference in Brunelli were inspected. [*Dizionario biografico degli Italiani*, “Gonzaga, Ercole”](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/ercole-gonzaga_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/).
- The guide to the original registers was Marco Iacovella’s study of the Gonzaga secretariats, § I.2.2. Its table locates the confidential registers for 1537–46 at the Vatican and distinguishes them from the ordinary letter-books at Mantua. The correspondence to Ferrante in the Gonzaga di Guastalla collection at Parma, described on pp. 25–26, remains an archival lead. No comprehensive printed edition of either cipher text was established in this search. [Iacovella, table on pp. 27–28](https://ricerca.sns.it/retrieve/e3aacdfd-f266-4c98-e053-3705fe0acb7e/Tesi-Iacovella.pdf).
