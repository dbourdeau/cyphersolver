## English

"Your Excellency will have learnt from my other letters, the common ones and mine, what conclusion His Majesty the
Lord King of Hungary had reached: that in effect he meant to work for the ruin of the Venetians, and would give the
League certain victory, provided the League gave him money for the first year, paying half of ten thousand horse and
ten thousand foot, and for the two following years money for a third of all these troops; for with a smaller number he
will not take up the enterprise, because [he wants to do the thing thoroughly]..."

## Why each word that is not sure is classed as it is

- {conclusione}: first sign read "-1" (a) blind; it is the looped con sign. `clusione` read as written.
- ‹de›: a clear `φб` group between "conclusione" and "haveva"; 7a has nothing there.
- {facto}: `8-1|ρ3`; the stroke of "-1" (a) is faint, read ω (i) blind.
- [la Maestà del S. Re de Hungaria]: one group of 6-7 signs (`4uſɔʒρρ` in the blind notation) that the alphabet does not
  read; 7a gives the royal title. Possibly the K group of `repeated_k.md`; not compared here (no I5655 image).
- {volere}: the low angular e of `key_working.md`, read as two signs blind.
- [attendere]: every sign read except one tall upright with a diagonal, which stands for tt here. New value, proposed by
  analogy with the ff and ss signs; not added to the key.
- {Venetiani}: the X group (`3ί2ии`) of `verified_groups.md`, referent verified there.
- [indubitata]: i-n-d-u ... i-t-a read; the b (`>`) read as g, the ending as "di".
- {dummodo}: one d read as m; wrong word division blind.
- [dacessino]: d-a ... o read; the middle signs not held on their shapes.
- {dinari}: ω/б look-alike.
- {pagasseno}: the short looped ss sign of `key_working.md`, read as r blind.
- {mita}: a stray stroke read as a.
- [dece] twice: the same third sign both times, read g blind; it stands for c here. Not added to the key.
- {milia} twice, {pede}, {vol}: spellings of the cipher (7a millia, pedi, vole).
- [anni]: first sign read p blind, a expected; "ani" by sense.
- {sequenti}: split in two words blind; looped Δ = q.
- {terzo}: stemless triangle = z (`z_control.md`), read p blind.
- ‹cum›: the `┴` sign, a in the key; 7a has cum. Perhaps a con/cum sign (compare {conclusione}); not settled.
- [perche]: an abbreviated group at the line end; line 9 goes on with "vole fare".
