K1 (30 Jan 1918, Lansing): August Thyseen article in JAPAN CHRONICLE of December eleventh created widespread interest here. Please request CHRONICLE state source of article and what the evidence of its authenticity. Telegraph reply.
K2 (7 Feb 1918, Frazer): Although repeated by the telegraph company, the first three message words your cipher telegram January 30, 5 p.m. mutilated and unintelligible. JAPAN CHRONICLE inform me the principal assertion in their editorial December eleventh based on statements widely published by Japanese press some years ago. FRAZER.
K4 (8 Feb 1918): Your February 7 ten a.m. Department's telegram of January 30, five p.m. refers to an article credited to a German merchant named August Thyseen which was quoted in the CHRONICLE of December eleventh. What evidence of its authenticity.
K5 (9 Feb 1918, Frazer): Your January 30, 5 P.M. CHRONICLE copied article from a Hong Kong newspaper which got it from the GLASGOW POST, Scotland, of unknown date. FRAZAR.
