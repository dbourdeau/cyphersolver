# R5586 (cart. 1598 no. 112), 8 February (Florence) (English)

[?] here with the Florentines there has been no [?] on behalf of messer Ludovico that I know of, to bargain over his [...] who gave the lord such news, but rather he suspected that I was {informer?} [?] true, and that I have been [?] certain [?] and {?} that, had I heard anything of substance, I would at once have informed Nicodemo, as I did of the three things [?]. I have indeed encouraged messer Ludovico to leave those lands for more [?] rest ... and often [?] Nicodemo, with him and with others, only to set right the lord's mind as I wish. Perhaps he would have done it, the restitution of [?] having been recalled ... [...] reason; and it seems to me that these men would not enter into such business without the lord's consent; I believe the same of the duke of Modena, also towards [?] of Madonna's mind [...]. Messer Ludovico, who because of the death of messer Ieronimo [...] would be [?] the lands of the Florentines [?]. Iovan Galeaso seeks through my messenger to have leave from messer Ludovico; not being able to refuse the errand, I shall discourage the matter or inform you; if messer Ludovico should nevertheless incline to the money, I shall leave here within four days. Florence, 8 February.
