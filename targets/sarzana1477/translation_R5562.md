# R5562 (cart. 1598 no. 88), 5 December (English)

I was in Florence recently, and I informed Nicodemo of everything I heard, to pass on to the lord. I found Iovan Galeasmo in that city, come to look for arrangements with the Florentines, raising soldiers, or with the lord; his demands are great, and with him was a {servant} of the archbishop, sent to sound things out here. [?] desire and expectation of new things; but if the lord should nevertheless see fit to make some arrangement with messer Ludovico, take care that his lordship has it guaranteed through Tomasino, who means to temper the affairs of Corsica at the first important movements that occur. In Florence I found things very much tangled; I write nothing more of it, since Nicodemo is there, whom I also informed of a Venetian ambassador who, according to what I had from Iovan Galeasmo, was to come to conduct negotiations in Florence and then go to Rome. [Place?], 5 December, etc.
