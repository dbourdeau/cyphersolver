# R5573 (cart. 1598 no. 99), 23 March (English)

Tomasino's messenger has returned from Corsica; he said he had found and left those affairs at peace, but that {he was} thinking of moving for his own advantage. The things that happened here seemed to me like those of [?]; [?]; I shall be in Milan after Easter and give fuller satisfaction. Sarzana, 23 March, etc.
