# R5575 (cart. 1598 no. 101), 20 February (English)

I reply to yours received yesterday. Nicodemo has always encouraged messer Ludovico concerning the lord's favour, but he has {written} to him, and sent word to me, that certain go-betweens who stand between the lord and him are the reason his affairs do not go well. Because of such reports messer Ludovico has for many days been suspicious of his own people, and he often showed me Nicodemo's letter; so, seeing him in such a mood, I arranged for the appointment to the chancellorship of Volterra to be sent to me, which I accepted not because I wanted it, but to remove from myself every hint of suspicion; and so it has turned out. The term does not begin until October, nor shall I go there even then, if I can avoid it; meanwhile I shall follow our arrangement with Nicodemo. I do not think you need make any other provision, because I think I have removed every rancour, also with messer Ludovico; and I regret having accepted the said office; nevertheless do as you think best. Iovan Galeaso told me these days in Florence that he had had a letter from Iovan Coscia, who wished to send one of his men to duke Iovani for a good reason, and that he wanted to send one Piero del Cerreto, a servant of the archbishop, {?}, familiar with and practised in the language and the country. Sarzana, 20 February, etc.
