# R5572 (cart. 1598 no. 98), 13 March (English)

Messer Ludovico has sent to Siena one Bartolomeo Borella, a Sienese, to find out and to negotiate, according as he learns how things pass; he would be glad to be made governor of Siena by Madonna the duchess, she keeping the Castelletto, and about this he is working through Piero de Cosmo, {messer Andrea?} dal Maino and others. Tomasino is sending one Marcelino to Corsica to raise the Corsicans; nevertheless, if he could make some honest settlement with Madonna, he would do it willingly. Sarzana, 13 March, etc.
