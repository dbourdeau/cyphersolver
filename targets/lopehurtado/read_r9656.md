## f. 334v cipher runs
- l1 `ʃ zan zʇ ʃu 8 ... ⊃ ... /` then the clear "Despues que escrevi a v. Mt dizen que Roma esta muy mejor. De que el papa esta muy contento". Run open.
- B: "hame mandado que torne a escrevir a v. Mt sobre `∂6 ʃta xul zar ∂∂8∂7`" = (B1) "sobre lo de ..."; `xul zar` = lo de.
- "y su Sd me ha dicho que escriva a v. Mt largo. / Lo de don Juan mande v. Mt satisfazer" (clear)
- `ton yof xorz 7∂∂ 8 ʇ8 z 3 ∂8 m yub taf ton zar` = que esta [muy sospechoso] el papa que de [alla] (B3-B4). `xorz` + spelled run = "muy sospechoso" (probable).
- `zub ʃu xil ∂̶77 ⊤74x ... ɡ8 xul ton xic xod / ɡ 3 8` = [..] se le [haya mandado] lo que ha fe-cho (B4): `xod` = fe and `xic` = ha, each now with a second context.
- Section B ends with the clear "guarde nuestro señor ... De Roma xxiij de noviembre 1522".

## Running reading (f. 334)
"The archbishop of Bari did not write to the Pope [any certain] news of the coming of the French, nor to anyone but
me; as far as I have been able to understand, everything was to refer to the man he is to send. The letters came by
a courier ... the Pope did not [..]. About the coming of the French, I asked His Holiness [..]; if they come [..]. He
told me that in everything he knows of the Pope's will, he will not fail Your Majesty ... he has told the Pope that
if the French come it will be to their harm ... [per the copy:] ... for peace ... the Pope is very content ... he
told me to write to Your Majesty at length. About Don Juan, Your Majesty should give satisfaction, because the Pope
is very suspicious that he was ordered from there to do what he has done."
