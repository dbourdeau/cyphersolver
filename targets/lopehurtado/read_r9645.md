## Conflicts with the 1524 key
- `yub` is glossed 'duque' in "al duque de Albania" (`zu yub zar`), but 1524 has yub = el. Either the glossator supplied 'duque' or the 1522 group differs.
- `ʒ` = c in three words (concertado, escoceses, hechado) but r in 'hizieron'. 1524 has ʒ = p (probable). At least two signs are being read as `ʒ`.
- `8` = b in 'Albania' and o in 'tos', but 1524 has 8 = e. These are probably misread signs.
- `zƀo` = na, but 1524 has xur = na.
- `ʃ` is used as the conjunction y, but 1524 has y = ʑφ.

## What stays unread and why
f.243r has no glosses, and neither do the upper f.243v cipher runs. Their code groups decode, but the letter runs between them do not form words at this resolution. The top of f.243r, under the show-through, is illegible. A larger RAH image of ff. 243r-v is needed.
