## Page 1

From each place I have the honour to send you my deep respects [795]. And I believe my letters written from Vilna have reached your hands. [775]

I am now with Mr Siruc, Castellan of Witebsk, my brother-in-law, six leagues beyond Kowno.

The news from this place is that Count Apraxin is still at his headquarters at [?Ludwinow], seven leagues from the frontier of Prussia, and it is believed he will not enter Prussia so quickly.

The shortage here is extraordinary, especially of [?flour], and even all the pastures around where the army passed, for three leagues, are completely ruined by a great number of horses.

Great provisions are carried continually from the general magazines of Kowno, of [?Skorul] and of Wilkomierz to the Russian army. This transport is devastating the country, taking horses and men without pay to carry its provisions.

Men are [?beaten], and it is very hard to get satisfaction for the damage done by the Cossacks and Kalmyks.
