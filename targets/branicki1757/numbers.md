# The number codes

Two codes are in these letters.

- Letters 446, 1398, 1399 and 1400 share one table. Its numbers run in alphabetical order, with a block of number words near the end.
- Letter 1401 has its own cipher and its own small code. Its numbers are not in alphabetical order.

## The rule for a value

A value is fixed when one of these holds:

- the clear text of the same letter names the same thing in the same place;
- the value fits every use, and it is used two or more times;
- a contemporary text confirms it, such as Matuszewicz's memoirs.

In the French table the value must also fit its place in the alphabetical order. A value that meets none of these stays a guess, and the reading shows it as `[?word]`. A history written later, such as Konopczyński (1909), can support a guess, but it does not fix it.

Letter 131 of the same fonds uses the same key and table. Its uses count as evidence here.

## Number words

Numbers 805 to 824 are 20 number words, in order.

| Numbers | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 805 to 813 | 1 to 9 |
| 814 to 822 | 10 to 90, in tens |
| 823 | cent |
| 824 | mille |

The letters use these:

- 805 in 1399: "au [805] d'août prochain", the first of August.
- 806 and 807 in 1399: "tous les [807], les [806] Bohuszowie", three and two.
- 809 in 1399: "[809] seigneurs", followed by exactly five listed shares.
- 806, 823, 824 in 1400: "[806] [823] [824] francs", two hundred thousand francs.
- 810, 811, 818, 823, 824 in 1400: the date line, "mil sept cent cinquante six".
- 816 in 1399: "en datte du [816] du passé", the thirtieth.
- 819, 824 in 1398: "[819] [824] hommes", sixty thousand men.

## Not words

- The series 830 to 850 falls at clause ends, and it acts as punctuation.
- Numbers from 851 up look like nulls.

The readings leave these out. The decryption files keep them.

## Letter 1401: fixed values

| Group | Value | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 125 | Stanisław | 12 uses, and every one fits King Stanisław: France cannot keep 125 on the Polish throne, 125 must abdicate, 125 keeps the royal title for life. Stanisław abdicated in January 1736. |
| 455 | August | 2 uses: the reign of 455 begins only after the abdication, and 455 will not reign before it. |
| 136, 142 | Warszawa | "do [136] dla porozumienia się tamże", "w [142] ... negocyacya". The clear text beside them names Warszawa twice. |

Two groups are not code. They are written without dots, and they spell words in the letter key:

- 435 is 4.3.5, "ham", twice: "z zaczepki Moskwy przez [435]", "przeciwko Tatarom y [435]".
- 4345 is 4.3.45, "han": "ieżeli [4345] in hunc finem nie są iuż w marsie".

Both are the Khan (Polish Cham, Chan), in a passage on the Turks and the Tatars. Each splits into key numbers in one way only. The reading writes them as written, "Ham" and "Han".
