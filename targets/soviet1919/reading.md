# Reading: German and English

Seven letters sent by communists in Germany to Soviet officials in Russia, December 1919 – February 1920. NARA RG 59, M336 roll 75, frames 0462–0464 (naId 574886726).

## r75-0462-1 — Anfang, 23 December 1919

**Summary.** The air consignment arrived; the writer asks for letters by Lenin, Trotsky and Zinoviev, rejects Osipoff as unreliable, and asks for Koriniez’s recall.

**Status.** Read; a contemporary English translation is in the same State Department file (frame 0469).

**German.** Flugsendung gut angekommen. Material nicht wiederholen, neues senden. Erwünscht wiederum Handschreiben von Lenin, Trotzki, Zinowiew für Italien, Frankreich. Osipoff bestimmt nicht mehr senden: unzuverlässig, unkonspirativ. Sendet statt ihm einen absolut sicheren Deutschen. Koriniez: Adresse ungültig; er selbst muss von euch abberufen werden, unfähig, schädlich.

**English.** Air consignment arrived safely. Do not repeat material; send new. Again wanted: letters in the hand of Lenin, Trotsky and Zinoviev for Italy and France. Definitely do not send Osipoff any more: unreliable, not conspiratorial. Instead of him send an absolutely reliable German. Koriniez: address void; he himself must be recalled by you; incapable, harmful.

**Notes.**
- A group after TSUWI is struck out on the copy and is not counted in the 296 letters. The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; 33 letters of the typed copy are blurred or mistyped, mostly M for N and K for E.

## r75-0462-2 — Fortsetzung 2, 23 December 1919

**Summary.** The writer reports couriers from Riga bringing only old material, gives safe courier addresses in Berlin, and says the Commissariat demands material.

**Status.** Read in part (about 91% of the letters).

**German.** […] auf eigene Faust nach Schweiz, Moskau gereist. Bericht Italien wahrscheinlich dort erhalten. Er höchst verdächtig. […] Neben Else Kuriere Olga, Hilde aus Riga angekommen, altes Material gebracht. Eine Kurieradresse Berlin: […]zierski, Regensburger Str. 9, Pension von […], absolut sichere. An Kopp, Fasanenstr. 27. Kommissariat verlangt Material; unmöglich. Doppelt senden. Trifft Arrangement Lewin–Feodoroff. Provokation […] jetzt frei.

**English.** […] travelled on his own initiative to Switzerland and Moscow. Report on Italy probably received there. He is highly suspicious. […] Besides Else, the couriers Olga and Hilde have arrived from Riga and brought old material. A courier address in Berlin: […]zierski, Regensburger Strasse 9, boarding house of […], absolutely safe. To Kopp, Fasanenstrasse 27. The Commissariat demands material; impossible. Send in duplicate. The Lewin–Feodoroff arrangement applies. Provocation […] now free.

**Notes.**
- The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; 89 letters of the typed copy are blotted or mistyped.
- KOMISSARIAT is spelled with a single M.

## r75-0462-3 — Fortsetzung 3, 23 December 1919

**Summary.** The writer reports negotiating with and paying Sylvia, says Litvinov answers neither couriers nor letters, and asks for Dzerzhinsky’s confidential agent.

**Status.** Read; a contemporary English translation is in the same State Department file (frame 0469).

**German.** Reisen laut Bestimmung. Pochitonoff angeblich Schweiz. Erwarte Mitteilung Ranson. Unbekannt, wem Geld übergeben. Habe mit Sylvia hier verhandelt, Geld gegeben. Brauchen mehr Handschriftliches englisch von ihr. Litwinoff reagiert nicht, weder Kuriere noch Briefe. Sehr wünschenswert Dzierzinskis Vertrauensmann hier. Große Möglichkeiten.

**English.** Journeys as arranged. Pochitonoff allegedly in Switzerland. Awaiting word from Ranson. Not known to whom the money was handed over. Have negotiated here with Sylvia; money given. We need more handwritten material in English from her. Litvinov does not respond, neither to couriers nor to letters. Dzerzhinsky’s confidential agent here is highly desirable. Great possibilities.

**Notes.**
- Three groups (15 letters) are missing from the typed copy after LMBME; the letters they carried (plaintext column 6, rows 1–4, and column 17, rows 5–15) are supplied from the sense: the N of Reisen, the I of Pochitonoff, the W of Schweiz, the N of Mitteilung, and B, R, B, S, C, A, I, W, I, N, E in übergeben, hier, gegeben, Handschriftliches, englisch, reagiert, Kuriere, wünschenswert, Dzierzinskis, Vertrauensmann, Möglichkeiten.
- The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; 35 letters of the typed copy are blotted or mistyped.
- BRIEFFE is read as Briefe.

## r75-0462-4 — Fortsetzung 4, 23 December 1919

**Summary.** The secretariat urgently asks for money and material, names its newly elected members, and reports contact with Holland and its own publications.

**Status.** Read; a contemporary English translation is in the same State Department file (frame 0470).

**German.** Sendet Geld! Italien, Frankreich braucht dringend. Gut gehen große Perlen, in England Saphiren. Sekretariat bittet dringend Material. Geld zentralistisch zusenden vor Konferenz. November Sekretariat gewählt: Paul Levi, Bronski, Zetkin, ich Sekretär. Kontakt mit Holland, dort einverstanden. „Kommunistische“ erscheint hier, auch gebe heraus „Russische Korrespondenz“.

**English.** Send money! Italy and France are in urgent need. Large pearls sell well; in England, sapphires. The Secretariat urgently asks for material. Send money centrally before the conference. In November the Secretariat was elected: Paul Levi, Bronski, Zetkin, myself as secretary. Contact with Holland; agreed there. The Communist appears here; I am also publishing the Russian Correspondence.

**Notes.**
- ZETKINICH is read as Zetkin, ich; the contemporary English translation has Zetkinich.
- The ciphertext ends KORRESPONDEN, without the final Z.
- The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; three letters of the typed copy are blotted, and the fourth letter of DDN?Ä is illegible (E in the plaintext).

## r75-0463-1 — Fortsetzung 5, 23 December 1919

**Summary.** A branch is set up in Vienna and contact made with all countries; a congress is due in January or February, and Radek or Bukharin is needed.

**Status.** Read; a contemporary English translation is in the same State Department file (frame 0470).

**German.** In Wien Filiale geschaffen. Von Carlo Moment keine Nachricht; er arbeitet vorzüglich. Abramowitsch, Paris, war dort. Bereits von hier Verbindung mit allen Ländern. Es geht schön vorwärts. Januar, Februar bestimmt Kongress. Radek wird ausführlich euch berichten. Absolut nötig hier Radek oder Bucharin. Bitte größere Summe, sobald fertig. Kopp momentan kann tatsächlich nicht. Ich heiße jetzt Thomas. Gruß, James.

**English.** Branch set up in Vienna. No news from Carlo at the moment; he works excellently. Abramowitsch, Paris, was there. Contact already established from here with all countries. Things are going forward well. Congress certainly in January or February. Radek will report to you in detail. Radek or Bukharin absolutely needed here. Please send a larger sum as soon as it is ready. Kopp really cannot at present. My name is now Thomas. Regards, James.

**Notes.**
- The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; 107 letters of the typed copy are blotted or mistyped. SCHÖN rests on two of them.

## r75-0463-2 — Schlussmitteilungen, 25 December 1919

**Summary.** Closing messages: Fedoroff’s stones handed over for France, a plea not to fund individual countries, and a request to support mainly the party press.

**Status.** Read; a contemporary English translation is in the same State Department file (frame 0470).

**German.** Fedoroffs neun Steine habe ich für Frankreich Abramowitsch gegeben. Fedoroff geht dorthin zur Verfügung unseres dortigen Kollegiums. Alle Zeitungen für euch gründlich nachschauen: absichtliche Unordnung, Material darin. Nochmals: In Anbetracht der Verluste durch Korruption bitten wir, einzelnen Ländern keine Gelder zu senden. Wir ersuchen um eine größere Summe für uns, somit für andere. Bei Brillanten entstehen Verluste, wenn kleine Kohlenstücke darin sind. Hauptsächlich Unterstützung der Parteipresse aller Länder, weniger der Organisationen. Salatt, Tscheche, beruft sich auf Balabanoff. Auftrag auf Zürich [?]. Bitte Antwort. Gruß. Jetzt Thomas.

**English.** I have given Fedoroff’s nine stones to Abramowitsch for France. Fedoroff is going there to be at the disposal of our local council. Examine all newspapers intended for you thoroughly: the disorder is intentional, and material is inside. Once again, in view of losses through corruption, please do not send funds to individual countries. We request a larger sum for ourselves and thus for others. Diamonds lose value when they contain small carbon particles. Support mainly the party press in all countries, and the organizations to a lesser extent. Salatt, a Czech, invokes Balabanoff. Order on Zurich [?]. Please reply. Regards. Now Thomas.

**Notes.**
- Zürich [?] is read from the letters ZURUCK; the contemporary English translation has “Order on Zuruch.”
- The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; 36 letters of the typed copy are blotted or mistyped; the two blotted letters of PL??F are illegible (S and E in the plaintext).
- The group GEFSEUOGNI has ten letters.

## r75-0464 — Zusatz, 19 February 1920

**Summary.** The writer reports the failed Holland conference and arrests, police pressure on the secretariat, a completed radio station, and urgent need of money for Bulgaria, Serbia and France.

**Status.** Read.

**German.** Konferenz Holland dank Unvorsichtigkeit Dortiger reingefallen. Klara und Sylvia verhaftet. Holländische Presse spricht von Brillantenverkauf durch Unsere und von zwanzig Millionen. Beschluss Exekutive hier auch. Merkliche vermehrte Tätigkeit Polizei gegen Sekretariat. Bronskis Photos unaufgeklärterweise in ihren Händen. Vermutung: durch Polen bei euch. Unlängst wurden verhaftet Lewi und Alexander. Berichtet, ob bei euch Stenographisten, System Stolze-Schrey oder Gabelsberger, sind; würde Stenogramme senden. Habe Aussichten, als Begleiter einer ärztlichen Mission oder Delegation Auswanderer zu euch zu kommen. Bitte um Meinung, ob euch erwünscht. Radiostation endlich ausgebaut, Fachmann engagiert, beginnen nächste Woche aufzunehmen. Wir benötigen Gelder dringend für Bulgarien, Serbien, Frankreich. Delegierten hier warten. Triestiner mit Geld Italien angekommen, er in Wien verhaftet. Geld hier, geht demnächst nach Italien. O[?]galski auf der Reise nach Amerika mit Geld hier angekommen. Deutsche Regierung bewilligt Tausch Journalisten, von hier Frankfurter Zeitung. Benützt Gelegenheit. Dringend nötig: tüchtige Kraft.

**English.** The conference in Holland failed through the carelessness of people there. Klara and Sylvia arrested. The Dutch press speaks of the sale of diamonds by our people and of twenty million. Executive decision here too. Noticeably increased police activity against the Secretariat. Bronski’s photos inexplicably in their hands. Suspicion: through Poles with you. Lewi and Alexander were arrested recently. Report whether you have stenographers with you, system Stolze-Schrey or Gabelsberger; I would send shorthand notes. I have prospects of coming to you as escort of a medical mission or of a delegation of emigrants. Please give your opinion whether this is welcome to you. Radio station finally built, specialist engaged; we begin receiving next week. We urgently need funds for Bulgaria, Serbia and France. Delegates are waiting here. The man from Trieste with money for Italy has arrived; he has been arrested in Vienna. The money is here and goes to Italy shortly. O[?]galski, on his way to America, has arrived here with money. The German government approves an exchange of journalists, from here the Frankfurter Zeitung. Use the opportunity. Urgently needed: a capable man.

**Notes.**
- The transcription gives the ciphertext as it fits the decryption; 115 letters of the typed copy are blotted or mistyped. One letter of the name O[?]galski is not legible.
