# Harley MS 286 f. 61 (DECODE R7759): Sir Simonds D'Ewes's cipher log of his son's fits, 1635-36

Status: read (96.5% of words, measured). Key recovered (key.md). Written up 22 Sept 2026 as `docs/harley286.html`.

## What it is

- DECODE R7759: "Unknown sender to unknown recipient, 1635, Non-decrypted, cipher type unknown", BL Harley MS 286
  f. 61; catalogue item 130 (class B). Two images: p1 the slip, p2 verso show-through only.
- The BL online catalogue (searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002046117) has "f. 61r: A short paper written by Sir
  Simonds D'Ewes, in his cipher" under **Harley MS 289**; DECODE says Harley 286. Discrepancy not resolved.
- Author: Sir Simonds D'Ewes (1602-1650), antiquary, in the private alphabet he invented at school (Autobiography).
  Not a letter: a private record, no recipient. Language Latin. Heading 1635 and margin dates 1635/1636 clear.
- Content: one entry per convulsion fit of his infant son Clopton D'Ewes (b. July 1634, d. Monday 9 May 1636),
  3 Apr 1635 to Jan 1636 (Julian), with day, hour, moon phase, severity; margin running count "huc usque xxxii".
  Autobiography and Correspondence (ed. Halliwell 1845, ii 143-144; lit/auto01.txt l. 6215-6250): "Forty and four
  convulsion fits he had undergone from the 3rd of April, in the year 1635, to the 4th of March then next
  ensuing"; blames a bad nurse, an issue opened in the neck, Dr Despotine, the rickets.

## Method

1. Images via DECODE cookie. BL catalogue identified D'Ewes.
2. Printed key searched: UMN 1915 thesis (conservancy.umn.edu item 037a7464-...) deciphered the 1622-24 diary
   (Harley 481) but prints no key table; Notestein 1923 and Bourcier 1974 mention cipher passages, no key.
3. Hand crib: recurring words read as plena luna, convulsio(nem), difficil-; month names and numerals gave the rest.
4. Checks: weekdays (3 Apr 1635 Fri, 23 Apr Thu, 23 May Sat = die Veneris/Iovis/Saturni) and moon phases
   (23 Apr full, 17/19 Jul full, 2 Aug new, 18 Aug full all match; 25 May "plena luna" and 12 Jun "decrescente"
   do not: numerals confirmed at 3x, D'Ewes's own slips). Autobiography confirms 3 Apr 1635 and the count.
5. Transcription pass 1 (subagent): 92.7%. Pass 2 on re-crops: 96.5%.

Files: transcription.tsv (glyphs, Latin, unread/total per line), cipher.txt (glyph column), key.md (alphabet),
reading.md (first working reading), profile.json.
