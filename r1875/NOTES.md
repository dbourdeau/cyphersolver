# DECODE R1875 — Sebastiano Foscarini (Escorial) to the Doge and Senate, 13 October 1761

Catalogue 265 ("... (Spain) to unknown recipient", ASVe IT ASVe 0045 010 (Serie) Busta 30 f. 178, 13 Oct 1761,
class C; DECODE status Non-decrypted, "homophonic; numerical").

**Result: read at the time.** The whole letter was enciphered; the Venetian chancery wrote its decipherment on the
despatch itself and struck the cipher lines through with heavy ink bars. Every word of the ciphered text is in the
decipherment. Transcribed here in `decipherment.txt`; the ciphered portion alone is `plain_ciphered.txt`
(312 words, 1,565 letters, measured). Key not rebuilt.

Sender: Sebastiano Foscarini, Venetian ambassador in Spain, signed. Recipient: Doge
Francesco Loredan and the Senate (Senato, Dispacci Spagna). Received 2 November 1761.

## Content

The court has moved to the Escorial and Foscarini has followed it. The broken-off peace talks between London and
Versailles (the Stanley–Bussy negotiation of 1761) feed endless conjecture in Madrid. The best-informed hold that
Spain can no longer look on while France is beaten in America and England's gains and sea power grow; others,
weighing Spain's army, navy and treasury, Charles III's peaceable temper and his ministers, think Spain will keep
its neutrality. The first answer that necessity overrides princes' wishes, and foresee a union with "another
court" to defend common interests — the Third Family Compact with France, signed 15 August 1761 and still secret.
Foscarini passes this on "stripped of any authority" but worth attention.

## Cipher

Digits in continuous runs, groups separated by points, many groups ending in 6, 7 or 9; the 1628 cipher of
Busta 30 (r1874) is a different system. The crittologia.eu page on Venetian nomenclators describes Foscarini's
ciphers: alphabet plus an orderly syllabary, no dictionary, decipherment written on the despatch and the cipher
barred to stop comparison. About 37 cipher lines carry the 1,565 letters. The bars make a group-exact
transcription hard (digits legible in part at full resolution); not attempted, since the reading does not
depend on it.

## Remaining gaps

None in the text. Two words struck by the decipherer on p. 3 (replaced interlinearly by "nel" and "negli") are
corrections, not text. The key is not rebuilt (outcome.key: none).
