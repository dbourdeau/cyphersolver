# Sir Edward Stafford to [Walsingham], 9 November 1586 (BL Harley MS 1582 ff. 76-77; DECODE R8504)

DECODE refill catalogue entry for "Sr. Ed. Stafford to unknown recipient", 9 Nov 1586 (rule-scored class C).
Worked 22 Sept 2026.

Outcome: **read.** The letter is a short holograph in clear English (f. 76r, signed "E Stafford"; f. 77 blank
apart from show-through). The only cipher is one word of seven signs on line 2, which reads **princes** with
Stafford's Walsingham letter key rebuilt in `harley1582r8500/` (same volume, same cipher).

## What the record is

DECODE R8504, 2 images: f. 76r text, f. 76v/77 blank. Opening (clear, read here): "Sr, I receaved as I was puttinge
penne to this paquet a paquet wth [cipher] from your honor sent into ...". The rest of the clear text (about ten
lines) is a courtesy/business note that is not needed for the cipher and is only roughly transcribed.
The recipient is not named on the page; "your honor" and the series (R8500-R8503, addressed to Walsingham) point
to Walsingham. DECODE "London" is the holding library, not the place of writing (Paris, Stafford's embassy).

## Reading

| ciphertext | key values (harley1582r8500/key.tsv) | reading |
|---|---|---|
| E Π x h ρ 6 II | E=p, Π=r (context), x=i, h=n, ρ=c (context), 6=e, II=s | princes |

Five of seven signs take the sibling key's values directly (E, x, h, 6, II). Π (an r-form, not the O+ sign) and ρ
(rho, u in R8501's single occurrence) are read r and c from the word; no other English word fits p?in?es.
Measured: 7 tokens, 7 distinct.
