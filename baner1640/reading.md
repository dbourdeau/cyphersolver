## Translation (sense)

Banér sent his letter of the 16th in duplicate, one copy [by] Jobst(?) [508], the other by Johann ... via [568]. He
has come to Hof and intends, as far as the reason of war and the enemy's countenance allow, "to march through the
Upper Palatinate and against the [442]" (the Bavarians/imperialists at Regensburg). He expects the enemy to gather
all his strength against him "and perhaps also draw in the [troops] acting in Silesia". Stålhandske is therefore to
"keep good watch, and as soon as [the enemy] ... [marches off] ..., follow him with good caution", and report from
time to time "through spies ..." on his and the enemy's situation, so that Banér, told "where he means to direct his
march", can come to meet him.
