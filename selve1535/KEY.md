# Selve's cipher (Venice, 1535–1536) — key as used

Base: S. Tomokiyo, cryptiana `francis.htm`, "Selve's Cipher" (`prior/francisSelve.png`), reconstructed from BnF fr.
3045 ff. 42–49. Extended here on fr. 3045 ff. 43v–48r and fr. 3091 f. 25 (marked +). Sign names are the look-alikes
used in `work/*.md` and `decode.py`.

Code: `/ X ʒ X / X` (fr. 3045 f. 44r) = a name, glossed "mons^r de Rang…" (Guido Rangone). A crossed ʒ-like sign before
"de la Forest" (f. 45r) is read as an abbreviation for "sieur" (probable). Numerals are written as figures.
