## Result (22 Sept 2026)

- **R4232 (5 July 1536), read**: 1,448 signs, 99.1% of non-null signs read (`R4232_signs.txt`, `READING_R4232.md`).
  The Emperor's plan against Marseille (a key to the kingdom; battle or capture; master of Provence, a land road
  Italy-Spain, the Mediterranean, Genoa-Naples-Sicily; the French galleys trapped in the port under guns on the
  hill of La Garde); de Vaulx's advice to send the fleet towards Naples; news from La Forest (the envoy at the
  Porte) of [N] May on an open breach with the Emperor; the Pope will grant Charles no tenths this year whatever
  Barbarossa does. The King's decipherer glossed four of the runs; every gloss agrees with the reading.
- **R3697 (14 Sept 1535), read in part**: 1,076 signs; unstruck cipher 94.9% (709/747), struck runs 40% (122/304),
  whole 79.1% (`R3697_signs.txt`, `READING_R3697.md`). A merchant must go to France by the Swiss road because the
  Imperial ambassador at Venice suspects him and has asked someone (Anthon[e] de Le[yve]?, not secured) to watch
  the other road; the same ambassador had urged him to go to the French court under colour of trade and learn
  how the King stood with the Emperor. Verso: a Hungarian matter (Hieronymus, Veszprém) and informants who report
  to the Turk through the merchant.
- Both documents together: 2,524 signs, 90.6% read; 97.7% of the cipher not under the decipherer's strike.

## Key (as used; Tomokiyo's table plus what the letters show)

Tomokiyo's table holds. Additions: a curled d-shaped sign = a (and u in "luy/pour" on v94); tall looped
∂-like = f; the slash serves c as well as l; the ℓ hook and the L/C hook both serve c and d; z is also t;
X plain = m and u, hatted X / looped = u/v; Ƶ = x; ʒ tailed = a/y, closed = i; q-shape = i; Latin R = o (1535
letter); β = v; hooked-top ‡ = o and h; ∽ = g and (1535) r; large lying S = short f. Nulls: small upright S,
dots, and **a null between doubled letters** (Suis·ses, ambas·sadeur, ar·rivé, af·faires, oc·casion, ac·cord,
al·ler, mar·chant, Empe·reur). One name code (6 signs, "/ X ʒ X / X") glossed "Rang…" (Guido Rangone); a crossed
sign before "de la Forest" read as an abbreviation for sieur (probable).
