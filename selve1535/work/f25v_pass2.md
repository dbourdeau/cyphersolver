# f. 25v, second pass (BnF fr. 3091, Selve to Francis I, Venice, 14 Sept 1535; DECODE R3697)

Image: `img/native_f28.jpg` (9037x5865), left half. **All coordinates below are NATIVE pixels** (not the
1100-px thumbnail coordinates of pass 1). Text block x ~1250-4200. Line centres (x-height): top cipher
lines y ~1150 / 1262 / 1376; lower block y ~3740 / 3848 / 3955 / 4060 / 4165. Strips were made at 1.3-1.45x
(4 pieces per line) and spot zooms at 2-3x. Past about 2x the native file only gets blurrier. Scripts are in the
session scratchpad (`st.py yc x0 x1 n scale up dn`, `z.py x0 x1 y0 y1 scale`).

Notation: the plain letter is the value of the sign. `.` = null, `●` = blot, `?` = unread, `[a|b]` = two readings,
`φ` = the doubling null. `ot` in the sign notes is Tomokiyo's `o+` (small o followed by a cross), which is **s**.
Pass 1 often wrote it `‡`/`S`, and that caused several misreadings. The page has four long diagonal
cancellation strokes. Where one crosses a sign, the sign takes on a long leg (an "R" or "k" look). Remove the
stroke before you read the sign.

## Lower block (not struck), margin figure "3"

Clear before (line y~3640): "... [?]nom(?) leur fust ung(?) ... chargé(?) de parler avecques ... entreprinse
cy dessus [struck: de par ce que(?)]". The line above it (y~3530) ends "... entendre(?) doubte. Et pour".

**L1 (y 3740)**
- signs: `n e S(f) a i l l o i e n t | p a s | d a d v i s | e r [e?] | e t | f a i(p-shape) r e | a d v i s e r | p(ʃ) a r● | d a u l t r e s | d e s`
- read: **ne failloient pas d'adviser et faire adviser par d'aultres des**
- confidence: high. `failloient` has the lying-S short f. In `faire` and `par` a diagonal cancellation crosses
  the p-shape (i) and the long ʃ (p), and that made pass 1's "R" and "k". The "k-shaped sign" is therefore **not**
  a new sign: it is ʃ = p under the stroke, followed by z = a and a blotted ꝏ = r. `d'adviser`: after `p ot`
  (is) come `ω ꝏ` and one small trailing `o`. That o is either part of the ꝏ or a stray e (1 sign doubtful).

**L2 (y 3848)**
- signs: `o c . c ●(u) r φ r a n c e s | d e c a | s a n s | e s p e [c] i f i e r | a u l t r e m e n t | e n t e n d r [e]`
- read: **occurrances de ça sans especifier aultrement entendre**
- confidence: high. `occurrances` = R / p ℓ ● ꝏ φ ꝏ z ▽ / ω ot. The first c is the slash, then a p-shape doubling
  null between the two c's, then the tall ℓ = c. The u is under a blot where a cancellation stroke crosses. φ is
  the null between the r's. `de ça` = L ω / z (slash = c). `sans` = ot z ▽ ot. `especifier` = ω ot ʃ ω [4-like
  hook = c] q S(f) p ω ꝏ: the lying S is the short f, and the c-sign is doubtful in shape but required.
  `aultrement` = 3 x° / 7 ꝏ ω x ‡ ▽ 7 (x = m). `entendre` = ω ▽ 7 ‡ ▽ L ꝏ(+e): the last e is merged into the
  ꝏ at the line end.

**L3 (y 3955)**
- signs: `[e] t l e s | a d v i s | q u i l z | d o n n e n t | a u | t u ● r c | e t | q u i l z | l u y | f o n t | d o n n e r`
- read: **et les advis qu'ilz donnent au Turc, et qu'ilz luy font donner**
- confidence: high. The e of `et` is only a faint stroke before the 7. `advis` = 3 L x° q ot. `qu'ilz` twice ends in
  a C with a long top bar and an inner loop, which is the circled z. `donnent`: a cancellation stroke crosses the
  second ▽, so it looks like R. `au Turc` = 3 ɵ | 7 x° ●(null blot) ꝏ /, with slash = c. `luy`: the slash is
  merged with the cancellation stroke, ɵ = u, tailed ʒ = y. `font` = S(short f) R ▽ 7. `donner` = L R ▽ ▽ ω ꝏ.

**L4 (y 4060)**
- signs: `p a r l e | m a r [o] c h a n t | s u s d i c t | [a] | q u a n d | e n o n t | q u e l q u e f o i s`
- read: **par le marchant susdict, [a] quand(?) en ont quelquefois**
- confidence: high for `par le marchant susdict` and `quelquefois`, medium for the middle. `marchant` =
  x z ꝏ [small o, extra] / ‡(h, hooked top) z ▽ 7. The extra o may be the second loop of ꝏ. `susdict` = ot x° ot L q / 7.
  `[a]` = a lone ʒ/3 before `quand`. As a it gives "a quand", which does not parse well. It may be a slip or null.
  `quand` = 6 x° ʒ ▽ + a big d-hook with a long top bar, crossed by a cancellation stroke. `en ont` =
  ω ▽ R ▽ 7: the second ▽ is drawn as a T with a bowl. `quelquefois` = 6 x° ‡ / | 6 x° ω S(f) R ʒ(i) ot.

**L5 (y 4165)**
- signs: `t e n u | p ● o p o s | c o m m [e] | d e u [x] | d e u s | s c a [u] r e [W] s | q ? ? ? (?)`
- read: **tenu propos comme(?) d'eux deux(?) sçaur[ez](?) ...** [4-5 signs unread], then clear "Quant(?) ... pour"
- confidence: `tenu propos` high (ʃ ● R ʃ R ot: the blot is a corrected r). The rest is low.
  - `comme(?)` = L + ‡ with a z-like hooked top (= o) + x x + a "4ℏ" group read as ω. `commun` is possible if the
    last group is x° ▽.
  - `d'eux(?)` = L ω + x° (blotted, cancellation stroke) + a 3-shaped sign read as the lying-8 x.
  - `deux(?)` = the C-with-long-top hook (d, as in `quand`) + ‡ x° ot. A z (ilz) reading of the hook gives nothing.
  - `sçaur..(?)` = ot / z + blotted ʒ with a superscript (u?) + ꝏ ω + heavy W + ot. It is the only W on the lower
    block. `sçaurez` would need W = z, and `sçaures` would need W = e.
  - `6 ‡ ʃ ɵ` (+ a cross that may be the cancellation stroke) is unread. It is probably `que`/`qu'...`, and the
    clear text begins right after it.

Clear after (y~4185 to 4290): "[struck clear: Quant(?) ... de] pour / continuer le propos dudict Duc ... dessus
davantaige, sinon que de la dicte paix aussy je / le[s](?) estoit force de sentir(?) ... le principe(?) qu'ilz
pourroient, comme ceulx qui attendoient a la ..."

## English gist

Lower block: *they did not fail to report, and to have others report, what happens here, without saying more
specifically; [one should] understand both the advice they give the Turk and the advice they have given to him
through the aforesaid merchant, when(?) they have sometimes spoken of it ...* The "they" are probably the
Imperial agents or the Venetians discussed in the clear text above. The merchant is the "marchant" of f. 25r
(r10). The Turk, "au Turc", is the Ottoman sultan. The last few words are unresolved.

Top run: the struck passage concerns a Hungarian affair. The cipher gives "amiablement" and "qu'il [a] de
Vesprim", and the margin note calls the man an ambassador, "Hiero[nimo]", with a charge concerning the bishopric
of Veszprém. This fits Hieronim Łaski, the envoy of John Zápolya. Łaski was never bishop of Veszprém, and the
note's second name word does not clearly read "Lasco", so the identification stays open. The f. 25r gloss
"Hieronymus" is the same man.

## Remaining gaps and why

1. **L5 end** (~16 doubtful and 7 unread signs, "comme d'eux deux sçaurez q..."): the signs are legible but the
   French does not settle. A cancellation diagonal crosses the "d'eux" group and the final group. The heavy W
   sign occurs here and nowhere else on the lower block. Not externally blocked: next steps are to compare with
   the ending of f. 25r's block 2 and with the 1536 letter's W/Z signs, and to test "commun"/"comme" and "sçaurez"
   against the clear sentence that follows ("pour continuer le propos dudict Duc").
2. **L4 "[a] quand ... en ont"**: the lone ʒ before `quand` has no clear role.
3. **Top struck lines T1-T3** (~120 signs): the horizontal strike runs through the x-height of every sign, and the
   Gallica native file (the largest available, ~2x the 4400 image) does not separate stroke from sign. This is an
   **external blocker** (image quality). It needs a sharper or colour/multispectral image from the BnF, or the
   original. The margin note reads in part and serves as a partial decipherment.

## Sign-table additions and corrections (from this pass)

- **ot (Tomokiyo o+) = s** is by far the most common s-sign on this page. Pass 1 often misread it as ‡ or S,
  which is why "d'aultres des", "sans", "especifier", "susdict" and "quelquefois" did not come out.
- **The "k-shaped sign" is not a sign.** It is a long ʃ (= p) crossed by a diagonal cancellation stroke (in "par").
  The same stroke turns p-shapes and ▽ into apparent "R"s (faire, donnent). Check for the diagonals before
  treating an R-with-leg as a new sign.
- **The C-with-long-top + inner loop** after "qu'il" is the circled **z** (qu'ilz ×2). A similar hook with a long
  top bar reads **d** in "quand" and, doubtfully, in "deux".
- **Heavy W** = **W/V** in "Vesprim" (T2). Its use in "en France" (f. 25r r8) and in L5 remains unexplained. It
  may be a double-value sign or a null there.
- **‡ with a z-like hooked top** = o in "comme" (L5) and = **h** in "marchant" (L4), as on f. 25r.
- **Slash / = c**: also in "de ça", "occurrances" (first c), "Turc", "especifier"(?) and "sçaur-".
- **Doubling null** confirmed again: a p-shape between the c's of "oc·currances" and φ between its r's.
- **ƀ (crossed f) = b** in "amiablement". **ʒ tailed = a, ʒ closed = i** in the same word.
- **x = m** (aultrement, marchant, amiablement, comme) and **x° / x‡ = u/v** (advis, qu'ilz, susdict, Turc).
- The lone **"3" after "susdict"** (L4) is unexplained: a slip or null.
