## English gist

Only fragments: the struck top passage concerns an ambassador "in Hungary" (marginal summary: "Hiero(nimo)
L..., ambassador for the charge he has of ... [the see of] Veszprém(?)"). The lower block speaks of people
"not of the opinion ... to advise ... otherwise to understand ... the advice they give ... the aforesaid ...
when ...". No continuous sense yet.
