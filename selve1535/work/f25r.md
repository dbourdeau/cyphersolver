`R` = a Latin capital R (= o, new); `β` = a b/β loop (= v, new); `ꟻ‡` = ‡ with a small hooked top (o, and h).

resolve; struck runs are only partly legible under the strike.

## English gist

Someone (the traveller discussed in the clear text) must go by the Swiss road, since the other road (through
Imperial territory) would not be safe: the Emperor's ambassador there, as soon as the man arrived, guessed he
had come [to go] to France and has had a watch kept to arrest him if he passes that way. In the second block,
the Imperial ambassador had told him he should find a way, or some other occasion, to make his journey to France
and go to the King's court, to learn how the King's affairs with the Emperor stood and whether there was any
agreement between them. The struck bottom passage (glossed) says that "he has spent his means and most of ...";
a gloss names "Hieronymus".

## Differences from the Tomokiyo table (and from the 1536 pages v84/v85)

1. **Null between doubled letters** — `φ` (a p-shape run into ω or oo) sits between the two halves of a doubled
   letter in every clear case (ss, rr, uu, cc, ff). This is the "blotted φ" of v85 l6/l8: not a blot but the
   doubling null. Also a "vf" hook sign in "ambas?sadeur".
2. **R (Latin capital R) = o** — new homophone (trouvast, occasion, court, vos, vous, ou, saison, point).
3. **β (loop + stem) = v** in "venu"; plain b = crossed f (ambassadeur).
4. **ꟻ‡ (‡ with a small hooked/3-like top)** serves o (seroit, pour, tost, coniecture, voyage) and h (chemin,
   l'heure). Tomokiyo's o "ɥ/24" is probably this sign; h £ looks the same.
5. **ʒ family**: tailed ʒ = a (a cause, avec, voyage "y" also tailed), closed-loop ʒ = i (chemin, Suisses);
   ʒ = y in "luy", "voyage". Decide by the French.
6. **Slash `/` = c as well as l** (dict, coniecture ×2, occasion, accord, France) — the v84 "ladicte" habit
   is systematic here.
7. **q-shape (9 with a descender) = i** alongside p (seroit, qui, dict, coniecture, estoient).
8. **Big S (lying S) = short f** (fust ×2, faire), as on v84/v85; no clear null S found on this page.
9. **x° (x with a dot/loop at the right) = u**; plain x = m (ambassadeur, Empereur, voyage? no). ɵ (looped o)
   = u/v (guet, pour?, trouvast, vos, avec).
10. `ω`/`W` = e throughout; a heavier "W" with a tall left stroke also appears (en France r8) unexplained.
