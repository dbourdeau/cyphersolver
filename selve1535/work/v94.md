## Continuous reading (clear words in brackets)

[... que sous(?)] le Pape l'avoit asseuré que l'Empereur n'auroit aucunes decimes de luy pour (?) quelque

## English gist

The Pope had assured him that the Emperor would get no clerical tenths from him to (resist/meet?) any landing
and armada that Barbarossa might make.

## Notes on the key

- The slash sign is used for c throughout this passage (aucunes, decimes, descente, face) as well as l (le,

- Crossed tall b in Barbarousse; tall looped ∂-like sign = f (face).
