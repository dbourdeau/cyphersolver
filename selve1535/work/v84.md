## Continuous reading (cipher in plain type, clear words in brackets)

[ville fut] en tres grand danger, combien que le nombre des assiegeans fust sans comparaison moindre que
celuy qu'il entend d'y mener. Et comme ainsi soit que ladicte ville soit prenable, comme il en faict son compte,
estant une clef de vostre royaulme si importante comme elle est, ce luy semble que, la voyant en dangier, vous
vous vouldrez tirer aupres avec vostre puissance pour la secourir. [Tourlucan(?) ainsy que son] allee a
Marseille sera cause de luy donner l'occasion du combat [qu'il desire pour sortir de ce pays dont il se treuve
chargé, ou bien le tiendra vostre puissance] loing, sans secourir ladicte ville, qu'il en viendra au dessus
et la conquestera, [qui est bien la chose que plus il desire, car elle le feroit] patron de la Prouvence, et ne
luy resteroit [view 85: pas beaucoup ...]

## English gist

(Marseille) would be in very great danger, even though the besiegers [at Fossano] were beyond comparison fewer
than the army he [the Emperor] means to lead there. Since the town can be taken, as he reckons, and is so
important a key to your kingdom, he thinks that, seeing it in danger, you will move up near it with your army to
relieve it. So his march on Marseille will give him the chance of the battle he wants, to get out of this
country, where he finds himself burdened; or else it will keep your army at a distance, without relieving the
town, so that he will get the upper hand and take it, which is what he wants most, since it would make him master of
Provence, and there would not remain to him (much ... [continues on view 85]).

## Notes on the key

- `∂` (a d-like sign with a curl, not the plain 3/z): reads **a** throughout (ainsi l4, voyant l8, aupres l9,
  allee l11, au dessus l16). An a homophone not separately listed in the table (perhaps the "3 open" form).
- The short f `ʃ` is drawn like an S lying on the line with a flat hook ("Sx" in fust l2, clef l6, faict l5);
  distinguish it from the null S, which is smaller and upright (l1, l4, l6 end, l8, l12, l15).
- The looped sign transcribed `Ⴟ` reads u/v (celuy, une, royaulme, luy, voyant, vous, vostre, vouldrez, au,
  dessus, viendra); plain `X` also u/v (qu, vous, ville). Both homophones of u.
- `ℓ` hook serves both c and d (as the brief warns); decided by the French each time (danger, grand, moindre,
  de luy, du combat, entend = d; comme, compte, clef, occasion, avec, puissance, secourir = c).
- `ω` = e confirmed (en, danger, estant, ne, allee).
- `ʒ` = y in dy, celuy, luy, royaulme, voyant; the sign in "de l[a|y] Prouvence" has the same tail, read a.
- "ladicte" twice written l-a-d-i-/-t-e (slash sign where c is expected): a consistent cipherer's habit or the
  slash doubling as c.
