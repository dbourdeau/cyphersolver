[Il est force, Sire, qu'il] passe par le chemin des Suisses, car l'aultre ne luy seroit pas seur, a cause que
l'ambassadeur qui est [..] la pour l'Empereur, tost apres que le susdict marchant fust arrivé, comme des l'heure
[de(s)sus?] adverty [..] de coniecture qu'il fust venu pour aller en France, a pr[........] tant [.....] de le
[...] luy tenir le guet, pour le retenir s'il passe par [ledit chemin] ...

Sire, je vous ay bien adverty ... ledict Gueny(?) marchant, [a]insi(?) com[me] j'ay faict par plusieurs foys ...
qu'il luy est party de Venise que [..] [ledict] ambassa[deur] luy avoit dict qu'il seroit bon qu'il trouvast
façon, soubz(?) umbre de marchandise ou d'aultre occasion, de faire son voyage en France et aller en vostre court,
pour entendre en quelz termes estoient vos affaires avec l'Empereur, et s'il se faisoit point quelque accord
entre vous [.....] ...

## English gist

The man must go by the Swiss road, since the other would not be safe for him: the Emperor's ambassador there,
as soon as this merchant arrived, guessed that he had come in order to go on to France, and has had a watch
set on him to seize him if he passes that way. Moreover the (Imperial) ambassador had told him it would be good
for him to find a way, under colour of trade or some other occasion, to make the journey to France and go to
the King's court, to learn in what terms the King's affairs with the Emperor stood and whether some agreement
was being made between them. (So the "merchant" is being used by the Imperial side as an informant; Selve warns
the King.)

## Sign-table additions (this pass)

1. `~o` / `∽` stroke with a lead tail = **r** (marchant r6, a pr.. r8); the first-pass "small ∽/σ stroke".
2. Null before -eur / in "marchant": the null is not only between doubles — mar·chant (r6, r10), Empe·reur
   (r5, r15). Fixed spelling habits of the cipherer.
3. Heavy **W** = null in France (r8), umbre (r12), entre (r16); but = e in "de" (r13). Decide by position.
4. `ƀ` = b in "bon" (r11) and "umbre" (r12), with R = o.
5. Filled blot = a (marchant r6), n (marchandise r12), s (seroit r11): blots stand for real letters, not nulls.
6. `k`-shaped sign (r7 start) = the tall C/L hook (d) with a lead-in stroke, not a new sign.
