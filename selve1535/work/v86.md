## Continuous reading (clear words in brackets)

[Sire, ledict Seigneur] de Vaulx est tousiours d'oppinion que pour la sauvete de voz galeres [... two clear
lines ...] de mettre hors vostre armee de mer et l'envoyer vers Naples. [Ce qui ... seullement la vous]
pourroit conserver, mectant dessus nombre de gens de guerre suffisant. [Mais vous pourroit ...]

## English gist

The said lord de Vaulx is still of the opinion that, for the safety of your galleys, [...] you should put your
sea force out and send it towards Naples; this alone could preserve it (the fleet / the position) for you,
putting a sufficient number of soldiers aboard.

## Notes on the key

- Tall looped d-like signs (with ascender loop) = f in "suffisant" (l9); the ∂ with a curl = a (Vaulx, sauvete,
  armee), as v84.
- Looped Ⴟ and plain X are both u/v (Vaulx, tousiours, pour, sauvete, envoyer, vers, dessus, guerre).
- The lying-8 with a bar = x (Vaulx).
- Tall L hook = c and d (de, d'oppinion, conserver, mectant, dessus).
- £ (key h) = h in "hors" (l5), but a £-like crossed hook stands for d in "de gens" (l8).
- ɥ/4 = o throughout; 6 (a b-like form) = q; ω = e (mer et, guerre).
- A small overbarred v on l1 where t is needed: possible t homophone, not in the table.
