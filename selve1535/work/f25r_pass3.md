## Gap 1: r7 (y 2570-2690)

Signs re-read at 2-4x:

- First sign (x 5180-5330): **one sign**, not "L + lead stroke": a small closed loop at the left joined by a long
  horizontal top bar to a C-hook at the right. It is the long-topped C (d-hook) with its bar extended to a loop,
  i.e. the family of the d / circled-z signs. Neither d nor z gives a word with what follows. Unread.
- `ω S x° ot` = **e f u s** (S = short f, as in "fust" r6/r7). Read.
- `ʒ | x ω ꝏ 7 ʒ` = **adverty**: tailed ʒ = a; a tall upright (the L-hook, d); x crossed by the diagonal
  cancellation (its bar is hidden by the stroke) = v; ω; ꝏ; 7; tailed ʒ = y. "adverty" stands.
- `x ‹( x°` (x 6130-6340, 4x): x = m; then two small open bowls `‹(` which together are the ω (e) written open
  (the same two-bowl shape as ω elsewhere, lighter); then x° = u. **meu** ("moved"): "meu de coniecture".
  Not "par"; there is no ʃ or ꝏ.
- Reading: **[.]e fus adverty, meu de coniecture qu'il fust venu** (B). The first sign should be the subject
  ("ie"); the key has no j and no sign of this shape for i. Left open.
- Sense: "... l'ambassadeur qui est [..] pour l'Empereur, tost apres que le susdict marchant fust arrivé, comme
  des l'heure [je] fus adverty, meu de coniecture qu'il fust venu pour aller en France, a ..." The clause
  "comme des l'heure je fus adverty" is Selve's aside; "meu de coniecture" goes with l'ambassadeur.
- Count: 16 signs before "de coniecture" (1 + 4 + 7 + 3 + the null-free "de"); unread 1 (first sign).

## Gap 2: r8/r9 (y 2660-2900)

Signs re-read at 3-4x, with the cancellation diagonals removed by eye:

r8 after "France" (`/ ‡` = ce): `3 | ʃ ∂ ɜ ω x° 7 q ot | 7 3 ▽ 7 | ‡ 4 ▽ W`

- `3` = a (x 6480).
- `ʃ` long, descends below the line = p. `∂` (oval with a lead stroke at top left) + small hook `ɜ` below right:
  either one sign (the ~o = r of "marchant") or r + closed ʒ (= i, f25v usage). `ω` = e (ıu shape, as in
  "aller" on the same line).
- The pass-2 "x o" (x 6830-6900) is a **cancellation diagonal crossing a short rising stroke, followed by a raised
  small o**. Taken together it is x° (u); read separately it could be `/ o` (l e). Not a plain x (m).
- `7 q ot` clear (t, i, s). `ot` = the o+ sign (small ∽-shaped o + bold cross), not ‡.
- `7 3 ▽ 7` clear.
- The pass-2 "‡ x q" before `▽ W` is **not three signs**: at 3x the "x" is the diagonal crossing the slanted arm of a
  **4** (diagonal arm, cross-bar, long vertical through the bar: x 7520-7590). So `‡ 4 ▽ W`: ‡ (joined by a long
  bar to the preceding 7; h if it is the hooked form, e otherwise), 4 = o, ▽ = n, heavy W.
- Letters: **a p r [i] e u t i s t a n t [h|e] o n [W]**.

r9: `●(solid blot) | L ‡ | / ω | ʃ £ ‡ | £‡ | / x ʒ | 7 ‡ ▽ q ꝏ | ...`

- The blot at the line start (x 5290-5370) is solid at every gamma: a physical loss.
- `L ‡ / ω` = de le (A).
- `ʃ £ ‡` (x 5580-5700): a long ʃ with a hooked top joined by a bar to a curled-top ‡, then ‡. Unread.
- `£‡` (x 5700-5760): the same shape as the `L‡` = "de" that opens the line (hooked L + ‡); read **de**.
- So r9 runs "de Le[ʃ£‡] de luy tenir le guet pour le retenir s'il pass[e] par".

Hypotheses tested against these signs:

|---|---|---|
| a promis / a promet... | ʃ, ∂ = r | no o sign; `x° 7 q ot 7` is not "is" |
| a promesse / promettant | 7 _ 7 double t with nulls between | two signs between the t's; no null of that shape |
| a prié ... | ʃ ∂ ɜ ω = p r i e (∂ = r, ɜ = closed ʒ = i) | then `x° 7 q ot 7` is left over |
| tant a mine / ministres / emine | – | the "x" and "q" were not signs (see above) |

Reading: **a pri[é](?) [x° 7 q ot 7] Anthon[e](?) de Le[yve](?) de luy tenir le guet, pour le retenir s'il
pass[e] par** (C). The frame "the Imperial ambassador has asked [someone] to keep watch for him and seize him
if he passes by [the other road]" is secure from `de luy tenir le guet pour le retenir s'il passe par`; the name
and the five signs before it are not. "tant" (`7 3 ▽ 7`), read in pass 2, is **withdrawn** as a word: under the
name hypothesis its first 7 closes the preceding group.

Count: r8 after "France" 17 signs; read as sense 5 (a p r i e, doubtful) ; unread 12. r9: 1 blot (physical), 3
unread (`ʃ £ ‡`), the rest read.

## Gap 3: r11 opening, r12 "soubz", short gaps r5, r10, r16

**r11 `ω x ꝏ z ‡` (x 5310-5570, y 3680-3760).** At 2.5x the "x" is the long cancellation diagonal crossing a
descender from the line above; there is no x sign. The group is `ω ꝏ z ‡` (e r a e / e r a s), then
`ʒ x ƀ z ot φ ot z` = ambassa. Clear before: "Il luy est party de [B..ge?] que [struck clear: bi pous Gueny]".
The slot wants "l'", "ledict", "cest", "vostre", "Mr": none fits `ω ꝏ z ‡`. Unread, 4 signs, legible
(unresolved, not physical). (If the diagonal hides a real x, `x ꝏ` = "Mr" would give "Mr l'ambassa[deur]" with
ω and z‡ unexplained; not counted.)

**r12 "soubz" (x 6030-6340, y 3760-3830), now A-.** `ot | 4 | x° | ƀ | ⓩ`: o+ (s); a 4-shape (diagonal, bar,
descender) = o; a dark x with a raised o at top right = x° (u); a tall f-shape with a bar = ƀ (b); a closed loop
sign = the circled z. Every sign agrees with the key: **soubz umbre de marchandise**.

**r12 "façon" (x 5780-5980).** `●(f) z / R ▽` confirmed at 3x (blot = the short f, then a c o n).

**r5 `7 ∽o / 3` after "est" (x 5400-5620, y 2330-2420).** At 2.5x: a clear 7 (bar + descending stroke), an
o with a leading tail (the ~o shape), a short slash crossed by the diagonal, a closed 3 (a). Candidates:
"est de ça" (the long-topped C/d-hook can look like 7; f25v writes "de ça" `L ω / z`), "est de la". Neither is
secured: the first sign is shaped as 7, and ~o is r, not e, everywhere else on the page. Unread, 2 signs
(`/ 3` = ça or la is readable in either case); sense "who is here for the Emperor" not in doubt.

**r10 `p ▽ ● ‹` after "marchant" (x 7850-8000, y 3380-3440).** `p` (i), `▽` (n), a dark blot sign (s, blots
stand for letters on this page), a small open curl, then the clear "com[me]". "[a]insi comme" (the a not
written) stays the reading, B-: 2 signs doubtful (blot, curl).

**r16 end `ɵ R x ot | ▽ ot ʃ ω` + erased tail (x 6500-6960, y 4170-4240).** "vous" = `ɵ R x ot` (the x crossed by the
diagonal). Then a v-shaped sign (▽ n, or ɵ u), ot, a long ʃ (p), ω/ꝏ, and **a faint erased stroke run** to x ~7300
(visible at gamma 1.3 as ghost strokes only). The group was abandoned and erased by the writer: "n s p e.." /
"u s p e.." does not finish. Physical (erasure). 4 signs + erased tail.

## Gap 4: struck runs (the glosses first, then the signs)

### Run A, line 3 (x 5880-6560, y 1150-1230; gloss y 1110-1150)

- Gloss, re-read at 3x: **"au marchant"** (not "au marq[uis]"): a u, space, m a r, a looped ch, a n t. Clear.
- Signs under the strike, aligned on the gloss: `3 x° | x z [ꝏ] φ(p+ω) / £ z ▽ 7`
  = a u | m a r . c h a n t. The same spelling, with the p+ω doubling null between r and c, as "marchant" in r10.
  Shapes agree for 3, x°, x, z, p+ω, /, £ (tall), z, 7; ꝏ and ▽ are blurred by the stroke but occupy the right
  places.
- Reading: **au marchant** (A-). Sentence: "Je fais d[e] vostre pour [struck clear] au marchant, duquel je vous ay
  ... escript". 11 signs (φ as one), 11 read, 2 of them doubtful.

- Gloss, re-read at 2.5x: **"ung(?) sansacque cy prochain"** ("a sanjak-bey near here"). The first word is a u with a
  looped lead (pass 1 "bing"); "dy" of pass 1 is **cy**: the "d" is the descender of the g in the line above, and
  the remaining two letters are secretary c + y.
- Signs, aligned: `? ▽ ? | ot? z ▽ ot● z ℓ 6? x°? ω | / ʒ | ʃ ꝏ ɥ / £ z q ▽ | ∂ ‡ ʃ ꝏ`
  - **prochain** = ʃ ꝏ ɥ(4-shape, o) / £ z q ▽: all eight shapes agree (A-).
  - **cy** = `/` (under the stroke) + tailed ʒ (y) (B).
  - **sansacque**: the two z (a) and the ▽ (n) are clear in place, the final e is a round o (= e); the s, c, q, u
    positions hold blurred signs that do not contradict the key (C).
  - **ung**: ▽ visible; u and g not separable from the strike.
  - After "prochain", before the clear text: `∂ ‡ ʃ ꝏ` (d? e p r). No gloss covers it; unread.
- Count: ~25 signs; agree with gloss by shape 13; in place but not confirmed 8; unread 4.
