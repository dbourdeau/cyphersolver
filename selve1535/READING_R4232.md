## English gist

The Emperor means to besiege Marseille, a key to the kingdom, reckoning either that Francis will come to relieve
it and give him the pitched battle he wants, or will stay away and let it fall; with Marseille he would be master
of Provence, have a free land route from Italy to Spain, take the Mediterranean from France and secure Genoa,
Naples and Sicily. With his stronger fleet he expects to bottle up the French galleys in Marseille harbour, which
guns on the hill of Notre-Dame-de-la-Garde would command. De Vaulx advises sending the French fleet out towards
Naples with enough soldiers aboard. A rumour about the delay will die down; news from La Forest (the French
envoy at the Porte) of [N] May bears on an open breach of the peace between Francis and the Emperor. The Pope has
assured Selve that he will grant the Emperor no clerical tenths this year, whatever landing or fleet Barbarossa
may bring.
