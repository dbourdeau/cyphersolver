# Florence 1414: letters to the Signoria in the "Cifra Decemviri di Balìa 1414"

Status: in progress (blocked: the ciphertexts are not online; ASF photographs needed)

Session of 23 September 2026. Catalogue entry 335. Goal set by Daniel: read the 1414 letters past the read bar
(95%), as was done for the 1425 Ricasoli letter (`florence1429/`), where Gabbrielli had rebuilt only part of the key.

## What is known

- **The key (partial).** Meister 1902 p. 49, "Florenz, Beispiele 1: Cifra Decemviri di Balia 1414 (Lettere della
  Signoria risponsive; filza Nr. 1)", from ASF *Chiavi delle cifre* II, pars 3 no. 1, the key volume the abbé
  P. D. Gabbrielli compiled in 1864. Transcribed in `KEY.md`: 35 letter signs over 18 letters (h, k, m, z blank)
  plus three word signs (per = 8, et = f, che = dum), no nulls, no name nomenclator. Somogyi 2016 counts the same
  38 signs ("Fi1"). Like the 1424 table, it was built from words Gabbrielli could read, so it is a seed.
- **The letters.** Meister names no sender, date or letter number. Gabbrielli's own vol. II has a sender index
  (Meister p. 45), which is not online.

## Where the letters are (shelfmark worked out this session)

- Gabbrielli's "Lettere della Signoria risponsive, filza 1" is an **old number**. In the current ASF inventory of
  *Signori, Dieci di Balìa, Otto di Pratica* (N/9, `n9_inventario.pdf` on the ASF site), *Signori, Responsive*
  filza 1 is 1340 (22 May – 30 Nov), not 1414. The filze that can hold 1414 letters are **Signori, Responsive 7**
  (1408 July 28 – 16th c.) and **8** (1402 Feb 6 – 16th c.), both miscellanies, and the mixed series
  **Signori, Dieci di Balìa, Otto di Pratica, Legazioni e commissarie, missive e responsive** no. 8
  (1408 Oct 12 – 1428 Nov 20) and no. 4 (1402 July 31 – 1512 July 30). *Dieci di Balìa, Responsive* starts only
  in January 1424, so the 1414 letters are not in the DECODE Dieci series.

## Result

No ciphertext of 1414 is reachable from here, so nothing can be read or solved in this session. The key half of the
problem is in hand (KEY.md); the text half needs photographs of the letters.
