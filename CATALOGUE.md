# Catalogue of unsolved historical ciphers for future work

Compiled 17 Sept 2026 from (a) the Gallica/BnF catalogue harvest in `gallica_sweep/` (331 SRU records whose
descriptions mention *chiffre*; 225 items described as "avec chiffre" or "en chiffre" **without** a noted
*déchiffrement*), (b) items that S. Tomokiyo's cryptiana articles mark "undeciphered" but which do not appear on his
Unsolved Historical Ciphers list, and (c) this repo's own sweep of BnF fr. 16127. Everything below is **unvalidated
until reviewed**: entries marked ◇ were selected from catalogue descriptions only and the leaf has *not* been viewed;
entries marked ● have been viewed on the image (fr. 16127; fr. 3484 f. 34).

**Refill of 18–19 Sept 2026 (entries 34 onwards): the DECODE database.** With most of the first 33 taken to the leaf,
the catalogue was refilled from DECODE (de-crypt.org), through its JSON API with a logged-in session: all 10,106
records listed, full metadata fetched for the 1,187 ciphertext records marked *Non-decrypted* (802) or *Partially
decrypted* (385), grouped by archive series, sender, recipient and half-century, and merged where one sender wrote
from one series. Groups were dropped when they are already a target here (Sessa, Buda, Del Vasto, Gramont, Sormano,
Brèves, Moray, du Croc…) or already solved (Villeroi 1577 by Ryabov, Catherine of Aragon 1509 by the Bosbachs,
Sabran 1636 here); 27 groups in all, listed with reasons in `catalogue_harvest/decode/excluded.json`. The 238 entries
that remain were **scored by rule** from the metadata (a key cited in DECODE's notes or on the leaf → class A; a key
record from the same archive series → A within ten years, else B; length, language and cipher type → solvability and
difficulty; who wrote to whom and series length → importance) and are tagged *rule-scored* on the site until
reviewed by hand. Three archive-specific cautions are written into the entries: Vatican nunciature ciphers were
normally deciphered on arrival (*decifrati* filed or printed), BL Add MS 32253–32310 and TNA SP 106 intercepts were
usually deciphered at the time, and DECODE's status is sometimes stale (ACA Reserva 12 was *Non-decrypted* while its
own references cited the 1931 reading). The 1,146 ciphertext records DECODE marks *N/A* or leaves blank were fetched
but not yet sorted. Scripts and data: `catalogue_harvest/decode/`.

Failure mode first: "avec chiffre" in Omont's inventories means the letter contains cipher, not that it was never
read. The Court's decipherment may sit in another volume, a modern edition (Scheurer's du Bellay, Mousset's Longlée,
Savasse's Maisse) may print a clear text, and Tomokiyo may already have a key that reads the leaf even where he wrote
"undeciphered". Each entry therefore lists what would confirm it is genuinely open. Coverage checks were made against
Tomokiyo's unsolved list and his French pages for Henri III, Henri IV, Nevers, Mayenne, the League and fr. 4715 (copies
in `gallica_sweep/src/`); his François I era article was **not** fetched (*), so 1520s–1550s entries carry that caveat.

## The 172 open targets, by priority

Scores (1–5, judgements made before viewing the leaf, data in `catalogue.json`): **importance** = what a full reading
would add to the record; **solvability** = odds of a full reading with the material online; **difficulty** = technical
work at the keyboard (1 align a known sibling, 5 statistics-only on a large nomenclator). **Priority** (0–10) blends
them with weights 0.4 / 0.35 / 0.25 on importance, solvability and ease (6 − difficulty); the site page lets you
reweight, filter and sort. Class **A** siblings with decipherment in the same volume (key recoverable by alignment, as for
Feuquières and Catinat); **B** partial key in print (Tomokiyo) or known family; **C** no key, no sibling.

## Method

- Harvest: Gallica SRU `dc.description all "chiffre" and dc.type all "manuscrit"` → `gallica_sweep/sru_chiffre_desc.json`;
  items split on Omont's numbering and kept when they say *chiffre* without *déchiffrement*
  (`gallica_sweep/bnf_candidates.txt`). Shelfmarks resolved from IIIF manifests (`gallica_sweep/ark_shelfmarks.json`).
- Coverage: string search of correspondents against cryptiana `unsolved.htm` and the six French article pages saved
  in `gallica_sweep/src/`.
- DECODE refill: `catalogue_harvest/decode/dump_list.py` (record list), `dump_views*.py` (full records),
  `digest.py` (groups + same-series keys), `rule_score.py` (scores, merges, exclusions from `overrides.json`),
  `merge.py` (into `catalogue.json`). Cookie in `bordeaux/decode/cookie.txt`, JWT from any RecordsView page.
- Not done: image inspection of ◇ entries; Tomokiyo's François I article; Scheurer, Savasse, Mousset editions;
  DECODE search for each item.

**Checked:** catalogue descriptions and item numbering; shelfmarks from the IIIF manifests; presence/absence of a *déchiffrement* item in the same
volume; fr. 3484 f. 34 on the image (cipher, no decipherment on the leaf); Tomokiyo's status wording for entries 11–14, 16–17, 19–21, 23–24; fr. 16127 on the image.
**Not checked:** the leaves of ◇ entries; printed editions that may contain clear texts; DECODE records.
**User must verify** before attacking any entry: open the ark at the item's folio and confirm no decipherment
follows, then search DECODE and Tomokiyo's site for the correspondent and date.
