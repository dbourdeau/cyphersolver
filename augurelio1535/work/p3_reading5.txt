English summary: ... they have to herr Caspar ... led him into the camp, the watch was settled, and I have had what follows written and reported to him promptly ... it was thought good ... the Turks have done this to their dignities. [Of the rare news we have here] such as follows I report to Your Graces: through herr Caspar it is learned that a powerful Hungarian lord called More Lesli (László Móré), with 4000 foot and horse, Hungarians, Turks and Bohemians, has had the strong castle of Palota besieged, and for a long time has committed great murder. At the diet of Pressburg both [kings] excluded it, so that whichever king comes first should strive to hand it over, together with Stuhlweissenburg. The castle of Palota was pledged for four years, valued at 134,000 gulden. Although at first, against herr Caspar's advice, the castle was bombarded in vain, he hopes to achieve something by digging and battering the wall. For that man's sake many have exerted themselves, since the Hungarians try to relieve it, but so far it has not been allowed, because he thinks he has the peace with the Turk. Therefore a Hungarian, Török Bálint, who one evening at Vienna was well drunk, was sent off with the answer about the relief: he is to talk of these things in the morning when he is sober, so that the Hungarians of his party are almost despised; and therefore the bishop of Veszprém was sent [..] with the recruitment.
