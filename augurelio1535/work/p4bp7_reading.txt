r9407 - Jörg Weinmeister to the Dukes of Bavaria, 10 December 1535
P4b (enclosed slip / Zettel of news) and P7 (subscription), read with the p3/p5 key.

== P4b ==
01 anheut dato ist ein Retz komen mit briefen komen, sagt
02 wie zu Kriechischen Weissenburg funftzig tausent turck-
03 -en sind auszogen hieher auf Ofen zu, und sagt das er wol
04 uon seinem frid, so wais er doch nit was fur ein frid ist,
05 wie dan eur F.G. aus [B] schreiben zu uernemen haben. Ist zu
06 besorgen, auch wie mir [B] selbs hat gesagt, der [∂] werde der
07 krieg den frid haben. Datum ut in litteris.
08 Iorg Weinmaister

== P7 ==
01 schreiben, denn ich mich [7] als meinen genedigisten herren
02 in demuetiger undertenigkait beuelhe. Datu[m]
03 Iorg Weinmaister

ENGLISH SUMMARY

P4b (a slip of news enclosed with the letter): "Today a Serb (Retz) came with letters,
and says that fifty thousand Turks have marched out from Griechisch-Weissenburg
(Belgrade) in this direction, towards Ofen (Buda); and he says that although he knows
well of his peace, he does not know what kind of peace it is, as Your Princely Graces
may gather from [his] letter. It is to be feared - as he himself told me - that we shall
have war rather than peace. Dated as in the letter. Jörg Weinmeister."

P7 (the subscription): "... letter; wherefore I commend myself [to Your Princely Graces]
as my most gracious lords in humble obedience. Dated [as above]. Jörg Weinmeister."
