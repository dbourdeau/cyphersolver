   plausible confusion - re-check on the image. No German word can be made of s + d/ch/c/ntd
   + e/a/o/m + r/n/t here.
L05 "letst" = d(l) b(e) l(t) 5(s) @(t) - all four values are in the key, the word is regular.
L07 "erst recht" = 4(e) g(r) u(s) | v(r) w(e) c(c) J(h) y(t): the t of "erst" is simply not
   written (scribal omission). All signs present take regular values.

   The second K, between "er" and "mit", stays unread (null or slip).

   "ziechen" is a normal spelling of ziehen. Resolves the old E = h.
