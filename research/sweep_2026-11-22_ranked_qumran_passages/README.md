# Fifteen ranked Hebrew cryptic-script passage candidates

These are individual reading problems from Qumran Cave 4. Nine occur in 4Q362 and six use edition designations in the 4Q249 group. They are roughly two millennia old. The year field provides a sorting estimate. Each photograph link opens a group plate. Match the edition fragment number to the plate before comparing ink.

Rank reflects the prospects for recovering a defensible reading and its potential value. Preference goes to a choice between specific letters, an earlier exposure or matching letterform, and a consequential change to the text. A circle in a diplomatic transcription identifies an unread trace. Physical gaps and words supplied from biblical parallels require separate treatment.

Oliveiro's section 5.1 supplies the 4Q362 locators and the remaining alternatives after decipherment. For the 4Q249 entries, compare Pfann's plates with the [museum plate inventory](https://lyingpen.uia.no/dssinventoryproject/museum-plates/). Ben-Dov suggests that 4Q249k and 4Q249l may belong to Midrash Moshe. They are counted here as two physical passages, each with its own unread signs.
