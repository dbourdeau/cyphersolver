The single-surname search added SP 53/16 no. 20 (Paget to Mary, in Phelippes’s hand), SP 53/17 no. 33 (a packet including a Paget-to-Mary letter) and SP 53/18 no. 95 (Mary to **Baron** Paget, identified in Cryptiana). None of these records describes Paget writing to Allen. The first three letters in the table supply dated, separately deciphered comparisons from the same sender. Tomokiyo transcribes the heading of SP 53/22 f. 47 as “Alphabet with Charles Pagett” and distinguishes the Baron’s alphabet on f. 46. Comparing the two original sheets establishes the same core cipher in f. 47r and old 97a. They share the pairs of signs under H and I as well as the distinctive word signs for *to* and *but*. The Chartley sheet adds nulls, a doubling mark and named signs for rulers and countries.
