It may please your matie. Not long since it was my happe to be wth the Spa.
Embasador who fel into talke of your maties sonne and marveled muche at
the course yt he did follow in taking the supremacye of the Churche in refusing
conference wth Catholikes and in treating wth the Q. of Englande wch he sayd
was a manifest signe of his resolution to remayne a Protestant and yt adding
therunto his coldnesse of the making of an Association wth your matie
did confirme as it were yn him smal hope of any good to be done by his meane. I
answered yt sauing his correction I was of a contrary[e] minde yt some thinges
