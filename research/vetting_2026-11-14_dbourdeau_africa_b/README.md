## Correction to the sweep

The British sweep recorded the 2937 telegram as dated 30 Aug 1884 from the archive.org OCR. The page image reads 'Cairo, August 20th, 10.30 P.M.' (the 30th is the date of the Egerton message relayed in clear by Kitchener, footnote 66). The lead's date was corrected on 14 Nov 2026.
