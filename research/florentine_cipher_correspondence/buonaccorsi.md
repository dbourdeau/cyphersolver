# Buonaccorsi to Machiavelli: printed evidence for leads 1550 and 1551

The 2022 national edition of Machiavelli’s *Lettere* identifies all six BNCF *Carte Machiavelli* shelfmarks in these two leads. Five letters have cipher passages whose surviving, uncancelled text the edition prints in Italian. The sixth, IV/105, is printed without cipher marks. All six are letters from Biagio Buonaccorsi in Florence to Niccolò Machiavelli. The five manuscripts grouped in lead 1550 span **6 September–6 October 1506**. Its date field currently ends on 30 September and its correspondent field also names the Dieci. These six shelfmarks supply no dispatch written by the Dieci. **Earlier work and the proposed update.** The repository’s 1506 vetting had compared IV/83, IV/84 and IV/105 with Villari and identified the two remaining IV/83 runs as unread. The 2022 edition supplies their text and expressly marks a further short cipher ending in §11. The repository’s 1509 vetting had already found the five passages of IV/104 in Mario Martelli’s *Tutte le opere* (Sansoni, 1971), no. 173, pp. 1113–1114. Its existing read verdict stands. Luigi Passerini first printed IV/104 in 1873. Its surviving corrections and precise cipher boundaries make the 2022 edition useful alongside Martelli. The editorial credits assign letters 97–197 to Alessio Decaria and the fresh decipherments to Marcello Simonetta, under Francesco Bausi’s direction. The present contribution identifies the published evidence. Direct checks of the glyphs on the manuscripts, a common-key test across years and independent collation of the Gaeta volumes lie outside this comparison. The discrepancies identified here concern the lead records’ descriptions and the history of printed readings.
