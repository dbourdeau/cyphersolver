# Six numbered names in the May 9 letter

On SP 12/252 f. 19r, William Gifford reports that 43 spoke to **23, Paget**, and **36, Dr Gifford**. The next lines twice gloss **93, Count Fuentes**. Further down, the letter names four men who dealt with Captain White of Flushing, in the order **40, Owen · 24, Holt · 23, Paget · 42, Moody**. The gloss above 40 is faint. Cotton Caligula B VIII f. 332v lists the same six distinct number–name pairs. Green’s 1869 abstract names the two hearers and Fuentes, and says that Owen, Paget and others dealt with White. The original includes Holt and Moody among those Gifford named. This is a comparison of historical annotations made with the roster in view. Of the six, **40 and 24** also appear with their names in the June original/decipher comparison. A further June entry can be verified there. After the protected word *write*, the original gives **20** where the decipher names **the bishop of Cassano**. Cotton lists the bishop at 20. This establishes shared numerical entries across the papers. The May tests of the alphabet remain as reported.
