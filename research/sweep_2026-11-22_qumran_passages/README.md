# Unresolved readings in Hebrew cryptic texts from Qumran

These catalogue leads identify specific passages for comparison with existing editions. The listed scholars established the script or supplied readings before this submission. This submission records published evidence only. The dates describe the artifacts at period precision. The numerical year is an approximate sorting value, not a dated colophon.

## 4Q298

Four consecutive unidentified signs precede a doubtful final kaf in the published transcription. Lines 8–10 preserve further words. Compare each trace in color and infrared with secure signs in the same hand before considering context. [Image source](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/4Q298-1?locale=he_IL) · [Scholarship](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q298/2026-10-06/index.html)

## 4Q186

Popović and Geller give competing reconstructions. The position of the detached fragment and some readings remain disputed. The text uses mixed scripts and left-to-right Hebrew. Register the old plate against infrared photographs. Test the line spacing independently of the proposed head-to-foot order. [Image source](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-370747) · [Scholarship](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10193507/1/Geller%202023%20Aest%204%20Qumran%20Phys.pdf)

## 4Q249

Ben-Dov reconstructed the column. Two occurrences of משפט אחד bracket damaged wording in the upper Cryptic A text. The undertext complicates stroke identification. Map surviving upper-text traces across the numbered fragments and test wording against the adjoining lines. [Image source](https://www.academia.edu/78124990/_An_Early_Halakhic_Midrash_in_the_Fragments_of_4Q249_Midrash_Moshe_Meghillot_15_2021_in_Hebrew) · [Scholarship](https://orion-bibliography.huji.ac.il/node/75930)

## 4Q249a

The encrypted Cave 4 copy and the ordinary-script Cave 1 copy (1QSa II 11) preserve different traces at the disputed sentence boundary involving מושב. See §24 of the reconstruction. Compare the traces independently in each witness before aligning them. Record differences between copies. [Image source](https://megillot.haifa.ac.il/images/RQ2901002.pdf) · [Scholarship](https://www.academia.edu/37080131/RECONSTRUCTION_OF_A_SINGLE_COPY_OF_THE_QUMRAN_CAVE_4_CRYPTIC_SCRIPT_SEREKH_HAEDAH_1)

## 4Q317

Jacobus questions whether the traces support the published restoration of day four and eleven parts obscured. The calendar and its cryptic alphabet are known. Derive the predicted day and quantity from adjacent dated entries, then check which letters the photographs actually preserve. [Image source](https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/article/the-mysterious-mount-zion-mug/4qcrypta-lunisolar-calendar_4q317/) · [Scholarship](https://www.academia.edu/4717689/Qumran_Calendars_and_the_Creation_A_Study_of_4QcryptA_Lunisolar_Calendar_4Q317_)

## PAM 43.697, fragment 50

Perrot identified four Cryptic B signs. Oliveiro discussed the possible vav in note 6 and omitted this scrap from his decipherment of the two larger manuscripts. Measure the four signs against the published Cryptic B forms and test the proposed attribution to 4Q362. [Image source](https://doi.org/10.2143/RQ.31.2.3287248) · [Scholarship](https://research.rug.nl/en/publications/cracking-another-code-of-the-dead-sea-scrolls-deciphering-cryptic/)
