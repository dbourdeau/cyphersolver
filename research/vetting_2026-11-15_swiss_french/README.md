## Findings for targets/affry1757 and targets/affry1757c (not folded into those targets yet)

1. **d'Affry's own clear drafts are in the Fribourg fonds.** AEF Affry 225 and 226 file "Brouillon d'une depeche / n. N" for d'Affry's numbered dispatches to Rouille/Bernis (n. 4 of 12 Dec 1755 to n. 400 of 24 Nov 1758). The intercepted DECODE letters carry the same numbers: R1068 is n. 172 of 26 Apr 1757 and AEF 225.275 is the draft n. 172 of 26 Apr 1757 ("d'Affry informe Rouille qu'il a transmis un courrier de Vienne a Londres", which matches the NOTES' "courier Vienna-London"); R1074/R2067 are n. 283 of 17 Jan 1758 and 226.168 is the draft n. 283 dated 19-20 Jan 1758 (content note: a Zealand shipowner, Mme Tronchin, the Yorke memorial: another letter of the same number or a different date, not checked). Also R1072 n. 123, R1076 n. 124, R1073 n. 133, R1064 n. 136, R1067 n. 161, R1070 n. 210 should have drafts in 225/226 (the unit titles were listed only partly). Not digitised ("Multimedias: Sans"): reading-room photographs or a reproduction order. Drafts may differ from the sent text, but they are an independent clear text for the 11 letters left in part.
2. **Stainville's replies and d'Affry's drafts to Stainville (series 257)** are the other half of the R1071 correspondence (affry1757c, code C): 257.6 (23 Sep 1757, ciphered) with 257.7 Transcription; 257.10/.11 (5 Nov 1757); 257.14/.15; 257.18/.19; 257.21-.24 (Choiseul 1758); 257.28/.29; and d'Affry's drafts 257.1 (19 Apr), .5 (4 Sep 1757), .13, .17, .20, .25. No draft is listed for 4-7 Aug 1757 (R1071's date), so no direct plaintext for R1071. Whether the Stainville-to-d'Affry cipher is code C is not known; a transcription beside a Stainville cipher would allow the test.
3. **Two key sheets in 280:** 280.209 "Chiffre avec Botteman" (numbers 1 to 60, a letter for each; code names for important persons on p. 2) and 280.210 "Premier chiffre remis par le duc de Choiseul et le chancelier du Muy" (coded messages). Not seen; possibly relevant to code C or to the Choiseul-to-d'Affry cipher of Dec 1758 on.
4. The Transcription/Dechiffrement pieces are not shown to be contemporary; Lyonet's decipherments (NA 1.01.50 inv. 221, 223) remain the contemporary route for 1755-58.
