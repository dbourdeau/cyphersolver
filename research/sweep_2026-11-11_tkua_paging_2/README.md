## The two open items of the first pass

**Order of 25 May 1673.** The closing 13 cipher lines are not printed or quoted in any edition opened:

- Vaupell, *Rigskansler Grev Griffenfeld* I (1880; archive.org `rigskanslergrevg01vaup`): prints the instructions for
  Lindenov (Dec 1672) and Juel (5 Apr 1673, 24 Aug 1673, 19 Feb 1674) and the order of 3 Oct 1674, and describes the
  proposal sent on 27 June 1673. Nothing of 25 May 1673.
- Jørgensen, *Peter Schumacher Griffenfeld* II (1894; `peterschumacherg02jruoft`), pp. 85-87 and notes: the instruction
  and Griffenfeld's private letter to Juel of 19 May 1673. No order of 25 May.
- Fryxell, *Handlingar rörande Sverges historia* (`handlingarrrand01fryxgoog`): abstracts of the envoys' relations
  (14 and 17 May 1673), not the king's orders. Danske Samlinger 2. række III (`danskesamlinger01smitgoog`): Swedish
  envoys' reports only.
- *Danmark-Norges Traktater* 6 (1665-75) is scanned on Arkivalieronline (epid 17287984, unit 214837, image = page + 1).
  Opened; index pages viewed; the 1673-74 Stockholm negotiation pages were not read through.

It stays a lead with no block: the loose decipherment leaf at image 9 may continue on an unfilmed verso, and that
needs the original. archive.org has no copy of Danmark-Norges Traktater.

**Sehested, Vienna 1657** (`sehested_block.json`): full block, verdict `open`, `key_in_volume`. Six searches. No
printed decipherment exists: Gigas (1883) quotes a few phrases, Fridericia (1894, note 62) thanks him for a
