  "672. Aglossa fss 69 over 28 fistularia no objection **wftzk ccuqk knpby xpoyt xuota** perhaps not necessary refer

  authority direct."

  the capitals K are the clerk's ordinary k. No decode on the form.
  Clear copy: **none in frames 89-152 or 297-311** (no other form numbered 1796 or dated 26.2.914 from Khartoum to
  Ferasudan seen; 65/1123 beside it is a different telegram, no. 1519 of 28.2.914). Frames 25-88 and 153-296 are
