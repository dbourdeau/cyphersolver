## Frames 0-3 (HIL/64/1-20), dates 1907 (May-Dec)
- **ARABIC CIPHER / CLEAR PAIR: HIL/64/17 (cipher) and HIL/64/16 (clear)**, frame index 2 (label 13-18), bottom middle and
  bottom left. Same telegram on two G 13 forms: no. 1028 (Arabic numerals), 41 words, station Misr (Cairo), date
  20 December [1]907 (read from the oval; the month word is small), to the Governor (muhafiz) of Alexandria, signed
  Nazir al-Dakhiliyya (Minister of the Interior). 64/16 is wholly clear Arabic; 64/17 has the same text with three
  runs of isolated Arabic letters in place of phrases. Runs as read from a 1,800-px region (img/v/region_hil64_002_30_62.jpg),
  UNCOLLATED, dots often absent:
  line 1 after "al-duwal khabaru nizarat al-kharijiyya": ر ى ط د ز ر و ص ب م ج  (about 11 letters)
  line 2 after "bi-sha'n": د ط د ف د ر ف ن م ق د ص ط ه و  (about 15), then "alladhina akhbar"
  line 3 after "anhuma": ع ق د د ن ق ص ط و ب ق ص د  (13), then "wa-haythu ihdahuma ... wa-huwa al-warid ..." in clear.
  Clear copy 64/16 has in those places (my reading) "bi-talabihi sirriyya", "al-waburayn ... asliha", and
  "janab qunsul Faransa" (13 letters for 13 cipher letters; n->qaf three times, alif->dal twice: looks like simple
  substitution). No decode written on the cipher form itself. Sure of: pair, same number/date/addressee. Letter readings: moderate.
- Code words: HIL/64/4 (Mulki Cairo to Dakhlieh Khartoum and Mudir Suakin, 18-12-7): Affulsus, Acclamatif, Acamacu, Aburrados (and one more A-word).
- Other Arabic forms clear: 64/6, 64/18 (Turkish/Ottoman?) clear script. French: 64/2, 64/5, 64/20.

## Frames 30-37 (HIL/64/178-225), Jan-Dec 1908
- **CIPHER / CLEAR PAIR: HIL/64/214 (cipher), frame index 36 (label 214-219), top left; clear copy HIL/64/62 with its
  second sheet HIL/64/61, frame index 10 (label 57-62).** G 14 received form: original no. 1054, 129 words, date 26-9-8
  (26 Sept 1908), from Khartoum to Mulki, Cairo. Opens in clear English ("458 Mudirs court here Davidson president
  Ghalib and Abcarius members has tried seven Katfia rebels and sentenced two namely Awad Elkerim brother of Abdelkader
  and Elagaz Omar to death the other to imprisonment for life stop") and then runs on in five-letter groups to the foot
  of the form; no signature on this sheet, no decode on the form. HIL/64/62 has the same number, word count, date,
  from/to and the same opening, and carries on in clear ("Two of these five being slaves will be recommended to mercy
  stop Court will assemble again to consider recommendations ... not enough evidence to prove", continued on 64/61
  "participation in murders and did not proceed with those charges stop Cases will be submitted ... for confirmation
  stop Wire Wingate / Kanuni Khartoum").
  Groups as read from a 1,400-px region (img/v/region_hil64_036_02_28.jpg), UNCOLLATED, about 46 groups:
  Fgmos / zghko kzvjk rkvut oyxik omvyy / rklkq wwwku pkpgs wklql / ogsdt snlgm vyyxo okwry kxtxv /
  ugsgs uovpk llkqs wwkuc / xgvsu ovugx okosz zvkju sgoku / gkuak pgspk xghrn gmvyy / usglk qswwk upgsw klglg msoku /
  gkugk pgspk xghog sdghk gmsok / agkug kpgsp kxghm klaxy osghx / ltkpm vghgn tlwnl pklor
  (l and b, u and n, q and g are hard to tell apart in this hand). "pgspk xgh" comes three times, where the clear
  text has recommended / recommendations / recommend: the cipher text is the clear text of 64/62, letter for letter
  regrouped in fives, so a letter substitution (or similar) recoverable from this pair. Same look as HIL/64/890-891
  (letters g k s x w dominant), so this pair is the likely key to that telegram. A continuation sheet of the cipher
  version (129 words; this sheet holds about 85) was not seen in frames 30-37.
  Sure of: cipher groups present, same telegram as 64/62 (number, date, words, opening all match). Group readings: moderate.
- Code words: 64/190 (Herbert Abbassia to Sekretair Khartoum, 16-4-8): affango, affaises (+1). 64/192 (Sekretair Khartoum to Nekoud Cairo, 19-4-8): flagelabas, affanghono, affaises. 64/221 (Herbert Abbassia to Sekretair Khartoum, 24-12-8): Flejacamos, Flejando, Adulterinos (x2), and two more. 64/223 (Ferasudan Cairo to Malisudan Khartoum, 26-12-8): abhaspeln.
