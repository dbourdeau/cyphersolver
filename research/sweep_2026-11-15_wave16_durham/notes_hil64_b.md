  Clear copy (read at 1,100 px): 764 "1295 Following is extract letter from the Siger to Hakimam begins have received
  communication for Governor Bank Abyssinia in which he informs me, Bank at present have to pay one per cent transit
  duty on all dollars sent Gambela via Sudan and asks me whether in view benefit" / 763 "conferred on Sudan trade and
  customs by presence of Bank at Gore Sudan government would not be disposed [make] exception in their favour and
  waive this duty stop I may add that Bank propose establish agencies at Gambela and Saiu shortly ends stop wire me
  to which transit duty reference is made and your view" / 762 "proposal stop as far as I know we retain one per

  whole duty paid is refunded. Ferasudan".
  Quick check against the clear text: simple substitution, words run together, one null first ("p" + mtxwyqx =
  extract; umxxmw = letter; kwve = from; xhm = the; bgjmw = siger; xv hyfgeye = to hakimam; jvomwdvw sydf = governor

  d=n, u=l, y=a, p=z, v=o, w=r, b=s. NOT the alphabet of the 1908 pairs (there t=o, h=f, i=u, s=w, e=y).
  Pair certain (same number, date, word count, address, clear passages); letter readings good but uncollated.
