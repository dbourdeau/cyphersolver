# Chrome wave 8 (15 Nov 2026): DARA (Aragon) and DigitArq non-ANTT archives

**Result: one productive source, DARA (Documentos y Archivos de Aragon), 10 leads (2854-2863), 6 promoted (2854-2857, 2860, 2861).** Pre-1480 cipher letters of Ferdinand and the Count of Cardona to John II sit in the Zurita collection of AHP Zaragoza, digitised and not described as cipher.

## DARA (`dara.aragon.es/opac/app/`)
- **Route:** Wicket app, server-rendered. In a tab on the origin: `fetch('/opac/app/results?q=<term>&ob=re:1')` gives page 1 (10 hits); the next page is the link whose href contains `navigator-next` (stateful: fetch it from the previous page's DOM, about 4 s per page). Item pages `/opac/app/item?i=<id>` are stateless; the id is in the results hrefs. Images: thumbnails `opacaraimagenes.aragon.es/<aa>/<bb>/min<file>.jpg` (cross-origin: show them in an `<img>`, `fetch` fails; the full size is not exposed) and a PDF viewer at `/opac/app/attachment?a=<aa>/<bb>/<file>.pdf`. The javascript tool blocks output that contains URLs with query strings: replace `?&=` before printing, or write into the DOM and use `get_page_text`. Fire-and-forget async loops stall in the background tab: keep each call under 40 s.
- **Terms run:** cifra (126 hits, mostly CIFRA photo-agency noise and Teruel/Zaragoza photographs; 'cifrado' and 'cifrada' return the same 126), en cifra (66), carta cifrada (19). Not run: lenguaje cifrado, clave, criptografia, descifrar, chiffre.
- **Found:** `C_CRAR/000001` (Cartas Reales del Archivo del Reino, the chronicler Zurita's collection at AHP Zaragoza, 66 letters 1300-1583, all digitised): the 43 letters before 1500 were looked at on their thumbnails. Cipher on 0020 (1477), 0024 (1478, full cipher page), 0026 (1478), 0039 (1497, half in cipher), 0040 (1497), 0029 (1479, the text mentions two cipher letters). 0010 (1472, Monreale to John II) and 0022 (1477) are uncertain at thumbnail size. Fundacion Alcort (Benasque): D/0055 (full cipher letter, 6 leaves), D/0056 (Naples 1575 to Philip II, cipher completed with Latin letters), and four Gonzaga of Guastalla letters of 1683-85 each with a cipher sheet (D/0040, 0043, 0047, 0051; lead 2862, not promoted: the 'Noticia' records D/0041, 0045, 0048 look like the clear text or decipherment filed beside them). D/0011 (in the 'Documentos cifrados' series, Trapani 1659) is a clear letter.
- **Prior art:** Tomokiyo 2018 reconstructs four Ferdinand-John II systems (1470, 1473, 1474-76, 1476-79) from BNE MSS/20211, not from Zaragoza; his PDF was not readable, so the vetting blocks say that the 1476-79 system is untested on these sheets. Lead 2858 (CRAR 0040, Claver, Milan, 19 Jun 1497) is a sibling of lead 1048 (23 Jun 1497) and of Parisi's Claver decipherments: not promoted.
- **Not done:** the 23 CRAR letters after 1500; page-by-page viewing of the CRAR PDFs; other cipher terms.

## DigitArq (`digitarq.arquivos.pt`), all archives except ANTT
`harvest_pt.py`: 22 terms over `/api/docs/search` without `archiveCode`, ANTT rows dropped: 203 records (AHU 180, ADVCT, ADPRT, ADEVR, ADPTG, ADLRA, ADSTB, ADBJA, ADAVR, CPF), in `pt_hits.jsonl`; details of 11 in `pt_details.json`. Nearly all noise (names such as Cifre, Nifra, Citra; 20th-century AHU 'Telegramas cifrados' files of 1947-76, outside the period; 'cifra' as an amount). Real: AHU OP16696 and OP17197 (1897-98, not online; lead 2863), AHU CU/001/0047/04298 (Angola 1760, the Jesuit Aquaviva cipher of 1587, 32 images: `key_leads.json`), AHU CU/034/0211/00028 (1644, a new cipher for India, description only). The district archives' own hosts redirect to the national host.
