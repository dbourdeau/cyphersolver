## Lead 1270 - Juan Manuel -> Catholic Monarchs and Charles V, seven letters Nov 1496 - Nov 1522 (BDH 0000186690)

Verdict: **unresolved, leaning open for any pre-1500 letter, but not promotable**: ciphertext not seen and the seven
letters are not individually dated or listed. The lead's framing needs correcting (below).

### (1) Ciphertext seen: NO
BDH blocked the same way. Europeana record (copied in research/bne_hispana/raw/leads.json l.276-380): creators Juan Manuel
(m. 1543), Isabel I, Fernando V, Carlos I; "Parcialmente cifradas"; "Siete cartas fechadas en Genova y Roma entre noviembre
de 1496 y noviembre de 1522"; stamps of Gayangos. The card does not say which letters are ciphered or how long.
Images (`pdf.raw?query=id:0000186690`) not reachable.

### (2) Prior art
- Tomokiyo AlonsoSanchez.htm (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/AlonsoSanchez.htm, fetched 6 Nov): covers Juan Manuel's
  **1522 letters to Charles V held at the RAH (BRAH 9/23, 9/24; DECODE R9499-R9529)**, not BNE. No mention of BNE, Gayangos,
  1497 or bdh0000186690. So "Tomokiyo covers the 1521-22 letters" concerns a different set of letters; the Nov 1522 member
  of this BNE volume may be unread, or may sit in the same cipher (his table, targets/sanchez1522).
- **Repo ID error:** the lead says the 1497 letter is "repo catalogue 1014". catalogue.json id 1014 is Dr Walles (TNA SP 29).
  The Juan Manuel item is **catalogue 1049**: 23 Jun 1497, Rome, RAH Salazar y Castro N-23 f. 36, a different holding, not this
  BDH volume. A duplicate is not established; it is a same-correspondent neighbour.
- Dating caveat: the "Juan Manuel m. 1543" attribution for 1496 Genoa/Rome letters is odd. The Belmonte biography
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Manuel,_lord_of_Belmonte) gives England 1499 and Rome 1520-22; in 1496 the Spanish
  ambassador at Rome was Garcilaso de la Vega (BNE RES/226/132, 8 Nov 1496; Vaquero, Lemir 19, 2015,
  https://parnaseo.uv.es/lemir/Revista/Revista19/04_Vaquero_Carmen_Lemir19.pdf; no cipher mentioned there). The Nov 1496 letter
  may be misattributed or by another hand; this affects its "oldest cipher" value and must be settled on the images.
- Web searches (3, 6 Nov 2026): no published decipherment of this BDH volume; DECODE has none (decode_ids empty); nothing in
  Galende/Parisi located for it. Lasry, Cowan 2026 not reached.

### (3) Keys
Tomokiyo's reconstructed Juan Manuel 1522 cipher table (targets/sanchez1522/NOTES.md l.72, l.354) is relevant to any 1521-22
letter in the volume; nothing for 1496-1519. Status: related key (1522 Rome cipher) held, untested here. The Catholic
Monarchs' general cipher (Galende/Parisi; targets/esp318, targets/garcilaso1498) could apply to the 1496 letters.

### (4) Duplicates
known.py "186690" and "Juan Manuel": the lead itself, bne_hispana candidates, catalogue 1049 (RAH, 1497, different holding),
sanchez1522 / garcilaso1498 notes (RAH items; R9486, f.18 "Don Juan Manuel"). No same-shelfmark duplicate.
