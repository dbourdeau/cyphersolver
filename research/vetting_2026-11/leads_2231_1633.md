## Lead 2231: Gyorgy I Rakoczi to Istvan Racz, 1642 (MNL OL E 190, 43. doboz, 5. tetel, no. 597; Lang C.Rag.05)

Verdict: open (not a duplicate, not read, no key found). Ciphertext not seen.

### Evidence

- Lang 2015 repertory (MTA dc_758_13, PDF page 263, downloaded and read 6 Nov): row C.Rag.05, date 1642, Rakoczi to
  "Galospetri Racz Istvan, envoy in Turkey", MOL E 190 43. doboz 5. tetel 597. sz.; Hungarian, "magyar betuk, monoalf."
  (letters, monoalphabetic); Kulcs: blank; Megfejtes: "nincs feltorve" (not broken); "majdnem teljes" (almost wholly cipher).
- The same page lists, directly above it, C.Ragy.58-77 (1639-46, Rakoczi and his envoys incl. Racz): "feloldva a kiadasban"
  (deciphered in the edition), Beke Antal and Barabas Samu, eds., *I. Rakoczi Gyorgy es a porta* (Budapest, MTA, 1888),
  pp. 480-82, 489-502, 593-595, 635-6, 641-2, 666-686, 694-96, 868-880. So Lang separates 597 from the printed, deciphered
  letters: it is a letter whose text the edition did not print.
- The edition (MEK 20116.pdf, 930 pp., downloaded to scratchpad, OCR text searched) prints Rakoczi's 1642 letters to Racz
  (6 Apr p.584-5, 8 May, 16 May, 28 May, 18 Jul = pp. 583-595). Relevant passages:
  - Rakoczi to Racz, Gyulafehervar 17 Mar 1642 (p. ~583-584): "Az titkos iras igazan vagyon irva, csak tudja kegyelmed
    megmagyarazni" and P.S. "Az czifrakkal valo irast, ha meg nem tudja kegyelmed olvasni, kuldje vissza, mi megforditvan
    tudositjuk kegyelmedet" (the cipher passage is a "czifra" script; Racz may send it back to be turned into clear).
  - Racz's reply, Constantinople, June 1642 (p. ~586): "Az czifrakkal valo nagysagod kegyelmes parancsolatjat en semmikeppen
    nem erthettem meg" (he could not read the cipher order at all, only inferred it from a summary letter).
    Candidate for no. 597: a Rakoczi letter in hand cipher of Feb-Mar 1642 that Racz could not read. This is an inference;
    the E 190 record page was not seen to confirm which letter 597 is.
  - Letter of 18 Jul 1642, p. 594 (footnote on p. 594): "Az dolt betuk titkos jegyekkel" (italic words in the original were
    secret signs): the editors deciphered short Rakoczi cipher passages inside plain letters and printed them in italics.
    That shows the Rakoczi hand-cipher was read by Barabas in 1888, but no key table is printed in the pages checked.
- The preface (Szilagyi, 4 Dec 1888) says Barabas Samu helped copy and decipher the cipher letters; the MEK record page lists
  Racz as capitiha Oct 1641 to Dec 1642.
- E 190 is a database of descriptions with placeholder thumbnails (catalogue entries already record this, e.g. the 1655 E 190
  lead: "no image viewer"). No image of no. 597 located. Hungaricana (blocked to scripts) and Chrome were not tried; the
  earlier sweep D (research/sweep_2026-11_D_east_north/) also found no images.

### Prior art searches

- known.py "E 190", "Rakoczi", "Racz": only other Rakoczi E 190 entries (catalogue: Papai to Rakoczi 1710 R731; Rakoczi-circle
  letters; a 1655 E 190 5.t. 788 item) and Pongracz/Karolyi; no entry for 43. doboz no. 597, Racz or Galospetri.
- DECODE dumps (decode/out_1, views, views_b; cryptoole/snapshot.json; hcportal/index.json): Rakoczi hits are all Ferenc II
  (1704-11: R639 etc.) and the Raday archive; nothing for Gyorgy I, Racz, or 1642. snapshot.json only repeats the sweep D line.
- targets/rakoczi1632 (BSB Clm 10401, Camerarius gloss, numbers 1-99 homophonic, Latin), rakoczi1704, rakoczi1707, rakoczi1711
  (Ferenc II, numeric, DECODE R639): not duplicates. 2231 is Hungarian letter-for-letter monoalphabetic, Gyorgy I, a different
  prince and system; none of those keys applies.
- Tomokiyo, Lasry/DECRYPT: web search (Hungarian and English) found nothing on Gyorgy I Rakoczi cipher letters; not
  checked page by page on cryptiana (only web search).
- Lang, *Real Life Cryptology* (2015/2020) is the source row; its key and decipherment columns are blank for C.Rag.05.

### Key status

No key held and none found. A Rakoczi cipher exists in print only as the italic passages of the 1888 edition; Lang's table
lists no key for 597. Racz himself could not read it, so even the recipient's key may not have matched. Not tested.

## Lead 1633: 'Nuernberger Geheimschrift von 1570', StAN Reichsstadt Nuernberg, Ratskanzlei, E-Laden, Akten 193

Verdict: open as far as checked, but weak: the item is probably a cipher key or table, not ciphertext. Ciphertext not seen.
Consider `not_cipher`/low value unless a view of fol. 252 shows a ciphered letter.

### Evidence

- Archivportal-D record (fetched 6 Nov): title "Die Verlaengerung und Erweiterung des Landsberger Bunds sowie der
  Bundesabschied zu Muenchen", signature StAN Reichsstadt Nuernberg, Ratskanzlei, E-Laden, Akten 193 (older S VII L 122
  (193 IV)); Laufzeit 1569-1572; "Enthaelt: Nuernberger Geheimschrift, von 1570, fol. 252" and a letter of Thomas Loeffelholz
  to the Aelteren, 10 Nov 1570; paper, partly bound. No digital copy or viewer; the DDB object page is only the catalogue entry.
- The wording names a "Geheimschrift" (a cipher), not a ciphered letter; fol. 252 may be a key sheet or a loose cipher, so the
  length of any ciphertext is unknown. The file as a whole is the League's extension and the Munich Bundesabschied (Bavaria,
  Nuernberg and others): material for Nuernberg's delegates, probably a cipher for their correspondence with the Rat.
- known.py "Loeffelholz", "E-Laden", "Ratskanzlei", "Geheimschrift": only catalogue 587 (1632-35 envoys, different file), a
  Losungamt/Ratskanzlei B 77 Zasius entry, lead 1462 Registerlein, and the closed not_cipher lead on Stadtarchiv Nuernberg B 11
  Nr. 372. No duplicate for Akten 193; no DECODE, cryptoole or hcportal hit for Nuernberg 1570 (the only DECODE Nuernberg hit is
  a 1908 postcard).
- Web searches (6 Nov 2026): no study of this cipher. Related literature surfaced: A. Wagner, "Nuernbergische Geheimschrift im
  15. und am Beginn des 16. Jahrhunderts", Archivalische Zeitschrift 9 (1884) 14-62 (older Nuernberg cipher, not 1570); L. von
  Rockinger, "Ueber Geheimschriftschluessel der bayerischen Kanzlei im 16. Jahrhundert" (1891), relevant to the Bavarian League
  partners. Neither verified here.

### Key status

No key held. The entry itself may be the key (a "Geheimschrift"); if fol. 252 is a table, a matching ciphered letter would be
elsewhere in the file or in the Nuernberg letter books. Not tested.

### Unchecked

- Wagner 1884 and Rockinger 1891 for a 1570 table; the Nuernberg Staatsarchiv Findbuch of the Ratskanzlei E-Laden for sibling files;
