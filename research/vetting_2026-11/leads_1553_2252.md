## Lead 1553: Lorenzo de' Medici to Pier Filippo Pandolfini (Milan), 22 Jan 1490, BNCF Autografi Palatini VI/62

**Verdict: open (provisional).** No decipherment found anywhere, but two checks could not be made (edition Lettere XVI,
and the images did not load), so do not promote before at least the edition is opened.

### (1) Ciphertext seen
- Internet Culturale detail page opened in Chrome 6 Nov 2026, no CAPTCHA this time:
  https://www.internetculturale.it/it/16/search/detail?instance=magindice&case=&id=oai%3Abncf.firenze.sbn.it%3A21%3AFI0098%3AAutografiPalatini%3Avol_VI_n_62
  Record text (read in full): "Lettera, Firenze 1490-01-22 ('Die xxij Jan(uarii) 1489')", digitisation "completo", 7 images
  (Copertina, c. 1r, 1v, 2r, 2v, 3r, 3v), c. 1 295x215 mm, c. 3 185x205 mm, cc. 2r and 3v blank, address on 2v
  "Petrofilippo de pandolfinis oratori flor. Medjolani". "C. 3 e un postscriptum cifrato: in interlinea sono decifrate solo
  alcune parole." Bibliography: Ricci, Censimento (1964) p. 155 only. Names in the letter (indexed by BNCF): Ludovico
  Sforza, Branda Castiglioni, Ferrante, Innocent VIII, Virginio Orsini, Antonio Rucellai, Duke of Calabria, Belprato,
  Gian Galeazzo, Isabella of Aragon.
- The viewer (iccuviewer/iccu.jsp, Bncf) opened, but only the cover image rendered ("Failed to load the image to
  display" on the page selector; the selector kept showing the cover, and one click made the tab drift to an unrelated
  bdh-rd.bne.es URL). **So I did not see c. 3 myself.** Extent therefore rests on the 29 Oct sweep note already in the
  lead: 7 lines of symbol cipher, about 30 words glossed interlinear (a short postscript, a few dozen cipher groups).
- Retry route: the viewer after a fresh reload, or the BNCF "Teca digitale" direct JPEG. Not blocked by a CAPTCHA.

### (2) Prior art
- known.py "Pandolfini", "Autografi Palatini", "Mss/2043": only leads 1552 (VI/38, 1481, printed deciphered in Lettere VI
  no. 542 A), 1553 itself and 1554-1559 siblings; nothing in DECODE.
- DECODE dumps (`research/catalogue_harvest/decode/*.json*`) and `research/cryptoole/snapshot.json`: no Pandolfini hit
  (grep, case-insensitive). Cryptiana entries in the snapshot: none for Pandolfini.
- Tomokiyo (cryptiana): nothing found in the snapshot; no web hit.
- Lasry/DECRYPT: nothing found (web searches below).
- Edition: **Lorenzo de' Medici, Lettere XVI (Sept 1489-Feb 1490, ed. L. Boninger, Giunti 2011) NOT opened** (not on
  Internet Archive per the lead; web search for "Lettere XVI Pandolfini 22 gennaio 1490" found nothing online). Vol. VI
  printed its Pandolfini slip deciphered (lead 1552), so XVI may well print this postscript deciphered. This is the
  biggest unchecked risk: the verdict could become `read`.
- Literature: web searches "Lorenzo de' Medici Lettere XVI Pandolfini 22 gennaio 1490 postscriptum cifra Milano" and
  "Marco Vito Medici cifre ... Pandolfini Milano 1490 cipher": no reading. Marco Vito (Vienna/Salerno, page
  https://dshcs.univie.ac.at/ueber-uns/schwerpunkte/antike-byzanz-mittelalter/doktorandinnen/marco-vito/ ) works on
  "the encrypted Medici documents between 1486 and 1491"; his published paper is on a Florentine polyalphabetic cipher
  (Livorno, for Piero Capponi; https://dspace.ut.ee/items/5edf1ecc-b0ef-4d76-991d-521c22f9f30b/full). No sign that he
  published this postscript. Overlap risk stays, unchecked beyond that.
