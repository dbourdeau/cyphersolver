## Lead 2254: Harborne -> Walsingham, SP 97/1 ff. 54-66 (+75)

**Verdict: duplicate / printed (close as read from existing decipherment: the Calendar prints the text). Do not promote.**

Evidence (all fetched 6 Nov 2026):
- Calendar of State Papers Foreign, Elizabeth, vol. 18 (BHO, https://www.british-history.ac.uk/cal-state-papers/foreign/vol18/pp346-358, pp432-444, pp491-506, pp562-573) and vol. 19 pp58-71. "Cipher, undeciphered" is the calendarer's endorsement note: the document has no contemporary decipher attached. The calendar nonetheless prints a full English rendering of each:
  - f. 54, 15 Feb 1584 = Cal. no. 424 (p. 355): duplicate of Harborne's 27 Jan letter (f. 48, which carries a period decipher per TNA "2 trans. and decipher") plus ~139 words of addition, printed.
  - f. 56, 28 Mar 1584 = no. 513 (p. 438): ~2,000 words printed, 3 pp.
  - f. 58, 29 Mar 1584 = no. 517 (p. 442): ~880 words printed, 1 p.
  - f. 62, 15 May 1584 = no. 599 (p. 501): ~880 words printed; first 2.5 pp are a copy of the 29 Mar letter; 4 pp.
  - f. 66, 28 Jun 1584 = no. 707 (p. 570): ~1,450 words printed, 2 pp.
  - f. 75, 15 Sep 1584 = vol. 19 p. 65: ~1,700 words printed, 3 pp.
  The calendar text is continuous prose (with Latin tags, a footnote that Harborne spells dates "letter by letter, in cipher"); editor's decipherment evidently from the originals. The lead's folio_note says letters of 28 Jun (21 pp.) and 15 Sep (33 pp.): the calendar gives 2 pp and 3 pp, so those page counts in the lead are wrong.
- TNA Discovery API (https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/API/records/v1/details/C6766273 etc.): every SP 97/1 item `digitised: false`; item descriptions carry no cipher note, only "For. Cal. 1583-4 p.xxx". No image reachable online (State Papers Online is subscription Gale, not checked).
- Deciphered siblings per TNA: ff. 36, 48, 68, 97, 113 ("and decipher"), plus 40, 107, 111 duplicates.
- Key: Conyers Read's pointer to SP 106/1 f. 3 not verified. The Klais transcription index (sites.google.com/site/klaispublications .../sp106-1-3_index) says SP 106/1 ff. 1-7 are an inserted index, f. 3 inside it; so f. 3 is likely an index leaf, not a key; key location unresolved. Not held in the repo.
- DECODE dumps (research/catalogue_harvest/decode/*.jsonl, cryptoole snapshot): no Harborne record. known.py "SP 97/1": only the two leads and Dudout sweep. targets/: walsingham1572, 1572nov, 1581, 1585 are different. Lead (Bodleian, Walsingham -> Harborne 1587, id ~2200) is a separate item.
- Web search (Tomokiyo, Lasry): nothing on Harborne ciphers. Literature: "Embassy of William Harborne to Constantinople" (TRHS) discusses correspondence, not the cipher; not read.
