## Badator (Basque archives portal): a large new route, not mined
- Not a Basque-archive search for cipher: **Badator indexes the RAH Colección Salazar y Castro catalogue (Indice de la coleccion de Don Luis de Salazar y Castro, 49 vols.) with one record per document and the index's own wording**, e.g. 'Original, en cifra.', 'Original, con algunos parrafos en cifra', 'Texto descifrado de los parrafos en cifra del documento resenado en el n 16412'. Route: `https://www.artxibo.euskadi.eus/webartxi00-container/es/ad53aArchivoHistoricoWar/badator/maintSimple?locale=es`, type the term in the simple search (a POST to `resultadoLista`; submit the form with `form.submit()` and let the page load), 100 rows a page, 'Siguiente' paging works in the DOM (`a` text 'Siguiente'); each row's 'Ver ficha' has the signatura. Do not change the `badid`/`_HDIV_STATE_` values of the ficha links by script (the HDIV token is a protection and the harness refused the loop): click the 'Ver ficha' links one at a time.
- Search 'cifra': 1,892 records (1,636 dated 1500-1549, mostly 1520-29; 78 for 1600-49, 72 for 1650-99). Row text gives date, place, description and the index's remark, but **not the signature**; 255 rows are originals 'en cifra' without the word descifr/traduc in the row, 387 rows are 'Texto descifrado' companions of other rows. Matching an original to its companion needs the ficha (the companion names the original's index number).
- Against the repo (`known.py`): the pre-1520 rows are all already in the ledgers (ia_pass2_c, catalogue 1041-1075); 1520-29 rows by Alonso Sanchez, Lope de Soria, Luis Fernandez de Cordoba, Lope Hurtado, Juan Manuel, Fernando Marin belong to existing target families (sanchez1522/1523, soria1523/1527, sessa1523/24, lopehurtado*; Tomokiyo 2025 AlonsoSanchez.htm) and were left; 90 rows remain by other senders: Felipe IV 1638-39 (9) and Austria (5) (A-87, Sarmiento 1638-39: `dudout_sarmiento_ms2093`), Medici cardinals 1655-56 (6), Juan Jose de Austria (2), Juan Perez 1526-27 (6), Herrera 1526 (2), Lannoy to Charles V 1523 (2), Prospero Colonna 1522-23 (2), Ludovico de Montalto 1522, Jeronimo and Antonioto Adorno 1522-23, Ramon de Cardona 1520 (target cardona1517 exists), Hugo de Moncada 1524 (target moncada1524), Mesina 1526, Diego de Aragon y Pignatelli 1554, Jeronimo de la Torre 1554, Guzman Ponce de Leon 1560, Vargas 1592, and 46 rows whose sender the row does not name.
