# Unread Hebrew in two Qumran Cryptic A entries

These leads concern surviving marks in a known substitution alphabet. The integer year is a sorting estimate for the Second Temple period. Neither fragment has an independently established year here.

- **4Q249r, joined fragments 2a–b, line 1.** Qumran-Digital leaves one sign unidentified before ḥet. Compare the readings reported in the [edition survey](https://zeitschrift-althebraistik.digitheo.de/ojs/index.php/zah/article/download/93138/92968) against DJD 36 plate XXXIX and the [IAA photograph of plate 598](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-496296). The digital transcription lists fragment 1 separately. Its first line is a different passage.
- **4Q249z, fragments 3 and 5.** Start with the unidentified signs flanking tav on fragment 3 line 2 and dalet on fragment 5 line 1. The miscellaneous collection spans several photographic plates. Match fragment contours in DJD 36 plates XLI–XLV against the archive before assigning a crop. [Online plate from the series](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-284441) · [Transcription](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q249z/2026-10-06/index.html?v=2026-10-06)

The two entries have distinct edition designations from the Midrash Moshe column and Serekh ha-Edah passage in PR #861. Count recovery only where a photographic trace supports the proposed letter. A supplied letter in a physical gap is a restoration.
