# Date of H. Saint Main’s letter to Fitzherbert

The entry is dated **18 January 1593** in Mary Anne Everett Green’s 1867 calendar, p. 309, item 18. The margin gives “Jan. 18.” against the name H. Saint Main. The same entry mentions a Parliament summoned for **19 February**, a different date within the letter. The current public catalogue entry 956 says 19 January. We could verify no support for that date in the calendar or the manuscript. The original is The National Archives, SP 12/244, ff.39–40. Gale supplies scans 40–42 in its Folger-accessible edition. [Original](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304381018/SPOL) · [Printed calendar, scan 325](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304300001/SPOL) · [Public catalogue at the commit examined](https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/blob/31eca71c141d48362531951c258ae96a3d0ad334/catalogue.json)

The closing is near the foot of f.40r on scan 41. Two of three readers independently reported “18 of Januarye after our accompt.” A third preferred 28, allowed 18, and thought the following word might read “afternoon.” The first reading accounts for the word at the start of the next line. The reverse carries a separate docket with 1592/3 and an abbreviated January. Two readers reported 18 there as well. Through-writing makes its day less distinct. Three independent readings preserve the ambiguity in the handwritten numeral. For the calendar date the printed page settles the earlier 18/19 discrepancy. No modern calendar conversion was attempted. The large 18 at the top of f.39r is the item number and was excluded as evidence for the day.

The supplied document comprises f.39r (scan 40), ff.39v–40r (scan 41) and f.40v (left side of scan 42). The right side of scan 42 starts another item. Several cipher passages on scan 41 have lighter interlinear English. No new plaintext is credited in this date and shelfmark correction.
