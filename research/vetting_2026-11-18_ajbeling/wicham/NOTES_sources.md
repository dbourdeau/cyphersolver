# The papers Winwood enclosed in April 1602

Wicham’s letter to Derick survives in a packet which Ralph Winwood sent to Cecil in Paris on 10 April 1602. *Memorials of Affairs of State*, I, p.404 prints the covering letter. Winwood says his informant gave him the letters, the ciphers and Sherwood’s address. In the examined edition another Winwood dispatch follows his signature. Mary Anne Everett Green’s **1870** calendar, pp.173–175, describes the individual enclosures. The **1867** calendar of 1591–1594 was the wrong volume in which to look for the Wicham enclosure. [Winwood, I, p.404](https://archive.org/details/memorialsofaffai01winw/page/404) · [Green, 1870, p.173](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_ELGK9pFXS50C/page/173)

The June manuscript (75.I) starts on the right of scan 162, SP 12/283a f.151r. Wicham dates his closing to the first of June on f.151v and signs it. The address is on f.152v. Green supplies the year in brackets, **1 June [1594]**. A later annotation on the address leaf gives the enclosure date in 1602. The facing recto, f.153r, carries a different September letter (75.II). Green dates it **21 September [1594?]**. There is no year in its closing. Folios 154 and 155 carry the third and fourth enclosures. The dates in Gale’s search-result metadata do not reliably distinguish the individual letter from the packet or preceding enclosure. [June original](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304680514/SPOL) · [September original](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304680515/SPOL)

Green explicitly lists **three keys** as enclosures 75.V–VII on p.175. The originals begin on f.156r and continue through f.158. Number tables, five shifted alphabets and a separate graphic alphabet survive. A source reader who had not seen the target letters transcribed each. The name-and-place lists contain 102 entries. Of those, 65 have an unambiguous label and a legible, unique literal counterpart under the reader’s conservative rule. The key predates our work, and Green had already located it. Our contribution is the correction of the repository’s source identification and a documented comparison of actual cipher positions with this archived table. [Printed key listing](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_ELGK9pFXS50C/page/175) · [Original keys](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304680518/SPOL)

The public repository’s lead 903 groups several references. The January search extract is John Bristowe [Moody] to William White, 28 January 1592, Green (1867) p.175, item 26. It mentions Allen. This is catalogue 955. The Portuguese item is the separate Andrada memorandum in the same calendar, p.16, item 68, already recorded as lead 1007. The 14 March 1593 draft is Barnes to Paget (lead 957). The June Wicham item is a real enclosure in the later volume. The year 1594 in a group heading does not apply to all these pieces. [Bristowe letter in Green](https://archive.org/details/cu31924091775274/page/175) · [Andrada item](https://archive.org/details/cu31924091775274/page/16)
