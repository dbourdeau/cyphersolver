# Condé network name key of 1651-1652, AD Dordogne 2 E 1841/18 (from PR #937, 27 Nov 2026)

One lead, filed by the maintainers at the integration of PR #937 (conde1651, Brice Dudout). The bundle AD Dordogne,
2 E 1841/18 (papers of Armand du Lau du Chambon) holds, besides the five 1651 cipher letters read in `targets/conde1651/`,
a period name table of another nomenclator (frames P1370715-717, "Noms propres de personnes, villes, bourgs et
rivières"). It is a second key in the same folder, so by the one-key-per-entry rule it is a lead of its own.

What is known comes from `targets/conde1651/NOTES.md` ("The Roman-numeral name table") and its transcription
`targets/conde1651/key_names_1652.tsv`:

- 110 entries (46 persons, 64 places) under initials A-V, Roman-numeral codes 17-186, plus a "Nombre" table that maps
  numbers to the capitals A-Z. Codes 1-16 and any letter or syllable table are missing: one part of a larger key.
- The names fit the Saintonge, Aunis and Guyenne war of the princes' Fronde, late 1651 to 1652; "M. de Chambon" (45) is
  du Lau himself, so the copy is his.
- It is not the key of the 1651 letters: 0 of 9 known name codes agree. None of the letters in the bundle uses it.
- Not in Brianson (BSHAP 1983), his key sheets or typescripts in the bundle, catalogue.json, leads.json or targets/
  (checked by the contributor, 23 Nov 2026).

The photographs were taken for the project by Catherine Schunck (SHAP) and are not distributed; the table was
transcribed by AI agents run by Brice Dudout and has not been checked by a person on the images. No cipher letter in
this key has been identified. The project has not seen the images and has not searched DECODE, Tomokiyo or Lasry for
this table: the lead is not vetted.

`candidates.json` holds the one row; it was merged into `research/leads.json` with
`research/archive_sweep_2026-10/merge.py --write`.
