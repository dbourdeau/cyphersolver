Conventions used
- [7] covers both the looped form (small circle joined to the left end of the top bar, as in the sheet example) and the plain "7"/narrow "1"-like form; both forms occur often.
- [8] covers the closed figure-8 (no stem) and the open-top form whose two upper arms cross or splay like a Greek gamma (as in the sheet example from B09). The gamma-like ones are marked 8?.
- [d+] is the sign with a loop at the foot, a tall stem and a crossbar near the top.
- [F] = F with a filled blob on the middle bar; [Fo] = F with an open small o at the right of the middle bar (B05, B08, B10, B11).
- 12, 13, 14 are written as numerals. "14" (A20) is not a sheet class; I wrote what I see.
- Clear words: {Cum} (A01, B06), {Porro} (A01, A08, B10), {Spo} {Dar} (A12). "Ras" in A03 uses the [Ras] code.

Doubtful places
- A03, B03, A16: O.? = circle with a horizontal bar or small loop inside (theta-like) rather than a clear centre dot; there is no theta class on the sheet, so I coded it O.
- A05: qo? before "a" has a P-shaped head over a foot loop instead of a round circle; #? before "n b" is drawn as a square box with the strokes running past it (could be a separate sign; nearest class is #).
- A09: first sign looks like #o with only one crossbar (#o?); the 2nd and 4th signs and the one after "a" are open-top or gamma-like 8s (8?); 3? = small reversed-c shape without the inner tick of 3.
- A10: the last two signs (k f) are damaged by a hole at the right edge; f? is uncertain.
- A15, A19, B03, B11: 8? = gamma-like open-top 8 (loop at the bottom, two arms rising).
- A17: q+? and the two qo? signs have open, horn-like tops instead of closed circles. The crossbar or foot loop decides the code.
- A19, B12, B18, B20 (twice): ^? = short caret "ʌ" with no ascender; the sheet [^] example has a longer top stroke. This could be a separate sign or a short [lam].
- A21: qo? after q+ has a P-shaped head.
- B01: the last sign is partly lost in a hole; what survives looks like n (n?).
- B05: ?? = small loop (o- or u shape?) at the paper fold just before the final 3. It is partly obscured, so I cannot code it.
- B06: the end "a T 8" was checked as cipher signs. It does not look like the Latin word "atq".
- B07: final U? at a hole (cup with a descending tail).
- B08: ?? = a damaged trace at a hole at the line end (a dark blot with a thin tail stroke to the right). It may be a sign or only damage.
- B09: final qo? faint at the fold.
- B10: final 7? broken by a hole.
- B11, B12: final n? partly in the fold or hole.
- B13: final Pi? partly at the fold.
- B18: final q+? has a filled or blotted head.
- Pi vs PI: I coded Pi when the right leg is hooked or carries a rightward tail and PI when both legs are plain. A few small ones (A05 PI, B04 PI) are borderline.
