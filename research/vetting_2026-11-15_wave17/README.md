# Wave 17 vetting (15-16 Nov 2026)

Vetting research for 22 leads with images online and no vetting block. The 15 Nov run fetched images
(`img/`, git-ignored) and was cut off by a rate limit before writing anything; the 16 Nov session
(Claude Opus 5.5) viewed them, fetched what was missing, did the prior-art checks and wrote
`proposals.json` (generated by `make_proposals.py`). Nothing was applied to `research/leads.json` or
`catalogue.json`; the lead agent applies the proposals.

Scripts: `fetch.py` (one image, polite, writes a reduced `_v.jpg`), `crop.py` (crop by fractions),
`make_proposals.py`. Run Python with `python -I -X utf8`.
