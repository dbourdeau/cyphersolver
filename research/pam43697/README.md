# Cryptic B on PAM 43.697, fragment 50

PAM 43.697, fragment 50 preserves four comparably legible signs in Cryptic B. Applying Emmanuel Oliveiro’s published alphabet gives the provisional reading **pe? · lamed · {yod/nun} · vav**, listed from the rightmost sign to the left. The hooked foot of the *lamed* adds independent evidence for Antony Perrot’s proposed attribution to **4Q362**. The two uncertain positions and the absence of secure word boundaries leave the wording unresolved.

## Earlier work

Perrot (2019, pp. 310–312) identified the Cave 4 scrap as Cryptic B and proposed that it belonged to 4Q362. He compared each of the four signs with a 4Q362 parallel. His comparisons to *qof*, *lamed*, a sideways Z, and *tav* describe the shapes as he saw them before the decipherment. Oliveiro (2026, p. 160 n. 6) agreed that one sign resembles the 4Q362 *vav* and measured it at 3.9 mm, compared with 2.2–3.4 mm for the manuscript’s examples. He set the fragment aside in his decipherment. The comparison below uses his photographic alphabet and descriptions, especially Table 1, pp. 166–168, and the discussion on pp. 169–172. [Perrot](https://doi.org/10.2143/RQ.31.2.3287248) · [Oliveiro](https://doi.org/10.1163/15685179-bja10074)

## Sign readings

The numbering follows Perrot. The comparisons use contrast-adjusted views of the 2015 infrared photograph and the published examples, with the three positions other than the *vav* assessed by their own visible features. [Fragment photograph](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-505778) · [Oliveiro’s illustrated alphabet](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/1536541346/dsd-article-p159_4.pdf)

| Position | Reading | Visible evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **פ?** (*pe*) | A dark shoulder above and to the right of the hole, together with a stroke extending below its left side, fits a right-hand bowl on a long descender. The hole interrupts the connection between these features. |
| 2 | **ל** (*lamed*) | A long shaft turns right at the foot and ends in a thick return, consistent with the hooked foot of 4Q362 *lamed*. The fine outline of the terminal loop is unclear. The two illustrated 4Q363 *lameds* have a simple open foot. |
| 3 | **{י/נ}** (*yod* or *nun*) | The leaning upright turns right at the base. The distinguishing feature of the illustrated 4Q362 *yod* is a downward tip at the far right. Dark material extends below the bend on the fragment, but a continuous terminal stroke is untraceable. The apparent height depends on whether this material is included. |
| 4 | **ו** (*vav*) | The angular top and descending central stroke support Oliveiro’s reading. His measurement is 0.5 mm above the maximum he reports for 4Q362. The resemblance and the size difference both matter for attribution. |

### Direct checks on the proposed pe

In **IAA-901-9, line 2**, the sign Oliveiro reads as *pe* has a clear right-hand bowl and a long descender. The neighboring *resh* loops to the left. In **IAA-901-22, line 2**, the sign between the two *hes* has a narrower bowl and a more slanted stem. The surviving strokes of fragment 50 are compatible with these forms, especially the narrower example, with the bowl-to-shaft join unresolved. The links open directly on the cited line. [IAA-901-9, line 2](https://iaa.iiifhosting.com/iiif/80a2df13adddf03db01331c72e908cb9bcb3f5e3222cd09af1b6624ee0272776/3307,2174,327,203/full/0/default.jpg) · [IAA-901-22, line 2](https://iaa.iiifhosting.com/iiif/67b424c47503dfc3f3fc1dea34fb080232e150317a80d709da4774c90f1d7162/3689,2637,328,226/full/0/default.jpg)

Table 1 counts one 4Q362 *pe*. Oliveiro’s transcriptions print an unmarked *pe* at both loci above (pp. 175, 180–181), and a further tentative *pe* in fragments 9–10 (p. 177). The two photographed signs support the shape comparison. The printed count cannot independently corroborate Perrot’s report of a single 4Q362 parallel for sign 1. [Oliveiro](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/1536541346/dsd-article-p159_4.pdf)
