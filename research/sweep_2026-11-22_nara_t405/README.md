# NARA T405 sweep (2026-11-22)

Run by Jacob Houtrouw (TheLightningCount) with Claude (Anthropic), model `claude-opus-5-5`, in two sessions: the survey and the reading of the frames on 13 Nov 2026, and the duplicate and prior-art checks, this folder and a check of two frames at full size on 22 and 23 Nov 2026. The second session revised it on 25 Nov 2026, after the maintainer's review of 23 Nov. It is the lead asked for on #542 on 14 Nov 2026 (https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver/issues/542#issuecomment-6879298673).

## Routes
- **NARA's catalogue search** for the file units of T405, "Number 1" to "Number 64": 63 found. Not returned: 19, 28 (1 of 2), 50, 58 (1 of 2) and 60 (1 of 2).
- **NARA's page text,** its own machine reading of the frames, from `catalog.archives.gov/proxy/extractedText/<NAID>`, fetched once for each unit on 13 Nov 2026.
- **The frame images,** from `catalog.archives.gov/medialz/dc-metro/rg-242/7794463/T405/...`, free and without a login.
- Nothing blocked.

## How it was searched
There were no search terms. The page text was filtered for runs of five-letter groups, the form of the 1939-40 traffic on roll 9 (catalogue entry 704). By the survey's note, on roll 9, where 24 frame ranges hold cipher groups, a filter of at least 10 five-letter tokens making up over 35% of a frame's tokens finds all 24.
- **Rolls 1 to 31** (31 units: roll 12 in two parts, of roll 28 only its second part, and no roll 19): the filter ran frame by frame. Besides roll 9, it found two frames of roll 1 (below) and a few frames on rolls 2, 5, 16 and 18. Those hold lists of ranks, names, abbreviations and place names, not cipher.
- **Rolls 32 to 64** (32 units): the survey's output holds one run for each, without frame numbers, so the filter did not run frame by frame on them, and why is not known. **They are not searched.**
- **Roll 1, frames 1048-1100,** were then opened as images: all 53 on a contact sheet, and frames 1056, 1093 and 1094 row by row at 0.8 of full size, with doubtful letters zoomed to 2.0.
- **Frames 1056 and 1094 were opened again at full size on 23 Nov 2026** (3632 px wide, 300 dpi) and looked over for any writing, in the margins and between the rows. There is none between the rows. The margins hold the registry's hand only: the documents' numbers and days, the receipt stamps' entries, pencilled folio numbers, two short marks, and on frame 1094 the journal number. None of it is a word or a group of letters.

## Finds
Three typed exercise messages ("Uebungsspruch") of the Reichswehr in five-letter groups, in the files of the 3. (Preußische) Fahrabteilung, Berlin-Lankwitz, 1933:
1. **Frame 1056** (a lead, row 1): from the 3. Division, Abt. Stonach, Berlin, 18 November 1933. Received on 24.11.33 and filed as 808/33. 86 letters. No decipherment on the frame, read row by row at 0.8 of full size and looked over at full size. The other frames of 1048-1100, apart from 1093 and 1094, were seen on a contact sheet at 0.04 of full size, and those of 1054-1058, 1092, 1095 and 1096 also in NARA's page text; none of them was read. **The answer is not identified.** Frame 1055, by NARA's page text the Fahrabteilung's draft cover letter of 30.11.33, sends "die Antwort auf den Übungsspruch vom 28.11.33". By the maintainer's review it is filed as 808/33 too (the page text gives "808/73"). The filing links the two frames, but the dates differ: this message is of 18 November and was received on 24 November. So that frame 1055 answers it is provisional, and the answer itself was not found.
2. **Frame 1094** (a lead, row 2): from the 3. Division, Stonach, Berlin, 22 July 1933. Received on 25.7.33. 108 letters. No decipherment on the frame, read row by row at 0.8 of full size and looked over at full size. A handwritten note on frame 1093 headed "aufgenommen" may be its decipherment, but as we read it the note does not fit this text's letters (row 2, `status`). No person has read that note, so it counts as unread here: row 2 holds only if the note is not this message's decipherment.
3. **Frame 1093** (left out): the Fahrabteilung's typed draft answer ("Entwurf") of July 1933 to frame 1094's message, 112 letters. Below the signature a handwritten note headed "Befördert" gives its plain text. As we read the note, its visible words account for 94 of the 112 letters, all of them among the message's. The 18 left would be a word that the folio number covers, and no German word we tried fits them. A cipher filed with its decipherment is not a lead (the sweep brief), so it has no row. It is the reason both rows are class A: a sibling deciphered in the same file. **That basis is provisional.** Frame 1093 cites frame 1094's message by its number and date, but the note's plain text is aligned with only 94 of its 112 letters, and no common system for the July and November messages is established. On 14 Nov 2026 a sealed plan tested single and double columnar transposition on frame 1093, with its plain text as a crib, and missed on every run. Nothing was run on frames 1056 and 1094.

**The system is not identified.** The index of coincidence is 0.070 over the 86 letters of frame 1056 and 0.072 over the 108 of frame 1094, so the letters are unevenly spread. Evenly spread letters, such as the Enigma gives, come to about 0.038 at these lengths, give or take about 0.003. None of 100,000 random draws of evenly spread letters reached 0.070. The figures alone do not identify the system. Frames 1058 and 1092 are lists of key documents issued, among them "Heeres-M-Schlüssel" Nr. 253 and 261 (NARA's page text); they name key documents and hold no key, so they are not key leads.

No person has checked the reading of the groups or of the handwritten notes.
