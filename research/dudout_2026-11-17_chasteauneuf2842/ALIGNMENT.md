# Catalogue 2842 (BnF fr. 15571): the two cipher blocks set against the slip of decipherment

Brice Dudout (L'Europe chiffrée), 17 Nov 2026, answering issue #744. Transcription of the signs and alignment by Claude
(Anthropic) from the Gallica images, one pass, not checked by a second reader. No image is included; images: Source
gallica.bnf.fr / BnF, ark:/12148/btv1b90618802.

## Result in short

- **Foot block (3 lines at the foot of the verso of the leaf foliated 34, view 40)**: aligned sign by sign with the slip,
  lines 1-3 up to "le Roy d'Espaigne". 85 of 92 cipher signs line up with the slip text, 1 of them doubtful; 7 do not (listed below; recounted 18 Nov).
- **Block at the top of the leaf foliated 36 (catalogue "f. 37", view 41), 6 lines**: aligned in stretches only. The
  stretches that line up are in the slip's order and cover all six lines ("Chose que [le roy] leur ay", "& des
  protestans", "en donnent occasion par le secours", "donner a ceux de la religion pour les maintenir contre les edicts
  du Roy"): 109 of 189 signs. 80 signs are **not aligned**: the rest of line 1, all of line 2, and short stretches in
  lines 3-6. For those the table values do not give the slip's words, or the slip was not read with enough certainty.
- So the alignment holds for the foot block and for 58 % of the top block; it is **not complete** for the
  top block. What it shows is that the slip deciphers the top block as well, since its words come in the same order over
  all six lines. It is not a full sign-by-sign proof.

## What this changes

- The slip deciphers the foot block (lines 1-3 to "d'Espaigne"): the alignment holds sign by sign, with 7 signs out (85 of 92 line up, 1 of them doubtful; recount of 18 Nov, PR #782).
- The slip also follows the top block: its later words ("& des protestans", "en donner occasion par le secours",
  "veulent donner a ceux de la religion pour les maintenir contre les edits du Roy") are in the top block, in the same
  order, and the block ends with the "le roy" sign where the slip ends "du Roy". This supports the reading that the slip
  deciphers both blocks in one run.
- It is not a complete alignment: line 2 and most of line 1 of the top block are not aligned, so the claim rests on the
  stretches above. A second reader of the slip (lines 3-4) and of the top block's lines 1-2 would settle it.
- Tomokiyo's remark (more words in the deciphered text before "protestans" than in the cipher) fits: if the slip is set
  against the foot block alone, the words from "Chose" to "protestans" have no cipher there. They are in the top block.
- For the table: several signs needed here are not in Tomokiyo's table (≡, Ꞩ, 5̶, ꝉ, ∀̶), and the 9-with-tail sign
  lines up with d in "donnent"/"donner" and with u in "depuis", "peu", "veulent". The "le roy(?)" code (a) is written
  "le" by the slip once and "Roy" once.
