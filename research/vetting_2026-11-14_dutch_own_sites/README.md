Run by a Claude Code session (Claude Sonnet 5.5). `sweep_2026-11-14_dutch_own_sites` (12 rows) merged as 2712-2723
(region and period values needed no change). Evidence is in `vetting.json` (built by `make.py`). Nothing is online for any of them
(Tresoar scan_count 0, NA clevel scans empty), so every vetting is `not online` from the archive's own description; the one
digitised item (2714) is a key sheet.

- **Promoted (3):** 2538 (Duke George of Saxony to Pflug 1514, 29 ciphered letters, 14 with a clear copy; the older lead covering the same bundle as
  2712, kept as one entry because it is one sender, recipient and key), 2713 (narrowed to Willem Lodewijk's four wholly ciphered letters 5592.53-56 of
  1593 with the cipher list 5592.52 as key in volume; catalogue 2547 has the partly ciphered 5592.23/.45/.51), 2716 (Parma/Rennenberg fiche, Brussels originals).
- **Closed (3):** 2712 duplicate of 2538; 2715 duplicate of catalogue 1982 (KHA A24-8); 2717 not a cipher (Greek-language passages).
- **Left as leads (6):** 2714 (Vogelesangh key sheet, viewed), 2719-2723 (NA key books, physical only), 2718 (introduction text only). All are keys without ciphertext.
- **Link to targets:** the key list Tresoar 323 inv. 5592.52 (used by Willem Lodewijk and the Frisian States, c. 1593) is the best candidate for the
  missing key of `targets/casimir1618` scan 027 (Willem Lodewijk, 5 Apr 1594); needs a Tresoar scan request. NA 1.10.110 nos. 184-186 are probably a copy of
  the Battier key held for `targets/battier1686` (NA 1.10.29 inv. 1209); physical only.
- **Not reached:** Rous 2022 (Steiner) sect. 4.1.1 on George's cipher and the Radboud full text of Ros 1964 (403) were not read; both could carry a key or decipherment.
