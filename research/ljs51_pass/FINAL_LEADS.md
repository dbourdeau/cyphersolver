Status: NOT published. Nothing here is in catalogue.json, research/leads.json, docs/ or targets/. Held for review.

Method: 212 pages read by agents from OPenn web JPEGs (1422 px); 209 cipher chains; 28 apparent gaps re-checked on the
images; three leads re-checked on the master TIFFs (2339 x 2959); DECODE (10,106 record summaries, read 27 Nov) has no
Arabic/Mamluk/Penn record; prior art (search-engine pass) found no study, edition or decipherment of LJS 51.
All Arabic readings are model readings (low to medium confidence); sign counts are approximate.

## A. Open: meets "ciphertext seen, prior art searched, duplicates searched, verdict open"

1. **f. 73v lines 8-12 (about 70 signs) with f. 74r lines 3-7 (about 76 signs).** Two ciphers on master TIFF; no
   plaintext found for the first; the only nearby plaintext (f. 74v l.2, 35 letters) is too short for either block.
   The two blocks do not share frequent signs, so they are not shown to be one alphabet. Key: none in the volume.
   Confidence: medium. Weakest of the open items; the compiler may give the answer in a lost or later remark.

## B. Open, but vetting incomplete (ciphertext seen only on web images)

2. **f. 12v l.11 to f. 13r l.4.** The f. 13r lines 1-4 are an angular sign style and may be a separate cipher with no
   plaintext; the pairing of the rest is inferred. Needs a master-TIFF check.
3. **f. 4v l.8 to f. 5r l.2 (about 123 signs).** Proposed plaintext (f. 5v l.6-8) shows about 96 letters: plaintext
   possibly missing, nulls present, or wrong pairing. Needs a master-TIFF check.

## C. Not open: the compiler's plaintext is on the page; the key is not recorded (corpus / key-recovery targets)

4. **f. 63v, tree figure ("qalam mushajjar").** 79 signs = 79 trees = 79 red letters = 79 letters of the couplet written
   in the margin turned 90 degrees (the page record had said no plaintext; that was wrong). A complete crib for two
   scripts: the sign alphabet and the tree script. No published decipherment of a tree script was found in the prior-art pass.
5. **f. 61r l.3-11 / f. 61v l.1-4.** The "non-words" on f. 61v are real antonym imperatives (a riddle), not a cipher.
   The cipher is on f. 61r (about 105-115 signs vs about 125 letters): known plaintext, no key recorded.
6. **ff. 14v-16v tashifi run.** Plaintext covers the whole run (770 letters vs about 766 signs, 94% alignment, key
   learned from the same alignment). Key: 15 sign classes of look-alike letters. Clean benchmark case.
7. **Red-glossed blocks (23 pages):** ff. 1r (plaintext lost, gloss survives), 1v, 31r, 37r, 51v, 52v, 53r, 54v, 57v, 59v,
   87r, 87v, 88r, 89v, 90r, 90v, 91r, 93v, 95v, 97r, 97v. The gloss is the plaintext letter over each sign
   (compiler states it on f. 64v). Not verified sign by sign.
8. **About 200 further chains** answered by the compiler's plaintext, keys not recorded.
