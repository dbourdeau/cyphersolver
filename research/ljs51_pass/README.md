Page-by-page pass over the 212 text pages of Penn LJS 51 (OPenn web images and master TIFFs) and the vetting of the
ciphers in the volume that no plaintext answers. The full corpus work (master transcription of 211 cipher chains, keys,
translations, identification of the compiler as very probably Ibn al-Durayhim) is in `targets/ljs51corpus/`.

- `candidates.json` (written by `make_candidates.py`): five hard open ciphers, merged into `research/leads.json` and
  promoted through the vetting gate on 28 Nov 2026 as catalogue 3938-3942 (ff. 1r, 12v l.8-10, 12v l.11-13, 3v-4r, 98v).
  The sixth open cipher, f. 30v l.7-13 (81 signs), was deliberately not promoted.
