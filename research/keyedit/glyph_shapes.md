# Glyph shapes per token (letters deliberately omitted)
Tokens not listed have no shape note. Family = look-alike group.

- `4`: plain closed 4, stem going below the line  (family 4)
- `4_`: 4 with a small foot serif at the stem bottom  (family 4)
- `z4`: 4 with a hooked lead-in curling left off the crossbar (ɀ4), or z joined to 4  (family 4)
- `4y`: 4 whose crossbar ends in a curl / ɣ-like  (family 4)
- `X`: compact ✗ with a horizontal BAR through the crossing (ӿ), or with the right leg in a J-hook  (family X)
- `#x`: large plain ✗, long straight strokes, no bar, usually at word/line end  (family X)
- `x`: small plain x, no bar  (family X)
- `Xb`: ✗ with a horizontal bar, larger (seen once for b)  (family X)
- `+`: compact + with bar at mid-height  (family +)
- `Lo`: ⊥: long bar ON THE BASELINE, vertical rising from it  (family +)
- `II`: tall ∐ with bars/serifs  (family I)
- `I`: plain Ɨ  (family I)
- `Ib`: bold-footed Ɨ  (family I)
- `o`: round closed δ with a straight ascender  (family δ)
- `dc`: δ whose ascender curls over into a long hook to the RIGHT  (family δ)
- `Q`: big open upper loop over a closed bowl / ∂ with curled top; also the 8-shaped closed top  (family δ)
- `D`: small open curl, no ascender (∂ small)  (family δ)
- `pi`: plain π  (family π)
- `tt`: π with a hooked tail on the right leg (ϖ)  (family π)
- `A`: A  (family A)
- `6^`: 6 whose top is a FLAT BAR running right (б)  (family 6)
- `6`: 6 with a rounded top  (family 6)
- `6c`: 6 with a loop/flourish on top  (family 6)
- `8`: plain free-standing 8  (family 8)
- `Z8`: 8 with a horizontal stroke through the waist, usually a lead-in from the left  (family 8)
- `28`: 28  (family 8)
- `56`: compact 56  (family 56)
- `s6`: flourished: long hooked ʃ-like 5, 6 set high  (family 56)
- `60`: 60  (family 60)
- `T`: flat ⊤  (family T)
- `Tp`: T with hooked/cross top and a descender (ፐ)  (family T)
- `Tz`: T with a z- or 2-tail  (family T)
- `Tr`: small T with a hook to the right  (family T)
- `12`: "12" / "1τ"  (family g)
- `q`: closed ring/ball on a straight stem (ϙ)  (family ϙ)
- `q9`: 9 with an open descender  (family ϙ)
- `E`: square E  (family E)
- `e3`: round epsilon ε  (family E)
- `H`: ℋ  (family H)
- `Hh`: ℋ with a 6-shaped lead-in loop  (family H)
- `7`: 7 / ɤ with a crossbar  (family 7)
- `fr`: small plain r  (family r)
- `rr`: single tall ʃ with a curled foot  (family ʃ)
- `w`: vv  (family vv)
- `3`: 3  (family misc)
- `f`: TALL crossed long-s  (family misc)
