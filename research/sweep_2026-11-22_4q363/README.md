# Two unresolved readings in 4Q363

Oliveiro published a decipherment of the Hebrew Cryptic B manuscripts in *Dead Sea Discoveries*. The project's earlier Hebrew survey already records that work. These leads identify two remaining problems in the author's transcription. The numeric year is a sorting estimate for the Second Temple period. [Published edition](https://doi.org/10.1163/15685179-bja10074)

- **Fragment 3, line 2.** Two consecutive signs between mem and nun remain unread. The line above repeats a phrase on fragment 1, which supplies examples of the same hand. Use the repeated line to calibrate comparisons before testing the lower line. [Target photograph](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-475159) · [Fragment 1 comparison](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-475151)
- **Fragment 6, line 1.** A damaged zigzag is the edition's sole tentative example of zayin. The missing lower portion limits identification. Record which surviving strokes favor each candidate and whether the identification can be narrowed. [Target photograph](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-475171)

The photographs are individual rectos. Their numbering agrees with sections 5.2.3 and 5.2.6 of the edition and with the [IAA group photograph](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-496249).
