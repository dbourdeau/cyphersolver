# Two Gifford dispatches in the Cotton notes

Cotton Caligula B VIII, f. 328v, gives the June dispute about a cardinalate and Englefield’s part in it. Across the opening, **f. 329r begins Gifford’s May 9 dispatch**. The four men who dealt with Captain White, White’s hanging at Flushing and the merchant’s fifty-year banishment follow in the same order as SP 12/252 f. 19r. Cotton writes **Liggons** above 43 in the June passage and twice in the May passage. The surname also occurs as *Ligons* in running prose. This identifies the person meant by the contemporary annotator to the extent of a surname. Green leaves the opening speaker as 43. The revised annotations near 93 and 94 support Fuentes at 93 and leave Ibarra uncertain at 94. [Cotton opening 333, ff. 328v–329r](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4318883115/SPOL) · [May original](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304480214/SPOL) · [Green, no. 8, p. 36](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_ozz8aAZ_niQC/page/n65/mode/2up)

Cotton f. 329r gives **“with one Captaine / White of Flushing”** where May group T08 yielded **ONE**. The registered May test still scored zero against the independent interlinear reading *our*. Cotton supplies post-test corroboration of this one word at its historical position. F. 329v explicitly marks the dispatch’s **15 May** continuation. On f. 330r, reports about Fitzherbert’s book, George Somerset and William Worthington continue in the order Green calendared under no. 8. An overwritten gloss at 31 resembles *Dr Worthington*, complicating the suggested Stanley reading elsewhere. No additional unglossed group was deciphered. [Cotton openings 333–334](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4318883115/SPOL) · [Green, pp. 37–38](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_ozz8aAZ_niQC/page/n66/mode/2up)

Green's no. 9 is a separately filed contemporary decipher of the May dispatch. It survives at The National Archives, SP 12/252, ff. 21r–23v. Five pages carry the text, with the endorsement on f. 22v. At the opening, the original on f. 19r and the Cotton copy give **43**. In the corresponding sentence on f. 21r the figure appears as **45**, with no name above it. The copy still labels 23 as Paget and 36 as Dr Gifford. Farther down the page it gives “one / Captayne white at Flushing”. Both contemporary copies thus support *one*, although the relationship between the copies is unknown. [Original f. 19r](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304480214/SPOL) · [Five-page decipher, beginning on f. 21r](https://link.gale.com/apps/doc/MC4304480215/SPOL) · [Green, no. 9 on p. 38](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_ozz8aAZ_niQC/page/n67/mode/1up)

The Cotton copy also helps with two earlier ambiguities. On f. 327v, 54 appears twice in the report about people sent to kill the unnamed person. The sentence reads “his matter is almost ripe”. Neither instance of the number has a personal name. On that same folio *Fitzherbert* stands over 31. On the facing f. 328r a gloss over 31 resembles *Dr Worthington*. These competing attributions explain why 31 remains open.
