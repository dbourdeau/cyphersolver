# Breslau cipher, 2 May 1577: source transcription draft

BnF fr. 3198 no. 37, fol. 75, Gallica canvas `f77`. The same passage occurs in the near-verbatim copy no. 38, fol. 76, canvas `f79`. Neither copy supplies a decipherment. This file preserves the digit stream and visible points without imposing a two-digit grouping or a key. `?` is an uncertain figure; `.` is a visible point, whose cipher function is unproved. The long strings below are editorial joins across small visual spaces, not manuscript word division.

Context in no. 37: “Il suivra encore la Court le temps de deux ans pour estre receu aux affaires d'icelle [cipher]. Le 2e du present …”

Context in no. 38: “Il suivra encore la Court le temps de deux ans pour estre receu aux affaires de la Court: [cipher]. Ceulx qui sont du conseil secret suivans la Court sont les deux archiducques et les seigneurs Harrach et Trautson.”

36.43136.5315551935431513556.130433021345.

The opening `330213456.` and close `130433021345.` are secure as individual figures. The runs after `plusieurs` are first-pass readings; digit order around `53/35` on line 2 needs comparison with no. 38. The points after 6 are drawn consistently, but may distinguish a symbol rather than punctuation.

The two sources appear to differ at the beginning of the second line: `53…` in no. 37 versus `35…` in no. 38. Reinspect the originals before selecting either value for cryptanalysis. Other apparent variation may be palaeographic, especially the writer's 2 and 7.
