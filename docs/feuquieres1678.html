Louis XIV to Marquis de Feuquières, 9 December 1678

Louis XIV's instructions to Feuquières discuss Sweden's military plans and the risk of sending a fleet into the Baltic. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 1,920 elements, 1,169 identified (60.9%).

03 The decipherment

f156r, lines 6–8

pouvoir reduire entierement les ennemis de la Suede par la force, que je crois important de profiter des dispositions où ils paroissent de s’accommoder avec cette Couronne,

Translation. to overcome Sweden’s enemies completely by force; I consider it important to take advantage of their apparent willingness to reach an accommodation with that Crown.
