…que no se les puede dar, y assi sera de la prudencia de S.S. embiar al legado y a Juan Francisco las ordenes …

BnF Français 23054 · contributed by George Lasry · Spanish

Philip II / Archduke Albert to Duke of Sessa / Philip II, 31 March 1597 / 25 August 1596

Philip II discusses Ottoman negotiations, aid to Transylvania and the Emperor, and the conversion of Condé’s mother. An earlier letter reports the capture of Hulst and payments to the army. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 379 elements, 340 identified (89.7%).

03 The decipherment

f145r, full cipher lines 6–8

…que no se les puede dar, y assi sera de la prudencia de S.S. embiar al legado y a Juan Francisco las ordenes convenientes de los socorros que deven hazer en este año con el Emperador y el transilva[no].

Translation. …which cannot be given to them. Thus His Holiness should prudently send the legate and Juan Francisco the appropriate instructions for the assistance they are to provide this year with the Emperor and the Transylvanian [ruler].
