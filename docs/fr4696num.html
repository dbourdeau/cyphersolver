BnF Français 4696 · contributed by George Lasry · Italian

Not established to Not established, 1589?

Intercepted letters disclose political loyalties in France. The Pope knows the cipher; the writer urges changing it, writing more cautiously to Italy, and sending the Duke of Mantua news of France by a secure route. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 1,744 elements, 1,692 identified; 1 obscured group of unknown length.

03 The decipherment

f55r, full cipher lines 1–6

Piu sobria sara V.Ecc. nel scriuere in Italia, masime in modo che posi risapersi tanto. Sara meglio l’auuisare il serenisimo signor duca di Mantua frequentemente et per uia sicura de i sucesi di Francia. Sara sopra modo grato a S.A. a quale et a V.Ecc. tornara molto a conto.

Translation. The more restrained Your Excellency is in writing to Italy, especially in a way that could become known, the better. Informing the Most Serene Duke of Mantua frequently, and by a secure route, of events in France will be exceedingly welcome to His Highness and will greatly benefit both him and Your Excellency.
