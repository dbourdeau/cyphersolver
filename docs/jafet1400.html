Letter to Francesc Climent · cipher not imaged · 15 Sep 1400 · deciphered by Puig y Puig, 1920

Jafet to Climent, 1400

A cipher letter to Benedict XIII’s agent Francesc Climent, deciphered and printed by a Barcelona canon in 1920; the original is not imaged, so its cipher has never been seen here.

01 The letter

Francesc Climent, called Çapera, was Benedict XIII’s chamberlain and treasurer and later bishop of Barcelona; he left his papers to the cathedral chapter. Among them Sebastián Puig y Puig found fourteen letters partly or wholly in cipher, deciphered them, and printed them in the appendix of Pedro de Luna, último papa de Aviñón (1920). This is one of them: P. Jafet to Climent, 15 Sep 1400 (appendix XXXVII n. 2, pp. 477 n. 2; doc. 1043). Puig prints about 14 deciphered words. Only one deciphered sentence is printed, in a footnote: some of Climent’s letters sunt intercepte hodie et recuperate, were intercepted that day and recovered.

The originals are not digitised, and the archivist Josep Mas noted that Puig studied many of the Schism papers at home in 1920–30 and did not return them all. Puig’s print may be the only witness.

02 The cipher and its key

Puig never printed a key, and the only image of any of these ciphers is his facsimile of one page of Benedict XIII’s own letter of 22 June 1400. From that page the key of Benedict’s letters was rebuilt here: a plain substitution, one sign per letter. Puig writes of “la clave o claves”, the key or keys, he recovered, and nothing shows whether this letter, from another writer, used the same one. So it has its own entry. Benedict XIII’s letters are on Benedict XIII to Climent, 1399–1403 (a different or unverified cipher, with its own page); the other letters of his circle each have their own page for the same reason: Santa Cruz to Climent, 1399, Exea to Climent, 1408, Comuel to Climent, December 1415, Comuel to Climent, June 1416.

03 What remains

  • The ciphertext. No image of the letter exists, so its cipher, its key and Puig’s reading cannot be checked. Blocker: needs the original in the Arxiu Capitular de Barcelona.
  • The rest of the letter (doc. 1043), which Puig cites but does not print. Blocker: needs the original. Not printed; the ACB original is not digitised, and may be among the papers Puig did not return.
