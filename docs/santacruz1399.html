Santa Cruz to Climent, 1399

A cipher letter to Benedict XIII’s agent Francesc Climent, deciphered and printed by a Barcelona canon in 1920; the original is not imaged, so its cipher has never been seen here.

01 The letter

Francesc Climent, called Çapera, was Benedict XIII’s chamberlain and treasurer and later bishop of Barcelona; he left his papers to the cathedral chapter. Among them Sebastián Puig y Puig found fourteen letters partly or wholly in cipher, deciphered them, and printed them in the appendix of Pedro de Luna, último papa de Aviñón (1920). This is one of them: Fr. P. de Santa Cruz to Climent, 22 Oct 1399 (appendix XXIX, pp. 469–470; doc. 922). Puig prints about 100 deciphered words. The French ambassadors leave Avignon angry, and the besieged papal household is left in periculo mortis.

The originals are not digitised, and the archivist Josep Mas noted that Puig studied many of the Schism papers at home in 1920–30 and did not return them all. Puig’s print may be the only witness.

02 The cipher and its key

Puig never printed a key, and the only image of any of these ciphers is his facsimile of one page of Benedict XIII’s own letter of 22 June 1400. From that page the key of Benedict’s letters was rebuilt here: a plain substitution, one sign per letter. Puig writes of “la clave o claves”, the key or keys, he recovered, and nothing shows whether this letter, from another writer, used the same one. So it has its own entry. Benedict XIII’s letters are on Benedict XIII to Climent, 1399–1403 (a different or unverified cipher, with its own page); the other letters of his circle each have their own page for the same reason: Jafet to Climent, 1400, Exea to Climent, 1408, Comuel to Climent, December 1415, Comuel to Climent, June 1416.

03 What remains

  • The ciphertext. No image of the letter exists, so its cipher, its key and Puig’s reading cannot be checked. Blocker: needs the original in the Arxiu Capitular de Barcelona.
