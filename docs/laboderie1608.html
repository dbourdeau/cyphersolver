La Boderie to Pierre Jeannin, 7 June1608

A report from England on negotiations in the Low Countries, expressing concern about placing too much hope in one course of action. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 1023 elements,923 identified (90.2%).

03 The decipherment

f59v, four full cipher lines

assez mal fondees, de sorte que tant s’en faut que je me promette rien de bien de ce commerage. J’ai peur qu’il ne produise que du mespris de part et d’autre, et de part et d’autre il semble que on y chemine que par maniere d’acquit.

Translation. Quite ill-founded, so that I am far from expecting any good from this godparent relationship. I fear that it will produce only contempt on both sides, and on both sides it seems that people are merely going through the motions.
