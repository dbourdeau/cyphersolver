Excerpt and Translation

f59v, four full cipher lines

Excerpt

assez mal fondees, de sorte que tant s’en faut que je me promette rien de bien de ce commerage. J’ai peur qu’il ne produise que du mespris de part et d’autre, et de part et d’autre il semble que on y chemine que par maniere d’acquit.

Translation

Quite ill-founded, so that I am far from expecting any good from this godparent relationship. I fear that it will produce only contempt on both sides, and on both sides it seems that people are merely going through the motions.
