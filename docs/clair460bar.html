Excerpt and Translation

p285, three complete cipher rows

Excerpt

Que la moitié de l’argent que Vre Al a ordonné que l’on me donna, ce qui est la seulle cause de ce que je nay pas esté il y a six semaine en chemin pour vous aller…

Translation

…the half of the money that Your Highness ordered to be given to me, which is the sole reason why I did not set out six weeks ago to come to you…
