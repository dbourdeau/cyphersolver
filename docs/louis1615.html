“Louis XIII to Melchior de Sabran and to Brûlart de Léon, 1615–1631” (catalogue 191) combined letters in two different ciphers. Each now has its own page:

  • Louis XIII to Melchior de Sabran, Dijon, 28 March 1631 (DECODE R2748, the Louis XIII–Sabran cipher, solved by George Lasry)
  • Louis XIII to Brûlart de Léon, 1615 and 1617 (DECODE R2785, R2786, the Brûlart de Léon cipher, Satoshi Tomokiyo’s key)
