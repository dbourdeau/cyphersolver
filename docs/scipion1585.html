BnF Français 4696 / 4698 · contributed by George Lasry · Italian

Scipion de Gonzague

→

Confidential warnings and cavalry arrangements: horses, payments, dispatches and service in war. The Français 4698 fragment contains a request for an appeal or intercession; most of that letter remains unread. The Paris letter of 16 February1590 discusses friendship, the King of Spain and a request; its substantial gaps remain unread. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 3,078 recorded groups, 1,147 identified; 8 further groups with unresolved boundaries.

03 The decipherment

f62v, three complete body lines 5–7

giudico ? il aiutarle. Quanto poi a la persona sva egli si ?ferisce prontisimo a servirla et particolar[men]te in caso di difesa o vero di esaltatione di lei, fuor di questi ? solo de sua salute, ri[gu]ardo al interesse

Translation. …helping you. As for his own person, he [offers?] himself very ready to serve you, especially in defending or advancing your position. Apart from these matters, ? only his welfare, with regard to the interest…

05 Our check

This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch A. Image checked: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9059540q/f127.item
  • Folio matches the report: yes; cipher present: yes
  • What we checked: Fr. 4696 f62r: Italian letter "Ill.mo et Ecc.mo S. mio" with clear text (Padre Toledo, Giulio Cesare, Pasqua) and Greek-like graphic cipher runs; excerpt is from f62v, not imaged here; sign-level decode with his table not completed (glyph forms at this scan only loosely match his crops)
  • Verdict: partly confirmed (method key recovered based on adjacent plaintext, extent partial). The check is a sample, not a re-solution.
  • Notes: Only 1147/3078 groups identified (37%); 4698 letter mostly unread. Interlinear on f5r gave the key. Two volumes/senders bundled in one entry.
