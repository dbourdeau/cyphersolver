BnF Français 22225 · contributed by George Lasry · French

Louis-Sauveur, marquis de Villeneuve to Jean Otter, 29 June and 19 August 1737

French commercial and diplomatic plans in Persia, including an approach to Chah Nadir and a consul at Ispahan; reports of Ottoman, Russian and Imperial military affairs. The June letter repeats the commercial instructions and discusses Otter’s journey, oriental languages and political work. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 815 elements, 811 identified (99.5%).

03 The decipherment

f145v — beginning of the excerpt; f146r — cipher continuation and cleartext

J’ay fait icy le plu[s] de caresses que j’ay pu à Kerim Bey ; & quoique je n’aye pu avoir avec luy aucune conférence, j’ay lieu d’estre content des disposition favorables qu’il a fait paroître pour notre nation au personnes qui ont été luy parler de ma part. Il faut continuer de cultiver son amitié et luy témoigner la satisfaction que j’ay des marques qu’il m’a donné de la sienne.

Translation. I have shown Kerim Bey as much favour as I could. Although I have been unable to confer with him, I have reason to be pleased with the favourable disposition toward our nation that he showed to the people who spoke to him on my behalf. You must continue to cultivate his friendship and tell him how pleased I am with the signs he has given me of his own.

f145v

l’avenir un consul à Ispahan. Le raport que vous nous faires de ce que vous aures pu decourvrir des disposition de ce prince à ce sujet doit estre accompagné d’un[e] relation exacte & circonstanciee de la situation des affaires de Perse & de quelques refflection mesme sur le tour que vous pouves prévoir qu’elle prendront, pour eviter que les demarches que [le] Roy pourroit faire en cette occasion ne nous missent en conpromis

Translation. […] in future? a consul at Isfahan. Your report on what you have been able to discover about this prince’s intentions on that subject must be accompanied by an accurate? and detailed? account of the state of affairs in Persia, and some? reflections? on the course you can? foresee they will take, so that any steps the King might take on this occasion do not compromise us.

f143v — five completecipherlines

Je vous fis conn[oistre] avant votre depart d’icy qu’il conviendroit au intérêt de la France de retablir le commerce que nous avions autrefois en Perse. Cette idée, qui ne venoit allors que de moy, me paroît avoir esté goutée.

Translation. Before you left here I made [known] to you that it would be in France’s interest to re-establish the trade we formerly had in Persia. This idea, which then came only from me, seems to have been welcomed.
