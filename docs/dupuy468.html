Ernestus dux and Joachimus; probably Ernst of Brunswick-Lüneburg and Joachim von Maltzan to Francis I, King of France, Eve of Saint Paul, at five in the morning; no year. Context suggests c.1518–1520;1519 remains

03 The decipherment

f28r, final four cipher lines

Tunc optimum erit ut christianissimus hoc fedus petat velle inire; hoc modo etiam si aliquando noluissent esse in federe cu[m] christianissimo, hoc modo astricti erunt esse confederatores christianissimi.

Translation. Then it will be best for the Most Christian King to ask to join this alliance. In this way, even if some had not wished to ally themselves with him, they will be bound to become his allies.
