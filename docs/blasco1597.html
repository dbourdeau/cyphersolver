BnF Français 23054 · contributed by George Lasry · Spanish

Juan Blasco de Aragon to Not established, 2 May 1597

Juan Blasco reports court negotiations, the prospect of coming with the princess, and efforts to obtain royal favors. He discusses a gift for Don Cristóbal and the support of Don Juan, Chinchón and Miranda. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 107 elements, 102 identified (95.3%).

03 The decipherment

f72r

por fuerça [?] con el parecer de todos los amigos. No ay duda que lo de venir con la princesa como dize V. E., siendo con las condiciones que V. E. apunta, seria lo que mejor estuviesse, pero hasta agora no ay nada en esto, a lo menos no lo sabemos.

Translation. By force [?], with the opinion of all our friends. There is no doubt that coming with the princess, as Your Excellency says, on the conditions Your Excellency specifies, would be best; but so far there is nothing in this, at least as far as we know.

f72v

Estamos tratando con don Baltasar de hacer algun presente a don Christoual, que es el que mand[a] todo, y no se perdera ocasion ninguna en que se ofrezca hacer el servicio de V. E. que no se haga.

Translation. We are discussing with Don Baltasar giving some present to Don Cristóbal, who commands everything; no opportunity to serve Your Excellency will be left unused.
