12 October 1901 · Mexico

Meza Salinas to Bernardo Reyes

Go to the full text ↓

Meza Salinas warns Bernardo Reyes that the Flores Magón brothers plan to distribute a pamphlet.

01 The cipher and key

The Becks reconstructed 46 number values from the Spanish context in the letter. The key produces each of the 23 literal runs, covering 142 groups. It preserves the written forms florse, insultads and presidnntn. Brice Dudout pointed out a period typescript of the decipherment in the adjacent archive record.

02 What remains open

The period typescript settles the three words where the cipher output has slips. The earlier review counts 21 words recovered from the key and three from the period sheet. The contributors report that they did not use that sheet to derive the key. Their browsing chronology is unavailable.

Reading

reading.md

Flores, insultados and presidente are attested by the contemporary typescript. The literal file preserves the cipher?s spellings. Accents, capitalization, word spacing and sentence punctuation are normalized; accents are not encoded by the numerical groups.

Translation

translation.md

12 October 1901

General Don Bernardo Reyes

Dear General and friend,

The Flores Magón brothers will publish a leaflet on the 23rd of this month in which you and the president will be insulted, supposedly with the aim of exposing the administration's immorality before the Pan-American Congress. Forty thousand copies will be distributed; they have already been ordered printed.

When I can speak with you, or with someone trustworthy, I will tell you everything that has been done and is being done in the states.

Your friend, who holds you in great esteem,

Meza Salinas

`Brothers` makes the plural `Los ... Magón` explicit; `hermanos` is not itself written. `Leaflet` renders `hoja suelta`, a printed sheet or broadside. `Supposedly` preserves the writer's distancing `dizque`. `The states` must not be expanded to `the United States`. The text does not name the president; identifying the officeholder is historical context rather than a decrypted name. No individual brother is named in the letter.
