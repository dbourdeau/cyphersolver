Berlin → Moscow · digit-pair table in a square transposition · May–June 1921 · table recovered by a contributor

Krassin’s Nr 451 and Lutowinoff’s Nr 523, Berlin to Moscow, 1921

Go to the full text ↓

Two Soviet radiograms to Chicherin that the Americans intercepted and could not read were deciphered from the ciphertext alone; they report the audit of the Berlin trade mission and protest against Kopp.

Daniel Bourdeau · posted 10 November 2026

Method: key recovered from ciphertext-only · Extent: complete · On the map

Contributed by Harumi-Matsuba (solution)

At a glanceCiphertext-only break · complete

The messages
Funkspruch Nr 451 of 30 May 1921 from Krasin (signed KRASSIN), repeated on 14 June, and Nr 523 of 14 June from Lutovinov (signed LUTOWINOFF), both from Berlin to Chicherin in Moscow.
Where
National Archives, RG 59, Central Decimal File 861.00, microfilm M316 roll 43 (NAID 604704577), frames 0427–0428 and 0432: typed copies of intercepts sent by Admiral Mark L. Bristol from Constantinople. Part of catalogue 669.
The cipher
Each letter is a pair of digits from a 5 × 6 table; the pairs fill an N × N square row by row and the square is sent column by column. N is spelled out before the groups: SOROK (40) and DWADZAT TCHETYRE (24).
How it was deciphered
Harumi-Matsuba, with Claude Fable 5.2, recovered the table by simulated annealing on Nr 451 alone, with no crib, and then deciphered Nr 523 with it unchanged. The project re-ran the decryption with its own code and got the same texts. The images were not seen here.
What they say
Krasin reports that the audit commission has found damaging material on the Berlin mission and asks Moscow to decide nothing before its report. Lutovinov protests against keeping Kopp in Berlin as polpred.
Still open
Two numbers and four pairs outside the table; eleven single wrong letters; no person has checked the transcription against the images.
Credit
The system, the table, the transcriptions and the reading are Harumi-Matsuba’s (issue #370), made with Anthropic Claude, with OpenAI Codex as a blind second transcriber.

01 The messages

From March 1921 the US High Commission at Constantinople sent Washington typed copies of Soviet radio traffic that a Russian operator took down; the State Department filed them in 861.00, now microfilm M316. The project’s work on the whole file is on the Kopp and Chicherin radiograms page, where the figure traffic had given no value. These two messages on roll 43 use a system of their own, so they have their own page.

  • Nr 451 (frames 0427–0428): “JUNE 14th 1921. FROM BERLIN TO MOSCOW”, “TSCHITSCHERIN MOSKAU”, “WIEDERHOLUNG FUNKSPRUCH Nr 451 VOM 30 MAI 1921. SOROK”, then 320 five-digit groups (1,600 digits), signed KRASSIN. The group /21545/ at the head of frame 0428 repeats the last group of 0427 and is left out.
  • Nr 523 (frame 0432): “TSCHITSCHERIN DLJA LITWINOVA MOSKAU”, “FUNKSPRUCH Nr 523 VOM 14 JUNI 1921. KOPIA TSCHITSCHERINU, MOLOTOWU SCHLJAPNIKOWU. DWADZAT TCHETYRE”, then 115 groups (575 digits, one short of 24 × 24), signed LUTOWINOFF.

The headings are as the contributor transcribed them. The digits come from the NARA catalogue’s OCR, with one digit corrected from the image. A blind second transcription of the images by OpenAI Codex agrees on 1,598 of 1,600 digits and on all 575 (his figures).

02 The cipher

Each letter becomes a pair of digits. The first digit is 6, 7, 8, 9 or 0, the second 0 to 5:

012345
6евьэкщ
7афязнр
8быглчю
9ж·шидо
0·тпхум

Outside the block, 06 is с, 10 a full stop, 33 a comma, and 00 fills the end of the square. The pairs are written row by row into a square of N × N digits, N/2 letters to a row. The square is then sent column by column, and the columns are not permuted. In Nr 451 the columns run first digit, second digit, and the rows read right to left. Nr 523 is the mirror image. The plaintext is telegraphic Russian, with й written и.

03 How it was found

The contributor noticed that the number word fits the digit count: 1,600 is 40 × 40. Cut into runs of N, the digits fall into columns of high digits alternating with columns of low digits. Pairing neighbouring columns gives about 30 kinds of pair, with an index of coincidence that suits one pair per letter. Simulated annealing on the 764 pairs of Nr 451 alone, scored with Russian letter 4-grams and given no crib, produced readable text. Three values were then corrected on reading (the full stop, the comma and ф), and 28 was taken out of the table.

The table was then applied unchanged to Nr 523, which had played no part in building it, and 279 of its 288 pairs read as sense. In the contributor’s control, none of 1,000 tables with the letter values shuffled scored as well as the real one on either message.

Checked here: a decoder written from the description in the issue, not from the contributor’s code, gives both texts identical pair for pair to his reading files. The project’s own n-gram validation has no Russian model, so it was not run. The NARA images could not be reached from here.

The contributor’s English: “The work of the Berlin trade representation is developing quite normally. From all business circles I receive the most flattering comments. The Auswärtiges Amt also notes a favourable change in the general atmosphere around the trade representation. From conversations with Lutovinov and Stomonyakov I conclude that the auditing commission has produced rich material which characterises the preceding period of the Berlin representation very unfavourably. They work like horses, and this delays the conclusion. I most urgently ask you to send Comrade Kulyabko to Berlin as soon as possible; his help in the work of the auditing commission is absolutely necessary. Under the agreement the visa must be given by Stähler, and I ask you to insist on this. In no case take any decisions or measures without waiting for the report of the auditing commission; otherwise you will confuse all its work and make it easier to cover the traces. When the audit is finished Lutovinov and Stomonyakov will come to Moscow to report in person.”

The pair 28 stands three times where ревизионная комиссия needs комисс, and 77 twice where работают and работу need раб. Both are outside the table and read from context only.

05 Nr 523, Lutovinov

вашэ письмо [16] июня получил. опре[о]эленоэ вами п[д]ложениэ коппа берлинэ нахожу абсолютно недопустимым. копп заявил, что наркоминдэл уже рэшил оставить эго берлине полпрэдом. эсли это так категоричэски протестую против его назначения до окон[ю]ания ревизии, а такжэ требую срочного указания ко[у]у сдать дела. черэз [76][53][18] часов сдаю бродовскому

“Received your letter of [..] June. I find the position you have defined for Kopp in Berlin absolutely inadmissible. Kopp has stated that the Narkomindel has already decided to leave him in Berlin as plenipotentiary. If this is so, I categorically protest against his appointment before the end of the audit, and I also demand urgent instructions to whom to hand over my duties. In [..] hours I hand over to Brodovsky.” In this message е is often written э.

06 What remains uncertain

  • Numbers: the day of the letter (pair 16) and the number of hours (76 53 18) in Nr 523. Each occurs once, so how digits were written cannot be worked out from two messages.
  • Pairs outside the table in Nr 451: 28 (комисс, three times), 77 (раб, twice), 91 (ц, once, before к москву) and 14 (once, taken as a slip for 94, д). Their values come from context only.
  • Eleven single wrong letters, seven in Nr 451 and four in Nr 523. The four in Nr 523 all look like a 4/5 confusion in the second digit; two readers agree on the typed digits there, so the contributor puts those errors in the sending or the interception. One digit of Nr 451 (высл[50]ть) is overstruck on the sheet and could be 5, 7, 0 or 9.
  • Counting each of these as unread, 750 of 764 pairs read in Nr 451 and 279 of 288 in Nr 523.
  • The transcription is by OCR and AI. No person has compared it with the images, and the project did not see the images.
  • The contributor found no earlier decipherment or printed text. The Soviet document editions (Sovetsko-germanskie otnosheniya vol. 2, Dokumenty vneshnei politiki SSSR vol. IV), the British GC&CS decrypts (HW 12) and the War Department copies (RG 457) were not read, and the text may be in print there.
  • The rest of the file’s figure traffic, in other systems, is unread (Kopp and Chicherin radiograms).

## Nr 451, Krassin to Chicherin, Berlin to Moscow, 30 May 1921 (repeat sent 14 June); M316 roll 43, frames 0427-0428

работа берлинского то[ф]гого представительства развивается вполне нормально. от все[м] деловых кругов получаю самые
лестные отзывы. аусвэртигес амт также констатирует благоприятное изменение общеи атмосферы торгового
представительства. из разговоров лутовиновым стомоняковым заключаю, что ревизионая [28]ия [14]ала бога[]ыи материал,
весьма невыгодно характеризующи предыдущи период берлинского представительства. [77]отают, лошади, чем задерживается
окончание. убедительнеише прошу, как можно скореэ высл[50]ть берлин т. кулябко, помощь которого в работе ревизионо[з]
[28]ии совершено необходима. визу, согласно договора, должен дать штелер, на чем прошу настоять. ни в каком случае не
предпринимаите никаких решении ли мер не подождав до[54]лада ревизионо[н] [28]ии. иначе спутаете всю ея [77]оту,
облегчите заметание следов. лутовинов стомоняков по окончании ревизии явятся для личного доклада [91]к москву

Proposed emendations: то[ф]гого → торгового; все[м] → всех; [28] → комисс (ревизионная комиссия); [14]ала → дала;
бога[]ыи → богатыи; [77] → раб (работают, работу); высл[50]ть → выслать; до[54]лада → доклада; [91]к → ЦК.

The work of the Berlin trade representation is developing quite normally. From all business circles I receive the
most flattering comments. The Auswärtiges Amt also notes a favourable change in the general atmosphere around the
trade representation. From conversations with Lutovinov and Stomonyakov I conclude that the auditing commission has
produced rich material which characterises the preceding period of the Berlin representation very unfavourably. They
work like horses, and this delays the conclusion. I most urgently ask you to send Comrade Kulyabko to Berlin as soon as
possible; his help in the work of the auditing commission is absolutely necessary. Under the agreement the visa must be
given by Stähler, and I ask you to insist on this. In no case take any decisions or measures without waiting for the
report of the auditing commission; otherwise you will confuse all its work and make it easier to cover the traces.
When the audit is finished Lutovinov and Stomonyakov will come to Moscow [to the CC] to report in person.
