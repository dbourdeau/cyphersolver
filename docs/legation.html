“George W. Erving at Madrid, 1807, and John Quincy Adams at St Petersburg, 1812” combined two letters in different American legation codes. Each now has its own page:

  • John Quincy Adams to the Secretary of State, St Petersburg, No. 88, 25 June 1812 (Adams’s own code, the = 1385, key recovered from the clerk’s decodes and Ford’s printed decipherments)
  • George W. Erving to James Madison, Madrid, 24 March 1807 (Pinckney’s legation code, the = 1651, from Madison’s own decode)
