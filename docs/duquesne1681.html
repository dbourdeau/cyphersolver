…muler nous sommes obligez de ne pas abandonner ce poste ni ces lieux sans les ordres expres

BnF NAF 22736 · contributed by George Lasry · French

Abraham Duquesne to Seignelay, 25 September 1681; later letter in the same September correspondence

→

Duquesne discusses negotiations with the Tripolins, the return of enslaved French subjects and captured ships, vessels suitable for use as fire-ships, treaty provisions and naval affairs. The later letter states the obligation to retain the position until express orders arrive and the hope of ending the campaign advantageously. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 751 elements, 743 identified (98.9%). His full report (24 October 2026) is reproduced below; the whole text measures 99.5% of words read as sense and the deciphered passages 99.3%.
