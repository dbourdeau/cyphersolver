What counts as further

One closed gap is a contribution: a value for a code group with every place it occurs, a passage re-read at the image, a key or a clear copy found in another volume or in print, a photograph from the archive. A reading becomes complete when at least 95% of its cipher tokens give sense, measured from a reading file, no gap is left untried, every letter is covered, and what stays open is scattered code groups or pieces blocked from outside (README, Conventions). Each step is credited on the write-up. How to send it: contribute.
