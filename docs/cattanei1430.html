These two letters are images 4 and 5 of DECODE R1854, which holds four letters to Gianfrancesco Gonzaga in four ciphers. The others have their own pages: a Gonzaga agent in Rome, 9 August 1428, an informant at Forlì, 6 November 1428 and Pandolfo Malatesta, 22 April 1428 (not solved). They were written up together until 14 October 2026.

01 Rome, February 1430: Francesco de’ Cattanei’s news

Cattanei’s letter of 13 February is wholly in cipher, a homophonic system with signs for et, con and -rum; he writes x as “cx” (dicxit, reducxit). It is a newsletter. The magnificent lord Carlo speaks of a marriage alliance with King Louis. A galley of Giovanni de Orlandis (a man named in the Rome letter of 1428) took a Catalan ship and was itself taken at Gaeta, a loss of five thousand ducats. The king of Castile presses Aragon. An envoy of the lords of Rimini left without obtaining anything. Then:

dominus a[rchi]episcopus de Patrasio cum licencia domini nostri dare volebat temporale dominium dominio Venetorum, et illud defenderet ab infidelibus. Dominus noster fecit proponere cardinalibus. Responderunt melius est perdere quam Venetis tradere, quia una vice recuperabitur; set si Veneti haberent, recuperare nunquam esset possibile.

The archbishop is Pandolfo Malatesta, the sender of the letter of April 1428 in the same record. Patrasio is the city’s name as the signs spell it, a Latin form of the Italian Patrasso: the two signs after Patras, read i and o with the key, are its ending, not a separate word (corrected 30 September 2026). Patras fell to the Palaiologoi in 1430. The rest of the letter concerns the chancellor of Niccolò da Tolentino, “creditur Nicolaus cito de Bertonorio debeat recedere” (Bertinoro; re-divided 5 October 2026), an abbot freed from excommunication, and six citizens of Bologna expected in Rome, “duos de maioribus, duos de mediocris, et duos de diminutivis”.

The three cipher lines on the verso of the second letter (21 February) report the Rimini envoys: “dominus Astorgius et dominus Bartolomeus anbasiatores dominorum de Arimino die lune comparuerunt coram comisariis et producxerunt mandatum et certas copias bullarum auctenticatas … credo parum iuvabit”, and in clear, “denarios non habeo pro expensis”. Full text: reading_p4.md.

02 Method

The transcriptions were made from the DECODE images, in crops at 4–13×; image 5 is photographed upside down. A homophonic annealer on the project’s shared Latin model gave Latin at once (post recessum, sollicitabo bullam, galeotam). A second transcription pass then checked every sign against the image with the key in hand and made 209 corrections, splitting look-alikes (a slanted and a rounded b, i and o; η and 9). The same key reads the verso. A test against chance and overfitting with a letter model (docs/_validate.py, on these two letters alone) passes.

03 What remains uncertain

  • 1,704 of 1,736 signs read as sense (13 February 1,505 of 1,537; 21 February 199 of 199), re-counted 5 October 2026.
  • Measured on the words of the readings (October 2026): the letter of 13 February (image 4) builds coherent sentences but reads at 90.6%, under the 95% bar; the verso lines of 21 February (image 5) read at 100%.
  • “leonebi … re[∂]bs” (l. 4; the image shows e b, so Leonelli is not forced), “aisignata pure st” (l. 5), “uniei … cille” (l. 11), “a-mhi episcopus” (l. 14, expected archiepiscopus): about 32 signs on a 786-pixel scan.
  • Names deciphered letter by letter but not identified: Trrusius(?), Berardelus, Bonifacius de Meana(?), Canedulum(?).

# R1854 image 4: Francesco de' Cattanei to Gianfrancesco Gonzaga, Rome, 13 February 1430: English translation

After the Florentine's departure I went to our lord [the Pope] [?...] fully about the coming of the Lord Marquis's
servant, who has not yet come. He answered: I shall do it gladly. The Magnificent Lord Carlo told me that if [?I
believed] the business [?...] did not have a place [?...] [?I would seek] a kinship with King Louis, and perhaps obtain
[?...] the petition [?...] Florence. I shall press for the bull and send it by [?Trrusius]. Giovanni de [?Orlandis] had a
galliot at sea, which took a Catalan ship and brought itself into the harbour of Gaeta; [?two] Catalan galliots came
there, recovered the ship with its goods and captives, and took away with them [?also] the galliot of the said Giovanni;
he loses the value of five thousand ducats. The King of Castile presses the King of Aragon hard; it is believed [?...]
will make him lose his state [?...]. [?Berardello], who had come here for those lords of Rimini to make an offer, et
cetera, left discontented and could obtain nothing. The Lord Archbishop of Patras, with our lord's licence, wished to
give the temporal lordship to the lordship of the [?Venetians], and they would defend it against the infidels. Our lord
had it proposed to the cardinals; they answered that it is better to lose it than to hand it to the Venetians, because
once [lost] it will be recovered; but if the Venetians had it, it would never be possible to recover it. Niccolò da
Tolentino's chancellor left here with his business well settled; hence it is believed that Niccolò must soon withdraw
from Bertinoro. Bonifacio de [?Meana] [?...] came here on the business of the lord abbot, his brother; our lord restored
the abbey [to him] and freed the said abbot entirely from all [charges] and freed him from excommunication. But the said
Bonifacio left here with a great company without taking leave of his host; it is presumed this must be because some
dispute has arisen between those of [?Canedulum] and them. Six citizens of Bologna are expected here, namely two of the
greater, two of the middling, and two of the lesser [orders]. ⟨Rome, 13 February 1430.⟩

Dominus Astorgius et dominus Bartolomeus anbasiatores dominorum de Arimino die lune comparuerunt coram comisariis, et

# R1854 image 5: Francesco de' Cattanei to Gianfrancesco Gonzaga, Rome, 21 February 1430 (verso): English translation

Lord Astorgio and Lord Bartolomeo, ambassadors of the lords of Rimini, appeared on Monday before the commissaries and
produced a mandate and certain authenticated copies of bulls. The said commissaries admitted them "if and in so far as"
[i.e. with reservation]. I believe it will help little. ⟨I have no money for expenses. Rome, 21 February 1430.⟩
