The Marburg cipher report: “damit ich aus allen fall entschuldigt sein”

Hessisches Staatsarchiv Marburg, HStAM 4 d Nr. 1218, no. 49 (DECODE R4500, “partially decrypted”). A German letter in clear text that switches to fourteen lines of digits and signs. The key was rebuilt from the cipher itself, and the reading was checked against a contemporary decipherment written over the first four lines.

Daniel Bourdeau · solved 22 September 2026

Summary. Volume 4 d Nr. 1218 at Marburg is Hesse-Kassel’s file of ciphers for officers and officials in the Thirty Years’ War. DECODE has put it online as about 120 key sheets and a few letters. R4500 is one of the letters. It reports troop movements (Rostock, companies on the march) in clear text, then continues for fourteen lines in digits 0–9 and a few signs. A contemporary hand wrote a decipherment over the first four cipher lines, which is why DECODE lists it as partly decrypted. None of the sign alphabets in the same volume fits. The key was rebuilt instead, by annealing against a sixteenth-century German language model and then fixing letters by hand from the words that came out: “damit ich”, “entschuldigt”, “underthenigst”. The contemporary gloss is the control. Its phrases “zumahl nicht”, “etwas tentiret werden” and “dieses orths halber” come out of the cipher with the rebuilt key. The whole passage reads except nine signs at the end of the first line. The writer has laid the matter before the Emperor and the Elector of Bavaria and asks to be held excused. About 97% of the signs read as sense.

01 The document and the key

One page. The letter has lost its date and signature, and DECODE gives only the volume’s span, 1635–1652. The cipher starts in the middle of a sentence (“noch nicht zu vernehmen,”) and runs until a clear closing that points to a “beygefügter Extract”. Words are separated by spaces.

The system is a letter substitution with a few homophones, written mostly in digits:

02 The reading

Normalised. Square brackets mark conjecture; … marks the unread lines.

… noch nicht zu vernehmen, die landts[…] …, wir dennoch nur seit ihr endes halber … ursach; das kann dieses orths halber observiren, dagegen den selben etwas tentiret werden solte, zumahl nicht unbewandt […] gebührend zur manutenirung dero Kays. Maj. und des Reichs wie auch [bey diesen …] ungelegen; dahero ich nicht unterlassen sollen, die notturft dieser [sachen] so wol allerhöchst ihrer Kays. Maiestet als auch ihrer Churf. Durchl. in Bayern ohnlängst nochmals … underthenigst und underthenigst anzudeuten, damit ich aus allen fall entschuldigt sein und dessen, meinem gebührenden bericht an gehörigen ort, nicht ermangeln möchte.

In English: this can be watched from here, and something should be attempted against them, especially since … the maintenance of His Imperial Majesty and the Empire …; I therefore could not fail to report the matter most humbly to His Imperial Majesty and to His Electoral Highness of Bavaria, so that I may be excused in any event, and not fall short of my duty to report to the proper place.

03 Method

Annealing without word spaces gave only frequency noise. After a second transcription that kept the word gaps and treated the joined “12” as one sign, a homophonic annealer on the de-1500s model produced recognisable words. Those words fixed the key. The gloss over lines 1–4 then settled the superscript sign (w) and 8 (o). At first it did not seem to follow the cipher line by line, but its phrases occur in cipher lines 3–5 in order. All 126 records of the volume (R4452–R4577) were downloaded to look for the key or a clear copy; none was found.

04 What remains uncertain

  • The last nine signs of cipher line 1, cramped under the faded gloss: unread. Line 2 reads after restoring an initial sign dropped in transcription on two words (graded C).
  • Single signs in lines 7 and 9 (“[bey diesen]”, “[sachen]”): conjectural, graded M.
  • Writer, recipient and date are unknown. The deferential tone toward the Emperor and Bavaria makes him an officer or agent reporting to Kassel from the imperial side.
