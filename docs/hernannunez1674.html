The letter
Four letters from the Spanish envoy in Stockholm to his colleague in Copenhagen, 1674, mostly clear Spanish with one to three cipher passages (1,716 groups in all).
Where
Same volume as the Marqués de los Balbases letters of 1677. The catalogue listed them as “noted”, deciphered in the margin, but how far that went was unchecked.
The cipher
The key rebuilt here two days earlier for the Balbases letters of 1677 to the same recipient.
How it was read
The margins were aligned with the cipher using seven anchors from the repeated phrase representado y representaré. The Balbases key, applied unchanged, reproduces the margins of all four letters; four values were added (167 Suecia, 161 resolución, os para, 6+ o).
What it says
Swedish diplomacy in the month Sweden entered the war on France’s side.
Still open
One illegible group; 99.3% of groups have a value without eleven uncertain ones.

01 The letters and the key

There are four despatches, of 5, 15, 19 and 26 December 1674 (DECODE R1012–R1015; the "1674–1677" in DECODE’s summary is a span, and the catalogue names give the dates). All are in the Brussels volume SEG 2559, Fuenmayor’s papers, which also holds the Carpio and Balbases letters of 1677. The cipher is the Balbases syllabic nomenclator: numbers for letters and consonant-vowel syllables (21 a, 28 s, 31 r, 46 de, 58 lo, 66 re, 70 ta), letter pairs with a disguised consonant (xo co, xi ci, af sa, if si, uf su, ac ma), and word codes: struck d que, bef Francia, dan -mente. 13 is a null in the Balbases key but e in this hand (see Method). The key is set out in full on the Balbases page. That it served Hernán Núñez in Stockholm in 1674 and Balbases at Nijmegen in 1677 suggests that Fuenmayor held one cipher for his correspondents among the northern Spanish envoys.

02 What the cipher says

These are the cipher passages, taken from the margins and checked group by group against the key. Spelling is normalised.

5 December 1674 (R1012)

…asegurándole en primer lugar que el reparo que hace del proyecto del tratado deste [reino] con esse reino es muy propio de su gran juicio y prudencia, y que creo ser cierto que estos ministros han convenido con el de Dinamarca en todos los puntos del proyecto que envié a V.S.; pero si la Dinamarca añadiese el de no oponerse a las armas que la Suecia pretenderá ahora, todos los artículos anteriores que contradijesen a esto quedarán anulados… y que las más veces no vienen a ser sino un juego de palabras con que pretextar lo que hacen y lo que no quieren hacer los príncipes. Ya tengo asegurado a V.S. que he representado y representaré siempre lo que importa que la Suecia tenga el freno desse reino.

A later passage concerns the Swedish envoy at Copenhagen. Núñez has told him of his obligations to his father-in-law, the Constable. Fuenmayor should believe him only where other sources confirm it, "siendo hombre lleno de artificios, sin más fundamento a veces que su propia inquietud".

15 December (R1013)

Con grandes promesas de la protección de la Suecia, que les ha costado buenos regalos, y deseando el enviado de Brandenburg enviar un gentilhombre suyo en su navío, estos diputados le respondieron no se atrevían por no dar celos a la Suecia… Hamburg, that is. The despatch goes on to hope that, frightened, the Hamburgers "quiten a los suecos mucha parte de la confianza", which would restrain Sweden and "alentará igualmente a los dinamarqueses".

19 December (R1014)

…la mira principal que este reino lleva en haber enviado a esse su embajador, según tengo entendido, es el tomar tiempo con esto para satisfacer a las instancias que hace este embajador de Francia, y el de no asistirle por ahora con el socorro que pretende en virtud de su tratado último. The marriage of Charles XI to the Danish king’s sister will be raised. The Anglo-Swedish treaty is only a renewal of the one Coventry concluded. …no hay otro medio de retener a la Suecia, y si esa corona se ajustase a la neutralidad, le faltaría a esta el único pretexto con que se [excusa con] la Francia.

26 December (R1015)

…antes de separarse este senado, que será dentro de muy pocos días, se tomará resolución en él sobre el asistir o no a la Francia a mano armada. Ha dos días que he cesado en el remedio de los baños… para salir cuanto antes a hacer las diligencias que me sean posibles para que dicha resolución nos sea favorable, prefiriendo la solicitud del real servicio al riesgo de la vida. The decision went against him: Swedish troops entered Brandenburg at the turn of the year, and Fehrbellin followed in June 1675.

03 Method

  1. DECODE’s ciphertext transcriptions (by MEG, 2020) were parsed into cipher groups and margin text. DECODE reads the clerk’s "q." as "g.3", so it was normalised to que.
  2. The monotone aligner written for Balbases 1677, run image by image, failed: the margin glosses cross page breaks. Run letter by letter, with seven anchors from representado y representaré (66 re, 30 p, ef se, 24 n, 70 ta, 48 do, 22 y), it converged in four rounds.
  3. The converged values (58 lo, 28 s, y n, uf su, 21 a, 31 r, struck d que) were recognised as the Balbases key. Applied unchanged, it decrypted all four letters in agreement with their margins.
  4. Second pass (22 Sept 2026): the 118 groups the key left without a value were each located on the sixteen DECODE images and compared with the margin beside them. 62 were DECODE misreadings (this hand’s closed 4 transcribed as 9, so “96” is 46 de and “189” is 184 sobre; g for y; groups split or run together), 48 were code groups the Balbases table lacks, fixed by the margin (bof guerra, cuf Inglaterra, 187 tiempo, 149 quanto, 111 comercio, C+ ll), 7 were nulls and 1 is illegible. Two key corrections apply throughout: 13 is e in this hand (13 8 el, 13 ef esse), and the underlined N is V.S. DECODE also omits two whole lines of R1012 (“…mas veces no vienen a ser sino un juego de pa-”).

04 What remains uncertain

  • Of 1,716 groups, one has no value (R1014, illegible). Eleven values from the second pass are graded M (uncertain): among them the triangle sign on R1012 (a, or a null), the nulls 500 and E, and R1015’s 21‡. The first pass had left 118 without a value (R1012 62, R1013 20, R1014 29, R1015 7).
  • Values added here: 167 Suecia (H: every context), 161 resolución (H), os para (C), 6+ o (C), 4 t (C). 30 = p is DECODE’s transcription of the Balbases 26 (C).
  • The Spanish above is normalised; it is not a diplomatic edition. The full key output for each letter, with its margin, is in the repository.

# R1012, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 5 Dec 1674

## Translation

Assuring you in the first place that the objection you make to the article of the draft of the treaty of this
[kingdom] with that kingdom is very much in keeping with your great judgement and prudence, and that I believe it
certain that these ministers have agreed with the [envoy] of Denmark on all the points of the draft which I sent to
Your Lordship; but if Denmark added that of not opposing the arms of Sweden, which is what this [kingdom] supposes and
now claims, all the preceding articles that contradicted this would remain annulled. And Your Lordship knows better
than I that there is no treaty in which there are not as many contradictions, almost as [?] [?], and that most times
they come to be nothing but a play of words with which to give a pretext for what princes do and for what they do not
wish to do. I have already assured Your Lordship that I have represented, and shall always represent, how much it
matters that Sweden should have the curb of that kingdom. I beg Your Lordship, if you have occasion to speak to him,
to give him to understand that I have written to you of the many obligations I have to his father-in-law the
Constable. I have thought I ought to tell him that from what Your Lordship writes me I find him as good a Dane as a
Spaniard, since it seems to me to be for his service to have this opinion held at that court. The said envoy is not
very partial by inclination; I have told him that I shall assure Your Lordship of this and of his merit, and that
perhaps he may be of service to Your Lordship in some things, provided you keep before you not to believe him except
in what is confirmed from another quarter, he being a man full of artifices, at times with no more foundation than
his own [?restlessness] […]

R1013, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, 15 Dec 1674

Hamburg has refused to carry a Brandenburg gentleman on its ship so as not to offend Sweden, bought with Swedish promises and presents; Núñez hopes Hamburg, intimidated, will withdraw its confidence in the Swedes, which would encourage the Danes.

# R1013, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 15 Dec 1674

## Reading

Con grandes promesas de la proteccion de la Suecia, que les ha costado buenos regalos; y desseando el embiado de
Brandembourg embiar un gentil hombre suyo en su navio, destos diputados le respondieron no se atrevian, por no dar
celos a la Suecia. Lo qual, junto a saver yo el gran fundamento que aqui hacen sobre la amistad con Hamburg y las
utilidades que della han sacado otras veces, pues, como V.S. save, le devieron el poner su exercito en estado de
invadir la Dinamarca en la ultima guerra que con ella tuvo este reyno, lo qual ha producido el buen efecto de
satisfacer mucho a este embiado del elector de Brandembourg; y espero produzca el de que, intimidados los
Hamburgeses, quiten a los Suecos mucha parte de la confianza que sobre ellos tienen, lo qual es cierto los retendra
mucho y alentara ygualmente a los Dinamarqueses.

# R1013, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 15 Dec 1674

## Translation

With great promises of the protection of Sweden, which has cost them good presents; and when the envoy of
Brandenburg wished to send a gentleman of his in their ship, these deputies answered him that they did not dare, so
as not to give umbrage to Sweden. This, together with my knowing the great reliance they place here on friendship
with Hamburg and the advantages they have drawn from it at other times (for, as Your Lordship knows, they owed to it
the putting of their army in a state to invade Denmark in the last war this kingdom had with her), has produced the
good effect of greatly satisfying this envoy of the Elector of Brandenburg; and I hope it will produce that of the
Hamburgers, being intimidated, taking away from the Swedes a great part of the confidence they place in them, which
will certainly hold them back much and will equally encourage the Danes.

R1014, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, 19 Dec 1674

Sweden's embassy to Copenhagen is meant to gain time against the French ambassador's demands for the aid due under the last treaty; talk of Charles XI's marriage to the Danish king's sister; keeping Denmark on side is the only way to hold Sweden back.

# R1014, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 19 Dec 1674

## Reading

Ya tengo dicho a V.S. en mis precedentes que la mira principal que este reyno lleva en haver embiado a esse su
embajador, segun tengo entendido, es el tomar tiempo con esto para satisfacer a las instancias que hace este
embajador de Francia, y el de no asistirle por aora con el socorro que pretende en virtud de su tratado ultimo; y no he
savido lleve dicho embajador orden de efectuar el casamiento de este rey con la hermana desse, pero tengo entendido
que hablara en ello. En quanto al tratado de que V.S. me habla entre este reyno y la Inglaterra, me asseguran que solo
es una renovacion del ultimo que ajusto en esta corte monsieur de Coventrie, embajador de aquel rey, de que embie
copia a V.S. en 24 de octubre; pero en el estado en que yo me hallo, casi privado del comercio de las gentes, puedo
engañarme en todo aun mas que hasta aqui. Y por lo que toca al conservar esa corona en nuestra union, mi parecer ha
sido, es y sera siempre que no ay otro medio de retener a la Suecia, y que si essa corona se ajustasse a la
neutralidad le faltara a esta el unico pretexto con que se escusa con la Francia, y parece imposible el que de[?]se
luego de socorrerla.

# R1014, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 19 Dec 1674

## Translation

I have already told Your Lordship in my previous letters that the principal aim this kingdom has in having sent its
ambassador to that [court], as I understand, is to gain time by this to satisfy the instances this ambassador of France
makes, and that of not assisting him for now with the succour he claims by virtue of their last treaty; and I have not
learned that the said ambassador carries orders to conclude the marriage of this king with the sister of that one,
but I understand that he will speak of it. As to the treaty between this kingdom and England of which Your Lordship
speaks to me, they assure me that it is only a renewal of the last one which Monsieur de Coventry, ambassador of that
king, concluded at this court, of which I sent a copy to Your Lordship on 24 October; but in the state in which I find
myself, almost deprived of dealings with people, I may be deceived in everything even more than hitherto. And as to
keeping that crown in our union, my opinion has been, is and always will be that there is no other means of holding
Sweden back, and that if that crown came to terms on neutrality, this one would lack the only pretext with which it
excuses itself to France, and it seems impossible that it should [?fail] then to succour her.

R1015, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, 26 Dec 1674

Before it rises the Swedish senate will decide whether to assist France 'a mano armada'; Núñez has broken off his baths to lobby. Sweden invaded Brandenburg that same month, which began the Scanian War.

# R1015, Hernán Núñez to Fuenmayor, Stockholm, 26 Dec 1674

## Translation

[…] to give you an account that, having been told that before this senate separates, which will be within very few
days, a resolution will be taken in it on whether or not to assist France with armed hand, two days ago I stopped the
remedy of the baths which I had been continuing for some days past, in order to go out as soon as possible to make
what efforts I can so that the said resolution may be favourable to us, preferring the care of the royal service to
the risk of my life, and to that of being left entirely deprived of health, which in my estimation is the greater
evil.
