Duke of Nevers to M. de Gondi, 27 March 1594

Arrangements to send money to Lyon and check Swiss muster rolls; a promised payment by the end of April and a mission to the aldermen of Lyon. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 245 elements, 230 identified (93.9%).

03 The decipherment

f91r — four complete body lines

facent monstre sur les vieu? rooles parce qu’il y va grand perte. Rasseure le Roy qui l’auroit le tout à la fyn d’avril selon que vous me dites. Au demeurant, j’ay despesché aussy ? Asdrubal vers le Corse et eschevyns de Lyon pour l’affaire

Translation. [They] muster on the old? rolls, because a great loss is at stake. Reassure the King that he would have everything by the end of April, as you tell me. Meanwhile, I have also sent ? Asdrubal to the Corsican and the aldermen of Lyon about the business.

04 Our check

This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch A. Image checked: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9058938q/f100.item
  • Folio matches the report: yes; cipher present: yes
  • What we checked: f91r opens "Monsieur de Gondy", clear French with mixed letter/number cipher runs; his key image is the original Fr. 3995 f122v-123r table (A=7/1/9, B=6/9 ..., Monosillables, Nulles 17 22), and the cipher sign repertoire (digits, Greek-like letters, nulls) matches it; a full 10-symbol decode was not completed
  • Verdict: partly confirmed (method read after matching with key from external sources, extent partial). The check is a sample, not a re-solution.
  • Notes: 93.9% identified and pages transcribed only in part. Key also in Cryptiana (no. 66), i.e. previously published key.
