Contributors

The ledger is a shared project. Anyone can add to it: a solution to a cipher already listed, a reading left partial taken further, a new unsolved cipher, a new cipher with its solution, or an independent check of a reading already here. Every write-up names who contributed and whose keys or decipherments it used; this page adds them up.

03 The project

The rest of the work is the project’s own: Daniel Bourdeau chose and checked the targets, and the transcription, cryptanalysis and write-ups were done with Anthropic’s Claude models in Claude Code (claude-opus-5-5, claude-opus-5, Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), agents run by Brice Dudout, chatgpt-astra-6). Of 703 profiled targets, 466 are the project’s own results, and 96 of them recovered a key by cryptanalysis. 237 targets came wholly or partly from outside contributors and are marked as such: they are left out of the LLM-performance figures.
