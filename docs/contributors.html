Contributors

The ledger is a shared project. Anyone can add to it at four levels: a solution to a cipher already listed, a new unsolved cipher, a new cipher with its solution, or an independent check of a reading already here. Every write-up names who contributed and whose keys or decipherments it used; this page adds them up.

03 The project

The rest of the work is the project’s own: Daniel Bourdeau chose and checked the targets, and the transcription, cryptanalysis and write-ups were done with Anthropic’s Claude models in Claude Code (claude-opus-5, claude-opus-5-5, chatgpt-astra-6, claude-fable-5-1). Of 386 profiled targets, 301 are the project’s own results, and 72 of them recovered a key by cryptanalysis. 85 targets came wholly or partly from outside contributors and are marked as such: they are left out of the LLM-performance figures. The project’s share is not ranked above: the table is for contributors.
