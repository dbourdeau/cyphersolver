Reference · corrections

Corrections

What this site has published and then had to correct or withdraw: the date, the pages, what changed, who reported it and why it was wrong. Newest first. A reading here stays open to correction; how to contest one.

Daniel Bourdeau · posted 16 November 2026

15 entries. The log starts on 16 November 2026; earlier entries were gathered from the dated notes already on the pages and in the repository, and are not complete before that date. The full history of every file is in the repository.

16 November 2026

The page said every departure of the reading from the mechanical decrypt was bracketed and counted 108. A letter-by-letter comparison found 362 of 1,709 words. All ten pages were then checked again against the images: the first transcription had merged signs (one code for three delta signs, one for two crosses), 379 words of the transcription and 34 lines of the reading were corrected, and 229 of 1,703 words now carry a bracket. Words read as sense: 86.5%, where the page had 94.0%.

Pages
memoire1560
Reported by
Cécile Pierrot and Camille Desenclos, The Conversation, 10 November 2026 (lines 9-14 of f. 152v)
Why it was wrong
Emendations were made without a mark, and the transcription used one code for signs the manuscript keeps apart.

16 November 2026

858 places where a reading printed a letter or word that its key does not give are now marked, in 31 targets; no wording was changed. Largest: kauderbach1754 (202 of 2,044 words), branicki1757 (150 of 2,247), santacruz1630 (119 words), lopehurtado1520 (59). For three contributed readings the marks are in a companion file and the contributor's text is left as given.

16 November 2026
