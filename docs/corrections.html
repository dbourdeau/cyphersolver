Corrections

What this site has published and then had to correct or withdraw: the date, the pages, what changed, who reported it and why it was wrong. Newest first. A reading here stays open to correction; how to contest one.

19 entries. The log starts on 16 November 2026; earlier entries were gathered from the dated notes already on the pages and in the repository, and are not complete before that date. The full history of every file is in the repository.

16 November 2026

The page said every departure of the reading from the mechanical decrypt was bracketed and counted 108. A letter-by-letter comparison found 362 of 1,709 words. All ten pages were then checked again against the images: the first transcription had merged signs (one code for three delta signs, one for two crosses), 379 words of the transcription and 34 lines of the reading were corrected, and 229 of 1,703 words now carry a bracket. The read share is now 0.865 (the scorer's measure, every bracket a hole), where the page had 94.0%.

16 November 2026

In the audit's 31 marked targets, 858 places where a reading printed a letter or word that its key does not give are now marked; no wording was changed. The audit found words departing from the key without a mark in 35 of the 54 targets its script flagged. Largest: kauderbach1754 (202 of 2,044 words), branicki1757 (150 of 2,247), santacruz1630 (119 words), lopehurtado1520 (59). For three contributed readings (branicki1757, lopehurtado1520, santacruz1630) the marks are in a companion file and the contributor's text is left as given. Counting marks as holes, eight texts classed as read now measure more than 5% holes; they have not been reclassed.

14 November 2026

Two counting rules were written into the README: unread words are counted by words, not by hole marks, and a hole marked with a size ([... 4]) counts as that many words; and a one-digit slip in a surviving period key counts as read when three conditions hold. Shares were recounted where the rules applied, for example ronquillo1676 R982 from 0.954 to 0.914 and fiesque1567 2 Dec 1568 from 0.953 to 0.943 (read to read in part). The list of every page affected is not kept in one place.
