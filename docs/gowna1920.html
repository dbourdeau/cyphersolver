01 The cipher and key

Beck recovered the reciprocal letter table from the damaged sheet. The keyword INTERVAL generates the table, but the issued keyword remains unknown. The method used already known examples of keyed reciprocal alphabets. A new replay reproduces all 19 submitted rows.

02 What remains open

The date word and opening and closing groups remain open. The collation gives 42 of 43 body words, or 42 of 45 units when the edge groups are included.

Reading

reading.md

> [Opening group] Description of two motor cars used at burning of Gowna Barrack morning [tenth] instant. No. one, large-sized dark-coloured open Ford car, appeared to be an old one. No. two, a grey-coloured motor van about size of police van, fully covered. [Closing group]

Spaces, punctuation and standard spelling are editorial. Both complete copies give `BARACK` and `OENTH`. The Longford form gives `APEAREDTOBEANOLDONE`, so the earlier brackets around “be” can come out. On your count that makes **42 of 45 units** secure in the combined reading (42 of 43 body words). The uncertain word is the day, for which the context supports “tenth”. The opening and closing groups can be transcribed in places, but their meaning is unknown. That count measures words understood across the copies. Individual sheets still have doubtful letters.
