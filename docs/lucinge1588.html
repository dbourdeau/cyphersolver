BnF Italien 2242 · contributed by George Lasry · French

René de Lucinge to Charles-Emmanuel I, Duke of Savoy, 9 June, 18 June and 3 August 1588

Lucinge discusses the king’s concessions, Rouen’s reception of the king, the Paris factions and the risks of civil war. Later letters concern Corbeil, Melun, marriage plans and the princess of Condé. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: Transcribed passages: 14,608 elements, 14,306 identified.
