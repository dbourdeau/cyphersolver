Campo a Landriano → the grand maître · invented-sign substitution with numbered codes · 30 August 1528 · read in part

Galeazzo Visconti to Montmorency from the camp at Landriano, 30 August 1528

BnF Français 3034 f. 154r–v, no. 68 (DECODE R4223, catalogue item 177, “unknown sender”). Thirty-nine lines of cipher in Italian, read with George Lasry’s 2023 key for Visconti’s cipher, extended on the letter and checked on two signed sibling letters.

Daniel Bourdeau · read in part 22 September 2026

Summary. DECODE lists R4223 as a two-page cipher letter from an unknown sender to Anne de Montmorency, “Non-decrypted”. It is unsigned, but the cipher is Galeazzo Visconti’s: Satoshi Tomokiyo’s cryptiana lists the leaf with two letters Visconti signed, and George Lasry’s key table of 5 November 2023 is headed “BNF Francais 3045f28 and 3034f154 – Italian”. Only the key had been published, not the text. The 39 cipher lines were transcribed sign by sign from the Gallica images and read with Lasry’s table, which the letter itself extended. 1247 of 1337 cipher signs read as sense (93.3%). Visconti describes a council of war held the day before at Landriano on whether the League army should march on Milan or on Pavia. He argued for Milan and lost: a scheme of “Francesco”, whispered to the Venetian officers, swung the Duke of Urbino and the proveditor to Pavia, and Visconti was interrupted when he spoke. The sibling letters show that the crossed sign is the figure 4, so the name groups 48, 4θ and 4r are numbered codes. No list of them survives, and they stay open with a few one-off signs.

01 The letter and the key

Français 3034 is a volume of original letters to Montmorency. No. 68, f. 154, opens with three words in clear, Poco scritti le lre, then runs in cipher for thirty lines on the recto and nine on the verso. The close and date are in clear: pregando n[ost]ro S[ign]or Dio dona a V. S. bona e longa vitta. Da al Campo a Landriano ali XXX d’aust. Landriano, south of Milan, was the camp of the League army in the late summer of 1528, after the collapse of Lautrec’s expedition to Naples.

The cipher replaces each letter with an invented sign. The signs include a tau for A, a round d for D, a bold k for R, a psi for I, a sign like ne for S and one like me for L, and a sign written like the word far for CHE. E has three forms and A two. Lasry’s table gave most of the letters. The letter added the values for ne, me, the small y (N, with a bent-arm form for B), θ (G), plain p (M), far and fu, and fixed q as H, or F in Francesco. It also showed that the long thin diagonal strokes under the lines are later word dividers, added by someone who worked on the letter. They are not parts of signs.

In English

Visconti writes that the council held the day before was meant to bring the captains present round to the view that the army should not go to Milan. That morning the Duke of Urbino came to the lodging of [48] to decide between Milan and Pavia, with all the captains, many of them, and the officers of the Signoria of Venice. It was arranged that each should give his opinion in turn. This, he says, was a device of Francesco’s, whispered to the Venetian officers, so that the vote would go against Milan, and the Duke of Urbino, the proveditor and most of the captains came down against Milan. Visconti was usually called first, “to open the way for the others”. This time Francesco was called first, then a few others, then Visconti. [4θ] said the army should go to Pavia. When Visconti began to argue for Milan, as he had written, Francesco was the first to interrupt, then others he is ashamed to name, and in the end he was let speak only with difficulty. “If it goes on this way we shall have a stew of bad digestion.” He sees much and can do little to remedy it. He asks Montmorency to see to matters for all the captains, in the service of [peu], perhaps the King. The Duke of Urbino and the proveditor have said that the army will go to cut the road between Milan and Pavia, and he will write the rest later.

“Francesco” may be Francesco Maria Sforza, the League’s duke of Milan, who wanted Pavia. The letter does not say so.

03 Method

The two pages are Gallica views 243 and 244 of btv1b90600370. They were deskewed and cut into line strips at full resolution. The line centres drift by up to 90 pixels across the page, so each strip was placed by measurement. A first rough transcription of 1344 tokens showed at once that Lasry’s table reads Italian. Each line was then transcribed again sign by sign against the table, and every value the table lacked was fixed from words that already read. A second pass redid the verso and retried every open spot on the recto.

Two other Visconti letters in the same cipher were then opened: Visconti to Angelo Bolano, Alessandria, 15 October 1528 (fr. 3045 f. 28), and Visconti to Francis I, in campo in la pieve de Loccate, 15 June 1529 (fr. 3096 f. 91). The key reads both (“mal tratamento”, “che disperat-”). In both, the crossed sign appears in numeral pairs, 42 and 48, so it is the figure 4. The name groups of this letter are therefore numbered codes, and 48 appears in the letter to the King as well. The groups rr and qq also recur there as units. None of these codes has a value anywhere.

04 What remains

  • Numbered codes, no key material: 48 (twice; the host of the council), 4θ (the captain who spoke for Pavia), 4r (before sapendolo dissimulare, and again in v03), rr (r21), qq (r09, before uolse), peu (twice; perhaps the King). Only a surviving nomenclator could fix them.
  • The final sign pair of so··, lo··, do·· (r10–12): the same pair each time. The sense suggests soi, loi, doi, one I value for the pair. That is a proposal only (I). It does not occur in the siblings.
  • One-off signs: [Y r] in r12, a small sign and a blot in r16, [v b] in r30, Π in v08 and v09. The lines read without them, so they may be nulls. That is not proved (M).
  • Illegible: only the blotted sign and the flourished ſt ligature in v01. A third pass (22 September 2026) enhanced the other spots on the Gallica native (autocontrast, unsharp mask, threshold, 2×): the middle of v03 is legible, and its ǂ-r pair is the numbered code 4r of r25, so that gap is a code; the r21 rr carries an overbar, so it is the code rr; fuo is Lombard fu, as in the letter to the King. The count is now 1247 of 1337 signs (93.3%).
  • Values read, sense open (M): la mia (r03), asciilati (r07), ascicsai pui (r09), acuvante (r21), lasoreno and se non (r26–27, C), and in v03 and v07 the runs marked (?).
