BnF Français 5581 · contributed by George Lasry · French

Writer in Mondesir, Isle de France

A private letter from Isle de France criticizes the governor’s conduct, weak coastal defence and food shortages caused by price controls. The writer also discusses an artillery appointment, the forge enterprise, debts, and selling his share to provide for his wife. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 4,923 elements: 3,950 deciphered, 949 separator marks (?), 24 unidentified.

03 The decipherment

f50v

même de son metier, aussi est on, on peu dire, ignorant presomptueux, entete comme un bas Breton qu’on est [?] se lai[s]se prevenir fortement souvent par le premier venu, entier dans ses idees qu'on a saisit promptement et mis à execution sans faire aucune reflexion ni consulter personne. Aussi fait on beaucoup de sottises et d'ordonnances dont on est obligé de revenir ou de laisser sans effets. [?] est vif jusque à l’emportement et aux exces…

Translation. Even in his own profession, he is, one may say, ignorant, presumptuous, and stubborn as a Breton. [?] lets himself be strongly prejudiced by the first person who comes along, clings to ideas hastily seized upon, and puts them into action without reflection or consulting anyone. This produces many foolish decisions and orders that have to be reversed or left without effect. [?] is impetuous, to the point of anger and excess.

f51r

la deffence sur l’agiotage des billets du Roy avec les piastres, sur lequel on a esté obligé de fermer les yeux comme sur la taxation des denrees de toute espece.

Translation. The ban on speculating in the king’s bills against piastres, on which they have been obliged to turn a blind eye, as with fixing the prices of provisions of every kind.

f56r, continuous paragraph, lines 3–6 — 56r

Et si après on veut maccorder des hommes et de largent et que jaye deux ans de paix pour me preparer a la guerre on verra la Différence du[c/n?] homme e un autre.

Translation. And if they then wish to give me men and money, and I have two years of peace to prepare for war, people will see the difference between [one?] man and another.
