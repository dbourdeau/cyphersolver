Avignon → Barcelona · one cipher line, unknown key · 4 December 1398 · attempted, not solved

Vico to Carbonell, 1398

A letter from the papal court at Avignon that ends in one line of cipher in an unknown key, known only from a footnote of 1920; the line was never printed and the original is not imaged.

Daniel Bourdeau · posted 15 October 2026

Fr. Pedro de Vico, at Avignon, to Guillem Carbonell, canon of Barcelona, 4 December 1398.

Unknown. Puig’s note 3: “Sigue una línea cifrada, de clave desconocida”, a ciphered line follows, in an unknown key.

All of it. Puig did not print the line, and no image of the letter exists.

01 The letter and its line

Sebastián Puig y Puig printed fourteen letters from the Schism papers of Francesc Climent, Benedict XIII’s agent in Barcelona, with their cipher passages deciphered. This letter, of 4 December 1398, is not one of them. He printed its clear text as appendix XIX and noted that a ciphered line follows, “de clave desconocida”, in a key he did not know, without printing it. Benedict XIII’s own cipher letters, whose key was rebuilt here from Puig’s one facsimile page, are on Benedict XIII to Climent, 1399–1403 (a different cipher, with its own page).

The original is not digitised, and the archivist Josep Mas noted that Puig studied many of these papers at home in 1920–30 and did not return them all. The line may survive in the Arxiu Capitular de Barcelona, or not at all.

02 What remains

  • The cipher line: not read. Blocker: needs the original. Without its signs nothing can be tried, not even Benedict XIII’s key.

03 Sources
