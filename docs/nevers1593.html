Nevers to Pisany, 1593: two letters in the numerical key no. 46

Tomokiyo’s Nevers cipher no. 46, a known key, deciphers Nevers’s two letters to Pisany of autumn 1593, the 8 September letter completely and the 14 October letter in long stretches.

Daniel Bourdeau · posted 17 September 2026 · updated 15 October 2026

Method: read with known key · Extent: partial (100% of the cipher)

Summary. In the autumn of 1593 Nevers travelled to Rome to ask Clement VIII to absolve the newly converted Henri IV; the Pope refused to receive him as ambassador. The BnF catalogue lists seven of his letters from the road as “avec chiffre”, three to Revol and one to Pisany in fr. 3985, three to Revol and one to Pisany in fr. 3986, and the new catalogue treated them as one office’s key with one passage already deciphered. They are in two keys. The letters to Pisany are in the numerical cipher that Tomokiyo catalogued from fr. 3995 as no. 46 (“Chelles, October 1592”): a two-digit alphabet with homophones, barred figures for common words, plain figures for a second word list, double-dotted figures for names, and the digit 1 dropped in as a null. Transcribed from the full-resolution table and checked against the office’s own decipherment of a Gondi letter in the same key, it gives a value for every figure of both Pisany letters. The 8 September letter is deciphered completely: l’on dissuadoit [le Roy] de rechercher [que le Pape] luy donnast l’absolution … [le duc de Nevers] alléguoit qu’il n’estoyt besoin qu’il allast sans cela, parce qu’il ne feroit rien … l’on n’a volonté de contanter le Pape, que l’on n’y aille poynt. The 14 October letter, written the day after Possevino told him at Poschiavo that he would not be received, is deciphered in long stretches: [le duc de Nevers] ne sera receu come [ambassadeur] pour pluseurs resons … n’avoir autre charge … [le cardinal Gondi, le duc de Mantoue] … m’avancer de plus pour faire ce que m’a esté escrit … tenir secret. The letters to Revol were written in the Court’s symbol cipher no. 60 and have their own page. Status: both letters deciphered, the 14 October letter with garbled stretches left; 390 of 391 figure groups take a value in the key.

01 The letters

The catalogue took seven ciphered copies of Nevers’s letters as one office’s key. They are in two: the five to Revol are in the Court’s symbol cipher no. 60 (their own page), the two to Pisany in no. 46, below.

fr. 3985 no. 116Nevers, 8 Sept 1593Pisanyf. 209 (canvas 419)no. 46, read
fr. 3986 no. 81“Ville” [Edolo], 14 Oct 1593Pisanyf. 168 (canvas 332)no. 46, read in stretches

Both volumes are microfilm digitisations on Gallica with every canvas labelled NP; the folio is close to half the canvas number, and the 1400 px canvases do not resolve two-digit figures, so the pages were fetched at full IIIF resolution (about 4,900 px wide). Tomokiyo’s Henri IV article states the division of keys: Gondi and Nevers used the symbol cipher no. 60 with the King and Revol from July 1593, and the numerical no. 46 with Vivonne (Pisany). The catalogue entry had not taken that in.

02 The key

Tomokiyo’s published image of the key is 225 pixels wide; the Gallica image of the sheet is 7,677. The table has a barred series from 99 down to 2 for common words (80̄ de, 56̄ je, 51̄ luy, 53̄ la, 11̄ que, 9̄ quil, 38̄ ne, 73̄ est, 5̄ recherche), a plain series on the figures the alphabet leaves free (24 sa, 28 sans, 29 se, 87 si, 45 tout, 62 vous, and the provinces), and a double-dotted series of names (99 Pape, 98 Roy, 88 Duc de Nevers, 70 Cardinal Gondi, 89 Duc de Mantoue, 54 Revol, 34 De Pizany, 1 Ambassadeur). In the letters the digit 1, and once 0, is dropped inside a group as a null, the device the sister key no. 45 prescribes in words. The conventions were checked against a letter the office deciphered interlinearly in the same key, Girolamo Gondi to Pisany of 22 September 1593 (fr. 3986 ff. 64–65v): personne, rencontre, congregation, absoudre, sainct, siege, ladicte, Romme all come out figure for figure.

03 8 September 1593, fr. 3985 f. 209

Three passages, 122 figure groups, all in the key.

  • “Car j’estois sur ce mesme faict” l’on dissuadoit [99̈] de rechercher [99̈] luy don[n]es[t] l’absolutio[n]. The copy has the Pope’s sign twice; one of them is the King’s (98̈) in sense. Tomokiyo deciphered this passage with the figure 14 for 74 and the name as Beaulieu; the figures are 74 and, in the next passage, 54 Revol.
  • “Car” [le duc de Nevers] alléguoit qu’il n’estoyt besoin qu’il a[l]la[s]t sans cela, parce qu’il ne feroit rien, then in clear “Je suys attendant des nouvelles de” [Revol] “à qui j’ay escript”. Nevers is named by his sign in his own letter; the 61 in allast is a slip for 65.
  • “Je suys d’opinion que” l’on n’a volonté de contanter le Pape, que l’on n’y aille poynt, and in clear “Et suys bien ayse que soyez de mesme volonté”. Three figures in this passage are the copyist’s (11 for the u of volonté, 14 inside de, 84 for 34).

04 14 October 1593, fr. 3986 f. 168

Seven full lines of figures, a short run and one more line: 269 groups, 268 in the key. The copyist’s hand makes 1 and 7, 0 and 8, 3 and 5 close, and the transcription still has garbled stretches. What gives continuous text: [le duc de Nevers] ne sera receu come [ambassadeur] pour pluseurs resons. Je luy ay demandé … se [le Pape] voloit que … e[ll]e m’a dit n’avoir autre charge, ne me declarans que … je me … conten[t] … qu’il de cela et luy … [p]l[a]inte de lettre m’est … faict aller … pluseurs resons … pour en par… [le cardinal Gondi] [le duc de Mantoue] avec de m’avancer de plus pour faire ce que m’a esté escrit, qui est conforme au nostre. Ce qu’il faict tenir secret, et le … dans sa … [le cardinal Gondi] a … s’est nostre [q]uestion, est bruit … secrete, tout yra … Tomokiyo’s fragment stopped at “Je luy ay demandé”. The doubtful groups are listed in the transcription file; a lexicon scorer over the alternative null placements did not settle them, and the next pass is a re-examination of those spots with the Gondi decipherment as the guide to this copyist.

05 The letters to Revol

Nevers’s letters of the same months to Louis de Revol, secretary of state, are in the Court’s symbol cipher no. 60, a different cipher, with its own page.
