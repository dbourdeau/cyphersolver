Girolamo Gondi to Duke of Nevers and Rethel, 15 May 1594

Negotiations over the Pope’s absolution of the French king, with warnings that the Duke of Sessa was delaying a settlement; references to Savoy and Monsieur de Maisse. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 1,469 elements, 1,449 identified (98.6%).

03 The decipherment

f9v, rows 10–13

luogo di queste parti doppa la reductione di parigi non abbiamo auto qui alcuna lettere di monsieur di reuol ne daltri ne fatta alcuna dimostratione di ralegramento con noi et si ua dubitando che si sia stato fatto qualche cattiuo uffitio da

Translation. … place in these parts. Since the submission of Paris, we have received no letters here from Monsieur de Revol or anyone else, nor any expression of congratulations; and there is suspicion that someone has done us a disservice …
