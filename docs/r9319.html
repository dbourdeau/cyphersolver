“Post Scripta”: dotted grilles of 1583

Grille sheets that picked a hidden message out of an innocent-looking letter; the letters they were laid over were not photographed, so the message cannot be recovered from these images.

Daniel Bourdeau · posted 15 October 2026

Method: not solved · Extent: text not obtained

not solved
On this pageThe recordWhat remainsSources

At a glanceNot solved · blocked by missing images

The document
Seventeen squared sheets with dots, docketed “Post Scripta” and “…83”: grilles for reading postscripts hidden in clear letters, 1583.
Where
Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv, Munich, Kurbayern Äußeres Archiv 4591 ff. 96–114; DECODE R9319, part of catalogue entry 161 (still open in the catalogue).
The device
Each sheet is laid over a clear cover letter; the dotted squares mark the letters of the secret text. One cover letter shows through under f. 102 (“Die 4 od 5000 Cronen…”).
Why it resists
The cover letters (ff. 115–118) are not among the DECODE images. Without them a grille gives nothing.
Still open
Everything, until images of ff. 115–118 are obtained from the archive.

Until 15 October 2026 this record was filed on one page with thirteen other ciphertexts of the same key volume. They are different ciphers, each with its own page; the largest group is System A′ of the dukes’ agents in Hungary, and the other piece of 1583 is a letter to Duke Wilhelm V.

01 The record

A grille of this kind is a sheet of squares the size of the writing on the cover letter, with dots in some squares. The recipient lays it over the letter, reads off the letters under the dots, and gets the hidden postscript; the letter itself reads as ordinary correspondence. The dockets date these sheets to [15]83. DECODE imaged the grilles but not the letters they belong to, which should follow on ff. 115–118.

02 What remains

  • The hidden texts. Next step: ask the Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv or DECODE for images of ff. 115–118, then lay each grille over its letter.

03 Sources

  • Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv, Kurbayern Äußeres Archiv 4591 ff. 96–114, via DECODE R9319 (login; publication of the images needs the archive’s permission, so this page shows none).
