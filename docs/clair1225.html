Paget? to Votre Excellence; identity unestablished, Genoa, 3 April and 28 August 1714

Negotiations to sell the island of Tabarka, secret intelligence and a disputed Genoese inheritance; the princess of Parma’s marriage to the king of Spain and the Farnese succession. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 495 elements, 487 identified (98.4%).

03 The decipherment

f58v

qualitez qu’on peut desirer. Elle achevera sa 22e année le 25 Octobre prochain, estant née le mesme mois en mil 692. Elle est bien faite, blonde, le visage rond, les traits fins et assez reguliers, et la taille belle, quoy que moyenne, beaucoup d’esprit et un air fort modeste.

Translation. Qualities one could wish for. She will complete her twenty-second year on 25 October next, having been born in the same month in 1692. She is well formed and blonde, with a round face, fine and fairly regular features, and a fine figure, though of medium size; much intelligence and a very modest manner.

05 Our check

This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch A. Image checked: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b9001034d/f60.item
  • Folio matches the report: yes; cipher present: yes
  • What we checked: f53r "Monseigneur", "Excellence ... le 19 du mois dernier"; codes with interlinear: "145.31.67.148.186.147.176" = Labbe Lomelini, "244.176.221" = Venise, "147.46.146.87 / 232.66.45.204" = l Isle de Tabarque, consistent with his Tabarka contents; f58v excerpt (princess of Parma) not compared; his key table not applied
  • Verdict: partly confirmed (method key recovered based on adjacent plaintext, extent complete). The check is a sample, not a re-solution.
  • Notes: Interlinear decipherments on ff53r-54r, 57v, 58r-59r give most of the plaintext. Princess of Parma = Elisabeth Farnese (born Oct 1692, married Philip V 1714) - excerpt facts are historically right.
