Siena no. 25: a lord on the road to Casole

An undated eleven-line slip in invented signs, which no key in the Sienese archive fits; its key came from the ciphertext alone, and it warns Siena that a lord and his men are coming through Radicondoli and Casole.

Daniel Bourdeau · posted 15 October 2026 · updated 23 October 2026

Method: key recovered from ciphertext-only · Extent: complete

The letter
A slip from Sienese envoys to the Signoria, all in cipher, unsigned and undated.
Where
Archivio di Stato di Siena, Concistoro 2308, fasc. 2 “Lettere in cifra”, no. 25; DECODE R4813, catalogued “1500–1599” (a placeholder) with no status.
The cipher
One sign per letter, 22 signs built on a running horizontal stroke, words divided by points; u and v share two signs.
How it was deciphered
None of the 43 keys filed in the same bundle fits. A staged annealing search with an Italian language model gave the key with no crib; two look-alike signs were then separated on the image.
What it says
A lord will leave by Lajatico, be at Radicondoli the next day and come on by Casole; Siena should provide “pane, viada, vituagia”.
Still open
Two words (18 signs) not understood and one code sign; the lord and the year.

This slip was filed on this site until 14 October 2026 with the rest of the Sienese bundle of cipher letters, in which each letter has its own key. The other letters whose keys were found are different ciphers, each with its own page: no. 18, no. 14, no. 4 (Borghesi, 1456) and nos. 6 and 24 (deciphered by George Lasry); the unread pieces and the 43 filed keys are described on the bundle’s page.

01The slip and its key

A slip of eleven lines, 496 signs in 65 words divided by points, all in cipher. Its 22 signs are built on a running horizontal stroke: uprights with one, two or three bars, crosses, a Greek-looking π. The transcription was made from the DECODE photograph; a staged annealing search over the words with an Italian language model gave the key with no crib, and a check of every doubtful word on the image separated two look-alike signs (a plain y is m, a hooked χ is r). 477 of the 496 signs (96.2%) fall in words that make Italian sense; 18 are in two words not yet understood and one sign, standing alone, is unread.

02What it says

Noi aviamo chompr(e)so che questo signiore no chavalcharà per lo modo che chostoro di qui vorevero, e diliverà senza altra presa fare di qui partirsi e d’esare chostà; e crediamo sarà sabato prossimo; e farano la via di Laiaticho, e poi di là si partirano, e l’altro dì sarano a Radichondoli, e per quello paese e ancho a Chasole, e per paese poi chostà. E però richordiamo a la signioria vostra che ne’ detti luoghi faciate provedere di pane, viada, vituagia, chome vi pare chovenirsi … per fante propio significharemo a la magnificie(n)za vostra.

“We have understood that this lord will not ride in the way those here would like, and has decided, without further delay, to leave here and come there; we think it will be next Saturday. They will take the road by Lajatico, leave from there, and the day after be at Radicondoli, and through that country and Casole on to you. So we remind your lordship to have bread, fodder and victuals laid in at those places as you think fit.” The envoys write from the Volterra side, on the road that enters the Sienese state at Radicondoli and Casole. The slip is undated and the lord is not named.

The word presa (“senza altra presa fare”) was checked again on the full-resolution image on 30 September 2026: its second sign is the hooked χ (r), which rules out spesa.

03What remains

  • Two words, 18 signs, not understood (read provisionally s’hadra and o chon grediamo), and one sign standing alone between points, probably a code; each occurs once.
  • The lord, the date and the place of writing.

Siena, Concistoro 2308 fasc. 2 no. 25 (DECODE R4813), undated

An undated, unsigned slip: a lord will not ride as the people there wished but will leave without further halt, by Lajatico, then Radicondoli and Casole, probably next Saturday; the Signoria of Siena is asked to provide bread, wine and fodder in those places. Key recovered ciphertext-only; 477 of 496 signs in words read as Italian.

What it is: a small slip (landscape), entirely in cipher, 11 lines (not 12: row-profile and line crops agree),
  written x ~ 8%-96%, y ~ 26%-72%.  No clear text, address, date or signature on the photographed side.

SYSTEM AS SEEN: word-divided.  Words are separated by a point (sometimes a gap only); ' / ' in the text.
The hand draws a running horizontal stroke at x-height through most uprights, so many signs are "uprights on
the line".  Segmentation rule used: each separate upright (with its own head/foot/crossing shape) = one sign;
the running stroke itself is not a sign.

SIGN LEGEND (21 names after rev. 2)
----------------------
  I     "⌶": upright with a serif bar at the top AND a serif bar at the foot (I with serifs)
  J     upright with a short spur to the LEFT at mid-height, joined to the running stroke ("⊣", a 4 without loop)
  +     plain cross: upright crossed once by the running stroke
  H     two short uprights side by side hanging on the running stroke ("ǁ" on the bar).  19 of the 21 H follow
        "+" directly ("+ H" = the "+ǁ" / "+++" group of three uprights on one bar); 2 do not (L07 "PH2 H I",
        L09 "HL H F").  Kept as separate tokens; an analyst may test "+ H" as ONE sign.
  F     cross whose bar runs on to the right and ends in a short second upright ("+ı" joined; the bar is held
        between a crossed upright and a short right upright)
  X3    "ǂ": upright crossed twice (running stroke + a second, lower bar)
  PI    "π": bar with two legs (left leg often slanting)
  HL    "Γ"/"ʃ": top stroke to the right from a long left stem that descends below the line, often curved
        (a large ʃ); distinct from S by size and the descent below the line
  S     "ς/ϛ": small zigzag on the line (short top stroke, diagonal down-left, foot turned right)
  Q     upright with a small closed loop at the head, crossed by the running stroke (looped-top cross)
  R     angular hooked sign: stem slanting down-left from a loop/hook at the top right ("ρ"/"9"-like but
        angular, open), usually attached to the running stroke
  9     figure 9: round closed bowl with a curved tail below
  B     "б/6": stem coming down from above the line, curling into a closed bowl on the line, stem continuing
        below the line
  X     "χ": x whose upper-left arm is curved/hooked and crosses to a foot at lower right.  The foot is sometimes
        very short, and then X looks like Y; 7 signs first read as Y were changed to X on re-check (rev. 2)
  XB    x whose upper arms close into a loop ("8"-like x); 2 cases only (L02, L11)
  Y     y: open V, the RIGHT arm running on as a long descender to lower left; no hook, no foot (rev. 2: 10 left)
  3     figure 3 / ʒ, descending below the line
  U     u/v open bowl
  PHI   "φ": closed bowl crossed by a long vertical
  PH2   φ with an extra open hook drawn on its LEFT ("ɕφ"): a distinct sign, not a variant of PHI in this hand
  TH    "Θ": circle with a horizontal bar (1 case, standing alone as a word, L09)
Marks: '?' uncertain reading, '??' illegible.  Notes in {braces} are not text.

# Siena, Concistoro 2308 fasc. 2 no. 25 (DECODE R4813), undated: English translation

We have understood that this lord will not ride in the way that these people here would like, and he will decide,
without making any further halt(?) here, to leave and to be there with you; and we believe it will be next Saturday;
and they will take the road of Laiatico, and then from there they will leave, and the next day they will be at
Radicondoli, and through that country and also at Casole, and through the country then to you. And therefore we remind
Your Lordship to have provision made in the said places of bread, fodder and victuals, as seems fitting to you, if
anything else s'hadra(?), or other things. This [TH] will decide, which for now we think, [?o chon grediamo], we shall
signify to Your Magnificence by a messenger of our own.
