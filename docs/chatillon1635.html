Châtillon to Servien, 3 août 1635

Châtillon criticizes the army’s condition and discusses withdrawal across the Rhine for greater safety. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 666 written positions, 624 identified (93.7%); one group boundary uncertain.

03 The decipherment

f127v, cipher continuation

Cette consideration les pourra empescher de s’engager si avant, mais Je Vous respon que nous ne jouons point de ruse cete fois, mais nous passons le Rhin pour nostre plus grande seurete afin d’avoir se grand fleuve entre les ennemis et nous.

Translation. This consideration may prevent them from advancing so far; but I assure you that we are playing no trick this time. We cross the Rhine for our greater safety, in order to have this great river between the enemy and ourselves.

05 Our check

This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch B. Image checked: none (no Gallica link in dossier; fr. 3758 ark not found offline, gallica.bnf.fr behind Cloudflare)
  • Folio matches the report: unclear; cipher present: yes
  • What we checked: his f127v crop: clear "mais Je Vous respon que" then 95(nous)...46(ennemi)...33(de)... codes consistent with his table, but sign-level read of 8+ symbols from the small crop was not reliable
  • Verdict: partly confirmed (method key recovered based on adjacent plaintext, extent partial). The check is a sample, not a re-solution.
  • Notes: Plaintext of the same letters elsewhere in the volume (f123v, f126r, f127r). 93.7% identified. Repo CATALOGUE lists Chatillon-Servien fr. 3758 nos.100,104 (ark btv1b9059471x?).
