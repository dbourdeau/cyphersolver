BnF Français 5160 · contributed by George Lasry · French

de Loménie Brienne to Servien, 10 January and 1 August 1653; letter B undated in selected cipher pages

→

Savoy’s policy toward Spain and the proposed marriage of the Infanta; the Duke of Mantua, Casale and the Monferrat dispute. Brienne also reports army news, the fall of Bordeaux and the succession to La Vieuville in financial administration. Deciphered by George Lasry, working with ChatGPT Astra 6 and Anthropic Opus 5.5, in his BnF cipher research of October 2026: 1,356 elements, 1,308 identified (96.5%); 40 nulls ?, 3 unidentified, 5 cancelled.

03 The decipherment

f18r

Les sages en jugent autrement je m’en remettray à leurs sentimens. Pour avoir droit de se plaindre du duc de Mantoue des graces qu'il depart aux Espagnols, il faudroit l'en rechercher de semblables ou de quipolentes, et sy il s'y rendoit difficile on pouroit avec justice luy reprocher sa conduitte. Mais de luy imputer…

Translation. The wise judge otherwise; I will defer to their judgment. To complain about the Duke of Mantua’s favors to the Spaniards, one would have to ask him for similar or equivalent favors; if he proved reluctant, one could then justly reproach his conduct. But to impute to him…

05 Our check

This result is George Lasry’s. This project did not re-solve the cipher: it checked one sample against the Gallica image and recorded what it found.

  • Batch B. Image checked: https://openapi.bnf.fr/iiif/image/v3/ark:/12148/btv1b9060495t/f24/full/1600,/0/default.jpg
  • Folio matches the report: yes; cipher present: yes
  • What we checked: f9r (canvas 24) after "ordres que vous avez receu": 36 60 33 n 39 x 21 , 6 28 ... -> de la co-n-du-i-te , que vo... = "de la conduite que vo(us)" with his table (36=de,60=la,33=co,39=du,21=te,6bar=que,28bar=vo); fits the f10r plaintext context. Excerpt f18r not imaged by me.
  • Verdict: confirmed (method key recovered based on adjacent plaintext, extent complete). The check is a sample, not a re-solution.
  • Notes: Clear copies of letters B and C in same volume (f10r, ff17r-v). 96.5% incl. 40 probable nulls; several nomenclator values marked ?. 1653 consistent (Bordeaux fell Jul/Aug 1653).
