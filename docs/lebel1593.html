Paris → Turin · homophonic cipher with a 300-number code · January–March 1593 · read in part

Lebel to Charles Emmanuel I of Savoy, 1593: three unread letters from the Estates of the League

Bibliothèque nationale de France, Français 3983 nos. 11 (f. 26, 17 January), 62 (f. 130, 10 March) and 100 (ff. 194–195, 27 March 1593). Satoshi Tomokiyo reconstructed the key from two letters deciphered at the time and published these three as images, with “many code groups yet undeciphered”. They are read here sign by sign.

Summary. The Unsolved Catalogue listed three letters from Lebel, Savoy’s agent in Paris, as having no decipherment noted. Tomokiyo’s article “Reading Undeciphered Letters to the Duke of Savoy (1593)” (cryptiana, 2017, revised 2023) gives the key he rebuilt from fr. 3983 no. 80 and fr. 3984 f. 182, and shows these three letters as images only: the key was in print, the readings were not. With his table, about a dozen corrections to its letter signs, and code values aligned from three letters deciphered in 1593 (fr. 3983 no. 80, fr. 3984 f. 182, fr. 3985 f. 23), all three letters read in substance: no. 11 about 93% of its letter signs, no. 62 recto about 94% and its bled-through verso about 62%, no. 100 about 85%. They follow Savoy’s hope of the French crown through the first months of the Estates General: Mayenne wanting the crown for himself or the regency, Spain paying him 40,000 écus down and assignations up to 260,000 and offering four millions, the Cardinal de Bourbon courting him with a marriage to his daughter, Philip II’s plans for the Infanta judged impossible, Feria’s jurist laughed at in Paris, and Mayenne’s councillors preparing a truce. Twenty-six code numbers occur in no surviving decipherment and stay open, listed in section 05.

01 The letters and the key

Lebel wrote to Charles Emmanuel I from Paris while the League’s Estates General met there to choose a Catholic king against Henri of Navarre. Fr. 3983 (Gallica ark btv1b9059406b) holds four of his letters in one cipher. No. 80 of 13 March was deciphered at the time on a separate sheet (f. 167); the other three are not.

The system is a homophonic substitution of one or two signs per letter (a = pp or 12, e = Ɱ or g, o = # or ✱, s = ooo or 4t), three nulls (4+, X, o-f), a sign for et, and a code of about 300 numbers set off by dots, with an alphabetical addendum above 300 added later in 1593. Words are not divided. The sign counts above are estimates from the line-by-line tallies, not measurements.

02 No. 11, 17 January 1593

Deciphered passages in italics, code numbers in brackets where the value is open.

…j’ay sceu et de bon lieu que le duc de Mayenne va de jour a aultre rentrant en opinion de [305] la couronne pour luy, pource principallement a ceste fantasie par [283 lettres?] de Rome … que le pape a dict qu’il seroit bien mal … si pouvant [130] la couronne pour luy, il la donneroit a un aultre. … A faulte de ce il desireroit fort la regence de l’estat, faisant naistre plusieurs difficultés sur tous les subjects qui pourront estre proposés, esperant que le temps luy donnant moien de regagner aultant du credit entre les François par quelques heureux succès de ses actions … Le second dessein sera fort a alterer par la resolution que le pape et le Roy d’Espaigne ont prise de vouloir l’election d’un roy desirée avec toutte affection des bons catholiques. … il taschera de favoriser celuy duquel il pourra non pas esperer mais recepvoir en effect plus de commodité; le Roy d’Espaigne luy [250] presenter quatre millions; le cardinal de Bourbon le [250] rechercher et solliciter, luy promettant d’espouser sa fille et tous les honneurs et biens qu’il vouldra desmander, disant que avec luy les catholiques principaulx qui suivent le party du Roy de Navarre se rangeront a l’union. Cest ouverture et proposition est favorisée … par deux des trois que le duc de Mayenne a choisis pour son conseil secret … qui sont [64] de [50], Villeroy, et le troisiesme qu’est le président Jannin s’y laira aisement ranger.

Mayenne, lieutenant-general of the League, wants the crown; failing it, the regency; failing that, he will back whichever candidate pays best. Spain offers four millions, the Cardinal de Bourbon a marriage with Mayenne’s daughter. His secret council is Villeroy, the président Jeannin and a third man whose title is coded [64] de [50]: historically Pierre d’Épinac, l’archevesque de Lyon, a value inferred from history alone.

03 No. 62, 10 March 1593

J’ay appris dès la mienne derniere que le mescontantemant qu’avoit le duc de Mayenne de [85] a esté remedié, luy ayant faict donner contantemant de ce que luy estoit deu, et estoit la somme plus grande que je n’ay escrit cy devant; il luy ont faict [275 offre?] quarante mille escus et donner assignation tant en [69] que Espagne, jusques au comptant de deux cents soixante mille escus. L’on estimoit que sur tant et tant de remonstrances qu’ont esté faittes aux [142 ministres?] du Roy d’Espaigne de l’impossibilité de ses pretantions … soit pour luy et l’Infante et le [163] qu’il se seroit resolu d’y faire [257] a quelque aultre de ses proches; mays ses ministres continuent plus que jamais en leur opinion, conduysant le duc de Feria ung docteur qui veult disputter la couronne par poincts de droict, dont l’on se mocque tout ouvertemant pardessa, ou l’on remarque aussy que le Roy d’Espaigne est tres mal conseillé de faire en ce tenps et en la face des estatz voir une armée moindre qu’aulcune qui soit encores venue, et l’argent plus court que jamays. J’ay sceu que les capitaines de Mayenne voiant peu de jour de pouvoir faire donner la couronne audict duc de Mayenne ny la faire tumber en la [240 maison?] de Borbon, tascheront de faire proposer une tresve; [ⅅX] partira bien tost … aller [253 trouver?] a Vostre Altesse de la part du duc de Mayenne. Je croy que ce sera le service de V.re Alt.e de luy donner tout contantemant et le caresser.

Here the harvested codes do the most: 24 l’Infante, 190 argent, 236 capitaines and 255 tresve are all glossed or aligned in the letters deciphered in 1593. The three cipher lines on the verso, after the news that Gondi, the Grand Duke of Tuscany’s envoy, is with the King of Navarre, lie under the recto’s bleed-through: a marriage [221], Lorraine [46] and the Duke of Lorraine [45] can be made out, about half the signs.

04 No. 100, 27 March 1593

…s’estant adressé a Vostre Altesse pour avoir preveu que si les premiers desseins du Roy d’Espaigne ne pouvoient reuscir, Vostre Altesse pourroit avec plus de raison que aultre [163 prince?] beaucoup esperer de cest’occasion … Je luy en disois de mesmes pour le regard de son compagnon et du président Jannin … Le [154 sieur?] Zamet seroit tres propre s’il en voulloit prendre la charge. … les desseins du Roy d’Espaigne pour l’Infante [163] … sont pleins de toutte impossibilité; les [257] que je re… du duc de Feria et du Commandeur Tassis me font esperer que ne pouvant reuscir lesdicts desseins du Roy d’Espaigne, elle soit resolue de faire parvenir a Vostre Altesse. … offices requis vers le pape et [22], pouvant asseurer Vostre Altesse que si le pape, voyant les desseins du Roy d’Espaigne ne pouvoir reuscir, inclinoit de faire [257] a Vostre Altesse … Sera ledict Cardinal de Pelvé … Il me semble qu’il seroit fort bien logé au [241 chasteau?] de Myollans.

Lebel has worked on one of Mayenne’s circle (probably Villeroy), and proposes money for him, his “compagnon” and Jeannin, paid secretly through the financier Sébastien Zamet. Letters from Feria and the Commandeur J. B. de Tassis let him hope that Spain, if its Infanta fails, will turn to Savoy; the Pope might do the same. The clear part adds the Provence governorship wanted for Vaudémont, Navarre’s blockade of Paris and Mayenne at the siege of Noyon; the last cipher line says Mayenne, once back, will send someone (the unlisted XC sign) to the Duke. The Miolans remark, whoever it concerns, points to the Savoy state prison.

05 Method, and what stays open

Each cipher line was cut from the Gallica images and transcribed sign by sign against Tomokiyo’s table, then read; the readings are in reading11_full.md, reading62_full.md and reading100_full.md. Corrections to the letter key found on the way: ∇ is also y; δ with a cross is d and also i/j; X is not always a null (it is an i in remedié); o→ is sometimes h; the long h stands for both n and l; ∠ and a barred λ are further s forms; an α-like sign is z (Zamet); a barred T is b; □ is u or v; “bm/vm” is d.

The code values came from the three letters deciphered in 1593. Glossed directly (grade H): 24 l’Infante, 71 Pelvé, 84 negotiation, 98 affaires, 192 choisir, 250 tout, 254 paix, 255 tresve, 297 catholiques, 327 Espagnols, 369 mandé. By alignment (C): 94, 100 condescendre, 102 aller, 104 dire, 115 celuy, 129 donner, 133 incontinant, 134 tost, 142 affectionné, 164 filz, 190 argent, 191 the jurist, 221 mariage, 236 capitaines. From context only (M): 235 président, 240 maison, 154 sieur, 161 ses mains, 163 prince, 241 chasteau, 283 lettres, 253 trouver, 275 offre. Second harvest (f. 182r-v re-read, esp. 336 no. 81): 85 Monsieur, 64 le (H), 113 celuy (the earlier 115 was a misread 113), 114 eulx, 331 disoit, 349 estoit, 368 ma, 408 part; 257 is glossed once “en la faveur de” and once fits “parvenir”, so it stays ambiguous. By history only (I): 50 = Lyon in “le [50]”, Mayenne’s third councillor being Pierre d’Épinac. Code 250 still sits uneasily in no. 11 (“luy [250] presenter”).

Open code numbers: 22, 27, 30, 49, 50, 69, 103, 106, 130, 150, 153, 161, 163, 227 (three times in no. 100), 230, 240, 241, 247, 253, 258, 260, 274, 275, 283, 302, 305, plus the ⅅX sign in no. 62 and the large XC sign in no. 100. Doubtful spans: seven short ones in no. 11 (ends of ll. 2, 20, the start of l. 6 under a smudge, v10), the three verso lines of no. 62 (about 62% of signs after subtracting the registered mirror image of the recto at native resolution: 221 mariage, niece du duc de Florance, the duc de Lorraine with the King of Navarre; one blot dense on both sides stays unread), and single letters in no. 100 (f. 194v l. 5).

Why these stay open: every surviving decipherment in this key has now been harvested: fr. 3983 f. 167, the glosses of fr. 3984 f. 182r-v and fr. 3985 f. 23, and the only glossed one of the three letters in BnF esp. 336 (no. 81, f. 159; nos. 79 and 80 are unglossed). None of the 26 open numbers occurs under a gloss. They can be fixed only by the office’s own nomenclator, not known to survive, or by context from further unglossed letters such as esp. 336 nos. 79–80, where 163 and 227 recur.
