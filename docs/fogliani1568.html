03 The decipherment

f103r, cipher lines 1–3; saved editorial reading — 103r

Io n[o]n posso mancare di dire a uostra alteza che mi pare che il [?] non habb[i?] fauorito le cos[e] del signor duca di quel modo che mi pare che douea, essendo lui che gouerna come fa in questi tempi, et se io pitessi dirle ogni cosa in poca scri…

Translation. I cannot fail to tell Your Highness that it seems to me that [?] has not [helped?] the duke’s affairs as he should have, since he governs as he does at present; and if I could tell you everything in a short wri…

f103r, cipher lines R3–R5

…che gouerna come fa in questi tempi, et se io pitessi dirle ogni cosa in poca scrittura, uostra alteza uedria che ha mostratto tenire poco conto di sua ecelenza, che mi ha fatto marauigliare assai, perche non lo haurei mai pensato, e[t] se…

Translation. …who governs as he does at present; and if I could tell you everything in a short letter, Your Highness would see that he has shown little regard for His Excellency. This has greatly astonished me, because I would never have expected it; and if…

f103v, cipher lines V1–V2

io fossi in parte che io le potessi parlare, le direi cose che le parerebb[o]no strane, come parsero a me quando soccesse il fatto. Io ho inteso in questo punto da una persona degna di fede et homo di qualita…

Translation. I were somewhere I could speak to you, I would tell you things that would seem strange to you, just as they seemed to me when the incident occurred. I have just heard from a trustworthy person and a man of standing…
