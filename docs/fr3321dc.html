03 The decipherment

f103r, first four lines

Les affaires sont en tel termes qu'infalliblement la Ruyne de Espernon s'en ensuivra, ou de cest estat. Car l'union est si forte qu'elle peult mieux donner la loy que la recepvoir. Demain la req[ueste] s'en doibt partir, vous en aurez Incontinent la coppie Comme on vous envoye du reste.

Translation. Affairs have reached such a point that Épernon’s ruin, or the ruin of this state, will inevitably follow. The union is so strong that it can dictate the law rather than receive it. The petition is to be sent tomorrow; you will immediately receive a copy, as the other material is being sent to you.
