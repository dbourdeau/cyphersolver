Excerpt

soies sure, que vous aures reusi dans vos entrepresi, enco[r] vous seres heureux, si vous non feres acun mau vaise usage —

Translation

Be assured that you will have succeeded in your undertakings, and that you will be happy if you make no bad use of it.

p351

Excerpt

Ceux, qui auront le bonheur, de décou[v]rir cet secret, seront heureux, etant bon chretien, et suivant ce, qui suit —

Translation

Those fortunate enough to discover this secret will be happy, provided they are good Christians and follow what comes next.
