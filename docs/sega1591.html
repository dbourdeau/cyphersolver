03 The decipherment

f148v, complete enciphered passage and cleartext opening

Che Lignerac è stato inuiato d'ordine di Umena in Poitu et Bretagna a solecitar la impresa di Tours per doue ua anco a questi efeto un religioso acciò se l uno fose impedito l altro pasi.

Translation. Lignerac has been sent by order of Umena to Poitou and Brittany to press the undertaking at Tours. A religious man is also going there for this purpose, so that if one were prevented, the other might get through.
