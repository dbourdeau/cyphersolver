Excerpt

Il n’y a point de doubte que S.A. ne ce sente griefvement offensé du refus faict de la levée des deux mil hommes sans congé ce qui n’est maintenant à propos. Regardez de reparer cela vous excusant…

Translation

There is no doubt that His Highness feels deeply offended by the refusal concerning the levy of the two thousand men without leave—something that is not appropriate at present. See that you put this right by excusing yourself…
