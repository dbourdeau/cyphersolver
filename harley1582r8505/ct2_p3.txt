# ============================ SIGN NAMES USED HERE ============================
# Names follow signs.md as it now stands (the f.264v pass revised it while this pass ran).
# Three signs carry values the old ct_p1..p4.txt files did not have, and they are the reason no
# sign in those files ever decoded to m, b or f:
#
#  :2 :3 :4   A CLUSTER OF 2, 3 OR 4 DOTS.  It is a LETTER, not punctuation: **m**.
#             ct_p3 wrote every cluster ':' and treated it as a word separator.  The dot count is
#             recorded only so the question can be re-opened; all counts behave alike.
#             Proof on this page: 3y900 "... :3 d G R PiG b L o o 7 :3 dd L ne L Xo E J Om w L n"
#             = "maison enne_mie de la chres..."; 3y301 "J PiG :3 :3 7 X L" = "comme le";
#             3y1004 "... y hg L w :3 L B 7" = "permette"; 3y301 "... E3 :3 sh dd Gm b n" = "moiens".
#             Statistically ':' = m also beats every other letter, and beats treating it as a
#             separator, on a word-segmentation score over all four sides.
#             CROP: img/m_sign_comme1.jpg - the two clusters spelling the "mm" of "comme" (3y301).
#
#  phi        A CLOSED OVAL PIERCED BY A LONG VERTICAL STEM, the stem showing above AND below the
#             oval (like printed phi).  It is **b**.  ct_p3 merged it with the tall long-s below
#             and so gave every one of them 'u'.  Proof: the line at 3y538/551 ends
#             "... wT E n Pi b | phi w psi n" = "...ra son bras", then the line-fill dash.
#             CROP: img/b_sign1.jpg - "b phi w psi n" = "-n b r a s".
#  f          A TALL LONG-S / f: hook or loop at the TOP OF THE STEM ONLY, nothing pierced.  = u.
#             (3y900 "hg sh f w X 7 wT L G Pi dd o ne wT L" = "pour le reioindre".)
#
#  eq         A SHORT VERTICAL STROKE WITH A SMALL CLUSTER OF 2-3 DOTS AT ITS UPPER RIGHT, written
#             as ONE sign.  It is **f**.  ct_p3 split it into 'I' + ':', i.e. a t-sign plus an
#             m-sign, which is why f never appeared and t and m were over-counted.
#             Proof: 3y1398 "...L :4 7 T wT L w psi eq 7 w :4 7 L o" = "demeurera ferme en";
#             3y1413 "X q y :2 E Xo eq psi dd wf L" = "luy mal faite".
#             A dot cluster with NO vertical in front of it is still :2/:3/:4 = m.
#             OPEN QUESTION: the two-dot cluster ':2' may itself be f rather than m.  :3 and :4 are
#             secure m (maison, ennemie, comme, mains, remedier, -ment, mariage, permette, armee,
#             moiens), but ':2' alone at 3.24 gives "du Roy son :2rere" = "son frere", i.e. exactly
#             where eq is wanted - most likely a faint vertical that was missed.  With only 11 ':2'
#             tokens on this page the segmentation score cannot separate m from f (-6337 vs -6318).
#             Left as read.
#
#  E3         EPSILON WITH TWO BOWLS OPEN TO THE LEFT ("reversed 3"), no crossing stroke.  = s.
#  E          LUNATE epsilon, a C with one middle bar.  = a.
#             ct_p3 had only 'E' and made every epsilon 'a'.  Verified here at 12x on four
#             epsilons: 3y282 opens "E3 sh dd b Gm o X L f w" = "soin en leur"; 3y1248 opens
#             "E3 sh wT B dd wT ne L" = "sortir de le"; 3y301 "7 T w E3 :3 sh dd Gm b n" =
#             "eurs moiens"; 3y1248 "... ne wT L E3 f wT X 7" = "espandre sur le".
#             EVERY OTHER 'E' IN THIS FILE IS UNVERIFIED on this distinction - the split arrived
#             late in the pass.  A dedicated epsilon-only pass is the single highest-value next
#             step; the a-count is still about 2.4 points above French.
#
#  #          TWO VERTICALS AND TWO HORIZONTALS.  A THIRD d-sign beside ne (1 vertical, 2 bars)
#             and H (2 verticals, 1 bar).  Left written '#'; dec.py decodes it d.
#             Proof: 3y1271 "hg d dd R # 7 J Gm Xo f y w0 q dd" = "pais de celui qui";
#             3y790 gives "ou sans doute" and "craindra"; 3y1398 "de ceus # e la religion".
#             Reached independently by three of the five readers, and it scores best of all 21
#             letters on the segmentation score.
#
# ------------- the shapes that decide a letter class, as seen on this page -------------
#  T   stem with a short bar to the LEFT at the top (or a lead-in dash at mid height) and a closed
#      loop at the foot = u.   b  loop at the foot, plain stem, NO top bar = n.
#      d  bowl with a stem CROSSING it and a small hook at the top left = a.   B  beta = t.
#      (b / T / d / B are the commonest confusion on the page.)
#  dd  a PLAIN c-bowl with a straight ascender on its right = i.
#      Eb  an EPSILON (bowl with a middle bar) with a vertical down its right side = t.  Near-twins.
#  hg  hourglass (X closed top and bottom) = p, repeatedly misread as X / Xo (chi) = l:
#      3y301 "L n hg w u B" = "esprit", 3y1004 "y hg L w :3 L B 7" = "permette".
#      That confusion alone accounts for the p deficit and the l excess in ct_p3.
#  J   reversed L, vertical on the right, foot running LEFT along the bottom = c.
#      L   L, vertical on the left, foot running RIGHT = e.
#  wT  omega + top bar + a vertical THROUGH it = r (one sign, not 'ne'+'w').
#      wf  omega + top bar + a stroke rising ABOVE the bar only = t.   w0  plain omega, no bar = q.
#  sh / PiG  3-4 rounded minims, or a Pi with three legs = o.   U  only two minims, with a tail = s.
#  U (s) vs Usq (g): U's bottom stroke runs on past the right minim as a tail; Usq is closed by a
#      bottom bar that stops.  Both occur on 3y1398 twelve sign-widths apart ("ceUs", "reliUsqion").
#  tl  one vertical crossed near mid height by ONE bar running only to the LEFT, stem showing above
#      and below = c.  ct_p3 read these as '7'; true 7 has its bar on top with nothing above it.
#  rho NEW, value unknown: a stem with a small closed loop at its UPPER RIGHT and a lead-in dash,
#      the stem running on down past the loop.  Not G (9-shaped) and not b (loop at the foot).
#      Written 'rho?'.  Three instances, on 3y1499 and 3y1531.
#
# ------------- what the new values do to the letter counts -------------
# Over the 3,825 sign tokens of ct_p1..p4, adding ':'=m and '#'=d puts m at 2.95% (French 3.0) and
# d at 4.26% (3.7).  Before this pass f stood at 0.03% (needs ~1.1), b at 0, p at 1.41% (needs 2.5),
# h at 0.18% (needs 0.7), against a 9.96% (7.6), u 9.83% (8.1), e 19.1% (17), l 6.64% (5.5).
# phi=b, eq=f, hg-for-X and E3=s each take a bite out of exactly the classes that were too fat.
# h (Om) is the one letter still unaccounted for.
#
