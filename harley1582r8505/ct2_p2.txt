# FOURTH distinction, found on this page and not in the ct2_p4 header:
#   hg  the HOURGLASS (⧗) - an X whose feet are joined by a horizontal bar, usually with a
#       bar or tick closing the top as well - is NOT the plain chi X.  It is **p**.
#       ct_p2 wrote X (or Xo) for most of them; that is why the old file had no p worth the
#       name and an excess of l.  Pinned at the very first word of the page: 2.1

# the reason this page had "oentilhomme", "alemaone" and "campaone":
#   Pi   π - the top bar OVERHANGS the right leg by a third of the sign's width, and the two

#        "conseruation des", end of 2.23; "eq Pi w :4 7" = "forme", 2.1.)
#   Br   a SQUARED ARCH - the top bar is flush with both legs, the corners are square, the
#        legs are vertical: a printed Π / n.  = g.  (Controls, all read at 12-18x on this
#        page: 2.2 "L Br Gm o wf ..." = "e gentil-"; 2.12 "E X 7 :3 E Br b 7" = "alemagne";
#        2.17 "J psi :3 hg Oi Br b 7" = "campagne"; 2.24 "Br T 7 wT L E" = "guere a".)
#   Usq  ⊔ - a square-bottomed U, bar at the FOOT.  Also = g.  (2.25 opens "Usq f L w Gm E"
#        = "guerre a"; 2.24 "PiG dd b Usq n" = "moings".)

#   file was re-checked for the overhang.
