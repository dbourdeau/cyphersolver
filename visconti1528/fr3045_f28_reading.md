# BnF fr. 3045 f.28: Visconti to Angelo Bolano, Alessandria, 15 Oct 1528 (first full pass)

Image: siblings/fr3045_f28_v54.jpg (Gallica btv1b9060156p view 54). Deskewed line strips (three line centres per line, measured
by projection at x=1000/2400/3700) are in the session scratchpad a5/ (L01_0..L20_2.png). **20 cipher lines**, not 21: the 21st line
is the clear close "la fine de la mia cum ricomandarme ala sua bona gra[tia]. Da Alexan[dria] ali XV de ottobre 1528".

Sign names as in KEY.md / final.md. Extra conventions used here: `L` = Lasry's A-form "L" (also written with dots, "L."; mostly A,
once or twice possibly I); `u` = Lasry's second R (bold u); `f` = N (Lasry N, crossed-f shape); `θ` = G (ogni, designati, Puglia) but
in "durissima" and "se vederà" a very similar loop reads D; `fir` = the sign siblings.md calls "far" = CHE; `ꞇ` hooked tau = Q.
`[..]` unread, `(?)` values read but sense open. This is a first pass: word readings marked (?) have not been re-checked glyph by glyph.

Gist: Savona has been relieved; the delay ("tarditate") will ruin everything; the allied (League) troops are desperate and all want to
go to winter quarters ("ale stancie"); the adventurer infantry are leaving for their captains' cowardice and deceptions; the Italians
have not had their money, which is in Puglia; the lances are few; the troops are discontented "per servicio di [ep]".
