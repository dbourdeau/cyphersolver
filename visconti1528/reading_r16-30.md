Tokens as in cipher.txt, plus: `d*` = E drawn as a round d with a long flat top bar (r18, r27); `q'` = the closed-bowl

the me-sign (M) likewise into `M d`. Those spurious extra n/d tokens are removed below.

## Key additions and corrections (with evidence)

1. **ne-sign = one glyph written "n" + looped e-tail.** "parso" r16 (o 7 k S n) reads P-A-R-S-O only
   if the "n" before the tail is part of S; same in "nostro" r17, "solito" r18, "posa" r30, "cose" r29, "questa" r19.
   The rough file's `n S` and `S d` pairs are single S.
2. **me-sign likewise swallows its e-tail**: "il camino" r19, "ali altri" r19/r20, "sapendolo" r26. The rough `M d` = M.
3. **E has a third form, d with a long flat top bar (d\*)**: "esere" r18 (S-E-M-P-R-E | d\*-S-E-R-E) and "dire" r27.
   The rough file had it as D.
4. **q' (closed bowl, stem rising through or above it) = F**, open "9"-shaped q = H. F in "Francisco" r20, "Francesco"
   r24, "fatica" r27, "fisse" r27, and probably "fine" r26; H in "ha" r20, "che" r16/r25, "chiamato" r18, "pochi" r20,
   "harà" r28. The shapes differ, but the difference is small (compare crops at r20 x≈480 "ha" vs x≈1260 "Francisco").
5. **z ("2") = A in r22 "da andare"**: the rough file had L there (D 7 L y ...). It reads D-A | A-N-D-A-R-E.
6. **y = B in "d'Urbino" r16** (D s k y Y X n), where the y looks like the ordinary y. Elsewhere y = N (canto, nostro,
   camino, andare, vergogno, pastone). Crossed psi X = N in "Urbino", matching Lasry's N row (not O).
7. **theta (8) = G twice in "vergogno" r25** (s d k 8 n 8 y n), and in "agio" r23 and "digestione" r28.
8. **plain p = M** in "maiore", "sempre", "primo", "camino", "modo", "mala", "interompere", "mia". The dot over the p of
   "modo" r28 is a stray point, not the hooked-p (N) form.
9. **Hooked tau Q confirmed**: "questa" r19, "quelo" r23, "qualche" r25, "que-sto" r27/28.
10. **Unresolved small signs**: "mur·" r16, "r r" and the heavy flagged p r21, "ǂr" r25, "v b" r30. They are all small
    r/u/v shapes, like Lasry's "Unknown" row (r·, v·). Dropping them gives good Italian in r16 ("il che è parso") and
    r30 ("in mia posa a provederli"), so they may be nulls, but that is not proved.
11. **Rough transcription errors fixed**: r17 `y n S` (not `y n S` + extra n), r18 `d* S d k d` (not `D S d d n d`),
    r19 `L 7 p Y y n` (not `Y Y`) and `Q s d S a 7` (extra d dropped), r20 F not H in Francisco, r22 z not L,
    r23 `S l k Y a n` (not `S d k`), r25 `L q d s y n` and `s d k 8 n 8 y n`.
