# Linear A: disagreements between lineara.xyz and SigLA

23 September 2026. Generated by `errata.py` from the collation in `collate_sigla.py`. Draft checklist for the editors;
not submitted anywhere.

Both editions go back largely to GORILA. For each SigLA multi-sign word that SigLA marks as confident and unerased
but that has no identical word in the same lineara.xyz document, the closest lineara.xyz word is shown. A
disagreement is not an error in either edition, since readings, ligatures and word division are editorial
choices. Documents with aliased names (e.g. HT 123+124a) are not compared.

## Systematic patterns

- **JA against PA3** (look-alike signs): SigLA reads PA3 where lineara.xyz reads JA on HT 115a (*47-NU-RA-PA3 /
  *47-NU-RA-JA) and HT 117a (TE-PA3-RE / TE-JA-RE); on HT 128a SigLA reads PA3 where lineara.xyz reads NU
  (TU-RU-PA3-SE-ME / TU-RU-NU-SE-ME).
- **I against *28B at Zakros**: lineara.xyz uses *28B where SigLA reads I on ZA 4a, ZA 5b and ZA 15a (I-NU-MA-RE),
  and ZA 6b (I-KU-JU-TI-I).
- **VIR joined to the following word**: SigLA includes AB100 (VIR) in the word on HT 11a, HT 93a, HT 128b and KH Wc 2100;
  lineara.xyz treats it as a separate logogram.
- **Word division**: six SigLA words equal two adjacent lineara.xyz words joined (e.g. HT 9a A-RU-KU-RO against
  A-RU | KU-RO, HT 95a DI-DE-RU-QE-RA2-U against DI-DE-RU | QE-RA2-U); the reverse occurs on the Zakros and Petras
  libation texts and PE 1.

## Readings that bear on published results

- **PH Wa 32**: SigLA **SU-KI-RA-TA**, lineara.xyz SU-KI-RI-TA. The identification with Linear B su-ki-ri-ta
  (Sybrita) depends on the RI reading.
- **HT 122a**: SigLA **PA-RI-NE**, lineara.xyz PA-TA-NE (compare PA-TA-NE on HT 94b, a KI-RO list).
- **HT 95b**: SigLA **DA-ZA**, lineara.xyz DA-ME (DA-ME recurs in the same list on HT 86a and HT 95a).
- **KN Zc 7**: SigLA A-KA-NU-ZE-TI, lineara.xyz A-KA-NU-ZA-TI.

## Corpus-level issues in lineara.xyz

- **HT34**: KI-RO quantity given as 37; GORILA and Younger read 30 with a 7 erased (30 [[7]]). On the tablet 100 - 70 = 30 (SA+MU+KU 100, PA3 70, KI-RO 30).
- **KH101**: Two records share the name KH101; one has an empty transcription.
- **KNZg57b**: Glyph array present but no transliteration array; KNZg58 is an empty record. Both are faces of the Anetaki ivory sceptre (Kanta et al., Ariadne 2025).
