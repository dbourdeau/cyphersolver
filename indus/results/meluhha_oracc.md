## The Irisagrig allotments

- Nisaba 15, 0371 (Irisagrig , Šū-Suen.06.12.00): na-na-sa₃; sa₆-ma-ar; "oil allotment of the men of Meluhha, royal donated slaves, shepherds of bezoars".
- CUSAS 40, 1354 (Irisagrig , Šū-Suen.06.03.00): na-na-za; sa₆-ma-ar; "oil allotment of the men of Meluhha, royal donated slaves, shepherds of bezoars".
- CUSAS 40, 1582 (Irisagrig , Šū-Suen.06.12.00): na-na-za; sa₆-ma-ar; "oil allotment of the men of Meluhha, royal donated slaves, shepherds of bezoars".
- The two names recur on 3 tablets of one year (Šu-Suen 6: month 3, and month 12 twice): a standing allotment, not a single mention. The first is spelt na-na-za twice and na-na-sa₃ once: an alternation of z and s that can point to a sound Sumerian writing lacked (an affricate), as foreign names often show.
