- 740 next to 520: 0; 520 next to 740: 0 (mutually exclusive, as the paradigm needs).
- 740: 1267 tokens; line-final 897 (71%); followed by 400 x166, 90 x85, 1 x20, 151 x9; preceded by a fish-series sign 97 (8%), by a numeral 87; commonest before: 176 x107, 100 x81, 760 x79, 32 x49, 752 x42, 220 x41.

- 3 long strokes + 520: 45 (the value never varies); a case ending straight after a bare numeral with a fixed value is unexpected; a lexical compound fits better.
