# The Harappan weights and the stroke numerals

- W1 472 weights (Hemmy in Marshall 1931 and Mackay 1938; Vats 1940 without the values flagged for 3/8 misprints, 92 kept); the best unit is 0.8585 g, with 384 weights (81%) within 5% of u x the binary-decimal series.
- the same with random series of 16 multiples (always containing 1), each with its own best unit: median 8 weights (2%), best 136. The binary-decimal series fits better than every random series.
- weights per series step: 1 x3, 2 x15, 4 x30, 8 x61, 16 x89, 32 x129, 64 x35, 160 x12, 200 x1, 320 x5, 1600 x2, 3200 x1, 12800 x1.

- W2 short strokes (the pairs left out): 422 tokens, values 1 x153, 3 x160, 4 x65, 5 x39, 6 x2, 7 x3; in the weight series (1, 2, 4, 8): 218 (52%).
- W2 long strokes (the pairs left out): 390 tokens, values 1 x145, 3 x209, 4 x18, 5 x15, 6 x3; in the weight series (1, 2, 4, 8): 163 (42%).
- W2 tiered strokes (the pairs left out): 185 tokens, values 3 x22, 4 x3, 5 x7, 6 x38, 7 x54, 8 x9, 12 x42, 24 x10; in the weight series (1, 2, 4, 8): 12 (6%).
- Of the values 1-12 that occur, 1, 2, 4, 8 are four of eight (half). The written numerals are dominated by 3 (short and long), a value the weight series does not have: the stroke numbers count things (or capacity units), not weight units.
