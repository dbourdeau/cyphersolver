# Thirteenth registered predictions: ten hypotheses

- samples: A 2562 lines, B 1554 lines; F 3666 intact home objects.

## H1 doubling means plurality
- A: doubled signs 400, 220, 390, 820, 615, 700, 892, 790; preceded by a numeral: doubled 23.4%, others 17.5%; difference +5.8 points, p = 0.0856.
- B: doubled signs 700, 400, 220, 405, 615, 390, 90, 832, 585; preceded by a numeral: doubled 25.7%, others 12.9%; difference +12.8 points, p = 0.0594.
- **H1 fails.**

## H2 compounds have a fixed order
- A: 155 pairs with count 10+, 29 bound; mean reversal ratio bound 0.027, others 0.199; rank difference -6.2, p = 0.2226.
- B: 71 pairs with count 10+, 15 bound; mean reversal ratio bound 0.016, others 0.050; rank difference -1.1, p = 0.4053.
- **H2 fails.**

## H3 diacritics keep the word class
- A: 52 family pairs; mean context cosine 0.117; p = 0.0001.
- B: 13 family pairs; mean context cosine 0.208; p = 0.0001.
- **H3 holds.**

## H4 name signs vary with distance, grammar does not
- sites (40+ intact texts, with coordinates): Chanhu-daro, Dholavira, Harappa, Kalibangan, Lothal, Mohenjo-daro.
- name signs (rarefied to 152 tokens): Mantel rho 0.314, p = 0.1211; grammar signs (rarefied to 100): rho 0.450, p = 0.0366.
- **H4 fails.**

## H5 frequent signs are simpler
- A: 221 signs; Spearman(complexity, log tokens) -0.129; p = 0.0254.
- B: 128 signs; Spearman(complexity, log tokens) -0.205; p = 0.0096.
- **H5 holds.**

## H6 tablets and sealings name two parties
- length 3-4: tablets + sealings with 2+ endings 0 of 590; seals 0 of 490.
- length 5-6: tablets + sealings with 2+ endings 0 of 249; seals 8 of 516.
- length 7+: tablets + sealings with 2+ endings 4 of 109; seals 15 of 310.
- stratified difference -0.5 points, p = 0.9675. **H6 fails.**

## H7 names are built head-final from shorter names
- A: minus first sign attested only 60, minus last sign only 27, both 9, neither 496; McNemar one-sided p = 0.0003.
- B: minus first sign attested only 18, minus last sign only 8, both 3, neither 139; McNemar one-sided p = 0.0378.
- **H7 holds.**

## H8 attributes are local, heads general
- 1207 names; MI(first sign, site) 0.564 bits, MI(last sign, site) 0.384; difference +0.180, permutation p = 0.0001. **H8 holds.**

## H9 graphic variants are local habits

## H10 heads come from a smaller inventory

- B: 400 names; entropy first sign 5.72 bits, last sign 5.24; difference +0.48, shuffle p = 0.0001.
