# Parpola 1994 rebus readings: corpus and lexical checks

## R1 Numerals before the plain fish

Parpola 1994: 194: "the numbers actually attested before the 'fish' sign in the Indus inscriptions are restricted to 3, 4, 6 and 7. The hypothesis of a Dravidian pun offers an alternative which explains this restriction". His Tamil list has star names in -min for 3 (mu-m-min), 5 (ai-m-min), 6 (aru-min) and 7 (elu-min), and 4 only through the homophone nal "four" / nal "day" (nal-min "asterism"); none for 1, 2, 8 or 12. Two long strokes (ICIT 32, M77 87) are left out here: Parpola reads them as 'intermediate space' (vel-min, Venus, R3 no. 15). The pair of short strokes (ICIT 2, M77 99) is his most frequent non-numeral sign.

## R4 Chance baseline for "the compound is attested in Tamil"

Picture concepts: a fixed list of 98 things the Indus signs are commonly taken to depict (set in the script before running it). For each, the Tamil words whose DEDR gloss names it; a hit is a word that is the first member of a Tamil star name in -min (31 distinct keys in Parpola's list) or of any -min compound (82 keys). Strict = same simplified form (no diacritics, geminates reduced) or +/- final u; loose = also ai/ay/ey and a dropped final -am/-m/-u/-i, the latitude used for mey/may/mai and vatam/vata.

- first sense of the gloss only: a Tamil word beginning a star name, strict 15/98 (15%), loose 29/98 (30%); beginning any -min compound, strict 43/98 (44%).
- any sense of the gloss: a Tamil word beginning a star name, strict 26/98 (27%), loose 47/98 (48%); beginning any -min compound, strict 67/98 (68%).

- median Tamil words per concept: 16 (the choice of synonym is part of the method).
