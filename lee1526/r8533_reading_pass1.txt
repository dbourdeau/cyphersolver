==== KEY NOTES vs Tomokiyo ====
- Confirmed: a=7 b=ɛ(ʒ-like) c=σ d=0 e=v(with tail) f=+ g=Δ h=3 i=- k=6 l=9 m=ɔ n=c o=⊥ p=† r=x s=4 t=ʒ u/v=δ y=ω th=π ch=÷
  codes and=‗(=) be=6 have=⊕ he=H hym=Hᵽ in=i is=-4/i? of=⊕/∂ sholde=∷ shal=∴ that=| they=T to=⸗ will=ᵽ/ɸ you=ɱ your grace=H(barred)
  French King=‡ (dagger cross), # (Tomokiyo 'nobles of Spain?') used in P2 L1 where Tuke reads 'would'.
- w is written as a hooked p-like sign (ᵽ) in words (writen, we, what), not only Tomokiyo's Υ; Υ also occurs (knowe, forwarde).
  So ᵽ is both the letter w and (standalone) the code 'will/wolde' - context decides.
- ℓ / ℯ (the loop signs Tomokiyo gives to 'for'/'if') behave as 'the' in several places ('colour ℓ thynge ℓ more', 'aske ℓ doughter',
  'ℓ duke of Richemonde'); P- sign before 'no thynge' = 'if'.
- ÷ standing alone (P4, four times: 'betwene ÷', 'the ÷ laboured', 'kinge ÷') is a code not in Tomokiyo's table (a person or party).
- Tuke's 'marquis' is written -,c,7,÷,δ,4 (twice), which the key renders 'in a ch u s'; either a miswritten word or a separate spelling.
