# Walsingham to Burghley, Paris, 12 November 1572 (BL Harley MS 260 ff. 364-365, DECODE R8359-R8360)

Catalogue item 126 ("Wallingham? to unknown recipient, 2 ciphertexts"). Session 2026-09-22.

## What the records are

Harley MS 260 is the letter-book of Walsingham's Paris embassy (fair copies, see `walsingham1572/`). The two DECODE
records are three consecutive pages, running headline "November 1572":

- R8359 image 1 = f. 364r: end of Walsingham to Burghley, Paris 12 Nov 1572 (clear: Cardinal of Lorraine and the
  ambassador in Turkey, Almains fear the Emperor's second son in Poland), signed "Fra: Walsingham"; then the start of
  a second letter of the same day, "To the Right hono:ble my very good lord the Lord of Bourleigh", on "the Scottish
  matters her Ma:tie desireth to be advertised of".
- R8359 image 2 = f. 364v, R8360 = f. 365r: the rest of that letter, dated "At Paris the xiith of November 1572",
  signed "Fra: Walsingham"; then the start of Walsingham to Sir Thomas Smith (Monsieur de Mansire sent to invite the
  Queen to be godmother), clear.
- Sender is Walsingham (DECODE "Wallingham?"), recipient Burghley (DECODE "unknown"), written at Paris, not London.
  Language English.

## The "cipher"

In the second letter the copyist boxed a digit where each cipher name stood: `[9]` five times and `[3]` once
("letters of recommendation unto [3]"), plus one blank ("As for the ... used by [blank] proceeded onely of
forgettfulness"). These are the same placeholder marks as in R8362-R8364. No cipher signs were copied. Digges,
*The Compleat Ambassador* (1655) (page not checked; djvu text in `walsingham1572/lit/digges1655.txt`, around line 23140),
prints the letter from this copy with the same marks ("[7", "[c|", "5!"). No decipherment is printed and none can be
made: the ciphertext was never copied into this volume.
