## English summary
Galeotto, Florence's envoy at Urbino, reports talks about the Countess ("Madonna", Caterina Colonna). Some parties
are to put themselves in the Count's hands. He advises that the Dieci send an ambassador, because the Count says
Madonna "is after all a woman, and suspicious". Men-at-arms and cavalry are discussed, and it is proposed that
Madonna's relatives and the citizens should secretly promise and bind themselves, whatever she might say. The
talks were long; he thinks the matter should be kept more secret.
