# Transcription brief for BnF fr. 3092 ff. 101-107 (DECODE R3699-R3701)

Cipher: Lasry's 2023 table for the BnF fr. 3029 cipher (image `../fr3029f134/key_f67.png`, sign descriptions in
`../fr3029f134/key.md`). Simple substitution, one sign per letter, French plaintext c. 1521, plus six null/space signs and
name signs Nom1-4 / Place1. Model transcription: `../fr3029f134/transcription.txt` (same cipher, read in full).

Hand notes established on f.101 line 1 ("on ne se devoit glorifier jusques a la fin et que tant de choses"):
- ♀ (circle on cross) = O; x = N or M (M = plain x, N = x with an extra stroke: decide by French); II (double T) = T
- the q-like / ϙ sign with a hooked tail = I (J); the ϙ circle-on-straight-stem = L: similar, decide by French
- ϕ (circle crossed by long vertical stem) = S; 6 (epsilon) = E; 7 = U/V; V = D; Δ = A; Π = C; Π with an inner stroke = Q;
  ₸ (T with two bars) = F; I-beam = G; h-with-o hook (ho) = R; 3-with-flat-top (ʒ/Z-like) = H; plain 3 = X; p-shape = Y;
  box with three legs (ⲙ) = P; S-shape = B; W-hook = Z (rare); "ᵽ/P"-ish forms: check against the key image.
- a small dot or slash between signs, ɔ, crossed 8, Ш, ℒ = null ("."). Tall ∫ = <N1>; H with bars = <N2>; I with foot loop
  = <N3>; 8 on a stem = <N4>; cross ✝ = <P1>.

Output format (one line per manuscript line, like fr3029f134/transcription.txt):
`103r01 <plaintext letters with word spaces, nulls as ".", name signs as <N1>..>   | optional note`
Use "?" for a sign you cannot read, "[...]" for struck-out text. Transcribe what the signs say, letter by letter: do NOT
normalise spelling, and do NOT supply words from sense where the signs differ. If a sign fits no French, write the key
letter the sign has and add a note. Where two readings are possible, zoom (crop the full-resolution image) before deciding.
Append each finished band of lines to the output file as you go (do not hold everything to the end).
