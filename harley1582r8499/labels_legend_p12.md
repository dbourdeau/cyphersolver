# Labels used in transcription_v2_p12.txt (p1.01-p1.30, p2.01-p2.32)

Each label was checked on the page images (my own crops of img/p1.jpg; img/L/p2_NN_K.jpg for page 2). Tokens not
listed keep their transcription.txt meaning. Key references are to key_R354.md and the key sheet images (kc*, kb*, key_null).

## The old `w` (31 on p1-2 + 1 `W`), now split three ways
| label | shape | key value | count |
|---|---|---|---|
| AND | ꝏ, "vo"-like: a wave or v-stroke ending in a closed loop | **and** (key ꝏ) | 26 (2 of them added where the transcription had no token: p2.18 after `C:`; p1.02 `W`) |
| OMEGA | plain small ω, no dot and no bar | key ω alone = **there**; ω̈ = z; ·ω̣· = d. No dot was visible on any of these, including p2.24 "was [ω] epartid" | 16 |
| HOOK_LOOP | a slanted stroke dropping into a small closed loop, like a reclining 6 or ɓ, with no bar | not in the key as drawn; it is the same sign as `6` without the overbar (see below) | 5 (w on p1.09 and p2.04 and p2.13; `v~` on p1.16; one `ce` on p1.13) |

**OMEGA_DOT (ω̣, d) was never seen on pages 1-2.** The "d" reading of `w` in "departed" p2.24 therefore stands on a plain ω.

`w=` is ω with an overbar and is labelled **OMEGA_BAR** (4: p1.19, p2.03, p2.18, p2.30). In the key ω̄ = **whereunto** (row "wherein / whereunto")
and ω with a long bar = thereabout.

## `x_` (18) and some `C`
| label | shape | value |
|---|---|---|
| XUND | looped x (ꭗ with a loop on the upper right arm) whose descender is crossed or underlined by a low horizontal bar | the key's i-sign x̲; context reads it as s ("west parts"). 27 in all: all 18 old `x_`, 6 old `C` (p1.23, p1.24, p1.30 ×2, p2.19, p2.27) and the old `3r` on p1.09 |

On p1.09 the old `x_` is really a crossed Y (**Y_CROSS**), the same glyph as `x+` on p1.11 and `ft` on p1.18. So Y_CROSS
(4) looks like `x+`/`Y+`: a Y with a horizontal bar through the stem.
**Old `C` and XUND look the same on the six places listed.** Other `C` tokens were left as they were because the crops did not settle them.

## Other relabels and fixes
- `6` = HOOK_LOOP with an overbar (a slanted stroke into a loop with a bar above). `-8` = a horizontal bar with a loop hanging below it (key δ̄ = c). On the images these are different signs. Four tokens corrected to `6`: p1.10 (2nd -8), p1.19 (2nd -8), p2.27 (2nd -8), p1.23 (`o=` was this sign, so that "pensions" o= reading needs re-checking). One `6` was inserted on p1.21 between `or` and `y=` (a sign the transcription had missed).
- HOOK_LOOP_UL: underlined HOOK_LOOP (old `v_`, p1.17).
- DARK_B: dark, heavily inked b/ƀ-like blot with an overbar (old `?` on p2.07 and p1.07).
- X_HOOK (p1.07, old `y`): a looped x with a long descending hook, like `xh`/`xo`. The next old `3` there is ξ, so it is now `xi`.
- G_BARDESC (p1.09, old `9_`): g with a bar across the descender. `9_` on p2.16 is a g with an underline and was kept.
- `H=` on p1.13 is underlined, so it is now `H_` (with). `q` with an overbar is now `q=` (p1.27, p1.28). The crossed `q` on p1.28 is now `q+`.
- `-a-` on p1.01 is now `-2` (the null —Ω).
- `e` on p2.26 is ε, so it is now `3r`.
- U_PLAIN (p2.32, old `u`): an ordinary Latin "u" followed by a dot, unlike the hooked ʋ that `u` is elsewhere.
- `4` and `-4` (Ƶ4, that) look identical on the images. `4` was kept but may be `-4` everywhere.
- Not changed, noted: `Ie` is an I-bar with a loop hanging from the foot (cf. the key's Tc/Ic sign, value not read). `N` is a roman N followed by a dot. `P_` is the £-like P, and its underline is often faint (e.g. p2.01). `2 .` is a "2" with a dot, not in the key.
