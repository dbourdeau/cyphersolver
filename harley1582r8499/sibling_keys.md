## Leads outside DECODE

- Harley MS 1582 holds other Wotton/Pickering letters that DECODE has not imaged (only ff.1-4, 8-10 and 263 are on DECODE).
  The BL digitised copy of the whole volume could hold another 1553-57 Wotton letter with glosses.
- SP 69 (State Papers Foreign, Mary) Wotton despatches are calendared in CSP Foreign, Mary (1861), which sometimes prints
  the contemporary decipherment. It is the cheapest source of cribs.
- SP 106/2 f.162 might have a companion leaf nearby (ff.160-165) that DECODE did not record separately.
