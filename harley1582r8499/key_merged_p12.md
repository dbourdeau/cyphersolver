## Conflicts resolved
- o= is o, not "had": "have money" p1.03 (o_ o= 7 ct 0 ^ H), "pensions" p1.23.
- / is "he": the key's "he !" sign, which the transcription writes as "/". This fits p2.23/30/31 and p1.03/p1.06.
- x_ is s, not i: "west parts" p2.06 and the p3 cases (keyfix_p36).
- > is o: "concluded" p1.16, "compassed" p2.24.
- y= is w: "west" p2.06.
- ß (ss) is r. The w reading from keyfix_p12 fails on "take part" p2.06.

## Still open (counted unread in final_p12.txt)
- w is both "and" (ꝏ) and d (small ω). It reads d in "departed" p2.24 ("was [w]epartid"). The two signs need separating on the images, line by line.
- ce (36 tokens) reads c in "commenced", e in "secretly" and perhaps a in "Cardinal". It is probably two or three look-alike signs, and the images are needed to separate them.
- @ (9 tokens) is written as "your highness", but ⊙ is the French king in the key. Several places ("the French king encreasidde the pensions") need the French king. Check each one on the images.
- y (18 tokens), glossed "us", rarely fits. It is probably the key's ·· = y, or another code.
- Unidentified codes: O (10), N (5), Ie (8), P_ (7), q (13, often "q ."), 2 (6), T, M, L2, V, the crowned box, 6, c,, b=, L, L=, p, phi.
